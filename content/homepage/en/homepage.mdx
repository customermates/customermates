faq:
  title: Frequently asked questions
  faqs:
    - id: faq-keep-crm
      title: Do I have to replace my CRM?
      content: "No. You can start by connecting LinkedIn or email and use Customermates for conversations, drafts and next steps while your current CRM keeps the pipeline. Import contacts from an Excel workbook whenever you want, and connect other tools through the REST API, webhooks or n8n. There is no built-in two-way sync with other CRMs."
    - id: faq-mate-send
      title: Does Mate send messages on its own?
      content: "Mate saves reply drafts for your review in the conversation. It sends when you ask it to, or when you set up a routine that sends. Your own AI client follows the permissions of the account it connects with."
    - id: faq-free
      title: Is there a free plan?
      content: "Yes, to try it alone: Customermates Cloud is free for [[commercial.limit.free.maxMembers]] person and [[commercial.limit.free.maxRecords]] records. Your first workspace starts with Pro free for [[commercial.trial.days]] days without a credit card and then moves to Free unless you choose a paid plan; every further workspace starts by choosing a plan. Free has no inbox, Mate or routines. You can also self-host the open-source core for free with the Starter feature set."
    - id: faq-who
      title: Who is Customermates for?
      content: "Small client-facing teams: small businesses, consultants, agencies, freelancers and startups. It also fits by workflow: client management, a sales pipeline, LinkedIn conversations or WhatsApp customer contact. Each of these has its own page under Solutions and an example workspace with sample data you can explore. The example lists show one way to configure Customermates; your own workspace starts empty and holds the lists you create."
    - id: faq-1
      title: How is Customermates different from other CRMs?
      content: "It is not the right fit for everyone, but it is different in ways that matter to small client-facing teams. External AI clients such as Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor operate the CRM directly through MCP with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools. On Customermates Cloud from Pro, email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram conversations sit next to the contact in one unified inbox. The core is open source (AGPL-3.0) and can be self-hosted, and the managed cloud database runs in the AWS Frankfurt region. If you need marketing automation, sequences, quotes or invoicing, a larger suite fits better."
    - id: faq-plans
      title: Which features need which plan?
      content: "Core CRM, MCP and the REST API are in every plan, including Free and self-hosting; webhooks and the n8n node come with every paid plan and self-hosting. The inbox is on Customermates Cloud from Pro: on Pro you connect your own accounts and share single threads; sharing a whole connected account with the team needs Business, Max or Enterprise. Mate, routines and AI setup run on Customermates Cloud only, within each paid plan's credits. A self-hosted instance has the Starter feature set without the inbox, Mate or AI setup."
    - id: faq-4
      title: Where is my data stored?
      content: The managed database runs in the AWS Frankfurt region, while application hosting and other providers, including US providers, are listed on our Subprocessors page. You can self-host the AGPL core CRM application and PostgreSQL with the Starter feature set; the unified inbox, calendar view, AI setup, Mate and Routines remain managed-cloud features, and connected providers remain separate.

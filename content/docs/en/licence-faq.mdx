title: "Customermates Licence FAQ: What You Can and Cannot Do"
description: "Answers on the Sustainable Use License for agencies, consultants, internal use, client setups, hosting, embedding and white-label, with the test to apply."
---

The Sustainable Use License covers the self-hosted Community Edition. It is free to use for your own business. Cloud is a paid subscription with its own terms; this page does not apply to it. The [Licence](/docs/licence) page explains both licences; this page answers concrete cases.

## What can I do with the Community Edition? [#what-can-i-do-with-the-community-edition]

- Use it as the CRM of your own business, for any number of users, records and workspaces.
- Run several instances for your own company.
- Read, change and extend the code for your own internal use.
- Use it for non-commercial or personal projects.
- Build your own tools around it with the REST API, webhooks, n8n and MCP.
- Share your copy or your changes free of charge and non-commercially, with the licence text attached.
- Charge for services around Customermates: setup, data modelling, migrations, training, workshops and consulting.

## What can't I do? [#what-cant-i-do]

- Host Customermates as a service for third parties, so that your customers sign in to your instance.
- Sell, resell or distribute Customermates or a modified version of it as a paid product.
- Offer a fork as your own CRM product, or publish a modified version under the Customermates name or logo.
- White-label it: remove or replace the Customermates name, logo or notices.
- Turn on or use an Enterprise Feature, or bypass the entitlement checks, without an enterprise licence.
- Remove or hide licence, copyright or other notices.

## What is the test? [#what-is-the-test]

Ask who administers the CRM workspace and whose customers pay for the CRM. If the CRM serves your own business, you are inside the Sustainable Use License. If the CRM, or access to it, is what you sell, you need an agreement with Customermates. Counting users, clients or revenue does not decide it.

## Can an agency use Customermates for its clients? [#can-an-agency-use-customermates-for-its-clients]

Yes, when the agency uses Customermates as its own CRM to manage its own clients, deals and tasks. That is an internal business purpose, and there is no limit on the number of clients. It changes when clients get access to a workspace you run for them; see the next question.

## Can I set up a client's instance and charge for it? [#can-i-set-up-a-clients-instance-and-charge-for-it]

Charging for the setup, the data model, the migration and the training is allowed. Deploying an instance for a client and charging the client for the instance itself needs an enterprise licence, because the instance is then what you sell. Ask before you start if you are unsure which side your setup falls on.

## Can I host Customermates for other companies? [#can-i-host-customermates-for-other-companies]

No. Hosting Customermates so that other companies sign in to your instance is not allowed under the Sustainable Use License, and it competes with Cloud. Ask for an agreement if you want to offer a hosted service.

## Can I embed Customermates in my product or white-label it? [#can-i-embed-customermates-in-my-product-or-white-label-it]

Only by agreement, and the Customermates branding stays visible. Embedding and white-labelling need a written agreement with Customermates that sets the terms and the fees.

## Can I run workshops and consulting around Customermates? [#can-i-run-workshops-and-consulting-around-customermates]

Yes. Consulting, training and workshops are allowed, and you may charge for them. The condition is that Customermates stays your own tool or a temporary, event-limited teaching setup, and that clients receive the result, not an instance to run.

## My company avoids code that limits commercial use. Can we still use Customermates? [#my-company-avoids-code-that-limits-commercial-use]

If you use Customermates for internal business purposes and do not make it available to your customers, yes. The restrictions concern selling, hosting for others and white-labelling, not running it for your own team.

## What do I need for Enterprise Features? [#what-do-i-need-for-enterprise-features]

An enterprise agreement. Enterprise Features are defined in `ee/LICENSE.md`, and [Compare editions](/docs/compare-editions) lists them. A Cloud plan does not unlock anything in a self-hosted installation, and a self-hosted installation cannot switch Enterprise Features on without an agreement.

## What if I want something the licence does not allow? [#what-if-i-want-something-the-licence-does-not-allow]

You need a separate commercial agreement. We want people to build with Customermates; we only ask for an agreement that sets out the terms. Use the [contact page](/contact).

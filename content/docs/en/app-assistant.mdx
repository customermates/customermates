Customermates Cloud includes **Mate**, a hosted in-app assistant. This page documents the panel for people and for AI agents that drive the UI. Self-hosted deployments have no hosted assistant; they connect external AI clients through the [MCP server](/docs/mcp) with your own provider instead.

## How do I open Mate?

Mate sits in the sidebar as **Ask AI** (`#nav-assistant`) and toggles with Cmd/Ctrl+J from anywhere in the app. Escape closes the panel unless a guided tour is running. Ask it questions about your workspace or the product, tell it to change data, have it operate the interface, or let it walk you through the app. It answers in your interface language and reads the product documentation in that language where a translation exists.

The panel is closed on your first visit and remembers whether you left it open or closed, per user and per workspace, across refreshes. Submitting a question or clicking a suggested prompt opens it. The only automatic open happens once on demo hosts. Demo embeds can force the initial presentation with the exact query parameter `agentChat=open` or `agentChat=closed` on the page URL; that parameter applies to the current page only and never overwrites your saved choice.

## What runs immediately and what asks first

Ordinary CRM work runs as soon as you ask: creating and updating records, notes, and record links; saving and discarding message drafts; sending emails and chat messages; inbox triage including moving email threads and changing a thread's state to unread, open, closed or spam; workspace settings; custom fields; creating and updating widgets and webhooks; changing a team member's role or status; generating account-connection links; social invitations and Sales Navigator list changes on a connected account; and routines (listing, creating, updating, pausing, running now). Mate says what it is about to change and reports exactly what changed. A routine Mate creates is saved as a draft unless you explicitly asked for it to be active; the runtime rejects a routine call that does not state this choice. To connect a messaging account, you open the generated link and complete its QR code or sign-in flow yourself; creating the link does not connect the account.

Only actions that cannot be taken back or that leave your workspace stop for your approval, every single time:

| Always asks | Tool and action | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Deleting records | `delete_records` | Hard delete, cannot be undone |
| Deleting a custom field | `manage_custom_columns` with `delete` | Destroys the column and every stored value |
| Deleting a widget | `manage_widgets` with `delete` | Removes the dashboard widget |
| Deleting or resending a webhook | `manage_webhooks` with `delete` or `resend_delivery` | Destroys configuration, or sends a new external request |
| Deleting a routine | `manage_routines` with `delete` | Removes the routine and its run history |
| Inviting team members | `manage_team` with `invite` | Sends real invitation emails |
| Sending a support request | `request_support` | Emails the Customermates team with your recent conversation |

Approval is requested by calling the tool: the call itself raises the approval card, and nothing happens until you answer it. The card names the action and its consequence and offers **Approve once** or **Cancel**. Decline it and nothing has changed. An unanswered card times out after 30 minutes; the request is then discarded and Mate is told that the approval timed out. There is no standing permission: each of these actions asks again next time.

Sending an email or chat message is deliberately not on that list: `send_email` and `send_chat_message` deliver the moment Mate calls them, so ask for a draft when you want to review the wording first. A draft waits in your inbox until you send it yourself, and Mate may discard a draft it saved without asking, because the text never left your workspace.

## Which tools can Mate use?

Mate uses the same MCP tool catalog as external AI clients, minus the deep-research `search` and `fetch` stubs, plus four interface tools and `load_toolset`, which only Mate has. The record, workspace, documentation, custom-field, interface and support tools are loaded on every turn. The messaging, social, widget, webhook, routine and administration sets load when your request wording, the page you are on, or an earlier turn in the conversation calls for them, or when Mate loads them itself with `load_toolset`. This keeps the tool catalog small for the model, which lowers cost and improves accuracy. The current page and the wording of an earlier message never remove a capability. Loading a tool set does not grant access: every call still enforces your workspace role, tenant, plan entitlements, connected-account prerequisites, and the approvals above.

## How does Mate operate the interface?

Mate has four interface tools: `list_ui_targets` finds stable target ids, `navigate` opens an app area by target id or one record's detail page by entity and record id, `highlight_element` points at a control, and `start_tour` composes a guided walkthrough. It does not click controls, type into forms, submit anything, or change display options, filters, sorting, grouping, or layouts. It never opens the record drawer: an existing record opens on its detail page, and to add a record it highlights the **Add** control and you fill in the form. When a control sits inside another panel, Mate names the prerequisite to open and then highlights the next control. A board groups records by a field; on a list with nothing to group by, Mate explains that prerequisite instead.

When you ask Mate to change data, it calls the matching tool directly and tells you what changed. When you would rather enter or refine something yourself, Mate points at the right control and **you** open it, type, select, and save. Your own unsaved edits are never overwritten.

Some settings have no tool, so Mate opens or names the page and you make the change there: the plan and billing on **My Company › Subscription**, role permissions on **My Company › Roles** (Mate can only move a member to another role, with `manage_team`), which deal field is the stage field on **My Company › Settings**, API keys on **My Profile › API & Connectors**, and your theme, display language and formatting locale on **My Profile › Settings**.

The targets are the sidebar entries, including every sub-page of **My Profile** and **My Company**; the toolbars of the five record lists, **Routines**, and the **Members**, **Roles**, **Webhooks**, **Audit Logs** and **Recent Deliveries** pages (**Add**, search and filter where the page has them, display options with the table and board layouts, and the import and export menu on the record lists); **Add widget** on the Dashboard; and **Connect channel** on **My Profile › Channels**. **Search**, **Documentation** and **Feedback** in the sidebar can be highlighted but not navigated to, and **Add** and **Ask AI** at the top of the sidebar are not targets. **Save** and **Reset** buttons, fields and controls inside dialogs, and the buttons on **My Company › Subscription** are not targets, so Mate names them instead of pointing at them.

## Guided tours

Ask for a tour of anything: a single page, a workflow, or the whole app. Mate composes each tour on the spot from what you asked, navigates between pages as the tour progresses, and explains each stop in your language. Close the overlay and the tour ends.

## Getting started on an empty workspace

Ask Mate to set up your workspace and it uses the same tool catalog it has for any other request: terminology, settings, custom fields, linked records, team access, connected-account links, webhooks, and dashboard widgets. It asks only for decisions that materially change the result; otherwise it proceeds from the goal and constraints you gave. It lists what it made, so you can adjust anything or ask it to delete records again, which asks for your approval like every delete.

Longer requests continue automatically through a bounded sequence of steps. Mate keeps the original request, carries forward a compact digest of completed work including the key numbers from earlier tool results, and re-reads workspace state when it needs details that were compacted. Longer setup and audit tasks therefore finish without a manual "continue" in the normal case, within hard cost, context, output, error, and runtime limits. If a limit is reached, completed changes remain in place, the activity list shows what ran, and the next request re-reads the workspace before continuing.

## Plans and credits

Hosted AI processing is paid with credits from a monthly allowance per active user. One credit corresponds to one US cent of measured provider cost. The allowance depends on the plan:

| Plan | Price per seat and month | Hosted AI credits per active user and month | Routines per user |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] | 200 | [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] |
| Pro | [[commercial.price.pro.monthly]] | 500 | [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]] |
| Business | [[commercial.price.business.monthly]] | 1,200 | [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedRoutinesPerUser]] |
| Enterprise | On request | As contracted | [[commercial.entitlement.enterprise.includedRoutinesPerUser]] |

The free trial runs for [[commercial.trial.days]] days with Pro features and 500 credits per active user. The allowance period starts at the moment the workspace was created and repeats monthly on that day and time in UTC (on the last day of a shorter month), whatever date your subscription is billed on; paid allowances reset monthly, and unused credits do not roll over. Only members whose status is **Active** count as seats and receive credits; on a paid plan, a seat activated mid-period gets a prorated share, rounded up. External MCP clients use your own AI provider and never consume these credits. Routines spend the credits of the routine's owner.

Credits are granted only while the trial runs or while the subscription status is **Active**; with any other status the allowance is 0 and **Ask AI** disappears from the sidebar. Mate cannot read or change the plan, the subscription status or billing, because no tool covers them; it can open **My Company › Subscription** (`#nav-company-subscription`, Cloud only). Who can change the plan, and how, is described in [My Company](/docs/app-company).

## Credits

The meter above the composer (`#agent-usage`) shows the share of the monthly allowance used, the remaining credits, the plan, and the reset date; it is hidden when your allowance is 0. Each request is settled on the measured provider cost, rounded up to whole credits, with a minimum of one credit. A simple question therefore costs one credit; a multi-step request that reads many records or runs several tools costs more, and a routine run is capped at 10 credits.

Before a request starts, Mate reserves a safety envelope for the model round it is about to run and for a few rounds ahead. With the default model the reservation is 6 credits per round and 12 credits in total, topped up round by round as the turn proceeds; a request cannot start when fewer than 6 credits remain, even though the meter may still show a small remainder. Reserved credits that the request did not use are released as soon as the provider usage is measured, so the meter can move down and then back up during a request. When the allowance is used up, new requests pause until the reset date and your history stays readable. The composer is then replaced by a notice with **View plans**, which opens **My Company › Subscription**; the same notice appears when the subscription grants no credits. When the notice says the hosted Assistant is temporarily unavailable, for example on an Enterprise workspace without a contracted allowance, its button is **Contact us** and opens an email to the Customermates team.

## Chat history

Chats are titled automatically. The history view lists active and archived chats, 25 at a time. Archiving hides a chat and can be undone. Deleting is only offered for archived chats, is refused while a turn is still running, and permanently removes the chat's messages and approvals; CRM records and billing entries remain.

## Support

Tell Mate when something does not work out and it offers to email a support request. After your confirmation, the recent conversation is included in an email to the Customermates team. Mate confirms only after the email provider accepts the message. The team replies to the email address on your account, not in the chat. If sending fails, Mate does not claim that the team was notified.

## Which ids does the panel have?

These ids belong to the assistant panel itself. They are DOM ids for external browser clients; Mate does not target its own panel.

| Action | Where | Anchor id | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open the assistant | Sidebar, above Add | `#nav-assistant` | Toggles the panel, same as Cmd/Ctrl+J |
| The panel itself | Floating card | `#agent-panel-dialog` | Non-modal dialog over the page |
| Write to Mate | Panel footer | `#agent-composer` | Enter sends, Shift+Enter breaks the line |
| Credit meter | Above the composer | `#agent-usage` | Shows usage and the reset date |
| Suggested prompts | Fresh chat | `#agent-suggestions` | Prefills the composer, does not send |
| Back from history | History view | `#agent-history-back` | Returns to the current chat |
| Archived chats | History view | `#agent-archive-summary` | Expands the archived list |
| Load more chats | History view | `#agent-load-more-active` / `#agent-load-more-archived` | Next page of chats |

## Availability

Every cloud plan includes the Mate entitlement; whether the assistant is live depends on the hosted environment's current configuration. It supports every interface language (English, German, Spanish, French, Italian); product documentation is retrieved in English or German and falls back to English for the other languages. Self-hosted deployments do not include the hosted assistant or routines; they connect external AI clients through the [MCP server](/docs/mcp) instead. An operator can switch the assistant off for a hosted environment, in which case the sidebar entry is absent.

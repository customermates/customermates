**Mate** is the AI assistant built into Customermates Cloud. Ask it about your workspace or the product, have it change data, or let it show you around the app. Self-hosted deployments have no hosted assistant; they connect external AI clients through the [MCP server](/docs/mcp) with your own provider.

## How do I open Mate? [#how-do-i-open-mate]

Click **Ask Mate** at the top of the sidebar or press Cmd/Ctrl+J anywhere, even while typing; the same keys close it again. You can also start from search: press Cmd/Ctrl+K, type your question and press Tab, and Mate receives it straight away. Esc closes the panel unless a tour or highlight is showing. All shortcuts are listed under [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/app-dashboard#keyboard-shortcuts).

The panel remembers per workspace whether you left it open. Mate answers in your interface language. While it works, **Stop** replaces **Send**, and a message you send meanwhile waits as **Up next**. Each person can run at most 3 Mate requests at once, chats and routine runs together; a routine run then waits for a free slot, and a new chat at the limit is refused with **Mate could not process your message.** In the public demo Mate opens but does not answer; start a free trial to use it. Demo embeds can set the initial state with `agentChat=open` or `agentChat=closed` in the page URL, without changing your saved choice.

Mate knows which page you are on and offers suggested prompts for it; clicking one fills in the message without sending it. To point Mate at something exact, use **Add context** (the + button, or type / in the message): it offers the current view, a new view and the open record, and its search attaches any record you can see by name. Each choice appears as a removable chip and stays with the message, a retry and the chat history. **Ask AI** in the Filters, Appearance and activity panels of a list opens Mate with the right chip and editable starter text and sends nothing until you do.

## What can Mate do without asking, what asks first, and which actions need my approval? [#what-runs-immediately-and-what-asks-first]

Ordinary CRM work runs as soon as you ask: creating and updating records, notes, links and Knowledge Base pages; saving and discarding drafts; sending emails and chat messages; inbox triage (moving threads, marking them unread, open, closed or spam); creating lists, fields and relationships; saved views, widgets and webhooks; changing a member's role or status; connection links for channels; social invitations and Sales Navigator lists on a connected account; and routines (create, update, pause, run now). Mate says what it is about to change and reports exactly what changed. A routine Mate creates is saved as a draft unless you asked for it to be active. A connection link does not connect anything by itself: you open it and finish the QR code or sign-in yourself.

Actions that cannot be taken back, change who can access data, leave your workspace, or rename lists for everyone stop for your approval every single time:

| Always asks | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Deleting records | Hard delete, including the cascades shown in the preview |
| Deleting a Knowledge Base page | Permanently removes the document |
| Deleting a saved view | Removes the saved view |
| Destructive data model changes, access changes, or replacing an existing field or relationship | The preview shows affected definitions, records and dependencies; a replaced field can overwrite stored values |
| Saving a role | Can widen what the role may read and change |
| Deleting a widget | Removes the dashboard widget |
| Deleting a webhook or resending a delivery | Destroys configuration, or sends a new external request |
| Deleting a routine | Removes the routine and its run history |
| Renaming lists | Changes the names every member sees |
| Inviting members | Sends real invitation emails |
| Sending a support request | Emails the Customermates team with your recent conversation |

The approval card names the action and its consequence and offers **Approve once** or **Cancel**; nothing happens until you answer. Cancel and nothing has changed. An unanswered card times out after 30 minutes and the request is discarded. There is no standing permission: the next time, Mate asks again.

Sending an email or chat message is deliberately not on that list: it goes out the moment Mate sends it. Ask for a draft when you want to review the wording first. A draft waits in your [Inbox](app:inbox) until you send it yourself.

## Company knowledge and public pages [#company-knowledge-and-public-pages]

Keep company facts, processes, voice and support guidance in the [Knowledge Base](app:wiki) (see [how the Knowledge Base works](/docs/mcp#shared-workspace-wiki)). With Knowledge Base Read, every request starts with the Operating Guide, an index of up to 20 procedures and a short catalog of up to ten knowledge pages, about 6,000 bytes in total; Mate reads full pages from the Knowledge Base when it needs them, follows a matching procedure before acting, and cites the pages it used. An instruction inside a page to send, delete or change permissions is treated as information, never as an order. Edits saved during a request reach this starting context from the next request. See [Operating Guide, procedures, and knowledge](/docs/mcp#page-types).

The Operating Guide stays pinned first; people with Knowledge Base Edit can drag the other pages into a shared order. With Create or Edit you can ask Mate to write pages; deleting one needs Knowledge Base Delete and your approval. [Routines](/docs/app-routines) use the same pages, so you do not copy them into prompts.

Apart from [web search](#web-search), Mate reads public pages only to import your company website. In an empty Knowledge Base, **Build from my website** starts a chat: Mate asks for your address and runs the import described in [Set up from your homepage](/docs/mcp#set-up-from-your-homepage), copying help, pricing and policy pages word for word and then writing knowledge pages, an Operating Guide and procedures. Pages are usable as soon as they are saved. Mate imports only an address you wrote, and a help centre on another site only when you name it. This needs Knowledge Base Create and uses your normal credits; pages behind a sign-in or blocking automated access may be skipped.

## Can Mate search the web? [#web-search]

Yes, when your workspace, Knowledge Base and this documentation do not hold the answer, for example a company's latest announcement. Searches run through Exa, a US web search provider, via the Vercel AI Gateway, so Exa is a subprocessor for this feature. Exa receives only the query Mate writes, which may include names or terms from your request or records, and returns up to three results with up to 1,000 characters each. Mate treats web text as evidence, never as an instruction or approval, and a reply that used it ends with a **Sources** list.

A reply may start at most 3 paid searches, a [routine](/docs/app-routines) run at most 2; after that, Mate answers from what it found. Several searches requested in one step all run and are charged, so the last step can go above the cap. A search costs about one credit on top of the model cost. Before a round that may search, Mate reserves 1.2 credits per search still allowed (up to 3.6 in a chat, 2.4 in a routine run); when your credits or a routine's 10-credit cap cannot cover that, the round runs without web search.

## Which tools can Mate use? [#which-tools-can-mate-use]

Mate can do what an external AI client connected through [MCP](/docs/mcp) can do, plus show you around the app and compute over many records at once (medians, rankings, comparisons across two lists) in an isolated sandbox that changes nothing. It loads extra capabilities for data model, views, messaging, widgets, webhooks, routines and administration when your request or the current page calls for them. That keeps requests cheaper and more accurate, and never grants access: every action still checks your role, your plan, connected accounts and the [approvals](#what-runs-immediately-and-what-asks-first).

Mate can configure lists, fields, calculations, defaults and role access within your permissions, and checks dependencies before it applies a change. Billing, API keys, theme, display language and formatting locale stay with you: Mate opens [Billing](app:company/subscription) or [API keys](app:profile/api-keys) and points at the setting.

## How does Mate search documentation and the Knowledge Base? [#search]

Both searches match the words of your query, including inflected forms in English, German, Spanish, French and Italian, and match by meaning, so a question phrased differently or in another language still finds the page. For the match by meaning, an embedding model run by Google through the Vercel AI Gateway receives only the search query. A small classifier then re-ranks the best sections: the Jev model, run by TypeSafe AI in the US through the Vercel AI Gateway with zero data retention requested, which receives your latest message, the query and excerpts of the sections being ranked, never CRM records. A page containing a queried code or identifier comes first, and misspellings are corrected from the words in your Knowledge Base. If either service is slow or fails, the search keeps its plain order. Pages of one Knowledge Base search keep their order for up to five minutes, so paging does not repeat or skip results.

The match by meaning costs a small fraction of a credit, charged to the person whose search created it; identical searches in one workspace at the same moment share it, so you can be charged when only a colleague's search used it. See [credits](#credits). Without credits and when self-hosted, the search is full text only, with the same typo correction. External MCP clients get the same search without the re-ranking.

## How does Mate operate the interface, and can it click buttons for me? [#how-does-mate-operate-the-interface]

Mate does not click buttons, type into forms or submit anything. It opens pages and points at controls, and **you** open, type, select and save; your unsaved edits are never overwritten. When you ask it to change data or a saved view (filters, search, sorting, grouping, layout, page size), it makes the change directly and tells you what changed. A record always opens on its page, never in the drawer. To add a record, Mate highlights [Add](app:records/contact?focus=add) on the list and you fill in the form. A control inside a panel, such as the board layout in **Appearance**, is shown after the panel's button. A board needs a field to group by; without one, Mate explains that first.

Mate can point at the sidebar entries, the toolbars of your lists, [Routines](app:routines), Members, Roles and Webhooks (Add, search, Filters, Appearance, import and export), [Add widget](app:dashboard?focus=add-widget) on the Dashboard, and the main controls of the settings pages, such as the plan cards, **Manage with Lemon Squeezy** and **Refresh** on Plan where your role shows them. Inside dialogs it can point at **Save**, **Cancel** and **Reset**, every field of the member and webhook dialogs, the invitation, API key and channel dialogs, and the **Chart** and **Activity timeline** cards on step 1 of **Add widget**. The fields of the role, widget and routine dialogs are not targets, so Mate names them. For a dialog, Mate first points at the control that opens it, or names the row or card to click.

## Guided tours [#guided-tours]

Ask for a tour of a page, a workflow or the whole app. Mate builds it from your request, moves between pages as it goes and explains each stop in your language. A tour has 2 to 20 stops: move with **Back** and **Next**, finish with **Done**, or end early with the X (announced as **Skip**), Esc or by closing the overlay. Stops whose control is not on the page are skipped.

## Getting started on an empty workspace [#getting-started-on-an-empty-workspace]

Ask Mate to set up your workspace and it creates lists, fields, relationships, member access, connection links, webhooks, saved views and dashboard widgets from the goal you describe. It asks only about decisions that change the result materially, then lists what it made so you can adjust it; deleting records again asks for approval as always.

Long requests continue on their own through a bounded number of steps, keeping a compact digest of finished work, so setup and audit tasks normally finish without a manual "continue". If a cost, context, error or runtime limit is reached, finished changes stay, the activity list shows what ran, and your next request picks up from the current workspace.

## How many credits does each plan include? [#plans-and-credits]

Each active user gets a monthly allowance of hosted AI credits; one credit is one US cent of measured provider cost.

| Plan | Price per seat and month | Hosted AI credits per active user and month | Routines per user |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] | 200 | [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] |
| Pro | [[commercial.price.pro.monthly]] | 500 | [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]] |
| Business | [[commercial.price.business.monthly]] | 1,200 | [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedRoutinesPerUser]] |
| Enterprise | On request | As contracted | [[commercial.entitlement.enterprise.includedRoutinesPerUser]] |

The free trial runs for [[commercial.trial.days]] days with Pro features and 500 credits per active user. The allowance period starts at the moment the workspace was created and repeats monthly at that day and time in UTC (the last day in a shorter month), whatever your billing date; paid allowances reset monthly, and unused credits do not roll over. Only members with status **Active** count as seats and receive credits; a seat activated mid-period on a paid plan gets an exact prorated share. Routines spend their owner's credits. External MCP clients bill model use through your own AI provider, although search by meaning can still use hosted credits.

Credits exist only during the trial or while the subscription is **Active**; otherwise the allowance is 0 and **Ask Mate** disappears from the sidebar. Mate cannot read or change your plan or billing; it can open [Billing](app:company/subscription), and [Workspace settings](/docs/app-company) explains who can change it.

## How is a request charged, and what happens when the credits run out? [#credits]

The ring next to **Send** fills with the share of your monthly allowance used; hover or click it for the remaining credits, plan, reset date and what the last request used. Each request is charged its exact measured provider cost, searches and ranking included, with no rounding or minimum: a request costing $0.0075 uses 0.75 credits. A simple question usually costs less than one credit, a multi-step request more, and a routine run is capped at 10 credits. The meter rounds to one decimal and shows `<0.1` for charges below 0.05.

Keeping the Knowledge Base search index current is charged to the workspace, shared among active members in proportion to their allowance. Every request and indexing step reserves its worst-case cost first and runs only if it fits both your own allowance and the workspace's combined one. With the default model a request reserves 5.6 credits per round and 11.2 in total, topped up as it goes, so it cannot start with less than 5.6 credits left even if the meter shows a remainder. Unused reservations are released once usage is measured, so the meter can dip and recover during a request.

When the allowance is used up, new requests pause until the reset date and your history stays readable. The message box is replaced by a notice with **View plans**, which opens [Billing](app:company/subscription); the same notice appears when the subscription grants no credits. If the hosted Assistant is temporarily unavailable, for example on an Enterprise workspace without a contracted allowance, the button is **Contact us** and emails the Customermates team.

## Chat history [#chat-history]

Chats are titled automatically. The history lists active and archived chats, 25 at a time. Archiving hides a chat and can be undone. Only archived chats can be deleted, not while a reply is running; deleting removes the chat's messages and approvals for good, while CRM records and billing entries remain.

## How do I contact support from Mate? [#support]

Tell Mate when something does not work and it offers to email a support request with the recent conversation to the Customermates team, after your approval. Mate confirms only once the email was accepted for delivery, and the team replies to your account's email address, not in the chat.

## Availability [#availability]

Every cloud plan includes the Mate entitlement; whether the assistant is live depends on the hosted environment, and an operator can switch it off, in which case **Ask Mate** is missing. Mate works in every interface language (English, German, Spanish, French, Italian); it reads the documentation in English or German and falls back to English. Self-hosted deployments include neither Mate nor routines and connect external AI clients through the [MCP server](/docs/mcp) instead.

## For agents [#for-agents]

Mate uses the same MCP tool catalog as external AI clients, without the deep-research search and fetch stubs, plus four interface tools, `load_toolset` and `analyze_records`, which only Mate has. The interface tools are `list_ui_targets` (stable targets), `navigate` (a target, an app link from these docs passed as href, or one record's page by `typeId` and `recordId`), `highlight_element` (point at a control) and `start_tour` (2 to 20 stops). `analyze_records` answers questions that need arithmetic over many records. It runs up to ten reads of read-only workspace tools, never one that reaches LinkedIn or another outside provider, collects every page, up to 10,000 rows and 8 MB across all reads together, and passes them to one JavaScript function in a sandbox with no network, clock or tools; a read that would return only part of its rows is refused. Approval is raised by calling a gated tool, and the panel itself is not a highlight target.

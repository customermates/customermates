Create a Customermates account, connect an AI client, and start issuing prompts. Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM. Claude and ChatGPT connect with a custom connector and need no API key. CLI clients and editors connect with an API key. This quickstart covers each step in its own section below.

## How do I sign up, and how long is the free trial?

Sign up at [customermates.com/auth/signup](/auth/signup). No credit card is required. The first [[commercial.trial.days]] days are a free trial of the Pro plan.

You choose a paid plan under **My Company → Subscription**. Choose one before the trial ends to keep access to the workspace; see [what happens when the trial ends](/docs/app-company#what-happens-when-the-trial-ends-or-a-payment-fails). Choosing a plan needs **Manage** **Yes** on the **Company** row of your role; the **Admin** role, which the person who creates the workspace gets, has it. The plans are compared under [Which plans are there?](/docs/app-company#which-plans-are-there). Self-hosting instead? See [Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting); a self-hosted instance has no trial and no subscription.

**Link:** the **Subscription** page, `/company/subscription`, Cloud URL `https://customermates.com/en/company/subscription`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-company-subscription`, then `highlight_element` with `company-subscription-plan-picker` for the plan cards, which show until the first checkout and only to roles with **Manage** on **Company**; the target outlines all cards, so Mate names the plan to choose.

## How do I connect Claude or ChatGPT?

Add a custom connector; no API key is needed.

- **Claude** (web, desktop, mobile): **Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector**.
- **ChatGPT**: **Settings → Connectors → Add connector → Custom connector (MCP)**, with **Authentication** set to OAuth.

Paste `https://customermates.com/api/v1/mcp`, sign in to Customermates, and click **Approve** on the **Authorize access** screen. Full steps: [Connect with a custom connector](/docs/connect-custom-connector).

The server sends its instructions to the client on connect, so the AI knows the tool surface and workspace conventions from the start. In clients that support MCP prompts, such as Claude, you can also run the built-in `get-started` prompt for a personalized start: your name, your role, and a first summary of your workspace.

## How do I connect a CLI or editor?

First create an API key: open **My Profile → API & Connectors**, press **Add**, and choose **Standard API key**, or let **Quick connections** create the key together with your client's setup snippet. Copy the 64-character key, which is shown once. Creating a key needs **Manage** **Yes** on the **API & Webhooks** row of your role, and the page itself needs **Read access** **All** on that row; the **Admin** role has both. Details: [API keys](/docs/api-keys). Then follow your client's page:

- [Claude Code](/docs/connect-cli#claude-code): one terminal command
- [Cursor](/docs/connect-cli#cursor): Settings UI, two clicks
- [Codex](/docs/connect-cli#codex): one TOML block in `~/.codex/config.toml`
- [Gemini](/docs/connect-cli#gemini-cli): one settings block

**Link:** the **API & Connectors** page, `/profile/api-keys`, Cloud URL `https://customermates.com/en/profile/api-keys`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-profile-api-keys`; `highlight_element` also takes `profile-api-keys-generate` for **Add** (roles with **API & Webhooks** **Manage**), then `api-key-option-standard` for **Standard API key** (prerequisite `profile-api-keys-generate`) and `api-key-name`, `api-key-expires` and `api-key-save` for **Name**, **Expires in** and **Save** (prerequisite `api-key-option-standard`). The **Quick connections** tiles are not highlight targets, so Mate names them.

## What should I try first?

- *"Set the status of the Acme deal to Won and add a note that the contract was signed today."*
- *"Pull the last ten contacts I created. Any without an email address?"*
- *"Create a contact for Jane Doe at Initech, link it to the Initech organization, and start a deal for 12 hours of consulting."*

The agent reports which tools it called and what changed. Field names such as a deal's status are workspace-configurable custom columns, not fixed product fields. Call `get_record_schema` to see the columns that exist in your workspace.

## How do I test a webhook?

Optional: to notify other tools when the CRM changes, open **My Company → Webhooks** and press **Add**. Set **URL** to a throwaway endpoint from [webhook.site](https://webhook.site). Under **Events**, select **Contact Created** (`contact.created`) and **Deal Updated** (`deal.updated`), then press **Save**. Adding a webhook needs **Manage** **Yes** on the **API & Webhooks** row, and the page itself needs **Read access** **All** on that row, as for API keys; the **Admin** role has both.

Create a contact or change a deal. Within a few seconds the delivery appears at webhook.site with the full payload, and **My Company → Recent Deliveries** lists it too. Full webhook docs: [Webhooks](/docs/webhooks).

**Link:** the **Webhooks** page, `/company/webhooks`, Cloud URL `https://customermates.com/en/company/webhooks`, and the **Recent Deliveries** page, `/company/webhook-deliveries`, Cloud URL `https://customermates.com/en/company/webhook-deliveries`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-company-webhooks` or `nav-company-webhook-deliveries`, and `highlight_element` with `company-webhooks-add` for **Add** (roles with **Manage** on **API & Webhooks**); once the **Webhook** dialog is open (prerequisite `company-webhooks-add`), `webhook-modal-url` and `webhook-modal-events` highlight **URL** and **Events**, and `webhook-modal-save` highlights **Save**.

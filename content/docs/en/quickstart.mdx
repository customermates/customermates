Create an account, connect an AI client and start asking. Claude and ChatGPT connect with a custom connector and need no API key; CLI clients and editors use an API key.

## How do I sign up, and how long is the free trial? [#how-do-i-sign-up-and-how-long-is-the-free-trial]

[Sign up](/auth/signup) without a credit card. The first [[commercial.trial.days]] days are a free trial of the Pro plan.

To keep access after the trial, choose a plan in [Plan](app:company/subscription?focus=plan-picker) before it ends; see [what happens when the trial ends](/docs/app-company#what-happens-when-the-trial-ends-or-a-payment-fails) and [which plans there are](/docs/app-company#which-plans-are-there). Choosing a plan needs **Edit** on the **Company** row of your role. The **Admin** role, which the person who creates the workspace gets, has it. A [self-hosted](/docs/self-hosting) instance has no trial and no plan.

## How do I connect Claude or ChatGPT? [#how-do-i-connect-claude-or-chatgpt]

Add a custom connector:

- **Claude** (web, desktop, mobile): **Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector**.
- **ChatGPT**: **Settings → Connectors → Add connector → Custom connector (MCP)**, with **Authentication** set to OAuth.

Paste the address you open Customermates at followed by `/api/v1/mcp`, sign in, and click **Approve** on the **Authorize access** screen. Full steps: [Connect with a custom connector](/docs/connect-custom-connector).

On connect the server briefs the client on its tools and your workspace. In clients that support MCP prompts, such as Claude, the built-in `get-started` prompt adds a personal start: your name, your role and a first summary of your workspace.

## How do I connect a CLI or editor? [#how-do-i-connect-a-cli-or-editor]

Open [API keys](app:profile/api-keys), click [Add](app:profile/api-keys?focus=generate) and choose **Standard API key**, or pick your client under **Quick connections** to get the key together with its setup snippet. Copy the key now: it is shown only once. Creating a key needs **Create** on the **API & Webhooks** row of your role; the **Admin** role has it. Details: [API keys](/docs/api-keys). Then follow your client's steps:

- [Claude Code](/docs/connect-cli#claude-code): one terminal command
- [Cursor](/docs/connect-cli#cursor): Settings UI, two clicks
- [Codex](/docs/connect-cli#codex): one TOML block in `~/.codex/config.toml`
- [Gemini](/docs/connect-cli#gemini-cli): one settings block

## What should I try first? [#what-should-i-try-first]

- *"Set the status of the Acme deal to Won and add a note that the contract was signed today."*
- *"Pull the last ten contacts I created. Any without an email address?"*
- *"Create a contact for Jane Doe at Initech, link it to the Initech organization, and start a deal for 12 hours of consulting."*

The agent reports what it did and what changed. Deal stages and other fields differ per workspace; the agent reads yours before it writes.

## How do I test a webhook? [#how-do-i-test-a-webhook]

Optional, to notify other tools when the CRM changes: open [Webhooks](app:company/webhooks) and click [Add](app:company/webhooks?focus=add). Set **URL** to a throwaway endpoint from [webhook.site](https://webhook.site), select **Record created** and **Record updated** under **Events**, choose the lists to watch and click **Save**. Adding a webhook needs **Create** on the **API & Webhooks** row, as for API keys.

Now create a contact or change a deal. Within seconds the delivery arrives at webhook.site with the full payload and shows up under [Deliveries](app:company/webhook-deliveries). More in [Webhooks](/docs/webhooks).

## What does the Dashboard show, and can I share widgets with my team? [#what-does-the-dashboard-show]

The [Dashboard](app:dashboard) is where you land after sign-in. It shows widgets over your CRM data: pipelines, task counts, recent activity, whatever you set up. Every widget belongs to you. Teammates see it only after you turn on **Share as a template** in its editor, and then they start their own copy from it. Adding widgets needs no role permission, but a chart counts only the records your role can read, so a teammate's copy can show other numbers. [Add widget](app:dashboard?focus=add-widget) appears only if your role can read at least one list. In a workspace without lists, the Dashboard says so and offers **Create list** to people who manage the data model.

## Can I have several dashboards? [#dashboard-views]

Yes. The view chips above the widgets switch to your own named dashboards, for example one per team or purpose; with none selected you see the main dashboard, and clicking the selected view again goes back to it. **New view** adds one, and V opens the view picker, as on a list. Each view keeps its own widgets and arrangement, a new widget lands on the view you are looking at, and a link to a view opens that dashboard for you. Views and their widgets are personal, so a colleague who opens your link sees their own dashboard. Deleting a view moves it to [Trash](/docs/app-trash) together with its widgets, and the confirmation names them; **Undo** or **Restore** brings both back.

## Which widget types exist, and which charts can they show? [#which-widget-types-exist]

Click [Add widget](app:dashboard?focus=add-widget) to open **Choose a Widget**. **Start from scratch** comes first, with one card per chart style (**Number**, **Bar chart**, **Horizontal bars**, **Area over time**, **Ranked table**, **Doughnut**, **Funnel** and **Radar**) plus **Activity timeline**, **Upcoming**, **Month overview** and **Map**; a card opens the editor with that style already chosen, and you pick the data. **Start from a template** lists widgets teammates shared. To have a widget built for you, describe it with **Ask Mate** in the widget editor.

- A chart aggregates one list: count, sum, average, minimum or maximum of a number field. Group by a field, by each record, by **Assigned to** (a record with two assignees counts for both) or through a relationship, for example Deal value by stage or line item amounts by service. Each source record counts once per group, so a deal linked to two organizations counts fully for both. To plot a time series, group by a date field, **Created at** or **Updated at** and choose a **Time interval** (day, week starting Monday, month, quarter or year). Times are bucketed in the time zone of the browser that saved the widget; date-only values never shift, and records without a date are counted below the chart.
- **Activity timeline** is a newest-first feed of changes, messages and channel activity. Its filters belong to that widget, so several timelines can show different things without affecting each other or a record's Activities view.
- **Upcoming** lists the records of one list by a date or date-time field for the next days (14 by default, up to 90), with an **Overdue** section for earlier dates and an optional amount column. Filter out finished records, for example tasks that are done, so that only open ones count as overdue.
- **Month overview** colors each day of a month by how many records fall on it or by the sum of a number or currency field. The arrows change the month, and a day opens a list of its records.
- **Map** places the records of one list at the position stored with an address field and groups records that are close together into numbered clusters. A single-choice field colors the pins in the colors of its options, the same as on charts, a number or currency field sizes them, and a pin opens a card with the name, the address, an optional detail field and **Open record**. A cluster takes the color of more than half of its records; without such a majority it is an uncolored ring, which the legend lists as **Mixed**.

Under **Appearance**, a chart can be a vertical or horizontal bar chart (with or without labels), a doughnut or a radar chart, or one of these:

| Style | Shows | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Number** | One overall value | **No grouping** |
| **Area over time** | A value per period; empty periods are zero for counts and sums and a gap otherwise | A time interval |
| **Ranked table** | Groups by value with their share of the total, top ten plus the rest | A grouping |
| **Funnel** | Each option in order with step-to-step conversion; a stage with probability 0, such as Lost, is listed below | A single choice field such as a stage |

A style that does not fit the grouping is disabled with a hint. Calculated values need access to their inputs.

### What can an activity timeline show, including calendar events? [#what-can-an-activity-timeline-show]

A timeline shows the same entries as [Activity](app:settings/activity): record changes with their previous and current values, admin and configuration changes, messages, channel activity such as accepted LinkedIn connections, and meetings from the calendar of a connected Gmail or Outlook account (the calendar layout of a list and the calendar widgets show records by their date fields, not meetings). Filter it by **Activity type**, **Provider**, **Channel**, **Conversation** or **Related records**, and limit it to record types or records, or to a **From** and **Until** date. Related records use **Includes any of**, **Excludes all of**, **Has a linked record** and **Has no linked record**. Different filters must all match; several values in one filter match any of them.

**Load older** shows the next page. A row can link one primary record and up to three related records, and a message can open its Inbox thread. Rows without a record stay visible without a link.

### How do calendar widgets handle dates and time zones? [#calendar-widgets]

A date-only field is a calendar day and never shifts. A date-time field is placed on the day it falls on in the widget's time zone, which is the time zone of the browser that created the widget; "today" and **Overdue** follow the same time zone. **Upcoming** shows up to 100 records and up to 20 overdue ones with their total. **Month overview** counts or sums every record of the month, and a day lists up to 100 of its records. Both use the same filters, record access and field permissions as charts, so a teammate's copy of a shared template shows only what that teammate may read.

### Where does the map come from, and which records are missing from it? [#map-widget]

The map shows country borders, regions and cities from [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) (public domain). These map files ship with Customermates and are served by your own Customermates server, so opening a map sends no request to an outside map, tile, font or geocoding service, and a self-hosted server shows maps without internet access. The map files load only when a map widget is on screen.

A record appears where its address field stores a position: the center of the postal code area once the postal code directory knows the code, or the building after an address search on a self-hosted server with Photon (see [How do I enter an address?](/docs/app-records#how-do-i-enter-an-address)). Records without an address, without a stored position, or whose address you may not read are counted in the hint below the map instead. A map shows at most 2,000 records; narrow the filters if the widget says it shows only part of them. Zoom with the buttons, the keyboard (+ and -, arrow keys to move, 0 to reset) or the mouse wheel while you hold Cmd/Ctrl; once you have selected the map, the wheel zooms without the key. On a touch screen, one finger scrolls the page and two fingers move and zoom the map. Select a cluster to zoom into it.

### Who sees what in a timeline? [#who-sees-what-in-a-timeline]

| Access | Visible content |
| --- | --- |
| Activity log only | Permitted changes |
| Inbox messages only, on a plan with messaging | Permitted messages, channel activity and meetings |
| Both | One merged timeline |
| Neither | A notice that no activity is available |

The two permissions are the **Activity log** and **Inbox messages** rows of the member's role in [Roles](app:settings/roles), each with **Read access** **All**. Messages, channel activity and meetings also need Customermates Cloud and a plan with messaging (Pro and above); changes stay visible without it. A deleted or no longer accessible filter value stops matching and shows as **Filter not available** on the chip and **Selection unavailable** in the picker, so you can remove it.

## How do I create or edit a chart or timeline widget in the widget editor? [#how-does-the-widget-editor-work]

Pick a chart style, **Activity timeline**, **Upcoming**, **Month overview**, **Map** or a template in **Choose a Widget**; all of them open the same editor. Give the widget a name, then switch between **Data** and **Appearance**. **Data** holds the source and what to count; filters and grouping are collapsible sections inside it, and once you group, **Group filters** limits which records appear as groups. Both filter sections show the same searchable Filters palette as a list, and linked fields appear as **List › Field**. **Appearance** holds the chart style, colors, the legend, **Show metric and filters** and **Share as a template**. The **Live preview** above the settings shows the widget with real data and updates as you change things; select **Dashboard size** to see it in the proportions it takes on the dashboard. Click **Save** in the footer.

Click a chart or one of its bars to open its data drawer: the chart remains at the top and the contributing records appear below, with the normal list filters and pagination. Selecting another group changes the records. **Open in list** opens the normal overview with the same selection and any extra filters; it queries the full matching data rather than a sample of record IDs. Clicking a row opens its record. To edit a widget, use the pencil shown on hover or keyboard focus, or choose **Edit** from its local menu (also available by right-click). Clicking a filter chip opens the editor at that filter. **Reset** restores the saved values once you changed something, and the trash icon in the editor's header moves the widget to [Trash](/docs/app-trash) after a confirmation. A new widget takes the first free spot. Drag a widget to move it and drag an edge or corner to resize it (moving is off on touch devices); the arrangement is saved for you only. Heights snap to rows of 54 px; chart and **Number** widgets are at least two rows tall, an **Activity timeline** at least six rows tall and two columns wide, a calendar widget at least five rows tall and two columns wide, and a **Map** at least five rows tall and three columns wide.

## Which controls does the sidebar add? [#which-controls-does-the-sidebar-add]

The sidebar holds your work and looks the same on every screen. At the top are **Search** (Cmd/Ctrl+K, see [Global search](/docs/app-search)), **Ask Mate** (Cmd/Ctrl+J, see [the assistant](/docs/app-assistant)) and **Add** (C). Below are **Overview** with the [Dashboard](app:dashboard), [Knowledge Base](app:wiki), [Inbox](app:inbox) and [Routines](app:routines), then **Data** with your lists, then your personal sections. Entries your role cannot open are hidden. **Inbox** counts unread conversations.

The sidebar is yours: drag an entry to reorder it or move it to another section, or use its **…** menu for **Move up**, **Move down**, **Move to section** (including **New section**) and **Hide from sidebar**. Click a section title to collapse it; its **…** menu (or a right-click) offers **Move up** and **Move down**, and on sections you created also **Rename** and **Delete section**. Deleting a section moves its entries back to their default place; hidden entries stay hidden. **Customize sidebar** in the personal menu opens a dialog with a switch per entry to show or hide it, **New section**, and, once you changed something, **Reset to default**, which after a confirmation restores the standard layout. Nobody else sees your changes.

The workspace name at the top opens the workspace menu: the workspaces you belong to, to switch between them, **Create workspace**, **Invite members** (if you may invite), [Members](app:settings/members), **Settings**, [Billing](app:settings/billing) with a chip for your plan and its status, and, for Customermates operators, **Operator Console**. Your avatar opens the personal menu: [Profile & preferences](app:settings/profile), **Theme** (System, Light or Dark), **Language**, **Keyboard shortcuts**, **Documentation**, **Send feedback**, **Customize sidebar** and **Sign Out**. **Settings** and G then S open Profile & preferences, and the sidebar switches to the settings navigation with Account ([Profile & preferences](app:settings/profile), [Workspaces](app:settings/workspaces), [Channels](app:settings/channels), [API keys](app:settings/api-keys)) and Workspace ([General](app:settings/general), [Members](app:settings/members), [Roles](app:settings/roles), [Billing](app:settings/billing), [Activity](app:settings/activity), [Webhooks](app:settings/webhooks)); **Back** at its top returns to the page you came from. **Customize sidebar** is not offered while the settings navigation is shown. Until you confirm your address, your avatar and the Profile & preferences entry show **Email not verified**.

## Which keyboard shortcuts are there? [#keyboard-shortcuts]

Press ? or choose **Keyboard shortcuts** in the personal menu to see every shortcut with the keys of your computer (⌘ on a Mac, Ctrl elsewhere), grouped and searchable. Cmd/Ctrl shortcuts and Esc work everywhere, even while you type. Single keys and G sequences work only when no text field has focus and no menu or dialog is open.

| Group | Keys | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General | Cmd/Ctrl+K or / | Search; type a question and press Tab to ask Mate instead |
| General | Cmd/Ctrl+J | Ask Mate (opens or closes the assistant) |
| General | ? | Show keyboard shortcuts |
| General | Cmd/Ctrl+\ | Show or hide the sidebar |
| Navigation | G then D, I, K, R, C | Dashboard, Inbox, Knowledge Base, Routines, Configure |
| Navigation | G then S | Settings |
| Navigation | G then W | Switch workspace, when you belong to more than one |
| Create | C | Add a record or list |
| Lists and views | G then 1 to 9 | Lists by their position in your sidebar; hidden lists are skipped |
| Lists and views | V | Switch view on a list or the Dashboard |
| Lists and views | F | Open search and filters on a list |
| Forms | Cmd/Ctrl+Enter | Save the open form |
| Forms | Esc | Close the topmost dialog or drawer |

After G you have about one second for the second key; a hint shows that the app is waiting. Shortcuts for pages your role cannot open are left out. Search lists the same commands with their keys: **Ask Mate** on top, the views of the list you are on, the pages, **Add** and, with more than one workspace, **Switch workspace…**. If you use speech input or press keys by accident, turn off **Single-key shortcuts** at the bottom of the shortcut dialog; Cmd/Ctrl shortcuts keep working. The setting is personal and follows you to every device.

## What happens on first run? [#what-happens-on-first-run]

New accounts go through the [onboarding wizard](/docs/app-onboarding) before they see the Dashboard: your profile, the Knowledge Base, team invites and connecting an AI client. The owner can skip website import, invitations and the AI connection; invited members see only their profile.

## For agents [#tips-for-agents]

Create and change widgets with `manage_widgets` rather than driving the editor; it supports the same options as the app. Read a widget before updating it, because a supplied `measure` or `displayOptions` object replaces the whole object, and pass the current `expectedRevision`, an `idempotencyKey` and, for updates, `expectedVersion`. A time series sets `groupBy.dateInterval` and an IANA `groupBy.timeZone`; timelines use `activityQuery`, calendar widgets `calendarQuery` (`layout` `upcoming` or `month`, the date field, an IANA `timeZone`) and maps `mapQuery` (the address field plus optional color, size and detail fields). `get` reads a calendar widget for another `window` of up to 42 days, such as one month or one day. The widget kind cannot change. An optional `layout` places a widget in the same call: `x` and `w` count columns of the 12-column grid, `y` and `h` count rows of 54 px. Chart and Number widgets need an `h` of at least 2, an activity timeline a `w` of at least 2 and an `h` of at least 6, a calendar a `w` of at least 2 and an `h` of at least 5, a map a `w` of at least 3 and an `h` of at least 5, and overlapping positions are refused. Empty rows between widgets are allowed; the dashboard moves widgets up into them. `get_calendars` reads calendar events. Exact schemas are in the [MCP catalog](/docs/mcp#tool-catalog).

## Which REST endpoints manage widgets? [#widget-rest-endpoints]

Over REST, `POST /api/v1/widgets/list` returns every widget across your dashboard views with its `viewId` and layout, `POST /api/v1/widgets/save` creates or updates one, `POST /api/v1/widgets/layout` moves and resizes several at once, and `POST /api/v1/widgets/delete` moves one to Trash. To create a timeline, calendar or map with `widgets/save`, send `kind: "activityTimeline"`, `"calendar"` or `"map"`; without `kind` a new widget is a chart, an update may leave out settings that stay the same, and a different `kind` on an update is refused with `widgetKindImmutable`. `POST /api/v1/widgets/read` returns one widget with its current results; for a calendar widget it also accepts a `window` with `from` and `to`. Dashboard views are saved views of the `dashboard` surface under `/api/v1/data-views/`.

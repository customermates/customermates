Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM. This page documents the **Dashboard** screen of the web app for humans and for AI agents that drive the UI. Route: `/dashboard`, locale-prefixed like `https://customermates.com/en/dashboard`, sidebar id `#nav-dashboard`.

## What does the Dashboard show?

The Dashboard is the landing screen after sign-in. It shows configurable widgets over your CRM data: pipelines, task counts, recent activity, whatever you set up. Every widget belongs to you as a user; teammates see it only after you share it with **Share as a template**, which lets them start their own copy from it. Adding widgets needs no role permission, but a chart counts only the records your role can read (**Read access** **Assigned** or **All**), so a teammate's copy of your template can show other numbers. **Add widget** appears only if your role can read at least one record type; the **Activity timeline** kind needs read access to the **Audit Log** or, on a plan with messaging, to **Inbox messages**.

**Link:** the **Dashboard** page, `/dashboard`, Cloud URL `https://customermates.com/en/dashboard`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-dashboard` or `dashboard-add-widget`; inside the editor `widget-modal-save` (**Create widget** on step 2, **Save changes** when editing) and `widget-modal-reset` (**Reset**, when editing with unsaved changes) are targets too, while the tabs, switches and widgets are not, so Mate names them.

## Which widget types exist?

Press **Add widget** (`#dashboard-add-widget`); creation starts with a choice between two kinds, or a template a teammate shared:

- **Chart**: aggregates one record type. On the **Data** tab choose the **Element** (the record type), a **Metric** and a **Grouping**. Every type can be counted; contacts, organizations and deals also offer deal value, plus weighted deal value once a deal stage field is set under **My Company › Settings**; services offer deal value and the number of services in deals; tasks can only be counted. Deal-based metrics need read access to deals. **Grouping** offers **No Grouping**, a single-select custom field of that record type, or, with a metric other than count, each record itself, for example deal value per contact (not for tasks). **Chart style** on the **Appearance** tab: **Vertical Bar Chart** or **Horizontal Bar Chart**, each also **with Labels**, **Doughnut Chart** or **Radar Chart**. Filters narrow the data set; a chart over contacts, organizations or services with a deal-based metric gets a separate filter for the related deals.
- **Activity timeline**: a newest-first feed of changes, messages and connected-account activity. Its filters belong to that widget, so several timeline widgets can show different views without changing each other or the timeline on a record page.

Over MCP, `manage_widgets` lists, reads, creates, updates and deletes both kinds with a reduced option set: a chart is created from name, entity type, display type, group-by and aggregation plus optional filters and group-by column, and an update can change everything except the record type and the widget kind (name, display type, aggregation, group-by, filters, axis direction, bar colors); **Share as a template**, **Show legend**, **Use option colors** and **Show metric and filters** are set in the app only. A timeline takes a name, timeline filters and the show-filters flag.

**Link:** the **Dashboard** page, `/dashboard`, Cloud URL `https://customermates.com/en/dashboard`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `dashboard-add-widget` for **Add widget**; the kind and template choices in the editor are not highlight targets, so Mate names them.

### What can an activity timeline show?

The **Record changes** category contains audit-log changes. **Messages** contains Inbox messages. **Account & calendar** contains connected-account activity, such as accepted LinkedIn connections, and calendar events.

All accessible records are the implicit baseline. Filter a timeline by category, provider, connected account, conversation, or related contacts, organizations, deals, services, and tasks. Each field can be configured once. Relationship filters use the same operators as record lists: **in** and **not in** accept one or more records, while **has some** and **has none** need no value. Different filter fields are AND-combined, and multiple values within one **in** filter are OR-combined. Unlinked entries fail positive relationship filters and pass negative ones. Filters become persistent when you save the widget.

Entries are shown newest first. Use **Load older** for the next bounded page. A row can show one primary record, up to three related records, and a remaining-record count. Permitted record links open the relevant record, and message details can open the permitted Inbox thread. Rows without a CRM association remain visible without a record link.

### Who sees what in a timeline?

| Access | Visible content |
|---|---|
| Audit log only | Permitted changes |
| Inbox messages only, on a plan with messaging | Permitted messages, connected-account activities, and calendar events |
| Both permissions, on a plan with messaging | One merged timeline containing all permitted sources |
| Neither | A non-sensitive unavailable state |

The two permissions are the **Audit Log** and **Inbox messages** rows of the member's role under **My Company › Roles**, each with **Read access** set to **All**. Audit-log changes are available independently of the messaging entitlement. Messages, connected-account activities, and calendar events require cloud mode, Inbox access, and a plan with messaging (Pro and above); on Starter, **Inbox** shows **Upgrade to unlock messaging** with an **Upgrade your plan** button that opens **My Company › Subscription**. A missing connected messaging account removes messaging-based sources but does not hide permitted audit changes. Deleted or newly inaccessible filter values stop matching, are shown as **Filter not available** on the filter chip and **Selection unavailable** in the value picker for safe removal, and never expose their raw ids.

## How do I create or edit a chart or timeline widget in the widget editor? [#how-does-the-widget-editor-work]

Adding a widget is a two-step wizard (**Step 1 of 2**). Step 1, **Choose a Widget**, offers the two kinds and, when any widget in the workspace is shared as a template, **Start from a template** (`#widget-template-heading`), each entry marked **Shared by** and the owner's name. Step 2 configures the widget. For a chart widget the modal is organized in three tabs: `#widget-tab-data` holds the name, record type, metric and grouping, `#widget-tab-filters` narrows what counts (`#widget-entity-filters-heading` marks the record filter block), and `#widget-tab-appearance` sets the chart style, colors, axes, **Show legend** for doughnuts, **Show metric and filters** and **Share as a template**. A live preview under `#widget-preview-heading` reflects every change before you save. While adding, the footer offers **Back** and `#widget-modal-save` (**Create widget**). An activity-timeline widget has two tabs: `#widget-tab-data`, which also holds its timeline filters, and `#widget-tab-appearance`.

To change a widget, click it, or click one of its filter chips to open the editor at that filter. The editor is titled **Edit** plus the widget's name; `#widget-modal-save` reads **Save changes**, and `#widget-modal-reset` (**Reset**) appears once you change something and restores the saved values. The trash icon **Delete widget** in the editor's header removes the widget after a confirmation. Drag a widget to move it and drag an edge or corner to resize it; moving is off on touch devices. The layout is saved for you only.

**Link:** the **Dashboard** page, `/dashboard`, Cloud URL `https://customermates.com/en/dashboard`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `dashboard-add-widget`, then `widget-modal-save` (**Create widget** / **Save changes**) or `widget-modal-reset` (**Reset**) once the editor is open, which for **Reset** means the user has clicked a widget (`list_ui_targets` gives `a widget card` as its prerequisite); the tabs, **Back**, **Delete widget** and the widgets themselves are not highlight targets, so Mate names them.

## Which controls does the sidebar add?

The sidebar is the same on every screen; its controls are documented here because the Dashboard is where you land.

Entries sit in three groups: **Overview** (**Dashboard**, plus **Inbox** and **Routines** on Cloud), **CRM** (the five record types, under your workspace's names for them) and **Workspace** (**My Profile** and **My Company**). An entry your role cannot read is hidden, a group with no visible entries disappears, and opening a hidden page by its URL brings you back to the Dashboard. **My Profile** and **My Company** only expand or collapse; their sub-entries open the pages and carry their own ids: `#nav-profile-settings`, `#nav-profile-connected-accounts`, `#nav-profile-api-keys`, `#nav-company-subscription`, `#nav-company-settings`, `#nav-company-members`, `#nav-company-roles`, `#nav-company-audit-logs`, `#nav-company-webhooks` and `#nav-company-webhook-deliveries`. Mate can navigate to each of them, but highlight one only while its group is expanded, which it is by default on that group's own pages. Three entries show a number: **Inbox** counts unread conversations, the tasks entry shows members who manage **Users & Roles** how many **Approve User** tasks are open, and **Channels** counts channels that need attention.

## What happens on first run?

New accounts land in the [onboarding wizard](/docs/app-onboarding) at `/onboarding/wizard` before they see the Dashboard. It has three steps: your profile, team invites, and connecting an AI client. Only the invite step carries the stable ids `#onboarding-next` and `#onboarding-back`.

## Tips for agents

- The hosted assistant can navigate here and highlight `#nav-dashboard` and `#dashboard-add-widget`; it creates widgets through `manage_widgets` instead of driving the modal.
- Rows never carry ids. Open a record via its page URL `/{type}/{id}`. The drawer deep-link `?open={entityType}:new` adds a record and works on every signed-in page, the Dashboard included.
- Prefer [MCP](/docs/mcp) tools over clicking when a tool exists. Reading and writing CRM data is faster and safer through tools than through the UI.
- `#nav-search`, `#nav-documentation` and `#nav-feedback` can be highlighted but are not navigation targets. Navigating to `#nav-profile` or `#nav-company` opens that group's **Settings** page. `#nav-add`, `#nav-assistant`, the two chips and the menu under your name are not assistant targets.

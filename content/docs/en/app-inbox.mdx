## What is the Inbox? [#what-is-the-inbox]

The [Inbox](app:inbox) is unified messaging on Customermates Cloud: email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram conversations in one list, most recent activity first. Click a conversation to read it next to the list; on narrow screens the list and the conversation take turns. From a conversation you reply on its original channel, set its state, move an email to another folder, share it with your team and match its participants to records. You connect the accounts themselves on [Channels](app:settings/channels), see [App guide, Profile](/docs/app-profile).

## Who can use the Inbox, and why is it locked on Starter or missing in the self-hosted version? [#who-can-use-the-inbox]

All four conditions must hold:

- **Customermates Cloud.** Self-hosted installations have no Inbox: the sidebar entry is hidden and opening it leads to the Dashboard.
- **A plan with messaging:** Pro, Business, Enterprise or a trial. On Starter the Inbox stays in the sidebar but shows **Upgrade to unlock messaging**, and **Upgrade your plan** opens [Billing](app:settings/billing).
- **A usable subscription:** status **Active** or a trial that has not ended. While the status is **Past Due** or **Cancelled**, the Inbox is locked the same way.
- **Your role:** **Inbox messages** with **Read access** set to **All**. With **None** the sidebar entry is hidden and opening the Inbox leads to the Dashboard. The **Admin** role always has access.

## Do I need a connected channel? [#do-i-need-a-connected-channel]

Not to open the Inbox: without a channel of your own you still see what teammates share. To send and receive on your own accounts you need one. An empty Inbox reads **Nothing here yet**; members with **Create** on **Inbox messages** get a **Channels** button below it (when Mate is available, starter prompts appear there instead). Add an email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram or Telegram account with [Connect channel](app:settings/channels?focus=connect), see [How do I connect a channel?](/docs/app-profile#how-do-i-connect-a-channel). Channels per member: Pro [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]], Business [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedAccountsPerUser]], Enterprise [[commercial.entitlement.enterprise.includedAccountsPerUser]]; a trial counts as Pro.

## Which conversations do I see, and can an admin read my private ones? [#which-conversations-do-i-see]

The list combines the channels you connected, channels a teammate shared with the whole workspace, and single conversations a teammate shared from a private channel. A teammate's private conversations never appear, whatever your role, **Admin** included.

For email accounts, only folders switched on in the account's **Folders** tab on [Channels](app:settings/channels) appear. By default every folder is on except trash, junk or spam, drafts and the all-mail folder; if mail from an archive is missing, check its switch. A conversation without any message appears only while it holds a saved draft.

## Which permissions does the Inbox need? [#which-permissions-does-the-inbox-need]

Roles are edited on [Roles](app:settings/roles). The Inbox uses the **Inbox messages** row: **Create**, **Edit**, **Delete**, and **Read access** **None** or **All**.

| To do this | The role needs |
| --- | --- |
| See the Inbox and read conversations | **Inbox messages**: **Read access** **All** |
| Reply, save or send drafts, start a conversation from a contact | **Inbox messages**: **Create** |
| Discard a draft | **Inbox messages**: **Delete** |
| Change the state, move an email, **Refresh**, **Resync thread**, load older messages, share a conversation | **Inbox messages**: **Edit** |
| Match a participant's address to a record, or create a record from it | Read and edit access to that record; create access for a new record |
| Link a record to this one conversation | **Inbox messages**: **Edit**, plus read and edit access to the record |
| Add a Channels field to a list | **Data model** |

Without **Create** the reply box is hidden. Without **Edit** the move action is hidden, the state is a read-only chip, and opening an unread conversation leaves it **Unread**. Sharing a single conversation also requires that you own its channel.

## How do I search, filter or refresh the Inbox? [#how-do-i-search-filter-or-refresh-the-inbox]

The top bar of the [Inbox](app:inbox) holds:

- **Filter** searches and filters: type text and choose the **contains** entry to find conversations by subject, message text, conversation name, and participant names or addresses (F opens it). It also offers **State**, **Linked records**, **Participants** (**not fully linked** finds conversations with someone who has no matching record you can read), **Provider**, **Channel**, **Email folder**, **Draft** (**has some** finds conversations with a saved draft), **Last message** (**Sent** or **Received**), **Last message date** and **Last activity**.
- **Refresh** pulls new messages right away (**Inbox refreshed.**) and reports channels that hit a rate limit, need reconnecting or could not be refreshed. Needs **Edit**.
- **Channels** opens [Channels](app:settings/channels), with a number when channels need attention. Needs **Create**.

**Email folder** groups folders under their account, so folders with the same name stay apart; **in** finds conversations with a visible message in any selected folder, **not in** excludes them. **Last message** and **Last message date** use the latest real sent or received message; drafts, deleted messages and system events do not count. For mail from three to seven days ago, add two **Last message date** conditions: **Older than…** 3 days and **7 days**. **Last activity** includes saved drafts. All filters respect account sharing and the enabled folders.

The views row offers **All** and your personal saved views. The list shows 25 conversations per page (5, 10, 25 or 100). The sidebar entry shows how many conversations you can see are **Unread**.

## How do I open a specific conversation? [#how-do-i-open-a-specific-conversation]

Click its row. The browser address then points to that conversation, so you can bookmark it or send it to a teammate who can see it; an address you cannot open brings you back to the Inbox. To find the conversations of one record, pick it in the **Linked records** filter: matching uses the record's Channels values and still checks your access to each conversation. Mate does not open a single conversation for you: it opens the Inbox and names the conversation.

With **Edit** on **Inbox messages**, scrolling to the top of a conversation fetches older messages from the provider, once per conversation until you reload. **Resync thread** in the header fetches the whole conversation again.

## What is in the conversation header? [#what-is-in-the-conversation-header]

Next to the conversation's name, left to right:

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Move conversation to folder…** (IMAP and Outlook) | Files the conversation in another folder; needs **Edit** |
| **Resync thread** | Fetches the conversation again; needs **Edit** |
| **Private Thread** or **Shared Thread** chip | Opens **Thread settings** |
| **Some participants have no linked record you can read** | Opens **Thread settings**; shown when you can create or edit records in a list with a Channels field |
| State chip | **Unread**, **Open**, **Closed** or **Spam** |

On narrow screens the chips show icons only.

Incoming messages sit on the left, outgoing on the right. An email's compact header shows sender, time and first recipient; **Details** (also under **Email actions**) shows **From**, **To**, **Cc** and **Bcc**. Bcc is visible only on outgoing mail, to the account owner and teammates with access to the whole account; sharing a single conversation never reveals it. Click an address to copy it. A folder button below the recipients shows where that email is filed. Received HTML keeps the sender's layout and colors, and remote images load only after **Load remote images**.

## What do the thread states mean? [#what-do-the-thread-states-mean]

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Unread** | New, or a new message came in |
| **Open** | Read, still needs attention |
| **Closed** | Done |
| **Spam** | Marked as spam |

Opening an unread conversation sets it to **Open** (only with **Edit**). The state is shared by everyone who sees the conversation. A new incoming message sets it back to **Unread**, even from **Closed** or **Spam**. **Spam** is only a label: it does not hide the conversation, block the sender or report anything to the provider; use the **State** filter to keep it out of view. Setting a state never sends anything.

## How do I move an email to another folder? [#how-do-i-move-an-email-to-another-folder]

In IMAP and Outlook conversations, click an email's folder button and pick a destination under **Move this email to…** to move only that email, or use **Move conversation to folder…** in the header to file the whole conversation. Messages move in the real mailbox too. Sent and Drafts are never targets, and messages in them stay put. Gmail conversations, members without **Edit** and teammates who see a conversation shared from a private channel can read the folder names but get no move options; only the channel owner files those.

You can keep working while the move runs. Messages moved into a folder that is switched off on the account's **Folders** tab disappear from the conversation; when nothing visible remains, the confirmation says it no longer appears in your Inbox. If the provider rate-limits partway, a message says so and you can move the rest later.

## How do I share a single conversation? [#how-do-i-share-a-single-conversation]

Click **Private Thread** or **Shared Thread** in the header to open **Thread settings**. Under **Visibility Settings**, the switch shares just this conversation with everyone whose role can read the Inbox (**Shared to CRM**); switching it off shows **Conversation is now private**. Only the channel owner can use it, on a plan with messaging and with **Edit**. Teammates can then read the conversation, change its state and resync it, but not move it, and only you can reply while the channel itself is private.

To share every conversation of a channel, switch on **Visibility** on the channel's **Details** tab on [Channels](app:settings/channels) (Business or Enterprise). The per-conversation switch then stays on and locked.

## How do I link participants and records? [#how-do-i-link-a-participant]

Messages are matched to records through the Channels field: a record whose Channels contain an email address, phone number or profile shows every conversation with that address. Open **Thread settings**. Under **People**, each participant shows the records you can see that match their address. **Link** adds the address to the Channels of a record in any list that has a Channels field; **Unlink record** removes only that match. One address can belong to several records, for example a contact and an organization, and every other conversation with the same address follows. Ordinary text fields are never used for matching.

Under **Conversation records**, **Link record** attaches only this conversation to a record, for example a deal in a list without a Channels field; other conversations with the participant stay separate. Linking never grants access to private messages or restricted records.

## How do I reply to a conversation? [#how-do-i-reply-to-a-conversation]

Click **Write a reply...** (email) or **Type a message...** (chat) below the conversation. Email replies show **Subject** and **To**, filled from the conversation; **Cc/Bcc** adds those fields, and any combination works as long as there is one valid recipient. You write email in a Markdown editor, and your signature, if switched on, is added when sending. Bcc recipients are never carried into a reply. Chat replies are plain text. Use **Attach files** and **Insert emoji** as needed, then **Send** or Cmd/Ctrl+Enter; **More send options** holds **Save as draft**.

If sending fails, **Retry** appears only when trying again can help (rate limit, temporary outage). When the provider rejects a message, change it first; after a timeout, check the conversation, because the message may already have gone out. A channel that needs reconnecting is reported before anything is sent, and only its owner can reconnect it. Replies go out from the conversation's own channel, so you can answer only on channels you own or that are shared with the workspace.

## How do I start a new conversation or write a new email to a contact? [#how-do-i-start-a-new-conversation]

The Inbox has no button for a new conversation. Open the contact in [Contacts](app:records/contact) and click the **New message** icon next to an email address or handle in its Channels. When several of your channels can send, pick one under **From**; for LinkedIn with Sales Navigator or Recruiter, **Send as** chooses **Classic**, **Sales Navigator InMail** or **Recruiter InMail**. After **Send** the message goes out in the background (**Sending message**, then **Message sent.**). This needs Customermates Cloud and **Create** on **Inbox messages**.

## How do drafts and attachments work? [#how-do-drafts-and-attachments-work]

**Save as draft** keeps a message without sending it; while you edit a saved draft it reads **Update draft**. A reply draft sits in its conversation with **Send draft**, **Edit** and **Discard**. A draft for a new conversation appears in the list as its own conversation ("Draft saved. Find it in the inbox."); the **Draft** filter with **has some** lists them all. There is one draft per conversation. A new LinkedIn InMail cannot be saved as a draft. Discarding cannot be undone. The app, the REST API and MCP share the same drafts.

**Attach files** adds up to 20 files per message, 15 MB together. Drafts cannot keep attachments ("Remove attachments before saving this draft."). In received messages supported media opens inline, other files download, and unsupported items show **Unsupported**.

## How do I set an email signature? [#how-do-i-set-an-email-signature]

The signature belongs to each email account you own: on [Channels](app:settings/channels), open the Gmail, Outlook or IMAP account and use its **Email** tab. Set font, size and link style, switch on **Add a signature**, choose a layout with or without a logo and write the text in Markdown. While it is on, it is added below every email sent from that account, from the Inbox, the REST API or MCP, so do not paste it into the message; the composer previews it. All fields: [Email appearance and signature](/docs/app-profile#email-appearance-and-signature).

## For agents [#tips-for-agents]

Prefer [MCP](/docs/mcp) tools over clicking: `get_messaging_threads` reads conversations, `update_messaging_thread` sets the state, `move_email_thread` files an email (it fails for a conversation shared from someone else's private channel), `send_email` and `send_chat_message` start conversations, and `save_message_draft` (REST `POST /v1/messaging/drafts`) prepares one for review; send or discard a draft with its message id and current `draftRevision`. `resolve_record_identifiers` finds the records that match an address, `manage_conversation_records` reads and changes the records linked to one conversation, and `mutate_crm_record` changes a record's Channels.

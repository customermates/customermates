After sign-up you choose **Create a new workspace** or **Join an existing workspace**; joining needs an invitation from a workspace administrator. The onboarding wizard follows. The workspace is created only when you save your profile. The wizard opens only until onboarding is finished; finishing it lands on the [Dashboard](app:dashboard).

## What are the four steps of the onboarding wizard? [#what-are-the-three-steps]

The wizard for a new workspace on Customermates Cloud has four steps, shown as **You**, **Workspace**, **Team** and **AI tools**. Self-hosted instances and the demo have no **Workspace** step: the wizard goes from the profile straight to **Team**. A new self-hosted workspace starts with no lists: people who manage the data model create them in [Configure](app:configure), and the empty [Dashboard](app:dashboard) offers them **Create list**. The demo shows its sample data. A workspace that already had lists before the AI workspace setup existed skips the **Workspace** step as well. On Customermates Cloud the profile and the workspace setup are required; self-hosted, only the profile is.

<Steps>
<Step title="Your profile">
Enter **First Name**, **Last Name** and **Country**; the email shows your sign-in address and cannot be changed here. On Customermates Cloud you also accept the terms and can enter your **Company website**: reading it starts as soon as you continue, so the next step can suggest answers. **Continue** saves the profile, creates the workspace and moves on. Once saved, the wizard cannot return to this step; change your name or country later in [Profile & preferences](app:settings/profile).
</Step>
<Step title="Set up your workspace">
Answer six short screens about your company, how you win customers, what follows a sale, your dashboard, your knowledge and your data. Each question is a choice, a short list or a number, and answers Customermates could read on your website are already filled in and marked **From your website**; confirm each screen with **Looks right**. The website is optional: without one, or with **Continue without a website** on the setup's first screen, the setup works from your answers alone. Your answers are saved per screen, so you can close the tab and continue later. Where an answer could mean more than one thing, a few extra questions follow. On the last screen you choose sample data, an empty start, or **Import a spreadsheet** (CSV or an Excel workbook in .xlsx format, up to 5 MB, 2,000 rows and 100 columns). A file that cannot be imported shows the reason, and a file chosen before it stays in use.

The plan then shows what will be built: your lists with their colours, the pipeline stages, the key fields, a dashboard sketch and the Knowledge Base pages. Every plan has a list of people and a tasks list, usually organizations and a deals list, and a catalog of your offerings when you sell products or services; see [which lists a workspace has](/docs/concepts#which-five-record-types-exist). **Build my workspace** builds it, and **Adjust answers** returns to the questions and drafts a new plan. Changes made there take effect only when you confirm the last screen; **Back** on the first screen returns to the current plan without them. Choosing another spreadsheet there replaces the file at once, so the current plan can no longer be built and a new one is drafted. Drafting is tried twice automatically; if both attempts fail, the plan screen offers **Try again** and **Adjust answers**.

While **Setting up your workspace** runs you watch the lists, the pipeline, sample records, dashboard widgets and pages appear; it keeps running if you close the tab and reopens when you return. The parts **Lists and fields**, **List views**, **Records** and **Dashboard** are each tried up to three times automatically; a part that still fails keeps everything finished so far and offers **Retry**, which starts three more tries. The **Knowledge Base** part waits for your website instead: if it cannot be read, the part offers **Retry** and **Continue without a website**, and the setup then finishes without pages from your website. If the workspace's setup budget is used up, the part stops with a message that says so and offers **Contact support**; everything finished so far stays. **Continue to invitations** appears once the whole build has completed; the setup cannot be left before that.
</Step>
<Step title="Invite your team">
**Share link** (open by default) shows an invitation link to copy: anyone with it can join, and it is valid for 7 days. **Send emails** takes up to 20 addresses, separated by a comma or Enter, and **Send invitations** emails them the link. **Next** moves on with or without invitations; **Back** is not offered right after the workspace setup, because the setup cannot be repeated. You can invite people later with [Add](app:settings/members?focus=add) on Members.
</Step>
<Step title="Connect your AI">
Connect an external AI client (Claude, ChatGPT and Codex, Cursor or Gemini) to your workspace's [MCP endpoint](/docs/mcp). Local setups (Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Codex, Cursor, Gemini) create an API key for that client that expires after 365 days and show its setup snippet with the expiry date; the Claude account and ChatGPT app methods sign in through a connector and create no key. On the client list, **Back** returns to the invite step and **Finish** completes onboarding without a connection. After choosing a client, **Back** returns to the list, and **Finish** becomes available once the key is created or a connector method is picked. The same setup stays available later; keys are managed on [API keys](app:settings/api-keys), and the in-app [Assistant](/docs/app-assistant) works without any of this.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What does an invited person see? [#what-does-an-invited-person-see]

Someone who opens an invitation sees only the profile step, without the progress bar, with **Invitation from ...** naming the inviter. After **Continue** they are registered with status **Waiting for approval** and every sign-in lands on **Waiting for Approval** (or first on **Email Verification**, if their email is still unverified 24 hours after registering). They never see the workspace setup, invite or AI steps. Someone whose role has **Edit** on **Users & Roles**, or an **Admin**, opens the person on [Members](app:settings/members), assigns a **Role** and sets the **Status** to **Active**; only then can they work in the workspace.

## Frequently asked questions [#frequently-asked-questions]

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Can I skip parts of the onboarding?">
Yes, the invite and AI steps: **Next** leaves the invite step without sending anything, and **Finish** on the AI client list completes onboarding without a connection. Invite people later on [Members](app:settings/members?focus=add) and manage AI keys on [API keys](app:settings/api-keys). The profile step is required, and on Customermates Cloud so is the workspace setup: it has no skip and moves on with **Continue to invitations** once the build has completed. Only your website is optional there.
</FaqItem>
<FaqItem question="Can I go back to the profile step?">
No. Once the profile is saved, the wizard cannot return to it. Change your name, country and other personal settings later in [Profile & preferences](app:settings/profile).
</FaqItem>
<FaqItem question="What happens to people who register through the invitation link?">
They join with status **Waiting for approval**, whether they used the shared link or an email invitation, and see **Waiting for Approval** until someone whose role has **Edit** on **Users & Roles**, or an **Admin**, assigns a role and sets them to **Active** on [Members](app:settings/members). The link stays valid for 7 days.
</FaqItem>
<FaqItem question="Do I need to connect an AI client to use the assistant?">
No. The built-in Assistant works without any external client. The AI step connects external tools such as Claude or Cursor to your workspace through MCP.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

## For agents [#for-agents]

The wizard has no Mate highlight targets. Fill each step's fields by their visible labels and press the step's main button (**Continue**, **Looks right**, **Build my workspace**, **Continue to invitations**, **Send invitations**, **Next**, **Back**, **Finish**).

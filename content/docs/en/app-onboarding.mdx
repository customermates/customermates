A fresh sign-up first lands on `/onboarding`, where you create a workspace or join one through an invitation, and then opens the onboarding wizard at `/onboarding/wizard`. It runs once; finishing it lands on the dashboard. This page documents the wizard for people and for AI agents that drive the UI.

## What are the three steps?

<Steps>
<Step title="Your profile">
Your name and country. The fields are ordinary form inputs (`firstName`, `lastName`, `country`) addressed by their visible labels; the terms checkbox (`agreeToTerms`) is rendered only in cloud mode. This step is required.
</Step>
<Step title="Invite your team">
Invite teammates by email, or copy the shareable link of the form `/invitation/{token}`. Anyone who registers through that link joins the workspace as **Waiting for Approval** until an admin assigns a role and activates them on the [Company page](/docs/app-company). This step can be skipped and repeated later from the Company page; **Next** (`#onboarding-next`) moves on to Connect your AI.
</Step>
<Step title="Connect your AI">
Connect an external AI client (Claude, ChatGPT and Codex, Cursor, or Gemini) to the workspace's [MCP endpoint](/docs/mcp). For the local setups (Claude Code or Claude Desktop, Codex, Cursor, Gemini) the flow creates an API key for the chosen client and shows its setup snippet; the Claude account and ChatGPT app methods connect through the hosted connector and create no key. This step can be skipped with **Skip for now**; the same flow stays available on the [Profile page](/docs/app-profile), and the in-app [Assistant](/docs/app-assistant) works without it. The wizard is finished with **Finish** in this step's own footer.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Which stable ids does the wizard have?

| Control | Id | Where it exists |
|---|---|---|
| Back to the profile step | `#onboarding-back` (**Back**) | Only on the invite step; disabled when the wizard opened on the invite step because the profile was already complete |
| Continue to Connect your AI | `#onboarding-next` (**Next**) | Only on the invite step |

The profile and AI steps use their own buttons without stable ids (**Back**, **Skip for now** and **Finish** on the AI step): drive those by their visible labels. Inside every step the fields are a regular form; fill it by label, then activate the step's primary button.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Can I skip steps?">
The invite and AI steps are skippable. Invitations can be sent later from the Company page, and the AI-client flow stays available on the Profile page. The profile step is required.
</FaqItem>
<FaqItem question="What happens to people who register through the invitation link?">
They join the workspace as Waiting for Approval. An admin assigns a role and activates them on the Company page; until then they cannot work in the workspace.
</FaqItem>
<FaqItem question="Do I need to connect an AI client to use the assistant?">
No. The built-in Assistant works without any external client. The AI step connects external tools such as Claude or Cursor to your workspace through MCP.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

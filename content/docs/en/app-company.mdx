Customermates is an open-source CRM with a built-in MCP server. This page documents the **My Company** section of the web app, page by page, for humans and for AI agents that drive the UI: what each setting does, who can change it, and where to find it.

## What lives under My Company? [#what-lives-on-the-company-screen]

Workspace-wide administration: the subscription (Cloud only), the workspace settings, the team and its permissions, outbound webhooks with their delivery history, and the audit trail. Everything that affects the whole workspace, not a single record, lives here.

In the sidebar, **My Company** (`#nav-company`) sits in the **Workspace** group next to **My Profile**. It is a toggle: clicking it expands or collapses its list of pages and opens no page itself. Expanding it collapses the other expanded group, and it opens by itself while you are on one of its pages. Each page has its own route, and the browser address puts the language in front of it (example: `/en/company/members`). The breadcrumb reads **My Company** › page name; the first crumb opens the first page you can see, and the second switches between the sibling pages.

Routes are relative and carry no language prefix: they work on the address the workspace runs on, and the app adds the user's language, so `/company/subscription` opens `/en/company/subscription` for an English user. For a full link, put the route after the origin of the `url` that `search_docs`, `get_docs_page` and `fetch` return; that origin is the instance's configured `BASE_URL`. Mate's `highlight_element` also takes each navigate target: `nav-company` points at the **My Company** toggle, and every other one at its page's sidebar entry. A sidebar entry can be highlighted only while **My Company** is expanded, which it is by default on its own pages unless the member collapsed it. **Add** targets exist only for roles that can manage the page, and the layout targets only while display options are open. Billing targets appear only for roles with **Company** **Manage**, and a dialog target only while its dialog is open: where a control opens the dialog, it is the target's prerequisite, and Mate asks the user to open it first. Where a row opens the dialog, `list_ui_targets` names that row as the prerequisite (for example `a member row`); rows have no target, so Mate asks the user to click the row.

Subscription is Cloud only. Audit Logs needs **Audit Log** read access **All**, and Webhooks and Recent Deliveries need **API & Webhooks** read access **All**. The **Users & Roles** read setting controls whether Members and Roles appear; **Assigned** limits the member list to your own row, and **None** hides both pages. Company read access controls Settings and Subscription. Editing requires the corresponding management permission. The built-in **Admin** role sees and can change everything.

### What does a member without a permission see?

A member without a permission sees fewer pages and controls:

- A page the role cannot read is missing from the sidebar, and opening its URL directly lands on the **Dashboard**.
- On a page the role can read but not manage, **Save**, **Reset** and **Add** are not shown, and forms and dialogs open read-only.
- On Cloud, while the subscription is unpaid or expired, or after the trial has ended, every page shows **Subscription Required** instead; see [what happens when the trial ends](#what-happens-when-the-trial-ends-or-a-payment-fails).
- On Cloud, when the Terms or the DPA change, the sidebar shows **Legal documents updated** with **Review Terms and DPA** and the date by which to accept. Only a member with the **Admin** role can accept, on the **Legal documents updated** screen (`/legal-update`), by confirming the checkbox and clicking **Accept and continue**; everyone else sees "A company administrator must accept by" that date. If nobody accepts in time, every page opens this screen until an Admin accepts.
- Role changes apply on the member's next page load or API call.

**Link:** `/dashboard`, where a page the role cannot read leads, and `/legal-update` for **Legal documents updated**, which opens only while an acceptance is pending. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-dashboard` for the Dashboard; **Legal documents updated** has no target, and Mate is not available there, so it can only give the link; the checkbox `#legal-acceptance` is an element id, not highlightable by Mate.

## Subscription page: plan and billing [#subscription-tab]

The link to billing is the **Subscription** page: it shows the plan, status, seats and billing of the workspace. Route `/company/subscription`, sidebar id `#nav-company-subscription`, Cloud only: on a self-hosted install the entry is hidden, the URL redirects to the Dashboard, and there is no subscription. Every member can open the page. Who may use the billing controls is under [Who can manage billing?](#who-can-manage-billing). Customermates bills through Lemon Squeezy, the payment provider that runs checkout and the customer portal.

| Field | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Plan** | The current plan, **Starter**, **Pro**, **Business** or **Enterprise**, with a status chip. Read-only: it is synced from billing and changes through the plan picker or the customer portal. |
| **Trial ends** | The date the free trial ends. Shown only while the status is **Trial**. |
| **Current period ends** | The end of the current billing period, synced from Lemon Squeezy: the renewal date, or after a cancellation the date the subscription ends. Shown once billing has set it. |
| **Active seats** | The number of billed seats. Before the first checkout it shows the number of **Active** members. The note below reads "You pay per active user. Seat changes are prorated on your next invoice." |

The top bar holds **Refresh** (`#company-subscription-refresh`) and **Manage with Lemon Squeezy** (`#company-subscription-manage`), and the plan picker below the fields carries `#company-subscription-plan-picker`. Plan prices and allowances are under [Which plans are there?](#which-plans-are-there); the hosted AI credits per plan are under [Plans and credits](/docs/app-assistant#plans-and-credits).

**Link:** the link to the **Subscription** page, `/company/subscription`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-company-subscription`; `highlight_element` also takes `company-subscription-refresh`, `company-subscription-manage` and `company-subscription-plan-picker`, each shown only to roles that manage billing, in the subscription state that offers it. The fields are not highlight targets, so Mate names them. On self-hosted, `list_ui_targets` still offers the targets, but the page redirects to the Dashboard.

### Who can manage billing? [#who-can-manage-billing]

Only roles with **Company** set to **Manage** **Yes**, and the **Admin** role, see the billing controls: the plan picker, **Manage with Lemon Squeezy** and **Refresh**. Everyone else sees the values read-only with a hint to ask a workspace administrator. The role's **Company** row is set under **My Company › Roles**.

**Link:** `/company/subscription`, and for the role's **Company** row `/company/roles`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-company-subscription` or `nav-company-roles`; the role rows are not highlight targets, so Mate names them.

### What does each subscription status mean?

The status chip next to **Plan** shows one of six statuses. The app sets **Trial** when a Cloud workspace is created; every other status comes from Lemon Squeezy, or from a correction by Customermates support. Nobody in the workspace can set it.

| Status | Meaning | Effect on the workspace |
|---|---|---|
| **Trial** | The free trial: [[commercial.trial.days]] days with Pro features, starting at sign-up | Everything works until **Trial ends** |
| **Active** | Paid and current | Everything the plan includes works |
| **Past Due** | Lemon Squeezy reports an overdue payment | Pages stay open, but the Inbox and messaging, connecting channels, shared accounts and the hosted assistant are refused, and hosted AI credits drop to 0 |
| **Cancelled** | The subscription was cancelled | Same as Past Due |
| **Unpaid** | Lemon Squeezy reports the subscription as unpaid | Every page redirects to **Subscription Required** |
| **Expired** | The subscription has ended | Every page redirects to **Subscription Required** |

A trial whose end date has passed without a checkout counts as expired, too. Refused messaging and channel actions show "Connecting channels requires an active subscription or a trial that hasn't expired." The hosted assistant is switched off instead, so **Ask Mate** disappears from the sidebar.

The chip under the Customermates name in the sidebar shows the plan name while the status is **Active**, and otherwise the plan with its status, for example **Pro · Trial · 3 days left**, **1 day left** or **ends today**. It is green, turns amber at 3 or fewer trial days, and is red for every other status. Clicking it opens **My Company › Subscription**. Self-hosted installs show no chip.

**Link:** `/company/subscription`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-company-subscription`; the status chip and the plan chip in the sidebar are not highlight targets, so Mate names them.

### Which plans are there, and what are their prices? [#which-plans-are-there]

Customermates Cloud has four plans, **Starter**, **Pro**, **Business** and **Enterprise**, and a free trial of [[commercial.trial.days]] days with Pro features.

| Plan | Price per seat and month | Connected accounts per user | Routines per user | Inbox and messaging | Shared accounts |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] | [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedAccountsPerUser]] | [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] | No | No |
| Pro | [[commercial.price.pro.monthly]] | [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] | [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]] | Yes | No |
| Business | [[commercial.price.business.monthly]] | [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedAccountsPerUser]] | [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedRoutinesPerUser]] | Yes | Yes |
| Enterprise | On request | [[commercial.entitlement.enterprise.includedAccountsPerUser]] | [[commercial.entitlement.enterprise.includedRoutinesPerUser]] | Yes | Yes |

Every plan includes the full CRM, the audit log, the API, the MCP server and the hosted assistant. Starter, Pro and Business are billed monthly per seat, in euros; there is no other billing interval. Hosted AI credits per active user and month: Starter 200, Pro 500, Business 1,200, Enterprise as contracted, and 500 during the trial. Each request is charged its exact provider cost and shown to one decimal; see [How many credits does each plan include?](/docs/app-assistant#plans-and-credits).

**Link:** `/company/subscription`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-company-subscription`, then `highlight_element` with `company-subscription-plan-picker` for the plan cards while the picker is shown; the target outlines all cards, so Mate names the plan.

### How do I choose, upgrade or change a plan? [#how-do-i-choose-or-change-a-plan]

To choose, upgrade or change a plan, use the plan picker, or **Manage with Lemon Squeezy** after the first checkout. While the workspace has no Lemon Squeezy subscription yet, which in practice means during the trial, roles that can manage Company see the plan picker below the fields: cards for **Starter**, **Pro** and **Business**, the last one marked **Most popular**. Each card shows the price per user and month, the features, the connected accounts per user (left out when a plan includes no connected accounts, as on Starter), the routines per user, and the hosted AI credits per active user and month.

Clicking a card opens the Lemon Squeezy checkout for as many seats as the workspace has **Active** members. After payment, Lemon Squeezy returns you to the **Subscription** page, and the plan, status and seats update once Lemon Squeezy confirms the payment. Checkout adds no second trial: billing starts at checkout, even when trial days are left.

After the first checkout the picker does not come back on its own; change the plan with **Manage with Lemon Squeezy** instead. Enterprise workspaces never see the picker, and a checkout is refused with "Enterprise workspaces are billed manually and cannot start self-service checkout."

**Link:** `/company/subscription`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-company-subscription`, then `highlight_element` with `company-subscription-plan-picker` while the picker is shown; the target outlines all three cards, so Mate names the card to click. After the first checkout, `company-subscription-manage` points at **Manage with Lemon Squeezy** instead.

### How do I cancel, change the payment method or get invoices?

Use **Manage with Lemon Squeezy** in the top bar, shown as the Lemon Squeezy icon only on narrow screens. The button appears for roles that can manage Company once the workspace has a self-serve Lemon Squeezy subscription, so not during a trial before checkout and never on Enterprise. It opens the Lemon Squeezy customer portal for the workspace's subscription, which is where billing is managed: changing the plan, updating the payment method, getting invoices and cancelling are what the portal is for. Clicking the button fetches the portal link at that moment and opens the portal in the same tab. If Lemon Squeezy has no portal for the subscription, the page shows "The billing portal is not available for this workspace. Contact support to make changes."

Changes made in the portal reach Customermates automatically. If the page still shows old values, **Refresh** re-reads the subscription from Lemon Squeezy (status, plan, seats and dates) and confirms with "Subscription refreshed successfully". It appears for the same roles once the workspace has a Lemon Squeezy subscription, except during a trial and on Enterprise. Once Lemon Squeezy reports a cancellation, the status shows **Cancelled**: as with **Past Due**, the Inbox and messaging, connecting channels, shared accounts and the hosted assistant are refused. A plan with fewer connected accounts removes accounts; see [plan changes](#what-happens-to-connected-accounts-when-the-plan-changes).

Members without Company Manage see neither button. The hosted assistant and MCP clients cannot read or change the plan, the status, the seats or anything else about billing.

**Link:** `/company/subscription`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-company-subscription`, then `highlight_element` with `company-subscription-manage` for **Manage with Lemon Squeezy** or `company-subscription-refresh` for **Refresh**; each is shown only to roles with **Company** set to **Manage** **Yes** and to the **Admin** role, **Manage with Lemon Squeezy** once the workspace has a self-serve Lemon Squeezy subscription and **Refresh** once it has a Lemon Squeezy subscription and outside the trial, neither on Enterprise.

### How are seats counted and billed?

A seat is a member with status **Active**. Members **Waiting for approval** and **Inactive** members are not billed. The checkout takes the number of Active members at that moment. After that, every status change on **Members**, or through `manage_team`, sends the new number of Active members to Lemon Squeezy right away, and the in-app note says seat changes are prorated on your next invoice. Enterprise seats are not synced to billing.

To lower the bill, set members who no longer need access to **Inactive** on [Members](#members-tab). That also pauses the routines they own and removes their connected messaging accounts at the next daily run.

**Link:** `/company/subscription` for **Active seats**, and `/company/members` to change a status. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-company-subscription` or `nav-company-members`; **Active seats** and the member rows are not highlight targets, so Mate names them. Once the user has opened a member row, `member-modal-status` points at **Status** and `member-modal-save` at **Save** (roles with Manage).

### What happens when the free trial ends or a payment fails, and why am I locked out? [#what-happens-when-the-trial-ends-or-a-payment-fails]

When the status is **Unpaid** or **Expired**, or the trial has passed its end date, every page opens **Subscription Required** (`/subscription-expired`) with the subtitle **Workspace access is paused**. **Past Due** and **Cancelled** do not lock the pages. What the page offers depends on the role:

| Who | What the page offers |
|---|---|
| Roles that can manage Company, and **Admin**, on Starter, Pro or Business | The plan picker to start a new checkout, plus **Contact Support** |
| The same roles on Enterprise | A note that the workspace is billed manually, **Contact Support** and **Retry** |
| Everyone else | "A company administrator must restore the subscription", **Contact Support** and **Retry** |

**Contact Support** opens an email to Customermates support with the subject "Subscription Inquiry"; **Retry** reloads the page. Once the subscription is usable again, the page sends you to **My Company** › **Subscription**. While it is shown, **Search**, **Ask Mate** and **Add** in the sidebar only check access again.

A daily job at 09:00 UTC takes these steps after a trial ends without a checkout:

1. Within the first day, an email offers to extend the trial.
2. After one day, every connected messaging account in the workspace is deleted.
3. After 3 to 4 days, a reminder email follows.
4. After 6 to 7 days, every member, admins included, is set **Inactive** and notified by email. Nobody can sign in afterwards, so restoring access goes through Customermates support.

A paid workspace that stays **Unpaid** or **Expired** for 3 days has all members set **Inactive** with an email, and the same daily job then deletes their connected accounts.

Only the web app is paused before that point. Until the members are set **Inactive**, their API keys and MCP connections keep reading and changing records, and webhooks keep firing for those changes; messaging, channels and the hosted assistant stop as soon as the subscription is no longer usable.

**Link:** `/subscription-expired` for **Subscription Required**; it opens only while access is paused, otherwise it redirects to `/company/subscription`. **Mate:** no target, and Mate is not available on this page, so it can only pass on the URL; the plan cards carry `#company-subscription-plan-picker` here too, but Mate cannot highlight them, and **Contact Support** and **Retry** have no id.

### What happens to connected accounts when the plan changes, for example a switch to Starter? [#what-happens-to-connected-accounts-when-the-plan-changes]

When Lemon Squeezy reports a different plan, through the automatic sync or **Refresh**, Customermates removes each user's newest connected accounts beyond the new plan's allowance per user. The Starter allowance is [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedAccountsPerUser]] per user, so every connected account is removed. The first checkout from the trial counts as a change from Pro: choosing Starter removes every connected account. Members with the **Admin** role get an email that lists the removed accounts. Routines above the new allowance are kept, because the allowance applies only when a routine is created.

**Link:** `/company/subscription`, and the remaining accounts on `/profile/connected-accounts`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-company-subscription` or `nav-profile-connected-accounts`, and `highlight_element` with `company-subscription-refresh` for **Refresh**; the account cards are not highlight targets, so Mate names them.

### How does an Enterprise workspace differ?

An **Enterprise** workspace is set up with Customermates and billed by invoice. The page shows **Plan** and the note "This workspace is on an Enterprise plan billed by invoice. Contact your account manager to make changes." **Trial ends**, **Current period ends**, **Active seats**, the plan picker, **Refresh** and **Manage with Lemon Squeezy** are hidden, self-service checkout is refused, and member status changes are not sent to billing.

**Link:** `/company/subscription`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-company-subscription`; **Plan** and the Enterprise note are not highlight targets, so Mate names them.

### Where do upgrade buttons lead?

On Starter, the Inbox and **My Profile** › **Channels** show **Upgrade to unlock messaging** with the button **Upgrade your plan**. A Pro user at the connected-account limit sees **Upgrade to connect more accounts** with **View plans**, and the hosted assistant's credit notice shows **View plans** too. All of them open **My Company** › **Subscription**. Only roles that can manage Company can act there, and once the workspace has a subscription, upgrading goes through **Manage with Lemon Squeezy**.

**Link:** `/company/subscription`; the buttons sit on `/inbox` and `/profile/connected-accounts`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-company-subscription`, `nav-inbox` or `nav-profile-connected-accounts`; **Upgrade your plan** and **View plans** are not highlight targets, so Mate names them.

## Company settings page [#settings-tab]

**My Company → Settings** at `/company/settings` contains the workspace currency and a link to **Configure**, the separate page for CRM lists.

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Currency** | Formats amounts in the workspace; switching does not convert amounts. Currency fields with their own configured currency retain that setting. |

The currency selector is `#company-settings-currency`. Members with **Company → Manage** can save a change with `#company-settings-save` or discard an unsaved change with `#company-settings-reset`; other members see the currency read-only. The **Configure** link under **Data model** (`#company-settings-data-model`) opens `/configure`. That separate page, also reachable as **Configure** in the sidebar's **CRM** group (`#nav-configure-records`), owns lists, fields, relationships, stage probabilities, and calculations.

**Link:** `/company/settings`. **Mate:** `nav-company-settings` opens the page; `company-settings-currency`, `company-settings-data-model`, `company-settings-save`, and `company-settings-reset` identify the available controls.

### Who can change the currency and the other settings? [#who-can-change-the-currency-and-the-other-settings]

**Company → Manage** controls currency changes on Settings. **Data model → Manage** controls type and field configuration; it can be delegated separately and does not grant access to records or permission to expand role grants. Role administration is required to expand access. The assistant uses `update_workspace_settings` for currency and `configure_record_model` for record configuration, with the same backend checks as the UI.

**Link:** `/company/settings` for currency; `/configure` for CRM configuration.

### Where do I set stage probabilities for weighting, and how do totals work? [#how-do-stage-probabilities-and-totals-work]

Open **Configure**, select **Deals**, and edit its stage select field to set a probability on each option. The shipped **Weighted value** calculation multiplies the calculated deal value by the selected stage probability divided by 100. A zero probability produces zero; a missing probability leaves weighted value absent. Changes to probabilities or calculations go through a dependency preview and recalculate affected records. You can change the formula or make deal value manually entered in the same configuration system. To show pipeline totals, add a Dashboard chart widget that sums **Value** or **Weighted value**, optionally grouped by stage. There is no separate pipeline-total form on Company Settings.

**Link:** `/configure` for stage metadata and calculations; `/dashboard` for totals. See [weighted pipeline](/docs/concepts#how-does-a-weighted-pipeline-work).

### Can I name Contacts anything I want, and how do I rename record types such as Deals? [#how-do-i-rename-record-types-in-the-data-model]

Open **Configure**, select the list in the left rail, and edit **Name** and **Navigation label** under **General**, then **Save** in the top bar. You can rename any of the five starting types or a customer-created type. Names are editable text, while stable type IDs keep records, links, views, widgets, and API references connected. The same validated configuration operation is available to the assistant through `get_record_model` and `configure_record_model`. A schema manager needs **Data model → Manage**; renaming does not require changing currency.

**Link:** `/configure`. **Mate:** navigate with `nav-configure-records`; `company-settings-data-model` is the link from Settings.

## Members page [#members-tab]

The **Members** page lists the people in the workspace and is where you invite teammates. Route `/company/members`, sidebar id `#nav-company-members`. Every member can open it: with **Users & Roles** read access **All** the list shows every member, with **Assigned** only your own row. Inviting needs **Users & Roles** set to **Manage** **Yes**; approving and editing other members also needs **Read access** **All** on **Users & Roles**, because **Assigned** lists only your own row. The **Admin** role can do both. The columns are **Name**, **Email**, **Role**, **Status**, **Updated at** and **Created at**.

| Action | Where | Anchor id | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| Invite members | Members toolbar, **Add** | `#company-members-add` | Opens the **Invite User** dialog |
| Share the invitation link | **Invite User**, tab **Share link**, open when the dialog opens | `#invite-modal-tab-link` / `#invite-modal-link` / `#invite-modal-copy-link` | Shows the **Invitation link** with a copy button and its expiry date |
| Invite by email | **Invite User**, tab **Send emails**, address input, **Send invitations** | `#invite-modal-tab-email` / `#invite-modal-emails` / `#invite-modal-send` | Emails the workspace link to up to 20 addresses |
| Edit or approve a member | Click the row, **User** dialog | Fields in the table below; `#member-modal-save` / `#member-modal-reset` | **Save** applies the role, status and profile fields; **Reset** discards changes |
| Search, filter, display options | Members toolbar | `#company-members-search` / `#company-members-filter` / `#company-members-display-options` | List controls; filters cover status, updated at and created at |
| Layout | Display-options popover | `#company-members-layout-table` / `#company-members-layout-board` | Table is the only usable layout; the board control is present but disabled because members have nothing to group by |

**Link:** the link to the **Members** page, `/company/members`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-company-members`; `highlight_element` also takes the toolbar ids in this table, `company-members-add` only for roles with Manage. In **Invite User** it takes `invite-modal-tab-link`, `invite-modal-link`, `invite-modal-copy-link` and `invite-modal-tab-email` (prerequisite `company-members-add`), and `invite-modal-emails` and `invite-modal-send` (prerequisite `invite-modal-tab-email`). In the **User** dialog it takes the `member-modal-*` field ids (read-only for roles without Manage), plus `member-modal-save` and `member-modal-reset` for roles with Manage only. The dialog opens from a row, so `list_ui_targets` gives `a member row` as the prerequisite instead of a target id, and Mate asks the user to click the row. Rows have no target, so Mate names them.

### How do invitations work?

To invite a teammate, click **Add** on the **Members** page; the **Invite User** dialog has two tabs. **Share link** shows one **Invitation link** of the form `/invitation/{token}` for the whole workspace. It is valid for 7 days, shows "Expires on" with the date, and is reused until then; the first time the dialog opens after it expired, a new link is created. Anyone who has the link can register, and there is no button to revoke it early.

**Send emails** is how you invite people by email: it takes 1 to 20 addresses at once, separated by a comma or Enter, and sends each address once. **Send invitations** emails every address the same workspace link and confirms with "Sent 3 invitations." (with the actual count).

People who register through the link join with status **Waiting for approval** and no role, and see a **Waiting for Approval** screen until someone approves them. An expired or altered link shows an error that asks them to request a new link from an administrator. Each registration also creates an **Approve User** task in **Tasks** for members whose **Tasks** read access is **All**; it disappears once the member is approved or set Inactive.

The hosted assistant's `manage_team` action `invite` sends the same emails and always asks for approval first, because it emails real people. No tool returns the invitation link.

**Link:** `/company/members`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-company-members`, then `highlight_element` with `company-members-add` for **Add**. Once the dialog is open (prerequisite `company-members-add`), it takes `invite-modal-tab-link` for **Share link**, `invite-modal-link` for the **Invitation link**, `invite-modal-copy-link` for the copy button and `invite-modal-tab-email` for **Send emails**; once that tab is open (prerequisite `invite-modal-tab-email`), `invite-modal-emails` for the addresses and `invite-modal-send` for **Send invitations**. The expiry date has no target, so Mate names it.

### How do I approve, edit, deactivate or remove a member? [#how-do-i-approve-deactivate-or-edit-a-member]

To approve, edit or deactivate a member, click the member's row to open the **User** dialog:

| Field | Id | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| **Email** | `#member-modal-email` | Read-only; it identifies the member |
| **First Name**, **Last Name** | `#member-modal-first-name`, `#member-modal-last-name` | Required |
| **Country** | `#member-modal-country` | Required |
| **Role** | `#member-modal-role` | Required; lists every role, the built-in one as **Admin** |
| **Avatar URL** | `#member-modal-avatar-url` | Optional link to a publicly accessible image |
| **Status** | `#member-modal-status` | **Active** or **Inactive**; **Waiting for approval** is set only by an invitation and cannot be chosen |

To approve a member, choose a **Role**, set **Status** to **Active** and click **Save**. They can sign in right away and count as a billed seat.

Members cannot be deleted; to remove someone, for example a user who left the company, set them **Inactive**. That blocks their sign-in ("Your account is currently inactive. Please contact your administrator to reactivate your account.") and every API key and MCP connection of theirs, removes their seat from billing, pauses the routines they own, and the daily job deletes their connected messaging accounts. **Inactive** is how access ends.

- Your own row opens read-only with "You can edit your own user account in your settings." Change your name in **My Profile** › **Settings**, and ask another member with Users & Roles Manage to change your role or status.
- The workspace always keeps at least one **Active** member with the **Admin** role. A save that would remove the last one fails with "At least one user must be a system administrator."
- Changes apply on the member's next page load or API call, and every save is recorded in the audit log.

`manage_team` action `update_member` changes a member's role and status without an approval prompt; for a member still **Waiting for approval**, pass both a `roleId` and the status `active`. Other members' names, country and avatar can only be changed in this dialog. `list_users` returns the members your role can read.

**Link:** `/company/members`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-company-members`; rows have no id, so Mate names the row. Once the user has opened the **User** dialog from a row, `highlight_element` takes the field ids in this table without `#`, read-only for roles without Manage and on your own row, plus `member-modal-save` for **Save** and `member-modal-reset` for **Reset** (roles with Manage only; **Reset** once something changed).

### How do I create a custom role in the role editor, for example to restrict a member to assigned records? [#how-does-the-role-editor-work]

The **Role** editor has **Name** (required, up to 255 characters) and **Description** (required, up to 500 characters), followed by workspace permissions and **Record types**.

Each record type has separate **Create**, **Edit** and **Delete** switches, plus **Read access**:

- **All** allows reading every record of that type in the workspace.
- **Assigned** allows reading records assigned to the member.
- **None** grants no read access. Editing or deleting also requires access to the individual record.

The list includes customer-created types and uses their current names. Renaming Projects to Initiatives keeps its grants. Archived types remain labeled in the editor. New roles start without record access; grant only the types they need. Embedded entries, such as deal line items, inherit access from their parent and have no separate row.

Workspace permissions retain the familiar **Manage** and **Read access** controls:

| Row | Manage | Read access | What it controls |
|---|---|---|---|
| **API & Webhooks** | Yes, No | All, None | Keys, webhooks and deliveries. Existing keys continue to use their owner's current record permissions. |
| **Users & Roles** | Yes, No | All, Assigned, None | Members and roles. Manage allows role administration; Assigned limits the member list to your own row. |
| **Company** | Yes, No | No selector | Workspace settings and billing. |
| **Data model** | Yes, No | No selector | Types, fields, relationships and calculations. This does not grant record access or role administration. |
| **Audit Log** | No selector | All, None | Audit pages and permitted record history. |
| **Inbox messages** (Cloud only) | Yes, No | All, None | Messaging features, subject to plan and inbox/account access. |
| **Routines** (Cloud only) | Yes, No | All, Assigned, None | All routines or the member's own routines. Deleting a routine or pausing someone else's requires Admin. |

A delegated data-model manager can use approved access presets when creating a type. Expanding grants requires role-administration authority. On self-hosted installations, Inbox messages and Routines are hidden. Editing a role's name or its record grants preserves unchanged system permissions.

**Link:** `/company/roles`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-company-roles`, then `highlight_element` with `company-roles-add` for a new role; once the editor is open, `role-modal-save` and `role-modal-reset` point at **Save** and **Reset**, and `role-modal-delete` at **Delete** where it is offered. The fields and the permission rows are not highlight targets, so Mate names them.

### What is the Admin role, and can I edit it? [#what-is-the-admin-role]

Every workspace starts with one built-in role, **Admin**, given to the person who created the workspace. It passes every permission check, shows every row as Yes and All, and its editor opens read-only with "This is a system role and cannot be edited or deleted." Nobody can change or delete it. The workspace must keep at least one **Active** member with this role, and only members with it can delete routines or pause someone else's routine.

**Link:** `/company/roles`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-company-roles`; the **Admin** row has no target, so Mate names it. Once the user has opened it, `role-modal-save` points at the disabled **Save** of the read-only editor, for roles with Manage.

### What are the rules for editing roles, and why can't I edit my own? [#what-are-the-rules-for-editing-roles]

- You cannot edit your own role. Its editor opens read-only with "This role is assigned to you and cannot be edited. Ask another administrator to change it."
- **Delete** appears only for a custom role that no member has, inactive members included. Move everyone to another role and remove references from access presets before deleting it.
- Changes apply to every member with the role on their next page load or API call, and they are recorded in the audit log.
- `manage_roles` reads, creates, edits and deletes roles through the same validated operations. Read the current role and configuration revision first; save with that revision and an idempotency key. Only supplied permission properties and type grants change; an empty action list revokes that type grant. Deletion requires confirmation in the assistant. `manage_team` assigns an existing role to a member.

**Link:** `/company/roles`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-company-roles`; rows have no target, so Mate names them. Once the editor is open, `role-modal-save` points at **Save**, disabled on your own role, and `role-modal-delete` at **Delete** where it is offered.

## Tips for agents

- Every section above closes with its route and the ids Mate can use; for a full link, put the route after the origin of the `url` that `search_docs`, `get_docs_page` and `fetch` return. **Subscription Required** and **Legal documents updated** have no target and Mate is not available there, so pass on their link.
- `#nav-company` only expands or collapses the sidebar group; it opens no page itself. The hosted assistant's `navigate` opens every page here that the user's role can open through its sidebar id, and `nav-company` itself opens **Settings**; a toolbar id also works and opens its page. For a page the role cannot open, `navigate` refuses instead of landing on the Dashboard. It can highlight the sidebar entries, toolbar controls, Subscription buttons and plan cards, the Settings currency and Data model link, form buttons, and the dialog fields and buttons listed above. `list_ui_targets` lists targets whatever the user's role or plan, so an **Add** or billing target can be missing on screen.
- Dialog targets (every `invite-modal-*`, `member-modal-*`, `role-modal-*` and `webhook-modal-*` id, and `webhook-delivery-modal-resend`) exist only while their dialog is open. `list_ui_targets` names the prerequisite: `company-members-add` for **Invite User**, `invite-modal-tab-email` for `invite-modal-emails` and `invite-modal-send`, `company-roles-add` for `role-modal-save` and `role-modal-reset`, and `company-webhooks-add` for the webhook fields, `webhook-modal-save` and `webhook-modal-reset`. The **User** dialog, **Event Details**, `role-modal-delete` and `webhook-modal-delete` need a row the user opens, and `list_ui_targets` names that row as the prerequisite (`a member row`, `a delivery row`, `a role row`, `a webhook row`); rows have no target, so ask the user to click it first. Mate cannot click a row, fill a field or submit a dialog.
- Rows never carry ids. Navigate to a page via its URL (for example `/company/webhooks`) and open a row by clicking it. Edits happen in dialogs, not in a drawer.
- Prefer [MCP](/docs/mcp) tools over clicking when a tool exists: `update_workspace_settings` changes currency, `configure_record_model` changes CRM types and calculations, `manage_roles` changes authorized role grants, `manage_team` invites and updates members, and `manage_webhooks` manages webhooks and deliveries.
- No tool reads or changes the subscription or invitation link. For "which plan are we on" or "how do I cancel", send the user to **My Company** › **Subscription**.

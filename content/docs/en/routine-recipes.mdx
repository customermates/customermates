---
title: "Routine recipes for small agencies"
description: "Four prompts to paste into Mate or a routine. Each does the record work and leaves anything that goes to a client as a draft for you to send."
---

Each recipe has the same shape: a trigger, the record work the agent does, and one thing you approve. Nothing goes to a client until you click **Send**. Routines run on Customermates Cloud; see [Routines](/docs/app-routines) for triggers, limits and what a run may do.

## 1. After the client call [#after-the-client-call]

**Where:** a chat with Mate (Cmd/Ctrl+J), right after the call. Paste your notes or attach a photo of them.

```
Add my call notes (text or a photo) to the matching deal, set the next step, and write the follow-up email to the contact. Save it as a draft. Don't send.
```

**You approve:** the follow-up draft in your [Inbox](app:inbox).

## 2. A LinkedIn reply arrives [#a-linkedin-reply-comes-in]

**Where:** [Routines](app:routines) > [Add](app:routines?focus=add) > **When something changes** > the event **Message Received** (`messaging.message.received`). It needs your LinkedIn account under [Channels](app:settings/channels).

```
If this message came in on LinkedIn: add what the person said as a note on their contact, set the next step, and save a short reply as a draft in the thread. Never send.
```

**You approve:** the reply draft in the LinkedIn thread.

## 3. A client orders by message [#a-client-orders-on-whatsapp]

**Where:** [Routines](app:routines) > [Add](app:routines?focus=add) > **When something changes** > the event **Message Received** (`messaging.message.received`). It needs your WhatsApp account under [Channels](app:settings/channels) and a list for projects or orders.

```
If this WhatsApp message asks for new work: create a project linked to the customer with what they asked for and when they need it, and save a reply as a draft. Never send.
```

**You approve:** the new project and the reply draft.

## 4. Monday 08:00 [#monday-0800]

**Where:** [Routines](app:routines) > [Add](app:routines?focus=add) > **On a schedule** > **Every week**, Monday, 08:00.

```
Sum up my open pipeline: total, weighted, biggest deal. List deals with no reply for 14 days and save a short check-in draft for each. Email me the summary.
```

**You approve:** each check-in draft; the summary goes only to you.

## Why drafts [#why-drafts]

Routines may send messages without asking (see [What a routine may do without approval](/docs/app-routines#what-a-routine-may-do-without-approval)). The prompts above say "Never send" on purpose, so every message to a client waits for you.

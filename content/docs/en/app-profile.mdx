Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM. This page documents the **My Profile** screen of the web app for humans and for AI agents that drive the UI.

## What lives on the Profile screen?

Everything that belongs to you as a user rather than to the company: your profile and preferences (**Settings**), the messaging channels you connect (**Channels**) and your API keys for MCP and REST clients (**API & Connectors**). Most changes here affect only you. Channels are the exception: a channel you share becomes visible to your teammates, and disconnecting a shared channel removes it for them too.

In the sidebar group **Workspace**, **My Profile** (`#nav-profile`) only expands or collapses its entries; it opens no page itself. Its entries open the pages, each on its own route, and the breadcrumb reads **My Profile › Settings** with a switcher to the other pages you can see. URLs are locale-prefixed, for example `https://customermates.com/en/profile/settings`.

The Cloud URLs are the English ones; swap `/en/` for the user's app language (`/de/`, `/fr/`, `/it/` or `/es/`). Mate, the hosted assistant, opens a page with `navigate` and outlines a control on the current page with `highlight_element`; it cannot click. The `nav-*` ids in the Navigate column also work with `highlight_element` and point at the sidebar entry; `nav-profile` points at the **My Profile** toggle. A sidebar entry can be highlighted only while **My Profile** is expanded. It is expanded by default on its own pages, unless the member collapses it. On a phone the sidebar must be open, and a sidebar collapsed to icons hides the entries. A control without a target is named by Mate instead.

**Settings** is listed for every member. **Channels** is listed on Customermates Cloud only, when your role's **Inbox messages** row has **Read access** **All**; on self-hosted its targets are still offered, but the page redirects to the Dashboard. **API & Connectors** is listed when the **API & Webhooks** row has **Read access** **All**.

Roles are edited under **My Company › Roles** by a member whose role has **Manage** on **Users & Roles**; see [App guide, Company](/docs/app-company). A new custom role starts with **Read access** **None** on both rows, so its members see only **Settings** until someone changes the role. The **Admin** role sees every entry the installation offers. Opening a hidden page by its URL brings you back to the Dashboard.

| Page | Route and Cloud URL | Navigate target | Highlight targets on the page | Explained in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Settings** | `/profile/settings`, `https://customermates.com/en/profile/settings` | `nav-profile-settings`, also `nav-profile` | None | [Settings](#settings-tab) |
| **Channels** | `/profile/connected-accounts`, `https://customermates.com/en/profile/connected-accounts` | `nav-profile-connected-accounts`, also `profile-connected-accounts-connect` | `profile-connected-accounts-connect` | [Channels](#channels-tab) |
| **API & Connectors** | `/profile/api-keys`, `https://customermates.com/en/profile/api-keys` | `nav-profile-api-keys` | None | [API & Connectors](#api--connectors-tab) |

## Settings tab

Route `/profile/settings`, sidebar entry **Settings**. Every signed-in member can open it, whatever the role, and the form changes only your own profile. Save or discard with **Save** (`#profile-settings-save`) and **Reset** (`#profile-settings-reset`) below the form; both buttons appear once the form has unsaved changes. **Save** stores all fields at once.

**Link:** the **Settings** page, `/profile/settings`, Cloud URL `https://customermates.com/en/profile/settings`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-profile-settings`; **Save** and **Reset** are element ids, not highlight targets, so Mate names them.

### How do I change my name, avatar, language or theme?

Change the field and click **Save**.

| Field | Options and rules | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **First Name**, **Last Name** | Required, up to 255 characters each | Your name wherever you appear in the workspace |
| **Country** | Required, chosen from the country list | Stored with your profile; it does not change how dates or numbers are written |
| **Avatar URL** | Optional link to a publicly reachable image; `https://` is added when you type an address without it; there is no upload | Your picture; with the field empty, your initials are shown |
| **Display Language** | **English**, **German**, **French**, **Italian**, **Spanish**, or **System** (your browser's language, English when it is none of the five) | The interface language, which the assistant also answers in. Saving a new value reloads the app in that language. Notification emails from Customermates, such as trial reminders, use it too; with **System** they are sent in English |
| **Formatting Locale** | **English** (US formats), **German**, **French**, **Italian**, **Spanish**, or **System** (the formats of your display language) | How numbers, dates, times and amounts are written, independent of the interface language |
| **Theme** | **System** (follows your operating system), **Dark**, **Light** | Light or dark interface |

The header above the form shows your name, your email address, **Email verified** or **Email not verified**, your status and your role. None of these can be changed here. The email address cannot be changed anywhere in the app. Role and status are set under **My Company › Members** by a member whose role has **Manage** on **Users & Roles**, who can also edit your first name, last name, country and avatar URL there.

The avatar menu at the bottom of the sidebar has a **Dark Mode** or **Light Mode** switch that also saves your theme, and **Sign Out**.

**Link:** `/profile/settings`, Cloud URL `https://customermates.com/en/profile/settings`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-profile-settings`; the fields and the avatar menu are not highlight targets, so Mate names them.

### How do I verify my email address?

While your address is unverified, the sidebar shows **Email not verified** under the brand name; it opens this page, where the top bar offers **Resend verification email**. A new account can be used for 24 hours without verification. After that, every page redirects to the verification screen until you confirm the address with the link from the email.

**Link:** `/profile/settings`, Cloud URL `https://customermates.com/en/profile/settings`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-profile-settings`; **Resend verification email** and the **Email not verified** badge have no id, so Mate names them.

### What can I not change here?

There is no setting for your password, email address, time zone, notifications or deleting your account. To set a new password, sign out and use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. Email signatures belong to each email account on the Channels page, see Email appearance and signature below.

In the assistant and over MCP, `update_workspace_settings` with `target: "profile"` changes **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Country** and **Avatar URL** only. **Display Language**, **Formatting Locale** and **Theme** are set in the app only. The REST API reads your profile with `GET /v1/users/me` but cannot change it.

**Link:** `/profile/settings`, Cloud URL `https://customermates.com/en/profile/settings`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-profile-settings`; **Sign Out** and the sign-in page with **Forgot password?** have no target, so Mate names them.

## API & Connectors tab [#api--connectors-tab]

Route `/profile/api-keys`, sidebar entry **API & Connectors**. Keys for MCP and REST clients; the full rules are on [API keys](/docs/api-keys). The page lists only your own keys. Opening it needs **Read access** **All** on your role's **API & Webhooks** row, and adding or deleting keys needs **Manage** **Yes** on that row. The same row also controls **My Company › Webhooks** and **Recent Deliveries**.

| Action | Where | Anchor id | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| Add a key | Top bar | `#profile-api-keys-generate` (**Add**) | Opens **Add an API key** with two routes |
| Create a standard key | **Standard API key** | `#api-key-save` (**Save**) | **Name** plus an optional **Expires in** date 1 to 365 days ahead (default **Never expires**); the key is shown once, so copy it immediately |
| Quick connections | **Quick connections** | none | Four client tiles, described below |
| Open a key | Key card | none | The card shows **Created at**, **Expires at** and **Last used**; the dialog shows the creation date, and expiry and last use only when set |
| Delete a key | Key dialog | none | **Delete**, with a confirmation; the key stops working immediately |

The quick route offers four tiles: **Claude**, **ChatGPT & Codex**, **Cursor** and **Gemini**. The local setups, meaning Claude Code or Claude Desktop, Codex, Cursor and Gemini, create a key named after the client that expires after 365 days, and show that client's setup snippet with the key filled in. The Claude account and ChatGPT app methods connect through the hosted connector instead and create no key.

There is no rotate or revoke button: create a new key, update the client, then delete the old one. Taking **API & Webhooks** away from a role does not disable keys its members already hold; to stop a member's keys, set that member **Inactive** under **My Company › Members**.

**Link:** the **API & Connectors** page, `/profile/api-keys`, Cloud URL `https://customermates.com/en/profile/api-keys`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-profile-api-keys`; **Add**, **Save**, the key cards and the dialog buttons are not highlight targets, so Mate names them.

## Channels tab

Route `/profile/connected-accounts`, sidebar entry **Channels** (`#nav-profile-connected-accounts`), Customermates Cloud only; you connect a channel with **Connect channel** (`#profile-connected-accounts-connect`). Channels are the email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram accounts that feed the [Inbox](/docs/app-inbox). The page lists your own channels first, then every channel a teammate has shared with the workspace. When channels need attention (**Reconnect needed**, **Permission issue** or **Error**), the sidebar entry shows their count, including teammates' shared channels that only their owner can fix.

| Action | Where | Anchor id | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| Connect a channel | Top bar | `#profile-connected-accounts-connect` (**Connect channel**) | Opens the channel picker; on narrow screens the button shows channel icons only |
| Connect from an empty page | Centered empty state | `#profile-connected-accounts-connect-empty` (**Connect channel**) | The same picker, shown when you have no channel yet and may connect; when the assistant is available, the empty state shows assistant starter prompts instead of this button |
| Open a channel | Channel card click | none | Opens the channel dialog on its **Details** tab |
| Details tab | Channel dialog | `#connected-account-tab-details` (**Details**) | **Provider**, **Status**, **Visibility**, **Owner**, **Folders**, **Connected** and **Last synced** |
| Share or unshare | Details tab | `#connected-account-visibility` | The **Visibility** switch between **Private** and **Shared**; owner only |
| Email tab | Channel dialog | `#connected-account-tab-email` (**Email**) | Appearance and signature; only on a Gmail, Outlook or IMAP account you own |
| Folders tab | Channel dialog | `#connected-account-tab-folders` (**Folders** with a count, for example **Folders 4/6**) | One switch per folder; only when the account reports folders |

**Link:** the **Channels** page, `/profile/connected-accounts`, Cloud URL `https://customermates.com/en/profile/connected-accounts`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-profile-connected-accounts`, and `highlight_element` with `profile-connected-accounts-connect` for **Connect channel**, which is on screen only when your role may connect and the page is not locked, loading or failed; the other element ids above and the cards are not highlight targets, so Mate names them.

### Who can use Channels?

Seeing the page needs **Read access** **All** on your role's **Inbox messages** row; with **None**, both **Channels** and the Inbox are hidden. Connecting a channel and changing, reactivating, resyncing or disconnecting it needs **Manage** **Yes** on the same row. With read access alone you see your channels and teammates' shared ones but cannot change anything. Whatever your role, including **Admin**, you can act only on channels you own.

The workspace plan must include messaging: Pro, Business, Enterprise, or a trial. On Starter the page shows **Upgrade to unlock messaging** with the button **Upgrade your plan**, which opens **My Company › Subscription**. Every action except disconnecting also needs a usable subscription, meaning status **Active** or a trial that has not ended; otherwise it is refused, and **Connect channel** shows "Connecting channels requires an active subscription or a trial that hasn't expired." Self-hosted installs have no Channels page.

**Link:** `/profile/connected-accounts`, Cloud URL `https://customermates.com/en/profile/connected-accounts`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-profile-connected-accounts`; on Starter the locked page has no **Connect channel** target, and **Upgrade your plan** has no id, so Mate names it or navigates with `nav-company-subscription`.

### How do I connect a channel?

Open **My Profile › Channels** (`#nav-profile-connected-accounts`), click **Connect channel** (`#profile-connected-accounts-connect`) and pick one of nine entries, for example **WhatsApp** for your WhatsApp chats in the Inbox:

| Entry | Connects |
|---|---|
| **Gmail**, **Outlook** | A Gmail or Outlook account; the card's **Provider** shows whether email, calendar or both are connected |
| **IMAP** | Any other email account over IMAP and SMTP |
| **WhatsApp** | Your WhatsApp account; you scan a QR code on the connection page |
| **LinkedIn (Classic)** | Your LinkedIn account |
| **LinkedIn (Classic & Sales Navigator)**, **LinkedIn (Classic & Recruiter)** | Your LinkedIn account together with Sales Navigator or Recruiter |
| **Instagram**, **Telegram** | Your Instagram or Telegram account |

Picking an entry sends the current browser tab to a secure connection page, not a popup. The link is valid for 30 minutes. When you finish there, you return to **Channels** and a message reports the result:

- **Channel connected**: your history imports in the background, and the card shows **Syncing in background** meanwhile. This usually takes a few minutes, for large accounts up to an hour.
- **Account already connected**: this account is already connected by a member of your workspace. If that is someone else, ask them to share it with the workspace instead of connecting it twice.
- **Invalid credentials**, **Link expired**, **Verification failed**, **Account disconnected** or **Channel connection failed**: start again from **Connect channel**.

**Link:** `/profile/connected-accounts`, Cloud URL `https://customermates.com/en/profile/connected-accounts`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-profile-connected-accounts`, then `highlight_element` with `profile-connected-accounts-connect`; the nine menu entries have no id, so Mate names them.

### How many channels can I connect?

Channels per member: Starter [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedAccountsPerUser]], Pro [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]], Business [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedAccountsPerUser]], Enterprise [[commercial.entitlement.enterprise.includedAccountsPerUser]]. A trial counts as Pro. Every channel you own counts, whatever its status, until you disconnect it; only channels with status **Deleted** do not count. Teammates' shared channels never use your allowance.

At the limit on Pro, **Connect channel** opens **Upgrade to connect more accounts** with **View plans**, which leads to **My Company › Subscription**. On Business, an error message points to Enterprise. After a downgrade, each member's newest channels above the new allowance are removed and marked **Deleted**, and members with the **Admin** role get an email.

**Link:** `/profile/connected-accounts`, Cloud URL `https://customermates.com/en/profile/connected-accounts`, and `/company/subscription` for **View plans**, Cloud URL `https://customermates.com/en/company/subscription`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-profile-connected-accounts`, then `highlight_element` with `profile-connected-accounts-connect`, or `nav-company-subscription` for the plans; **View plans** and the upgrade dialog have no id, so Mate names them.

### What does each status mean?

| Status | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Connected** | Working normally | Nothing; **Resync** re-imports the history |
| **Connecting** | The connection is still being set up | Wait; **Resync** is available |
| **Reconnect needed** | The provider reports that the login no longer works or is incomplete | The owner clicks **Reactivate** |
| **Permission issue** | Shown when the provider reports missing permissions | The owner clicks **Reactivate** |
| **Error** | The provider reports an error | The owner clicks **Reactivate** |
| **Stopped** | The provider has paused the account | The owner clicks **Reactivate** |
| **Deleted** | The account was removed at the provider, or automatically: after a plan downgrade, or by the daily clean-up once a trial has been over for a day, once a paid subscription has been **Unpaid** or **Expired** for three days, or after the owner was set **Inactive** | The card stays listed, and the owner is offered only **Disconnect**; connect the account again if the plan allows |

**Link:** `/profile/connected-accounts`, Cloud URL `https://customermates.com/en/profile/connected-accounts`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-profile-connected-accounts`; the status chips, the cards, **Resync**, **Reactivate** and **Disconnect** have no id, so Mate names them.

### Private or shared?

A new channel is **Private**: only you see its conversations. The owner switches **Visibility** on the **Details** tab to **Shared**, and the change applies at once with the message **Account shared with your workspace**. A shared channel, with all its conversations, becomes visible to every member whose role has **Inbox messages** read access, and those with **Manage** on that row can also reply and send from it. Switching back to **Private** hides it again.

Sharing needs the Business or Enterprise plan; on other plans the switch is disabled with the hint "Shared accounts require the Business plan or higher." It also needs **Manage** on **Inbox messages** and a usable subscription. Teammates see your shared channel on their Channels page, where they can open its details and folders but cannot change, reactivate, resync or disconnect it. Single conversations can also be shared from the Inbox, see [App guide, Inbox](/docs/app-inbox).

**Link:** `/profile/connected-accounts`, Cloud URL `https://customermates.com/en/profile/connected-accounts`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-profile-connected-accounts`; the channel card and the **Visibility** switch (`#connected-account-visibility`) are not highlight targets, so Mate names them.

### What does the Folders tab control?

Email accounts whose provider reports folders get a **Folders** tab with one switch per folder. A switch decides whether that folder's messages show in the Inbox, for you and for everyone who can see the channel; switching a folder off hides its messages without deleting them. The change applies at once.

By default every folder is on except trash, junk or spam, drafts and the all-mail folder; when the provider does not label its folders, names containing junk, spam, trash, deleted, draft or outbox are treated the same way. Switching on one of the excluded folders starts a background import of its history. Only the owner with **Manage** on **Inbox messages** can switch folders; everyone else sees **Synced** or **Hidden**.

**Link:** `/profile/connected-accounts`, Cloud URL `https://customermates.com/en/profile/connected-accounts`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-profile-connected-accounts`; the **Folders** tab (`#connected-account-tab-folders`) and its switches are not highlight targets, so Mate names them.

### Email appearance and signature

The **Email** tab sets how messages sent from that account look. It appears only on a Gmail, Outlook or IMAP account you own, editing needs **Manage** on **Inbox messages**, and **Save email settings** stores both sections.

| Field | Options | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Font family** | **Sans serif (Arial)**, **Serif (Georgia)**, **Monospace (Courier New)** | Sans serif (Arial) |
| **Font size** | 10 to 20 | 13 |
| **Link colour** | A six-digit hex value (`#RRGGBB`) with a colour picker beside it | `#7161e8` |
| **Link style** | **Underlined**, **No underline** | Underlined |

The link colour is checked as you type, and a warning appears when its contrast is below 3:1 on a light or a dark background; it does not block saving. Appearance applies to emails written as plain text or Markdown, which includes everything you write in the Inbox. An email sent over the REST API or MCP with an explicit HTML body keeps its own styling.

The signature is off for a new account; switch on **Add a signature** to edit it. Choose a **Layout** of **Text only**, **Logo above** (preselected) or **Logo beside**. Logo layouts need a **Logo URL**, a public HTTPS image address of up to 500 characters; it starts out as the Customermates icon, so replace it with your own logo. **Layout options** for logo layouts set **Logo size** (**Small**, **Medium**, **Large**; default Medium), **Divider** (**None**, **Thin line**; default None) and **Spacing** (**Compact**, **Comfortable**; default Comfortable). **Signature content** is one Markdown block of up to 2,000 characters. Switching the signature off keeps the saved text for later, and the **Preview** shows the result.

While **Add a signature** is on, the saved signature is added below every email sent from that account, whether it is sent from the Inbox, the REST API or an MCP tool, HTML bodies included, so agents must not repeat the signature in the message body.

**Link:** `/profile/connected-accounts`, Cloud URL `https://customermates.com/en/profile/connected-accounts`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-profile-connected-accounts`; the **Email** tab (`#connected-account-tab-email`), its fields and **Save email settings** are not highlight targets, so Mate names them.

### How do I reactivate, resync or disconnect a channel?

Open the channel from its card. The actions sit in the channel dialog and appear only for the owner:

- **Reactivate**, for **Reconnect needed**, **Permission issue**, **Error** or **Stopped**: sends the tab to the connection page for the same account (valid for 30 minutes) and brings you back to **Channels**.
- **Resync**, for **Connected** or **Connecting**: re-imports the history in the background, which usually takes 1 to 3 minutes.
- **Disconnect**: asks for confirmation, then removes the account at the provider and permanently deletes the conversations, messages, calendars and events Customermates imported from it. A shared channel disappears for your teammates as well.

All three need **Manage** on **Inbox messages**.

**Link:** `/profile/connected-accounts`, Cloud URL `https://customermates.com/en/profile/connected-accounts`. **Mate:** `navigate` and `highlight_element` with `nav-profile-connected-accounts`; **Reactivate**, **Resync** and **Disconnect** have no id, so Mate names them.

## Tips for agents

- The hosted assistant Mate has five targets here, `#nav-profile`, `#nav-profile-settings`, `#nav-profile-connected-accounts`, `#nav-profile-api-keys` and `#profile-connected-accounts-connect`, passed without `#`. `navigate` takes all five (`nav-profile` opens **Settings**, the Connect target opens **Channels**) and `highlight_element` outlines them; it cannot press them. Ask it for a tour of connecting an account and it walks you there. The save buttons, the **Add** button on **API & Connectors**, the empty-state button, the channel dialog tabs and the **Visibility** switch are element ids only, not assistant targets.
- Cards never carry ids. Open a key or a channel by clicking its card; the pages are plain routes.
- Prefer MCP tools when a tool exists: `connect_messaging_account` creates the connection link for one of the nine channel entries, under the same plan, permission and allowance rules, and `get_workspace_context` lists your own and shared channels with their status, and returns an empty list when your role has no read access on **Inbox messages**. No tool changes visibility, folders, appearance or the signature, reactivates, resyncs or disconnects a channel, changes the display language, formatting locale or theme, or creates an API key; use the pages above. The REST endpoint `GET /v1/messaging/connected-accounts` only reads.
- A generated API key is shown exactly once, whether created as a standard key or through a quick connection's local setup. Copy it in the same step.

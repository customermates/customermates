## What belongs to you rather than the workspace? [#what-lives-on-the-profile-screen]

The Account part of Settings holds three pages: [Profile & preferences](app:settings/profile) (name, avatar, language, formats, theme), [Channels](app:settings/channels) (the accounts that feed the [Inbox](/docs/app-inbox)) and [API keys](app:settings/api-keys) (keys for MCP and REST clients). Click your avatar at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Profile & preferences**, or choose **Settings** in the workspace menu (**Customermates** at the top of the sidebar) or press G then S; each opens Profile & preferences, and the settings nav lists the other pages. **Back** at the top of that nav returns to where you were. Changes here affect only you, except a channel you share with the workspace.

Every member sees Profile & preferences. Channels needs **Read access** **All** on your role's **Inbox messages** row and exists on Customermates Cloud only. API keys needs **Read access** **All** on **API & Webhooks**. A new custom role starts with **None** on both rows; roles are edited in [Roles](app:settings/roles), see [App guide, Company](/docs/app-company).

## Profile & preferences [#settings-tab]

Change any field in [Profile & preferences](app:settings/profile) and click **Save**, which stores the whole form. **Save** and **Reset** appear once something changed.

### How do I change my name, avatar, language or theme? [#how-do-i-change-my-name-avatar-language-or-theme]

| Field | Rules and effect |
|---|---|
| **First Name**, **Last Name** | Required, up to 255 characters; your name across the workspace |
| **Country** | Required; stored with your profile, it does not change formats |
| **Avatar URL** | Optional link to a public image (no upload; `https://` is added); empty shows your initials |
| [Display Language](app:settings/profile?focus=display-language) | **English**, **German**, **French**, **Italian**, **Spanish** or **System** (browser language, else English). The interface and Mate use it, and so do notification emails (English with **System**). Saving reloads the app |
| [Formatting Locale](app:settings/profile?focus=formatting-locale) | The same choices; how numbers, dates and amounts are written, independent of the display language. **System** follows the display language |
| [Theme](app:settings/profile?focus=theme) | **System**, **Dark** or **Light** |

**Theme** and **Language** can also be switched in the personal menu. The header above the form shows your email, verification status, member status and role; these are read-only here. Role and status, and your name, country and avatar, can also be set by a member with **Edit** on **Users & Roles** in [Members](app:settings/members).

### How do I turn off single-key shortcuts? [#keyboard-shortcut-preferences]

Switch off **Single-key shortcuts** in the **Keyboard shortcuts** dialog (press ? or choose **Keyboard shortcuts** in the personal menu). It disables C, /, V, ? and the G sequences, which helps with speech input or accidental key presses. Shortcuts with Cmd/Ctrl and Esc keep working. The full list is in [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/app-dashboard#keyboard-shortcuts).

### How do I verify my email address or resend the verification email? [#how-do-i-verify-my-email-address]

While your address is unverified, your avatar in the sidebar and the **Profile & preferences** entry in the personal menu show **Email not verified**; Profile & preferences then offers [Resend verification email](app:settings/profile?focus=verify-email). A new account works for 24 hours without verification; after that every page leads to the verification screen until you confirm the link in the email.

### What can I not change here, such as my password? [#what-can-i-not-change-here]

There is no setting for password, email address, time zone, notifications or deleting your account. For a new password, sign out and use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. Email signatures belong to each email channel, see [email appearance and signature](#email-appearance-and-signature).

## API keys [#api--connectors-tab]

[API keys](app:settings/api-keys) lists only your own keys. Click [Add](app:settings/api-keys?focus=generate) (needs **Create** on **API & Webhooks**) to open **Add an API key**:

- **Standard API key**: a **Name** and an optional **Expires in** date 1 to 365 days ahead (default **Never expires**). The key is shown once, so copy it immediately.
- **Quick connections**: tiles for **Claude**, **ChatGPT & Codex**, **Cursor** and **Gemini**. Local setups (Claude Code or Desktop, Codex, Cursor, Gemini) create a key named after the client that expires after 365 days and show the setup snippet with the key filled in. The Claude account and ChatGPT app methods use the hosted connector and create no key.

A key card shows **Created at**, **Expires at** and **Last used**; open it to **Delete** the key (needs **Delete**), which stops it at once. To rotate, create a new key, update the client, then delete the old one. Removing **API & Webhooks** from a role does not disable existing keys; set the member **Inactive** in [Members](app:settings/members) instead. Full rules: [API keys](/docs/api-keys).

## Channels [#channels-tab]

[Channels](app:settings/channels) lists your email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram accounts first, then every channel a teammate shared. When channels show **Reconnect needed**, **Permission issue** or **Error**, the Channels entry shows their count. Clicking a card opens the channel dialog with the tabs **Details** (provider, status, **Visibility**, owner, folders, connected and last synced), **Email** and **Folders**. Whatever your role, you can change only channels you own.

### Who can use Channels? [#who-can-use-channels]

Seeing the page needs **Read access** **All** on **Inbox messages**; connecting needs **Create**, changing, reactivating or resyncing **Edit**, disconnecting **Delete**. The plan must include messaging (Pro, Business, Enterprise or a trial); on Starter the page shows **Upgrade your plan**, which opens [Billing](app:settings/billing). Every action except disconnecting also needs an active subscription or a running trial. Self-hosted installs have no Channels page.

### How do I connect a channel? [#how-do-i-connect-a-channel]

Click [Connect channel](app:settings/channels?focus=connect) and pick an entry:

| Entry | Connects |
|---|---|
| **Gmail**, **Outlook** | Email, calendar or both; the card's **Provider** shows which |
| **IMAP** | Any other email account over IMAP and SMTP |
| **WhatsApp** | Your WhatsApp account, by QR code |
| **LinkedIn (Classic)**, **LinkedIn (Classic & Sales Navigator)**, **LinkedIn (Classic & Recruiter)** | Your LinkedIn account, optionally with Sales Navigator or Recruiter |
| **Instagram**, **Telegram** | Your Instagram or Telegram account |

The current tab goes to a secure connection page (30 minutes to finish) and then back to Channels with the result. **Channel connected** starts the history import in the background (**Syncing in background**, usually minutes, up to an hour for large accounts). **Account already connected** means a workspace member already connected it; ask them to share it. On any other error, start again from **Connect channel**.

### How many channels can I connect? [#how-many-channels-can-i-connect]

Per member: Starter [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedAccountsPerUser]], Pro [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]], Business [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedAccountsPerUser]], Enterprise [[commercial.entitlement.enterprise.includedAccountsPerUser]]; a trial counts as Pro. Every channel you own counts until you disconnect it, except **Deleted** ones; teammates' shared channels never count. At the limit on Pro, **Connect channel** offers **View plans**, which opens [Billing](app:settings/billing). After a downgrade, each member's newest channels above the allowance become **Deleted** and members with the **Admin** role get an email.

### What does each channel status mean? [#what-does-each-status-mean]

| Status | Meaning and action |
|---|---|
| **Connected** | Works; **Resync** re-imports the history |
| **Connecting** | Still being set up; **Resync** is available |
| **Reconnect needed**, **Permission issue**, **Error**, **Stopped** | The provider reports a login, permission or other problem, or paused the account; the owner clicks **Reactivate** |
| **Deleted** | Removed at the provider or automatically (after a downgrade, a day after a trial ended, three days after a paid subscription became **Unpaid** or **Expired**, or when the owner was set **Inactive**); only **Disconnect** remains |

### How do I share a channel with teammates or keep it private? [#private-or-shared]

A new channel is **Private**. The owner switches **Visibility** on the **Details** tab to **Shared**; it then shows, with all conversations, to every member with read access on **Inbox messages**, and members with **Create** can reply from it. Sharing needs the Business or Enterprise plan, **Edit** on **Inbox messages** and an active subscription. Teammates can open a shared channel's details and folders but not change it. Single conversations can be shared from the [Inbox](/docs/app-inbox).

### What does the Folders tab control? [#what-does-the-folders-tab-control]

For email accounts that report folders, the **Folders** tab has one switch per folder: off hides that folder's messages in the Inbox for everyone who sees the channel, without deleting them. By default all folders are on except trash, junk or spam, drafts and all mail. Switching one of those on imports its history. Only the owner with **Edit** can switch; others see **Synced** or **Hidden**.

### How do I change the font, link colour and signature of outgoing emails? [#email-appearance-and-signature]

The **Email** tab appears on Gmail, Outlook and IMAP accounts you own (editing needs **Edit**) and is stored with **Save email settings**.

| Field | Options (default) |
|---|---|
| **Font family** | **Sans serif (Arial)**, **Serif (Georgia)**, **Monospace (Courier New)** (Sans serif) |
| **Font size** | 10 to 20 (13) |
| **Link colour** | Hex value with colour picker (`#7161e8`); a warning shows below 3:1 contrast |
| **Link style** | **Underlined**, **No underline** (Underlined) |

Appearance applies to plain text and Markdown emails, which includes everything written in the Inbox; an explicit HTML body keeps its styling. Switch on **Add a signature** to edit it: **Layout** **Text only**, **Logo above** (default) or **Logo beside**; logo layouts need a public HTTPS **Logo URL** (replace the Customermates icon) and offer **Logo size**, **Divider** and **Spacing**. **Signature content** is Markdown up to 2,000 characters, with a **Preview**. While on, the signature is added below every email from that account, from the Inbox, REST or MCP, HTML included.

### How do I reactivate, resync or disconnect a channel? [#how-do-i-reactivate-resync-or-disconnect-a-channel]

Open a channel you own:

- **Reactivate** (for **Reconnect needed**, **Permission issue**, **Error**, **Stopped**): goes to the connection page for the same account and back.
- **Resync** (for **Connected**, **Connecting**): re-imports the history, usually in 1 to 3 minutes.
- **Disconnect**: after confirmation, removes the account and permanently deletes the conversations, messages, calendars and events imported from it, for teammates too if it was shared.

**Reactivate** and **Resync** need **Edit** on **Inbox messages**, **Disconnect** needs **Delete**.

## For agents [#tips-for-agents]

Over MCP and in Mate, `update_workspace_settings` with `target: "profile"` changes only first name, last name, country and avatar URL; `connect_messaging_account` creates a connection link under the same plan, permission and allowance rules. Language, formats, theme, shortcuts, channel visibility, folders, appearance, signature, reactivate, resync, disconnect and API keys are set in the app only. Do not repeat a channel's signature in a message body. A new API key is shown once.

Global search opens over the page you are on, from anywhere in the app: press Cmd/Ctrl+K, press / when no field has focus, or click **Search** in the sidebar.

## What does global search find, and which contact fields does it match? [#what-does-global-search-find]

Search looks through records in every list you can read, including lists your workspace created, and groups the hits by list. It matches record titles and searchable field values, including email fields, and finds contacts by any email address or phone number in their **Channels**. Values you are not allowed to see are not searched. If your role reads a list only with **Assigned**, you only get records you are assigned to. **Load more** fetches further hits.

Before you type, **Recently opened** shows your last 8 picks from search, stored in this browser only; **Clear** empties it. The same box also offers pages to go to, **Add** to create a record and, on a list, its saved views. Arrow keys move through the hits and Enter opens one. Selecting a record opens it in the details drawer.

Messages are not searched here; the [Inbox](app:inbox) has its own search. For a filtered or sorted list of records, use a list instead, for example [Contacts](app:records/contact) with [Filters](app:records/contact?focus=filter). To add a record, see [CRM records](/docs/app-records#how-do-i-add-a-record).

## For agents [#which-stable-ids-does-it-have]

Mate can point at **Search** in the sidebar but cannot open the search box or type into it. For filtered, sorted or aggregated lookups, use the record query tools instead of global search.

## Frequently asked questions [#frequently-asked-questions]

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="What is the keyboard shortcut?">
Cmd+K on a Mac, Ctrl+K on Windows and Linux, from anywhere in the app, or / when no text field has focus. Type a question and press Tab to hand it to [Mate](/docs/app-assistant) instead. **Search** in the sidebar shows the key for your computer and opens the same search. All shortcuts are listed in [Which keyboard shortcuts are there?](/docs/app-dashboard#keyboard-shortcuts).
</FaqItem>
<FaqItem question="Where do results open?">
In the record's details drawer, over the page you were on. From there you can open the full record page, described in [CRM records](/docs/app-records#what-is-the-shared-layout-of-a-record-type).
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

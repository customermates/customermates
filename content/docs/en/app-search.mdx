Global search is a modal, not a page: it opens from anywhere in the app with **Cmd/Ctrl+K** or by activating `#nav-search` in the sidebar. This page documents it for people and for AI agents that drive the UI.

## What does global search find, and which contact fields does it match? [#what-does-global-search-find]

Global search returns a bounded page of accessible records across configured types, including custom types. You can search contacts by email address or phone number through their identity channels. Search also matches permitted titles and searchable field values, including configured email fields. Use **Load more** when additional results are available. Restricted values do not participate in search.

You only see the record types your role can read, and **Assigned** read access limits the hits to records you are assigned to. Before you type, **Recently opened** shows your last 8 picks from this search, kept in this browser only; **Clear** empties it. Arrow keys move through the hits and Enter opens one.

Messages are not searched; the Inbox has its own search.

Selecting a result opens the record's detail page at `/records/<typeId>/<recordId>`. To add a record instead, use the **Add** control on its list page or the sidebar type picker documented in [CRM Records](/docs/app-records).

**Link:** no page; open the modal on any app page with Cmd+K or Ctrl+K, or with **Search** in the sidebar. **Mate:** `highlight_element` with `nav-search` (it cannot `navigate` there or open the modal); the search field and the results are not highlight targets, so Mate names them.

## Which stable ids does global search have? [#which-stable-ids-does-it-have]

| Control | Id |
| --- | --- |
| Sidebar trigger | `#nav-search` |
| Search field wrapper inside the modal | `#global-search-input` |

For an agent, typing happens in the text input inside `#global-search-input` after opening the modal; results are regular options selected by their visible name. For filtered or sorted lookups use a list page or `query_crm_records`; use `query_crm_measure` for aggregates. The hosted assistant can highlight `#nav-search` but cannot open the modal or type into it.

**Link:** no page; open the modal anywhere in the app with Cmd+K or Ctrl+K. **Mate:** `highlight_element` with `nav-search`; `#global-search-input` is not a highlight target, so Mate names the search field.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="What is the keyboard shortcut?">
Cmd+K or Ctrl+K, on any operating system, from anywhere in the app. The sidebar trigger **Search**, which shows ⌘K, opens the same modal.
</FaqItem>
<FaqItem question="Where do results open?">
On the record's detail page at `/records/<typeId>/<recordId>`, documented in CRM Records.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

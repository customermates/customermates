## How do I combine dates from different lists? [#combine-date-sources]

Open [Calendar](app:calendar) and choose **Add date source**. Pick a list and a date, date-time or range field. A separate **End date** can extend a single date field into a span; both fields must have the same type. Choose a source color or a choice field's option colors. Add another source for a different list or another date in the same list. Each source uses the existing list filter palette and can have its own search and filters. Up to 12 sources can be combined.

The checkboxes show or hide each source immediately. **Source settings** changes dates and filters; **Open in list** opens that list with the source's filters. Click an event to open its record in the normal record drawer. Calendar entries use your current record and field permissions. Missing or restricted dates are not placed on the calendar. If a field or filter becomes unavailable, the source is marked unavailable; its filters are not silently discarded.

Use **Save** to keep your personal sources, enabled calendars, time zone, display mode and sort order. The existing unsaved-change bar lets you save or revert them. Changing your calendar does not change a list's saved view.

## What do the different views show? [#calendar-views]

**Month** shows date spans with a list of overflow events for busy days. **Week** shows all-day records above an hourly schedule for timed records and connected events. **Quarter** shows three months together. **Timeline** places each record on its own row across the quarter, with a horizontally scrollable day scale. It shows date ranges without dependencies or automatic scheduling. On phones the calendar uses **Agenda**. Search narrows the entries on screen; source filters also apply to exports. Sorting affects the agenda and timeline.

A date-only value stays on its calendar date. Date-times use the selected time zone. Drag a CRM entry to another day in the month or quarter, or use Space, the arrow keys and Space again; its duration is preserved. Only writable input dates can move, and saves use the normal record version and permission checks. Open the record to change its time or duration.

Each source displays up to 500 records in the requested period, and connected calendars up to 2,000 events. A notice explains when results are incomplete. Choose a shorter period or narrower source filters before exporting an incomplete result.

## Which meetings appear in my calendar? [#connected-calendar-events]

Where connected messaging is available, **My connected calendars** lists your Google and Outlook calendars, including shared schedules under their usual sharing rules. Check the calendars to include. These meetings are read-only here; their detail opens the connected calendar for editing. Cancelled events are hidden. Imported recurring instances are shown, and recurring series rules are expanded for the displayed period, including time zones and exclusions. Invalid or unsupported recurrence rules are reported as incomplete results.

Connected calendars require the existing messaging entitlement and inbox read permission. Self-hosted workspaces can use record dates, every calendar layout, exports and subscriptions without a calendar provider.

## How do I use this calendar in another app? [#calendar-export-and-subscription]

**Export & subscribe → Download .ics** downloads a snapshot of the displayed period and enabled sources. Import it into Apple Calendar, Outlook, Google Calendar or another iCalendar-compatible app. A snapshot does not refresh. Connected events are excluded by default to avoid duplicates; you can explicitly include selected connected events.

**Create subscription URL** creates a private read-only feed for the enabled sources, source filters and time zone at that moment. Paste the URL into your calendar app's “subscribe by URL” option. It covers the previous 30 days and next 335 days; your calendar app decides the refresh interval. A later source or filter change needs a new subscription URL. Up to ten subscriptions can be active.

Copy the URL when it is created: it is shown only once. Anyone with it can read the selected events while you retain access. Every refresh checks your active membership and current permissions. **Revoke subscription** immediately stops future fetches; calendar apps may keep previously downloaded events. Changes flow from Customermates to the calendar app. Editing an imported event does not update its CRM record; this is not a two-way calendar connection.

## How do I combine dates from different lists? [#combine-date-sources]

Open [Calendar](app:calendar) and choose **Add date source**. Pick a list and a date, date-time or range field. A separate **End date** can extend a single date field into a span; both fields must have the same type. Choose a source color or a choice field's option colors. Add another source for a different list or another date in the same list. Each source has its own **Filters**, set with the same filter palette as a list. Up to 12 sources can be combined.

**Date sources** in the top bar lists your sources and connected calendars. The checkboxes show or hide each source immediately. A source's menu opens **Date source settings** to change its dates, color and filters, **Open in list** to open that list with the source's filters, and **Remove date source**. Click an event to open its record in the normal record drawer. Calendar entries use your current record and field permissions. Missing or restricted dates are not placed on the calendar. If a field or filter becomes unavailable, the source is marked unavailable; its filters are not silently discarded.

Until you change anything, the calendar shows a default: the first single date field of each list, or its first date range when it has none, up to 12 lists, in the list's color. Your sources, enabled calendars, time zone and display mode are saved automatically and only for you, from your first change on. Changing your calendar does not change a list's saved view.

## What do the different views show? [#calendar-views]

**Month** shows date spans with a list of overflow events for busy days. **Week** shows all-day entries and entries lasting a day or longer above an hourly schedule for shorter timed records and connected events. **Quarter** fits the three months of a quarter on one screen in week columns. **Timeline** shows the same quarter day by day, scrolls sideways and shades weekends. Both group the entries by source, place entries that do not overlap on a shared row, and show a title beside a bar that is too short for it; a line marks today. They show date ranges without dependencies or automatic scheduling. On phones the calendar uses **Agenda**. A date range continues across weeks and months as one bar per week, the agenda lists it on every day it covers, and a bar cut off at the edge of the period has a square end. Hover over or focus an entry to see its full title, its dates and its source.

A date-only value stays on its calendar date. Date-times use the time zone chosen in **Date sources**. Drag a CRM entry to another day in any view except the agenda, or use Space, the arrow keys and Space again. An entry moves by as many days as you drag it, wherever you grab a longer span, and its duration and time of day are preserved. In the hourly part of the week, dragging up or down also changes the time in steps of 15 minutes. An entry with a date range or an end date has a handle at each end: drag it to change only the start or only the end. While you drag, the entry shows its new dates. On a touch screen, press and hold an entry briefly before dragging it. Only writable input dates can change, and saves use the normal record version and permission checks; when a save is refused, the entry returns to its dates.

To add a record on a date, double-click an empty day, a free spot in a timeline row or an hour of the week, or use **+** on a day of the month. The record opens with that date filled in. A date and time starts at the chosen hour, or at 9:00, and a date-time range lasts one hour; change both times in the date picker. With several sources, choose the list for the new record first. Click the period title to jump to another date.

Each source displays up to 500 records in the requested period, and connected calendars up to 2,000 events. A notice explains when results are incomplete. Exports need a complete result: choose a shorter period or narrower source filters first.

## Which meetings appear in my calendar? [#connected-calendar-events]

Where connected messaging is available, **My connected calendars** lists your Google and Outlook calendars, including shared schedules under their usual sharing rules. Check the calendars to include. These meetings are read-only here; their detail opens the connected calendar for editing. Cancelled events are hidden. Imported recurring instances are shown, and recurring series rules are expanded for the displayed period, including time zones and exclusions. Invalid or unsupported recurrence rules are reported as incomplete results.

Connected calendars require the existing messaging entitlement and inbox read permission. Self-hosted workspaces can use record dates, every calendar layout, exports and subscriptions without a calendar provider.

## How do I use this calendar in another app? [#calendar-export-and-subscription]

**Export & subscribe → Download .ics** downloads a snapshot of the displayed period and enabled sources. While the displayed period is incomplete, **Download .ics** and **Create subscription URL** are unavailable. Import it into Apple Calendar, Outlook, Google Calendar or another iCalendar-compatible app. A snapshot does not refresh. Connected events are excluded by default to avoid duplicates; you can explicitly include selected connected events.

**Create subscription URL** creates a private read-only feed for the enabled sources, source filters and time zone at that moment. Paste the URL into your calendar app's “subscribe by URL” option. It covers the previous 30 days and next 335 days; your calendar app decides the refresh interval. A later source or filter change needs a new subscription URL. Up to ten subscriptions can be active; each one is listed with the date it was created.

Copy the URL when it is created: it is shown only once. Anyone with it can read the selected events while you retain access. Every refresh checks your active membership and current permissions. **Revoke subscription** immediately stops future fetches; calendar apps may keep previously downloaded events. Changes flow from Customermates to the calendar app. Editing an imported event does not update its CRM record; this is not a two-way calendar connection.

## How do I schedule a meeting? [#schedule-meetings]

Click **Schedule meeting** in the header of any record, for example a person, an organization or a deal. On [Calendar](app:calendar), double-click an empty time slot in the week or month view (or use **+** on a day of the month) and choose **New meeting**; it is offered next to creating a record when a list source allows it. Both open the same window.

### What can I set? [#schedule-meeting-options]

The window has these fields:

- A title, the date, the start time and the length (15 to 120 minutes).
- Free times for that day, taken from your synced calendars within working hours (Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 in your time zone). You can still type any start time.
- The calendar, shown only when you have more than one connected calendar.
- Attendees: email addresses are suggested from the record and from the people linked to it, and you can type more.
- A location, a description and, if you want one, a video link: Google Meet for Google calendars, Microsoft Teams for Outlook.
- A switch to send invitations. Google or Microsoft sends them.
- An optional follow-up: choose a list and one of its date or date-time fields, and the app creates a record in that list at the meeting start, linked to the record.

### What happens after I save? [#schedule-meeting-result]

The event is created in your own connected Google or Microsoft calendar. It appears on [Calendar](app:calendar) and on the activity timeline of the records it belongs to, and of people whose email address is an attendee. Only your own connected calendars can be written. If the connected account lost its calendar permission, the app asks you to reconnect it under [Channels & calendars](app:settings/channels).

### What do I need to schedule meetings? [#schedule-meeting-requirements]

A connected Google or Microsoft account and the plan the [Inbox](/docs/app-inbox#who-can-use-the-inbox) needs: Pro or higher. Scheduling meetings is not available when self-hosting, because the calendar connection runs through the hosted service, and not in the demo workspace.

### Can an agent schedule meetings? [#schedule-meeting-agents]

Yes, with the MCP tool `schedule_meeting`, whose actions are `options`, `slots`, `create`, `update` and `cancel`. `slots` finds free times; `create`, `update` and `cancel` change meetings in the caller's own connected calendar. Like the other messaging tools, it needs Pro or higher and Customermates Cloud. Exact schemas are in the [MCP catalog](/docs/mcp#tool-catalog).

## How do I let people book a meeting with me? [#booking-pages]

A booking page is a public link that anyone can open without an account, like a small scheduling page of your own. Visitors pick a free time from your calendar, and each booking creates a calendar event, a person and a record in your CRM. Create and manage pages under [Booking pages](app:settings/booking) in Settings, next to [Channels & calendars](app:settings/channels). Each user can have up to 10 booking pages.

### What can I set on a booking page? [#booking-page-settings]

- A title, a description and the calendar that receives the events.
- The meeting length (15 to 120 minutes), the buffer between meetings, the minimum notice and how many days ahead visitors can book (up to 90).
- The time zone, the working hours for each weekday and, if you want one, a video link.
- The list in which the visitor's person is found or created (one person per email address), the list that receives one record per booking and the field of that list that gets the meeting start.
- Up to 8 questions. Each question is a field of the booking list (text, link, single choice, number, yes/no or date) and can be required.
- A message shown after booking.
- The **Accept bookings** switch.

Switch **Accept bookings** off to pause a page without deleting it. Deleting a page removes its link and its booking history; meetings that are already booked stay in your calendar.

### What happens when someone books? [#booking-page-visitors]

The visitor sees free times from your synced calendar, in the visitor's own time zone. Busy time hides slots, and bookings cannot overlap because a time is reserved first. The visitor enters a name and an email address, answers your questions and confirms. The app then creates the calendar event with the visitor as an attendee (Google or Microsoft sends the invitation), finds or creates the person by email address, creates the booking record and links the event to them. Every booking also produces the `form.submitted` event with the booking as its payload, so rules can react to it.

Booking pages protect themselves with rate limits per visitor and per link, a hidden honeypot field, a maximum of 3 upcoming bookings per email address per page and limits on the size of the input.

### What do I need for booking pages? [#booking-page-requirements]

The same as for scheduling meetings: a connected Google or Microsoft account, the plan the [Inbox](/docs/app-inbox#who-can-use-the-inbox) needs (Pro or higher) and Customermates Cloud. Booking pages are not available when self-hosting or in the demo workspace. If the plan or the calendar connection of the person who owns a page ends, the page shows that it is not available.

### Can an agent manage booking pages? [#booking-page-agents]

Yes, with the MCP tool `manage_booking_links`, whose actions are `list`, `get`, `save` and `delete`. Like the other messaging tools, it needs Pro or higher and Customermates Cloud. Exact schemas are in the [MCP catalog](/docs/mcp#tool-catalog).

## For agents [#tips-for-agents]

Change a person's calendar with `manage_calendar` rather than driving the page; it changes only the caller's own calendar. Call `options` first: it lists the lists with date fields, their choice fields for colors and, where connected messaging is available, the caller's connected calendars, all by stable ID. `read` returns the configuration and `saved`, which stays false while the default applies. `update` takes a `config` with only the keys to change: `sources` and `calendarIds` replace the whole list, and a source's `filters` use the filter fields of `manage_data_views` `config` for the list's surface. Pass `timeZone` with the first update, because the default is UTC. `reset` returns to the default. `subscriptions` lists the subscription URLs and `revoke` revokes one by `id`; creating a subscription URL stays in the app. There is no REST endpoint for the calendar. Exact schemas are in the [MCP catalog](/docs/mcp#tool-catalog).

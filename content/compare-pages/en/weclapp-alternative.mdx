## Who weclapp is a good choice for

weclapp was founded in 2008 by Ertan Özdil, has belonged to the Exact Group since 2022 and is based in Kitzingen. It targets small and mid-sized companies in wholesale, e-commerce, IT services, agencies, consulting and retail.

weclapp describes itself plainly: neither pure accounting software nor a pure CRM, with its focus on inventory, purchasing and sales processes, invoicing and e-commerce, complemented by an integrated CRM and project features. The CRM side covers lead qualification statuses, opportunities with automatic assignment of sales reps, campaigns, Web2Lead contact forms, a customer map with sales regions and commission accounting. Mailboxes connect with automatic filing to the customer, address books sync with Outlook and Google, and newsletters go out through Mailchimp, CleverReach or Brevo.

If an opportunity should become a quote, an order, a delivery and an invoice without anyone typing the data twice, weclapp does that in one system. A CRM on its own cannot.

## Why teams look for a weclapp alternative

**The CRM module starts on the second package.** ERP Starter at €39 includes contact management, quotes, invoices, accounting and e-invoicing, but not the CRM module. Opportunities, campaigns and the sales pipeline come with ERP Dienstleistung. A sales team that only needs a pipeline pays for project management, time tracking and accounting along with it.

**CRM-only seats need a package first.** weclapp sells a CRM single licence from €68 a month for staff who only need CRM access, but only together with ERP Dienstleistung or ERP Handel.

**Annual contracts bind you to the term.** Monthly licences can be cancelled with one month's notice; annual licences only at the end of the term. Customermates bills monthly and has no annual plans.

**Client conversations happen outside email.** weclapp's CRM pages describe mailbox connections and Outlook integration. Consultancies and agencies often talk to clients on WhatsApp or LinkedIn as well; Customermates keeps those threads in the same [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) as email, linked to the contact.

## Where Customermates fits

Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM built in Germany for consultants, agencies, other relationship-driven service businesses and founders. It covers everything up to the signed deal and does not write invoices.

**A small sales model.** Contacts, organizations, deals, services and tasks, with custom fields of ten types. The deal stage is a field with a probability per stage, shown as a board, and Customermates adds up a weighted pipeline total from those probabilities. Saved views are personal. Dashboards, roles and an audit log are on every plan.

**Conversations next to the deal.** From Pro, Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram arrive in one list. You reply on the original channel, and threads with a linked contact show on that contact's timeline.

**AI that does the CRM upkeep.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes threads, drafts replies and updates records. It uses hosted AI credits, with a monthly allowance per active user shown on the [pricing page](/pricing). If you prefer Claude, ChatGPT or another client, connect it to the [MCP server](/docs/mcp), with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan and on self-hosted installs.

**Open source, with a hosting choice.** The AGPL-3.0 core runs on your own server with Docker Compose and PostgreSQL, including REST, webhooks and MCP, but a [self-hosted install](/features/self-hosted) has no inbox, no Mate and no hosted credits. In Customermates Cloud, the database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region. That is a selected setting, not a contractual residency guarantee; subprocessors, including US providers, are listed in the legal pages next to the DPA.

- **No inventory, purchasing, shop or marketplace connections.**
- **No project management or time tracking.**
- **No task due dates, reminders or notifications.** Tasks have assignees, links and notes. For follow-up dates, add a custom date field to deals and filter a saved view on it.
- **No web forms, newsletters or bulk email.** Messages go out one at a time, written by you or drafted by Mate.
- **No certification and no native mobile apps.** Customermates runs in the phone's browser.

## Keeping invoicing where it is

The usual setup after leaving an ERP: Customermates up to the won deal, your accounting tool for everything after it. There are two ways to hand over.

**With webhooks, on any plan.** Customermates sends a `deal.updated` event when a deal changes, including its stage. A workflow in your own n8n instance, which runs separately, checks for the Won stage and calls your accounting tool. Lexware Office, for example, has a public REST API with endpoints for contacts and invoices. The [webhook documentation](/docs/webhooks) lists every event.

**With a routine, in Customermates Cloud.** A routine runs a saved instruction when a record changes, uses hosted AI credits and runs as its owner. Under Events pick Deal Updated, add the stage field under Changed fields, set a record filter on Won and save an instruction like this:

> When a deal moves to Won, create a task "Hand over for invoicing" linked to the deal and its organization, assign it to Anna, and list the deal's services and value in the task notes. Do not send any message.

Mate can send messages without asking again, so the last sentence matters if the routine should only prepare work.

## Other weclapp alternatives worth a look

Customermates publishes this list. Details come from each vendor's own pages, checked on 8 October 2026.

- **Odoo** is the closest match if you want to stay with one suite. The Standard plan includes all apps, including CRM, accounting, inventory and projects, at one price per user on Odoo Online; One App Free gives you a single app for unlimited users. Custom adds Odoo.sh or on-premise hosting, Studio and external API access. See [Odoo compared with Customermates](/compare/odoo).
- **Xentral** is built around online shops, marketplaces and shipping. Its plans start at €99 a month (Launch) and run to a starting price of €849 (Pro), with Scale on request; every plan has unlimited users, and the final price depends on sales volume and scope. Data sits in the AWS Frankfurt region, and an MCP server is included from the Business plan.
- **Pipedrive** suits sales teams that want a pipeline tool with email sequences and no ERP. Lite costs €14 per seat per month billed yearly or €24 monthly; Growth, which adds sequences and automations, costs €39 or €49.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="How much does weclapp cost?">
ERP Starter costs €39 per user per month, monthly only; ERP Dienstleistung €86 on an annual contract or €95 monthly; ERP Handel €163 or €179. All prices are net plus VAT, Enterprise is priced on request, and the trial runs 30 days.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does weclapp have a CRM without the ERP?">
Not as a package of its own. The CRM module starts in ERP Dienstleistung, and the CRM single licence from €68 a month can only be added to Dienstleistung or Handel.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Who is behind weclapp?">
weclapp GmbH, founded in 2008 by Ertan Özdil and headquartered in Kitzingen. The Exact Group acquired it in 2022, and Paul Ramakers and Alexander Rahusen are its managing directors.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="weclapp or Odoo: which fits a small company?">
Both are suites. weclapp sells fixed packages by industry, with data stored only in Germany. Odoo sells all apps at one per-user price, or one app free, and Odoo Custom can run on your own server. If you mainly need a CRM, compare both with a CRM-only tool before paying for a suite.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I host weclapp myself, or is there an open-source alternative?">
weclapp offers weclappON for your own server or a Docker host, at the same licence fees as the cloud; weclapp's support portal lists integrations that do not work there. Customermates is open source under AGPL-3.0 and can be self-hosted, without the inbox and Mate.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can Customermates write invoices?">
No. Customermates has no quotes and no invoices, so your accounting tool keeps that job; webhooks and a separately run n8n instance can pass a won deal to it.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

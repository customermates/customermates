## Setup & Onboarding

weclapp is an ERP system with CRM features, not a CRM with optional ERP. Setting up weclapp means configuring the entire business suite: chart of accounts, tax settings, warehouse management, and CRM modules together. Even if you only need CRM, the system's ERP backbone adds setup complexity. Most businesses need days to weeks for a proper implementation, and many rely on weclapp partners for guided onboarding.

Customermates is ready in two minutes. Set up your pipelines, import contacts, and start tracking deals. No accounting configuration, no warehouse settings, no ERP overhead.

## Pricing & Cost Model

weclapp is sold either as ERP packages or as single-module licences. The ERP packages run from €39/user/month (ERP Starter) to €86 (ERP Services) and €163 (ERP Trade), billed annually. A standalone CRM licence starts at €68/user/month. A 30-day trial is available. Since the Exact acquisition, pricing structures have been consolidating, and larger deployments require contacting sales.

For teams that only need CRM, either option costs more than necessary: the ERP Starter at €39 bundles accounting, inventory, and purchasing you may never use, while the dedicated CRM licence is €68/user/month on its own.

Customermates starts at €12/user/month (Starter), with every core CRM feature included. No modules to select, no ERP components to pay for. A team of 10 pays €120/month with Customermates Starter vs. €680/month with weclapp's standalone CRM licence.

weclapp is proprietary software, now owned by Exact (a Dutch ERP company). This acquisition introduces uncertainty about the product's long-term roadmap, pricing evolution, and strategic direction.

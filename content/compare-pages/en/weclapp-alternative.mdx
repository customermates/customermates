> weclapp is a German cloud ERP platform that includes CRM functionality as part of a broader business suite encompassing accounting, inventory management, purchasing, and project management. Now part of Exact (acquired in 2023), weclapp serves businesses that need integrated ERP and CRM. However, for teams that primarily need CRM, weclapp's ERP-first approach means paying for and navigating complexity you do not need. Customermates provides focused, open-source CRM at a fraction of the price. AI agents (Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini) operate the CRM through a native MCP integration with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools, and a native unified inbox brings email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Telegram together from the Pro tier. Add a cloud database in Frankfurt, a self-hosting option and an n8n community node, all starting at [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]]/user/month.

## Setup & Onboarding

weclapp is an ERP system with CRM features, not a CRM with optional ERP. Setting up weclapp means configuring the entire business suite: chart of accounts, tax settings, warehouse management, and CRM modules together. Even if you only need CRM, the system's ERP backbone adds setup complexity. Most businesses need days to weeks for a proper implementation, and many rely on weclapp partners for guided onboarding.

Customermates' managed cloud is ready as soon as you sign up. Set up your deal stages, import contacts from XLSX workbooks, and start tracking deals. No accounting configuration, no warehouse settings, no ERP overhead.

## Pricing & Cost Model

weclapp is sold either as ERP packages or as single-module licences. The ERP packages run from €39/user/month (ERP Starter) to €86 (ERP Services) and €163 (ERP Trade), billed annually. A standalone CRM licence starts at €68/user/month. A 30-day trial is available. Since the Exact acquisition, pricing structures have been consolidating, and larger deployments require contacting sales.

For teams that only need CRM, either option costs more than necessary: the ERP Starter at €39 bundles accounting, inventory, and purchasing you may never use, while the dedicated CRM licence is €68/user/month on its own.

Customermates starts at [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]]/user/month (Starter), with every core CRM feature included. No modules to select, no ERP components to pay for. A team of 10 pays [[commercial.price.starter.monthly.seats.10]]/month with Customermates Starter vs. €680/month with weclapp's standalone CRM licence.

## Open-Source & Independence

Customermates is open-source. You can inspect the code and self-host the platform on infrastructure you own, which reduces vendor dependency.

weclapp is proprietary software, now owned by Exact (a Dutch ERP company). Your data lives on the vendor's infrastructure, and you cannot run the software yourself.

Customermates gives you independence: open-source code under the AGPL and the option to self-host, so you are not tied to one vendor's roadmap.

## Feature Comparison

weclapp's CRM module covers the essentials: contacts, organizations, opportunities, quotes, and campaign management. Its strength lies in the connection to ERP: when you need CRM data to flow directly into invoicing, inventory, and purchasing processes, weclapp's integrated approach has clear value.

However, as a CRM tool in isolation, weclapp's capabilities are limited compared to dedicated CRM platforms. Pipeline management is functional but basic. The user interface prioritizes ERP workflows and can feel cumbersome for pure CRM usage. Mobile optimization is limited, and the overall user experience reflects ERP software conventions rather than modern CRM design.

Customermates delivers focused CRM: contacts, organizations, deals, pipelines, tasks, services, AI agents that operate the CRM (via native MCP), webhooks and the REST API on Starter, plus the native unified inbox (email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram) from Pro. Every feature is designed for CRM workflows, not adapted from an ERP context. The interface is modern, responsive, and built for how sales and service teams actually work.

## AI Agents That Operate the CRM

weclapp is an ERP-focused platform; check its current documentation for AI features and whether external AI clients can operate its CRM module.

Customermates connects Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini to your CRM through a native MCP integration ([[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools) at every tier, starting at [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]]/user/month. weclapp offers integrations primarily within its own ERP ecosystem and with common German business tools (DATEV, various shop systems, shipping providers). CRM-specific automation and third-party CRM integrations are more limited compared to dedicated CRM platforms.

Customermates offers full REST API access, webhooks and MCP at the base price, plus a community node for a separately operated n8n instance. Through n8n you can connect Slack, Google Workspace, Notion and many other tools without custom development.

> The right EspoCRM alternative depends on what you need that EspoCRM does not give you. For another established open-source CRM with quotations and marketing features, look at SuiteCRM. For unlimited custom objects with a free self-hosted core, look at Twenty. For CRM, accounting and inventory in one system, look at Odoo. Customermates fits small client-facing teams whose clients write on WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram or Telegram as well as by email, and who want an AI assistant or their own AI client to keep the CRM current. If you depend on custom entities, campaigns or a customer portal, EspoCRM remains the better tool. Customermates publishes this comparison; every EspoCRM and third-party detail comes from the vendors' own sites, checked on 8 October 2026.

## Which alternative fits which team

| Your situation | Look at | Why |
|---|---|---|
| You model your own record types, such as properties, policies or cases | Stay with EspoCRM, or Twenty | EspoCRM's Entity Manager creates custom entities and relationships; Twenty has unlimited custom objects. Customermates has five fixed record types |
| You send newsletters or campaigns from the CRM | Stay with EspoCRM | Mass email and campaigns are in EspoCRM's core; Customermates has no bulk sending |
| You need quotations or inventory beside the CRM | EspoCRM with the Sales Pack, SuiteCRM or Odoo | Customermates has neither |
| Clients reach you on WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram or Telegram as well as email | Customermates Pro or Business | These channels share one inbox with your mailboxes; EspoCRM's extension catalogue lists none of them |
| You want an assistant that drafts follow-ups and updates records on a schedule | Customermates Cloud | Mate and Routines run there on hosted AI credits |
| You self-host and want an AI client connection without licensing add-ons per instance | Customermates core, or EspoCRM core with its free MCP Extension | Both are AGPL; reports, workflows and BPM in EspoCRM remain paid extensions |
| You want CRM, accounting and stock in one system | Odoo | See the [Odoo comparison](/compare/odoo) |

## Who EspoCRM is a good choice for

EspoCRM has been around for 12 years and says more than 50,000 companies use it. It is a classic sales and service CRM. Leads convert into accounts, contacts and opportunities. Opportunities move through Prospecting, Qualification, Proposal and Negotiation to Closed Won or Closed Lost, each stage with its own probability. The core adds a calendar for meetings and calls, a Stream of record changes, a customer self-service portal, Kanban boards, mass email and campaigns. An administrator can create custom entities, fields and relationships in the Entity Manager, and formula scripts calculate fields or trigger actions.

The deeper modules are extensions. The Advanced Pack adds reports, workflows and business process management. The Sales Pack adds products, quotations, purchases and inventory. Others cover project management, VoIP, Zoom, Stripe, MailChimp, Outlook and Google, where the Google Integration syncs Google Calendar, connects Google Contacts and handles secure sign-in for email accounts. Intelligence, the AI extension, offers summaries, intelligent paste and an AI email composer on OpenAI, Gemini, Claude or another OpenAI-compatible provider. EspoCRM also distributes a free MCP Extension under AGPLv3, which gives AI clients create, read, update and delete tools plus access to the Stream.

EspoCRM's pricing page lists all extensions as included in its Cloud plans. If your team wants to design its own data model and run marketing from the CRM, EspoCRM covers more ground than Customermates does.

## Why teams look for an EspoCRM alternative

**Self-hosted extensions are time-limited.** The EspoCRM core is free under AGPLv3. Reports, workflows, BPM, quotations and the AI extension are paid. EspoCRM's extension licence agreement grants use "on a single EspoCRM Application instance on a single server for the Licensed Period". When that period ends, you renew or uninstall the extension. The extension pages show no prices, so ask EspoCRM for current prices before you budget.

**Cloud plans are billed for six or twelve months.** EspoCRM Cloud Basic costs €12.90 per user per month with at least 3 users, Enterprise €22.00 with at least 5, and Ultimate €59.00 with at least 10. Basic and Enterprise are billed for 6 months or a year. Users added after the purchase are charged monthly.

**Conversations outside email.** EspoCRM syncs and sends email in the core. Its extension catalogue lists no official integration for WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram or Telegram, so those threads stay outside the CRM unless you add a third-party integration.

**Running PHP yourself.** A self-hosted EspoCRM needs Apache, Nginx or IIS, PHP 8.3 to 8.5 with a list of required PHP extensions, and MySQL, MariaDB or PostgreSQL. EspoCRM also documents a Docker setup. Customermates runs as two containers: the app and PostgreSQL 16.

## Where Customermates fits

Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM for small client-facing teams: consultants, agencies, other service businesses and founders. It is built in Germany.

**Client conversations next to the deal.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one list, and you reply on the original channel. Link a participant to a contact once, and the conversation is filed with that contact. Starter has no inbox.

**AI that does the CRM work.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes threads, drafts replies, updates records and builds views. It uses hosted AI credits, a monthly allowance per active user shown on the [pricing page](/pricing). Mate asks for a fresh approval before every deletion. Sending a message runs without one, so tell it to save drafts when you want to review first. Routines run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes, always as their owner.

**Your own AI client over MCP.** The [MCP server](/docs/mcp) is included on every plan and on self-hosted installs, with a catalogue of [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools. Claude, ChatGPT and other clients connect with OAuth or an API key and act with the connected user's permissions. Its messaging tools work only on Pro and higher in Customermates Cloud, where the same connection lists inbox threads, saves drafts and sends messages. Model calls from your client run on your own AI provider.

**Self-hosting without add-on licences.** The [self-hosted edition](/features/self-hosted) is the AGPL core with REST, webhooks and MCP, deployed with Docker Compose. Nothing extra is licensed per instance. It has no inbox, no Mate and no hosted credits.

**Where the cloud runs.** In Customermates Cloud, the primary database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, a [DPA](/dpa) is available, and the subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the legal pages. Customermates holds no compliance certification.

## Where Customermates is the wrong choice

- **No custom entities.** You work with contacts, organizations, deals, services and tasks. You can rename them from presets and add custom fields of ten types, but you cannot add a sixth record type.
- **No mass email, campaigns or sequences.** Messages go one at a time, written by you or drafted by Mate.
- **No customer portal, quotations, inventory or telephony.**
- **No BPM or visual workflow designer.** Automation runs through Routines in Customermates Cloud, webhooks and the separate n8n community node.
- **Tasks have no built-in due date.** Add a custom date field if you need one.
- **No native mobile apps and no single sign-on.** Saved views are personal, and permissions apply per record type and action, not per field.

## Moving from EspoCRM

Most EspoCRM sales data has a direct home. Under Data model in the company settings, you can call organizations "Accounts" and deals "Opportunities", so your team keeps its vocabulary.

| In EspoCRM | In Customermates |
|---|---|
| Accounts | Organizations, renamed "Accounts" |
| Contacts | Contacts |
| Leads | Contacts with a single-select field such as "Lifecycle" (Lead, Customer) |
| Opportunities | Deals, renamed "Opportunities" |
| Products from the Sales Pack | Services, renamed "Products", linked to deals with quantities |
| Custom entities | Custom fields on the closest record type |
| Campaigns, mass email, portal | No equivalent; keep a dedicated tool |

The stages line up almost one to one. A new Customermates workspace starts with Prospecting 10%, Qualification 20%, Demo 40%, Proposal 60%, Negotiation 80%, Won 100% and Lost 0%. Delete Demo if you never used it, and copy your EspoCRM probabilities into the stage weights. The weighted pipeline then multiplies each deal's value by its stage probability. It is arithmetic on your own numbers and does not predict anything.

## Other EspoCRM alternatives worth a look

Customermates publishes this list. Details come from each vendor's site, checked on 8 October 2026.

- **SuiteCRM** is the closest match in scope. It is open source, you can host it on your own server, and SuiteCRM 8 is the current major version. Its site highlights reporting and dashboards, customer segmentation, targeted marketing and quotations, and it offers a hosted trial, support, implementation, training and a managed package called SuiteASSURED.
- **Twenty** suits teams that want custom objects in a hosted product with a free self-hosted core. It has unlimited custom objects, an MCP server, AI agents, webhooks and REST and GraphQL APIs. Cloud Pro costs $9 per user per month billed yearly. Organization, at $19 per user per month billed yearly, adds row-level permissions and SAML or OIDC single sign-on. You can self-host the open-source core for free. The [Twenty comparison](/compare/twenty-alternative) goes into detail.
- **Odoo** fits when the CRM is one part of running the business. Its free plan covers one app with unlimited users on Odoo Online, the paid plans include all apps from accounting to inventory and HR, and the Community edition is a free open-source download.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is EspoCRM free?">
The core is. EspoCRM is open source under AGPLv3 and free to self-host. Reports, workflows, BPM, quotations and AI come as paid extensions, licensed per instance for a limited period. EspoCRM Cloud starts at €12.90 per user per month on Basic, with at least 3 users and billing for 6 months or a year, and it includes all extensions.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does EspoCRM have an MCP server?">
Yes. EspoCRM distributes a free MCP Extension under AGPLv3. It runs through EspoCRM's REST API, exposes create, read, update and delete tools and Stream access, and lets an administrator choose which tools AI clients see. Customermates has its MCP server built in, with a catalogue of [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools, on every plan and on self-hosted installs; its inbox tools work on Pro and higher in Customermates Cloud.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What is the best open-source alternative to EspoCRM?">
It depends on the gap. SuiteCRM is closest in scope, Twenty offers unlimited custom objects, and Odoo adds accounting and inventory. Customermates fits small teams that want WhatsApp, LinkedIn and email conversations next to their deals.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is SuiteCRM really free?">
The software is. SuiteCRM is open source and you can host it on your own server at no licence cost. The SuiteCRM site also offers a hosted option, support, implementation, training and the SuiteASSURED package, so your real cost is hosting plus whatever help you buy.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can Customermates send email campaigns like EspoCRM?">
No. Customermates sends one message at a time, with no bulk sends or sequences. If campaigns matter, keep EspoCRM or use a dedicated email marketing tool and connect it to CRM events through webhooks.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

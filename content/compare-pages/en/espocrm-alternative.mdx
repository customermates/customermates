> [EspoCRM](https://www.espocrm.com/) is a long-standing open-source CRM written in PHP. Since version 8.1 in January 2024 it is licensed under the [AGPL-3.0](https://www.espocrm.com/blog/espocrm-license/), and it runs on your own server or on EspoCRM Cloud. Customermates is a younger open-source CRM with an AGPL core, a unified inbox for email and chat channels, and a built-in MCP server for AI clients. Both are serious options; they suit different teams.

## Why teams look for an EspoCRM alternative

EspoCRM is flexible and mature. Teams that look elsewhere usually name one of these reasons:

- **Extensions add up.** Reports, workflows and BPM come with the [Advanced Pack](https://www.espocrm.com/extensions/), Gmail and Outlook sync with separate integration extensions, and AI features with the Intelligence extension. On a self-hosted installation, each is a yearly licence per instance.
- **Messaging beyond email.** EspoCRM handles email well, but WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram or Telegram conversations are not part of the core product.
- **AI clients.** EspoCRM's AI features live in its Intelligence extension. There is no official MCP server; the MCP servers that exist are community projects.
- **Stack preference.** Some teams prefer a TypeScript and PostgreSQL stack over PHP.

## Where EspoCRM is the better fit

- **You need custom entity types.** EspoCRM's entity manager lets you add new record types; Customermates has a fixed set of five record families extended with custom fields.
- **You send campaigns from the CRM.** EspoCRM includes mass email and campaigns. Customermates messaging is one-to-one by design; there are no bulk sends.
- **You want deep BPM.** The Advanced Pack brings business process management that Customermates does not attempt.
- **Your team runs PHP applications** and wants the CRM on the same stack.

## Where Customermates is the better fit

- **Conversations across channels.** On Customermates Cloud from Pro, email (Gmail, Outlook, IMAP), LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram arrive in one [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox), linked to contacts.
- **AI clients that operate the CRM.** Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and other MCP clients connect to the [built-in MCP server](/docs/mcp) on every plan and every installation, with the permissions of the connected user. On Customermates Cloud, the Mate assistant works in the app and asks for a fresh approval before destructive actions and sends.
- **No extension licences.** The [self-hosted edition](/features/self-hosted) is the open-source core with the Starter feature set; there is nothing extra to license per instance. The inbox and Mate are cloud-only.
- **Monthly billing** from [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per user, without a minimum user count.

## Self-hosting compared

Both products can run on your infrastructure. EspoCRM publishes an installer and a Docker image and supports MySQL, MariaDB or PostgreSQL. Customermates ships a Docker Compose file with two services: the application and PostgreSQL 16. In both cases your team owns updates, backups, TLS and monitoring. The difference is what the self-hosted edition includes: EspoCRM's core plus any extensions you license, or the Customermates core with REST, webhooks and MCP. See the [open-source CRM guide](/blog/open-source-crm) for a licence-first checklist.

## Migrating from EspoCRM

1. **Export** accounts, contacts and opportunities from EspoCRM to spreadsheets.
2. **Map** EspoCRM fields to Customermates organizations, contacts and deals, and create custom fields for the rest.
3. **Import** each record type as an XLSX workbook (organizations first, then contacts, then deals), or move data through the REST API.
4. **Rebuild** stages as the deal-stage options and assign probabilities.
5. **Connect** your mailboxes and chat channels on Pro and link conversations to contacts.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is EspoCRM open source?">
Yes. EspoCRM is licensed under the AGPL-3.0 since version 8.1, released in January 2024. Its paid extensions are sold under separate yearly licences.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is Customermates open source like EspoCRM?">
Yes. First-party code outside the `ee/` directory is AGPL-3.0-only; code in `ee/` uses a separate commercial licence, except client material that Section 6 of that licence keeps under the AGPL. The self-hosted edition is the open-source core with the Starter feature set.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does EspoCRM have an MCP server?">
We found no official EspoCRM MCP server as of 27 September 2026; the available MCP servers are community projects. Customermates includes an MCP server with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan and installation.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Which is cheaper, EspoCRM or Customermates?">
It depends on the setup. Self-hosted, both core products have no licence fee, but EspoCRM extensions such as the Advanced Pack are yearly licences. In the cloud, EspoCRM Basic lists €12.90 per user per month with a three-user minimum and 6 or 12 month billing; Customermates starts at [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per user per month, billed monthly.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can Customermates send email campaigns like EspoCRM?">
No. Customermates messaging is one-to-one, with no bulk sends. Use a dedicated email provider for campaigns and connect it to CRM events through webhooks.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

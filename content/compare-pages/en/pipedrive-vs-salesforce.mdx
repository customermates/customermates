> Pick Pipedrive if your team sells from one visual pipeline and a list of scheduled activities, and prefers four fixed plans to a platform it has to configure. Pick Salesforce if sales, service and marketing should share one customizable system, or if two founders want to start for free: Salesforce Free Suite costs nothing for up to two users, and Pipedrive has no free plan. Salesforce is not always the expensive one. Starter Suite costs $25 per user and syncs email, which Pipedrive only starts on Growth at €39 billed yearly. From Pro Suite, at $100 billed annually under a contract, Salesforce costs more than any Pipedrive plan. Customermates publishes this comparison and is a third option, described in its own section below. Every Pipedrive and Salesforce detail comes from the vendors' own pages, checked on 8 October 2026.

## Which one fits your team

| Your situation | Better fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Two founders, no CRM budget yet | Salesforce Free Suite | Free for up to 2 users with no end date, as long as someone logs in at least every 60 days. Pipedrive offers a 14-day trial, then a paid plan |
| Five people who want email in the CRM at a low seat price | Salesforce Starter Suite | $25 per user, billed monthly or annually, with email, event and contact sync. Pipedrive syncs email from Growth |
| Sellers who work from a pipeline board and a daily activity list | Pipedrive | Lead, calendar and pipeline management on every plan, unlimited pipelines, native iOS and Android apps |
| Rule-based automations and email sequences in a packaged plan | Pipedrive Growth or higher | Both start on Growth, with 50 to 250 automations and 5 to 50 sequences depending on plan |
| Sales, service and marketing on one platform, with quoting and forecasting | Salesforce Pro Suite or higher | Pro Suite adds quoting, forecasting, more customization and AppExchange apps; it is billed annually and needs a contract |
| Agentforce for Sales included in the seat price | Salesforce Max | Max lists Agentforce for Sales and Agentforce Coworker; for Core and above, Salesforce says AI can be added |
| Clients write on LinkedIn or WhatsApp as often as by email, or you want an open-source CRM | Customermates | See the third option below |

## What makes Pipedrive different from Salesforce

**Pipedrive is one sales CRM in four plans.** Every plan, Lite included, covers leads, deals, contacts and calendar, pipeline management, AI-assisted reports, meeting intelligence and more than 500 integrations. Growth brings full email sync with tracking, automations, email sequences and forecast reports. Premium adds lead generation and routing, custom scoring with company data enrichment, AI email tools, and contracts with e-signatures. Ultimate adds account security rules and alerts, phone and email enrichment, and a sandbox account.

**Salesforce is a platform sold in editions.** The small-business suites, Free, Starter and Pro, bundle sales tools with service, marketing and commerce features; Salesforce describes Pro Suite as "more automation and customization across sales, service, marketing, and commerce". Core, Advanced and Max are the larger Sales Cloud editions above them. Pro Suite adds Flow Builder (5 flows per org), the Lightning App Builder and AppExchange; approval processes start with Core. That depth is the reason to choose Salesforce, and somebody on your team will own its configuration.

In short, Pipedrive decides what a small sales team needs and packages it. Salesforce gives you the parts and a lot of room.

## Where each one stops

**Pipedrive counts usage per plan.** According to its [usage limits article](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/usage-limits-in-pipedrive):

| Limit | Lite | Growth | Premium | Ultimate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Leads and deals | 2,500 × seats | 5,000 × seats | 15,000 × seats | 20,000 × seats |
| Custom fields | 30 | 100 | 300 | 500 |
| Automations | none | 50 | 150 | 250 |
| Email sequences | none | 5 | 25 | 50 |
| Synced email accounts per user | none | 1 | 3 | 5 |

Leads and deals never exceed 300,000 per company. At a limit, Pipedrive keeps your data but stops you adding more until you archive, buy a top-up or upgrade. Campaigns (from €13.33) and Web Visitors (from €41) are priced as add-ons.

**Salesforce limits sit in the edition and the contract.** Free Suite stops at 2 users and 10 GB of data storage per org, and has neither Flow Builder nor the Salesforce Mobile App. Pro Suite allows 5 flows per org. Starter Suite has no user maximum. Pro Suite and everything above it are billed annually, and Pro Suite needs a contract.

## AI and MCP

Pipedrive puts AI-assisted reports on every plan and AI email tools on Premium. Its [MCP server](https://www.pipedrive.com/en/features/mcp-server) is on every plan: ChatGPT, Claude and other assistants can query deals, update records and create activities, within token limits that depend on the plan.

Salesforce lists assistive AI with an employee agent on Starter Suite. Agentforce for Sales and Agentforce Coworker come with Max, and AI can be added to Core and above. Salesforce announced its [hosted MCP servers](https://developer.salesforce.com/blogs/2026/04/salesforce-hosted-mcp-servers-are-now-generally-available) as generally available in April 2026 for Enterprise Edition orgs and above. How that maps onto the current Core, Advanced and Max editions is a question to ask Salesforce. More background: [Agentforce explained](/blog/agentforce) and [Salesforce and MCP](/blog/salesforce-mcp).

## Data location and GDPR

Pipedrive [hosts on AWS](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/how-secure-is-my-data-in-pipedrive) in seven regions, Frankfurt and Dublin among them. EU sign-ups are placed in the EU region, though Pipedrive notes that an account can end up in another data center. Salesforce runs the [Hyperforce EU Operating Zone](https://www.salesforce.com/eu/platform/public-cloud-infrastructure/operating-zone/), in which a vetted set of products, Sales Cloud among them, store and process data only in the EU and Switzerland, with in-region support. Ask which of your products and editions it covers before you sign. Both are cloud services, and neither is open source.

## A third option: Customermates

Customermates publishes this page, so read this section as a vendor describing its own product. Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM for small client-facing teams such as consultants, agencies and founders, built in Germany. In scope it is closer to Pipedrive than to Salesforce: contacts, organizations, deals, services and tasks, custom fields of ten types and no plan-based record caps. It differs from both in four ways:

- **Conversations next to the deal.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) puts Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one list linked to your contacts, and you reply on the original channel. Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] connected account per user, Business [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedAccountsPerUser]]. Starter has no inbox.
- **AI that does the CRM work.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes threads, drafts replies and updates records. It uses hosted AI credits, a monthly allowance per active user listed on the [pricing page](/pricing). Routines run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes. Claude, ChatGPT or another client can use the [MCP server](/docs/mcp) with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan, billed by your own AI provider.
- **Open source.** The AGPL-3.0 core [runs on your own server](/features/self-hosted) with Docker Compose, including REST, webhooks and MCP, but without the inbox and Mate. In Customermates Cloud the database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, other providers, including US ones, are listed on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [DPA](/dpa) is available.
- **Monthly prices per user.** Monthly is the only billing cycle. Five people pay [[commercial.price.starter.monthly.seats.5]] a month on Starter or [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.5]] on Pro.

A five-person consultancy would set it up like this. Create the offerings as services first, because a deal's value is the sum of its linked services times their quantity. Under My Company › Settings, give the stage options probabilities such as Lead 10%, Proposal sent 50%, Negotiation 75%, Won 100% and Lost 0%; Customermates then shows each deal's weighted value and a weighted pipeline total. That is arithmetic on your own numbers and does not predict anything. Add a single-select field "Source" with Referral, LinkedIn and Website. Each person saves a board of their assigned deals grouped by stage, because saved views are personal. In Customermates Cloud, each partner can own a routine like this one, which runs as its owner and uses hosted AI credits:

> Every Monday at 8:00, list my open deals in "Proposal sent" that nobody has updated for 10 days, with the last message from each contact. Save a short follow-up draft in that conversation. Do not send anything.

Moving in works one record type at a time from XLSX workbooks: organizations, then contacts, then deals. Pipedrive exports Excel directly; from Salesforce, export a report as .xlsx with the Details Only option. Customermates does not accept CSV files. The [records documentation](/docs/app-records) covers the import wizard.

Customermates has no activity reminders or task due dates, no email sequences or campaigns, and no quoting, revenue forecasting or lead scoring. It has no telephony, no single sign-on and no native mobile apps. If you need those, Pipedrive or Salesforce covers them. There is no free plan either, only a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is Pipedrive similar to Salesforce?">
In the core job, yes: both manage leads, contacts, organizations and deals in a pipeline. The difference is scope. Pipedrive is a sales CRM with a fixed feature set per plan, while Salesforce is a configurable platform whose suites add service, marketing and commerce tools.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How much is Pipedrive per month?">
Billed yearly, Pipedrive costs €14 per seat per month on Lite, €39 on Growth, €59 on Premium and €79 on Ultimate, excluding VAT, or $14 to $79 in US dollars. Monthly billing costs more per seat. There is a 14-day free trial and no free plan.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is Salesforce cheaper than Pipedrive for a small team?">
At the entry level it can be. Free Suite costs nothing for two users, and Starter Suite at $25 per user includes email sync, which Pipedrive starts on Growth at €39. From Pro Suite at $100 per user, billed annually under a contract, Salesforce costs more than every Pipedrive plan.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What are the disadvantages of Pipedrive?">
Most of them sit in the plan structure. Lite has no email sync, automations or sequences. Every plan caps leads and deals per seat, up to 300,000 per company, and custom fields from 30 to 500. Campaigns and Web Visitors cost extra. Pipedrive runs only as a hosted service, in a region assigned at sign-up. If none of that touches your process, these are not real disadvantages.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Pipedrive or Salesforce for a startup?">
With two founders and no budget, Salesforce Free Suite is the cheaper start, and Starter Suite carries on from there without a user maximum. If your first salespeople work an activity list from their phones, Pipedrive packages exactly that, with native iOS and Android apps. Pro Suite's annual contract is a real commitment for a company that may change its sales process twice in a year.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a Pipedrive Salesforce integration?">
What Pipedrive documents is a move, not a live sync. Its [Import2 article](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/importing-data-from-a-previous-crm-using-import2) describes migrating from Salesforce through the partner Import2, free for smaller databases and paid for larger ones. A global admin starts it, and users should exist in Pipedrive first, because records otherwise belong to whoever runs the import. In the other direction, Pipedrive [exports](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/exporting-data-from-pipedrive) leads, deals, organizations, people, activities and notes as Excel files under Tools and apps › Export data. To keep both systems in sync, you would need a third-party connector.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

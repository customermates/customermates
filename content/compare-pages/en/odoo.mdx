> Whether you need an Odoo CRM alternative depends on what else runs in your Odoo database. If won opportunities turn into quotations, invoices and stock movements there, a standalone CRM is a step backwards: stay with Odoo, or compare it with ERPNext and Zoho One, which are suites too. If you use Odoo mainly for the pipeline, and your clients reach you on WhatsApp or LinkedIn as often as by email, a focused CRM fits better. Customermates is one, built for consultants, agencies and other small service teams. And if Odoo's free One App plan already covers your CRM, no paid tool beats it on price. Customermates publishes this comparison; every Odoo and third-party detail comes from the vendors' own sites, checked on 8 October 2026.

| Your situation | Look at | Why |
|---|---|---|
| CRM, quotations, accounting and inventory should share one database | Odoo, ERPNext or Zoho One | Customermates has no quotations, accounting or stock |
| You only need a pipeline, and one Odoo app is enough | Odoo One App Free | One app with unlimited users costs €0 on Odoo Online |

| You want to self-host an open-source CRM, not a suite | Customermates, Frappe CRM or Twenty | Odoo Community includes CRM too, inside the broader suite |

## Who Odoo CRM is a good choice for

Odoo is a suite of business apps, and CRM is one of them. The CRM app manages leads and a drag-and-drop pipeline, schedules calls, meetings and mailings, and creates quotations. Its reporting covers revenue forecasts, team performance and custom dashboards. The Community edition, open source under LGPLv3, includes it.

Lead handling is where Odoo goes furthest. Predictive lead scoring calculates each opportunity's probability of success from your won and lost history with a naive Bayes model, using stage and sales team plus optional fields such as country and source. Assignment rules route leads to teams and salespeople, and lead mining generates new leads by country, company size and industry, at one In-App Purchase credit per lead plus one per contact.

The bigger reason to choose Odoo is what sits next to the CRM. Standard and Custom include all apps for one price per user: sales, accounting, inventory, HR, project, point of sale and more. If a won deal should flow into sales orders, invoicing and stock in the same database, Odoo covers ground that Customermates does not. On Odoo Online you choose the hosting region, Europe among them.

## Why teams look for an Odoo CRM alternative

**The free plan covers one app.** One App Free gives you one app with unlimited users on Odoo Online, free for good. Installing a second app moves the database to Standard. Installing Studio or adding companies moves it to Custom.

**The prices on the pricing page are introductory.** On 8 October 2026, Odoo showed Standard at €19.90 per user per month billed yearly and Custom at €35.90, with a note that the discount is valid for 12 months for the users in the first order. After that, Standard costs €24.90 per user per month billed yearly or €31.10 billed monthly, and Custom €44.90 or €56.90.

**The external API is on Custom.** Odoo's external API lets outside software read and write Odoo data, over the JSON-2 API in Odoo 19 or the older XML-RPC and JSON-RPC endpoints. The pricing page lists it only on the Custom plan. Connecting n8n, a reporting tool or your own scripts to a hosted Odoo therefore means Custom.

**Chat channels take extra setup.** The CRM app page names email, live chat, SMS and VoIP. WhatsApp is a separate app that works only in Odoo Enterprise, needs a WhatsApp Business Platform account at Meta, and sends business-initiated messages from templates Meta has approved. SMS runs on paid In-App Purchase credits.

**Community leaves Enterprise apps out.** Studio, WhatsApp, VoIP, the Android and iOS versions and hosting on Odoo Online or Odoo.sh are Enterprise only. Implementation is extra on every plan: small companies can buy Success Packs from Odoo, and mid-size companies usually hire a partner.

## Where Customermates fits

Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM for small client-facing teams, built in Germany: consultants, agencies, other relationship-driven service businesses and founders.

**Conversations next to the deal.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one list, and you reply on the original channel. Link a participant to a contact once, and every conversation with that address shows up on the contact's timeline. Starter has no inbox.

**Mate does the CRM work.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes threads, drafts replies, updates records and builds views. It uses hosted AI credits, a monthly allowance per active user listed on the [pricing page](/pricing). Mate asks for a fresh approval before every deletion, but sending a message needs none, so tell it to save drafts when you want to read them first. Routines run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes, always as their owner.

**Your own AI client over MCP.** The [MCP server](/docs/mcp) offers [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan and on self-hosted installs. Claude, ChatGPT and other clients connect with OAuth or an API key, act with the connected user's permissions and run on your own AI provider.

**Self-hosting and hosting.** The [self-hosted edition](/features/self-hosted) is the AGPL-3.0 core with REST, webhooks and MCP, run with Docker Compose as two containers, the app and PostgreSQL 16. It has no inbox, no Mate and no hosted credits. Customermates Cloud has its primary database configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, offers a [data processing agreement](/dpa) and lists its subprocessors, US providers among them, on the legal pages. Customermates holds no compliance certification.

## Where Customermates is the wrong choice

- **No ERP functions.** There are no quotations, invoices, accounting, inventory, point of sale or HR. If those must share a database with the CRM, stay with a suite. The guide to [CRM and ERP](/blog/crm-and-erp) explains how teams split the two.
- **No lead scoring, no lead database and no data enrichment.** The weighted pipeline multiplies each deal's value by its stage weight and does not predict anything. If you have LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Pro and higher can search it through your connected account.
- **No mass email, campaigns or sequences.** Messages go out one at a time, written by you or drafted by Mate.
- **No telephony, no SMS, no native mobile apps and no single sign-on.**
- **Tasks have no built-in due date or reminders.** Add a custom date field if you need one.
- **No custom objects.** You get five record types (contacts, organizations, deals, services, tasks) and custom fields of ten types. Saved views are personal, and permissions apply per record type and action, not per field.

## Switching from Odoo CRM

Most Odoo CRM data has a direct home. Under **My Company › Settings**, in the **Data model** section, you can call deals "Opportunities" and organizations "Companies", so your team keeps its vocabulary.

| In Odoo | In Customermates |
|---|---|
| Contacts that are companies | Organizations, renamed "Companies" if you like |
| Contacts that are individuals | Contacts, linked to their organization |
| Opportunities in the pipeline | Deals, renamed "Opportunities" |
| Leads, if you use them | Contacts, with the default "Sales Pipeline" field: New, Contact, Qualified, In Progress, Won, Lost |
| Products on opportunities | Services, renamed "Products", linked to deals with a quantity |
| Scheduled activities | Tasks with assigned users, plus a custom date field for the deadline |
| Odoo tags | A single-select custom field per tag group, one value per field; Customermates has no record tags |
| Quotations and invoices | No equivalent; keep Odoo or your accounting tool for them |

A new workspace starts with the stages Prospecting 10%, Qualification 20%, Demo 40%, Proposal 60%, Negotiation 80%, Won 100% and Lost 0%. Rename them to your Odoo stages and set your own weights. Odoo's per-opportunity probability does not carry over: every deal in a stage gets that stage's weight.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export from Odoo">In each list view, select the records, open Action and choose Export, in the Excel format and not CSV. Customermates imports .xlsx workbooks only, so save an .xls file as .xlsx in Excel or LibreOffice first.</Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the workspace">Choose your record names under Data model, create custom fields for the Odoo columns you keep, and set the stage options and weights.</Step>
  <Step title="Import one record type at a time">Organizations first, then contacts, then deals. Link columns match records by name, so a contact's company column finds the organization you imported before. The [records documentation](/docs/app-records) lists the import limits.</Step>
  <Step title="Rebuild your pipeline view">Create a board of deals grouped by Stage, filtered to the deals where you are in Assigned, and save it as a view. Views are personal, so each person saves their own.</Step>
  <Step title="Connect channels and add a routine">On Pro or during the trial, connect a mailbox, LinkedIn or WhatsApp, then set up a routine in Customermates Cloud like the one below.</Step>
</Steps>

An example routine, scheduled daily at 8:00: "Find my deals in the Proposal stage. For each one, read the latest conversation with its contacts. If the client has not written for seven days, save a short follow-up draft in that thread that refers to the proposal. Do not send anything." The last sentence matters. A routine runs as its owner, with the owner's channels, and sending needs no approval. The [routines documentation](/docs/app-routines) covers schedules and record events.

## Other Odoo CRM alternatives worth a look

Customermates publishes this list. Details come from each vendor's site, checked on 8 October 2026.

- **ERPNext** is the closest open-source suite, covering accounting, sales, CRM, stock, manufacturing, projects, point of sale and HR with payroll. There is no per-user fee: you self-host it or pay for Frappe Cloud hosting, which is priced by compute.
- **Zoho One** bundles more than 40 integrated business apps under one licence, priced either for all employees or for a flexible number of users. It suits teams that want a cloud suite from one vendor and do not need open source.
- **Frappe CRM** is a focused, fully open-source CRM for leads, contacts, deals and organizations, with WhatsApp integration and lead sync from Facebook and Instagram. Frappe prices hosting by compute, from $5 a month with unlimited users.
- **Twenty** is an open-source CRM with unlimited custom objects, AI agents and REST and GraphQL APIs. The Pro plan costs $9 per user per month billed yearly, and the open-source core is free to self-host.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is Odoo CRM free?">
For one app, yes. One App Free gives you the CRM with unlimited users on Odoo Online, and the Community edition with CRM is free to self-host under LGPLv3. A second app on Odoo Online means paying for Standard: from €19.90 per user per month in the first year and from €24.90 after that, plus implementation and In-App Purchase credits where you need them. Customermates has no free cloud plan but a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial, and its self-hosted core is free under AGPL-3.0.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Odoo CRM have an API?">
Yes. Odoo 19 documents a JSON-2 API alongside the older XML-RPC and JSON-RPC endpoints, but on Odoo's subscription plans external API access is listed on Custom only. Customermates includes its REST API, [[derived.webhooks.events.total]] webhook events and the MCP server on every plan.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Which is better, ERPNext or Odoo?">
It depends on how you want to pay and how much you want to run yourself. Both are suites with accounting, inventory and CRM. ERPNext has no per-user fee: you pay for hosting, on your own server or on Frappe Cloud. Odoo charges per user on Standard and Custom, hosting on Odoo Online included, and has a free plan for a single app.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there an open-source alternative to Odoo CRM?">
Yes, several. Odoo Community is open source itself and includes the CRM. If you want a focused open-source CRM rather than a suite, Customermates (AGPL-3.0), Frappe CRM and Twenty can all be self-hosted. ERPNext is the open-source choice if you want a whole suite.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I keep Odoo for accounting and use Customermates as the CRM?">
Yes. If Odoo then runs a single app, such as Invoicing, One App Free covers it. Moving data by hand works through Excel exports. Automatic sync is harder: a workflow in your own n8n instance can react to Customermates webhooks, but writing into a hosted Odoo needs the external API, which Odoo lists on Custom.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

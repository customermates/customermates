> For a small team, HubSpot vs Zoho CRM comes down to what you pay per seat once the entry plan is too small, and whether marketing and service belong in the same system. Once you need more than an entry plan, Zoho CRM costs far less: Professional is €23 per user per month billed yearly, against €90 per seat for HubSpot Professional, which also requires a paid onboarding package. HubSpot gives you a free plan, a Starter plan on a limited-time discount and one platform for sales, marketing and service. But Starter stops at 10 users, and custom reports, custom objects and forecasting all need Professional.
>
> Customermates publishes this comparison and makes a third CRM, described in its own section below. Every HubSpot and Zoho detail comes from the vendors' own websites, checked on 8 October 2026.

## Which one fits which team

| Your situation | Better fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Two or three people who want to start free | Zoho CRM Free or HubSpot Free | Zoho's free edition covers 3 users, HubSpot's free plan 2 |
| A sales team of up to 10 on a tight budget | Zoho CRM Standard, or HubSpot Starter while its time-limited offer runs | Zoho Standard includes workflows, cadences, dashboards and sales forecasting; HubSpot Starter is capped at 10 users |
| Email campaigns, website chat and sales in one tool | HubSpot | Starter already sends email campaigns and runs website chat; Professional adds social media and a help desk |
| Forecasts without paying for a top tier | Zoho CRM Standard | Zoho lists sales forecasting from Standard, HubSpot from Professional |
| Quotes from the CRM | Zoho CRM Professional or HubSpot Professional | Zoho's CPQ and HubSpot's AI-powered quotes both start at Professional |
| WhatsApp next to your deals on a small budget | Zoho CRM, any paid edition | Zoho connects the WhatsApp Business API in every paid edition; HubSpot asks for Marketing Hub or Service Hub Professional |
| An open-source license or your own server | Neither | Neither pricing page lists a self-hosted edition |

Which interface is easier is a judgement, and you can test it without paying. Both vendors have free plans, and Zoho also offers a 15-day trial of its paid editions. Build the same pipeline with your own stages in both and give it a week.

Comparing more than two tools? [How to compare CRM systems](/blog/crm-comparison) has a scorecard, and [Pipedrive vs Zoho](/compare/pipedrive-vs-zoho) covers another common pairing.

## Prices checked on 8 October 2026

Prices per user per month from [HubSpot's pricing page](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/) and [Zoho CRM's pricing page](https://www.zoho.com/crm/zohocrm-pricing.html), in euros. HubSpot showed these euro prices when opened from Germany. Zoho lists the same figures in euros and US dollars.

| Tier | HubSpot | Zoho CRM |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Up to 2 users | Up to 3 users |
| Entry | Starter: €20 list price; time-limited offer €10 monthly or €7 billed yearly (50% and 65% off) | Standard: €20 monthly or €14 billed yearly |
| Middle | Professional: €100 monthly or €90 billed yearly, plus required onboarding | Professional: €35 monthly or €23 billed yearly |
| Upper | Enterprise: €175, plus required onboarding | Enterprise: €50 monthly or €40 billed yearly |
| Top | No further plan | Ultimate: €65 monthly or €52 billed yearly |

Two details change the budget:

- **HubSpot prices depend on your region.** In euros, the pricing page sells Free, Starter, Professional and Enterprise as one platform for sales, marketing and service, with 5,000, 10,000 or 15,000 HubSpot Credits per month on the paid plans. It says Professional and Enterprise require a paid onboarding package but does not show its euro price. In US dollars, HubSpot's [Sales Hub pricing page](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/sales) still sells Sales Hub on its own: Starter at a $20 list price, with a time-limited offer of $7 per seat billed yearly, Professional at $90 billed yearly or $100 monthly plus a one-time $1,500 onboarding fee, and Enterprise from $150 per seat plus $3,500 for onboarding. Open the page from your own region before you budget.
- **Zoho's editions build on each other.** Standard has workflows, AI agents, cadences, reports and dashboards, and sales forecasting. Professional adds CPQ, email intelligence, process automation and inventory management. Enterprise adds an AI sales assistant, territory management, custom functions and client portals. Ultimate raises feature limits and adds consulting and migration support.

On yearly billing, five HubSpot Professional seats cost €5,400 a year before onboarding. Five Zoho CRM Professional seats cost €1,380. At the entry level, Zoho Standard costs €14 per seat billed yearly against €20 for HubSpot Starter at list price; only while HubSpot's time-limited offer runs does the order flip, with Starter at €7. That only helps if Starter covers your process. If you need custom reports, custom objects or forecasts, compare HubSpot Professional with Zoho Standard or Professional instead.

## Where HubSpot is the stronger choice

HubSpot fits teams that want sales, marketing and service in one system. On the euro platform, Starter already sends targeted email campaigns, chats with website visitors, makes and receives calls, and records and transcribes meetings with AI. Professional adds advanced automation, social media management, AI-powered quotes, a help desk, custom objects, custom reports and revenue forecasts across pipelines. Every plan, Free included, connects a Gmail, Outlook or Microsoft Exchange inbox and includes the Breeze Assistant.

If you work with an AI client such as Claude, the HubSpot MCP server gives MCP-compatible tools read and write access to CRM objects, among them contacts, companies and deals.

The limits that matter for small teams:

- The step from Starter to Professional multiplies the seat price and adds onboarding.
- WhatsApp needs Marketing Hub or Service Hub Professional or Enterprise and a WhatsApp Business account. Only messages sent after you connect are synced.

## Where Zoho CRM is the stronger choice

Zoho CRM fits teams that want sales depth at a lower seat price. Forecasting and cadences for scheduled follow-ups are in the lowest paid edition, and CPQ and process automation follow in Professional. You can add custom modules for your own record types; Zoho's edition comparison sets how many each edition allows.

WhatsApp is part of every paid edition and the trial. Zoho connects through the WhatsApp Business API, which needs a Meta business account, a verified business and a phone number that is not used on another product; Zoho's guide notes that businesses are charged per conversation. Zoho MCP connects AI agents to Zoho CRM and to other Zoho apps such as Mail, Calendar, Desk and Projects.

The limits that matter for small teams:

- Features are spread over four paid editions, so check which edition holds each one before you buy.
- The data center follows the country you pick at sign-up, so a European team should check that field.

## Data location and GDPR

- **HubSpot** runs an EU data center in Germany. New accounts get a data center based on their IP address at sign-up, and accounts on Starter, Professional or Enterprise can change it. HubSpot lists cases where data is processed outside the chosen region, among them integrations, usage analytics sent to the USA, subprocessors and support.
- **Zoho** runs its EU data center in Amsterdam, with Dublin as the secondary site. The country chosen at sign-up decides the data center, and that field is prefilled from your IP address.

Neither location makes you compliant on its own. As the controller, you still check the contract terms and subprocessors for your use.

## Moving data between HubSpot, Zoho or another CRM

HubSpot exports records as CSV, XLS or XLSX on every plan, up to 300 exports in a rolling 24 hours. Zoho CRM exports module data as CSV or XLSX in a zip file, up to 50,000 records per XLSX export or 200,000 per CSV export, for users with the Export permission on that module.

If Customermates is the target, export as XLSX and import one record type at a time. Link columns match existing records by name, and new contacts need a first and a last name; the [records guide](/docs/app-records) has the details.

## A third option: Customermates

Customermates publishes this page, so read this section as the vendor's own description.

Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM for small client-facing teams, built in Germany: consultants, agencies, other service businesses and founders. It is narrower than either HubSpot or Zoho. You get contacts, organizations, deals, services and tasks with custom fields, personal saved views and a deal pipeline whose stage probabilities give a weighted pipeline total.

- **Conversations next to the records.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) puts Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram in one list, and you reply on the original channel. WhatsApp connects by scanning a QR code, and the history imports in the background. Each Pro user connects [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] account, each Business user [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedAccountsPerUser]]. Starter has no inbox.
- **AI that does CRM work.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes conversations, drafts replies and updates records. It uses hosted AI credits, a monthly allowance per active user listed on the [pricing page](/pricing). Routines run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes. Your own client, such as Claude or ChatGPT, connects through the [MCP server](/docs/mcp) with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan.
- **Open source.** The AGPL-3.0 core runs on your own server with Docker Compose, including REST, webhooks and MCP, but without the inbox, Mate and hosted credits. In Customermates Cloud the database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, and a [data processing agreement](/dpa) is available.

Five users pay [[commercial.price.starter.monthly.seats.5]] a month on Starter or [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.5]] on Pro, billed monthly only, after a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial.

It is the wrong choice in these cases:

- You send email campaigns or sequences: Customermates has no bulk email and sends one message at a time.
- You need forecasts beyond a weighted pipeline, quotes or record types of your own: Customermates does not offer them.
- You want a free plan or a native mobile app: Customermates has no free plan and no native app, while HubSpot and Zoho have both.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="How much does HubSpot cost per month?">
In euros, HubSpot Free costs nothing for up to 2 users. Starter lists at €20 per seat; during a time-limited offer it costs €10 per seat billed monthly or €7 billed yearly. Professional costs €100 or €90 plus required onboarding, and Enterprise €175. These prices are from HubSpot's pricing page on 8 October 2026. In US dollars, Sales Hub Professional is $90 per seat billed yearly plus a one-time $1,500 onboarding fee.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is Zoho CRM cheaper than HubSpot?">
Yes, at list prices in every paid tier. Only HubSpot's time-limited Starter offer undercuts Zoho Standard, and only while it runs. Billed yearly, Zoho Standard costs €14 per user per month, Professional €23 and Enterprise €40. HubSpot Professional costs €90 per seat plus onboarding.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What are the downsides of HubSpot and Zoho?">
HubSpot's main cost is the step from Starter to Professional: a much higher seat price plus onboarding, and Starter stops at 10 users. WhatsApp also needs a Professional hub. Zoho spreads its features over four paid editions, so CPQ and process automation need Professional and custom functions need Enterprise. Its data center follows the country chosen at sign-up.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Which is better for sales forecasting, HubSpot or Zoho?">
Zoho CRM includes sales forecasting from its Standard edition. HubSpot includes revenue forecasts across pipelines from Professional. Customermates does not forecast: it multiplies each deal value by its stage probability and shows a weighted pipeline total.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Do HubSpot and Zoho work with Claude or ChatGPT?">
Both offer MCP servers. The HubSpot MCP server gives MCP-compatible AI tools read and write access to CRM objects such as contacts, companies and deals. Zoho MCP covers Zoho CRM and other Zoho apps. Customermates includes an MCP server with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan and on self-hosted installs.
</FaqItem>

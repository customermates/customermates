> The right Vtiger alternative depends on which part of Vtiger you actually use. If help desk, invoicing or inventory run in Vtiger today, look at SuiteCRM or Odoo, which cover those areas too. If you self-host the open-source edition and want another self-hosted open-source CRM, look at EspoCRM. For a large cloud CRM with a free tier, look at Zoho CRM. Customermates fits small client-facing teams, such as consultants and agencies, who mostly need contacts, deals and their email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp conversations in one place, kept current by an AI assistant or their own AI client. Customermates publishes this comparison; every Vtiger and third-party detail comes from the vendors' own sites, checked on 8 October 2026.

## Which alternative fits which team

| Your situation | Look at | Why |
|---|---|---|
| You run sales, help desk and invoicing in one system | Stay with Vtiger, or SuiteCRM or Odoo | Customermates has no help desk and does no invoicing |
| You send email campaigns from the CRM | Stay with Vtiger | Campaigns are in every Vtiger plan, even the free one; Customermates sends one message at a time |
| Your salespeople work mainly from their phones | Stay with Vtiger | Vtiger Cloud has iPhone and Android apps; Customermates has no native app |
| You self-host Vtiger and are tired of maintaining PHP and MySQL | Customermates self-hosted, or Vtiger Cloud | Customermates runs as two containers; Vtiger Cloud takes the server off your hands |
| Clients write on WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram or Telegram as well as by email | Customermates Pro or Business | All of these land in one inbox next to the contact, without a messaging provider account of your own |
| You want Claude, ChatGPT or another AI client to read and update the CRM | Customermates, any plan or self-hosted | The MCP server is built in |

## Who Vtiger is a good choice for

Vtiger is organised around five apps: Marketing, Sales, Help Desk, Projects and Inventory. The free One Pilot plan, for up to 10 users and 3,000 records, already includes a deal pipeline, email campaigns of up to 1,000 emails a month, web-to-lead forms, email sequences, workflows, a help desk with SLAs, project tasks with a Gantt view and the mobile app.

One Growth adds invoicing, inventory, sales orders, a customer portal, customizable dashboards and AI features, for up to 15 users and 100,000 records. One Professional adds lead scoring, sales forecasting, live web chat, social ticketing and the Process Designer for approvals and business rules. Single App licences cost less for people who work in one app, for example $20 instead of $30 on Professional, billed yearly.

The open-source edition is a separate product: Vtiger CRM 8.4.0 on PHP 8 with MySQL and Apache, available as bundles for Linux and Windows or as Docker, with a marketplace of more than 200 extensions and language packs.

If your team uses three or more of those apps every day, Vtiger covers ground that Customermates does not.

## Why teams look for a Vtiger alternative

**The open-source edition lacks much of the cloud.** Vtiger's own comparison table marks email marketing, advanced reporting, global search, the custom module builder, the iPhone and Android apps, integrations such as Outlook, Office 365, QuickBooks and Zapier, and Calculus AI as cloud only. Vtiger is candid about the cost as well: "Open source CRM is not free!", because hosting, maintenance, support and upgrades add up. Self-hosted Customermates lacks the inbox, Mate and hosted AI credits; the rest is the same core CRM as Starter, with REST, webhooks and MCP.

**The price depends on billing period and licence mix.** One Professional costs $30 per user per month billed yearly and $42 billed monthly; One Enterprise costs $42 or $58. Local taxes come on top. Customermates bills monthly only, in euros, with one price per plan.

**WhatsApp needs a provider and a higher plan.** Vtiger's WhatsApp integration runs through Twilio, Gupshup, Clickatell, Wablas or imimobile, and Vtiger lists it for One Professional and One Enterprise. In Customermates Pro, each person connects [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] account, which can be a mailbox, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram or Telegram.

**The hosting region follows your trial.** Vtiger runs servers in the US, the UK, the EU (Ireland and Frankfurt), the UAE, Australia, Singapore, Japan and India, and stores your data in the region you were in when you started the trial. If you need a specific region, confirm it with Vtiger first. Customermates Cloud has one: its primary database is configured in AWS Frankfurt. That is a product setting, not a contractual residency guarantee, and the subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the legal pages.

These rows buy different things:

- **One Pilot is hard to beat if it fits.** Five people pay nothing for up to 3,000 records.
- **One Growth and Customermates Starter have similar list prices.** Growth includes help desk, invoicing, campaigns and the mobile app. Starter is the core CRM with Mate on hosted AI credits and [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] routine per user, and it has no inbox.

Hosted AI credits come as a monthly allowance per active user; the [pricing page](/pricing) shows the amounts. Self-hosted, both open-source editions cost nothing in licence fees. You pay for the server and the time for updates, backups and TLS.

## Where Customermates fits

Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM for small client-facing teams: consultants, agencies, other service businesses and founders. It is built in Germany.

**Client conversations next to the deal.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one list, and you reply on the original channel. Link a participant to a contact once, and the conversation is filed with that contact.

**AI that does the CRM work.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes threads, drafts replies, updates records and manages views, using hosted AI credits. It asks for a fresh approval before every deletion. Sending a message runs without one, so tell it to save drafts when you want to review first. [Routines](/docs/app-routines) run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes, always as their owner.

**Your own AI client over MCP.** The [MCP server](/docs/mcp) offers [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan and on self-hosted installs. Claude, ChatGPT and other clients connect with OAuth or an API key and act with the connected user's permissions. Model calls from your client run on your own AI provider and use no hosted credits.

**Self-hosting with two containers.** The [self-hosted edition](/features/self-hosted) runs the AGPL core as the app plus PostgreSQL 16 under Docker Compose.

## Where Customermates is the wrong choice

- **No help desk, tickets, SLAs or customer portal.**
- **No quotes, invoices, sales orders, purchase orders or inventory.**
- **No email campaigns, sequences, web forms or landing pages.** Messages go one at a time, written by you or drafted by Mate.
- **No project management.** You can rename deals to "Projects", but there is no Gantt chart and tasks have no built-in due date. Add a custom date field if you need one.
- **No native mobile apps, telephony or SMS.**
- **No custom modules.** You get five record types and ten field types.
- **No lead scoring or sales forecasting.** The weighted pipeline multiplies each deal value by its stage probability and predicts nothing.
- **No single sign-on.** Saved views are personal, and permissions apply per record type and action, not per field.

If two or more of these are daily work for your team, stay with Vtiger or look at SuiteCRM or Odoo.

## Moving from Vtiger

Most Vtiger sales data has a direct home. Under Data model in the company settings, you can rename organizations to "Accounts" and deals to "Opportunities", so your team keeps its vocabulary.

| In Vtiger | In Customermates |
|---|---|
| Organizations | Organizations, or renamed "Accounts" |
| Contacts | Contacts |
| Leads | Contacts, with the default "Sales Pipeline" single-select set to New |
| Deals | Deals, or renamed "Opportunities" |
| Products and services | Services, or renamed "Products", linked to deals with quantities |
| Tasks | Tasks, plus a custom date field for due dates |
| Cases, quotes, invoices, projects, campaigns | No equivalent; keep Vtiger or a dedicated tool for them |

A new Customermates workspace starts with the stages Prospecting 10%, Qualification 20%, Demo 40%, Proposal 60%, Negotiation 80%, Won 100% and Lost 0%. Rename them to match your Vtiger stages and adjust the probabilities in the company settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export from Vtiger">In each module's list view, choose Export and export all data. Vtiger exports CSV files, not Excel.</Step>
  <Step title="Save each file as XLSX">Customermates imports XLSX workbooks only. Open each export in Excel or LibreOffice and save it as an `.xlsx` file.</Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the workspace">Choose your record names under Data model, create custom fields for the Vtiger fields you keep, and set the stage names and probabilities.</Step>
  <Step title="Import one record type at a time">Use Add from file: organizations first, then contacts, then deals. Link columns are matched by record name, so a contact's organization column finds the organization you imported before. The [records documentation](/docs/app-records) lists the limits.</Step>
  <Step title="Connect channels and AI">On Pro or during the trial, connect a mailbox, LinkedIn or WhatsApp, then set up a routine or connect your own AI client.</Step>
</Steps>

## Other Vtiger alternatives worth a look

Customermates publishes this list. Details come from each vendor's site, checked on 8 October 2026.

- **SuiteCRM** comes closest to Vtiger's breadth in open source. You can host it on your own server, and SuiteCRM 8 is the current major version. Its site highlights quotations, reporting and dashboards, marketing automation and customer service. SuiteCRM Hosted has a free trial, and SuiteASSURED is a supported build with a warranty and care hours for professional services.
- **EspoCRM** is an open-source CRM under AGPLv3 that you can download and self-host for free. EspoCRM Cloud Basic costs €12.90 per user per month with at least 3 users, billed for 6 months or a year. The [EspoCRM comparison](/compare/espocrm-alternative) goes into detail.
- **Zoho CRM** is a cloud CRM with a free edition for up to 3 users and the paid editions Standard, Professional, Enterprise and Ultimate; yearly plans save up to 34%. See the [Zoho CRM comparison](/compare/zoho-crm-alternative).
- **Odoo** fits when CRM, accounting and stock should run in one system. One App Free covers one app with unlimited users on Odoo Online, Standard includes all apps, Custom adds hosting on Odoo.sh or on premise, and the Community edition is a free open-source download. See the [Odoo comparison](/compare/odoo).

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is Vtiger CRM free?">
Two parts of it are. One Pilot is a free cloud plan for up to 10 users and 3,000 records. The open-source edition is free to download under the Vtiger Public License 1.1, but you run the server, and features such as email marketing and the mobile apps are cloud only. Paid plans start with One Growth at $12 per user per month, billed yearly.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a 100% free CRM?">
Free in licence fees, yes. Vtiger One Pilot is free up to 10 users and Zoho CRM's free edition up to 3. Open-source CRMs such as Customermates, SuiteCRM, EspoCRM and Vtiger's open-source edition cost nothing to license when you host them yourself, but you pay for the server and the time to keep it updated and backed up.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What is the best open-source alternative to Vtiger?">
It depends on what you use Vtiger for. SuiteCRM is closest in breadth, with quotations, marketing and customer service. EspoCRM is free to self-host; some official extensions, such as those included in its cloud plans, are sold separately. Customermates fits small teams that mainly need contacts, deals and client conversations in one place.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Which languages does Customermates support?">
The app interface comes in English, German, French, Italian and Spanish, and the documentation in English and German. The core is open source under AGPL-3.0. For its open-source edition, Vtiger lists more than 30 languages.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I move my Vtiger data to Customermates?">
Yes, one record type at a time. Export each Vtiger list view, save the file as XLSX, and import organizations, contacts and deals in that order so links resolve by name. Cases, quotes, invoices and campaigns have no equivalent.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can Customermates replace Vtiger's help desk or invoicing?">
No. Customermates covers contacts, organizations, deals, services and tasks, plus the unified inbox from Pro. If you rely on ticketing, invoicing or inventory, keep Vtiger or choose SuiteCRM or Odoo. Those tools can still receive CRM events from Customermates through webhooks and the REST API.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

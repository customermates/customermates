> Pick Pipedrive if selling is the main job of your CRM: it is built around the pipeline and the next scheduled activity, and its Growth plan adds email sync, automations and sequences. Pick monday CRM if your team already runs client projects on monday.com boards and wants deals in the same account. On yearly billing, monday CRM is cheaper per seat at every listed tier, but it bills at least three seats and its entry plans have low caps: Basic allows 5 columns per board and 1,000 active contacts and deals in total. Customermates publishes this comparison and describes its own product in a separate section below.

## Which one fits your team

| Your situation | Better fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Your salespeople work from a list of calls and meetings | Pipedrive | Pipelines, activities and the calendar are on every plan |
| Client projects already run on monday.com | monday CRM | Deals sit in the same account as the delivery boards |
| One or two people | Pipedrive Lite | No seat minimum; monday CRM bills at least 3 seats |
| Email sequences at the lowest seat price | monday CRM Pro | Sequences and mass email at €28 per seat billed yearly; Pipedrive's sequences start on Growth at €39 |
| Thousands of contacts, fewer deals | Pipedrive | Its limit counts leads and deals only; monday CRM counts contacts and deals together |
| Deal records with many fields | Pipedrive Growth or monday CRM Pro | Growth allows 100 custom fields per company; monday CRM caps a board at 5 columns on Basic and 15 on Standard |
| Web forms, chatbot or e-signatures | Pipedrive Premium | LeadBooster and contracts with e-signatures are included from Premium |
| EU residency that also binds subprocessors | monday CRM, top tier | monday.com promises region-bound EU residency for Enterprise accounts only |

## Sales CRM or work platform

Pipedrive is a sales CRM. Its records are leads, deals, people and organizations, and the pipeline view with scheduled activities is the center of the product. Pipedrive says every plan includes unlimited pipelines. Post-sale work is extra: Projects is an add-on from €16 on Lite and Growth and is included from Premium.

monday CRM is monday.com's sales product, built on the same board platform as monday work management. Leads, contacts and deals are items on boards, and every field is a column. That makes a pipeline quick to reshape, and it lets the plan decide how far you can go. monday.com describes the CRM as covering pre- and post-sales on one platform. For a team that hands won deals to delivery on monday.com, that is the main argument.

If your search for "monday" meant monday work management, the general project tool: it has a free plan for up to 2 seats, but monday CRM is a separate product with its own price list.

## Plan limits side by side

Both vendors cap usage per plan, but they count different things.

| Limit | Pipedrive Lite / Growth / Premium / Ultimate | monday CRM Basic / Standard / Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Records | Leads and deals: 2,500 / 5,000 / 15,000 / 20,000 per seat, at most 300,000 | Active contacts and deals together: 1,000 / 10,000 / 100,000 |
| Fields | 30 / 100 / 300 / 500 custom fields per company | 5 / 15 / 75 columns per board |
| Automations | None / 50 / 150 / 250 automations you can set up | Not listed / 250 / 25,000 custom automations a month |
| Synced email accounts per user | None / 1 / 3 / 5 | Gmail and Outlook integrations |
| Email sequences | None / 5 / 25 / 50 | Pro only |
| Reports | 15 / 50 / 250 / 500 Insights reports | 1 / 5 / 50 custom dashboards |

For five seats on the entry plans, Pipedrive Lite holds 12,500 leads and deals, and Pipedrive sets no limit for contacts. monday CRM Basic holds 1,000 contacts and deals in total. When Pipedrive hits a limit, your data stays, but you cannot add more by hand until you archive, buy a top-up or upgrade.

The automation caps also measure different things. Pipedrive limits how many automations you have. monday CRM limits how many run each month, so a busy month can use up Standard's 250.

## Email, AI and integrations

**Email.** Pipedrive syncs Gmail or Outlook from Growth; Lite has no email sync. monday CRM names Gmail and Outlook among its integrations and gathers emails, calls and meetings in a comms hub from Standard.

**AI.** Pipedrive includes AI-assisted reports on every plan and AI email tools from Premium. monday CRM includes AI agents from Standard for outreach calls, lead sourcing, meeting prep and note taking, paid from a package of AI credits, and Pro adds an AI workflow builder.

**Integrations.** Pipedrive lists more than 500 integrations on its pricing page. monday CRM names Outlook, Gmail, Zoom, QuickBooks, Google Workspace and Docusign "and hundreds more".

## Data location and GDPR

Pipedrive hosts on AWS in Sydney, Montreal, Frankfurt, Dublin, London, US East and US West. New EU sign-ups are hosted in an EU region. Pipedrive's help center adds that an account can still end up in a different data center, for example through IP mapping, and that support will tell you where yours is.

monday.com hosts in the US, the EU (Germany) and Australia. Only Enterprise accounts in the EU, the tier the CRM pricing page now calls Ultimate, get region-bound residency in which subprocessors also keep the data in the EU. On other plans, monday.com may use subprocessors that host parts of the data outside the assigned region. A data processing addendum is available.

On the plans small teams buy, both normally host an EU account's main data in the EU. Neither promises that every copy stays there.

## Connecting Pipedrive to monday.com, or moving over

**Using both.** Pipedrive's help center documents a monday.com integration that creates a monday.com item when a new deal, person, organization or activity is added in Pipedrive. For deals it carries organization, contact person, stage, status, value and owner. The sync runs one way, from Pipedrive to monday.com, so edits in monday.com do not flow back, and the article describes creating items for new records, not updating them later. That is enough to start a delivery item per new deal. It is not a way to run one pipeline in two tools.

**Moving from Pipedrive to monday CRM.** In Pipedrive, open Tools and apps, then Export data, and export each item type as Excel or CSV. Only global admins and users with export permission can do this, and each person exports only what they can see. monday.com says you can bring Pipedrive data into monday CRM workflows, and its Ultimate tier includes a white-glove migration. On the other plans you import the exported files yourself. Export before you cancel, because a cancelled Pipedrive account loses access at the end of the billing cycle.

## A third option: Customermates

Customermates publishes this page, so read this section as a vendor describing its own product. Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM for small client-facing teams such as consultants, agencies, other service businesses and founders, built in Germany. Like Pipedrive, it is a CRM first: contacts, organizations, deals, services and tasks are separate, linked record types with custom fields of ten types and no plan-based record caps. Where it differs from both:

- **Conversations next to the deal.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) puts Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram in one list linked to your contacts, and you reply on the original channel. Each Pro user connects [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] account, each Business user [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedAccountsPerUser]]. Starter has no inbox.
- **AI that keeps the records current.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes threads, drafts replies and updates records. It uses hosted AI credits, a monthly allowance per active user listed on the [pricing page](/pricing). Routines run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes. Claude, ChatGPT or another AI client can work with the CRM through MCP on every plan.
- **Hosting choice.** In Customermates Cloud the database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region. The AGPL-3.0 core also runs on your own server with Docker Compose; a [self-hosted install](/features/self-hosted) has REST, webhooks and MCP but no inbox and no Mate.
- **Monthly prices per user, no seat minimum.** Five people pay [[commercial.price.starter.monthly.seats.5]] a month on Starter or [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.5]] on Pro.

A four-person consultancy coming from either tool could set it up like this:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Import one record type at a time">Export organizations, people and deals from Pipedrive as Excel, not CSV. Import organizations first, then contacts, then deals, because link columns are matched by record name. The [records documentation](/docs/app-records) lists the file limits.</Step>
  <Step title="Weight the stages">Give the deal stage field probabilities such as Lead 10%, Discovery call 25%, Proposal sent 50%, Negotiation 75% and Won 100%. A deal's value comes from its linked services and quantities, and the weighted pipeline is value times stage probability. That is a fixed calculation, not a prediction.</Step>
  <Step title="Add fields and views">Add a single-select field "Source" with Referral, LinkedIn, Website and Event. Each person saves a board view of the deals assigned to them, grouped by stage. Saved views are personal, so everyone saves their own.</Step>
  <Step title="Set up a weekly routine (Customermates Cloud)">Every Monday at 08:00: "List my deals in Proposal sent. For each, read the latest conversation with the linked contact and save a short follow-up draft in that thread. Do not send anything." A routine runs as its owner, with that owner's channels, and on Pro each user gets [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]] routines.</Step>
</Steps>

Customermates does not fit if you need email sequences or bulk email, web forms, e-signatures, telephony, lead scoring or a rule-based automation builder. Tasks have no built-in due date and no reminders (a custom date field can hold a due date, but nothing alerts you), so a team that works from Pipedrive's activity list will miss it. There are no native mobile apps, while Pipedrive and monday.com both offer iOS and Android apps. And if delivery should live on project boards, monday CRM covers that and Customermates does not. There is no free plan, only a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is monday CRM better than Pipedrive for customizable pipelines?">
It is more flexible in shape and more limited in size. A monday CRM pipeline is a board you design column by column, but Basic allows 5 columns and Standard 15, so a detailed deal record needs Pro. Pipedrive says every plan has unlimited pipelines, and Growth allows 100 custom fields. Teams that restructure often will find monday CRM easier; teams with many fields per deal get further with Pipedrive Growth.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Which is better for project management and sales in one tool?">
monday CRM, if delivery happens on boards. monday.com builds the CRM on the same platform as its work management product, so sales and delivery share one account. Pipedrive's answer is its Projects add-on, from €16 below Premium and included from Premium, which keeps project work inside the CRM.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What are the downsides of Pipedrive?">
For a small team, mostly the plan step. Lite has no email sync, automations or sequences, so a team that wants its email in the CRM needs Growth at €39 per seat billed yearly. Records, custom fields and automations are capped per plan, and there is no self-hosted edition.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How do I cancel Pipedrive without losing data?">
Export first: Tools and apps, then Export data, one item type at a time. Then a user with account settings access opens the Billing tab and selects "Cancel my subscription" at the bottom. You keep access until the end of the billing cycle and lose it after that. Pipedrive deletes cancelled accounts after six months.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What are the main alternatives to Pipedrive and monday CRM?">
Other CRMs worth comparing with both are HubSpot, Zoho CRM and Salesforce. The comparisons [HubSpot vs Pipedrive](/compare/hubspot-vs-pipedrive) and [Pipedrive vs Zoho](/compare/pipedrive-vs-zoho) use the same method as this page.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

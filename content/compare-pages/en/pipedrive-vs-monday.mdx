> Pipedrive and Monday CRM are two of the most popular CRM choices for small and mid-sized sales teams, but they come from different worlds. Pipedrive was built as a sales CRM from the start: pipeline management is its core identity, with activity-based selling and deal-centric workflows. Monday CRM grew out of Monday.com's project management platform, adding sales features as an expansion to coordinate teams across broader business operations. Both are capable within their strengths, but both limit functionality by plan tier and both bring pricing trade-offs as teams grow. Customermates offers a third option: a purpose-built CRM with every core feature included from [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]]/user/month, open-source and self-hostable. AI clients such as Claude or ChatGPT operate the CRM through MCP and take over the data entry, and on Pro and above a unified inbox brings email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Telegram into one place.

## Sales CRM vs. project management CRM

This distinction matters more than any feature comparison. Pipedrive thinks in deals, activities, and pipeline stages. Every screen, workflow, and metric is designed around moving deals forward. The platform originated from salespeople solving their own problem: how to keep track of what needs to happen next to close a deal.

Monday CRM thinks in boards, columns, and automations. CRM functionality is layered onto a project management interface designed for cross-team coordination. Modern platforms like Monday.com allow smooth coordination between teams, but the CRM layer is secondary to the project management foundation.

For sales teams, this means Pipedrive feels native: deal tracking, activity scheduling, email integration, and sales reporting are first-class features. Monday CRM feels flexible: you can build almost any workflow with boards, but CRM-specific depth is limited.

Customermates takes the dedicated CRM approach. Like Pipedrive, contacts, organizations, and deals are native concepts. Unlike Pipedrive, every core CRM feature is included in every plan starting at [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]]/user/month, and the platform is open-source, so you can run it on your own server.

## What are the cons of Pipedrive?

Pipedrive is a strong sales CRM, but several limitations matter as teams grow:

1. **Feature gating by tier**: The Lite plan (€14/seat, annual) is deliberately limited: 2,500 open deals per seat, no two-way email sync, no automations. Both require Growth (€39/seat). Custom scoring requires Premium (€59/seat). Meaningful CRM functionality starts at €39 per seat per month.

2. **Add-ons**: LeadBooster, Smart Docs, and Projects are paid add-ons on Lite and Growth and only included from Premium; Campaigns and Web Visitors are paid add-ons on every plan. LeadBooster alone costs from €32.50 per month billed annually.

3. **Deal limits on lower tiers**: Lite allows 2,500 open deals per seat. Growth raises it to 5,000 and Premium to 15,000. Ultimate (€79/seat) reaches the highest limit (20,000 per seat, up to 300,000 per company). For high-volume sales teams, this creates artificial constraints.

4. **Plan-bound support**: phone support starts on Premium, and extended phone support on Ultimate.

5. **No self-hosting**: Like most SaaS CRMs, Pipedrive offers no option for on-premise deployment or self-hosted instances.

Customermates addresses these limitations with no core-feature tier gating, no add-ons, no plan-based record caps, and a self-hostable open-source core.

## Feature comparison

### Pipeline management

Pipedrive's pipeline view is its flagship feature. Visual deal stages, drag-and-drop management, probability-weighted forecast reports (from Growth), and activity-based selling are deeply integrated. Rotting deals are highlighted when they stagnate, and lost reasons help teams learn from missed deals. It is one of the best pipeline experiences in the CRM market.

Monday CRM provides pipeline views through Kanban boards. The visual experience is clean and customizable, but it lacks some of the sales-specific depth that Pipedrive offers natively.

Customermates delivers the pipeline as one configurable stage column with a Kanban board. Each stage carries a probability, so deal values and pipeline totals are weighted automatically. This is a deterministic calculation, not predictive forecasting. If you run different kinds of deals, separate them with a custom single-select field plus saved views or grouping.

### Contact and deal management

Pipedrive provides solid contact and organization management with deal-contact linking, activity tracking, and contact timelines. Open deals are limited by plan: 2,500 per seat on Lite, 5,000 on Growth, 15,000 on Premium, 20,000 on Ultimate.

Monday CRM stores contacts as board items, capped at 1,000, 10,000, or 100,000 active contacts and deals depending on the plan. Relationship mapping between contacts, organizations, and deals is basic.

Customermates provides contacts, organizations, deals, services, and tasks as native linked records with typed custom columns and an activity history. There are no plan-based record caps.

### Email integration

Pipedrive offers two-way email sync from the Growth plan (€39/seat) onward, including email tracking, templates, and email scheduling. The Lite plan (€14) has no two-way sync, which limits sales teams.

Monday CRM offers email integration with Gmail and Outlook, but communication features are less central than in a dedicated sales CRM.

Customermates connects Gmail, Outlook, or IMAP mailboxes to the unified inbox starting on Pro ([[commercial.price.pro.monthly]]/user/month), next to LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Telegram; every other core CRM feature is already available on Starter ([[commercial.price.starter.monthly]]/user/month).

### Automation

Pipedrive unlocks workflow automation starting with the Growth plan. Automations include deal stage changes, activity creation, email sending, and webhook triggers. The number of active automations is capped by tier (50 on Growth, 150 on Premium, 250 on Ultimate).

Monday CRM includes custom automations from the Standard plan, capped at 250 actions per month. Pro raises the cap to 25,000. Automations are visual and easy to create but limited in scope compared to dedicated CRM automation.

Customermates has no built-in rules engine. Automation runs through webhooks on CRM events, the REST API, a community node for your own separately operated [n8n](https://n8n.io/) instance, and AI clients over MCP. In the managed cloud, routines let the hosted assistant run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a CRM event happens; they use the plan's hosted AI credits.

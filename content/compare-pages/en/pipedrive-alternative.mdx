> The right Pipedrive alternatives depend on what pushes you away. If Growth is the price of getting email, WhatsApp and automations into the CRM, compare HubSpot, Zoho CRM and Customermates; if you want an open-source licence or your own server, look at Twenty or Customermates. Stay with Pipedrive if your team works from scheduled activities, email sequences, web forms or a phone app, because Customermates has none of them. Customermates publishes this comparison; every Pipedrive detail comes from pipedrive.com and was checked on 9 November 2026.

## What is the best alternative to Pipedrive?

It depends on the job you hired Pipedrive for. This table matches the usual reasons to the CRM that fits them.

| Your situation | Better fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Your team works from a daily list of scheduled calls and meetings | Pipedrive | Activities, notifications and calendar sync are on every plan; Customermates has no built-in task due dates or reminders |
| You send email sequences or campaigns from the CRM | Pipedrive Growth or higher, or folk Premium | Pipedrive sequences start on Growth and Campaigns is an add-on; folk lists email sequences on Premium; Customermates has no sequences or campaigns |
| You capture leads on your website or send contracts for e-signature | Pipedrive Premium | LeadBooster and contracts with e-signatures are included from Premium; Customermates has no forms, quotes or e-signatures |
| Your salespeople work from their phones, or you need single sign-on | Pipedrive | Native iOS and Android apps and single sign-on on every plan; Customermates has no native app or SSO and runs in the mobile browser |
| Clients write on WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram or Telegram as well as email | Customermates Pro or Business | All of these channels share one inbox and link to the contact |
| You prospect on LinkedIn and sell through relationships | folk | Its Standard plan lists a LinkedIn extension, email, calendar and WhatsApp sync and contact enrichment |
| You want monthly billing without a surcharge | Customermates | Monthly is its only billing cycle; Pipedrive charges more per seat when you pay monthly instead of yearly |
| You need an open-source licence or want to host the CRM yourself | Customermates or Twenty | Both run on your own server (Customermates without its inbox and Mate); Pipedrive sells hosted plans only |
| One to three people and no budget | HubSpot's free plan or Zoho CRM's free edition | Free for up to two users and up to three users; neither Pipedrive nor Customermates has a free plan |

## Who is Pipedrive the better choice for?

Pipedrive is built around the visual pipeline and the next scheduled activity. Every plan, Lite included, has customizable pipelines, activity and calendar management with notifications, a leads inbox, more than 500 marketplace integrations, AI-assisted report creation and an official MCP server for ChatGPT, Claude and other AI assistants. Native iOS and Android apps, single sign-on, device history and audit log, and two-factor authentication come with all four plans. Pipedrive Nova, its meeting intelligence tool, records calls and writes summaries and transcripts on every plan, at no additional cost.

Growth adds what most teams buy Pipedrive for: two-way email sync with open and click tracking, automations with if/else steps, email sequences, a meeting scheduler, forecast reports and the WhatsApp integration, which Pipedrive still labels a beta. Premium adds LeadBooster for capturing and routing leads, custom scoring, company data enrichment, AI email writing and summaries, a team inbox, and contracts with e-signatures. Ultimate adds security alerts and rules, a sandbox account, contact data enrichment and extended phone support hours.

## Why do teams switch from Pipedrive?

We cannot see why individual customers leave. Pipedrive's own pages show where a small team's costs and limits grow fastest.

**Most of the daily work starts on Growth.** Two-way email sync, WhatsApp and automations are not part of Lite. A team that wants its email in the CRM pays for Growth: €39 per seat per month billed yearly, or €49 on monthly billing. AI email writing, a shared team inbox and more than one synced mailbox per person start on Premium, at €59 or €79.

**Every plan has usage limits.** Pipedrive caps leads and deals per company at a multiple of your seats: 2,500 per seat on Lite, 5,000 on Growth, 15,000 on Premium and 20,000 on Ultimate, never more than 300,000. The count includes active leads and open, won and lost deals that are not archived or deleted. At the limit, Pipedrive keeps your data but blocks adding more by hand until you archive, buy a top-up or upgrade. Top-ups exist, but Pipedrive's top-up article says the price depends on your billing frequency and shows it only when you select the add-on.

**Add-ons come on top.** LeadBooster and Smart Docs (each listed from €32.50) and Projects (from €16) are paid add-ons on Lite and Growth and included from Premium. Campaigns (from €13.33) and Web Visitors (from €41) cost extra on every plan. These are starting prices on Pipedrive's pricing page, which names no billing cycle for them.

**The region follows your signup.** Pipedrive runs on AWS in seven regions, Frankfurt and Dublin among them, and hosts new EU accounts primarily in the EU region. Its help center notes that an account can still end up in a different data center; support tells you where yours is. There is no self-hosted edition.

**Email and WhatsApp are in the plans, other channels are not.** Pipedrive syncs email and connects a WhatsApp Business account. Meta then requires an approved template to open a conversation when the contact has not written in the past 24 hours, and WhatsApp charges for each template message. LinkedIn, Instagram and Telegram conversations do not appear in Pipedrive's plan comparison; whether a Marketplace app covers them is outside this comparison.

## Is there a free or open-source alternative to Pipedrive?

Neither Pipedrive nor Customermates has a free plan: Pipedrive offers a 14-day trial without a credit card, Customermates a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial. Free plans exist elsewhere. HubSpot's free plan covers up to two users with no credit card, and Zoho CRM's free edition covers up to three users.

If you want a licence you can run yourself, two open-source CRMs are worth a look. Twenty says self-hosting its open-source core is free, and the AGPL-3.0 core of Customermates runs on your own server with Docker Compose. A self-hosted Customermates install has no inbox, no Mate and no hosted credits. The [free CRM guide](/blog/free-crm) shows where free plans stop, and the [open-source CRM guide](/blog/open-source-crm) compares self-hosted options.

## Where Customermates fits (our product)

Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM for small client-facing teams: consultants, agencies, other service businesses and founders. It is built in Germany.

**One inbox for every channel.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn (including Sales Navigator and Recruiter), WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one list, and you reply on the original channel. WhatsApp connects when you scan a QR code with your own account. Link a participant to a contact once, and every conversation with that address or handle shows on the contact's timeline. Starter has no inbox.

**AI that does the data entry.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes threads, drafts replies, updates records and builds saved views. It uses hosted AI credits, a monthly allowance per active user listed on the pricing page, with no top-ups or overage charges. Mate asks for a fresh approval before every deletion but sends messages without one, so tell it to save drafts when you want to review first. Routines run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes.

Pipedrive has an MCP server too. The difference is reach: the Customermates [MCP server](/docs/mcp) offers [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan and on self-hosted installs, and on Pro or higher in Customermates Cloud they also read inbox threads and save message drafts. Model calls run on your own AI provider.

**Open source, with a hosting choice.** The core is AGPL-3.0 and runs on your own server with Docker Compose and PostgreSQL 16, including REST, webhooks and MCP. A [self-hosted install](/features/self-hosted) has no inbox, no Mate and no hosted credits. In Customermates Cloud the database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, a DPA is available, and the subprocessors, US providers included, are listed on the legal pages. Customermates holds no compliance certification. The audit log is part of every plan.

## Where Customermates is the wrong choice

Check these before you start a trial:

- **No activity calendar.** A tasks list is an ordinary list with a name, notes and relationships; due dates and statuses are fields you add yourself. There are no built-in due dates, no reminders and no notification centre. For a team that works from Pipedrive's activity list, this is the biggest change.
- **No sequences, bulk email, web forms, quotes, e-signatures, telephony, call recording, enrichment or lead scoring.** Outreach goes one message at a time, written by you or drafted by Mate. Invoices stay in your accounting software; won deals can go by webhook to an n8n instance you run separately and on from there.
- **No rule builder.** Routines are AI instructions, not if/then rules. For deterministic workflows, use webhooks, the REST API or an n8n instance you run separately.
- **No native mobile apps and no SSO.** Permissions apply per list and action, not per field.
- **One stage list for all deals.** If you run several Pipedrive pipelines, add a single choice field for the pipeline and save one board view per pipeline. The stages stay shared.

## A worked setup: a five-person consultancy leaving Pipedrive

Take a consultancy with three partners and two associates. The partners prospect on LinkedIn and close by email. The associates answer delivery questions on WhatsApp. In Pipedrive they run Growth with one pipeline.

| In Pipedrive | In Customermates |
|---|---|
| People | Contacts |
| Organizations | Organizations |
| Deals and their owner | Deals and their assigned users |
| Pipeline stages | The Stage field, one option per stage, each with a win probability |
| Products | Services, renamed "Packages" if that fits |
| Labels | A single choice custom field, such as "Source" |
| Activities | Tasks, with a custom date field where the date matters |
| Notes | The record's notes field |
| Files | Not imported; add them to a File field afterwards. Email attachments stay in inbox threads |

They rename the options of the Stage field to Lead, Qualified, Proposal sent, Negotiation, Won and Lost, and in Configure for Deals give them probabilities of 10, 25, 50, 75, 100 and 0 percent. Customermates multiplies each deal's value by its stage probability, and a chart widget on the Dashboard sums the weighted values. That is arithmetic on their own numbers and does not predict anything.

The partners need LinkedIn and email each, so the workspace runs Business: [[commercial.price.business.monthly.seats.5]] a month for five people. If one channel per person were enough, Pro at [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.5]] would do.

Each partner owns one routine. It runs as that partner, so it sees their deals and saves drafts in their channels, and it uses hosted AI credits in Customermates Cloud:

> Every weekday at 7:30, list my open deals that nobody has updated in 14 days, with the last message from each contact. For deals in "Proposal sent", save a short follow-up draft in my latest conversation with the contact. Do not send anything.

## How do you replace Pipedrive with Customermates in five steps?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export from Pipedrive">Open Tools and apps › Export data and download organizations, people, deals and products as Excel files, one item type each. Notes and activities are separate exports. Only global admins and users with export permission can do this.</Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the workspace">Set up the Stage field as in the example above, create single choice fields for your labels and add custom fields for everything else you keep. Sixteen field types are available, among them money, date, web address and address; phone numbers and email addresses go into the Contact details field.</Step>
  <Step title="Import one list at a time">Under More actions › Add from file, import organizations first, then contacts, then services and deals. A file holds up to 10 MB and a sheet up to 5,000 rows, so split large files; both of Pipedrive's export formats, Excel and CSV, import as they are. Link columns match records by name, so a deal's organization column finds the organization you imported before. New contacts need a first and a last name. Map email and phone columns to the contact's Contact details field; contact details are added to new contacts only.</Step>
  <Step title="Carry over notes and open activities">Each record has one notes field. Map a notes column during import, or let an AI client over MCP append the exported notes to the matching records. Open activities can come in as tasks, with a custom date field for the date.</Step>
  <Step title="Connect channels, then cancel Pipedrive">On Pro or during the trial, connect a mailbox, LinkedIn or WhatsApp under Settings › Channels. Cancel Pipedrive only after your team has checked the imported records. The [records documentation](/docs/app-records) explains every step of the import wizard.</Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is there a free version of Pipedrive?">
No. Pipedrive's pricing page lists four paid plans, Lite, Growth, Premium and Ultimate, plus a 14-day free trial without a credit card. Lite costs €24 per seat per month on monthly billing, or €14 when paid once a year. Customermates has no free plan either; it offers a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What are the downsides of Pipedrive?">
They sit in the plan structure. Email sync, WhatsApp (still a beta) and automations start on Growth, every plan caps leads and deals per seat, and several add-ons cost extra below Premium. Pipedrive is hosted only, in a region assigned at signup. If none of that affects your team, Pipedrive is a solid choice.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a cheaper alternative to Pipedrive?">
Often, for the same job. Customermates Starter at [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per user per month costs less than Pipedrive Lite, and Pro at [[commercial.price.pro.monthly]] less than Growth. Once each person needs two channels, Customermates Business costs more than Pipedrive Premium billed yearly. Zoho CRM Standard lists €14 billed yearly and €20 billed monthly on Zoho's euro page, close to Pipedrive Lite's €14 and €24. HubSpot's free plan costs nothing for up to two users.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Which is better, Pipedrive or HubSpot?">
Pipedrive if you want a CRM built around the pipeline and the next activity, with four per-seat plans and no free tier. HubSpot if two free users are enough for now and you accept a one-time onboarding purchase when you move up to Professional. The [HubSpot vs Pipedrive comparison](/compare/hubspot-vs-pipedrive) goes through both.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I move my data from Pipedrive to Customermates?">
Yes. Export each item type from Pipedrive as an Excel file and import it into Customermates one list at a time: organizations, contacts, deals. Files and email history stay behind. Once you connect a mailbox, conversations show on a contact when the address matches one in the contact's contact details.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Customermates have automations like Pipedrive?">
Not the same kind. Pipedrive's automations are rules with triggers, delays and if/else steps, from Growth. Customermates has no rule builder; its routines run a saved instruction for Mate on a schedule or when a record changes, in Customermates Cloud with hosted AI credits: [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] per user on Starter and [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]] on Pro. For deterministic workflows there are webhooks, the REST API and a community node for your own n8n.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

> [Pipedrive](https://www.pipedrive.com/) is a well-known, sales-focused CRM built around visual pipeline management. It has earned a reputation for being more approachable than enterprise platforms like Salesforce. However, its plan-based deal limits, plan-gated features, and add-ons make it expensive as teams grow. Customermates is an open-source Pipedrive alternative built around two things: AI clients (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini) that operate the CRM through MCP and take over the data entry, and, on Pro and above, a unified inbox for email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Telegram. On top of that: transparent tiers from [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]]/user/month, no plan-based record caps, and the option to self-host.

## Why Switch from Pipedrive?

Teams switch from Pipedrive for several recurring reasons. The most common complaints include hitting deal limits on lower plans, paying for add-ons below the Premium tier, no automations unless you upgrade, and the wish to control where the data lives. If any of these sound familiar, Customermates addresses them directly.

Customermates is built on a different philosophy: every core CRM feature is available in every plan, starting at [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]]/user/month. There are no plan-based record caps, no core-feature tiers to navigate, and no add-on upsells. [Workflow automation](/features/workflow-automation) runs through your own separately operated n8n instance, webhooks, and, in the managed cloud, routines. The managed cloud database runs in the AWS Frankfurt region, and the open-source core can be self-hosted.

## Setup and Onboarding

Customermates' managed cloud is ready as soon as you sign up. Configure your [deal pipeline](/features/pipeline) stages, import your [contacts](/features/contact-management) from XLSX workbooks, and begin working. The interface is designed to be understood without lengthy training.

Pipedrive is considered user-friendly compared to enterprise CRMs, but onboarding still requires meaningful time investment. The system is tightly built around its sales pipeline model. Teams with workflows that extend beyond a classic B2B sales process often find themselves adapting to Pipedrive's structure rather than the other way around. Users frequently report that the learning curve is steeper than expected, particularly when configuring automations and custom fields.

## Feature Comparison

Both systems cover CRM fundamentals: [contact management](/features/contact-management), [deal pipelines](/features/pipeline), [task management](/features/task-management), and [email integration](/features/email-integration) (Pro+ on Customermates). Pipedrive adds specialized sales features like forecast reports, custom scoring, and AI email tools, spread across its higher plans.

The critical distinction is flexibility. Pipedrive caps open deals on every plan: 2,500 per seat on Lite, 5,000 on Growth, 15,000 on Premium, and 20,000 on Ultimate, with a maximum of 300,000 per company. Custom fields are also plan-dependent, from 30 on Lite to 500 on Ultimate. Growing teams hit these ceilings and face forced upgrades.

Customermates imposes no plan-based record caps. Contacts, deals, and typed custom columns are available in every plan at the base price. Automation surfaces (webhooks, the REST API, MCP, and a community node for your own n8n) are available from the start, not locked behind a Growth plan upgrade. AI clients (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini) operate the CRM directly through MCP, so notes, stage changes, and [lead tracking](/features/lead-tracking) stay current without manual data entry.

### Pipeline and Deal Management

Both platforms offer pipeline views with drag-and-drop functionality. Pipedrive pioneered the visual sales pipeline and remains strong here. Customermates provides [pipeline views](/features/pipeline) as a table or a Kanban board, with stage probabilities that weight deal values and pipeline totals. Separate kinds of deals with a custom single-select field plus saved views or grouping.

### Reporting and Dashboards

Pipedrive limits reporting capabilities on lower plans. Forecast reports start on Growth, and reports on custom fields and collaborative dashboards require Premium or Ultimate. Customermates includes [reporting](/features/reporting) dashboards and stage-weighted pipeline totals at the base price, so every team member has visibility from day one. It does not offer predictive forecasting.

### Automation Capabilities

Pipedrive reserves workflow automation for the Growth plan (€39/seat/month) and above, with 50 active automations on Growth, 150 on Premium, and 250 on Ultimate. Customermates has no built-in rules engine; it offers [workflow automation](/features/workflow-automation) through webhooks, the REST API, and a community node for your own separately operated n8n instance, which connects to hundreds of third-party services. In the managed cloud, routines run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a CRM event happens, using the plan's hosted AI credits.

## Data Protection and GDPR

In the managed cloud, the Customermates application database runs in the AWS Frankfurt region; hosting and other subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [DPA](/dpa) is available. Customermates holds no compliance certification. If you need full control over where data lives, [self-host](/features/self-hosted) the open-source core.

Pipedrive also offers a DPA and runs on its own hosting regions and subprocessors. Check which region your account uses and review Pipedrive's DPA and subprocessor list against your requirements, particularly in finance, healthcare, or the public sector.

For teams with strict requirements on data location, self-hosting Customermates puts the infrastructure decision in your hands.

## Integration and Automation

Customermates connects to separately operated [n8n](https://n8n.io/) through REST, webhooks, MCP, and a community node. Documented [API](/features/api) access starts at the base price. Customermates has no native Slack integration; external [Slack](/features/slack-integration) workflows, email providers, and custom [CRM integrations](/features/integrations) run through those surfaces and remain subject to their own plans, limits, and costs.

Pipedrive offers a marketplace with 500+ integrations and API access on every plan. However, workflow automation is only available from the Growth plan (€39/seat/month), and more automations and if/else steps require Premium or higher.

## Migrating from Pipedrive to Customermates

Switching CRMs does not have to be painful. Customermates imports and exports records as XLSX workbooks, one record type at a time, and larger or custom transfers can use the REST API or an AI client over MCP. File attachments do not transfer. Many teams complete the transition in a day:

1. Export your Pipedrive data (people, organizations, deals) and save each record type as an XLSX workbook
2. Sign up for Customermates and configure your [pipeline stages](/features/pipeline)
3. Import each workbook, starting with contacts and organizations, then deals
4. Connect your automations through webhooks or your own [n8n](/features/workflow-automation) instance
5. Invite your team and start working

No consultants required. No multi-week implementation projects.

### Can I self-host Customermates?

Yes. Customermates is open source and supports [self-hosting](/features/self-hosted) with Docker Compose and PostgreSQL 16. You run it on your own infrastructure and own backups and updates. A self-hosted instance has the Starter feature set with MCP, REST, and webhooks, but not the unified inbox or the hosted assistant.

### Does Customermates have an API?

Yes. Customermates provides a [REST API](/features/api) with an OpenAPI description in every plan, plus webhooks and MCP. You can integrate Customermates with other tools or build custom workflows using the API.

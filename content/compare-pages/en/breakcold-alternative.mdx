## What is Breakcold, and who is it a good choice for?

Breakcold calls itself "the AI Native CRM for modern salespeople and AI agents". Its pitch is selling on LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Telegram and email from one place, and the pages we read back that up.

- **One inbox for five channels.** Email through Gmail, Outlook or IMAP, LinkedIn DMs, InMails, connection requests and Sales Navigator threads, WhatsApp 1:1 and group chats, and Telegram DMs and groups, each matched to a record. A feed of your prospects' LinkedIn posts lets you like, comment and message from the deal.
- **Enrichment and calls.** Person and company enrichment is on every plan, and Breakcold says it fills more than 50 data points. Call recording with summaries and a meeting recorder for Zoom and Google Meet are on every plan too.
- **Chief of Staff.** Every plan includes an AI assistant that chats with your CRM, moves pipelines from what was said, drafts replies on every channel and writes a weekly report. Five specialists take on single jobs, among them a follow-up planner that catches quiet conversations and drafts the follow-up. In Ask mode, every email, message and change waits on an approval card, and single actions can be switched to Auto.
- **MCP.** Breakcold's MCP page lists 62 tools, including tools that send and reply on its messaging channels, OAuth for end-user clients and an MIT-licensed Agent Skill.
- **Agencies.** One workspace is included and each extra one costs $10 a month, so a client can live in its own workspace.

If that list describes how you work, switching will probably cost you more than it saves.

## How much does Breakcold cost, and is there a free plan?

Prices checked on 10 November 2026 on [Breakcold's pricing page](https://www.breakcold.com/pricing). Breakcold sells one product in three sizes: X1 at $49, X5 at $99 and X20 at $199 per seat per month on monthly billing. Every plan has the full CRM, Chief of Staff and two connected accounts per seat. The plans differ only in how much AI work each seat gets per week: one, five or twenty times the base allowance. Quarterly billing takes 10% off and yearly billing 20%, billed upfront. Extra connected accounts and extra workspaces cost $10 a month each, and resetting a used-up weekly allowance costs $50.

The pricing page lists no free plan. It offers a 14-day free trial and says a card is required; two other Breakcold pages, its [MCP page](https://www.breakcold.com/crm-mcp) and its [LinkedIn CRM page](https://www.breakcold.com/crm/crm-for-linkedin), say no credit card is needed, so confirm at sign-up. Breakcold prices in US dollars on the page we read, and Customermates bills monthly in euros; the table does not convert between them.

These rows do not buy the same things, so read them in pairs:

- **Breakcold X1 and Customermates Pro are the closest pair.** Each includes an inbox, an AI assistant and an MCP server. Breakcold adds enrichment, call recording and a second account per seat. Customermates Pro adds Instagram, an open-source core and a Free plan to fall back on after the trial.
- **Two channels per person is where Breakcold is cheaper.** One Customermates Pro account is LinkedIn or email, not both. If every person needs both beside each other, the fair pair is Breakcold X1 and Customermates Business, which also lets you share a whole account with the team.
- **Breakcold's price follows AI use, not features.** X20 costs four times X1 per seat for the same CRM, because it carries twenty times the weekly AI allowance.
- **Customermates Starter is the cheapest row, but it has no inbox.** It suits a team that wants records, Mate and the API without synced conversations.

## Why do teams look for a Breakcold alternative?

**No free plan, and a card for the trial.** After the 14 days, the pricing page lists $49 per seat per month as the way in. A solo founder who wants a free tier to fall back on will not find one there.

**The plan is sized by AI use.** Chief of Staff, chat and background tasks draw from one weekly allowance per seat. The pricing FAQ says background work stops at half the allowance so chat always has room, and that a used-up week can be reset for $50 or the team can move up a plan. How much AI work each seat will do is hard to estimate before you start.

**A hosted service in two regions.** Breakcold is operated by Logike SAS in Paris and offers US and EU regions. Its privacy policy says it does not promise that all processing stays in one country, and its data processing addendum lists Convex for the database, Cloudflare and Vercel for the application, and AI requests that go through OpenRouter to Anthropic and OpenAI. As the controller, you assess whether that fits your own data processing agreement.

**No self-hosted edition.** The CRM is not listed as open source or as something you can run on your own server. Only the Agent Skill that sits on its MCP server is MIT-licensed.

## Where does Customermates fit?

Customermates is our product, so check each line against its own docs. It is an open-source CRM made in Germany for small client-facing teams: the conversation sits on the client, a date field holds the next step, and Mate or your own AI client drafts the reply that you review and send.

**Conversations beside each client.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one list, and you reply on the original channel. [LinkedIn in Customermates](/features/linkedin-integration) includes Sales Navigator and Recruiter InMail. Link a participant to a contact once, and every conversation with that address or handle shows on the contact's timeline.

**A next step on every client.** Add a date field such as "Next contact" to contacts or deals, sort a saved view by it and add an Upcoming widget to the dashboard. The [follow-up guide](/features/follow-up) walks through it. Nothing pings you; you open the list.

**AI that drafts for you.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes threads, drafts replies, updates records and builds views, using hosted AI credits: a monthly allowance per active user, listed on the [pricing page](/pricing). Routines run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes. Mate sends a message without asking when you tell it to send, so ask for drafts when you want to review first. Prefer Claude or ChatGPT? Connect it to the [MCP server](/docs/mcp), with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan, Free included, and on self-hosted installs. Its model calls then run on your own AI provider, and our [guide to CRMs with Claude](/blog/claude-crm) shows how the options compare.

**Open source, with a hosting choice.** The AGPL-3.0 core runs with Docker Compose and PostgreSQL 16, including REST, webhooks and MCP. A [self-hosted install](/features/self-hosted) has no inbox, no Mate and no hosted credits. In Customermates Cloud the database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, a [DPA](/dpa) is available, and the subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the legal pages. Customermates holds no compliance certification.

## Where is Customermates the wrong choice?

Check these before you start a trial:

- **No enrichment, no call recording and no telephony.** Contact data is what your team or your AI client enters, and meetings stay in your meeting tool.
- **No sequences and no bulk email.** Messages go out one at a time, written by you or drafted by Mate. Breakcold's pricing and features pages list no sequence builder either; its features page points to agents such as OpenClaw and Hermes AI for outreach. Neither tool is the pick for multi-step campaigns.
- **No browser extension.** Profiles do not get captured into the CRM with one click.
- **No reminders and no task due dates.** A date field and an Upcoming widget show what is due, but nothing sends a notification.
- **No automatic detection of silent threads.** Nothing watches for a conversation that went quiet unless you write a routine for it.
- **One connected account per user on Pro.** Two channels per person need Business.
- **No approval queue for sends.** Mate sends when you tell it to, which is why this page recommends drafts.
- **No native mobile app.** Customermates runs in the phone's browser.
- **A small free plan.** Free covers [[commercial.limit.free.maxMembers]] person and [[commercial.limit.free.maxRecords]] records, without the inbox, Mate or routines; more needs a paid plan after the [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial.

## What does a follow-up setup look like in Customermates?

Take a three-person consultancy that sells projects to clients it first meets on LinkedIn. Two partners answer on LinkedIn; the third works from Gmail.

| In Breakcold | In Customermates |
|---|---|
| People and companies | Contacts and organizations |
| A pipeline with stages | Deals renamed "Projects", with a stage field: Lead 10%, Proposal 40%, Negotiation 70%, Won 100%, Lost 0% |
| A task with a reminder | A date field "Next contact" and an Upcoming widget; no reminder is sent |
| Chief of Staff drafts in the tasks queue | Mate or a routine saves a draft in the conversation; you review and send |
| One inbox for every channel | The unified inbox with LinkedIn and Gmail connected |
| Enrichment and call recording | Not available; notes by hand or through your AI client |

Each partner connects one channel, LinkedIn or Gmail. One account each fits Pro, at [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.3]] a month for the team. When someone needs LinkedIn and Gmail side by side, the workspace moves to Business at [[commercial.price.business.monthly.seats.3]].

Each partner also owns a routine for the Monday follow-up. A routine runs as its owner, so it sees that person's contacts and saves drafts in their own channels. It runs in Customermates Cloud and uses hosted AI credits:

> Every Monday at 8:00, find the contacts assigned to me whose "Next contact" date is today or earlier. For each one, read our latest conversation and save a short follow-up draft in it. Do not send anything. List the contacts in the run summary.

## How do I move from Breakcold to Customermates?

The how-to-switch steps above the article cover the basics. Three details are specific to a move from Breakcold:

- **Getting the data out.** Breakcold's [API documentation](https://docs.breakcold.com/api/overview) describes a REST API for records, tasks, notes and inbox workflows. We found no export guide on breakcold.com, and its help center was not available on 10 November 2026, so look for an export in the app or ask Breakcold's support before you plan the move.
- **Importing.** Customermates reads a JSON export of a list, .xlsx, .xls and .csv files through Add from file, up to 10 MiB and 5,000 non-blank rows per sheet. Import organizations first, then contacts, then deals. Link columns match records by name, and new contacts need a first and a last name. Map email addresses and phone numbers to the contact's Contact details field. Limits are in the [records guide](/docs/app-records).
- **Conversations.** Connecting LinkedIn, Gmail or WhatsApp in Customermates starts a history import in the background, so past threads show on the contacts whose address or handle matches. Link the rest once by hand.

## Why Teams Look for a Zoho Alternative

Zoho CRM is one of the most widely used CRM platforms globally, offering modules for sales, marketing, service, and more. Yet many teams run into the same frustrations after a few months:

- **Overwhelming complexity.** Zoho packs dozens of modules, submenus, and configuration layers into a single interface. Finding the right feature requires navigating a labyrinth of settings. Even experienced users report spending too much time on administration rather than selling.
- **Tiered feature restrictions.** Workflow rules, custom fields, reports, API access, and storage are all capped based on your plan. The free plan allows only one workflow rule and five custom views per module. Even the Standard plan keeps many critical features restricted.
- **Hidden costs.** Premium support, additional storage, data backups, and portals for external users are paid add-ons. What appears affordable at sign-up frequently doubles once a team needs production-grade functionality.
- **Hosting region questions.** Zoho runs regional data centres, including in the EU, and the region usually follows where the account is created. Check which region and subprocessors apply to your account.
- **AI locked behind expensive tiers.** Zoho's AI assistant Zia is available from the Enterprise plan at €40/user/month.

If these pain points sound familiar, it is worth evaluating a Zoho alternative that removes these barriers entirely.

## Data Protection & GDPR

In the managed cloud, the Customermates application database runs in the AWS Frankfurt region; hosting and other subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [DPA](/dpa) is available. Customermates holds no certification. If you need full control over where data lives, [self-host](/features/self-hosted) the open-source core.

Zoho CRM runs regional data centres, including in the EU, and its subprocessors span several jurisdictions. For European companies handling customer data under [GDPR requirements](https://gdpr.eu/), review Zoho's DPA and the region of your account.

In n8n you can, for example, push contact changes to your [accounting software](/features/integrations) or post [Slack messages](/features/slack-integration) on deal updates; [reporting dashboards](/features/reporting) are built into Customermates.

Zoho CRM offers many integrations, but most are limited to higher-tier plans. API access is missing entirely from the free plan and only available from Standard onwards. Workflow automation is heavily restricted: one rule on Free, ten on Standard, only from Professional does it become genuinely usable. For teams that rely on [sales automation](/features/sales-automation), this means paying €23+/user/month before the CRM does real work for them.

## How to Migrate from Zoho to Customermates

Switching from Zoho CRM to Customermates is straightforward:

1. **Export your Zoho data**, Zoho allows CSV exports of contacts, deals, and accounts from the Data Administration settings. Save them as XLSX workbooks.
2. **Import into Customermates**, Import each record type as an XLSX workbook, organizations first, or load data through the REST API.
3. **Set up your pipelines**, Recreate your [deal stages](/features/pipeline) and customize fields to match your workflow.
4. **Connect integrations**, Connect mailboxes in the unified inbox (Pro and up) and reconnect third-party tools through webhooks, the REST API or a separately operated [n8n](/features/workflow-automation).
5. **Invite your team**, Share access with your team and start working immediately. No training required.

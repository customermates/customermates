## Who Zoho CRM is a good choice for

Zoho CRM is the sales CRM in a large family of Zoho business apps, among them Zoho Desk, Zoho Forms, Zoho Sign and Bigin. Its strength is range: depending on the edition, one product covers leads, contacts and deals, calling, mass email, web forms, quotes, invoices and inventory.

The editions build on each other:

- **Free:** three users, workflow automation, up to 10 email templates, standard reports, spreadsheet import and export, 5,000 API calls a day and the mobile app.
- **Standard:** Gmail and Outlook integration, mass email, follow-up cadences, built-in calling, multiple pipelines, sales forecasting, web forms, data enrichment and custom modules.
- **Professional:** process management, inventory management for invoices, sales orders and purchase orders, CPQ for quotes, AI agents, predictive intelligence, unlimited reports and a Google Ads integration.
- **Enterprise:** Zia's analyses, predictions and recommendations, multi-team management, approval processes, extended field types, a sandbox and field-level encryption.
- **Ultimate:** higher limits, consulting and migration assistance.

If your team sells by phone, sends quotes from the CRM, needs approvals before a deal moves on, or already runs on other Zoho apps, those are good reasons to stay.

## Why teams look for a Zoho CRM alternative

**The headline price is the yearly price.** Standard costs $14 per user per month billed yearly and $20 billed monthly. Professional costs $23 or $35, Enterprise $40 or $50, Ultimate $52 or $65. Zoho lists the same figures in euros, adds local taxes such as VAT, and says yearly billing saves up to 34%.

**Features arrive edition by edition.** Forecasting starts on Standard. Process management, quotes, inventory and predictive intelligence need Professional. Zia's analyses and recommendations, approval processes and the sandbox need Enterprise. Editions are priced per user, so a team that wants one Enterprise feature pays the Enterprise price for every seat.

**Some costs sit outside the edition.** Zoho's WhatsApp integration needs a verified business, a WhatsApp Business account and a number registered for the WhatsApp Business API, and conversations are charged against credits you buy in advance. Paid editions include two free data backups a month as ZIP files, Ultimate four; on the Free edition you buy them.

**Small teams use a fraction of it.** If you mostly track contacts, deals and conversations, Zoho itself offers Bigin, a simpler pipeline CRM for small businesses moving off spreadsheets.

**Hosted by Zoho only.** The country you choose at sign-up decides which Zoho data centre holds your data. In Europe, Zoho runs data centres in Amsterdam and Dublin. Zoho offers no self-hosted edition and no open-source licence for Zoho CRM.

## Where Customermates fits

Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM for small client-facing teams: consultants, agencies, other service businesses and founders. It is built in Germany.

**Conversations next to the deal.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) puts Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn (including Sales Navigator and Recruiter), WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one list, and you reply on the original channel. WhatsApp connects the account you already use: you scan a QR code instead of registering a business number. Link a participant to a contact once, and every conversation with that address or handle appears on the contact's timeline.

**AI that keeps the records current.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes conversations, drafts replies, updates records and builds saved views, using hosted AI credits. It asks for a fresh approval before every deletion. It sends a message without one when you ask it to send, so ask for a draft when you want to read it first. [Routines](/docs/app-routines) run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes, always as their owner. Prefer Claude, ChatGPT or another client you already pay for? Connect it to the [MCP server](/docs/mcp), with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan and on self-hosted installs; its model calls run on your own AI provider.

**Open source, a cloud configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, or your own server.** The core is AGPL-3.0 and runs with Docker Compose and PostgreSQL 16, including REST, webhooks and MCP. A [self-hosted install](/features/self-hosted) has no subscription, and also no inbox, no Mate and no hosted credits. In Customermates Cloud the database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, a DPA is available, and the subprocessors, including US providers, are listed. Customermates holds no compliance certification. The audit log records changes to records and workspace settings on every plan.

## Where Customermates is the wrong choice

Check these before you start a trial:

- **No quotes, invoicing or inventory.** Zoho's CPQ and inventory management have no counterpart, and invoices stay in your accounting software.
- **No rule builder.** There are no workflow rules, approval processes or process steps. Routines in Customermates Cloud follow written instructions, and webhooks with the REST API feed an n8n instance that you run yourself.
- **No telephony, no mass email and no sequences.** Calls happen on your own phone system, and messages go out one at a time, written by you or drafted by Mate.
- **No web forms, scoring, enrichment or AI forecasting.** The weighted pipeline multiplies each deal's value by its stage probability. That is arithmetic on your own numbers, and it does not predict anything.
- **A fixed data model.** You get five record types that you can rename and ten field types, with no formula, lookup or multi-select fields and no custom modules. Permissions apply per record type and action, not per field, and saved views are personal.
- **No native mobile apps and no free plan.** Customermates runs in the phone's browser, and after a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial you choose a paid plan.

## A worked setup: a web agency leaving Zoho CRM Standard

Take a three-person web agency. The founder finds new clients on LinkedIn, and two account managers run projects by email. In Zoho CRM Standard they used leads, accounts, deals with one pipeline, products and a workflow rule that created a kick-off task for every won deal.

| In Zoho CRM | In Customermates |
|---|---|
| Leads | Contacts, with a single-select field "Lifecycle" set to Lead |
| Contacts | Contacts, with "Lifecycle" set to Client |
| Accounts | Organizations, renamed "Accounts" in the Data model |
| Deals and stages | Deals with the Stage field and a win probability per stage |
| Products | Services, renamed "Products", linked to deals with a quantity |
| Tasks, meetings and calls | Tasks, plus a custom date field if you want a date, because tasks have no built-in due date |
| Notes | The notes field, one rich-text field per record |
| Attachments | Not imported, since records have no file attachments; keep them in the Zoho backup |
| Tags | A single-select field, since records have no tags |
| Workflow rules | Routines in Customermates Cloud, or webhooks to your own n8n |

The founder edits the Stage field's options to Brief received, Proposal sent, Negotiation, Won and Lost, and under My Company › Settings gives them win probabilities of 20%, 50%, 75%, 100% and 0%. She saves a board of all open deals grouped by Stage. Each account manager saves a table of their own deals grouped by organization; saved views are personal, so each person builds their own.

Each person connects one account, LinkedIn for the founder and Gmail for the other two, so Pro fits at [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.3]] a month for the team. If the founder also wants WhatsApp next to LinkedIn, the workspace moves to Business at [[commercial.price.business.monthly.seats.3]].

The Zoho workflow rule becomes a routine owned by the founder. Its trigger is When something changes, with the event Deal Updated, the changed field Stage and the record filter Stage is Won. A routine runs as its owner, so her role needs read access to every deal. It runs in Customermates Cloud and uses hosted AI credits:

> This deal just moved to Won. Create a task "Kick-off call" linked to the deal, its contacts and its organization, and assign it to the deal's assigned users. Add a five-line summary of the latest conversation with the main contact to the deal's notes. Do not send any message.

## Switching from Zoho CRM in five steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export each module as an Excel file">In Zoho CRM, open Setup › Data Administration › Export, pick a module and choose XLSX. Zoho's help pages put the limit at 50,000 records per XLSX export and keep the download link for seven days. Export Accounts, Contacts, Leads, Products and Deals; notes are a separate choice in the module list. A full data backup under Setup › Data Administration › Data Backup is a ZIP of CSV files with attachments. Customermates cannot import that ZIP, so keep it as your archive.</Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the workspace">Invite your teammates first, so deal owners can be matched to users. Under My Company › Settings, rename record types in the Data model and set the stage probabilities. Create custom fields for the Zoho fields you keep, such as lead source, industry and Lifecycle.</Step>
  <Step title="Import one record type at a time">On each list, open Import and export, then Add from file: organizations first, then contacts and leads, then services, then deals. Link columns match records by name, so a contact's Account Name finds the organization you imported before, and an owner column maps to assigned users. Map email addresses and phone numbers to the contact's channels. Size limits are in the [records documentation](/docs/app-records).</Step>
  <Step title="Connect channels and AI">On Pro or during the trial, connect a mailbox, LinkedIn or WhatsApp under My Profile › Channels. Zoho's email history does not transfer; the inbox shows the conversations of the accounts you connect. Then set up routines, or connect your own AI client through MCP.</Step>
  <Step title="Replace each workflow rule">List your Zoho workflow rules and decide for each one: a routine, a webhook into your own n8n, or nothing. Rules that only create tasks or update a field often collapse into one routine.</Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is Zoho CRM still free?">
Yes. The Free edition is free with no time limit for up to three users. It includes workflow automation, standard reports, up to 10 email templates, spreadsheet import and export, 5,000 API calls a day and the mobile app. A fourth user means moving to a paid edition. Customermates Cloud has no free plan; a self-hosted Customermates install has no subscription, but also no inbox and no Mate.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What is the difference between Bigin and Zoho CRM?">
Bigin is Zoho's simpler CRM for small businesses that manage a sales pipeline, and its plans are capped by pipelines and records: one pipeline and 500 records on Free, three pipelines and 50,000 records on Express, five and 100,000 on Premier. Zoho CRM adds what Bigin leaves out, such as process management, quotes, inventory and Zia, depending on the edition.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is Zoho an Indian or a Chinese company, and where is my data stored?">
Zoho is not Chinese. It started in 1996 as AdventNet in New Jersey, is privately held, and has its headquarters in Chennai, India. Your Zoho CRM data sits in the Zoho data centre that matches the country you choose at sign-up; in Europe that means Amsterdam and Dublin. Customermates is built in Germany and has its cloud database configured in the AWS Frankfurt region.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I use Zoho CRM with Claude or ChatGPT?">
Yes, through Zoho MCP, a separate Zoho service that exposes Zoho CRM and other Zoho apps to AI clients such as Claude. Customermates includes an MCP server on every plan and on self-hosted installs, and in Customermates Cloud also Mate, which works inside the app on hosted AI credits.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there an open-source alternative to Zoho CRM?">
Yes. Customermates has an AGPL-3.0 core that you run yourself with Docker Compose, including the REST API, webhooks and the MCP server. The inbox and Mate exist only in Customermates Cloud, so a self-hosted install is the CRM core without conversations.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

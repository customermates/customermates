Kondo is a fast inbox for LinkedIn and WhatsApp with labels, snippets and reminders. Customermates is a CRM that puts LinkedIn next to email, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram on the client record from Pro, with drafts you review and send. If keyboard-speed triage of a very busy LinkedIn inbox is the whole job, Kondo for LinkedIn is the better pick. ## What is Kondo for LinkedIn, and who is it a good choice for?

Kondo calls itself "the pro DM inbox for LinkedIn, WhatsApp and iMessage, built for you and your AI agents", and "Superhuman for DMs". Its strength is speed in the conversation list itself. If most of your week is answering LinkedIn messages, Kondo is built for that and Customermates is not.

- **Triage.** Kondo is built to be driven from the keyboard, with a command bar on Cmd or Ctrl+K. You archive, label and reply without the mouse, and filters such as "Waiting for their reply" narrow the list.
- **Labels as inboxes.** A chat can carry several labels, and each label gets its own inbox in the sidebar, so leads, customers and candidates stay apart.
- **Follow-ups.** A reminder puts a chat away until it is due, then returns it to the top. If the person writes first, the reminder is cancelled and the chat comes back at once. Snippets fill in names and your own placeholders.
- **Where it runs.** LinkedIn runs as its own window in Chrome, Edge or Brave, and WhatsApp in Kondo Desktop for macOS or Linux. Kondo says your chats stay on your device and its servers hold only the metadata you create, such as labels, reminders and snippets.
- **Business plan.** Adds the Sales Navigator inbox, team analytics, MCP and CRM integrations. Chats, notes and labels sync to HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio, Clarify, Notion and Google Sheets, or go out through webhooks, Zapier, Make and Clay.
- **Agents.** Through MCP, Claude, ChatGPT, Codex or Cursor can search chats, draft replies, label and set reminders. Kondo says it never sends a message for you, and Kondo has to be open while the agent works.

If that describes your week, switching will probably cost you more than it saves.

## Why do teams look for a Kondo alternative?

**Email is not in the inbox.** Kondo's pricing page names LinkedIn and WhatsApp, and its platform list has no email. A client who moves from a LinkedIn message to an email thread continues in another tool, and nothing ties the two together.

**The deal lives somewhere else.** Labels, reminders and notes belong to a chat. To get them onto a pipeline, Kondo syncs to a CRM, which needs the Business plan, and Customermates is not among the destinations Kondo lists.

**One LinkedIn account per Kondo account.** Kondo's help says it has no unified inbox for several LinkedIn accounts. It suggests a separate Chrome profile for each one.

**Kondo has to stay open.** Its MCP server answers only while the Kondo tab or app is open, and the agent writes drafts that you send from Kondo.

**A price per person for an inbox.** Basic costs $35 per user per month on monthly billing. The Sales Navigator inbox, CRM integrations, analytics and MCP need Business at $45.

## Where does Customermates fit?

Customermates is our product, so weigh this section against the Kondo one. It starts where an inbox ends: the conversation sits on the client, next to the deal, and you or your AI client draft the reply and the next step. It is open source and built in Germany.

**LinkedIn beside email on the client.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn (including Sales Navigator and Recruiter InMail), WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one list, and you reply on the original channel. Link a participant to a contact once and every conversation with that address or LinkedIn identity shows on the contact. Linking a conversation to a deal is a step you or an agent take on purpose. [LinkedIn in Customermates](/features/linkedin-integration) has the details.

**A next step on every open conversation.** Customermates has no built-in reminders or task due dates. You add a date field such as "Next step" to deals or contacts, sort a saved view by it and share the view with the workspace, and the Upcoming widget on the dashboard lists them by that date, with an Overdue section for earlier ones. Nobody is reminded automatically, so the list helps only when someone opens it. [Follow-up in Customermates](/features/follow-up) shows the setup.

**Drafts you review and send.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate saves a draft reply in the conversation, and you edit and send it. Your own Claude or ChatGPT can do the same through the [MCP server](/docs/mcp), with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan and on self-hosted installs, using your own AI provider. Mate sends a message as soon as you tell it to send, so ask for a draft when you want to review first. Mate uses hosted AI credits, with a monthly allowance per active user on the [pricing page](/pricing).

**How LinkedIn connects.** You sign in through a hosted flow of the transport provider Unipile, and Customermates receives neither your LinkedIn password nor OAuth tokens. Synced threads and messages are stored in the Customermates database, which is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region in Customermates Cloud. A [DPA](/dpa) is available, and the subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the legal pages. Customermates applies a conservative request budget per channel and pauses before LinkedIn would push back. Kondo works the other way round, inside your own session, so decide which model you prefer.

## Where is Customermates the wrong choice?

Check these before you start a trial:

- **It is not a keyboard-first inbox.** The [documented Inbox](/docs/app-inbox) has thread states, filters, saved views and drafts. It has no snooze, no snippets and no label inboxes. For hundreds of LinkedIn messages a day, Kondo is faster.
- **Messages cannot stay on your computer.** Connected conversations are synced into the Customermates database.
- **The inbox starts at Pro and exists only in Customermates Cloud.** Free, Starter and self-hosted installs have no connected accounts, and one account per Pro user means LinkedIn plus a mailbox needs Business.
- **No reminders that bring a chat back.** A date field and a saved view replace them, and someone has to open the view.
- **No sequences, no bulk messaging, no enrichment and no native mobile app.** Messages go out one at a time, written by you or drafted by Mate. Kondo says it does not automate either.
- **A small free plan.** Free covers [[commercial.limit.free.maxMembers]] person and [[commercial.limit.free.maxRecords]] records, without the inbox, Mate or routines; more needs a paid plan after the [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial.

## A worked setup: a consultant moving from Kondo

Take a solo consultant who runs client and prospect conversations through LinkedIn messages. In Kondo the labels are Hot, Proposal sent and Client, and clients increasingly continue by email.

| In Kondo | In Customermates |
|---|---|
| Labels such as Hot and Proposal sent | A stage field on each deal: Lead 10%, Proposal sent 50%, Negotiation 70%, Won 100%, Lost 0% |
| A reminder "in four days" | A date field "Next step" on the deal and a saved view sorted by it |
| A snippet for the first reply | A draft from Mate or your own AI client |
| Notes on a person | The notes field on the contact |
| LinkedIn chats in the Kondo inbox | LinkedIn connected under Channels, each thread linked to its deal |
| A HubSpot sync | Not needed, because the deal is already in the CRM |

Customermates multiplies each deal's value by its stage probability, and a chart widget on the dashboard sums the weighted values. That is arithmetic on your own numbers, and it does not predict anything.

One Pro seat connects LinkedIn. When the mailbox has to sit beside it, the workspace moves to Business. The consultant also owns one routine for the Monday check. A routine runs as its owner, so it sees that person's deals and saves drafts in their own channels. It runs in Customermates Cloud and uses hosted AI credits, and Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]] routines per user:

> Every Monday at 8:00, find my deals whose "Next step" date is today or earlier. For each one, read the latest conversation with the contact and save a short follow-up draft in it. Do not send anything. List the deals in the run summary.

> Look for a folk CRM alternative when you want to answer LinkedIn and WhatsApp messages from inside the CRM, need deals and an API without moving to Premium, or want a CRM you can host yourself. folk logs LinkedIn conversations one at a time through its folkX extension, puts the deals object and the REST API on Premium, and sells hosted plans only. Customermates syncs email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one inbox from Pro, includes deals, REST, webhooks and MCP on every plan, and its open-source core runs on your own server. If profile capture, an email finder, campaigns or a phone app carry your sales process, folk remains the better fit. Customermates publishes this comparison; every folk detail comes from folk.app and its help center and was checked on 8 October 2026.

## Who folk is a good choice for

folk calls itself "the AI CRM for your entire sales motion". Its strength is the start of a relationship: finding people, saving them and reaching out. If most of your week is prospecting on LinkedIn and sending outreach, folk is built for that and Customermates is not.

- **Capture.** The folkX Chrome extension saves profiles from LinkedIn, Sales Navigator, X, Instagram and Gmail. The mobile app scans business cards and records voice notes.
- **Reach out.** Standard includes 500 email-finder lookups and 2,000 messages per member a month, templates for LinkedIn and Gmail, and email campaigns with analytics. Sequences come with Premium.
- **Sync.** Emails and calendar events from Google or Microsoft and WhatsApp 1:1 chats land on each contact's profile in real time.
- **Assist.** Follow-up and recap assistants are on every plan; the research and workflow assistants run on monthly quotas.
- **Connect AI.** An MCP server on every plan lets Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor search and update the workspace after an OAuth sign-in.

## Why teams look for a folk alternative

**Deals and the API sit on Premium.** Standard includes pipeline management. The deals object, custom objects, dashboards, workspace roles and the REST API need Premium, at $60 per member a month or $48 billed yearly (€54 or €43).

**LinkedIn is captured, not synced.** folk's help center says it has no native LinkedIn integration for automatic syncing. To log a LinkedIn conversation, you open the thread and click "Add to folk" in the extension, once per conversation. Email and WhatsApp 1:1 chats do sync on their own.

**Standard shows 30 interactions per contact.** On Standard you see at most 30 emails, meetings and chats per contact, from up to three years back. Premium shows the full history.

**US hosting, according to its help center.** folk is run by Folk Inc., a Delaware corporation. Its help center places the infrastructure on AWS in North Virginia (us-east-1), with EU transfers under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; its privacy policy names AWS servers in Ireland. If the region matters for your data processing agreement, ask folk which statement applies. folk reports SOC 2 Type 1; Customermates holds no certification.

**Exports leave history behind.** folk exports one group at a time as CSV files of contacts and notes, but not interactions, reminders or some field types, and there is no export of the whole workspace.

## Where Customermates fits

Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM for small client-facing teams: consultants, agencies, other service businesses and founders. It is built in Germany.

**Conversations you can answer.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one list, and you reply on the original channel. [LinkedIn in Customermates](/features/linkedin-integration) includes Sales Navigator and Recruiter InMail. Link a participant to a contact once, and every conversation with that address or handle shows on the contact's timeline.

**Deals and the API on every plan.** Five record types, custom fields of ten types, table and board layouts, and a stage field with probabilities for a weighted pipeline total. Roles, REST, webhooks and MCP are part of Starter.

**AI that does the CRM work.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate prepares thread summaries and reply drafts, updates records and builds views, using hosted AI credits: a monthly allowance per active user, listed on the [pricing page](/pricing). Routines run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes. Mate sends a message without asking when you tell it to send, so ask for drafts when you want to review first. Your own Claude or ChatGPT can work through the [MCP server](/docs/mcp) instead, with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan and on self-hosted installs.

**Your server, if you want one.** The AGPL-3.0 core runs with Docker Compose and PostgreSQL 16, with REST, webhooks and MCP, but without the inbox, Mate or hosted credits. In Customermates Cloud the database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, a DPA is available, and the subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the legal pages.

## Where Customermates is the wrong choice

- **No browser extension and no enrichment.** Contacts come from the inbox, an XLSX import, the API or your AI client, and nothing looks up missing email addresses for you.
- **No campaigns, sequences or bulk email.** Messages go out one at a time, written by you or drafted by Mate.
- **No native mobile app.** Customermates runs in the phone's browser.
- **No reminders and no task due dates.** Add a date field such as "Next contact" and sort a saved view by it. Saved views are personal, so each person keeps their own.
- **No custom objects.** You can rename the five record types from presets, for example deals as projects, but you cannot add a sixth.
- **No free plan.** There is a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial, then a paid plan.

## A worked setup: a consultancy moving from folk

A four-person consultancy on folk Standard keeps two groups, "Prospects" and "Clients", runs a pipeline view on a status field and logs LinkedIn chats with folkX. Two partners prospect on LinkedIn; the other two look after clients in Gmail.

| In folk | In Customermates |
|---|---|
| People and companies | Contacts and organizations |
| Groups "Prospects" and "Clients" | A single-select field "Relationship" on contacts, plus one saved view per value |
| Pipeline view on a status field | Deals renamed "Projects", with a stage field: Lead 10%, Proposal 40%, Negotiation 70%, Won 100%, Lost 0% |
| Notes | The notes field on each record |
| Reminders | No reminders; a date field "Next contact" and a saved view sorted by it |
| LinkedIn chats logged one by one | LinkedIn connected to the inbox |

A project's value comes from the services linked to it, such as a "Strategy workshop" package, and the stage probability turns it into the weighted total.

Each partner connects one channel, LinkedIn or Gmail. One account each fits Pro, at [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.4]] a month for the team. When someone needs Gmail and WhatsApp side by side, the workspace moves to Business at [[commercial.price.business.monthly.seats.4]].

Each partner also owns a routine for the Friday follow-up. A routine runs as its owner, so it sees that person's contacts and drafts in their channels. It runs in Customermates Cloud and uses hosted AI credits:

> Every Friday at 16:00, find the contacts assigned to me whose "Next contact" date is today or earlier. For each one, read our latest conversation and save a short follow-up draft in it. Do not send anything. List the contacts in the run summary.

## Switching from folk in five steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export each group from folk">In each group, open the menu at the top right and choose "Export view as CSV". The export holds the displayed columns, so show every field you want to keep first. You get a contacts file and a notes file; interactions, reminders and some fields, such as contact custom fields, are not included.</Step>
  <Step title="Save the files as XLSX">Customermates does not read CSV files, so open each one in a spreadsheet app and save it as an Excel workbook.</Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the workspace">Rename deals to Projects if that fits, create the "Relationship" and "Next contact" fields, and set the stage probabilities.</Step>
  <Step title="Import one record type at a time">Import organizations first, then contacts, then projects. Link columns match records by name, so a contact's company column finds the organization you imported before. New contacts need a first and a last name, and email addresses or WhatsApp numbers can map to the contact's channels. Copy folk's notes into a notes column before the import, or let your AI client append them over MCP afterwards. Row and size limits are in the [records guide](/docs/app-records).</Step>
  <Step title="Connect channels and routines">On Pro or during the trial, connect a mailbox, LinkedIn or WhatsApp under My Profile, Channels. Then set up the Friday routine, or connect your own AI client through MCP.</Step>
</Steps>

## Other folk alternatives worth a look

Customermates publishes this list. Prices and features come from each vendor's pricing page, checked on 8 October 2026.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="What is folk CRM?">
folk is a hosted relationship CRM from Folk Inc., a Delaware corporation, which describes it as "the AI CRM for your entire sales motion". People and companies live in groups with table and pipeline views, email, calendar and WhatsApp sync to each contact, and the folkX extension captures LinkedIn profiles. Every plan includes an email finder, email campaigns and AI assistants.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is folk CRM free?">
folk's pricing page lists no free plan among its plans. A new workspace gets a 2-week trial with Premium features and no credit card; without an upgrade, folk blocks the account, and you can ask for one more week. Standard then costs $30 per member a month, or $24 billed yearly. Customermates has no free plan either, only a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial, but its open-source core can be self-hosted without a licence fee.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a free alternative to folk?">
For very small teams, yes: Attio Free covers up to 3 seats, and HubSpot's free tools up to 2 users. If you can run a server, Twenty and the Customermates core are open source; the Customermates inbox and Mate exist only in Customermates Cloud.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is folk CRM good?">
For relationship-driven prospecting, yes: profile capture, an email finder, campaigns and a mobile app come on the entry plan. It fits less well when you need deals and the API without Premium, want LinkedIn conversations to sync on their own, or need software you can host yourself.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does folk sync LinkedIn messages?">
Not automatically. You open each thread and log it with "Add to folk" in the folkX extension. In Customermates Pro, conversations from a connected LinkedIn account, Sales Navigator and Recruiter InMail included, arrive in the inbox and you reply from there.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Where does folk store data, and is it GDPR compliant?">
folk says it is GDPR compliant and offers a data processing agreement on request. Its help center and its privacy policy name different AWS locations, North Virginia and Ireland, so confirm the region with folk; as the controller, you make the final assessment. Customermates has its cloud database configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, offers a DPA and lists its subprocessors, including US providers.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

> **Short answer:** Customermates is a cobra CRM alternative for small client-facing teams that mainly need contacts, a deal pipeline and their client conversations in one place, and want the code open. It costs [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per user per month on Starter; cobra CRM Cloud Essential starts at €36. cobra is the better choice if you send newsletters, run events, write serial letters from Word templates or phone through NFON or Teams from the CRM: Customermates has none of these. Customermates publishes this comparison, and every cobra detail comes from cobra.de, checked on 8 October 2026.

## Which CRM fits which team

| Your situation | Better fit | Why |

| You want REST API and webhooks on the entry plan | Customermates | REST API and webhooks are on every plan. cobra CRM Cloud has the full API and webhooks on Max; cobra One Essential has a REST API with 3 endpoints |
| You want open-source code and a CRM you can host yourself | Customermates | AGPL-3.0 core with Docker Compose; the inbox and Mate stay in Customermates Cloud |
| You run on-premise today and want vendor support for it | cobra One or combit CRM | Both sell supported on-premise editions |

## Who cobra CRM is a good choice for

cobra has built CRM software in Konstanz since 1985 and has been part of Buhl Data Service since 2007. Its focus groups are associations, public institutions, universities and education, wholesale and professional services. The product reflects that: address management with business cards and relationship diagrams, Word templates for single and serial letters, newsletters, event management with ticket payment, web forms, NFON and Teams telephony and native apps for iOS and Android.

There are two product lines at the same prices. cobra CRM Cloud runs in the browser from cobra's cloud in Neunkirchen (Siegerland) and includes cobra AI as a fair-use flat rate: touchpoint summaries, reply suggestions and a "next best touchpoint". cobra One is the on-premise edition for your own servers or a cloud you choose; cobra says it uses different technology, so features do not map one to one.

If your organisation runs members, seminars or campaigns through the CRM, cobra covers more of that work than Customermates does.

## Why teams look for a cobra CRM alternative

**The entry plan carries a lot you may not use.** Cloud Essential costs from €36 per user per month and includes event portals, web forms and NFON telephony. A sales team that only needs contacts, deals and email still starts there.

**Integration depth follows the plan.** In cobra CRM Cloud, Essential and Grow connect to other systems through certified partner apps. The full API, 50 webhooks, single sign-on, field permissions for custom objects and the 365-day change log come with Max, from €79. Saved filters and custom table views start on Grow.

**Conversations beyond Outlook.** cobra's pricing page lists Outlook as its Microsoft 365 integration and NFON or Teams for calls; its integrations page shows SMS and WhatsApp sending as a marketing interface. If your clients write to you on WhatsApp or LinkedIn, Customermates keeps those threads in the same [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) as email, linked to the contact.

**Open source.** cobra sells per-user licences, and its pages name no open-source licence. The Customermates core is AGPL-3.0, so you can read the code and run it on your own server.

## Where Customermates fits

Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM built in Germany for consultants, agencies, other relationship-driven service businesses and founders.

**Conversations next to the deal.** From Pro, the inbox brings Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one list. You reply on the original channel, and every thread with a linked contact shows on that contact's timeline.

**AI help with CRM upkeep, in Customermates Cloud.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes threads, drafts replies, updates records and builds views. It uses hosted AI credits, with a monthly allowance per active user shown on the [pricing page](/pricing). Routines run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes. If you prefer Claude, ChatGPT or another client, connect it to the [MCP server](/docs/mcp), which offers [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan and on self-hosted installs.

**A small, fixed model.** Contacts, organizations, deals, services and tasks, with custom fields of ten types, table and board layouts, dashboards, roles and an audit log on every plan. The interface is available in English, German, French, Italian and Spanish.

**Open source, with a hosting choice.** The AGPL-3.0 core runs on your own server with Docker Compose and PostgreSQL, including REST, webhooks and MCP. A [self-hosted install](/features/self-hosted) has no inbox, no Mate and no hosted credits. In Customermates Cloud, the primary database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region (a selected setting, not a contractual guarantee), a [DPA](/dpa) is available and the subprocessors, including US providers, are listed in the legal pages.

## Where Customermates is the wrong choice

- **No newsletters, campaigns or bulk email.** Messages go out one at a time, written by you or drafted by Mate.
- **No events, web forms or Word templates.** Serial letters and event registration stay in another tool.
- **No telephony or SMS.** Store phone numbers in a Phone field and call from your own phone system.
- **No native mobile apps and no single sign-on.** Customermates runs in the phone's browser.
- **No custom objects and no field permissions.** Permissions apply per record type and action. Saved views are personal and cannot be shared.
- **No ready-made ERP or DATEV connectors.** Connect other systems through the REST API, webhooks or your own n8n instance.
- **No certification.** Customermates holds no compliance certification.

## Moving from cobra to Customermates

A worked example: a five-person consultancy on cobra CRM Cloud Essential, selling projects and retainers, with keywords such as "Industry" and "Region" on its addresses.

| In cobra | In Customermates |
|---|---|
| Company addresses | Organizations, renamed "Companies" in the Data model |
| Contact persons | Contacts, linked to their company |
| Sales opportunities | Deals, renamed "Opportunities" |
| Keywords and distribution lists | One single-select field per keyword group, plus a saved view filtered on it |
| Communication history (touchpoints) | The record's notes; new changes appear in the activity history |
| Newsletters, events, Word templates | No equivalent; keep a separate tool |

The opportunity stages become a stage field with probabilities, for example Inquiry 10%, First meeting 25%, Proposal sent 50%, Negotiation 75%, Won 100% and Lost 0%. Customermates multiplies each deal value by its stage probability and shows a weighted pipeline total. That is arithmetic on your own numbers; it does not predict anything.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export from cobra">cobra's pricing page lists export to Excel on every cloud plan. Export companies, contact persons and opportunities as separate workbooks and save them as .xlsx files. On cobra One, ask your cobra partner which export your version offers.</Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the workspace">Rename record types under My Company, Settings, Data model. Create one single-select field per keyword group, and set up the stage field with its probabilities.</Step>
  <Step title="Import one record type at a time">Import organizations first, then contacts, then deals. A link column finds the company you imported before by name. A workbook currently holds up to 10,000 rows, so split a larger address list. New contacts need a first and a last name, and a Notes column goes into the record's notes. The [records documentation](/docs/app-records) has the details.</Step>
  <Step title="Rebuild lists as views">Turn each cobra list into a saved view with a filter. Views are personal, so each person saves their own.</Step>
  <Step title="Connect channels and AI">On Pro or during the trial, connect a mailbox, LinkedIn or WhatsApp under My Profile, Channels. Then set up a routine for Mate, or connect your own AI client through MCP.</Step>
</Steps>

Each consultant can then own one routine for the weekly follow-up. A routine runs as its owner, with that person's channels, in Customermates Cloud, and uses hosted AI credits:

> Every Monday at 8:00, find my deals in "Proposal sent" where the contact has not written for 14 days. For each one, save a short follow-up draft in my latest conversation with that contact. Do not send anything.

Mate can send messages without asking again, so the last sentence matters if you want to review first.

cobra CRM Cloud runs in the browser from cobra's cloud and includes cobra AI as a fair-use flat rate. cobra One is the on-premise line that you run on your own servers or in a cloud you choose; on Essential and Grow you license your own AI provider. cobra says the two lines use different technology, so their features are not directly comparable.

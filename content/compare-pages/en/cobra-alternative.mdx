> **Short answer:** [cobra CRM](https://cobra.de/) is a German CRM with a cloud edition hosted in Germany and an on-premise edition, both from €36 per user per month ([cobra pricing](https://cobra.de/preise/), as of September 2026). Customermates is an open-source alternative from [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per user per month that you can use in the managed cloud or run on your own server. It adds a unified inbox for email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram on Pro and up, and lets AI work directly in the CRM: the hosted assistant Mate in the cloud, or your own AI client such as Claude or ChatGPT through MCP with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools.

## Why Look for a cobra CRM Alternative?

cobra has served German small and mid-sized businesses for decades, with strong address management, campaigns and document templates. Teams usually start looking elsewhere for one of these reasons:

- **Price per seat.** cobra's plans start at €36, €54 and €79 per user per month (Essential, Grow, Max). Customermates starts at [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] with the full core CRM in every plan.
- **Open source.** cobra is proprietary. Customermates' core is AGPL-3.0, so you can inspect the code and [self-host](/features/self-hosted) it with Docker.
- **Messaging beyond email.** Sales conversations increasingly happen on LinkedIn and WhatsApp. Customermates brings them into one [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) next to email.
- **AI that does the data entry.** With Customermates, an AI assistant can update deal stages, log notes and draft follow-ups in the CRM, within the permissions of the user it acts for.

If cobra's newsletter tooling, document templates or on-premise edition are central to how you work, cobra may still be the better fit. The comparison below shows where each one is stronger.

## Setup & Onboarding

cobra's cloud edition offers a free test account right after registration, and the on-premise edition is set up with cobra or a partner in your own data centre.

Customermates' managed cloud is ready as soon as you sign up. Set up your [deal pipeline](/features/pipeline) stages, import [contacts](/features/contact-management) and other records from XLSX workbooks one record type at a time, and start working. Self-hosting runs the open-source edition with Docker and PostgreSQL on your own infrastructure.

## Pricing: cobra CRM vs. Customermates

cobra publishes three plans for both its cloud and on-premise editions: Essential from €36, Grow from €54 and Max from €79 per user per month. Grow adds custom objects, marketing automation and a newsletter allowance; Max adds unlimited custom objects, an AI flat rate and more storage.

Customermates Starter costs [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per user per month and includes contacts, organizations, deals, services, tasks, custom columns, views, dashboards, permissions, audit log, REST API, webhooks and MCP. Pro ([[commercial.price.pro.monthly]]) adds the unified inbox with one connected account per user, and Business ([[commercial.price.business.monthly]]) adds three connected accounts per user and shared accounts. See the [pricing page](/pricing) for the current plan contract.

## Core CRM Features

Both products cover contacts, organizations, deals and pipelines. cobra's roots are in address management, campaigns and document templates, which is why many German businesses have relied on it for years.

Customermates is built around two things: [contacts and organizations](/features/contact-management), deals, [pipelines](/features/pipeline), [tasks](/features/task-management) and services that AI can operate directly, and a [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) that puts email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram next to the records they belong to. Deal stages carry weights, so the pipeline shows weighted deal values. Customermates does not include newsletter or marketing-automation tooling; if you send newsletters from your CRM today, plan a separate tool for that.

## Data Protection & Hosting

cobra hosts its cloud edition in a data centre in Germany and offers an on-premise edition for your own data centre.

In Customermates' managed cloud, the application database runs in the AWS Frankfurt region; hosting and other subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [DPA](/dpa) is available. If you need to control the full stack, you can [self-host](/features/self-hosted) the open-source edition. Note that self-hosting gives you the open-source CRM with MCP for your own AI clients, but not the unified inbox or the hosted assistant, which run on the managed cloud only.

## Integration & Automation

Customermates offers a REST API with an OpenAPI description, webhooks for CRM events, MCP for AI clients, and a community node for your own [n8n](https://n8n.io/) instance, which connects the CRM to hundreds of other services. On the managed cloud, routines run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a CRM event happens.

cobra lists API access and automation features that grow with the plan, and an AI flat rate on Max. Compare the exact integration you need on both sides during the trial.

## Who Should Switch from cobra CRM?

Customermates is a strong cobra CRM alternative for teams that:

- Want a lower price per seat with the full core CRM in every plan
- Prefer **open-source software** they can inspect and self-host
- Sell over LinkedIn, WhatsApp or Instagram as well as email and want one inbox in the CRM
- Want AI to keep the CRM current instead of typing updates by hand

Stay with cobra if newsletters and marketing automation inside the CRM, or a vendor-supported on-premise installation, matter more to you than these points.

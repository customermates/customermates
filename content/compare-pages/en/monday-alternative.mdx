> A good monday CRM alternative depends on why you are leaving. If a two-person firm pays for a third seat, if the Basic plan's five columns per board are too few, or if clients write on WhatsApp and LinkedIn more than by email, Customermates is worth a trial: it bills per user from one seat and puts five channels in one inbox from Pro. If you rely on AI lead sourcing, quotes, email sequences or mobile apps, or your projects already run on monday.com boards, stay with monday CRM. Customermates publishes this comparison, and every monday detail comes from monday.com's own pages.

## Who monday CRM is a good choice for

monday CRM is built on the same board platform as monday work management. Every field is a column you add, which makes a pipeline quick to reshape. monday.com describes the CRM as covering the revenue cycle "from lead management to customer success" on one platform, and that is the strongest argument for teams whose delivery already lives in monday.com.

The paid tiers add a lot on top. From Standard, monday CRM includes a comms hub, iOS and Android apps, and AI agents: a Lead Sourcing Agent that "finds, enriches, and scores new leads daily", a calling agent for outreach and qualification, and a meeting prep agent. Every plan creates quotes and invoices, from 20 a month on Basic to 250 on Pro. Pro adds mass emails, email sequences and an AI workflow builder. monday.com also runs a hosted MCP server for AI clients.

If that list describes how you sell, monday CRM is the stronger product for you.

## Why teams look for a monday CRM alternative

**Three seats minimum.** monday CRM costs €12, €17 or €28 per seat per month on Basic, Standard and Pro billed yearly, or €18, €25 and €41 billed monthly. Every plan bills at least three seats. The top plan, Ultimate, is quoted individually.

**Limits move with the plan.** Basic allows 1,000 active contacts and deals, five columns per board and one custom dashboard, with no custom automations. Five columns fill up fast on a deal board: stage, value, owner, close date, contact. Standard raises this to 10,000 contacts and deals, 15 columns and 250 custom automations a month; Pro to 100,000, 75 columns and 25,000 automations.

**Chat channels are not on the CRM pages.** monday CRM names Gmail, Outlook, Slack, Zoom, QuickBooks, Google Workspace and DocuSign among its integrations. WhatsApp, LinkedIn and Telegram are not named on its CRM or features pages; the monday marketplace lists third-party apps such as a WhatsApp integration by TimelinesAI.

**Cloud only, and full EU residency only for Enterprise accounts.** monday.com hosts in three regions: United States, European Union (Germany) and Asia Pacific (Australia). Its privacy trust center says only Enterprise accounts in the EU region get region-bound residency, in which sub-processors also keep customer data in the EU. For other accounts, sub-processors outside the region may be used. monday.com's pricing and CRM pages offer no self-hosted edition.

## Where Customermates fits

Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM for small client-facing teams: consultants, agencies, other service businesses and founders.

**A CRM model instead of boards you design.** Contacts, organizations, deals, services and tasks come linked to each other, and you can rename the record types, for example deals to projects. The deal stage is a single-select field with a win probability per option, and the weighted pipeline is value times probability, a fixed calculation. Any list switches between table and board, so a monday-style status board is a layout setting. The [records documentation](/docs/app-records) has the details.

**Conversations next to the deal.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one list linked to your contacts, and you reply on the original channel.

**AI that keeps records current.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes threads, drafts replies and updates records. It uses hosted AI credits, a monthly allowance per active user listed on the [pricing page](/pricing). Routines run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes. If you prefer Claude, ChatGPT or another AI client, connect it through [MCP](/docs/mcp) on every plan.

**A hosting choice.** In Customermates Cloud the database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, and a [DPA](/dpa) and the [subprocessor list](/subprocessors), US providers included, are public. The AGPL-3.0 core also runs on your own server with Docker Compose; a [self-hosted install](/features/self-hosted) has REST, webhooks and MCP but no inbox and no Mate.

<AcquisitionCallout title="Where Customermates is the wrong choice" variant="boundary">
It is not project management: no Gantt charts or timelines, and tasks have no built-in due date or status, only custom fields you add. It has no lead sourcing, enrichment or scoring, no calling, no quotes or invoices, no sequences or mass email. There are no native mobile apps and no SSO. Saved views are personal. Customermates Cloud has no free plan, only a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial; the self-hosted core has no licence cost. Customermates holds no compliance certification.
</AcquisitionCallout>

## Moving a monday CRM board to Customermates

| In monday CRM | In Customermates |
|---|---|
| Boards of contacts, accounts and deals | Contacts, organizations and deals |
| Status column for the deal stage | Stage, a single-select field with win probabilities |
| Other status or dropdown columns | Single-select custom fields |
| People column | Assigned users |
| Date column, such as close date | Date custom field |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export each board">In monday.com, open the board menu, choose More actions, then Export board to Excel. Only table views export. You can include subitems and updates; updates land on a separate sheet of the workbook.</Step>
  <Step title="Tidy the workbook">The Customermates import reads the first sheet and lets you match its columns to fields. Delete title rows above the headers, and paste updates you want to keep into a notes column.</Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the workspace">Create your custom fields and stage options, and set the win probabilities under My Company › Settings. Invite colleagues before importing deals, so the people column can be matched to them.</Step>
  <Step title="Import one record type at a time">Under Import and export, choose Add from file: organizations first, then contacts, then deals. Link columns match records by name, so a deal finds the organization you imported. File limits are in the [records documentation](/docs/app-records).</Step>
  <Step title="Connect channels and rebuild automations">On Pro or during the trial, connect a mailbox, LinkedIn or WhatsApp under My Profile › Channels. Rebuild the monday automations you relied on as routines, or with webhooks and your own tools.</Step>
</Steps>

A stage rule makes a good first routine (Customermates Cloud, uses hosted AI credits; the email draft needs a connected mailbox, so Pro or higher). Choose When something changes, pick the event Deal Updated, put Stage under Changed fields and add the record filter Stage is Won. Instruction: "Create a task called Kick-off call, link it to this deal and its organization, and assign it to me. Save an email draft to the deal's contact proposing a kick-off call next week. Do not send it." Keep the last sentence: Mate sends messages when an instruction asks it to, so a routine that should only draft has to say so. A routine runs as its owner, with that owner's channels; the [routines documentation](/docs/app-routines) lists its limits.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is monday a good CRM tool?">
For teams that already work in monday.com, yes. It shares the platform's boards and automations and, from Standard, adds AI agents, the comms hub and mobile apps. The trade-offs are the three-seat minimum, per-plan limits on contacts, columns and automations, and no self-hosted edition.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a free version of monday CRM?">
No. monday CRM has a 14-day trial and no permanent free plan; nonprofits can apply for discounted plans. The free plan for up to two seats belongs to monday work management. Customermates Cloud has no free plan either, only a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial; the self-hosted core has no licence cost, but no inbox and no Mate.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What are the best monday CRM alternatives?">
It depends on the gap. For a sales pipeline with email sync, compare Pipedrive and HubSpot. For an open-source CRM you can host yourself, or for WhatsApp and LinkedIn in the same inbox as email, Customermates is one option. If you need projects rather than a CRM, look at OpenProject.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Where does monday.com store data for European customers?">
monday.com has an EU region in Germany. According to its privacy trust center, only Enterprise accounts in the EU region keep customer data in the EU including sub-processors; on other plans sub-processors outside the region may be used. Customermates Cloud has its database configured in the AWS Frankfurt region and lists its subprocessors, including US providers.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can Customermates replace monday work management?">
No. Customermates is a CRM with table and board views, not a project tool. You can keep projects in monday.com and move only the client side: export the client boards to Excel and import them one record type at a time.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

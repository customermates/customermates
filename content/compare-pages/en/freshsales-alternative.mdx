> [Freshsales](https://www.freshworks.com/crm/sales/) by Freshworks is a sales-focused CRM with AI-powered features through Freddy AI. It offers a free plan and positions itself as affordable, but essential features like API access, advanced workflows, and audit logs are locked behind higher-priced tiers. What starts at $9 per user quickly becomes $39-59 when you need the tools that matter. Customermates is an open-source Freshsales alternative with transparent tiers from [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]]/user/month, full API access included at every tier, a self-hosting option, and no core CRM feature gated behind a higher plan. Two things set it apart: AI assistants like Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini operate the CRM directly through a native MCP integration ([[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools), doing the data entry, and a native unified inbox brings email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Telegram into one place from the Pro tier, with the hosted assistant able to read and draft across every channel and send after your confirmation.

## Why Switch from Freshsales?

Teams move away from Freshsales for several recurring reasons. The free plan is heavily restricted and serves primarily as a lead-generation tool. Paid plans gate critical features behind expensive tiers: API access requires Pro ($39/user/month), audit logs require Pro, and advanced workflow automation is limited on lower plans. Freddy AI bot sessions are capped and billed as add-ons.

Customermates takes a different approach. Every core CRM feature ships at every tier, starting at [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per user per month. [API access](/features/api), webhooks and MCP have no plan restrictions, a separately operated [n8n](/features/workflow-automation) instance connects through the community node, and your own AI client (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini) helps manage [lead tracking](/features/lead-tracking) and follow-ups over MCP, billed by your AI provider rather than by bot session.

## Setup and Onboarding

Customermates' managed cloud is ready as soon as you sign up. Configure your [deal stages](/features/pipeline), import your [contacts](/features/contact-management) from an XLSX workbook, and begin working. The interface is designed to be understood without training or onboarding sessions.

Freshsales offers a 21-day free trial of its full feature set, which sounds generous but creates a problem: teams configure workflows and integrations during the trial, only to lose access to many of those features when the trial ends and they choose a lower-priced plan. The actual onboarding for a production setup typically takes hours to days, depending on how many Freshworks ecosystem products you want to connect.

## Pricing: Transparent Tiers vs. Feature-Gated Plans

The pricing difference between Customermates and Freshsales is significant. Customermates starts at **[[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per user per month** (Starter), with every core CRM feature, API access, webhooks, and MCP access for external AI clients included. Higher tiers (Pro at [[commercial.price.pro.monthly]], Business at [[commercial.price.business.monthly]]) add the unified inbox and a larger hosted AI allowance; they do not gate core CRM functionality. [Freshsales pricing](https://www.freshworks.com/crm/pricing/) has three paid tiers plus a free plan (as of 2026):

- **Growth**: $9/user/month (annual) or $11/user/month (monthly)

The Growth plan appears affordable but is heavily restricted: no API access, limited workflows (5 sequences), no audit logs, no territory management, and limited custom reports. To work productively, most teams need at least the Pro plan at $39/user/month.

## AI Features: Freddy AI vs. Practical Automation

Freshsales markets Freddy AI heavily, offering lead scoring, deal insights, forecasting, and AI-generated emails. However, most Freddy AI features are reserved for Pro and Enterprise plans. Bot sessions are limited to 500 per account and additional sessions require a paid add-on. The result is that AI becomes a cost center rather than a productivity multiplier.

Customermates does not offer lead scoring or AI forecasting. Instead, AI agents (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini) connect over MCP and help with [lead qualification](/features/lead-tracking), note summarization and updates, paid through your own AI provider. On the cloud, the hosted Mate assistant works with monthly AI credits included in each plan. With a separately operated [n8n](/features/workflow-automation) instance you can build AI workflows that connect to external services.

## Feature Comparison

Both systems cover CRM fundamentals: [contact management](/features/contact-management), [deal pipelines](/features/pipeline), [task management](/features/task-management), and [email integration](/features/email-integration) (Pro+ on Customermates). The critical difference is access.

Freshsales distributes features across its plan tiers. The Growth plan limits you to basic functionality. Custom fields, workflow sequences, territory management, and advanced reporting are gated behind Pro or Enterprise. This creates a constant pressure to upgrade.

Customermates gives every team the full core CRM from day one. [Custom fields](/features/contact-management), [reporting dashboards](/features/reporting), the audit log, REST API, webhooks and MCP are all included at the base price; the unified inbox starts on Pro.

### Workflows and Automation

Freshsales limits workflow sequences to 5 on the Growth plan, 10 on Pro, and 25 on Enterprise. Marketing journeys and sales sequences have separate limits per tier.

Customermates has no built-in workflow builder or email sequences. Automation runs through [webhooks, the REST API and a separately operated n8n](/features/workflow-automation), and on the cloud through routines that run a saved prompt on a schedule or CRM event. You can connect CRM events to third-party integrations and AI-driven processes outside the CRM.

### API and Integrations

Freshsales reserves API access for the Pro plan ($39/user/month). If you need to connect your CRM to other business tools programmatically, you must upgrade. The Freshworks marketplace offers integrations, but many advanced connectors also require higher plans.

Customermates provides full [API access](/features/api) at the base price. With your own [n8n instance](/features/workflow-automation) and the community node, you can connect to many third-party services without plan restrictions, for example [Slack via n8n](/features/slack-integration) or custom [CRM integrations](/features/integrations). Connected mailboxes in the [unified inbox](/features/email-integration) start on Pro.

## Data Protection and GDPR

In the managed cloud, the Customermates application database runs in the AWS Frankfurt region; hosting and other subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [DPA](/dpa) is available. Customermates holds no compliance certification. If you need full control over where data lives, [self-host](/features/self-hosted) the open-source core.

Freshworks runs its products in several data centre regions and uses subprocessors of its own. Check which region your Freshsales account uses and review Freshworks' DPA and subprocessor list for your requirements.

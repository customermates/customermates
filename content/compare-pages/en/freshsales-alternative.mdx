> Look for a Freshsales alternative when your clients write to you on LinkedIn, WhatsApp or Telegram more than they pick up the phone, or when you want a CRM you can run on your own server. Freshsales is built around phone, email and website chat: a dialer comes with every plan, sales sequences start on Pro, and its chat channels are WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, LINE and Google Business Messages. Customermates puts Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram in one inbox from Pro, and its open-source core runs on your own server. If you call prospects all day or send email sequences, Freshsales will serve you better than Customermates. Customermates publishes this comparison; every Freshsales detail comes from freshworks.com and was checked on 8 October 2026.

## Who Freshsales is a good choice for

Freshsales is the sales CRM of Freshworks, the company that also makes Freshdesk, Freshchat, Freshservice and Freshmarketer. Its strength is selling by phone and email from one screen. Every plan, Free included, has a built-in phone with local and toll-free numbers, two-way email sync, a website chat widget and apps for iOS and Android.

Growth adds call recording, a team inbox, bulk email of up to 250 messages per user per day, web forms and 20 workflows. Pro adds a power dialer, sales sequences, up to 10 pipelines, custom reports and the Freddy AI insights: contact scoring, deal insights and next best action. Enterprise adds forecasting insights, field-level permissions, up to 10 custom modules, a sandbox, audit logs and automatic profile enrichment.

If your team works the phone, runs sequences or already supports customers in Freshdesk, those are good reasons to stay.

## Why teams look for a Freshsales alternative

**The advertised price is the yearly rate.** Growth costs $9 per user per month billed yearly and $11 billed monthly. Pro costs $39 or $47, Enterprise $59 or $71. Freshworks lists the same figures in euros.

**The step up is where the cost sits.** Sequences, multiple pipelines and the Freddy AI insights need Pro, at roughly four times the Growth price. Audit logs, field-level permissions and custom modules need Enterprise. API access starts on Growth; the Free plan has none.

**Some parts are sold separately.** Paid plans include 500 Freddy AI Agent sessions once per account for customer-facing chatbots; after that, sessions cost $49 per 100. Branded documents cost $19 per user per month, and extra workflows $5 for 10.

**Chat channels lean towards customer service.** WhatsApp and Facebook Messenger messages are answered through the chat widget, and LINE, Google Business Messages and SMS providers connect from Growth. The plan comparison lists no LinkedIn, Instagram or Telegram channel, which matters if that is where your clients write.

**Hosted plans only.** Freshworks sells Freshsales as hosted plans. Its pricing pages list no self-hosted edition and no open-source licence.

## Where Customermates fits

Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM for small client-facing teams: consultants, agencies, other service businesses and founders. It is built in Germany.

**Conversations next to the deal.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn (including Sales Navigator and Recruiter), WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one list, and you reply on the original channel. Link a participant to a contact once, and every conversation with that address or handle appears on the contact's timeline.

**AI that does the CRM upkeep.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes conversations, drafts replies, updates records and builds saved views, using hosted AI credits. It asks for a fresh approval before every delete, but sends a message without one when you ask it to send, so ask for a draft when you want to read it first. [Routines](/docs/app-routines) run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes. Prefer Claude, ChatGPT or another client you already pay for? Connect it to the [MCP server](/docs/mcp), with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan and on self-hosted installs.

**Open source, a cloud configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, or your own server.** The core is AGPL-3.0 and runs with Docker Compose and PostgreSQL 16, including REST, webhooks and MCP. A [self-hosted install](/features/self-hosted) has no subscription, and also no inbox, no Mate and no hosted credits. In Customermates Cloud the database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, a [DPA](/dpa) is available, and the [subprocessors](/subprocessors), including US providers, are listed. Customermates holds no compliance certification. The audit log records changes to records and workspace settings on every plan.

## Where Customermates is the wrong choice

Check these before you start a trial:

- **No telephony.** There is no dialer, no call recording and no SMS. A phone number goes into a custom field, and calls happen on your own phone system.
- **No sequences and no bulk email.** Outreach goes one message at a time, written by you or drafted by Mate.
- **No chat widget and no web forms.** Leads from your website reach Customermates through the REST API or a workflow in an n8n instance you run yourself.
- **No scoring, enrichment or AI forecasting.** The weighted pipeline multiplies each deal's value by its stage probability. That is arithmetic on your own numbers, and it does not predict anything.
- **No custom modules or field-level permissions.** You work with five record types that you can rename, and permissions apply per record type and action. Saved views are personal and cannot be shared.
- **No native mobile apps and no free plan.** Customermates runs in the phone's browser, and after a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial you choose a paid plan.

## A worked setup: a consultancy moving from Freshsales Growth

Take a four-person consultancy that sells advisory projects. Two consultants hear from clients mostly on LinkedIn, the other two by email.

| In Freshsales | In Customermates |
|---|---|
| Contacts | Contacts |
| Accounts | Organizations, renamed "Accounts" from the presets |
| Deals in the sales pipeline | Deals with a Stage field and win probabilities |
| Product catalog | Services, renamed "Products", linked to deals with a quantity |
| Contact lifecycle stage | A single-select field "Lifecycle stage" and a saved view for each stage you work |
| Record tags | A single-select field, because Customermates has no tags |
| Tasks | Tasks, with a custom date field "Due" because tasks have no built-in due date |
| Phone numbers and call logs | A Phone custom field; calls are not logged |

The team renames the Stage options to Lead, Qualified, Proposal sent, Negotiation, Won and Lost, and enters win probabilities of 10%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100% and 0% under My Company › Settings. Each consultant saves a board of their own deals grouped by stage. The partner keeps a table of all open deals grouped by assigned user.

Each person connects one channel, two LinkedIn accounts and two Gmail mailboxes, so Pro fits, at [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.4]] a month for the team. If one consultant wants LinkedIn and email side by side, the workspace moves to Business at [[commercial.price.business.monthly.seats.4]].

Freshsales sequences have no counterpart, so each consultant owns one routine that prepares follow-ups instead. A routine runs as its owner, so it sees that person's deals and saves drafts in their own channels. It runs in Customermates Cloud and uses hosted AI credits:

> Every day at 8:00, find my deals in "Proposal sent" where the contact's last message is more than seven days old. For each deal, save a short follow-up draft in my most recent conversation with that contact. Do not send anything. List the deals in the run summary.

## Switching from Freshsales in five steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export from Freshsales">According to Freshworks' help center, an admin opens Admin Settings › Account, clicks Export data and picks the record types to include. Freshworks emails the export as a ZIP file. You can export up to five times a day, and the full time range only once.</Step>
  <Step title="Save each file as an Excel workbook">Customermates imports .xlsx workbooks only; CSV files are not accepted. Open each record type's file in Excel or LibreOffice and save it as .xlsx, one record type per workbook.</Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the workspace">Rename record types if your words differ, create custom fields for the Freshsales fields you keep, such as lifecycle stage, phone and lead source, and set the stage field with its probabilities.</Step>
  <Step title="Import one record type at a time">On each list, open Import and export, then Add from file: organizations first, then contacts, then deals. If your export has no accounts file, build the organizations workbook from the account column of the contacts file. Link columns match records by name, so a contact's account column finds the organization imported before. Map email addresses and WhatsApp numbers to the contact's channels. Size limits are in the [records documentation](/docs/app-records).</Step>
  <Step title="Connect channels and AI">On Pro or during the trial, connect a mailbox, LinkedIn or WhatsApp under My Profile › Channels. Email history from the export does not import; the inbox shows conversations from the accounts you connect. Then set up routines, or connect your own AI client through MCP.</Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Are Freshsales and Freshworks the same?">
No. Freshworks is the company, and Freshsales is its sales CRM. Freshworks also sells Freshdesk and Freshchat for customer service, Freshservice for IT service management and Freshmarketer for marketing.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What is the best Freshsales alternative?">
It depends on why you are leaving. If you want follow-up cadences and sales forecasting on an entry paid plan, look at Zoho CRM. For marketing and sales from one vendor, look at HubSpot. For a simple contact CRM with data stored in Germany and a flat package price, look at CentralStationCRM. If your clients write on LinkedIn, WhatsApp and email and you want AI to keep the CRM current, Customermates fits; we publish this page, so weigh that accordingly.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a free alternative to Freshsales?">
Yes, with limits. Zoho CRM is free for up to three users, HubSpot's free tools for up to two, and CentralStationCRM's Starter package for three users and 200 contacts. Customermates Cloud has no free plan. A self-hosted Customermates install has no subscription, so you pay only for your own server and providers, but it has no inbox and no Mate.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Freshsales have an API?">
Yes, from the Growth plan; the Free plan has no API access. Customermates includes the REST API, webhooks and the MCP server on every plan and on self-hosted installs.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Where does Freshsales store data, and is it GDPR compliant?">
From Growth, Freshsales lets you store data in a US, EU, Indian or Australian data center. Whether your use is GDPR compliant is for you to assess as the controller, with the vendor's data processing terms. Customermates has its cloud database configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, offers a DPA and lists its subprocessors, including US providers.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is Freshsales a good CRM?">
For teams that sell by phone and email, Freshsales covers a lot in one product: the built-in phone, two-way email sync and the chat widget are on every plan, and sequences, a power dialer and the Freddy AI insights come with Pro. It fits less well if your clients mostly write on LinkedIn, Instagram or Telegram, which the plan comparison does not list, or if you need to host the CRM yourself.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

## Who is HubSpot the better choice for?

HubSpot sells one platform for sales, marketing and service, so any HubSpot CRM comparison starts with how much of it you use. On its euro pricing page, the free plan gives up to two users contacts, companies, deals, the Breeze assistant, website chat, tickets, meeting booking and two-way sync with dozens of apps. Gmail, Outlook and Exchange inboxes connect on every plan, as does the HubSpot mobile app.

Starter adds calling, AI meeting recording, targeted email campaigns, simple automation and AI agents. Professional adds advanced automation, automated outreach and follow-up, revenue forecasting, custom objects, custom reports, AI quotes, a help desk and single sign-on. Enterprise adds team hierarchies, a sandbox and control over which data each user can access and edit. If your team uses several of these together, switching will probably cost you more than it saves.

## Why are people leaving HubSpot?

We cannot see why individual customers leave. HubSpot's own pages show where a small team's costs and limits grow fastest.

**The step to Professional is large.** Starter costs €7 per seat per month billed yearly or €10 billed monthly, labelled a limited-time offer (65% and 50% off, with no end date shown); HubSpot's pricing supplement lists €20 as the Starter starting price. Starter stops at 10 users. Professional costs €90 billed yearly or €100 billed monthly, and HubSpot's pricing FAQ says Professional and Enterprise customers must buy onboarding. Its onboarding page lists a one-time €2,500 for Professional and €5,000 for Enterprise. For three people on Professional billed yearly that is €3,240 for the year plus €2,500, so €5,740 in year one, against €252 on Starter.

**AI and automation use credits.** HubSpot Credits are spent by AI agents, automation and one-to-many email. Starter includes 5,000 a month, Professional 10,000 and Enterprise 15,000. Unused credits do not roll over, and extra credits cost €0.010 each in HubSpot's supplement.

**WhatsApp needs a higher tier, and LinkedIn is not an inbox channel.** Team email, website chat, Facebook Messenger and forms reach HubSpot's inbox on every plan. WhatsApp requires Marketing Hub or Service Hub Professional or Enterprise and a WhatsApp Business account; HubSpot's guide says a personal WhatsApp account cannot be connected. HubSpot's list of channel types has no LinkedIn or Instagram; other apps come in as custom channels built on its API, from Sales Hub or Service Hub Professional.

**Hosted by HubSpot only.** HubSpot runs on AWS in the US, Germany, Canada and Australia, and paid accounts can change their data center. Its hosting FAQ says data can still be processed outside that location in limited cases, for example by subprocessors or support staff, and that usage data goes to the US. There is no self-hosted edition.

## Is HubSpot still free?

Yes. HubSpot Free costs nothing for up to two users and up to 3,000 records, and it includes contacts, companies, deals, activity tracking, the Breeze assistant, website chat, tickets and two-way sync with dozens of apps. A third user means a paid plan, and Starter allows up to 10 paid users. Calling, AI meeting recording and AI agents with simple automation start on Starter. Customermates Free covers [[commercial.limit.free.maxMembers]] person and [[commercial.limit.free.maxRecords]] records at no cost, but without the inbox, Mate or routines.

## Is HubSpot hard to use?

We publish no ease-of-use rating and cannot measure it for your team. What HubSpot's pricing page does show is how wide the platform is: its plan comparison lists dozens of features across sales, marketing and service, and the setup work grows with the plan. On Professional and Enterprise HubSpot requires an onboarding purchase, so plan time or money for setup there. A small team that only needs contacts, companies and deals has less to configure on HubSpot Free or Starter. The most reliable test is your own data: export one view from HubSpot, import it into the trial of each tool you are considering and see which one your team opens without being told to.

## Where Customermates fits (our product)

If HubSpot's costs or channel limits are what you want to leave behind, Customermates is a smaller CRM built around follow-up: conversations on the client record from Pro, a reply and next step drafted by Mate or your own AI agent, and you review, edit and send. It is open source and built in Germany.

**Conversations next to the deal.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn (including Sales Navigator and Recruiter), WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one list. You connect your own WhatsApp account with a QR code, link participants to contacts and reply on the original channel. Starter has no inbox; the trial works like Pro.

**AI that does the CRM work.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes threads, drafts replies and updates records, using hosted AI credits with a monthly allowance per active user (see [pricing](/pricing)); there are no overage charges. Mate asks for approval before every delete, but sends messages without one, so tell it to save drafts when you want to review first. Routines run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes: [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] per user on Starter, [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]] on Pro. If you prefer Claude, ChatGPT or another client, connect it to the [MCP server](/docs/mcp) with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan; its model calls run on your own AI provider.

**Open source, with a hosting choice.** The AGPL-3.0 core runs on your own server with Docker Compose, including REST, webhooks and MCP. A [self-hosted install](/features/self-hosted) has no inbox, no Mate and no hosted credits. In Customermates Cloud the database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, a DPA is available, and subprocessors, including US providers, are listed. Customermates holds no compliance certification. Starter, Pro and Business are self-serve, with no onboarding fee.

## A worked setup: a consultancy leaving HubSpot

A four-person consultancy has outgrown HubSpot Free, which covers two users, and needs a paid plan. Two partners win work through LinkedIn and email; two consultants talk to clients on WhatsApp.

| In HubSpot | In Customermates |
|---|---|
| Companies | Organizations, renamed "Companies" |
| Deals | Deals, renamed "Projects" |
| Products | Services, renamed "Packages", added to projects as line items |
| Lifecycle stage | A Single choice field "Client status" and a saved view filtered on it |
| Deal owner | Assigned users on the deal |
| Deal stages | The Stage field with a probability per option |

The stages are Inquiry 10%, Scoping call 25%, Proposal sent 50%, Verbal yes 80%, Won 100% and Lost 0%. Customermates multiplies each project's value by its stage probability, and a chart widget on the Dashboard sums the weighted values: arithmetic on your own numbers, not a prediction.

Each person connects one account, so Pro fits at [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.4]] a month for the team; a partner who needs LinkedIn and email side by side moves the workspace to Business. Everyone saves their own board of open projects grouped by stage. Each partner owns a routine scheduled for Fridays at 15:00. It runs as its owner in Customermates Cloud, uses hosted AI credits, and carries this instruction:

> Find my projects in "Proposal sent" where the contact has not written in seven days. For each one, save a short follow-up draft in my latest conversation with that contact. Do not send anything. List the projects in the run summary.

## Switching from HubSpot in five steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export from HubSpot">Open companies, contacts and deals in turn, choose Export, pick XLSX and select all properties. HubSpot emails a download link that expires after 30 days.</Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the workspace">Create custom fields for the properties you keep. Give the Stage field the same stage names as HubSpot; the import flags every row whose stage matches no option. Multiple-checkbox properties map to a Multiple choice field.</Step>
  <Step title="Import one list at a time">Open More actions, then Add from file. Import organizations, then contacts, then deals, so link columns find records by name. If the file has a column named ID or Record ID, set it to Do not add; otherwise the wizard treats it as a Customermates record to update. Limits and mapping rules are in the [records guide](/docs/app-records).</Step>
  <Step title="Bring over what the import skips">The import takes records, fields, notes and contact channels, not HubSpot's activity timeline. Copy what you need into a notes column first.</Step>
  <Step title="Connect channels, then run both in parallel">Connect a mailbox, LinkedIn or WhatsApp under Settings, Channels, set up routines or your MCP client, and keep HubSpot for reference for a few weeks without entering new data there.</Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="What are the downsides of HubSpot for a small team?">
Mainly four: the price step from Starter to Professional with its required onboarding, the 10-user cap on Starter, WhatsApp only from Marketing Hub or Service Hub Professional with a Business account, and no option to run it on your own servers. If you need marketing, sales and service in one system, HubSpot covers more of it than any alternative on this page.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Do I have to buy HubSpot onboarding?">
On Professional and Enterprise, yes: HubSpot's pricing FAQ says these plans are required to purchase onboarding, and its onboarding page lists €2,500 for Professional and €5,000 for Enterprise as one-time fees. Starter does not require it. Customermates has no onboarding fee.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a free alternative to HubSpot?">
For two users, HubSpot Free is hard to beat. Zoho CRM's free edition covers three. If you can run a server, Twenty and the AGPL-3.0 core of Customermates self-host without a licence fee, though self-hosted Customermates has no inbox and no Mate.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What are open-source alternatives to HubSpot?">
Twenty, with custom objects and its own workflows, and Customermates, whose cloud version adds the unified inbox and Mate to its AGPL-3.0 core. Neither replaces HubSpot's marketing tools.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is Zoho or HubSpot better for a small team?">
Zoho CRM is free for three users and includes sales forecasts from its first paid edition, Standard. HubSpot is free for two and puts marketing and service tools on the same platform. Which matters more depends on whether you need those tools.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does HubSpot store data in the EU?">
It can. HubSpot hosts in Germany as well as the US, Canada and Australia, and paid accounts can choose their data center, but its FAQ names exceptions such as subprocessors, support access and usage data sent to the US. Customermates has its cloud database configured in the AWS Frankfurt region and lists its subprocessors, including US providers.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How do I move my data from HubSpot to Customermates?">
Export companies, contacts and deals as XLSX, set up fields and stage names, then import organizations, contacts and deals in that order. Leave any Record ID column unmapped.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

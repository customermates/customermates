## Why Teams Look for a HubSpot Alternative

[HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/) is one of the best-known CRM platforms. It started as an inbound marketing tool and has grown into an enterprise platform spanning sales, marketing, service, and content management. That breadth comes with serious trade-offs.

The most common reasons teams leave HubSpot are escalating costs and platform complexity that requires dedicated administrators. According to [G2 reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/hubspot-crm/reviews), the most frequent complaints center on pricing surprises when scaling and the difficulty of configuring advanced features without consulting help.

For small and mid-sized businesses that need a CRM that works from day one (without a six-figure annual contract), a focused HubSpot alternative makes more sense.

## Pricing: Transparent Tiers vs. Feature-Gated Ladders

The pricing philosophy is the most striking difference between Customermates and HubSpot. Customermates starts at **[[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per user per month** (Starter), and every core CRM feature (contacts, deals, custom fields, roles and permissions, audit log, REST API and webhooks, and MCP access for external AI clients) ships in every tier. Higher tiers (Pro at [[commercial.price.pro.monthly]] and Business at [[commercial.price.business.monthly]]) add messaging and a larger hosted AI allowance: the unified inbox for email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Telegram, more connected accounts, the read-only calendar view, and more monthly Assistant credits. No core CRM feature is ever locked behind a paywall. Check the [pricing page](/pricing) for full details.

HubSpot uses a complex hub-and-tier model. Each hub (Marketing, Sales, Service, Content) has three paid tiers. For Sales Hub, HubSpot's pricing page lists (as of September 2026):

- **Starter**: €20 per seat/month, basic features only
- **Professional**: €90 per seat/month billed annually (€100 monthly), plus a one-time €1,470 onboarding fee; unlocks automation, custom reporting, and sequences
- **Enterprise**: from €150 per seat/month plus €3,420 onboarding; required for audit logs, custom objects, and advanced permissions

For a team of 10 users needing automation and reporting, 10 Sales Hub Professional seats cost **€900/month** billed annually, plus the €1,470 onboarding fee (as of September 2026). The same team on Customermates Starter pays **[[commercial.price.starter.monthly.seats.10]]/month**, with core CRM functionality, [reporting](/features/reporting), webhooks, the REST API and MCP access for external AI clients included; none of it is gated behind a higher tier. Customermates has no built-in workflow builder or sequences; [automation](/features/workflow-automation) runs through webhooks, REST and a separately operated n8n.

Over the first 12 months, that is **€12,270** for HubSpot Sales Hub Professional (€10,800 in seats plus €1,470 onboarding) versus **[[commercial.price.starter.monthly.seats.10.months.12]]** for Customermates Starter.

## Setup and Onboarding

With Customermates, you [sign up](/auth/signup) and the managed cloud is ready right away. The interface is self-explanatory: import your [contacts](/features/contact-management) from an XLSX workbook, set up your [deal stages](/features/pipeline), assign [tasks](/features/task-management), and begin [tracking sales](/features/sales-tracking) with real data immediately.

HubSpot requires serious onboarding investment. Before you can effectively use the platform, you need to understand its hierarchy of hubs, lifecycle stages, property types, and tool-within-tool structures. Many teams hire dedicated HubSpot administrators or external consultants just to configure the system properly. That cost rarely appears in the initial pricing comparison.

For smaller teams without a dedicated operations person, this overhead is rarely justified. What should be a productivity tool becomes a project in itself.

## Core CRM Features Compared

Both Customermates and HubSpot cover the CRM essentials: [contact and organization management](/features/contact-management), [deal tracking with pipelines](/features/pipeline), [task management](/features/task-management), [email integration](/features/email-integration) (Pro+ on Customermates), and team collaboration. The fundamental difference lies in philosophy and access.

### Contact and Deal Management

Customermates offers unlimited contacts, organizations, and deals, plus custom fields across ten field types, on every plan. HubSpot also provides unlimited contacts on its free tier, but contact scoring, calculated properties, and custom objects require Professional or Enterprise plans.

Both platforms support [pipeline views](/features/pipeline) in table and Kanban layouts. Customermates includes both on every plan; HubSpot restricts some pipeline features to paid tiers.

### Reporting and Analytics

Customermates includes [reporting and analytics](/features/reporting) in the base plan. You can build custom dashboards, see weighted pipeline values from per-stage probabilities, and group deals by owner without any upgrade.

HubSpot limits free and Starter users to basic, pre-built reports. Custom report builders, advanced analytics, and revenue attribution require the Professional tier (€90 per seat/month billed annually, plus €1,470 onboarding). For data-driven teams, this is a significant limitation.

### AI Agents

Customermates integrates natively with your AI tools. Connect Claude, ChatGPT, or any MCP-compatible assistant through the built-in MCP server ([[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools) to help your team review [leads](/features/lead-management), draft follow-up messages, and keep records current. On the cloud, the hosted Mate assistant also works across the unified inbox (Pro and up) for email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Telegram. MCP access is included on every plan; you pay your AI provider for the client you connect. HubSpot also publishes its own MCP server; the [HubSpot MCP guide](/blog/hubspot-mcp) explains what it covers and where it stops.

HubSpot offers AI features under its "Breeze" brand, but many advanced AI capabilities, such as predictive lead scoring and AI-powered content recommendations, are locked behind higher-tier plans or sold as paid add-ons.

## Automation and Integration

### Workflow Automation

Customermates connects to a separately operated [n8n](https://n8n.io/) instance through REST, webhooks, MCP, and a community node. Use n8n's connector catalog for accounting, external [Slack notifications](/features/slack-integration), email providers, or custom reporting; its hosting, plans, limits, and costs remain separate. Documented [API](/features/api) access starts at the base price.

HubSpot offers an extensive marketplace with hundreds of integrations. However, the most powerful automation features (custom workflow actions, advanced sequences, and higher API rate limits) are tied to Professional and Enterprise plans. Building the integrations you actually need often requires a plan upgrade.

### Sales Automation

[Sales automation](/features/sales-automation) in Customermates works through AI rather than a rule engine. Your own AI client over MCP, or the hosted Mate assistant on the cloud, can move deal stages, log notes and draft follow-ups, and cloud routines run a saved prompt on a schedule or CRM event. There are no email sequences, reminders or built-in workflow rules.

HubSpot's sales automation is powerful at the enterprise level, but its sequences, playbooks, and forecasting tools are gated behind Sales Hub Professional (€90 per seat/month billed annually) or Enterprise (from €150 per seat/month), as of September 2026.

## Data Protection and GDPR

For European businesses, data protection is a core requirement under GDPR, and it depends on how you use any tool as the controller.

In the managed [cloud CRM](/features/cloud-crm), the Customermates application database runs in the AWS Frankfurt region; hosting and other subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [DPA](/dpa) is available. Customermates holds no compliance certification. If you need full control over where data lives, [self-host](/features/self-hosted) the open-source core.

HubSpot offers data centres in the US and the EU (including Germany since 2021, selectable for paid accounts) and publishes its own DPA and subprocessor list. Review which region your account uses and which subprocessors apply before you decide.

## Open-Source Advantage

Customermates is fully open-source. This means:

- **Transparency**: You can inspect every line of code that handles your data
- **Less vendor lock-in**: Export records as XLSX workbooks or self-host at any time
- **Community-driven development**: Feature requests and bug fixes from a growing community
- **Custom extensions**: Build on top of the platform without waiting for vendor roadmaps

HubSpot is proprietary software. You cannot audit the code, you cannot self-host, and migrating away requires exporting data through limited export tools. Your CRM data lives entirely within HubSpot's ecosystem.

For teams that value long-term flexibility and data ownership, open-source is not just a technical choice; it is a business strategy.

HubSpot offers tiered support matching its tiered pricing. Free and Starter users get community forums and limited email support. Phone support requires Professional plans. Dedicated account managers are reserved for Enterprise customers.

## How to Migrate from HubSpot to Customermates

Switching CRMs sounds daunting, but the migration from HubSpot to Customermates is straightforward:

1. **Export your data** from HubSpot using its built-in export tools (contacts, companies, deals)
2. **Import into Customermates** as XLSX workbooks, one record type at a time, or through the [API](/features/api) or an AI agent over MCP
3. **Set up your pipelines** and custom fields to match your existing workflow
4. **Connect integrations** through [n8n](/features/workflow-automation) or the open API
5. **Invite your team**: the interface is self-explanatory, so onboarding takes minutes, not weeks

Most teams complete the full migration in less than a day.

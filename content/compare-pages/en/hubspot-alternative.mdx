> The right HubSpot alternative depends on what is pushing you away. If you mainly need a sales pipeline and want to skip the step up to HubSpot Professional, compare Pipedrive and Zoho CRM. If you want open-source software on your own server, look at Twenty or Customermates. If your clients write on WhatsApp and LinkedIn as often as by email, Customermates puts those conversations next to your deals from its Pro plan. If you depend on HubSpot's campaigns, forms, help desk or calling, stay. Customermates publishes this comparison; HubSpot details were checked on hubspot.com on 8 October 2026.

## Which alternative fits which reason

| Your reason for leaving | Look at | Why |
|---|---|---|
| You only use the CRM, and Professional's price or required onboarding is the problem | Pipedrive or Zoho CRM | Both sell a sales CRM on its own, per seat; Zoho has a free edition for three users |
| You want an open-source licence or your own server | Twenty or Customermates | HubSpot sells hosted plans only |
| You need record types of your own, such as properties or funds | Twenty, or stay on HubSpot Professional | Customermates has five fixed record types |

| You run email campaigns, forms, landing pages or a help desk | Stay on HubSpot | No alternative here replaces all of it |
| Two people, and the free plan covers what you do | Stay on HubSpot Free | No paid plan beats free |

## Who HubSpot is a good choice for

HubSpot sells one platform for sales, marketing and service. On its euro pricing page, the free plan gives up to two users contacts, companies, deals, the Breeze assistant, website chat, tickets, meeting booking and two-way sync with dozens of apps. Gmail, Outlook and Exchange inboxes connect on every plan, as does the HubSpot mobile app.

Starter adds calling, AI meeting recording, simple automation and AI agents. Professional adds advanced automation, sequences, revenue forecasting, custom objects, custom reports, quotes, a help desk and single sign-on. Enterprise adds team hierarchies, a sandbox and control over which data each user can access and edit. If your team uses several of these together, switching will probably cost you more than it saves.

## Why teams look for a HubSpot alternative

**The step to Professional is large.** Starter lists at €20 per seat per month, with a time-limited offer of €7 billed yearly or €10 monthly when checked, and stops at 10 users. Professional costs €90 or €100 per seat, and HubSpot's pricing FAQ says Professional and Enterprise customers must buy onboarding. Its US dollar page puts that at a one-time $1,500 for Sales Hub Professional and $3,500 for Enterprise.

**AI and automation use credits.** HubSpot Credits are spent by AI agents, automation and one-to-many email. On the euro page, Starter includes 5,000 a month, Professional 10,000 and Enterprise 15,000. Unused credits do not roll over.

**WhatsApp needs a higher tier, and LinkedIn is not an inbox channel.** Team email, website chat, Facebook Messenger and forms reach HubSpot's inbox on every plan. WhatsApp requires Marketing Hub or Service Hub Professional or Enterprise and a WhatsApp Business account. HubSpot's list of channel types has no LinkedIn or Instagram; other apps come in as custom channels built on its API, from Sales Hub or Service Hub Professional.

**Hosted by HubSpot only.** HubSpot runs on AWS in the US, Germany, Canada and Australia, and paid accounts can change their data center. Its hosting FAQ says data can still be processed outside that location in limited cases, for example by subprocessors or support staff, and that usage data goes to the US. There is no self-hosted edition.

## Where Customermates fits

Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM for small client-facing teams: consultants, agencies, other service businesses and founders. It is built in Germany.

**Conversations next to the deal.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn (including Sales Navigator and Recruiter), WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one list. You connect your own WhatsApp account with a QR code, link participants to contacts and reply on the original channel. Starter has no inbox; the trial works like Pro.

**AI that does the CRM work.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes threads, drafts replies and updates records, using hosted AI credits with a monthly allowance per active user (see [pricing](/pricing)); there are no overage charges. Mate asks for approval before every delete, but sends messages without one, so tell it to save drafts when you want to review first. Routines run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes: [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] per user on Starter, [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]] on Pro. If you prefer Claude, ChatGPT or another client, connect it to the [MCP server](/docs/mcp) with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan; its model calls run on your own AI provider.

**Open source, with a hosting choice.** The AGPL-3.0 core runs on your own server with Docker Compose, including REST, webhooks and MCP. A [self-hosted install](/features/self-hosted) has no inbox, no Mate and no hosted credits. In Customermates Cloud the database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, a DPA is available, and subprocessors, including US providers, are listed. Customermates holds no compliance certification. Starter, Pro and Business are self-serve, with no onboarding fee.

## Where Customermates is the wrong choice

- **No marketing or phone tools.** No email campaigns, sequences, bulk email, web forms, landing pages, website chat, calling or meeting recording.
- **No custom objects.** You get contacts, organizations, deals, services and tasks, which you can rename, plus custom fields of ten types.
- **No help desk, quotes or invoices.**
- **Simple tasks.** Tasks have assignees, relations, notes and custom fields, but no due dates or reminders.
- **No native mobile apps and no single sign-on.** Saved views are personal, and permissions apply per record type and action, not per field.
- **No free plan,** only a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial.

## A worked setup: a consultancy leaving HubSpot

A four-person consultancy on HubSpot Free needs a paid plan. Two partners win work through LinkedIn and email; two consultants talk to clients on WhatsApp.

| In HubSpot | In Customermates |
|---|---|
| Companies | Organizations, renamed "Companies" |
| Deals | Deals, renamed "Projects" |
| Products | Services, renamed "Packages", linked to projects |
| Lifecycle stage | A Single Select field "Client status" and a saved view filtered on it |
| Deal owner | Assigned users on the deal |
| Deal stages | The Stage field with a probability per option |

The stages are Inquiry 10%, Scoping call 25%, Proposal sent 50%, Verbal yes 80%, Won 100% and Lost 0%. Customermates multiplies each project's value by its stage probability and shows a weighted pipeline total: arithmetic on your own numbers, not a prediction.

Each person connects one account, so Pro fits at [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.4]] a month for the team; a partner who needs LinkedIn and email side by side moves the workspace to Business. Everyone saves their own board of open projects grouped by stage. Each partner owns a routine scheduled for Fridays at 15:00. It runs as its owner in Customermates Cloud, uses hosted AI credits, and carries this instruction:

> Find my projects in "Proposal sent" where the contact has not written in seven days. For each one, save a short follow-up draft in my latest conversation with that contact. Do not send anything. List the projects in the run summary.

## Switching from HubSpot in five steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export from HubSpot">Open companies, contacts and deals in turn, choose Export, pick XLSX and select all properties. HubSpot emails a download link that expires after 30 days.</Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the workspace">Create custom fields for the properties you keep. Give the Stage field the same stage names as HubSpot; the import flags every row whose stage matches no option. Multiple-checkbox properties have no direct match: use a text field or several Single Select fields.</Step>
  <Step title="Import one record type at a time">Open Import and export, then Add from file. Import organizations, then contacts, then deals, so link columns find records by name. If the file has a column named ID or Record ID, set it to Do not add; otherwise the wizard treats it as a Customermates record to update. Limits and mapping rules are in the [records guide](/docs/app-records).</Step>
  <Step title="Bring over what the import skips">The import takes records, fields, notes and contact channels, not HubSpot's activity timeline. Copy what you need into a notes column first.</Step>
  <Step title="Connect channels, then run both in parallel">Connect a mailbox, LinkedIn or WhatsApp under My Profile, Channels, set up routines or your MCP client, and keep HubSpot for reference for a few weeks without entering new data there.</Step>
</Steps>

## Other HubSpot alternatives worth a look

Customermates publishes this list. Details come from each vendor's own pages, checked on 8 October 2026.

- **Pipedrive** is a pipeline-first sales CRM with four per-seat plans, Lite, Growth, Premium and Ultimate, billed monthly or yearly at a discount, plus add-ons for prospecting, documents and email marketing. It fits sales teams that want deals and activities without a marketing suite. See [HubSpot vs Pipedrive](/compare/hubspot-vs-pipedrive).
- **Zoho CRM** comes in five editions, from Free to Ultimate. The free edition covers three users with contact management, workflow automation, standard reports and mobile apps. Standard, the first paid edition, adds cadences, dashboards and sales forecasts; the Zia AI sales assistant starts on Enterprise. See [HubSpot vs Zoho](/compare/hubspot-vs-zoho).
- **Twenty** is an open-source CRM you can self-host, with unlimited custom objects, workflows and an MCP server in every cloud workspace, and a 30-day cloud trial. It fits teams that want open source and a flexible data model. See the [Twenty comparison](/compare/twenty-alternative).

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="What are the downsides of HubSpot for a small team?">
Mainly four: the price step from Starter to Professional with its required onboarding, the 10-user cap on Starter, WhatsApp only from Marketing Hub or Service Hub Professional with a Business account, and no option to run it on your own servers. If you need marketing, sales and service in one system, HubSpot covers more of it than any alternative on this page.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a free alternative to HubSpot?">
For two users, HubSpot Free is hard to beat. Zoho CRM's free edition covers three. If you can run a server, Twenty and the AGPL-3.0 core of Customermates self-host without a licence fee, though self-hosted Customermates has no inbox and no Mate.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What are open-source alternatives to HubSpot?">
Twenty, with custom objects and its own workflows, and Customermates, whose cloud version adds the unified inbox and Mate to its AGPL-3.0 core. Neither replaces HubSpot's marketing tools.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is Zoho or HubSpot better for a small team?">
Zoho CRM is free for three users and includes sales forecasts from its first paid edition, Standard. HubSpot is free for two and puts marketing and service tools on the same platform. Which matters more depends on whether you need those tools.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does HubSpot store data in the EU?">
It can. HubSpot hosts in Germany as well as the US, Canada and Australia, and paid accounts can choose their data center, but its FAQ names exceptions such as subprocessors, support access and usage data sent to the US. Customermates has its cloud database configured in the AWS Frankfurt region and lists its subprocessors, including US providers.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How do I move my data from HubSpot to Customermates?">
Export companies, contacts and deals as XLSX, set up fields and stage names, then import organizations, contacts and deals in that order. Leave any Record ID column unmapped.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

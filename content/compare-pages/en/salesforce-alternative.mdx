> A good Salesforce alternative depends on which part of Salesforce you would miss. If you rely on forecasting, quotes, approval flows or AppExchange apps, stay, or compare it with HubSpot and Zoho CRM. If you want open-source software on your own server, look at SuiteCRM, Twenty or Customermates. If you are a small consultancy or agency whose clients write on WhatsApp and LinkedIn as often as by email, and you would rather pay month by month than sign for a year, Customermates is built for that case. Customermates publishes this comparison; every Salesforce detail comes from salesforce.com and was checked on 8 October 2026.

## Which alternative fits which reason

| Your reason for leaving | Look at | Why |
|---|---|---|
| You are two people with a simple pipeline | Salesforce Free Suite, HubSpot's free CRM | Both are free for two users. Zoho CRM's free edition covers three |
| Pro Suite's seat price and yearly contract | Zoho CRM, HubSpot, Customermates | Compare their plans with Pro Suite. Customermates bills monthly |
| You want open source or your own server | SuiteCRM, Twenty, Customermates | All three can be self-hosted |
| Clients write on WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram or Telegram | Customermates Pro or Business | These channels land in one inbox next to the deal |
| You use forecasting, quotes, territories or approval flows | Stay on Salesforce | Customermates has none of them |
| You are replacing Service Cloud | A help desk tool | Customermates has no cases or tickets |

## Who Salesforce is a good choice for

Salesforce sells Sales Cloud as suites for smaller companies and editions for larger sales teams. Free Suite comes with two user licences and covers leads, opportunities, customer cases and simple email marketing. It stays free as long as you log in at least once every 60 days. Starter Suite adds sales flows, lead routing and automatic sync of emails, events and contacts. Pro Suite adds sales quoting and forecasting, Flow Builder (five flows per org) and AppExchange.

Core, Advanced and Max add more on top: Slack Business+, Tableau Next, approval processes and sales territories from Core, security and backup products from Advanced, and Agentforce for Sales, Sales Planning and Salesforce Maps in Max.

If your team uses several of these and someone owns the configuration, a smaller CRM will not replace it. The switch would cost you more than it saves.

## Why teams look for a Salesforce alternative

**Most editions are billed annually.** Starter Suite costs $25 per user per month, billed monthly or annually. Pro Suite, Core, Advanced and Max are billed annually only.

**The step after Starter Suite is steep.** Pro Suite costs four times as much per seat, and Core almost doubles that again. Agentforce for Sales is an add-on from $125 per user per month unless you are on Max.

**Chat channels sit in Service Cloud.** Sales Cloud syncs email. On the Service Cloud pricing page, Digital Channels is "available for purchase" in Core and Advanced and included in Max; Starter and Pro Suite do not list it. No price is given.

**The platform needs an owner.** Flow Builder, approval processes, record types and page layouts give an admin a lot of control. Someone has to build and maintain them, and in a five-person firm that is usually the founder.

**It is hosted by Salesforce only.** Sales Cloud is a proprietary subscription. For EU data residency, the Hyperforce EU Operating Zone processes and stores customer data in the EU and Switzerland, with EU-based support, for a select set of products. The page gives no price.

## Where Customermates fits

Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM for small client-facing teams: consultants, agencies, other relationship-driven service businesses and founders.

**Conversations next to the deal.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn (including Sales Navigator and Recruiter), WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one list. You link the people in a thread to contacts and reply on the original channel. A thread stays private until its owner shares it with the CRM; sharing a whole connected account needs Business.

**AI that keeps the records current.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes threads, drafts replies and updates contacts and deals, using hosted AI credits. It asks for a fresh approval before deletions, but it sends a message without one when you tell it to, so ask for drafts when you want to read first. Routines run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes, as their owner: [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] per user on Starter, [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]] on Pro, [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedRoutinesPerUser]] on Business.

Already pay for Claude or ChatGPT? Connect it to the [MCP server](/docs/mcp) instead. Its model calls run on your own AI provider, not on hosted credits.

**Open source, with a hosting choice.** The AGPL-3.0 core runs on your own server with Docker Compose and PostgreSQL 16, including the [REST API](/features/api), webhooks and MCP. A [self-hosted install](/features/self-hosted) has no inbox, no Mate and no hosted credits. In Customermates Cloud the database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, a [DPA](/dpa) is available, and the [subprocessors](/subprocessors), including US providers, are listed.

## Where Customermates is the wrong choice

- **No forecasting, quotes or territories.** The weighted pipeline multiplies each deal's value by its stage probability. It predicts nothing.
- **No custom objects.** You get contacts, organizations, deals, services and tasks. You can rename them and add custom fields of ten types, but not add a sixth record type.
- **No workflow builder or approval processes.** Automation runs through routines, webhooks, the REST API and a separately operated n8n.
- **No marketing or help desk.** There are no campaigns, sequences, web forms, cases or tickets.
- **Simple tasks.** Tasks have assignees, relations, notes and custom fields. They have no due dates or reminders.
- **No native mobile app, single sign-on or field-level permissions.** Roles decide per record type whether someone sees all records, only their own, or none. Saved views are personal.
- **No free plan and no certification.** There is a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial, then a paid plan.

## A worked setup: a consultancy leaving Starter Suite

Take a five-person consultancy. Two partners win work by email and LinkedIn; three consultants talk to clients on WhatsApp.

| In Salesforce | In Customermates |
|---|---|
| Accounts | Organizations, renamed "Accounts" in the company settings |
| Contacts and leads | Contacts, plus a single-select field "Lead status" and a saved view "Open leads" |
| Opportunities | Deals, renamed "Opportunities" |
| Stage and probability | The stage field, a single-select with a probability per option |
| Products and amount | Services linked to the deal with a quantity; their sum is the deal value |
| Opportunity owner | Assigned users on the deal |
| Picklists | Single-select custom fields |
| Close date | A custom date field |

There is no lead conversion step: a lead becomes a client when you change its status. A deal's value comes from its linked services, so an opportunity that only has an amount needs a service such as "Consulting day" with the right quantity. Keep your stage names and probabilities, and you see each deal's weighted value and the weighted pipeline total.

The partners want email and LinkedIn side by side, so the firm picks Business at [[commercial.price.business.monthly.seats.5]] a month. With one account per person, Pro at [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.5]] would do. Each partner owns a routine in Customermates Cloud, which uses hosted AI credits:

> Every Monday at 8:00, find my opportunities in "Proposal sent" where the contact has not written in ten days. For each one, save a short follow-up draft in my latest thread with that contact. Do not send anything. List the opportunities in the run summary.

## Switching from Salesforce

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export from Salesforce">In Setup, type Data Export into Quick Find and choose Export Now. Salesforce emails a link to a zip archive and keeps it for 48 hours. On an annual contract, check your renewal date first.</Step>
  <Step title="Convert the files">Customermates imports Excel workbooks only. Save the account, contact, lead and opportunity files as .xlsx. A file currently holds up to 10,000 rows, so split larger ones.</Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the workspace">Rename the record types, give the stage field one option per Salesforce stage with its probability, and create single-select fields for the picklists you keep.</Step>
  <Step title="Import one record type at a time">Start with accounts, then contacts, then opportunities, so links to existing records resolve. The [records guide](/docs/app-records) explains column mapping.</Step>
  <Step title="Connect channels and AI">Connect a mailbox, LinkedIn or WhatsApp, then set up a routine or your own AI client over MCP. Activity history does not come across, so keep Salesforce read-only until the team has moved.</Step>
</Steps>

## Other Salesforce alternatives worth a look

Customermates publishes this list. Details come from each vendor's own pages, checked on 8 October 2026.

- **HubSpot** is free for up to two users, with no credit card required. It fits teams that want to start free and grow into marketing tools. See [HubSpot vs Salesforce](/compare/hubspot-vs-salesforce).
- **Zoho CRM** has a free edition for up to three users and four paid editions, from Standard to Ultimate. It fits teams that want a broad suite. See [Zoho vs Salesforce](/compare/zoho-vs-salesforce).
- **Twenty** calls itself "the open alternative to Salesforce, designed for AI". Its open-source core is free to self-host with Docker Compose, all cloud plans include unlimited custom objects, and the Organization plan adds SAML/OIDC single sign-on and row-level permissions. It fits teams that want to model their own objects.
- **SuiteCRM** is published under AGPLv3 and built on PHP, Symfony and MySQL. You host it yourself or use SuiteCRM Hosted. It covers sales with quotations, marketing and service, and fits teams that want a broad module set on their own servers.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is there a free alternative to Salesforce?">
For two users, Salesforce's own Free Suite costs nothing as long as someone logs in every 60 days. HubSpot's free CRM also covers two users, Zoho CRM's free edition three. SuiteCRM, Twenty and the AGPL-3.0 core of Customermates can be self-hosted without a licence fee; self-hosted Customermates has no inbox and no Mate. Customermates Cloud has a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial and no free plan.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What is a good open-source alternative to Salesforce?">
SuiteCRM has the widest module set, including marketing, service and quotations. Twenty suits teams that want their own objects. Customermates has a fixed record model and focuses on client conversations and AI: the core runs with Docker Compose, and the cloud version adds the unified inbox and Mate. The [open-source CRM guide](/blog/open-source-crm) compares more options.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How much does Salesforce cost per month?">
From $0 for two users on Free Suite to $550 per user on Max. Starter Suite costs $25 per user, billed monthly or annually; Pro Suite ($100), Core ($195), Advanced ($395) and Max are billed annually. Five people on Pro Suite pay €500 a month, €6,000 a year, before add-ons. The [Salesforce pricing guide](/blog/salesforce-pricing) goes into the editions in detail.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Which CRM is best for a small business?">
The one that matches how you win work. If you sell through forecasts and quotes, Salesforce Pro Suite has both; check the paid plans of HubSpot and Zoho CRM against the same list. If you are two or three people and want to start free, HubSpot or Zoho CRM. If your client work runs through email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp and nobody wants to maintain records by hand, Customermates.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a better CRM than Salesforce?">
Not one that is better for every team. If you use Salesforce's forecasting, territories, approval flows and AppExchange, it is hard to replace. A smaller team that uses a fraction of it is often better served by a CRM it can set up on its own and pay for monthly.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Salesforce store data in the EU?">
It can. The Hyperforce EU Operating Zone keeps processing and storage in the EU and Switzerland, with EU-based support, for a select set of products; the page gives no price. Customermates Cloud has its database configured in the AWS Frankfurt region and lists its subprocessors, including US providers.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

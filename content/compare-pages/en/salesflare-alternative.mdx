The automatic data collection that makes Salesflare attractive also raises data privacy concerns, as the system pulls data from your email accounts, calendars, and social profiles.

Customermates takes a different approach. Every core CRM feature is available starting at [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per user per month. Webhooks, the REST API and MCP are included from Starter, a separately operated [n8n](/features/workflow-automation) instance connects through the community node, [permission management](/features/contact-management) works at every tier, and you maintain full control over which data enters your CRM.

## Setup and Onboarding

Customermates' managed cloud is ready as soon as you sign up. Configure your [deal stages](/features/pipeline), import your [contacts](/features/contact-management) from an XLSX workbook, and begin working. No email account connections or social profile authorizations required.

Salesflare's main selling point is that it automatically pulls data from your email, calendar, and LinkedIn. The system then needs time to scan and index your existing contacts. Setup typically takes 1-2 days, and the automatic data enrichment process continues for weeks as the system learns your contact network.

## Automatic Data Collection vs. Conscious Control

Salesflare's core value proposition is automatic data entry. The system extracts contact information from emails, enriches profiles from LinkedIn, logs meetings from your calendar, and suggests follow-ups automatically. This sounds powerful but comes with significant trade-offs.

First, automation requires giving Salesflare access to your email accounts, calendars, and social networks. Your communication data is actively scanned and processed. For teams with strict data handling policies, this is a concern.

Second, automatic data recognition is imperfect. Contacts can be incorrectly assigned, meetings misinterpreted, and follow-up suggestions may not match your actual sales process. The system requires ongoing monitoring and correction.

Third, you lose control over what enters your CRM. Salesflare pulls in contacts from every email thread, including spam, newsletters, and one-time conversations. Maintaining a clean database requires regular cleanup.

Customermates prioritizes conscious data control. You decide which contacts, deals, and interactions enter the system. Combined with [AI agents that operate the CRM](/features/lead-tracking) to categorize and prioritize leads, you get AI assistance while you decide what enters the CRM.

## Data Protection and GDPR

In the managed cloud, the Customermates application database runs in the AWS Frankfurt region; hosting and other subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [DPA](/dpa) is available. Customermates holds no certification. If you need full control over where data lives, [self-host](/features/self-hosted) the open-source core.

Salesflare is a Belgian company and publishes its own privacy documentation. Its automatic data collection model means your CRM actively pulls information from external sources like email accounts, LinkedIn, and calendars. Customer data is not just stored in Salesflare but continuously synchronized from multiple external sources. Organizations with strict data minimization policies should check how that automated collection fits GDPR's data minimization principle (Article 5(1)(c)).

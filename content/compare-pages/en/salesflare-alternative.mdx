## Who Salesflare is a good choice for

Salesflare is a CRM for B2B sales teams, made by Salesflare BVBA in Antwerp. Its idea is that nobody types data in. On every plan it pulls in contact details, logs emails and meetings, and fills contact and company profiles with public data, including details from email signatures. It tracks emails, links and website visits and sends email campaigns. It integrates with Gmail and Outlook, works on LinkedIn through a Chrome extension, and has mobile apps.

On an account's timeline, Salesflare's AI summarizes emails, calls and meetings, suggests next steps and answers questions about the account. An Assist button in the email composer writes, shortens, translates and personalizes emails in a style learned from your recent mail. Salesflare is listed in the ChatGPT app directory and the Claude connector directory, and its MCP server lets those clients look up accounts and contacts and create or update tasks and opportunities. A REST API, with API keys created in Salesflare's settings, covers other integrations. Customer data is stored on Google Cloud servers in St. Ghislain, Belgium.

If your selling happens in Gmail or Outlook and you want the CRM to keep up without anyone typing, Salesflare does that well. Switching would cost you its main feature.

## Why teams look for a Salesflare alternative

**Team features start on Pro.** User permissions, custom dashboards and email workflows are Pro features, so a team that needs them pays €64 per user per month, or €49 billed yearly, instead of Growth's €39 or €29. Enterprise needs at least five users and adds setup and training, data migration and a dedicated account manager.

**Lead search runs on credits.** Growth includes 5 lead credits a month, Pro 100 and Enterprise 250. More come as add-ons, starting at $39 for 250.

**Capture is the default.** Salesflare builds the CRM from the mail and meetings it can read. Some teams want the reverse and decide person by person who enters the CRM, for example to keep private or one-off correspondents out. Customermates works that way.

**Chat channels stay outside.** LinkedIn messages reach Salesflare when you copy them in as internal notes from the side panel. For WhatsApp, Salesflare's integrations directory lists no direct integration, only third-party inbox tools such as Helpwise.

**Hosted only.** Salesflare's pricing page lists three hosted plans and no self-hosted or open-source edition.

## Where Customermates fits

Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM for small client-facing teams: consultants, agencies, other service businesses and founders. It is built in Germany.

**Conversations you link on purpose.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, [LinkedIn](/features/linkedin-integration) (including Sales Navigator and Recruiter), WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one list, and you reply on the original channel. Nothing lands on a contact by itself. You open a conversation's participants, click Link and pick a contact or create one. That address or handle then becomes one of the contact's channels, and conversations with it show on the contact's timeline. Starter has no inbox.

**AI that does the CRM work.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes threads, drafts replies, creates and updates records and builds views. It uses hosted AI credits, with a monthly allowance per active user listed on the [pricing page](/pricing). Mate asks for a fresh approval before it deletes anything. Sending a message you asked for runs without one, so tell it to save drafts when you want to review first. [Routines](/docs/app-routines) run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes: [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] per user on Starter, [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]] on Pro.

Prefer Claude, ChatGPT or another client you already pay for? Connect it to the [MCP server](/docs/mcp), which offers [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan and on self-hosted installs. Its model calls then run on your own AI provider.

**Team basics from Starter.** Roles set permissions per record type and action. Dashboards, custom fields in ten types, the REST API and [[derived.webhooks.events.total]] webhook event types come with every plan.

**Open source, with a hosting choice.** The core is AGPL-3.0 and runs on your own server with Docker Compose and PostgreSQL 16, including REST, webhooks and MCP. A [self-hosted install](/features/self-hosted) has no inbox, no Mate and no hosted credits, and it has no subscription or seat fees either: you pay for your own infrastructure. In Customermates Cloud the database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, a [DPA](/dpa) is available, and the [subprocessors](/subprocessors), including US providers, are listed. Customermates holds no compliance certification.

## Where Customermates is the wrong choice

Besides the gaps in the table above (no automatic capture, no tracking, no campaigns and no native phone apps), check these before you start a trial:

- **No enrichment and no sequences.** Contacts come from your team, an import, Mate or your own AI client, and outreach goes one message at a time, written by you or drafted by Mate.
- **No task due dates and no reminders.** Tasks have assignees, links to records and notes. A follow-up date is a custom date field you sort by.
- **Personal views, coarser permissions.** Saved views cannot be shared, and permissions apply per record type and action, not per field.
- **No free cloud plan.** There is a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial, then a paid plan.

## A worked setup: a consultancy moving from Salesflare

Take a five-person B2B consultancy. Two partners sell by email; three consultants stay in touch with clients on LinkedIn and WhatsApp.

| In Salesflare | In Customermates |
|---|---|
| Accounts | Organizations |
| Contacts | Contacts |
| Opportunities | Deals |
| Pipeline stages | A stage field with probabilities |
| Tasks with a date | Tasks with a custom date field "Due on", sorted ascending |
| Groupings you filter contacts by | A Single Select field such as "Segment", plus a saved view filtered on it |

The stages are Lead 10%, Qualified 30%, Proposal sent 50%, Negotiation 70%, Won 100% and Lost 0%. Customermates multiplies each deal's value by its stage probability and shows a weighted pipeline total. That is arithmetic on your own numbers, not a prediction.

The partners connect Gmail, and each consultant connects either LinkedIn or WhatsApp. One account per person fits Pro, at [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.5]] a month for the team. When a consultant needs LinkedIn and WhatsApp side by side, the workspace moves to Business at [[commercial.price.business.monthly.seats.5]].

To replace automatic capture with a rule you can read, each person sets up one routine. It runs in Customermates Cloud, uses hosted AI credits and runs as its owner, so it only sees that person's channels:

> Every day at 8:00, look at my inbox conversations from the last 24 hours in which a participant is not linked to a contact. Skip newsletters and automated senders. For each person who writes about a project, a proposal or an existing client, create a contact with their name and their email address or handle as a channel, link it to their organization if that organization already exists, and list the new contacts in the run summary. Do not send any messages.

Runs are capped per hour and in credits per run; the [routines guide](/docs/app-routines) lists the limits.

## Switching from Salesflare in five steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export from Salesflare">Open Accounts, Contacts and Opportunities in turn, use the three-dot menu and export each list. Choose Excel, not CSV, because Customermates reads .xlsx workbooks only.</Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the workspace">Create custom fields for the columns you keep, Single Select fields for any groupings, and set up the stage field with its probabilities.</Step>
  <Step title="Import one record type at a time">On each list, open Import and export, then Add from file. Import organizations first, then contacts, then deals. Link columns match records by name, so a contact's account column finds the organization you imported before. Map email addresses to the contact's channels, so conversations land on the right contact once the inbox is connected. Size limits are in the [records documentation](/docs/app-records).</Step>
  <Step title="Connect channels">On Pro or during the trial, open My Profile, Channels and connect a mailbox, LinkedIn or WhatsApp. Conversations come from the accounts you connect, not from the Salesflare export.</Step>
  <Step title="Hand the routine work to AI">Set up the capture routine above for Mate, or connect your own AI client through MCP and give it the same instruction when you need it.</Step>
</Steps>

## Other Salesflare alternatives worth a look

Customermates publishes this list. Prices and features come from each vendor's pricing page, checked on 8 October 2026.

- **Pipedrive** is built around the pipeline view. Email sync, email tracking, automations and sequences start on its Growth plan. AI email tools and permissions for groups and individual users come with Premium. The trial runs 14 days without a credit card. It suits teams that want tracking and follow-up automation in a pipeline-first tool. The [Pipedrive comparison](/compare/pipedrive-alternative) goes into detail.
- **folk** suits relationship-driven teams that prospect on LinkedIn. Standard costs €27 per member per month on monthly billing or €22 billed yearly and includes a LinkedIn extension, email, calendar and WhatsApp sync, email campaigns, contact enrichment, a mobile app and MCP. Email sequences, deals, dashboards and API access need Premium at €54 or €43. See [folk compared with Customermates](/compare/folk).
- **Capsule CRM** has a free plan for two users, 250 contacts and one pipeline. Workflow automations, email sync, team and access controls, AI summaries and AI contact enrichment start on Growth, and there are apps for iOS and Android. The trial runs 14 days without a card.

Lead credits beyond the included ones are add-ons from $39 for 250. There is a 30-day trial without a credit card. <FaqItem question="Is there a free alternative to Salesflare?">
Capsule CRM has a free plan for two users and 250 contacts; Salesflare's pricing page shows no free plan. Customermates has no free cloud plan, but its open-source core can be self-hosted without subscription or seat fees. You pay for the server and get the CRM, REST, webhooks and MCP, without the inbox and Mate.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Salesflare work with WhatsApp and LinkedIn messages?">
LinkedIn partly: Salesflare's Chrome side panel lets you add contacts and accounts from LinkedIn profiles, and you copy LinkedIn messages into Salesflare as internal notes. For WhatsApp, its integrations directory lists no direct integration, only third-party inbox tools such as Helpwise. In Customermates Pro and Business, LinkedIn and WhatsApp conversations sit in the unified inbox, and you reply from there.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I use ChatGPT or Claude with Salesflare or Customermates?">
With both. Salesflare is listed in the ChatGPT app directory and the Claude connector directory, and its MCP server lets those clients look up accounts and contacts and create or update tasks and opportunities. Customermates offers an MCP server with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan and on self-hosted installs; the inbox tools need Pro.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Where does Salesflare store data, and is it GDPR compliant?">
Salesflare stores customer data on Google Cloud servers in St. Ghislain, Belgium. Whether your use of any CRM complies with the GDPR is for you to assess as the controller. Customermates has its cloud database configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, offers a DPA and lists its subprocessors, including US providers; self-hosting lets you choose where the data lives.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How do I move my data from Salesflare to Customermates?">
Export accounts, contacts and opportunities from Salesflare as Excel files, then import them one record type at a time: organizations, contacts, deals. Conversations are not imported; they come from the mailbox or messaging account you connect on Pro.

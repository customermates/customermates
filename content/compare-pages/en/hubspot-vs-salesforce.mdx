> For most small teams, HubSpot is the easier choice. It is free for up to two users, Professional can be paid month by month, and marketing, service and content hubs can be added to the same account later. Salesforce makes more sense when you want sales quoting and forecasting in the mid tier, an EU-only operating option, or a path towards editions with Agentforce and sales planning. At five seats the mid tiers cost about the same in year one: $6,900 for HubSpot Professional including its required onboarding, $6,000 for Salesforce Pro Suite. Customermates publishes this comparison and sells its own CRM, which appears only in the labelled third-option section. Every HubSpot and Salesforce detail comes from the vendors' own pages, checked on 8 October 2026.

## Which one should you choose?

| Your situation | Better fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| One or two people who want to start at $0 | HubSpot Free | Free for up to two users, no credit card |
| You want the mid tier without an annual contract | HubSpot Professional | $100 per seat on monthly billing; Salesforce bills Pro Suite and up annually |
| Marketing should work in the same account as sales | HubSpot | Marketing Hub, Service Hub and Content Hub are sold for the same account as Sales Hub |
| You need quotes and forecasts without paying for a top tier | Salesforce Pro Suite | Sales quoting and forecasting are part of Pro Suite |
| Customer data and support must stay inside the EU | Salesforce with the EU Operating Zone | Data is processed and stored in the EU and Switzerland, with EU-based support |
| You expect to need Salesforce's planning and mapping tools later | Salesforce Max | Max adds Sales Planning, Maps and Salesforce Spiff |
| Marketing already runs HubSpot and sales runs Salesforce | Both, connected | HubSpot's Salesforce connector needs a Professional or Enterprise hub |
| A small client-facing team whose clients write on email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp | Look at a smaller CRM | See the third option below |

## What five seats cost

Prices checked on 8 October 2026 from [HubSpot's Sales Hub pricing page](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/sales) and [Salesforce's Sales Cloud pricing page](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/pricing/), in US dollars per seat or user per month. Salesforce lists the same figures in euros.

| Plan | Per seat / month | Five seats, first year |
|---|---|---|
| HubSpot Free | $0 | Not possible: Free covers two users |
| HubSpot Starter | $20 list price | $1,200 |
| HubSpot Professional | $90 with annual commitment | $6,900, including $1,500 onboarding |
| HubSpot Enterprise | $150 | $12,500, including $3,500 onboarding |
| Salesforce Starter Suite | $25 | $1,500 |
| Salesforce Pro Suite | $100, billed annually | $6,000 |
| Salesforce Core | $195, billed annually | $11,700 |

Five things change the arithmetic:

- **HubSpot's onboarding is a one-time fee.** From year two, five Professional seats cost $5,400 a year, less than Pro Suite. On monthly billing the same seats cost $500 a month.
- **HubSpot showed a time-limited Starter price** of $7 per seat on 8 October 2026. Budget with the $20 list price, because offers end.
- **HubSpot's pricing page depends on your region.** From Europe, hubspot.de and hubspot.com can show the bundled Customer Platform (all hubs together) in euros instead of Sales Hub: Starter 7 € as a time-limited offer (65% off the 20 € list price), Professional 90 € and Enterprise 175 € per seat and month, billed annually, with larger credit allowances. Those bundle prices are not comparable with the Sales Hub figures above.
- **AI is priced differently.** HubSpot's paid plans include HubSpot Credits that power its AI agents and features; credits reset monthly and do not roll over. Salesforce says AI can be added to Core and above: Agentforce for Sales costs from $125 per user per month, at least $7,500 a year for five users. Max includes it.
- **Salesforce support is tiered.** The Standard Success Plan is included. Premier costs 30% of net license fees; Core and the editions above it include Premier Success.

Our [Salesforce pricing guide](/blog/salesforce-pricing) goes through every edition in more detail.

## Where HubSpot is stronger

**A free start that works.** Two users get the CRM, email tracking and a meeting link at no cost. You can test HubSpot with real deals before anyone signs anything.

**Monthly billing up to Professional.** Starter and Professional can be paid month by month. A team that might shrink or switch is not tied to a twelve-month contract.

**Automation and AI arrive early.** Starter already includes up to 50 workflows with restricted triggers and actions, and 500 HubSpot Credits a month.

**Room to add marketing.** HubSpot sells Marketing Hub, Service Hub, Content Hub and Data Hub as separate hubs for the same account. You can add email marketing next year without moving your contacts.

## Where Salesforce is stronger

**More in the mid tier.** Pro Suite includes sales quoting and forecasting and allows greater customization. HubSpot keeps forecasting and custom reports for Professional, which costs about the same.

**A longer runway.** Core adds Momentum and Premier Success. Advanced adds Backup & Recover, Data Detect and Security Center. Max bundles Agentforce for Sales, Sales Planning, Maps and Salesforce Spiff. Few small teams need these, but a company that will need them later avoids a migration.

**An EU operating zone.** Salesforce's Hyperforce EU Operating Zone keeps customer data processed and stored in the EU and Switzerland, isolates access to that region and staffs support with EU-based personnel. HubSpot also has a data center in Germany, but its own FAQ says support staff and subprocessors in other regions may still process data.

## Running HubSpot and Salesforce together

If marketing works in HubSpot and sales in Salesforce, HubSpot's own connector syncs the two, with conditions:

- It needs a Professional or Enterprise subscription of a HubSpot hub, such as Marketing Hub or Sales Hub.
- Salesforce must be an edition with API access, or Salesforce Professional. Group Edition is not supported.
- One contact sync can take up to four Salesforce API calls, and the integration has a daily API limit. Check your Salesforce API allowance before you sync a large database.

The combined setup therefore costs at least one HubSpot Professional subscription on top of your Salesforce licences. Before you connect two platforms, price out moving one team onto the other.

## EU data location and GDPR

**HubSpot** hosts on AWS in the US (East and West), Canada, Australia and Germany. An account gets a data center based on its IP address at sign-up, and accounts on Starter, Professional or Enterprise can move. Some data still leaves the region: usage data goes to the US, and subprocessors and support staff may process data elsewhere.

**Salesforce** runs on Hyperforce, its public cloud infrastructure. For EU-only processing, the EU Operating Zone adds the storage, access and support commitments described above. It covers a vetted set of Sales Cloud, Service Cloud and platform products, so confirm that yours are on the list.

In both cases you are the controller. Sign the vendor's data processing agreement and read its subprocessor list before you import customer data.

### A worked setup for a five-person consultancy

Rename deals to "Projects" and give the stage field these options and probabilities: Lead 10%, Qualified 30%, Proposal sent 60%, Won 100%, Lost 0%. Add a single-select field "Source" with Referral, LinkedIn and Website. Each consultant saves a board of their open projects grouped by stage; the founder keeps a table grouped by assigned user, with the weighted total per group.

Each consultant also owns one routine, which runs in Customermates Cloud and uses hosted AI credits:

> Every Friday at 16:00, find my projects in "Proposal sent" where the contact has not written for seven days. Save a short follow-up draft in my latest conversation with each contact. Do not send anything.

The last sentence matters. Mate sends messages when an instruction asks it to, so a routine meant only for drafts has to say so.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is HubSpot cheaper than Salesforce?">
At the entry level, yes: a $20 list price per seat for HubSpot Starter against $25 for Salesforce Starter Suite. In the mid tier, five seats cost $6,900 in year one on HubSpot Professional (with onboarding) and $6,000 on Salesforce Pro Suite. From year two HubSpot is cheaper. Salesforce's Agentforce for Sales needs Core or higher and costs extra there, unless you buy Max.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Do HubSpot and Salesforce have free plans?">
Both do. HubSpot's free tools cover up to two users with no credit card. Salesforce offers a Free Suite at $0 with basic account, contact, lead and opportunity management, and a free 30-day trial of Sales Cloud.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can you use HubSpot and Salesforce together?">
Yes, through HubSpot's Salesforce connector. You need a Professional or Enterprise HubSpot hub and a Salesforce edition with API access or Salesforce Professional; Group Edition is not supported. Each synced contact can use up to four Salesforce API calls.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Which is better for a small business?">
For most small businesses HubSpot: two users are free, Professional can be paid monthly, and paid plans include AI credits. Pick Salesforce when the mid tier must already include quoting and forecasting, or when you need the EU Operating Zone.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Where do HubSpot and Salesforce store data for EU customers?">
HubSpot assigns a data center by IP address at sign-up, with one in Germany on AWS; paid accounts can switch. Salesforce runs on Hyperforce and offers an EU Operating Zone that keeps processing, storage and support in the EU and Switzerland for vetted products.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a simpler CRM than HubSpot or Salesforce?">
That depends on what you would drop. Customermates covers contacts, organizations, deals and a multi-channel inbox for small client-facing teams, without marketing tools. If you mainly need a sales pipeline, our [Pipedrive vs Salesforce comparison](/compare/pipedrive-vs-salesforce) looks at another common option.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

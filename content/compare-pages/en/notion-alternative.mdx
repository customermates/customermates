> Keep your CRM in Notion while a couple of people track a short client list and the deal pages double as project notes. Move it to a dedicated CRM when client conversations happen outside Notion, when you want a weighted pipeline without maintaining formulas, or when an AI assistant should do the updating. Notion gives you databases, relations, formulas and automations, and you design the CRM yourself. Customermates starts with contacts, organizations, deals with a weighted stage field, services and tasks, and from Pro it puts email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram conversations next to those records. If you need record types of your own, Attio or Twenty are closer to Notion's flexibility. Customermates publishes this comparison; every Notion detail comes from notion.com and was checked on 8 October 2026.

## Which tool fits which team

| Your situation | Better fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A short client list, with deals and meeting notes in the same pages as your docs | Notion Free or Plus | A database with a Status property covers it, and you already have Notion |
| You need your own record types, such as investors, properties or candidates, with formulas and rollups | Attio, Twenty or Airtable | Customermates has five fixed record types and no formula fields |
| Clients write by email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp, and you want those threads on the contact | Customermates Pro or Business | All of these channels arrive in one inbox, linked to contacts |
| You want deal value times stage probability without building it | Customermates, any plan | The stage field with probabilities exists from sign-up |
| You need EU hosting without an Enterprise contract, or want to host the CRM yourself | Customermates | Cloud database configured in the AWS Frankfurt region on every plan; AGPL core for self-hosting, without inbox or Mate |
| You need SAML SSO or native mobile apps | Notion Business | Notion has both; Customermates has no SSO and no native app |

## Who Notion is a good choice for as a CRM

Notion's help center lists more than 20 property types, among them Status, Relation, Rollup, Formula, Multi-select, Person and Files. With them you can model contacts, companies and deals, link them and compute a weighted value with a formula. The template gallery has a CRM category with free and paid templates, many from independent creators.

On paid plans, database automations run when a page is added or a property changes, or on a daily, weekly or monthly schedule. They can edit properties, add pages to another database, notify members, send an email from your Gmail account or call a webhook. Notion Agent, the in-app assistant, is included on Business, and Notion MCP lets clients such as Claude Code, Cursor or Codex read and update your pages. If the CRM is a small part of a workspace your team already writes in, a database next to the docs is a sound choice.

## Why teams look for a Notion CRM alternative

**You maintain the CRM yourself.** Every property, relation, formula and automation is something a person on your team designed and has to keep working. When the sales process changes, someone edits the schema and everything that depends on it.

**Conversations run through agents, and only for mail.** To read and answer email, Notion uses Custom Agents with a Mail connection, which works with Gmail or Outlook and can read, draft and send. LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram are not part of it. Custom Agents are free to try, then cost $10 per 1,000 Notion credits.

**The full assistant needs Business.** On Free and Plus, Notion Agent is a limited trial.

**Data sits in the US unless you are on Enterprise.** Notion stores data in the United States by default. Enterprise customers can request EU data residency in Frankfurt, with Ireland as backup; Notion Calendar, Notion Mail and beta services are not covered. Notion sells hosted plans only.

## Where Customermates fits

Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM for small client-facing teams: consultants, agencies, other service businesses and founders. It is built in Germany.

**The pipeline exists before you build anything.** A new workspace starts with a Stage field on deals and probabilities already set: Prospecting 10%, Qualification 20%, Demo 40%, Proposal 60%, Negotiation 80%, Won 100%, Lost 0%. A deal's value is the sum of its linked services, price times quantity, and Customermates multiplies it by the stage probability for a weighted value per deal and a weighted [pipeline total](/features/pipeline). That is arithmetic on your numbers, not a prediction.

**Conversations sit next to the deal.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one list, and you reply on the original channel. Link a participant to a contact once, and every conversation with that address or handle shows on the contact's timeline.

**AI keeps the records current.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes threads, drafts replies, updates records and builds views, using hosted AI credits with a monthly allowance per active user (see [pricing](/pricing)). It asks for a fresh approval before every delete but sends messages without one, so tell it to save drafts when you want to check first. Routines run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes: [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] per user on Starter, [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]] on Pro. Prefer Claude or ChatGPT? Connect it to the [MCP server](/docs/mcp), which offers [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan; the model calls then run on your own AI provider.

**Your choice of hosting.** The cloud database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region; a DPA is available and the subprocessors, including US providers, are listed. The AGPL-3.0 core runs on your own server with Docker Compose, including REST, webhooks and MCP, but a [self-hosted install](/features/self-hosted) has no inbox and no Mate. Customermates holds no compliance certification.

## Where Customermates is the wrong choice

- **No record types of your own.** Contacts, organizations, deals, services and tasks can take preset names (deals as projects, organizations as companies), but you cannot add a sixth type.
- **Ten field types and no formulas.** There is no number, checkbox, multi-select, formula or rollup field.
- **No docs or wiki.** Each record has one rich-text notes field that can hold images and tables. Proposals and handbooks stay in Notion.
- **No files on records.** Attachments exist only inside inbox conversations; put a file's address in a Link field.
- **No reminders and no task due dates.** Add a Date field and sort a saved view by it, or let a routine check it.
- **No native mobile apps, no SSO, no shared views and no free plan.** Customermates runs in the phone's browser, saved views are personal, and a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial leads to a paid plan.

## A worked setup: a four-person consultancy leaving Notion

The consultancy's Notion CRM has three databases, Clients, Contacts and Deals, with meeting notes inside each deal page.

| In Notion | In Customermates |
|---|---|
| Clients database | Organizations, renamed "Companies" |
| Contacts, related to Clients | Contacts, linked to their organization by name during import |
| Status property (Lead, Proposal, Won, Lost) | The Stage field, with your options and probabilities |
| Amount (Number) | Services with a price, linked to each deal; a Currency field works too but does not feed the weighted pipeline |
| Service line (Multi-select) | One linked service per line |
| Owner (Person) | Assigned users |
| Next step (Date) | A Date field, plus a saved view sorted by it |
| Weighted value (Formula) | The built-in weighted value |
| Pipeline per client (Rollup) | A dashboard chart summing deal value, grouped by organization |
| Proposal PDF (Files) | A Link field pointing to the file |
| Page body with meeting notes | The record's notes |

Each consultant saves a board of their own deals grouped by stage, and the founder keeps a table grouped by assigned user. On Pro, each person connects the mailbox or WhatsApp account clients use, at [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.4]] a month for the team. One routine per person replaces the Monday filter they used in Notion. It runs as its owner in Customermates Cloud and uses hosted AI credits:

> Every Monday at 8:00, find my deals in "Proposal" whose "Next step" date has passed or whose contact has not written in ten days. For each one, save a short follow-up draft in my latest conversation with that contact and do not send it. List the deals in the run summary.

## Moving from Notion in five steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export each database">In Notion, open the database's ••• menu, choose Export and pick Markdown & CSV. A full-page database comes out as one table file plus a Markdown file for every page.</Step>
  <Step title="Save it as a workbook">Customermates does not read CSV, so open the file in Excel, Numbers or LibreOffice and save it as .xlsx. Split names into first and last name, because new contacts need both.</Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the workspace">Pick record type names under My Company, Settings, Data model, adjust the Stage options and probabilities, and add the fields from the table above.</Step>
  <Step title="Import one record type at a time">Under Import and export, choose Add from file: organizations first, then contacts, services and deals. Link columns match records by name, ignoring case. Map email addresses and WhatsApp numbers to the contact's channels. The [records guide](/docs/app-records) lists the file limits.</Step>
  <Step title="Bring over notes and views">Meeting notes are in the Markdown files, not the table. Paste the ones you need into each record's notes, or have an AI client write them over MCP. Then rebuild your views; each person saves their own.</Step>
</Steps>

## Other Notion CRM alternatives worth a look

Customermates publishes this list. Details come from each vendor's pricing page, checked on 8 October 2026.

- **Attio** is the CRM closest to Notion's way of modelling data, with objects, relationship and formula attributes, email and calendar sync, and automatic enrichment, which Customermates does not offer. According to [Attio's pricing page](https://attio.com/pricing), Free covers up to 3 seats and 50,000 records; Plus costs €39 per user per month (€31 billed yearly) and Pro €93 (€74), with a 14-day Pro trial. See the [Attio comparison](/compare/attio-alternative).
- **Twenty** is an open-source CRM with unlimited custom objects, so a Notion schema can be rebuilt closely and hosted yourself. Every cloud workspace has an MCP server. On [Twenty's pricing page](https://twenty.com/pricing), Cloud Pro costs $9 per user per month billed yearly and Organization $19, after a 30-day trial.
- **Airtable** suits teams that want to keep building their own system, with relational databases, interfaces and automations. Per [Airtable's pricing page](https://www.airtable.com/pricing), Team costs $20 and Business $45 per user per month, billed yearly; Free costs nothing.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Can Notion work as a CRM?">
Yes, if you build it. You create databases for contacts, companies and deals, connect them with Relation properties, track stages with a Status property and add formulas, views and automations, or start from a gallery template. You design and maintain that model yourself, and Notion's mail connection for agents covers Gmail and Outlook, not chat channels such as WhatsApp or LinkedIn.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What does Notion cost for a small team CRM?">
Free is meant for individuals; with 2 or more members a workspace gets a limited number of blocks. Plus costs €11.50 ($12) per member per month on monthly billing and Business €23.50 ($24), less when billed yearly. Custom Agents use Notion credits on top. Customermates Starter costs [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per user per month and Pro [[commercial.price.pro.monthly]], billed monthly.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Why are people moving their CRM away from Notion?">
For a CRM, the reasons you can check are the four above: in-house maintenance, mail-only agent connections, Notion Agent on Business only, and EU residency on Enterprise only. Moving the CRM does not mean leaving Notion; docs and wikis can stay there.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I keep Notion and use Customermates for the CRM?">
Yes. Both have a REST API and webhooks, and n8n has nodes for both, so a workflow can create a Notion project page when a deal reaches Won. n8n runs separately; it is not part of Customermates.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there an open-source Notion alternative for CRM?">
Yes. Twenty is open source with custom objects, the closest match for a flexible Notion schema. Customermates has an AGPL-3.0 core you can host with Docker Compose; the inbox and Mate exist only in Customermates Cloud.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Where does Notion store my CRM data?">
In the United States by default, according to Notion's help center; Enterprise workspaces can request EU data residency in Frankfurt. Customermates Cloud has its database configured in the AWS Frankfurt region on every plan, offers a DPA and lists its subprocessors, including US providers.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

## Why Teams Search for a Notion Alternative

Notion is a powerful workspace for documentation, wikis, and lightweight project management. But when teams try to use it as a CRM, they quickly hit walls:

- **No native pipelines.** You must build Kanban boards from scratch and manually move cards between stages; there is no deal pipeline with per-stage probabilities and weighted values.
- **No email inbox.** Notion has no CRM inbox that links Gmail or Outlook conversations to contacts and deals.
- **No CRM reporting.** There are no built-in sales dashboards or [weighted pipeline reports](/features/reporting).
- **No CRM-specific data model.** Notion's automations handle generic database triggers, but deals, stages and [follow-ups](/features/follow-up) are whatever you build yourself.
- **Closed source, no self-hosting.** Notion runs only as a hosted service; EU data residency depends on the plan.

These are not edge cases; they are the daily requirements of any team managing customer relationships. A real [CRM system](/blog/crm-systems) covers most of them out of the box.

## Data Protection & GDPR

Managed Customermates keeps its application database in the AWS Frankfurt region; hosting and other subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [DPA](/dpa) is available. [Self-hosting](/features/self-hosted) lets the operator choose the database region and makes the operator responsible for connected services and the full processing chain.

Notion is a US-based company that hosts on AWS, in the US by default, and offers EU data residency on higher plans. Check its DPA and the region available on your plan before storing customer data there. Compare how other CRM providers handle this in our [CRM comparison](/blog/crm-comparison) guide.

## Integration & Automation

Customermates gives you full [API access](/features/api), webhooks for your own [n8n](https://n8n.io/) instance, and MCP access for external AI clients from day one. Customermates has no built-in workflow builder or reminders. Webhooks fire on CRM events such as contact updates or deal changes, so your n8n instance can react to them, and an AI client can move deals or draft [follow-ups](/features/follow-up). Through n8n you can reach Slack and many other services; LinkedIn conversations arrive in the unified inbox on Pro. See [integrations](/features/integrations).

Notion offers a public API and basic automations (database triggers, simple notifications), but these are general-purpose tools, not CRM automations. There is no native way to automate deal workflows, send CRM emails, or trigger sales-specific actions. Connecting Notion to external tools for CRM functionality adds complexity, fragility, and maintenance overhead.

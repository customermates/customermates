> The main reason to pick Customermates over Twenty is where your clients write. Twenty's own documentation covers email and calendar sync; Customermates brings Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one inbox from Pro, next to the deal. If your process depends on custom objects, single sign-on, row-level permissions or GraphQL, Twenty is the better product, and it costs less per seat. Customermates publishes this comparison. Every Twenty detail comes from twenty.com, Twenty's documentation and its GitHub repository, checked on 8 October 2026.

## Which CRM fits which team

| Your situation | Better fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Your process needs its own record types, such as properties, investments or funds | Twenty | Unlimited custom objects on every plan; Customermates has five fixed record types |
| You need SAML/OIDC single sign-on or row-level permissions | Twenty Organization | Customermates has no single sign-on, and its roles work per record type |
| Developers will extend the CRM in TypeScript or query it through GraphQL | Twenty | Its Apps framework and GraphQL API exist for that; Customermates offers REST, webhooks and MCP |
| Each person syncs several mailboxes and calendars | Twenty | Unlimited email and calendar accounts per user on every plan |
| You want the lowest cloud price per seat | Twenty Pro | $9 per user per month billed yearly |
| Clients write on WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram or Telegram as well as email | Customermates Pro or Business | All of these channels sit in one inbox, and you reply on the original channel |
| You want an assistant that reads WhatsApp, LinkedIn and email threads and drafts the replies | Customermates Cloud | Mate and routines work on records and inbox threads, using hosted AI credits |

## Who Twenty is a good choice for

Twenty calls itself "the open alternative to Salesforce, designed for AI" and says on GitHub that it gives technical teams the building blocks for a custom CRM.

**A data model you design.** Custom objects, custom fields and custom views are unlimited on every plan, and you build them in Settings without code. Views come as table, Kanban or calendar. Developers can go further with the Apps framework (`npx create-twenty-app`) and ship objects, server-side logic, React components and AI skills in TypeScript.

**Email and calendar as the conversation layer.** Twenty connects Google and Microsoft accounts directly and any other provider through IMAP, SMTP and CalDAV, with unlimited accounts per user. Each account chooses what teammates see: metadata only, subject and metadata, or full content.

**Access control for growing teams.** Field-level permissions are on every plan. Organization ($19 per user per month, billed yearly) adds row-level permissions, SAML/OIDC single sign-on, a custom domain and priority support. Enterprise starts at $50,000 per year and adds single-tenant isolation, IP allow-listing and SCIM provisioning.

**AI and APIs.** Workflows and AI agents come with every plan. Every Cloud workspace ships with an MCP server that Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor connect to by OAuth. REST and GraphQL APIs and webhooks are included.

**Licence and hosting.** The repository is mostly AGPLv3; files marked `/* @license Enterprise */` fall under a commercial licence, and some packages, such as the SDKs, are MIT. Self-hosting the open-source core is free. Twenty Cloud runs on AWS in Frankfurt, and Twenty's legal FAQ states SOC 2 compliance.

If most of that matches how you work, Twenty is the stronger fit and switching would cost you more than it saves.

## Why teams look for a Twenty alternative

**Clients who write on more than email.** Twenty's email and calendar guide lists Google, Microsoft and IMAP/SMTP/CalDAV accounts and no chat channel. A Twenty partner, Next Page Technologies, has published an open-source WhatsApp integration for Twenty workflows built on Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API. It sends messages as a workflow step. For LinkedIn, Instagram and Telegram, Twenty's documentation lists no channel. For consultants and agencies whose clients answer on WhatsApp or LinkedIn, that gap decides the choice.

**Someone has to own the model.** Twenty's flexibility means a person on your team designs the objects, fields, views and workflows and keeps them tidy. A five-person service team without that person may prefer a fixed model that it only adjusts with custom fields.

## Where Customermates fits

Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM for small client-facing teams: consultants, agencies, other relationship-driven service businesses and founders. It is built in Germany.

**Conversations next to the deal.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) puts Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn (including Sales Navigator and Recruiter), WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram in one list. You reply on the original channel, link a participant to a contact once, and open every conversation with that contact from the contact page.

**Mate in Customermates Cloud.** Mate summarizes threads on every channel, drafts replies, updates records and builds views. It uses hosted AI credits, with a monthly allowance per active user listed on the [pricing page](/pricing). Deletions and a few admin actions wait for a fresh approval every time; sending a message does not, so ask Mate to save drafts when you want to read first. Routines run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes, and each runs as its owner with that owner's channels.

**Your own AI client through MCP.** The [MCP server](/docs/mcp) offers [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan and on self-hosted installs. Claude, ChatGPT or another MCP client then runs on your own AI provider.

**Open source with a hosting choice.** The core is AGPL-3.0 and runs on your own server with Docker Compose, including REST, webhooks and MCP. A [self-hosted install](/features/self-hosted) has no inbox, no Mate and no hosted credits. In Customermates Cloud the database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, a DPA is available and the subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the legal pages.

## Where Customermates is the wrong choice

- **No custom objects.** You work with contacts, organizations, deals, services and tasks. You can rename them from presets (deals as opportunities, projects or jobs; services as products or packages) and add custom fields of ten types, but not a sixth record type.
- **No single sign-on and no field-level permissions.** Roles decide per record type whether a member sees all records, only the ones assigned to them, or none. Saved views are personal and cannot be shared.
- **No GraphQL and no app framework.** Integrations use REST, webhooks, MCP or n8n, which runs separately.
- **Calendar is read-only.** Customermates does not create or book calendar events.
- **No compliance certification.** Twenty states SOC 2 compliance; Customermates holds none.
- **No free plan.** There is a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial, then a paid plan.

## Moving from Twenty: what maps where

Take a five-person consultancy that runs on Twenty's People, Companies and Opportunities, plus one custom object for engagements.

| In Twenty | In Customermates |
|---|---|
| People | Contacts |
| Companies | Organizations |
| Opportunities | Deals, renamed with the "Opportunity" preset so your team keeps its words |
| Pipeline stages | A stage field (single-select) with a probability per stage |
| A custom object, such as Engagements | Services renamed "Package", linked to each opportunity, or a single-select field |
| Tasks | Tasks; if you track a due date, add a custom date field, because tasks have no built-in due date or status |
| Connected mailboxes | Reconnect them under My Profile, Channels (Pro); their history imports in the background |
| Workflows | Routines in Customermates Cloud, or webhooks to n8n |

With stages Lead 10%, Qualified 30%, Proposal 50%, Negotiation 70%, Won 100% and Lost 0%, Customermates shows each opportunity's weighted value and a weighted pipeline total: value times stage probability, not a prediction.

Each of the five owns one routine for the Monday follow-up. It runs in Customermates Cloud and uses hosted AI credits:

> Every Monday at 8:00, find my opportunities in "Proposal" where the contact has not written in ten days. For each one, save a short follow-up draft in my latest conversation with that contact. Do not send anything. List the opportunities in the run summary.

## Other Twenty alternatives worth a look

Customermates publishes this list. Prices and features come from each vendor's own pages, checked on 8 October 2026.

- **EspoCRM** suits teams that want a self-hosted open-source CRM with custom entities. It is licensed under AGPLv3, runs on PHP with MySQL, MariaDB or PostgreSQL and installs with Docker or a script. Hosted plans start at €12.90 per user per month with a three-user minimum. See [EspoCRM compared with Customermates](/compare/espocrm-alternative).
- **Attio** suits teams that want custom objects in a hosted product. Its plans cap the number of objects: up to 3 on Free, 5 on Plus, 12 on Pro and unlimited on Enterprise. The free plan covers three seats. Plus costs €39 and Pro €93 per user per month on monthly billing. See the [Attio comparison](/compare/attio-alternative).
- **folk** fits relationship-driven teams that prospect on LinkedIn. Standard costs $30 per member per month, or $24 billed yearly, and includes a LinkedIn extension, email, calendar and WhatsApp sync, enrichment and email campaigns. Custom objects and deals need Premium at $60, or $48 billed yearly. See [folk compared with Customermates](/compare/folk).

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is Twenty CRM free?">
Self-hosting the open-source core is free. Twenty Cloud starts with Pro at $9 per user per month billed yearly, and Organization costs $19; both come with a 30-day trial without a credit card. Customermates has no free cloud plan, a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial, and a self-hosted core without licence fees.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is Twenty a good CRM for a small team?">
Yes, if someone on the team enjoys shaping the data model or a developer helps. If your clients mostly write on WhatsApp or LinkedIn and nobody wants to build the CRM, a product with those channels in its inbox needs less setup.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Twenty support WhatsApp or LinkedIn messages?">
Not in Twenty's own documentation, which covers email and calendar. A partner offers an open-source WhatsApp integration that sends messages from Twenty workflows through Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API. Customermates has WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram and Telegram in its unified inbox in Customermates Cloud from Pro.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Twenty have an MCP server?">
Yes. Twenty says every Cloud workspace ships with a native MCP server that you connect by OAuth. Customermates includes an MCP server with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan and on self-hosted installs.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What is the best open-source alternative to Twenty?">
It depends on what you miss in Twenty. EspoCRM is a self-hosted open-source CRM that also supports custom entities. Customermates is the open-source option when chat channels and an AI assistant matter more than custom objects; its inbox and Mate run only in Customermates Cloud.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Where is my data stored with Twenty and with Customermates?">
Twenty Cloud runs on AWS servers in Frankfurt, according to Twenty's legal FAQ. The Customermates Cloud database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region too, and both vendors offer a DPA and list their subprocessors. Self-hosting either product keeps the data on your own server.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

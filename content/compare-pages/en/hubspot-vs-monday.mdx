> Pick HubSpot if one or two people need a free start, or if marketing and sales should work on the same records, and if you can pay Professional prices once you need sequences, custom reports or forecasting. Pick monday CRM if your team already plans its work on monday.com boards and wants deal tracking next to it at a lower price per seat, and if a three-seat minimum and per-plan caps on columns and automations fit. Customermates publishes this comparison and makes a third option, described in its own section below. Every HubSpot and monday.com detail comes from the vendors' own pages and was checked on 8 October 2026.

## Which one fits your team

| Your situation | Better fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| One or two people, no CRM budget yet | HubSpot Free | Free for up to 2 users, with contacts, companies, deals and a Gmail, Outlook or Exchange inbox. monday CRM has no free plan |
| Client projects already run on monday.com | monday CRM | Deals sit on boards in the same account as the delivery work |
| Up to ten people who want calling and simple automation in an entry tier | HubSpot Starter | Calling, meeting recording and simple automation are in Starter, which stops at 10 users |
| You need email sequences and watch the cost per seat | monday CRM Pro | Sequences start on Pro at €28 per seat billed yearly; HubSpot puts them on Professional at €90 |
| Email campaigns and sales in one system | HubSpot | Email campaigns start on Starter, on the same platform as the CRM |
| Custom reports or revenue forecasting | HubSpot Professional | HubSpot lists both from Professional |
| EU residency that covers subprocessors as well | monday CRM, top tier | monday.com promises region-bound EU residency for its top tier only |

If Pipedrive is on your shortlist too, [HubSpot vs Pipedrive](/compare/hubspot-vs-pipedrive) and [Pipedrive vs monday](/compare/pipedrive-vs-monday) use the same method.

## How the two are built

HubSpot is organized around CRM records: contacts, companies, deals and tickets, with sales, marketing and service tools working on the same records. On its euro pricing page, HubSpot sells those tools together per tier. Breeze Assistant, HubSpot's AI assistant, is on every plan, Free included.

monday CRM runs on monday.com's work platform. Leads, contacts and deals are items on boards, and each field is a column. That makes the setup quick to reshape, and it makes the plan part of your data model. A board holds at most 5 columns on Basic, 15 on Standard and 75 on Pro. Basic also stops at 1,000 active contacts and deals, Standard at 10,000 and Pro at 100,000. Five columns is tight for a deal record that needs value, stage, owner, close date and source.

## What it costs

Prices checked on 8 October 2026 from [HubSpot's pricing page](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/sales) and [monday CRM's pricing page](https://monday.com/crm/pricing), in euros, per seat per month. Prices in other currencies differ; in US dollars, HubSpot listed Sales Hub Professional at $90 per seat billed yearly or $100 billed monthly.

|---|---|---|---|
| HubSpot Free | €0 | €0 | Up to 2 users |

What that means for a small team, billed yearly:

- **Two people.** HubSpot Free costs nothing. monday CRM bills at least 3 seats, so Basic comes to 3 × €12 = €36 a month.
- **Five people, pipeline only.** HubSpot Starter is 5 × €20 = €100 a month at list price, or 5 × €7 = €35 while the time-limited offer runs. monday CRM Standard is 5 × €17 = €85.
- **Five people who send sequences.** monday CRM Pro is 5 × €28 = €140 a month. HubSpot Professional is 5 × €90 = €450, plus the onboarding package; on the euro page that tier also includes HubSpot's marketing and service tools.
- **Monthly billing** raises the monday.com figures by close to half: €125 for five people on Standard, €205 on Pro. HubSpot Professional becomes €500.

## Features small teams compare

**Email and calls.** HubSpot connects a Gmail, Outlook or Microsoft Exchange inbox on every plan and adds calling from Starter. Sequences need Sales Hub or Service Hub Professional or Enterprise, and the user needs an assigned Sales or Service seat. monday CRM lists Gmail and Outlook among its integrations. From Standard, a communication timeline shows each person's emails, calls, meetings and notes in one place, and Pro adds mass email and email sequences.

**Automation.** HubSpot Starter creates automations from records and lets you build AI agents with simple automation; advanced automation needs Professional. monday CRM counts automations: 250 a month on Standard, 25,000 on Pro. On Standard that is about 12 per working day.

**AI.** HubSpot's paid plans include HubSpot Credits, which pay for AI agents, automation and one-to-many email as you use them, and you can buy more. Every paid monday CRM plan includes a package of AI credits. Standard and Pro list agents for outreach calls, lead sourcing, meeting prep and note taking, and Pro adds an AI workflow builder. Both vendors also run an MCP server for outside AI clients. HubSpot's remote server reads and writes contacts, companies, deals, tickets, notes and tasks; monday.com's hosted server reads and writes board data, and its calls count toward the account's daily API limit.

**Reporting.** HubSpot builds custom reports and forecasts revenue across pipelines from Professional. monday CRM counts dashboards instead: 1 custom dashboard on Basic, 5 on Standard, 50 on Pro.

**Integrations.** HubSpot says it offers more than 2,000 pre-built integrations. monday CRM names Outlook, Gmail, Zoom, QuickBooks and Docusign "and hundreds more".

## Data location and GDPR

HubSpot hosts on AWS in the US, Canada, Australia and the EU, and the EU data center is in Germany. An account gets its data center from the IP location at sign-up; accounts with a paid subscription can move it later. HubSpot's FAQ lists cases in which customer data is processed outside that location, among them third-party apps, support access and subprocessors.

monday.com has data regions in the US, the EU (Germany) and Australia. Only Enterprise accounts, the tier its CRM pricing page now calls Ultimate, get region-bound residency in which subprocessors also host the data in the EU. On other plans, monday.com may use subprocessors that host parts of the data outside the assigned region, with transfers covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

So below monday.com's top tier, both products keep the main data in Germany when the account is set up in the EU, and both can still move some of it out.

## Can HubSpot and monday.com work together?

Yes. monday.com publishes an app on HubSpot's App Marketplace that connects HubSpot contacts, companies and deals with monday.com, and the listing names a monday.com Standard or Pro plan as a requirement. That fits a split in which marketing and sales stay in HubSpot and delivery runs on monday.com boards. If you are leaving HubSpot for monday CRM entirely, Ultimate includes a white-glove migration; the smaller plans do not list one.

## A third option: Customermates

Customermates publishes this page, so read this section as a vendor describing its own product. Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM for small client-facing teams such as consultants, agencies, other service businesses and founders, built in Germany. Its record model is closer to HubSpot's than to monday.com's boards: contacts, organizations, deals, services and tasks, with custom fields of ten types. Four points matter for this comparison:

- **Conversations next to the deal.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) puts Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram in one list linked to your contacts, and you reply on the original channel. Starter has no inbox.
- **AI that does the CRM work.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes threads, drafts replies and updates records. It uses hosted AI credits, a monthly allowance per active user listed on the [pricing page](/pricing). Routines run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes. Claude, ChatGPT or another client can use the [MCP server](/docs/mcp) with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan.
- **Open source.** The AGPL-3.0 core runs on your own server with Docker Compose. A [self-hosted install](/features/self-hosted) has no inbox and no Mate. In Customermates Cloud the database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, and a [DPA](/dpa) is available.
- **Monthly prices per user.** There is no seat minimum and no onboarding fee. Five people pay [[commercial.price.starter.monthly.seats.5]] a month on Starter or [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.5]] on Pro.

A five-person agency would rename deals to "Projects", give the stage field probabilities such as Lead 10%, Proposal sent 50% and Won 100% to get a weighted pipeline value, and add a single-select field "Client status" with a saved board view per person. Saved views are personal, so each person saves their own.

Customermates does not offer marketing email, sequences or bulk sending, calling, lead scoring, data enrichment, custom objects or native mobile apps. If you need any of these, look at HubSpot or monday CRM. There is also no free plan, only a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is monday CRM a real CRM or a project management tool?">
It is a CRM product of its own, separate from monday work management, with Gmail and Outlook integrations, a communication timeline from Standard and email sequences on Pro. What sets it apart is the data model: records are items on boards, so column and dashboard caps per plan shape how much you can track. If your sales process needs more than 15 fields per deal, budget for Pro.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does HubSpot or monday CRM have a free plan?">
HubSpot does: its free plan covers up to 2 users with contacts, companies, deals, the mobile app and Breeze Assistant. monday CRM has no free plan. It offers a 14-day trial, and qualified nonprofits can get discounted pricing.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What are the downsides of HubSpot for a small team?">
Mostly the price step. Free stops at 2 users and Starter at 10. Sequences, custom reports and forecasting need Professional, at €90 per seat per month billed yearly, plus a required onboarding package. For five people that is €450 a month before onboarding, against €140 for five seats of monday CRM Pro, which also includes sequences. In return, the euro Professional tier bundles HubSpot's marketing and service tools.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Which handles complex deals with many stakeholders better?">
HubSpot goes further. It lists collaborative deal plans and revenue forecasting from Professional, and team hierarchies and deal approvals by stage on Enterprise. monday CRM's Ultimate tier adds advanced permissions and log rules that file emails and activities on the right records. For a small team with a handful of contacts per deal, either works; the gap shows with large account teams.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Which is better for agencies and consultants?">
It depends on where the work happens. If delivery already runs on monday.com boards, monday CRM keeps sales and projects in one account. If you win clients through inbound marketing, HubSpot keeps campaigns and deals on the same records. If your clients write on WhatsApp, LinkedIn and email at once, Customermates, which publishes this page, puts those conversations next to the deal from its Pro plan.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

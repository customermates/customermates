> The best Attio alternative depends on what you miss in Attio: Twenty is the closest open-source match for its data model, folk suits LinkedIn-led prospecting, Pipedrive and HubSpot suit pipeline-first teams and a free start, and Customermates suits small client-facing teams whose clients write on email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp and who may want to host the CRM themselves. If you depend on Attio's built-in enrichment, call recording or sequences, Attio stays the stronger product. Customermates publishes this comparison; every Attio detail comes from attio.com and was checked on 12 November 2026.

## Attio alternatives, ranked

The ranking follows one job: keeping every client conversation next to the deal. Customermates publishes this list and ranks first on that measure, so check each line on the vendor's own site. Prices and features come from each vendor's pricing page, all checked on 12 November 2026.

1. **Customermates** suits small client-facing teams whose clients write on email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram. From Pro at [[commercial.price.pro.monthly]] per user per month, those threads sit on the client record and Mate drafts the reply for you to review and send. The AGPL-3.0 core can be self-hosted, without the inbox and Mate.
2. **Twenty** is the closest open-source match for Attio's data model. It has unlimited custom objects, an MCP server in every cloud workspace and a self-hosted edition. Cloud Pro costs $9 per user per month billed yearly. Organization, at $39 per user per month billed yearly, adds SAML/OIDC single sign-on and field-level and row-level permissions. The trial runs 30 days. The [Twenty comparison](/compare/twenty-alternative) goes into detail.
3. **folk** suits relationship-driven teams that prospect on LinkedIn. Standard costs $24 per member per month billed yearly or $30 monthly and includes a LinkedIn extension, email, calendar and WhatsApp sync, contact enrichment and email campaigns. Custom objects, deals and email sequences need Premium at $48 or $60. See [folk compared with Customermates](/compare/folk).
4. **Breakcold** suits teams that sell on LinkedIn. Its pricing page lists plans from $49 per seat per month billed monthly, with two connected accounts per seat and Claude, ChatGPT and MCP access on every plan. See [Breakcold compared with Customermates](/compare/breakcold-alternative).
5. **HubSpot** suits teams that want a free start. Its free CRM covers up to 2 users. Starter lists at $20 per seat per month; new customers pay a promotional $7 with annual payment or $10 month to month, for a limited time (US pricing page, in dollars). See [HubSpot compared with Customermates](/compare/hubspot-alternative).
6. **Pipedrive** suits pipeline-first sales teams. Lite costs €14 per seat per month billed yearly. Growth, at €39, adds full email sync with tracking, automations and email sequences. See [Pipedrive compared with Customermates](/compare/pipedrive-alternative).

## Who Attio is a good choice for

Attio calls itself "the CRM for agentic revenue". Its real strength is the data model. A workspace holds objects such as people, companies and deals, and from Pro you add custom objects and custom relationship attributes: up to 12 objects on Pro, unlimited on Ultra. Enrichment fills company and people records on every plan, Free included. Employee count and ARR start on Plus; funding raised starts on Pro.

The rest of the package is broad. Ask Attio, AI agents, workflows, the REST API, webhooks and an MCP server are on every plan. Pro adds Call Intelligence, which records and transcribes video calls, plus email sequences, advanced permissions and up to 100 reports. Attio has apps for iOS, Android and the Mac and a Chrome extension. Ultra includes SSO, SCIM and audit logs, and Pro can add them with the Governance add-on at €25 per user per month, billed yearly. Venture-backed startups with up to 10 users get 80% off annual Pro in their first year.

If that list describes how you work, switching will probably cost you more than it saves.

## Why teams look for an Attio alternative

**The deeper features sit on Pro.** Custom objects, sequences, Call Intelligence and advanced permissions all need Pro. Attio prices a workspace: Pro costs $125 a month with one user included on monthly billing, or $100 billed yearly, and every further user adds $99 or $79 (in euros: €110 or €85, plus €93 or €74 per user). Plus costs $50 or $40 with one user, adds $44 or $35 per extra user (€45 or €35, plus €39 or €31) and stops at 10 users and 5 objects.

**AI and workflows run on credits.** Every plan includes seat credits for Ask Attio and a pool of workspace credits for AI features and workflows. When a workspace on a paid plan needs more, Attio sells packs of 5,000 to 50,000 workspace credits, starting at €85 a month on monthly billing or €70 billed yearly (the euro prices on Attio's pricing page).

**Email means Google or Microsoft.** Attio syncs Gmail and Microsoft 365 directly. Its help center, checked on 12 November 2026, says Attio supports direct syncing only for Gmail and Microsoft 365 accounts: other providers and protocols such as IMAP or SMTP cannot be connected, on-premises Exchange is not supported, and Microsoft 365 shared mailboxes cannot be synced directly. Mail from elsewhere comes in by forwarding to an Attio address. WhatsApp and LinkedIn conversations reach Attio through third-party apps in its App Store, such as TimelinesAI for WhatsApp and SuperLinkIn for LinkedIn, which save the messages as notes on the person record.

**Hosted plans only.** Attio's pricing page lists Free, Plus, Pro and Ultra as hosted plans, with no self-hosted edition and no open-source licence. Its data processing addendum names Google Cloud as its hosting provider and uses EU standard contractual clauses for transfers.

## Where Customermates fits

If your clients answer on LinkedIn or WhatsApp and Attio keeps those threads in add-on apps, Customermates keeps them on the client record from the start. Mate or your own AI agent drafts the reply and the next step, and you review, edit and send. The core is open source and built in Germany.

**Conversations next to the deal.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn (including Sales Navigator and Recruiter), WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one list, and you reply on the original channel. Link a participant to a contact once, and every conversation with that address or handle shows on the contact's timeline. Starter has no inbox.

**AI that does the CRM work.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes threads, drafts replies, updates records and builds views. It uses hosted AI credits, with a monthly allowance per active user listed on the [pricing page](/pricing), and there are no top-ups or overage charges. Mate asks for a fresh approval before every delete. Sending a message runs without one, so tell it to save drafts when you want to review first. Routines run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes: [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] per user on Starter, [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]] on Pro.

Prefer Claude, ChatGPT or another client you already pay for? Connect it to the [MCP server](/docs/mcp), which offers [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan and on self-hosted installs. Its model calls then run on your own AI provider.

**Open source, with a hosting choice.** The core is AGPL-3.0 and runs on your own server with Docker Compose and PostgreSQL 16, including REST, webhooks and MCP. A [self-hosted install](/features/self-hosted) has no inbox, no Mate and no hosted credits. In Customermates Cloud the database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, a [DPA](/dpa) is available, and the subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the legal pages. Customermates holds no compliance certification.

## Where Customermates is the wrong choice

Check these before you start a trial:

- **No enrichment, call recording or telephony.** Contact data is what your team or your AI client enters.
- **No sequences and no bulk email.** Outreach goes one message at a time, written by you or drafted by Mate.
- **No native mobile apps and no SSO.** Customermates runs in the phone's browser. Permissions apply per list and action, not per field.
- **A small free plan.** Free covers [[commercial.limit.free.maxMembers]] person and [[commercial.limit.free.maxRecords]] records, without the inbox, Mate or routines; more needs a paid plan after the [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial.

## A worked setup: an agency moving from Attio

Take a five-person agency that sells projects and retainers. Three account leads work mostly by email; two talk to clients mostly on WhatsApp.

| In Attio | In Customermates |
|---|---|
| People | Contacts |
| Companies | Organizations |
| Deals | Deals, renamed "Projects" |
| A custom "Retainers" object | Services, renamed "Packages", added to each project as line items |
| A list such as "Active clients" | A single choice field "Client status" plus a saved view filtered on it |
| Deal stages | A stage field with probabilities |

The stages are Lead 10%, Qualified 30%, Proposal sent 50%, Negotiation 70%, Won 100% and Lost 0%. Customermates multiplies each project's value by its stage probability, and a chart widget on the Dashboard sums the weighted values. That is arithmetic on your own numbers, and it does not predict anything.

The three account leads connect Gmail; the other two connect WhatsApp. One account each fits Pro, at [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.5]] a month for the team. When one person needs email and WhatsApp together, the workspace moves to Business at [[commercial.price.business.monthly.seats.5]]. Each person saves a board of open projects grouped by stage, and the founder keeps a table grouped by owner.

Each of the five owns one routine for the Monday follow-up check. A routine runs as its owner, so it sees that person's projects and saves drafts in their own channels. It runs in Customermates Cloud and uses hosted AI credits:

> Every Monday at 8:00, find my projects in "Proposal sent" where the contact has not written in ten days. For each one, save a short follow-up draft in my latest conversation with that contact, and list the projects in the run summary.

## Switching from Attio in five steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export from Attio">Open each view in Attio and choose Import / Export, then Export view as Excel. The file holds the columns visible in the view, so show every attribute you want to keep first. Attio does not export enriched values; those cells come out blank.</Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the workspace">Rename lists in Data model if your words differ, create custom fields for the attributes you keep, and set up the stage field with its probabilities.</Step>
  <Step title="Import one list at a time">Import organizations first, then contacts, then deals. A workbook can hold up to 10,000 rows and 10 MB. Link columns match records by name, so a contact's company column finds the organization you imported before. New contacts need a first and a last name. Map email addresses and phone numbers to the contact's Contact details field; contact details are added to new contacts only.</Step>
  <Step title="Rebuild lists as views">Turn each Attio list into a single choice field and a saved view. Share the views the whole team needs from the view's **Share…** menu. A notes column in the workbook maps to the record's notes; notes you add later can be written in bulk by an AI client over MCP, up to 100 records per call.</Step>
  <Step title="Connect channels and AI">On Pro or during the trial, connect a mailbox, LinkedIn or WhatsApp under Settings, Channels & calendars. Then set up routines for Mate, or connect your own AI client through MCP.</Step>

<FaqItem question="How do I move my data from Attio to Customermates?">

</FaqItem>

> Look for an Attio alternative when your clients write on channels Attio does not sync itself, or when you need a CRM you can host yourself. Attio syncs Gmail and Microsoft 365 directly, brings in WhatsApp and LinkedIn through third-party apps and sells hosted plans only. Customermates puts Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram in one inbox from Pro, and its open-source core runs on your own server. If you depend on custom objects or built-in data enrichment, Attio is the stronger product, and Twenty or folk come closer to it than Customermates. Customermates publishes this comparison; every Attio detail comes from attio.com and was checked on 8 October 2026.

## Which CRM fits which team

| Your situation | Better fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Up to three people on Gmail or Microsoft 365, and you want enrichment at no cost | Attio Free | The free plan covers 3 seats and 50,000 records, with company and people enrichment |
| Your process needs its own record types, such as investors, properties or funds | Attio Pro or Twenty | Both offer custom objects; Customermates has five fixed record types |
| You record sales calls or send email sequences from the CRM | Attio Pro | Call Intelligence and sequences are Pro features; Customermates has neither |
| Clients write on WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram or Telegram as well as email | Customermates Pro or Business | All of these channels are native in one inbox |
| Your mailbox runs on IMAP, outside Google and Microsoft 365 | Customermates Pro or Business | Attio syncs only Gmail and Microsoft 365 directly |
| You need an open-source licence or want to host the CRM yourself | Customermates or Twenty | Both can be self-hosted (Customermates without the inbox and Mate); Attio sells hosted plans only |
| You need SSO, SCIM or native mobile apps | Attio | Customermates offers none of them |

## Who Attio is a good choice for

Attio calls itself "the CRM for agentic revenue". Its real strength is the data model. A workspace holds objects such as people, companies and deals, and from Pro you add custom objects and custom relationship attributes: up to 12 objects on Pro, unlimited on Enterprise. Enrichment fills company and people records on every plan, Free included. Employee count and ARR start on Plus; funding raised starts on Pro.

The rest of the package is broad. Ask Attio, AI agents, workflows, the REST API, webhooks and an MCP server are on every plan. Pro adds Call Intelligence, which records and transcribes video calls, plus email sequences, advanced permissions and up to 100 reports. Attio has apps for iOS, Android and the Mac and a Chrome extension. Enterprise adds SSO and SCIM. Eligible startups can apply to a startup program that takes up to 80% off.

If that list describes how you work, switching will probably cost you more than it saves.

## Why teams look for an Attio alternative

**The deeper features sit on Pro.** Custom objects, sequences, Call Intelligence and advanced permissions all need Pro: $99 per user per month on monthly billing, or $79 billed yearly (€93 or €74 in euros). Plus costs $44 or $35 (€39 or €31) and stops at 10 seats and 5 objects.

**AI and workflows run on credits.** Every plan includes seat credits for Ask Attio and a pool of workspace credits for AI features and workflows. When a workspace on a paid plan needs more, Attio sells packs of 5,000 to 50,000 workspace credits, starting at $85 a month on monthly billing or $70 billed yearly.

**Email means Google or Microsoft.** Attio syncs Gmail and Microsoft 365 directly. Its help center says other providers and IMAP cannot be connected; mail from elsewhere comes in by forwarding. WhatsApp and LinkedIn conversations reach Attio through third-party apps in its App Store, such as TimelinesAI for WhatsApp and SuperLinkIn for LinkedIn, which save the messages as notes on the person record.

**Hosted plans only.** Attio's pricing page lists Free, Plus, Pro and Enterprise as hosted plans, with no self-hosted edition and no open-source licence. Its data processing addendum names Google Cloud as its hosting provider and uses EU standard contractual clauses for transfers.

## Where Customermates fits

Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM for small client-facing teams: consultants, agencies, other service businesses and founders. It is built in Germany.

**Conversations next to the deal.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn (including Sales Navigator and Recruiter), WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one list, and you reply on the original channel. Link a participant to a contact once, and every conversation with that address or handle shows on the contact's timeline. Starter has no inbox.

**AI that does the CRM work.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes threads, drafts replies, updates records and builds views. It uses hosted AI credits, with a monthly allowance per active user listed on the [pricing page](/pricing), and there are no top-ups or overage charges. Mate asks for a fresh approval before every delete. Sending a message runs without one, so tell it to save drafts when you want to review first. Routines run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes: [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] per user on Starter, [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]] on Pro.

Prefer Claude, ChatGPT or another client you already pay for? Connect it to the [MCP server](/docs/mcp), which offers [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan and on self-hosted installs. Its model calls then run on your own AI provider.

**Open source, with a hosting choice.** The core is AGPL-3.0 and runs on your own server with Docker Compose and PostgreSQL 16, including REST, webhooks and MCP. A [self-hosted install](/features/self-hosted) has no inbox, no Mate and no hosted credits. In Customermates Cloud the database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, a [DPA](/dpa) is available, and the subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the legal pages. Customermates holds no compliance certification.

## Where Customermates is the wrong choice

Check these before you start a trial:

- **No custom objects.** You work with contacts, organizations, deals, services and tasks. You can rename them from presets (deals as projects or jobs, services as products or packages) and add custom fields of ten types, but you cannot add a sixth record type.
- **No enrichment, call recording or telephony.** Contact data is what your team or your AI client enters.
- **No sequences and no bulk email.** Outreach goes one message at a time, written by you or drafted by Mate.
- **No native mobile apps and no SSO.** Customermates runs in the phone's browser. Saved views are personal and cannot be shared, and permissions apply per record type and action, not per field.
- **No free plan.** There is a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial, then a paid plan.

## A worked setup: an agency moving from Attio

Take a five-person agency that sells projects and retainers. Three account leads work mostly by email; two talk to clients mostly on WhatsApp.

| In Attio | In Customermates |
|---|---|
| People | Contacts |
| Companies | Organizations |
| Deals | Deals, renamed "Projects" |
| A custom "Retainers" object | Services, renamed "Packages", linked to each project |
| A list such as "Active clients" | A single-select field "Client status" plus a saved view filtered on it |
| Deal stages | A stage field with probabilities |

The stages are Lead 10%, Qualified 30%, Proposal sent 50%, Negotiation 70%, Won 100% and Lost 0%. Customermates multiplies each project's value by its stage probability and shows a weighted pipeline total. That is arithmetic on your own numbers, and it does not predict anything.

The three account leads connect Gmail; the other two connect WhatsApp. One account each fits Pro, at [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.5]] a month for the team. When one person needs email and WhatsApp together, the workspace moves to Business at [[commercial.price.business.monthly.seats.5]]. Each person saves a board of open projects grouped by stage, and the founder keeps a table grouped by owner.

Each of the five owns one routine for the Monday follow-up check. A routine runs as its owner, so it sees that person's projects and saves drafts in their own channels. It runs in Customermates Cloud and uses hosted AI credits:

> Every Monday at 8:00, find my projects in "Proposal sent" where the contact has not written in ten days. For each one, save a short follow-up draft in my latest conversation with that contact, and list the projects in the run summary.

## Switching from Attio in five steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export from Attio">Open each view in Attio and choose Import / Export, then Export view as Excel. The file holds the columns visible in the view, so show every attribute you want to keep first. Attio does not export enriched values; those cells come out blank.</Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the workspace">Rename record types if your words differ, create custom fields for the attributes you keep, and set up the stage field with its probabilities.</Step>
  <Step title="Import one record type at a time">Import organizations first, then contacts, then deals. A workbook can hold up to 10,000 rows and 10 MB. Link columns match records by name, so a contact's company column finds the organization you imported before. New contacts need a first and a last name. Map email addresses and WhatsApp numbers to the contact's channels; channels are added to new contacts only.</Step>
  <Step title="Rebuild lists as views">Turn each Attio list into a single-select field and a saved view. Views are personal, so each person saves their own. A notes column in the workbook maps to the record's notes; notes you add later can be written in bulk by an AI client over MCP, up to 100 records per call.</Step>
  <Step title="Connect channels and AI">On Pro or during the trial, connect a mailbox, LinkedIn or WhatsApp under My Profile, Channels. Then set up routines for Mate, or connect your own AI client through MCP.</Step>
</Steps>

Cloud Pro costs $9 per user per month billed yearly. Organization, at $19 per user per month billed yearly, adds SAML/OIDC single sign-on and row-level permissions. The trial runs 30 days. ## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is Attio free?">
Yes, for small teams. Attio Free covers up to 3 seats, 50,000 records and 3 objects, with email and calendar sync, enrichment and a monthly credit allowance. Paid plans start with Plus at $44 per user per month, or $35 billed yearly. Attio also offers a 14-day trial of Pro. Customermates has no free plan; it has a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial and paid plans billed monthly.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there an open-source alternative to Attio?">
Yes, two. Twenty is closest to Attio's data model, with custom objects, and can be self-hosted. Customermates has an AGPL-3.0 core that you can self-host with Docker Compose; the inbox and Mate exist only in Customermates Cloud.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Attio support WhatsApp and LinkedIn messages?">
Through third-party apps. Attio's App Store lists TimelinesAI for WhatsApp and SuperLinkIn for LinkedIn, separate products that save messages as notes on the person record. In Customermates, both channels are part of the unified inbox from Pro, and you reply from there.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Attio have an MCP server like Customermates?">
Yes. Attio lists an MCP server on every plan, including Free, and you connect it with OAuth. Customermates includes an MCP server with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan and on self-hosted installs, so Claude, ChatGPT or another MCP client can read and update the CRM.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is Attio GDPR compliant?">
As the controller, you assess that for your own use. Attio's data processing addendum is part of its customer agreement, names Google Cloud as its hosting provider and covers transfers with EU standard contractual clauses. Customermates has its cloud database configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, offers a DPA and lists its subprocessors, including US providers.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How do I move my data from Attio to Customermates?">
Export each Attio view as an Excel file and import it one record type at a time: organizations, contacts, deals. Enriched values stay blank, because Attio does not export them. The five steps above have the details.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

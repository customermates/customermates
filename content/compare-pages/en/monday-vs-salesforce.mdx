> For most teams of three to ten people, monday CRM is the cheaper way to run a pipeline: Basic and Standard cost €12 and €17 per seat a month billed yearly, while Salesforce charges €25 per user for Starter Suite and €100 for Pro Suite, which adds sales quoting and forecasting. Pick Salesforce when you need those functions or a path into its larger editions. For two people, Salesforce's Free Suite costs nothing, while monday CRM bills at least three seats. If your team already plans client work on monday.com boards, monday CRM keeps sales on the same platform. Customermates publishes this comparison and makes a third option, described in its own section. Every monday.com and Salesforce detail comes from the vendors' own pages, checked on 8 October 2026.

## How the two are built

**monday CRM** is one product on monday.com's work platform, next to work management, service and development. Leads, contacts and deals are items on boards, and each field is a column. That makes the setup quick to change, and it makes the plan part of your data model. A board holds at most 5 columns on Basic, 15 on Standard and 75 on Pro. Basic stops at 1,000 active contacts and deals, Standard at 10,000 and Pro at 100,000. A deal board with value, stage, owner, close date and source already needs five columns.

**Salesforce Sales Cloud** starts from CRM objects. Accounts, contacts, leads and opportunities are in every suite, the Free Suite included. Starter Suite adds built-in sales flows and lead routing. Pro Suite adds sales quoting and forecasting plus access to AppExchange apps, and it is sold yearly with a contract. Core, Advanced and Max are the larger editions: Advanced adds enhanced security and Backup & Recover, and Max includes Agentforce for Sales.

So you pick a monday CRM plan by how many columns, contacts and automations you need. You pick a Salesforce plan by which sales functions you need, and the step from Starter Suite to Pro Suite is the expensive one.

Reading the table:

- **Two people pay nothing on Salesforce's Free Suite** if its features cover the work. On monday CRM they pay for a third seat nobody uses.

- **Pro Suite is the big step.** At €100 per user with a yearly contract, ten people pay €12,000 a year, more than three times monday CRM Pro billed yearly. Check that you need quoting, forecasting and AppExchange apps before you sign.

## Where they differ day to day

**Email.** Salesforce lists email sync from the Free Suite and email marketing from the Starter Suite. monday CRM shows every email, call, meeting and note for a person in one timeline from Standard, and adds mass email and email sequences on Pro.

**Automation.** monday CRM counts custom automations per month: 250 on Standard and 25,000 on Pro, with none listed on Basic. On Standard that is about 12 per working day. Salesforce automates with Flow Builder, which is limited to five flows per org on the Free Suite.

**AI.** From Standard, monday CRM plans include AI features, and the pricing page lists agents for lead sourcing, meeting prep and meeting notes; Basic lists none. Salesforce sells Agentforce as an add-on on the editions below Max and includes Agentforce for Sales in Max. Both vendors also let outside AI clients in through MCP. monday MCP works on all monday.com plans at no extra cost, though your AI tool may charge. Salesforce runs MCP servers for Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and other AI platforms; our [guide to Salesforce MCP](/blog/salesforce-mcp) explains which server needs which edition.

**Mobile.** monday CRM's iOS and Android apps start on Standard. Salesforce includes the Salesforce Mobile App.

**Data location.** monday.com hosts accounts in the US, the EU (Germany) or Australia. Only Enterprise accounts in the EU, the tier monday CRM now calls Ultimate, keep sub-processor hosting in the EU as well. On other plans, monday.com may use sub-processors that host parts of the data outside the region. Salesforce offers the Hyperforce EU Operating Zone, which stores and processes data in the EU with support from EU-based staff; ask Salesforce in writing whether your edition and contract include it.

## A third option: Customermates

Customermates publishes this page, so read this section as a vendor describing its own product. Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM built in Germany for small client-facing teams: consultants, agencies, other service businesses and founders. It covers less ground than either product above and is built for one job, keeping the conversations that move a deal next to that deal.

- **Conversations next to the record.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one list linked to contacts, organizations and deals, and you reply on the original channel. Starter has no inbox.
- **Mate for CRM work.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes threads, drafts replies and updates records, using hosted AI credits. If you prefer your own Claude or ChatGPT, connect it to the [MCP server](/docs/mcp), which has [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan.
- **Open source with a hosting choice.** The AGPL-3.0 core runs on your own server with Docker Compose, including REST, webhooks and MCP. A [self-hosted install](/features/self-hosted) has no inbox, no Mate and no hosted credits. In Customermates Cloud, the database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region.
- **Monthly prices per user, no seat minimum.** Five people pay [[commercial.price.starter.monthly.seats.5]] a month on Starter, [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.5]] on Pro with [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] connected account each, or [[commercial.price.business.monthly.seats.5]] on Business with [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedAccountsPerUser]] each. Each active user also gets a monthly allowance of hosted AI credits, listed on the [pricing page](/pricing).

A five-person consultancy would give the Stage field probabilities such as Inquiry 10%, Proposal sent 50% and Won 100%, so every deal shows a weighted value and the pipeline a weighted total. It would add a Single Select field "Client status" and save one board view per person, because saved views are personal. On Pro, with each partner's mailbox connected, each partner can own a [routine](/docs/app-routines) that runs in Customermates Cloud as that partner:

> Every Friday at 15:00, find my deals in "Proposal sent" where the contact has not written in seven days. Save a short follow-up draft in my latest conversation with that contact. Do not send anything.

The last sentence matters, because Mate sends messages without asking again when an instruction tells it to.

Customermates is the wrong choice if you need what it lacks: it has no project management beyond tasks, no quotes or invoices, no mass email or sequences, no calling, no custom objects and no native mobile apps. Its weighted pipeline is arithmetic on your own numbers, not a forecast, and tasks have no due dates. For delivery boards, stay with monday CRM. For quoting and forecasts, Salesforce lists both from Pro Suite. There is no free plan, only a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is monday part of Salesforce?">
No. monday CRM is made by monday.com Ltd., a separate company whose platform also covers work management, service and development. Salesforce makes Sales Cloud. The two products can be connected, as the next answer explains.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can monday.com and Salesforce be integrated?">
Yes. monday.com's Salesforce integration reflects new Salesforce accounts, leads and contacts in monday.com automatically. That suits a team that sells in Salesforce and runs delivery on monday.com boards. Check the plan requirements on both sides before you build on it. If you are moving from Salesforce to monday CRM entirely, monday.com lists a white-glove migration on Ultimate; on the smaller plans you export and import the data yourself.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is monday CRM free?">
No. monday CRM offers a 14-day trial but no free plan, and its paid plans start at three seats. Of the two vendors, only Salesforce has a free option, the Free Suite for two users. Customermates has no free plan either, only a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can monday.com be used as a CRM?">
Yes. monday CRM is a CRM product of its own on the monday.com platform, separate from monday work management, and it keeps leads, contacts and deals as items on boards. With five columns per board and no custom automations listed, Basic covers little more than a contact list. For a sales pipeline, Standard is the realistic starting point. Salesforce Sales Cloud, by contrast, is built around accounts, leads and opportunities.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Do monday CRM and Salesforce work with Claude or ChatGPT?">
Both run MCP servers that AI clients can connect to. monday MCP lets an AI tool update board data and create tasks, on all monday.com plans at no extra cost. Salesforce's MCP servers work with Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and other AI platforms. Customermates has an MCP server with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan, self-hosted installs included.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

> Look for a GoHighLevel alternative when you use HighLevel mainly for contacts, pipelines and client conversations, while its funnels, SMS campaigns and reseller tools sit unused. A CRM-first tool covers that part without the rest of the suite. Customermates is one: an open-source CRM built in Germany that puts email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram next to your deals from Pro. If you build funnels, call and text clients, or resell software under your own brand, HighLevel is the stronger product and Customermates cannot replace it. Customermates publishes this comparison; HighLevel details come from gohighlevel.com and were checked on 8 October 2026.

## Which tool fits which team

| Your situation | Better fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| You run marketing for several clients, each with its own account | HighLevel | Sub-accounts and SaaS Mode are built for this; Customermates has one workspace per company |
| You build funnels, websites or courses and send email or SMS campaigns | HighLevel or systeme.io | Customermates has no page builder, campaigns or SMS |
| You call clients from the CRM or need website chat | HighLevel | Customermates has no telephony and no chat widget |
| Ten or more people need access and price per head decides | HighLevel | Unlimited users for one flat fee |
| Clients write to you on LinkedIn, WhatsApp, email or Telegram, and those threads belong next to the deal | Customermates Pro or Business | One inbox for all of them; HighLevel's Conversations page lists neither LinkedIn nor Telegram |
| You want the CRM database in the EU, on your own server, or open source | Customermates | Cloud database configured in the AWS Frankfurt region; self-hostable AGPL-3.0 core |
| You want Claude or ChatGPT to update the CRM | Either | Both offer an MCP server |

## Who HighLevel is a good choice for

HighLevel is built for agencies that run marketing for several businesses from one login. Each client gets a sub-account: three on Starter at $97 a month, unlimited on Unlimited at $297 and on Agency Pro at $497. Every plan includes unlimited contacts and users, with no long-term contract.

Next to CRM and pipelines, the platform has a website and funnel builder, email and SMS marketing, calling, booking calendars, reputation management, courses, payments, workflow automation and a mobile app. Agency Pro adds SaaS Mode, for selling HighLevel subscriptions to your clients, and lets you rebill their phone and email usage with a markup.

If your agency uses most of that, one HighLevel bill replaces several tools, and a CRM like Customermates covers a small slice of it.

## Why teams look for a GoHighLevel alternative

**They use one corner of the platform.** A consultancy that tracks deals and answers client messages needs the CRM, the pipeline and Conversations. The funnels, courses and reseller settings still come with the account.

**Usage charges come on top of the plan.** HighLevel's pricing page says phone numbers, SMS, emails sent and premium AI usage are charged by use, and telecom usage fees can apply during the trial too. WhatsApp is an add-on at $10 a month per sub-account. The AI Employee plans cost $50 or $97 a month per sub-account.

**LinkedIn is not in the inbox.** HighLevel's Conversations page lists email, SMS, Google Business Profile, website chat, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Instagram DMs, phone calls and voicemail. LinkedIn and Telegram are not on that list, so consultants who win work through LinkedIn keep those threads elsewhere.

**Data goes to the USA, and the code stays closed.** HighLevel's data processing agreement uses standard contractual clauses so that customers can "lawfully send personal data to us in the USA", and HighLevel is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. It is proprietary, and its pricing page offers no self-hosted edition. If you want the CRM database in the EU or on your own server, you need a different product.

## Where Customermates fits

Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM for small client-facing teams such as consultants, agencies and founders, built in Germany.

**Conversations next to the deal.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn (including Sales Navigator and Recruiter), WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one list, and you reply on the original channel. Once you link a participant to a contact, the contact's timeline shows every conversation with that address or handle. Starter has no inbox.

**AI that does the CRM work.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes threads, drafts replies and updates records, using hosted AI credits: a monthly allowance per active user, listed on the pricing page. Mate asks for a fresh approval before every delete, but sends messages without one, so tell it to save drafts when you want to review first. Routines run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes: [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] per user on Starter, [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]] on Pro. Prefer Claude or ChatGPT? Connect it to the [MCP server](/docs/mcp), with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan; the model calls then run on your own AI provider.

**Open source, with a hosting choice.** The AGPL-3.0 core runs on your own server with Docker Compose, including REST, webhooks and MCP. A [self-hosted install](/features/self-hosted) has no inbox, no Mate and no hosted credits. In Customermates Cloud the database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, a [DPA](/dpa) is available, and the [subprocessors](/subprocessors), including US providers, are listed. Customermates holds no compliance certification.

## Where Customermates is the wrong choice

Check this list against what you use in HighLevel today:

- **No funnels, websites, forms, courses or communities.** Customermates has no page builder and no embeddable web forms.
- **No SMS, no calling and no marketing email.** It sends one message at a time from the inbox, with no campaigns, sequences or bulk email.
- **No booking.** Pro reads your calendar but cannot book or create events.
- **No payments and no invoices.** Billing your clients stays in another tool.
- **No sub-accounts, no white-label and no reseller mode.** One company is one workspace.
- **No native mobile app.** Customermates runs in the phone's browser.
- **No tags, no task due dates and no reminders.** Use a single-select field instead of tags. A routine can look for quiet deals and draft follow-ups, but it sends no reminder notifications.
- **Saved views are personal.** There are no team views.

## A worked setup: a four-person consultancy leaving HighLevel

Two partners and two consultants sell advisory projects. They used HighLevel for contacts, one pipeline and email, never for funnels or SMS. Three write to clients by email; one partner works mostly through LinkedIn.

| In HighLevel | In Customermates |
|---|---|
| Contacts | Contacts |
| Companies | Organizations |
| Opportunities in one pipeline | Deals with a Stage field and stage probabilities |
| Tags such as "referral" or "past client" | No tags: a single-select field "Source", one value per contact |
| Custom fields | Custom fields of ten types, such as currency, single select and date range |
| Smart Lists | Saved views, one set per person |
| Conversations (email) | Unified inbox: three Gmail accounts and one LinkedIn account |
| A workflow that reminds the owner about quiet deals | A routine in Customermates Cloud |

The stages are Inquiry 10%, Scoping call 25%, Proposal sent 50%, Negotiation 75%, Won 100% and Lost 0%. Customermates multiplies each deal's value by its stage probability and shows a weighted pipeline total. That is arithmetic on your own numbers, nothing more.

Each person connects one account, so Pro fits, at [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.4]] a month for the team. If the LinkedIn partner also wants a mailbox in the inbox, the workspace moves to Business at [[commercial.price.business.monthly.seats.4]].

Each of the four owns one routine, which runs as its owner: it sees that person's deals and uses their channels. It runs in Customermates Cloud on hosted AI credits:

> Every Monday at 8:00, find my deals in "Proposal sent" where the contact has not written in ten days. For each one, save a short follow-up draft in my latest conversation with that contact. Do not send anything. List the deals in the run summary.

## Switching from GoHighLevel in five steps

Before you start, decide where funnels, SMS, calling, booking and payments go, if you use them. Customermates takes over contacts, deals and conversations.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export contacts and companies">On the Contacts page, show every column you need with Manage Fields, since the export holds only visible columns, then export. The file appears under Bulk Actions for 30 days; only agency and location admins can download it. Companies export from Contacts, Companies. Both arrive as CSV files. A contact export includes only the latest note, cut to 255 characters.</Step>
  <Step title="Save each file as an Excel workbook">Customermates does not accept CSV, so open each file and save it as .xlsx before you import it.</Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the workspace">Create the custom fields you keep, turn the tags that matter into single-select fields (Customermates has no tags), and set up the Stage field with its probabilities.</Step>
  <Step title="Import one record type at a time">Use the Import and export menu on each list. Import organizations first, then contacts, so a contact's company column finds its organization by name. New contacts need a first and a last name. Map email addresses and WhatsApp numbers to channels; a plain phone number goes into a custom Phone column. Re-create open opportunities as deals, or let your AI client create them over MCP. The [records documentation](/docs/app-records) has the details.</Step>
  <Step title="Connect channels and set up the routine">On Pro or during the trial, connect each person's mailbox, LinkedIn or WhatsApp under My Profile, Channels. Then add the routine, or connect your own AI client through MCP.</Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Are HighLevel and GoHighLevel the same?">
Yes. HighLevel is an all-in-one marketing and sales platform built for agencies. The company and its product are called HighLevel; the website and help center run on gohighlevel.com, which is where the name GoHighLevel comes from. Its CRM, with contacts, companies and pipelines, is one part of the platform.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How much does GoHighLevel cost, and is there a free version?">
Starter costs $97 a month, Unlimited $297 and Agency Pro $497; a year costs ten monthly payments. Enterprise is priced on request. There is no free plan, only a 14-day trial on every plan, and billing starts automatically when it ends unless you cancel. Usage charges for phone, SMS, email and premium AI come on top. Customermates Cloud has no free plan either; it has a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial without a credit card. The open-source core is free to self-host, without the inbox and Mate.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Which is better, HubSpot or GoHighLevel?">
It depends on how you sell. HighLevel charges one flat fee per account with unlimited users, which suits agencies running many client sub-accounts. HubSpot sells marketing, sales and service software per seat, with free tools for up to two users, which suits a company growing its own team.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can Claude or ChatGPT work with GoHighLevel and Customermates?">
Yes, with both. HighLevel runs an official MCP server for Claude, ChatGPT and Codex, connected with OAuth, that reaches more than 550 operations across its platform. Customermates has an MCP server with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan and on self-hosted installs, connected with an API key or OAuth.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there an open-source GoHighLevel alternative?">
For the CRM part, yes. Customermates has an AGPL-3.0 core you can self-host; the inbox and Mate exist only in Customermates Cloud. It does not replace HighLevel's funnels, SMS or reseller tools. The [open-source CRM guide](/blog/open-source-crm) compares more options.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is GoHighLevel GDPR compliant?">
As the controller, you assess that for your own use. HighLevel offers a data processing agreement that covers transfers to the USA with standard contractual clauses, and it is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Customermates has its cloud database configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, offers a DPA and lists its subprocessors, including US providers.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

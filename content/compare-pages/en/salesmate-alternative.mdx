> [Salesmate](https://www.salesmate.io/) is a feature-rich CRM for small and medium-sized businesses with a strong focus on sales, built-in calling, and email sequences. It packs many capabilities into its platform, but distributes them across four pricing tiers with aggressive upselling. What starts at $23 per user quickly becomes $39 to $63 when you need the features that actually matter. On top of that, built-in calling incurs separate charges for phone numbers, SMS, and call credits. Customermates is an open-source Salesmate alternative built around two things Salesmate does not offer: AI agents that operate the CRM (Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini read and write your records through a native MCP integration with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools, so deal stages, follow-ups, and notes stay current on their own) and a native unified inbox that brings email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Telegram into one place from the Pro tier. Add transparent tiers from [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]]/user/month, every core CRM feature included, a self-hosting option, and no telephony fees, because Customermates has no calling or SMS.

## Why Switch from Salesmate?

Teams move away from Salesmate for several recurring reasons. The Basic plan ($23/user/month) is missing product management, email sequences, and advanced permission controls. The AI assistant Sandy is locked behind the Pro plan ($39/user/month). Custom modules and layouts require the Business plan ($63/user/month). And built-in calling, one of Salesmate's headline features, adds unpredictable costs through phone number rentals, SMS credits, and per-minute call charges.

Customermates takes a different approach. Every core CRM feature ships at every tier, starting at [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per user per month. Webhooks, the REST API and MCP are included from Starter, a separately operated [n8n](/features/workflow-automation) instance connects through the community node, and AI agents (Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini) read and write your CRM through a native MCP integration, so [lead tracking](/features/lead-tracking) and follow-ups stay current without manual data entry. You are never forced into a proprietary telephony system.

## Setup and Onboarding

Customermates' managed cloud is ready as soon as you sign up. Configure your [deal stages](/features/pipeline), import your [contacts](/features/contact-management) from an XLSX workbook, and begin working. The interface is clean and requires no training.

## Hidden Costs: Built-in Calling and SMS

Salesmate heavily markets its built-in calling feature, but the costs are not transparent. Phone numbers, SMS credits, and call minutes are billed separately, and the power dialer requires the Business plan. To make the most of Salesmate's telephony, you need a higher tier plus ongoing per-usage charges.

These costs depend on call volume and SMS usage and are hard to predict.

Customermates has no calling, SMS or call logging. If you need telephony, you run a separate provider such as Aircall or Twilio, and you can build your own connection that writes call notes into the CRM through the [API](/features/api) or a separately operated [n8n](/features/workflow-automation) instance.

## AI: Sandy the Assistant vs. Agents That Operate the CRM

Salesmate's AI assistant Sandy offers conversation summaries, email drafting, and predictive insights. Sandy is available from the Pro plan ($39/user/month); the Basic plan has no AI at all. Sandy is a chat assistant that sits beside the CRM.

Customermates takes a different approach: the AI operates the CRM itself. Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini connect through a native MCP integration ([[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools) and read and write your records directly, so they help with [lead management](/features/lead-tracking), note summarization, and follow-up drafts from the Starter tier; you pay your AI provider for the client. With a separately operated [n8n](/features/workflow-automation) instance reacting to webhooks, you can build AI-driven workflows that connect to external services.

## Who Salesmate is a good choice for

Salesmate is a hosted CRM aimed at startups and small to mid-sized businesses. Its strength is reaching customers on many channels from one screen. Full email sync with templates and tracking, web forms and a meeting scheduler come with Basic. Calling and texting are an add-on with local and toll-free numbers, and customers can chat with you on Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and your website.

Pro adds sequences, product and quote management, tickets, a team inbox, SSO and Sandy AI. Sandy is part of what Salesmate calls its AI Employee, which transcribes sales calls, summarizes conversations and drafts emails. Business adds custom modules, a power dialer and voicemail drop. Every plan includes apps for iPhone, iPad and Android and access to Salesmate's REST APIs.

## Why teams look for a Salesmate alternative

**The advertised price is the yearly rate.** The pricing page opens on yearly billing: Basic $23, Pro $39 and Business $63 per user per month. Switch to monthly billing and the same plans cost $29, $49 and $79.

**The features people ask about sit one or two plans up.** Sequences, Sandy AI and field-based permissions need Pro. The power dialer and custom modules need Business, and audit logs need Enterprise, whose onboarding packages start at $1,999.

**Usage is billed on top of seats.** Phone numbers start at $1.1 a month, and you top up credits for calls and texts. Smart Flow automations run on monthly credits per user, and Salesmate's AI agents (Skara) on AI agent credits, with more of both sold as add-ons. The seat price alone does not give you the monthly total.

**LinkedIn and Telegram are not on the channel list.** Salesmate's conversations page names Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, website chat, text messages and email. If your clients write to you in LinkedIn messages, that gap matters.

**Hosted only, and the region is not stated.** The pricing page lists no self-hosted edition and no open-source licence. The security page says Salesmate runs dedicated VPCs on Google Cloud infrastructure but names no region, so ask before you sign if location matters for your GDPR assessment.

## Where Customermates fits

Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM built in Germany for small client-facing teams: consultants, agencies, other service businesses and founders.

**Conversations next to the deal.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn (including Sales Navigator and Recruiter), WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one list, and you reply on the original channel. Link a participant to a contact once, and every conversation with that address or handle shows up on the contact's timeline.

**AI that keeps the records current.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes conversations, drafts replies and updates records, using hosted AI credits. It asks for a fresh approval before every delete, but sends a message without asking again when you tell it to send. [Routines](/docs/app-routines) run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes. Prefer Claude, ChatGPT or another client you already pay for? Connect it to the [MCP server](/docs/mcp), with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan and on self-hosted installs.

**Open source, a cloud configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, or your own server.** The core is AGPL-3.0 and runs with Docker Compose and PostgreSQL 16, including REST, webhooks and MCP. A [self-hosted install](/features/self-hosted) has no subscription, and also no inbox, no Mate and no hosted credits. In Customermates Cloud the database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, a [DPA](/dpa) is available, and the [subprocessors](/subprocessors), including US providers, are listed. The audit log records changes to records and workspace settings on every plan.

## Where Customermates is the wrong choice

- **No telephony and no SMS.** No dialer, no call recording, no transcription. A phone number goes into a Phone field.
- **No sequences and no campaigns.** You write or approve each message and send it on its own.
- **No help desk.** No tickets, no live chat widget, no chatbots and no surveys.
- **No web forms and no meeting scheduler.** Website leads arrive through the REST API or an [n8n instance you run yourself](/features/workflow-automation). Pro reads your calendar but does not book meetings.
- **No lead scoring, no enrichment and no AI forecasting.** The weighted pipeline multiplies each deal's value by its stage probability. That is arithmetic on your own numbers, not a prediction.
- **No custom modules, no field-level permissions and no SSO.** You get five record types you can rename and permissions per record type and action. Saved views are personal.
- **No native mobile apps, no free plan and no certification.** Customermates runs in the phone's browser. Salesmate's security page states ISO 27001:2013 and SOC 2; Customermates holds no certification.

## A worked setup: an agency moving from Salesmate Pro

Take a five-person brand and web agency. Two account managers hear from clients mostly on WhatsApp, the founder on LinkedIn, the two project leads by email. Nobody makes sales calls from the CRM.

| In Salesmate | In Customermates |
|---|---|
| Contacts | Contacts; email address, WhatsApp number and LinkedIn profile are stored as channels, a landline goes in a Phone field |
| Companies | Organizations, renamed "Companies" in the Data model under My Company › Settings |
| Deals in the pipeline | Deals with a Stage field and win probabilities |
| Products | Services, renamed "Products", linked to deals with a quantity |
| Activities | Tasks, plus a custom date field "Due", because tasks have no built-in due date |
| Sequences | No counterpart; a routine prepares drafts instead |
| Tickets and call logs | Stay in your help desk and phone system |

The agency renames the Stage options to Lead, Briefing, Proposal sent, Negotiation, Won and Lost, and sets win probabilities of 10%, 30%, 50%, 75%, 100% and 0% under My Company › Settings. Each account manager saves a board of their own deals grouped by Stage. The founder keeps a table of open deals grouped by assigned user.

Each person connects one channel, so Pro fits, at [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.5]] a month for the team. If the founder wants LinkedIn and email side by side, the workspace moves to Business at [[commercial.price.business.monthly.seats.5]].

Each account manager also sets up a routine for their own deals. A routine runs as its owner, so it sees that person's deals and conversations, and it uses hosted AI credits in Customermates Cloud. The trigger is **When something changes** with the event Deal Updated, the changed field Stage, and record filters for Stage is Won and deals assigned to the owner:

> A deal of mine just moved to Won. Create a task "Kickoff call" linked to the deal and its contacts and assign it to me. In the task notes, summarize my last three conversations with these contacts in five bullet points: scope, budget, deadlines, open questions and who decides. Do not send any message.

## Switching from Salesmate in five steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export from Salesmate before you cancel">In Salesmate you pick the columns you want, start the export and get a download link when it is ready. Salesmate's security page says data is deleted 30 days after a trial expires or a subscription is cancelled, so export first.</Step>
  <Step title="Save each record type as an Excel workbook">Customermates imports .xlsx workbooks only. Open each exported file in Excel or LibreOffice and save it as .xlsx, one record type per workbook.</Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the workspace">Rename record types in the Data model, create custom fields for the Salesmate fields you keep, such as a lead source, and set the Stage field with its probabilities.</Step>
  <Step title="Import one record type at a time">On each list, open Import and export, then Add from file: organizations first, then contacts, then deals. Link columns match records by name, so a contact's company column finds the organization imported before. Map email addresses and WhatsApp numbers to the contact's channels. Size limits are in the [records documentation](/docs/app-records).</Step>
  <Step title="Connect channels and AI">On Pro or during the trial, connect a mailbox, LinkedIn or WhatsApp under My Profile › Channels. Message history stored in Salesmate does not come across; the inbox shows conversations from the accounts you connect. Then set up routines, or connect your own AI client through MCP.</Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is Salesmate a CRM?">
Yes. Salesmate is a hosted CRM built around contacts, companies and deals. Around that core it sells marketing and support tools, such as email campaigns, live chat and help desk tickets, plus calling and texting as an add-on.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How much does Salesmate cost?">
Billed yearly, Basic costs $23, Pro $39 and Business $63 per user per month. Billed monthly, they cost $29, $49 and $79. Enterprise is priced on request, with onboarding packages from $1,999. Phone numbers start at $1.1 a month, plus calling and SMS credits. The trial runs 15 days.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Which Salesmate alternative is best?">
That depends on why you are leaving. For heavy calling with a power or predictive dialer, look at Close; it also prices calls at cost. For a free start with phone and email, Freshsales fits. If clients write to you on LinkedIn, WhatsApp and email and you want AI to keep the CRM current, Customermates fits. Customermates publishes this page, so weigh the recommendation accordingly.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a free alternative to Salesmate?">
Yes, with limits. Freshsales has a free plan for up to 3 users. Salesmate has no free plan, only a 15-day trial, and neither does Customermates Cloud. A self-hosted Customermates install has no subscription, so you pay only for your own server, but it comes without the inbox and without Mate.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I connect ChatGPT or Claude to Salesmate or Customermates?">
Salesmate announced an MCP server for ChatGPT, Claude and other MCP clients on 31 July 2026, in its community's planned release section; the pricing page does not mention it yet. Customermates has an MCP server with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan and on self-hosted installs, and you pay your AI provider for the client. In Customermates Cloud, Mate does the same work inside the app on hosted AI credits.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Where does Salesmate store data, and is it GDPR compliant?">
Salesmate's security page says data runs in dedicated VPCs on Google Cloud infrastructure and that Salesmate is ISO 27001:2013 and SOC 2 certified. It names no region. Whether your use is GDPR compliant is for you to assess as the controller. Customermates has its cloud database configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, offers a DPA and lists its subprocessors, including US providers. It holds no certification.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

> A Microsoft Dynamics alternative makes sense when a small team needs a CRM, not a business platform. Dynamics 365 Sales starts at $65 per user per month, paid yearly, and Copilot, custom apps and forecasting begin one plan up, at $105. Customermates bills monthly, keeps Outlook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram conversations next to your deals from Pro, and has an open-source core you can host yourself. If you also run Business Central or Finance, or quote and invoice from the CRM, stay with Dynamics or look at the suites further down. Customermates publishes this comparison; every Microsoft detail comes from microsoft.com or learn.microsoft.com and was checked on 8 October 2026.

## Which one fits your team

| Your situation | Better fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| You run Business Central or Finance and want CRM from the same vendor | Dynamics 365 | Microsoft sells its CRM and ERP apps as one family on Dataverse |
| You quote, take orders and invoice from the CRM | Dynamics 365 Sales | Quotes, orders and invoices are in every Sales plan; Customermates has none |
| You need a native mobile app, territories or call analysis | Dynamics 365 Sales | Customermates has no mobile app, no territories and no telephony |
| You must run the CRM on your own servers with vendor support | Dynamics 365 Customer Engagement (on-premises) | Microsoft supports version 9 until January 2029 |
| A small team works its clients by email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp | Customermates Pro or Business | All of those channels sit in one inbox next to contacts and deals |
| You want Claude or ChatGPT to work in the CRM | Customermates | The MCP server is on every plan and runs on your own AI provider |
| You want to pay month by month | Customermates | Every Dynamics 365 Sales plan is paid yearly |

## Who Dynamics 365 Sales is a good choice for

Dynamics 365 is a family of business apps. Microsoft lists Sales, Customer Insights, Customer Service and Contact Center as CRM, and Business Central, Finance, Supply Chain Management, Commerce, Project Operations and Human Resources as ERP. Sales and Customer Service keep their data in Microsoft Dataverse, so Power Apps and Power BI can work on the same tables. If your finance team already lives in Business Central, that alone is a strong reason to stay.

Sales Professional is broad for an entry plan: lead, opportunity, account and contact management, product and price lists, quotes, orders and invoices, a mobile app, Microsoft 365 interoperation, reports and dashboards. Enterprise adds custom apps, full customization, forecasting and territory management, a base capacity of conversation intelligence, and Copilot in Dynamics, Outlook and Teams. Premium adds Sales Insights, AI-powered recommended actions and data enrichment, plus 1,000 Copilot Credits per user per month. Microsoft Relationship Sales bundles Enterprise with LinkedIn Sales Navigator, from 10 seats.

Microsoft also commits to storing and processing Dynamics 365 customer data inside its EU Data Boundary, with documented exceptions, when the tenant and every environment sit in the "European Union and EFTA" region and the billing address is in an EU Data Boundary country. Both conditions are required.

## Why teams look for a Microsoft Dynamics alternative

**Every plan is paid yearly.** Professional costs $65, Enterprise $105 and Premium $150 per user per month, paid yearly. In Germany the same plans list at €56.30, €91.00 and €130.00 before VAT. Premium and Relationship Sales are sold through Microsoft's sales team.

**Copilot, custom apps and forecasting start one plan up.** Professional has limited customization, no custom apps and no Copilot. Moving to Enterprise for those costs $40 more per user per month. Automation runs on Power Automate and custom apps on Power Apps, and Microsoft's footnotes say both need additional licences. The Copilot features in Teams do too.

**AI is metered in Copilot Credits.** The Qualification, Opportunity, Research and Close agents are available on every plan, but they run on Copilot Credits, which only Premium includes. The Dynamics 365 Sales MCP Server needs admin permissions in Dynamics and in Copilot Studio, consumes Copilot Studio credits per tool, and, in Microsoft's words, "Claude Desktop isn't supported at this time."

**The trial expects a Microsoft tenant.** The 30-day trial signs up with a Microsoft work account and asks for credit card details. Without a work account, Microsoft sends you to a Microsoft 365 trial first.

**On-premises has an end date.** Dynamics 365 Customer Engagement (on-premises) version 9 has mainstream support until 12 January 2027 and extended support until 9 January 2029, and Microsoft encourages those customers to consider its cloud. If you still run Dynamics CRM on your own servers, the choice is the Microsoft cloud or another CRM.

## Where Customermates fits

Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM for small client-facing teams: consultants, agencies, other service businesses and founders. It is built in Germany.

**Outlook stays, and the CRM gets the conversations.** From Pro, you connect an Outlook mailbox, or Gmail, IMAP, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram or Telegram, and the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) shows every conversation in one list. You reply on the original channel. Link a participant to a contact once and the conversation shows on that contact. Calendar events from the connected account appear read-only. Teams is not an inbox channel.

**AI that does the CRM work.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes threads, drafts replies and updates records. It uses hosted AI credits: every plan includes a monthly allowance per active user, listed on the [pricing page](/pricing). Mate asks for a fresh approval before it deletes anything, but when you ask it to send a message, it sends, so tell it to save drafts if you want to read them first. [Routines](/docs/app-routines) run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes, as their owner: [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] per user on Starter, [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]] on Pro, [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedRoutinesPerUser]] on Business.

Prefer Claude, ChatGPT or another client you already pay for? Connect it to the [MCP server](/docs/mcp) with an API key or OAuth. It offers [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan and on self-hosted installs, and its model calls run on your own AI provider.

**Open source, with a hosting choice.** The AGPL-3.0 core runs on your own server with Docker Compose and PostgreSQL 16, including REST, webhooks and MCP. A [self-hosted install](/features/self-hosted) has no inbox, no Mate and no hosted credits. In Customermates Cloud the database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, a [DPA](/dpa) is available, and the [subprocessors](/subprocessors), including US providers, are listed. Customermates holds no compliance certification.

## Where Customermates is the wrong choice

- **One vendor for CRM and ERP.** Customermates has no accounting, quotes or invoices. If Dynamics also runs your finance, read [how CRM and ERP fit together](/blog/crm-and-erp) before you split them.
- **Custom record types.** There are five: contacts, organizations, deals, services and tasks. You can rename them from a list of preset names, such as Accounts or Opportunities, and add custom fields, but not a sixth type.
- **Several currencies.** A workspace has one currency for deal values and pipeline totals.
- **Fine-grained security.** Roles set permissions per record type and action. There is no field-level security and no SSO.
- **Contact Center or Customer Service.** Microsoft sells both as separate Dynamics 365 apps. Customermates has no telephony, cases or tickets.
- **Bundled sales tooling.** No mobile app, call recording, data enrichment, lead scoring, email sequences or bulk email. The weighted pipeline is arithmetic on stage probabilities, not a forecast.
- **A free plan.** There is a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial, then a paid plan. Saved views are personal and cannot be shared.

## A worked setup: a consulting firm leaving Sales Professional

Take a five-person consulting firm on Sales Professional. Three partners work clients by Outlook; two associates also talk to clients on LinkedIn.

| In Dynamics 365 Sales | In Customermates |
|---|---|
| Accounts | Organizations, renamed "Accounts" under Data model |
| Contacts | Contacts |
| Leads | Contacts with a Single Select field "Lead status": New, Contacted, Qualified, Disqualified |
| Opportunities | Deals, renamed "Opportunities" |
| Products and price lists | Services, renamed "Products", each with a name and a price |
| Tasks | Tasks with assignees; add a date field "Due date", because tasks have none built in |
| Email in Outlook | The connected Outlook mailbox in the unified inbox |

A new workspace starts with the stages Prospecting 10%, Qualification 20%, Demo 40%, Proposal 60%, Negotiation 80%, Won 100% and Lost 0%. Rename them to match your Dynamics process. Customermates multiplies each opportunity's value by its stage probability and shows a weighted pipeline total.

With one account each, the team runs on Pro at [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.5]] a month. If an associate needs Outlook and LinkedIn side by side, the workspace moves to Business at [[commercial.price.business.monthly.seats.5]]. Each person saves a board of open opportunities grouped by stage; the managing partner keeps a table grouped by assigned user.

Each partner owns one routine in Customermates Cloud. It runs as its owner, sees that person's opportunities, uses their channels and spends hosted AI credits:

> Every Monday at 8:00, find my opportunities in Proposal where the contact has not written in ten days. Save a short follow-up draft in my latest conversation with each contact. Do not send anything. List the opportunities in the run summary.

## Switching from Dynamics 365 in five steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export your views to Excel">In Dynamics, open a table such as Accounts, pick a view with every column you want to keep, and choose Export to Excel on the command bar. You get an .xlsx workbook. If an export times out, Microsoft suggests smaller batches with tighter filters or fewer columns.</Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the workspace">Under My Company, Settings, Data model, rename the record types (Accounts, Opportunities, Products), set the stages and their probabilities, and create custom fields for the columns you keep.</Step>
  <Step title="Import one record type at a time">Open Import and export on each list, then Add from file: accounts first, then contacts, then opportunities, because link columns find records by name. New contacts need a first and a last name. Set any Dynamics ID column to Do not add. The [records guide](/docs/app-records) lists the file limits.</Step>
  <Step title="Connect Outlook">On Pro or during the trial, connect your mailbox under My Profile, Channels, and link participants to contacts as conversations come in.</Step>
  <Step title="Add views and AI">Save your boards and tables, then set up a routine for Mate or connect your own AI client through MCP. Activity history does not come across, so keep Dynamics read-only until the licence term ends.</Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is Microsoft Dynamics a CRM or an ERP?">
Both, as separate apps. Microsoft lists Sales, Customer Insights, Customer Service and Contact Center as CRM, and Business Central, Finance, Supply Chain Management, Commerce, Project Operations and Human Resources as ERP. Customermates replaces only the CRM side.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is Microsoft Dynamics ending?">
No. Dynamics 365 is Microsoft's current line of cloud apps, and apps such as Sales and Customer Service are where Dynamics CRM continues in the cloud. The on-premises CRM has an end date: Dynamics 365 Customer Engagement (on-premises) version 9 has mainstream support until 12 January 2027 and extended support until 9 January 2029.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is Dynamics 365 the same as Dataverse?">
No. Dataverse is the data platform underneath. Dynamics 365 Sales and Customer Service store their data there, which is why Power Apps and Power Automate can work on the same tables.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a free alternative to Microsoft Dynamics 365?">
Salesforce (Free Suite), HubSpot, Zoho CRM and Odoo each have a free plan with limits on users, features or apps. Customermates has no free plan; it has a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial, and its open-source core costs no licence fee if you host it yourself, without the inbox and Mate.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Customermates work with Outlook and Microsoft 365?">
Yes, from Pro. You connect an Outlook mailbox, its conversations appear in the unified inbox next to your contacts, and calendar events show read-only. Customermates does not sync Outlook contacts, does not write calendar events and does not connect to Teams. The [Outlook integration page](/features/outlook-integration) has the details.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can CRM data stay in the EU with either product?">
Yes, in different ways. Microsoft's EU Data Boundary covers Dynamics 365 when the tenant, every environment and the billing address are in the EU or EFTA, with documented exceptions. Customermates Cloud has its database configured in the AWS Frankfurt region and lists its subprocessors, including US providers; self-hosting puts the data on your own server.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

> Pick Zoho CRM if cost and commitment decide: it is free for up to three users, the paid editions cost $14 to $52 per user per month billed yearly, and you can pay any of them month to month without a contract. Pick Salesforce if you will use what the higher price buys. Pro Suite ($100) and Core ($195), both per user per month and billed annually, add a configurable app platform and AppExchange, and Core adds Slack Business+ and Tableau Next. For five people that is $70 a month on Zoho CRM Standard, $125 on Salesforce Starter Suite and $500 on Pro Suite. Customermates publishes this comparison and makes a third option, described in its own section below. Every Zoho and Salesforce detail comes from the vendors' own pages, checked on 8 October 2026.

## Which one fits your team

| Your situation | Better fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Three people or fewer, no CRM budget yet | Zoho CRM Free | Free for up to 3 users, with workflows, the mobile app and 5,000 API calls a day. Salesforce Free Suite comes with 2 user licences |
| A small sales team that phones and emails from the CRM | Zoho CRM Standard | Built-in calling, cadences, several pipelines and sales forecasting from $14 per user billed yearly. Salesforce forecasting starts at Pro Suite, $100 per user |
| You want to pay month by month | Zoho CRM, or Salesforce Starter Suite | Zoho bills every paid edition monthly or yearly with no contract. Starter Suite is the only paid Salesforce edition billed monthly |
| The company already works in Slack and wants Tableau dashboards | Salesforce Core | Slack Business+, Tableau Next and the Premier Success plan are part of the $195 edition |
| You need approval processes and sales territories | Zoho CRM Enterprise on a budget, Salesforce Core for the wider platform | Zoho includes both from Enterprise at $40 per user billed yearly. Salesforce includes them from Core at $195, together with AppExchange access, Slack Business+ and Tableau Next |
| You connect other tools through the API on a mid-range budget | Zoho CRM | Every Zoho edition includes API access. Salesforce Pro Suite charges $25 per user per month extra for it |
| Your contract must keep data and support inside the EU | Salesforce with the Hyperforce EU Operating Zone | Processing and storage stay in the EU and Switzerland for a vetted set of products, with EU-based support. Zoho's EU region also stores data in Amsterdam and Dublin, so ask both vendors where support staff work |

If HubSpot or Pipedrive is also on your shortlist, [HubSpot vs Salesforce](/compare/hubspot-vs-salesforce) and [Pipedrive vs Zoho](/compare/pipedrive-vs-zoho) use the same method.

### Cost for five people, per month

- **Zoho CRM Standard:** 5 × $14 = $70 billed yearly, or $100 billed monthly.
- **Zoho CRM Professional:** 5 × $23 = $115, or $175 billed monthly.
- **Salesforce Starter Suite:** 5 × $25 = $125, monthly or yearly.
- **Salesforce Pro Suite:** 5 × $100 = $500, or $625 once you add API access at $25 per user.
- **Salesforce Core:** 5 × $195 = $975, which is $11,700 a year. Salesforce says most of its products use annual contracts.

The free editions stop at different points. Zoho's covers three users, so a team pays from its fourth person. Salesforce Free Suite comes with two licences.

Most of the price gap pays for the platform. From Pro Suite, Salesforce adds Lightning App Builder and AppExchange access; its own comparison page cites more than 9,000 pre-built apps and integrations. Core bundles Slack Business+, Tableau Next and the Premier Success plan, which costs 30% of net licence fees when bought on Pro Suite. Zoho puts more day-to-day sales tooling into its cheaper editions: calling, cadences and custom modules from Standard, CPQ and inventory from Professional, approvals and territory management from Enterprise. Mass email and Gmail or Outlook integration are in every Salesforce suite, the free one included. Salesforce's own comparison page describes Zoho as ideal for "startups or very small businesses (under 10 employees) with a limited budget".

## Data location and GDPR

Zoho runs data centers in several regions. The EU region's primary site is in Amsterdam and its secondary site in Dublin. Your account lands in the data center that matches the country chosen at sign-up, which Zoho pre-fills from your IP address, so pick an EU country there if you want the EU region.

Salesforce offers the Hyperforce EU Operating Zone. For a vetted set of products across Sales Cloud, Service Cloud and the platform, it processes and stores customer data only in the EU and Switzerland, and support comes from EU-based staff. Ask which of your editions and add-ons are covered before you sign.

The hosting region does not settle GDPR on its own. Read each vendor's data processing agreement and subprocessor list for the products you buy.

It covers less ground than either Zoho CRM or Salesforce and differs from both in four ways:

- **AI that keeps the CRM current.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes conversations, drafts replies and updates records. It uses hosted AI credits, a monthly allowance per active user listed on the [pricing page](/pricing). [Routines](/docs/app-routines) run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes. If you would rather use Claude, ChatGPT or another client, connect it to the [MCP server](/docs/mcp), which has [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan.
- **Open source.** The AGPL-3.0 core runs on your own server with Docker Compose. A [self-hosted install](/features/self-hosted) has REST, webhooks and MCP, but no inbox and no Mate. In Customermates Cloud the database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region; the app host and other subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [DPA](/dpa) is available. Customermates holds no compliance certification.
- **Monthly prices per user.** Five people pay [[commercial.price.starter.monthly.seats.5]] a month on Starter or [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.5]] on Pro, with no yearly contract.

On price alone, Zoho CRM Standard costs five people less than Customermates Pro and includes calling and bulk email. Pro makes sense when your clients write on LinkedIn and WhatsApp as often as by email, and you want the CRM kept up to date from those conversations.

### Example: a five-person consultancy

The team has outgrown Zoho CRM Free, which stops at three users. In Customermates it renames record types from the preset names: deals become "Projects", services become "Packages", contacts become "Clients". Each package is linked to a project. The stage field gets a probability per option: Inquiry 10%, Scoping call 25%, Proposal sent 50%, Verbal yes 80%, Won 100%, Lost 0%. Customermates multiplies each project's value by its stage probability and shows a weighted pipeline total. That is arithmetic on your own numbers, not a prediction. A single-select field "Practice area" groups the work, and each person saves a board of open projects grouped by stage. Saved views are personal, so everyone keeps their own.

Each partner owns a routine. A routine runs in Customermates Cloud as its owner, with that owner's records and channels, and uses hosted AI credits. Mate sends messages without a fresh approval when an instruction asks it to, so this one says to draft only:

> Every Monday at 8:00, find my projects in the stage "Proposal sent" whose client has not written for seven days. Save a short follow-up draft in my latest conversation with that client. Send nothing. List the projects in the run summary.

### What Customermates does not do

Customermates has no telephony, no bulk email or cadences, no custom modules, no approval processes, no CPQ or inventory, no lead scoring, no forecasting beyond weighted stages and no native mobile apps. Tasks carry assignees, links, notes and custom fields, but no due dates. Customermates Cloud has no free plan, only a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial. If your team needs those features, Zoho CRM or Salesforce fits better.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is Zoho CRM better than Salesforce?">
For a small team with a limited budget, Zoho CRM usually gives more per dollar: calling, cadences and sales forecasting come with Standard at $14 per user billed yearly, and the free edition covers three users. Salesforce is the better choice when you will use AppExchange apps, Slack Business+ and Tableau Next, and have someone to administer the platform. That bundle starts at Core, $195 per user per month billed annually. Salesforce's own comparison page calls Zoho ideal for businesses under 10 employees with a limited budget.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What are the pricing differences between Salesforce and Zoho?">
Zoho CRM costs $14, $23, $40 or $52 per user per month billed yearly, and $20 to $65 billed monthly. Salesforce charges $25 for Starter Suite, $100 for Pro Suite and $195, $395 or $550 for Core, Advanced and Max, with everything above Starter Suite billed annually. Salesforce also sells add-ons on top, such as API access on Pro Suite at $25 per user or Agentforce for Sales from $125 per user.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Why do teams leave Salesforce?">
No general reason can be verified, so this answer sticks to what you can check: the price structure. Every edition above Starter Suite is billed annually, API access costs $25 per user extra on Pro Suite, and Agentforce for Sales is an add-on from $125 per user per month unless you buy Max. A team that mainly tracks a pipeline pays for platform features it does not use. If that describes your team, compare Zoho CRM or a smaller CRM before you renew.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Zoho One or Salesforce for a professional services firm?">
Zoho One bundles more than 40 Zoho apps, CRM included, on one licence. It costs $37 per employee per month billed yearly, or $45 billed monthly, if you license every paid employee; a plan for a flexible number of users costs more per user. For ten people that is $370 a month on the all-employee plan, against $1,950 for ten Salesforce Core licences, before any other Salesforce cloud. If the firm only needs the client and pipeline side, Zoho CRM Standard alone costs $140 a month for ten people billed yearly. What a services firm needs from a CRM is covered in the guide for [professional services](/for/professional-services).
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How hard is it to move from Zoho CRM to Salesforce, or the other way round?">
Plan it as a data project. Map Zoho modules to Salesforce objects, or the reverse, and recreate stages and custom fields before you import. Moving to Zoho, its Ultimate edition includes migration assistance. Both free tiers also let you test the target system with real records before you commit.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

> The right SuiteCRM alternative depends on what you want to stop doing. To keep an open-source CRM in PHP with a smaller footprint and a per-user cloud price, look at EspoCRM. For custom objects in a modern hosted product with a free self-hosted core, look at Twenty. For CRM, accounting and inventory in one system, look at Odoo. Customermates fits small client-facing teams whose clients write on WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram or Telegram as well as by email, and who want an AI assistant or their own AI client to keep the CRM current. If you depend on quotes, invoices, cases or campaigns inside the CRM, SuiteCRM remains the better tool. Customermates publishes this comparison; every SuiteCRM and third-party detail comes from the vendors' own sites, checked on 27 October 2026.

## What SuiteCRM is and who it suits

SuiteCRM is an open-source CRM written in PHP and published under AGPL-3.0 by SuiteCRM Ltd. It started as a fork of the last open-source edition of SugarCRM and now ships two lines: SuiteCRM 7.15 and SuiteCRM 8.10, whose interface is rebuilt in Angular. Both are free to download and run on your own server.

The scope is wide. SuiteCRM's user guide covers leads, accounts, contacts and opportunities; quotes, invoices, contracts and PDF templates; campaigns, target lists, surveys and events; cases, a knowledge base and bug tracking; reports, scheduled reports and workflows; projects; calendar sync and maps. Administrators build custom fields and modules in Studio and Module Builder, set roles and security groups, and can connect single sign-on through Azure SAML. Developers get the API v8.

If you run sales, marketing and service in one system and have someone who maintains it, SuiteCRM covers more ground than Customermates does.

## Why teams look for a SuiteCRM alternative

**You pay for the work around the software.** SuiteCRM charges no licence or per-user fee. Its pricing page instead lists the services you may need: fully managed hosting from £50 a month, Standard Support at £1,200 for 10 hours (required on managed hosting, 20% off when bought with it), SuiteASSURED from £3,350 a year, and a Quick Start implementation from £2,520. Self-hosted, the software is free and your own team runs updates and security.

**Running PHP yourself.** SuiteCRM 8.10 needs Linux, Unix or macOS with PHP 8.2 to 8.4, Apache 2.4, and MySQL 8.0 or 8.4 or MariaDB 10.6 to 11.8, according to the compatibility matrix. Each minor release moves those versions, so upgrades include server work. Customermates runs as two containers: the app and PostgreSQL 16.

**Breadth you do not use.** A small consultancy or agency often needs contacts, deals and conversations, not cases, surveys, contracts and campaigns. Every extra module is something to configure, hide or train people around.

**Conversations outside email.** SuiteCRM handles inbound and outbound email accounts, including Microsoft and Google OAuth. Its user guide lists no WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram or Telegram integration, so those threads stay outside the CRM unless you add a third-party add-on.

## Where Customermates fits

Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM for small client-facing teams: consultants, agencies, other service businesses and founders. It is built in Germany.

**Client conversations next to the deal.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one list, and you reply on the original channel. Link a participant to a contact once, and the conversation is filed with that contact. Starter has no inbox.

**AI that does the CRM work.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes threads, drafts replies, updates records and builds views. It uses hosted AI credits, a monthly allowance per active user shown on the [pricing page](/pricing). Mate asks for a fresh approval before every deletion. Sending a message runs without one, so tell it to save drafts when you want to review first. Routines run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes, always as their owner.

**Your own AI client over MCP.** The [MCP server](/docs/mcp) is included on every plan and on self-hosted installs, with a catalogue of [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools. Claude, ChatGPT and other clients connect with OAuth or an API key and act with the connected user's permissions. Model calls from your client run on your own AI provider.

**Self-hosting without PHP.** The [self-hosted edition](/features/self-hosted) is the AGPL core with REST, webhooks and MCP, deployed with Docker Compose. It has no inbox, no Mate and no hosted credits.

**Where the cloud runs.** In Customermates Cloud, the primary database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, a [DPA](/dpa) is available, and the subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the legal pages. Customermates holds no compliance certification.

## Where Customermates is the wrong choice

- **No quotes, invoices, contracts or inventory.**
- **No cases, ticket queues or knowledge base for customers.**
- **No campaigns, target lists, mass email or sequences.** Messages go one at a time, written by you or drafted by Mate.
- **No single sign-on, no native mobile apps, no telephony.** Saved views are personal, and permissions apply per list and action, not per field.
- **Tasks have no built-in due date.** Add a custom date field if you need one.

## Moving from SuiteCRM

Most SuiteCRM sales data has a direct home. In Configure, you can name your lists "Accounts" and "Opportunities", so your team keeps its vocabulary.

| In SuiteCRM | In Customermates |
|---|---|
| Accounts | An organizations list named "Accounts" |
| Contacts | Contacts |
| Leads | Contacts with a single choice field such as "Lifecycle" (Lead, Customer) |
| Opportunities | A deals list named "Opportunities" |
| Products on quotes | A services list, added to deals as line items with quantities |
| Custom modules | Your own lists, with fields and relationships |
| Quotes, invoices, cases, campaigns | No equivalent; keep a dedicated tool |

Add a Stage single choice field to your opportunities with one option per SuiteCRM sales stage, and copy each stage's probability into the option's **Probability %**. The weighted value then multiplies each deal's value by its stage probability. It is arithmetic on your own numbers and does not predict anything.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export from SuiteCRM">Export each list you want to keep from its list view: accounts, contacts, leads and opportunities. Open the CSV files in Excel and save them as .xlsx.</Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the workspace">Create or rename lists in Configure, add custom fields for the attributes you keep, and set the stage probabilities. In Customermates Cloud, AI setup can build the lists for you.</Step>
  <Step title="Import one list at a time">Import organizations first, then contacts, then deals. Link columns match records by name, so a contact's account column finds the organization you imported before. The [records documentation](/docs/app-records) lists the limits.</Step>
  <Step title="Rebuild list filters as views">Turn each saved SuiteCRM filter into a saved view. Views are personal, so each person saves their own.</Step>
  <Step title="Connect channels and AI">On Pro or during the trial, connect a mailbox, LinkedIn or WhatsApp, then set up routines for Mate or connect your own AI client over MCP.</Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is SuiteCRM free?">
The software is. SuiteCRM is open source under AGPL-3.0 and free to download and host on your own server, with no per-user fees. SuiteCRM Ltd sells managed hosting from £50 a month, support hours, the SuiteASSURED package and implementation, so your real cost is hosting plus whatever help you buy.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is SuiteCRM 7 or SuiteCRM 8 the current version?">
Both are maintained. On the download page, SuiteCRM 7.15 is the latest release of the established line and SuiteCRM 8.10 the current release of the rebuilt line. SuiteCRM 8.10 supports PHP 8.2 to 8.4 with MySQL or MariaDB.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does SuiteCRM have an AI assistant or an MCP server?">
SuiteCRM's site and documentation describe an API (v8) but no built-in AI assistant and no MCP server. Customermates has its MCP server built in, with a catalogue of [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan and on self-hosted installs, and Mate as a hosted assistant in Customermates Cloud.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What is the best open-source alternative to SuiteCRM?">
It depends on the gap. EspoCRM is closest in scope with a lighter setup, Twenty offers custom objects in a modern stack, and Odoo adds accounting and inventory. Customermates fits small teams that want WhatsApp, LinkedIn and email conversations next to their deals and an AI client that can update the CRM.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I move my SuiteCRM data to Customermates?">
Yes, list by list. Export accounts, contacts, leads and opportunities from SuiteCRM, save them as Excel workbooks and import organizations first, then contacts, then deals. Quotes, invoices, cases and campaigns have no equivalent in Customermates.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

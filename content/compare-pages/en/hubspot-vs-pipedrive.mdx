> HubSpot and Pipedrive are two of the most popular CRM platforms for small and mid-sized teams, but they serve fundamentally different needs. HubSpot is the all-in-one go-to-market platform (marketing automation, CRM, and customer service under one roof) that starts free but becomes expensive fast. Pipedrive is the focused sales CRM built for deal velocity, with a kanban pipeline that is quick to set up but gates features behind plan tiers. Customermates offers a third option built around two features: AI clients (Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini) operate the CRM directly through MCP ([[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools) so it stays current, and on Pro and above a unified inbox brings email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Telegram into one place, operable by the same AI. Every core CRM feature ships from [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]]/user/month, and the open-source core can also run on your own server.

## What is HubSpot's biggest competitor?

In the SMB and mid-market CRM space, Pipedrive is one of HubSpot's biggest direct competitors. Both platforms target the same audience: growing sales teams that need effective deal management without enterprise complexity. On G2, HubSpot holds a 4.4/5 rating across 8,500+ reviews while Pipedrive scores 4.2/5 across 1,500+ reviews. HubSpot rates higher on requirements coverage and support quality; Pipedrive leads on setup ease and pure CRM usability.

But increasingly, the real competition comes from modern alternatives like Customermates that combine the simplicity of Pipedrive with a full core CRM, without HubSpot's price escalation or Pipedrive's feature gating.

Here is who should choose what:

- **Choose HubSpot** if your priority is inbound marketing with CRM (email campaigns, landing pages, content management, and lead nurturing) and you have the budget for the Professional tier (€90 per seat per month billed annually, plus €1,470 onboarding) where the real value unlocks.
- **Choose Pipedrive** if you are a sales-focused team that needs fast setup, strong visual pipeline management, and straightforward deal tracking without marketing complexity.
- **Choose Customermates** if you want the simplicity of Pipedrive combined with a full core CRM: contacts, deals, AI that operates the CRM, a unified inbox on Pro, and integration surfaces for your own automation, from [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]]/user/month with no core CRM feature gated, a cloud database in Frankfurt, and the option to self-host.

## Setup and onboarding

HubSpot has a polished onboarding experience, but the platform's sprawling feature set creates complexity. Understanding the relationship between contacts, companies, deals, and tickets (plus the marketing hub's lifecycle stages, lead scoring, and property mapping) takes real investment. Most teams need days to weeks for a proper setup, and many eventually hire certified partners. Professional and Enterprise also require a one-time onboarding fee.

Pipedrive is one of the fastest CRMs to set up. Its pipeline-centric design is intuitive for sales teams, and basic configuration is quick. According to G2, Pipedrive consistently scores higher than HubSpot on ease of setup and use. The knowledge base is well organized and the developer ecosystem is active. Live chat is available on every plan; phone support starts on Premium. Customizing beyond the default pipeline view and setting up advanced workflows requires higher-tier plans.

Customermates' managed cloud is ready as soon as you sign up. Configure your deal stages, import records from XLSX workbooks one record type at a time, and start tracking deals. The interface is purpose-built for CRM without the overhead of a marketing suite.

## Pricing breakdown

Prices from the vendors' pricing pages ([HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/sales), [Pipedrive](https://www.pipedrive.com/en/pricing)), checked September 2026; EUR per seat per month, annual billing unless stated.

**HubSpot Sales Hub pricing (per-seat model):**

- Free: up to 2 users, basic contact and deal management
- Starter (€20): core sales tools and HubSpot credits
- Professional (€90, or €100 billed monthly): custom reporting, sequences, workflow automation, AI features, plus a required one-time onboarding fee of €1,470
- Enterprise (from €150): advanced permissions, custom objects, and more, plus a required one-time onboarding fee of €3,420

The jump from Starter to Professional is where most teams feel the cost. Features like workflow automation, custom reporting, and sequences, which many consider standard CRM functionality, require the Professional plan. HubSpot also offers payments, sequences, lead rotation, and quote-based workflows at higher tiers.

**Pipedrive pricing (plans renamed to Lite, Growth, Premium, and Ultimate in 2025):**

- Lite (€14, or €24 billed monthly): pipeline and lead management, 2,500 open deals per seat, 30 custom fields
- Growth (€39, or €49 monthly): two-way email sync, automations, sequences, forecast reports
- Premium (€59, or €79 monthly): LeadBooster, Smart Docs and Projects included, custom scoring, AI email tools, phone support
- Ultimate (€79, or €99 monthly): highest limits, enhanced security, data enrichment, extended phone support

Pipedrive is more predictable than HubSpot on pricing, but key features are distributed across tiers. There are no automations on the Lite tier. Workflow automation requires Growth (€39), AI email tools require Premium (€59), and add-ons like LeadBooster (below Premium), Campaigns, and Web Visitors add up. Pipedrive has no built-in marketing suite comparable to HubSpot's.

- No core CRM feature is ever gated behind a higher tier; messaging capacity and the monthly hosted AI allowance scale

## What CRM is better than HubSpot?

That depends on what "better" means for your team. Pipedrive is better for pure sales pipeline management: its kanban board with drag-and-drop, rotting-deal highlights, and activity-based selling approach is hard to beat. HubSpot is better for marketing-to-sales handoff with forms, lead capture, chatbots, and content tools.

But both force trade-offs. Pipedrive excels at one thing and lacks depth elsewhere. HubSpot offers breadth but locks most of it behind expensive tiers.

Customermates delivers contacts, organizations, deals, a configurable pipeline, tasks, services, MCP access for AI clients, and integration surfaces for separately operated n8n on Starter, with the unified inbox for email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Telegram starting on Pro. It combines Pipedrive's focus on usability with a core CRM that is not split across tiers.

## Feature comparison

HubSpot excels where marketing meets sales. Its email marketing tools, landing pages, forms, and content management are tightly integrated with CRM functionality. Higher tiers add lead scoring, conversation intelligence, payment processing, and e-signatures. For teams that need marketing automation alongside deal tracking, it is a strong choice on higher-tier plans. However, the CRM-specific features on Starter and Free tiers are limited compared to dedicated CRM platforms.

Pipedrive focuses on the sales pipeline. Visual deal management with drag-and-drop kanban boards, activity scheduling, contact timelines, and rotting-deal highlights are its core strengths. Pipedrive offers 30 custom fields on Lite and up to 500 on Ultimate, plus native mobile apps. Data enrichment arrives only on the upper plans, and Pipedrive has no marketing suite beyond the Campaigns add-on.

Customermates delivers the daily core of a CRM in every plan: linked records, typed custom columns, table and board layouts, dashboards, permissions, and an audit log.

## AI capabilities

**HubSpot Breeze AI** offers content generation, email writing, and agents funded by HubSpot credits. The monthly credit allowance grows with the plan (500 on Starter, 3,000 on Professional, 5,000 on Enterprise), so the most useful features sit on Professional and Enterprise.

**Pipedrive AI** includes AI-assisted reporting and a Pipedrive MCP server on every plan, while AI email writing and summaries start on Premium (€59 per seat per month billed annually).

**Customermates** gives every plan MCP access for external AI clients ([[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools), starting at [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]]/user/month; you bring and pay for the AI client. In the managed cloud, the hosted assistant Mate works with a monthly credit allowance that scales with the plan. Either way, AI can summarize notes, update deal stages, and draft follow-ups, and it asks for confirmation before destructive actions or outbound sends in Mate.

## What are the disadvantages of HubSpot?

The most frequently cited disadvantages include:

1. **Steep price jumps**: Starter at €20 per seat per month to Professional at €90 (billed annually) is a significant jump, and most valuable features require Professional.
2. **Marketing overhead**: sales-only teams pay for marketing functionality they never use.
3. **Limited pure CRM depth on lower tiers**: Pipedrive's Lite plan offers more sales-specific features than HubSpot's Free or Starter.
4. **Credit-based AI**: AI agents and advanced features draw on monthly HubSpot credits that depend on the plan.
5. **Onboarding fees**: Professional requires a one-time onboarding fee of €1,470 and Enterprise €3,420 on top of per-seat costs.

Customermates avoids these: published tiers from [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]]/user/month, CRM-focused design, every core CRM feature from day one, and no onboarding fees or minimum user requirements.

## Data protection and hosting

HubSpot and Pipedrive both offer data processing agreements and run on their own hosting regions and subprocessors. Check which region your account uses and review each vendor's DPA and subprocessor list against your requirements.

In the managed cloud, the Customermates application database runs in the AWS Frankfurt region; hosting and other subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [DPA](/dpa) is available. Customermates holds no compliance certification. If you need full control over where data lives, [self-host](/features/self-hosted) the open-source core.

## Integrations and automation

HubSpot has one of the largest integration marketplaces in the CRM space with over 1,500 apps. Built-in automation through workflows is powerful but tier-gated; the Free and Starter plans offer minimal automation capabilities.

Pipedrive offers 500+ marketplace integrations and a REST API with webhooks on every plan. Automations start on Growth, and the number of active automations is capped per company (50 on Growth, 150 on Premium, 250 on Ultimate). Pipedrive has an active developer community, and Zapier compatibility fills integration gaps.

Customermates has no built-in rules engine. It offers a REST API with an OpenAPI description, webhooks for CRM events, MCP for AI clients, and a community node for your own separately operated n8n instance. In the managed cloud, routines run a saved instruction for the hosted assistant on a schedule or when a CRM event happens. Being open-source, you can extend the platform without marketplace dependency.

## Scalability

Pipedrive is cost-effective for small teams but less attractive as a business grows; its Ultimate tier mainly adds higher limits, security, and support. HubSpot scales horizontally (additional Hubs for Marketing, Service, Operations, Content) and vertically (tier upgrades add significant functionality), but this pre-integrated ecosystem comes with escalating costs.

Customermates scales differently. Add users at the same tier price with no core CRM feature restrictions; messaging capacity and the monthly hosted AI allowance change if you move to Pro or Business. For teams that want to run the CRM themselves, self-hosting the open-source core gives full control over the infrastructure, without the unified inbox or hosted assistant, which are cloud-only.

## Conclusion

HubSpot and Pipedrive both force trade-offs. HubSpot offers breadth at the cost of complexity and escalating prices. Pipedrive offers simplicity at the cost of depth and feature gating. Customermates provides a modern alternative: complete core CRM functionality from [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]]/user/month, open-source, with a cloud database in Frankfurt or on your own server. For teams that want an effective CRM without marketing bloat or pipeline-only limitations, it is a strong choice.

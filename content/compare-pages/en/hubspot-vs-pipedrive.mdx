> Pipedrive sells four sales-only plans from €14 to €99 per seat a month, and its sequences and automations start on Growth at €39 billed yearly. HubSpot is free for up to two users, but sequences, revenue forecasting, custom reports and single sign-on start on Professional at €90 to €100 per seat, plus a required onboarding purchase. A team that only sells usually gets those tools for less with Pipedrive. A team that wants email campaigns, website chat and sales on the same contact records gets more from HubSpot. Customermates publishes this comparison and makes a CRM of its own, which appears only in the labelled section near the end. Every HubSpot and Pipedrive detail comes from the vendors' own websites, checked on 8 October 2026.

## Which one fits your team

| Your situation | Better fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| One or two people, no CRM budget yet | HubSpot Free | Free for up to 2 users, with contacts, companies, deals, a Gmail, Outlook or Exchange inbox and the mobile app |
| A sales team that wants sequences and automation at a modest price | Pipedrive Growth | Sequences and up to 50 automations from €39 per seat billed yearly, €49 monthly |
| Marketing email and website chat next to sales, in one account | HubSpot Free or Starter, later Professional | HubSpot's plan table lists email campaigns and website chat on every plan, Free included; Pipedrive sells email marketing as an add-on |
| More than 10 people on an entry plan | Pipedrive | HubSpot Starter takes up to 10 paid users, so a larger team lands on Professional |
| Single sign-on on a small budget | Pipedrive | SAML single sign-on is on every Pipedrive plan; HubSpot lists it from Professional |
| Call recording with AI transcripts | Either | Pipedrive includes Nova on every plan; HubSpot records and transcribes meetings from Starter |
| Client conversations on WhatsApp and LinkedIn as well as email, or a CRM you can host yourself | A third option | See the Customermates section below |

## What five people pay per month

Prices checked on 8 October 2026 from the pricing pages of [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing) and [Pipedrive](https://www.pipedrive.com/en/pricing), in euros as the vendors list them for Germany.

| Plan | Per seat, monthly billing | Per seat, billed yearly | Five seats, monthly billing | Five seats, billed yearly |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HubSpot Starter, list price | €20 | €20 | €100 | €100 |
| HubSpot Starter, time-limited offer | €10 | €7 | €50 | €35 |
| HubSpot Professional | €100 | €90 | €500 | €450 |
| HubSpot Enterprise | Not listed | €175 | Not listed | €875 |
| Pipedrive Lite | €24 | €14 | €120 | €70 |
| Pipedrive Growth | €49 | €39 | €245 | €195 |
| Pipedrive Premium | €79 | €59 | €395 | €295 |
| Pipedrive Ultimate | €99 | €79 | €495 | €395 |

Four costs sit outside the table:

- **HubSpot's Starter price is a promotion.** HubSpot marks it as a limited-time discount of 50% on monthly billing and 65% billed yearly, which puts the regular price at €20 per seat.
- **HubSpot onboarding.** Professional and Enterprise customers must buy onboarding; Starter customers do not. The euro page states the requirement without an amount. HubSpot's US Sales Hub page lists $1,500 for Professional and $3,500 for Enterprise. Five Professional seats billed yearly cost €5,400 in the first year before that fee.
- **HubSpot Credits.** Paid plans include a monthly credit allowance that AI agents and other features draw on. Unused credits do not roll over, and HubSpot sells more in packs or pay-as-you-go.
- **Pipedrive add-ons.** LeadBooster, Smart Docs and Projects are paid add-ons on Lite and Growth and included from Premium. Campaigns, Pipedrive's email marketing tool, and Web Visitors cost extra on every plan, with starting prices from €13.33 (Campaigns) to €41 (Web Visitors). Pipedrive states its prices exclude VAT and that implementation is free on plans above USD 400 a year.

For a team that only sells, the fair comparison is Pipedrive Growth against HubSpot Professional, because those are the tiers where sequences and forecasting begin. That is €195 to €245 a month against €450 to €500 plus onboarding. If HubSpot Starter's simple automation is enough, compare it with Pipedrive Lite: at list price Starter costs €20 per seat on either billing, against €14 billed yearly or €24 monthly for Lite. HubSpot's time-limited offer lowers Starter to €7 or €10 while it runs. For a longer breakdown of Pipedrive's tiers, see [Pipedrive pricing explained](/blog/pipedrive-pricing).

## Where HubSpot is the stronger choice

HubSpot is one account for sales, marketing and service. Its plan table lists email campaigns, segments and website chat even on Free. Starter adds calling, simple automation and AI meeting recording. Professional adds advanced automation, social media management, Marketing Studio campaigns, AI-built quotes and a help desk. If leads arrive through your website and marketing hands them to sales, keeping both on the same contact records is the main reason to pick HubSpot over Pipedrive.

The free plan is usable for up to two people, and paid accounts can move their data to HubSpot's EU data center in Germany.

The limits show up as the team grows. Starter stops at 10 paid users. Sequences, forecasting, custom reports and single sign-on need Professional, which costs four to five times Starter's regular price per seat and requires paid onboarding.

## Where Pipedrive is the stronger choice

Pipedrive is built around the deal pipeline. Its four plans share one structure, so moving up raises limits and adds tools rather than switching you to a different product. Growth already has two-way email sync, sequences, automations, forecast reports and the WhatsApp integration. Every plan includes the native iOS and Android apps, SAML single sign-on, Nova call recording with summaries and transcripts, and the MCP server.

The trade-offs are just as concrete. There is no free plan. Lite has no two-way email sync, automations or sequences. AI email writing, phone support, LeadBooster and Smart Docs start on Premium. The plans cap leads and deals, custom fields, automations and reports. For marketing, Pipedrive offers the Campaigns and Web Visitors add-ons, not a marketing suite like HubSpot's.

## Data hosting and GDPR

HubSpot runs on AWS in the US, Canada, Australia and the EU (Germany). Each account is assigned a data center by IP location at sign-up, and accounts on Starter, Professional or Enterprise can change it. HubSpot notes that its sub-processors may process personal data outside the hosting location.

Pipedrive hosts on AWS in Sydney, Montreal, Frankfurt, Dublin, London and the US. Pipedrive states that customers from the EU are hosted in its EU regions.

For either vendor, read the data processing agreement and the sub-processor list before you move customer data. The hosting region alone does not settle a GDPR assessment.

## A third option: Customermates

Customermates publishes this page, so read this section as a vendor describing its own product. Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM built in Germany for small client-facing teams: consultants, agencies, other service businesses and founders. It replaces neither HubSpot's marketing tools nor Pipedrive's sales automation. It fits when most of your selling happens in conversations and you want the CRM to keep itself current.

- **Conversations next to the deal.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram into one list, and each conversation shows on the linked contact's timeline. Starter has no inbox.
- **AI that does the CRM work.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes threads, drafts replies and updates records, using hosted AI credits with a monthly allowance per active user (see [pricing](/pricing)). Mate sends a message without asking again when you tell it to send, so ask for drafts when you want to review first. Claude, ChatGPT or another client connects through the [MCP server](/docs/mcp), with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan.
- **Hosting choice.** The cloud database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, a [DPA](/dpa) is available, and the [subprocessors](/subprocessors), including US providers, are listed. Customermates holds no compliance certification. The AGPL core can be [self-hosted](/features/self-hosted) with REST, webhooks and MCP, without the inbox and Mate.
- **Cost for five people, billed monthly.** Starter [[commercial.price.starter.monthly.seats.5]], Pro [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.5]], Business [[commercial.price.business.monthly.seats.5]]. There is a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial and no free plan.

A new workspace starts with a deal Stage field: Prospecting 10%, Qualification 20%, Demo 40%, Proposal 60%, Negotiation 80%, Won 100% and Lost 0%. The deals board groups by stage, and each deal's value times its stage probability adds up to a weighted pipeline total. That is arithmetic on your own numbers, not a forecast. A [routine](/docs/app-routines) in Customermates Cloud, which uses hosted AI credits, can then handle the weekly follow-up check:

> Every Monday at 8:00, list my deals in Proposal that have not been updated for ten days. For each one, save a short follow-up draft in my latest conversation with the deal's contact. Do not send anything.

What Customermates does not have: a free plan, email sequences or bulk email, email marketing or web forms, lead scoring or data enrichment, call recording or telephony, native mobile apps, single sign-on, and record types beyond its five (contacts, organizations, deals, services and tasks). Saved views are personal. If you need any of these, HubSpot or Pipedrive is the better pick.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is Pipedrive cheaper than HubSpot?">
At the tier where sequences and automation start, yes. Pipedrive Growth costs €39 per seat billed yearly or €49 monthly. HubSpot Professional costs €90 or €100 per seat plus required onboarding. At the entry level it depends on billing and on HubSpot's offer: Starter lists at €20 per seat, against €14 billed yearly or €24 monthly for Pipedrive Lite, and on 8 October 2026 HubSpot showed a time-limited offer of €7 or €10.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is HubSpot's free CRM enough for a small sales team?">
For one or two people, often yes. HubSpot Free covers up to 2 users with contacts, companies, deals, activity tracking, an inbox connection to Gmail, Outlook or Exchange, the Breeze Assistant and the mobile app. Automation from your records and email support start on Starter, and a third user means a paid plan.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Who owns Pipedrive?">
Pipedrive announced on 12 November 2020 that Vista Equity Partners was taking a majority stake. Its earlier investors, including Bessemer Venture Partners and Insight Partners, stayed on as minority investors. Pipedrive was founded in 2010.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What are the disadvantages of Pipedrive?">
There is no free plan, only a 14-day trial. Lite has no two-way email sync, automations or sequences. AI email writing, phone support, LeadBooster and Smart Docs start on Premium. The plans cap leads and deals, custom fields and automations, and email marketing is the paid Campaigns add-on.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What are the disadvantages of HubSpot?">
The step from Starter to Professional is large: €90 to €100 per seat plus a required onboarding purchase, and that is where sequences, forecasting, custom reports and single sign-on start. Starter takes at most 10 paid users. AI agents draw on monthly HubSpot Credits, which do not roll over.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Do HubSpot and Pipedrive work with Claude or ChatGPT?">
Both run MCP servers. Pipedrive's works on every plan within the plan's token limits and has setup guides for ChatGPT and Claude. HubSpot's remote MCP server gives MCP-compatible AI tools read and write access to CRM records such as contacts, companies and deals. Customermates offers [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] MCP tools on every plan, including self-hosted installs.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

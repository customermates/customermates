> Pick Pipedrive if your team works deal by deal in a visual pipeline and budgets for the Growth plan, because email sync and automations start there. Pick Zoho CRM if you want a lower seat price or a free start for up to three users, or if quotes, invoices and approval steps should live in the CRM, and you accept more configuration. Billed yearly, five people pay €195 a month for Pipedrive Growth and €70 for Zoho CRM Standard. Customermates publishes this comparison and makes a third option, described in its own section below. Every Pipedrive and Zoho detail comes from the vendors' own pages and was checked on 8 October 2026.

## Which one fits your team

| Your situation | Better fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Up to three people, no CRM budget yet | Zoho CRM Free | Free for up to 3 users and 5,000 records, with workflow rules, standard reports and the mobile app. Pipedrive has a 14-day trial and paid plans only |
| Email in the CRM at the lowest seat price | Zoho CRM Standard | €14 per seat billed yearly, with Gmail and Outlook integration and multiple pipelines. Pipedrive syncs email from Growth at €39 |
| Salespeople who plan their day around deals and activities | Pipedrive | Lead, calendar and pipeline management on every plan; Growth adds email sync with tracking, automations and a meeting scheduler |
| Quotes, orders and invoices next to the deals | Zoho CRM Professional | Products, price books, quotes, orders and invoices start on Professional |
| Many custom fields per deal | Pipedrive Growth or Zoho CRM Professional | Pipedrive allows 100 custom fields per company on Growth. Zoho lists 10 per module on Standard and 155 on Professional |
| Contracts with e-signatures | Pipedrive Premium | Contracts and e-signatures are part of Premium |
| AI that estimates which deals will close | Zoho CRM Professional or Enterprise | Predictive intelligence starts on Professional; Enterprise extends the AI features. Pipedrive Premium has custom scoring and AI email tools |

## How the two are built

Pipedrive opens on the deal pipeline. Every plan includes lead, calendar and pipeline management, AI-powered reporting, meeting intelligence and more than 500 integrations. The four current plans are Lite, Growth, Premium and Ultimate. Growth adds full email sync with tracking, automations, email sequences and forecast reports. Premium adds lead generation and routing through LeadBooster, custom scoring, AI email tools, and contracts with e-signatures. Ultimate adds enhanced security, phone and email enrichment, a sandbox and extended phone support.

Zoho CRM comes in five editions, from Free to Ultimate, and sits next to many other Zoho apps. Standard brings email integration, built-in calling, web forms, sales forecasting and custom modules. Professional adds process management (Zoho calls it Blueprint), CPQ, inventory and unlimited reports. Enterprise adds multi-team management, approval processes, field-level encryption and a sandbox. That breadth means more decisions during setup: which edition, which modules, which rules. Zoho also sells Bigin, a smaller CRM for small businesses, covered in the FAQ.

What that means for a small team:

- **Three people.** Zoho CRM Free costs nothing. Pipedrive Lite is 3 × €14 = €42 a month, billed yearly.
- **Five people with email in the CRM, billed yearly.** Pipedrive Growth is 5 × €39 = €195 a month. Zoho CRM Standard is 5 × €14 = €70, Professional 5 × €23 = €115.
- **The same five, billed monthly.** Zoho CRM Standard comes to €100 and Professional to €175. Pipedrive Growth costs more than the €195 above.
- **Ten people on the plans with the broader AI features.** Pipedrive Premium is 10 × €59 = €590 a month billed yearly. Zoho CRM Enterprise is 10 × €40 = €400.

## Features small teams compare

**Pipeline and deals.** Pipedrive caps leads and deals per seat, so a two-person team on Lite can hold 5,000 at once; won and lost deals count until you archive them. Zoho CRM has multiple pipelines from Standard and limits storage by edition instead: 5,000 records on Free, 100,000 on Standard.

**Email.** Pipedrive's email sync starts on Growth with one email account per user, three on Premium and five on Ultimate. Zoho CRM Standard integrates Gmail and Outlook, and its BCC dropbox logs mail you send from any client.

**Automation.** Pipedrive's automations start on Growth and allow 3, 10 or 20 if/else conditions per automation, depending on the plan. Zoho CRM includes workflow rules in every edition, the free one too. Professional adds Blueprint, which guides a deal through set stages, with 3 active blueprints; Enterprise allows 50 and adds approval processes.

**AI and MCP.** Every Pipedrive plan includes AI-powered reports and Pipedrive's MCP server, which connects ChatGPT, Claude and other assistants to read and update CRM data. Use counts against token limits included in the plan, and extra tokens can be bought. AI email tools are on Premium. Zoho puts predictive intelligence on Professional and extended AI on Enterprise. Zoho MCP connects Claude, GPT and other MCP clients to Zoho CRM.

**Mobile and integrations.** Both have native mobile apps; Zoho includes its app in every edition, Free too. Pipedrive lists more than 500 integrations. Zoho CRM connects to Zoom and Microsoft Teams from Standard and to QuickBooks on Enterprise, besides the other Zoho apps.

## Data location and GDPR

Pipedrive hosts on AWS in seven regions, two of them in the EU: Frankfurt and Dublin. EU clients are primarily hosted in the EU. Pipedrive's help center adds that an account can end up in a different data center, for example through IP mappings, and that support tells you where yours is.

Zoho operates data centers in nine regions. The EU data center is in Amsterdam, with Dublin as secondary site. Zoho assigns the data center from the country you pick at sign-up, which is preset from your IP address, so a European team should check that field before creating the account.

Hosting region alone does not settle where every service processes data. Before you sign, read each vendor's data processing agreement and subprocessor list.

## Moving from Pipedrive to Zoho CRM

In Pipedrive, global admins and users with the right permission export leads, deals, organizations, people, products, activities and notes by data type, as Excel or CSV files; exports stay available for 28 days. Zoho CRM's data migration tool lists Pipedrive among the systems it imports from, and the Ultimate edition includes migration assistance. Plan the move around custom fields: a Pipedrive Growth account with 60 custom fields on deals will not fit into Zoho CRM Standard's 10 per module.

## A third option: Customermates

Customermates publishes this page, so read this section as a vendor describing its own product. It is an open-source, AI-native CRM for small client-facing teams such as consultants, agencies, other service businesses and founders, built in Germany. Contacts, organizations, deals, services and tasks sit in one record model with custom fields of ten types. It differs from Pipedrive and Zoho CRM in four ways:

- **Conversations next to the deal.** From Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) puts Gmail, Outlook and IMAP mailboxes, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram in one list linked to your contacts, and you reply on the original channel. Starter has no inbox.
- **AI for the routine CRM work.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate summarizes threads, drafts replies and updates records, using hosted AI credits. [Routines](/docs/app-routines) run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes. Claude, ChatGPT or another client can work with the CRM through the [MCP server](/docs/mcp), with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan.
- **Open source.** The AGPL-3.0 core runs on your own server with Docker Compose, including REST, webhooks and MCP. A [self-hosted install](/features/self-hosted) has no inbox and no Mate. In Customermates Cloud the database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region; other subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [DPA](/dpa) is available.
- **Monthly prices per user, no plan-based record caps.** Five people pay [[commercial.price.starter.monthly.seats.5]] a month on Starter or [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.5]] on Pro, with no yearly contract. The [pricing page](/pricing) lists the monthly hosted AI credits per active user.

A five-person consultancy would set it up like this. Under My Company › Settings, the deal stage field gets win probabilities, for example Discovery 10%, Proposal sent 50%, Negotiation 75% and Won 100%. Every deal then shows a weighted value, the Settings page shows the weighted pipeline total, and a dashboard chart can sum weighted deal values by stage. A deal's value comes from the services linked to it and their quantities, so a "Workshop day" service at your day rate prices deals the same way every time. A single-select field "Engagement type" with Retainer, Project and Workshop separates the kinds of work on one board, and each person saves a board view filtered to the deals they are assigned to; saved views are personal. On Pro, each person connects [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] account, for example their mailbox. A weekly routine in Customermates Cloud could read: "Every Monday at 8:00, find my deals in Proposal sent with no message in the last ten days, summarize each thread and save a reply draft. Do not send anything." Keep that last sentence: a routine can send messages unless its instruction says otherwise.

Customermates has no free plan, no native mobile apps and no built-in calling. It also lacks email sequences, lead scoring, quotes, invoices and inventory, and its weighted pipeline uses fixed stage probabilities rather than predictions. If your process needs one of these, Zoho CRM or Pipedrive is the better fit. Moving in works through XLSX import, one record type at a time: organizations, then contacts, then deals, as described in the [records documentation](/docs/app-records).

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is Pipedrive or Zoho CRM better for a small business?">
Neither wins outright. Billed yearly, each Zoho CRM edition costs the same as or less than the Pipedrive plan at the same step, and Zoho reaches further into quotes, invoices and process rules. Pipedrive keeps the product centred on the pipeline, with fewer plans to choose from. Check the record and field limits in the tables above against your own data before deciding.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How much is Pipedrive per month?">
Billed yearly, Pipedrive costs €14 per seat per month on Lite, €39 on Growth, €59 on Premium and €79 on Ultimate. Monthly billing costs more, and Pipedrive says the yearly plan saves up to 42%. Add-ons such as Campaigns or Projects come on top. There is a 14-day trial without a credit card.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What are the disadvantages of Pipedrive?">
They sit in the plan structure. Email sync and automations start on Growth, every plan caps leads and deals per seat, custom fields are capped per company, and LeadBooster costs extra below Premium. AI email tools need Premium. If you only manage a pipeline and your team stays small, none of this may matter.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What are the disadvantages of Zoho CRM?">
The edition you need can be higher than the first price card suggests. Standard allows 10 custom fields per module and 100,000 records, Blueprint starts on Professional and approval processes on Enterprise. Paid editions come with Zoho's classic support; premium and enterprise support are paid plans on top.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How do I migrate from Pipedrive to Zoho CRM?">
Export each data type from Pipedrive as an Excel or CSV file (global admin rights or the export permission needed), then run Zoho CRM's data migration, which lists Pipedrive as a supported source. Recreate your custom fields in Zoho first and check that your edition allows enough of them per module. Move organizations and people before deals so the links can be matched.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How does Zoho Bigin compare with Pipedrive?">
Bigin is Zoho's smaller CRM for small businesses, so it is the closer match to Pipedrive's focus on deals. It has a free plan for a single user and paid plans called Express, Premier and Bigin 360. If Bigin is on your list, compare it with Pipedrive on the features you use every day, such as email sync and automations.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

# Subprocessors

_Last updated: 3 August 2026_

## 1. About This List

A subprocessor is a third-party service provider that Customermates engages to process personal data on Customermates' behalf in the course of providing the Customermates platform.

This list is incorporated by reference into our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) and our [Data Processing Agreement](/dpa). We keep it up to date and review it whenever we add, replace, or remove a subprocessor.

## 2. Roles

Our subprocessors support two different types of processing:

- For website, account, billing, security, and support data, Customermates acts as the controller and engages the subprocessors listed below to process that data on its behalf.
- For connected-account content, a customer's connected mailboxes, messages, contacts, and calendar, together with the personal data of the people they communicate with, the customer is the controller, Customermates acts as a processor on the customer's behalf, and the relevant providers below (in particular Unipile, our hosting provider Vercel insofar as the application processes this content, and our database host Neon insofar as it stores this content) act as subprocessors. The underlying communication platforms (Google, Microsoft, LinkedIn, Meta/WhatsApp/Instagram, Telegram) act as independent controllers for their own services.

Where a subprocessor processes personal data on Customermates' behalf, that processing is governed by a data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR, either concluded directly with the subprocessor or published by it and applicable to our use of the service. The basis that applies to each provider is set out in our Privacy Policy. Lemon Squeezy is listed for completeness: for the purchase transaction it acts as merchant of record under its own terms and as an independent controller rather than on our behalf. Where personal data is transferred to a third country, the basis relied on is stated for each provider below and in our Privacy Policy, including any case in which no Chapter V instrument is relied on.

## 4. Changes to This List

Before we engage a new subprocessor, or replace an existing one, for processing carried out on a customer's behalf, we will inform affected customers in advance and give them the opportunity to object, in accordance with our [Data Processing Agreement](/dpa). If a customer objects on reasonable data-protection grounds and we cannot accommodate the objection, either party may terminate the affected services as set out in the Data Processing Agreement.

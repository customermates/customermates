Contact management software keeps every person and company you work with in one shared place, together with what is going on with them. Client management software goes one step further: it answers who the client is, who you talk to there, what is open and what happens next. Customermates covers both for small client-facing teams. It is an open-source, AI-native CRM made in Germany.

<ArticleSummary title="At a glance">
  <SummaryItem label="Every plan">Configurable lists for clients, contacts and opportunities, typed fields, relationships, filters, personal saved views, table, board or calendar, roles, Excel import and export, REST, webhooks and MCP. Self-hosted included.</SummaryItem>

## See it in an example workspace

The [client management workspace](https://demo.customermates.com/en/w/client-management/dashboard) is an example with sample data, not a customer account. It has five lists: Clients, Contacts, Contracts, Opportunities and Tasks. Each client carries a relationship status (New relationship, Active discussion, Customer, Dormant), an account manager and a configured date field for the next action. Each contract is one service at one site with a monthly fee and a review date, and a calculated field adds up the client's annual value from its contracts. Contracts carry the site address and the signed agreement as a PDF, the dashboard puts the sites on a map and lists the reviews due in the next 30 days, and a calendar view shows the review dates by month.

Open one client and you see its people, its contracts, its open opportunities, its tasks and the supported conversations of the people linked to it, on one record. Nothing there is copied into a second data silo: the contact in the client record is the same contact you search for, filter on or update.

Your own workspace does not start with these lists. A new workspace starts without lists. On Customermates Cloud, the AI workspace setup builds lists, stages, a dashboard and Knowledge Base pages from your description. It runs once during onboarding, is capped by setup credits, and you can skip it with **Start with an empty workspace**. Otherwise, people with Configure rights create the lists and fields in **Configure**. When self-hosting, that is the only way.

## Structured client details

A client record holds the fields you decide on. The field types are Text, Formatted text, Number, Money, Yes or no, Date, Date and time, Date range, Date and time range, Single choice, Multiple choice, Web address, Member, Address, File and Phone, email & social. Calculated fields add values from other fields or linked records.

The **Contact details** field holds the phone numbers, email addresses and LinkedIn, Instagram and Telegram identities that match messages to a person. A number there can be called and also matches WhatsApp chats from the same number. One address can belong to several records, for example a contact and a client.

An **Address** field holds one postal address: Customermates checks it against the address format of its country, suggests city and region for the postal code and opens the address in maps on click. A **File** field holds documents such as contracts or proposals, up to 20 files per field, and the text of those files is found by search.

Notes live on each record. Global search (Cmd/Ctrl+K) finds records by name and contact details; other fields are reached through filters.

## Linked records instead of a flat list

Relationships connect the lists. A contact can belong to several clients, an opportunity points to its client and its people, and a task points to whatever it is about. If your team talks about accounts rather than clients, rename the list for the whole workspace in **Configure**.

Where a spreadsheet keeps one row per person and repeats the company name, Customermates keeps the company once and links the people to it. Open the client and its people are listed there; open a person and their clients are one click away.

## Configurable next actions

Customermates has no built-in reminders or due-date logic. You add a date field such as **Next action** to clients or opportunities and save a view filtered to dates that have passed. On Customermates Cloud, a [routine](/docs/app-routines) can list those records every Monday morning. Board views grouped by relationship status show at a glance who is new, active or dormant.

## Moving off Excel or Google Sheets

Many teams arrive with a spreadsheet. Moving it is practical, but not automatic:

- **Format.** A list imports from an Excel workbook (.xlsx) through a wizard: pick the sheet, map the columns, check the preview, import. Google Sheets files are not imported directly: download the sheet as .xlsx, and save an Outlook or Google Contacts download as .xlsx in Excel. A JSON export of the same list can also be imported again.
- **Order.** Import clients before contacts so the company column links to existing records. Map email, phone and messaging columns to the **Contact details** field.
- **No undo.** An import cannot be rolled back. Clean duplicates first, because there is no merge function afterwards. Test with a small file.
- **No attachments.** Files that sit next to your spreadsheet, such as contracts or PDFs, are not part of the import. Upload the ones you need to a File field on the record afterwards.
- **Corrections in bulk.** Export the list, edit it in Excel and import it again as **Existing records**.
- **No ongoing sync.** After the import, your spreadsheet and Customermates are separate lists.

The [records guide](/docs/app-records#how-do-i-import-or-export-records) documents limits and options.

## Conversations beside the relationship

On Customermates Cloud, Pro and higher connect Gmail, Outlook, IMAP, LinkedIn (including supported Sales Navigator and Recruiter chats), WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram through the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox). A participant is associated with a contact through the identity in its Contact details field. When an unknown address writes, you link that participant to an existing or new contact. A conversation is linked to an opportunity or client only when you or an agent do it explicitly; nothing is filed to a deal automatically.

Plan limits apply. Starter and self-hosted have no connected messaging accounts. Pro covers your own accounts, with [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] connected account per user included, plus threads shared individually. Sharing a whole account with the team needs Business, Max or Enterprise.

## AI on your client records: Mate and MCP

On Customermates Cloud, Mate, the hosted assistant, works on records in plain language within your plan's AI credits:

```text
Create contacts for everyone in this email signature and link them to the client Hartmann Consulting. Check first whether any of them already exist.
```

Mate sees what you may see. It creates and updates records and sends messages as soon as you ask; deletions, imports and access changes stop for your approval every time. Mate is not available when self-hosting.

On every plan, self-hosted included, your own AI client such as Claude or ChatGPT can read and update records you have access to through [MCP](/docs/mcp), billed by your AI provider.

## Where the data lives

On Customermates Cloud the database runs in the AWS Frankfurt region. Service providers, including US companies, are on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [data processing agreement](/dpa) is available. EU hosting is not a certification, and Customermates holds none. To keep data on your own servers, run the AGPL core [self-hosted](/features/self-hosted), without the inbox and without Mate.

## Contact management software or a phone address book?

| Your situation | Look at |
|---|---|
| One person who needs names and numbers on a phone | The address book of your phone, Outlook or Google account |
| A shared spreadsheet that still works for two people | Keep it until links, history or access rights become the problem |
| A small team that shares clients, opportunities and conversations | A CRM such as Customermates |

Customermates does not sync with phone or Outlook address books. It is where a team manages client relationships, not a replacement for the contacts app on your phone. For more options side by side, read the [client database software comparison](/blog/client-database-software).

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="What is the difference between contact management and client management?">
Contact management stores people, companies and their details. Client management adds what you do with them: opportunities, next actions, tasks and conversations. In Customermates both sit in linked lists of one workspace.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I use tags for contacts?">
No. Add a multiple-choice field instead, for example **Segment** with the values you would have used as tags. You can filter and group by it.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I attach files to a client or contact?">
Yes. Add a File field to the list; it holds up to 20 files, and search finds the text inside them. Attachments of supported messages stay in their inbox thread.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I import from Excel, Google Sheets or Outlook?">
Yes, as an Excel workbook (.xlsx). Google Sheets files are not imported directly, so download the sheet or your contacts as .xlsx first, import clients before contacts, and test with a small file: an import cannot be undone.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does a new workspace come with Clients and Contacts lists?">
No. A new workspace starts without lists. On Customermates Cloud the AI workspace setup can build them during onboarding, otherwise you create them in Configure. The lists in the example workspace show one possible setup.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How many contacts can I manage?">
The plans set no contact limit. One export covers up to 5,000 records of the current view.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

Customermates connects to Outlook on the server side: you sign in to your Outlook account once, and its email threads appear in the CRM's [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox), matched to contacts by email address. You can answer from the CRM or keep writing in Outlook, because sent mail from Outlook reaches the same thread. Moving an email to a folder in Customermates moves it in your real mailbox too. Calendar events come in read-only. There is nothing to install in Outlook, and the integration needs Customermates Cloud on Pro or higher.

## Three kinds of Outlook CRM integration

"CRM for Outlook" covers three different designs. Decide which one you want before you compare vendors. Customermates publishes this page; the vendor examples below come from each vendor's own documentation, checked on 8 October 2026.

| Design | Where you work | Example | Choose it if |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Add-in in Outlook | In Outlook, with a CRM pane beside the email | [HubSpot Sales Office 365 add-in](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/connected-email/get-started-with-the-hubspot-sales-office-365-add-in) (tracks and logs emails in Outlook for Windows, Mac and the web); [Dynamics 365 App for Outlook](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/outlook-app/overview) (links emails and appointments to records, can synchronize contacts with Exchange) | Your team lives in Outlook all day and wants CRM data next to each email |
| CRM inside Outlook | Outlook is the CRM interface | [eWay-CRM](https://www.eway-crm.com/), which describes itself as turning Outlook into a CRM | You want no second app at all and your team works in Outlook |
| Mailbox connected to the CRM | In the CRM, Outlook keeps running beside it | Customermates | You also talk to clients on LinkedIn or WhatsApp, or want an AI assistant to read and draft from the same threads |

If you need Outlook contacts kept in sync both ways, or a CRM pane inside Outlook, Customermates is the wrong fit. Look at an add-in-based CRM instead. The [Outlook CRM comparison](/blog/outlook-crm-integration) goes through more options.

## What the Outlook connection does

**Email threads in the inbox.** Sent and received mail of the connected account shows up as threads in the inbox, newest activity first, next to LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram conversations if you connect those. Search covers subject, message text and participant names or addresses, and a filter narrows the list to one email folder.

**Folders, both ways.** The channel's **Folders** tab has one switch per Outlook folder. By default every folder is on except trash, junk, drafts and the all-mail folder. In an Outlook thread you can move one email or the whole conversation to another folder, and the move happens in your real mailbox. Gmail accounts can't do this; it works for Outlook and IMAP.

**Contacts linked by address.** A contact's email address connects it to every thread with that address. For a sender who is not linked yet, open **Thread settings**, click **Link** and pick a contact or create one from the name. The address is then added to that contact, so all earlier and later threads with it show on the contact. Threads reach deals and organizations through the contact; they are not filed on each deal separately.

**Replies and new emails from the CRM.** Reply in the thread's composer, with To, Cc and Bcc, Markdown formatting and attached files. Start a new email from a contact's **Channels** block. Each Outlook account has its own font, link colour and signature on its **Email** tab. Once switched on, the signature is added to every email sent from that account, whether from the inbox, the API or MCP.

**Calendar, read-only.** Events from the connected Outlook calendar appear in the **Account & calendar** category of activity timeline widgets, and AI clients read them over MCP with `get_calendars`. Customermates has no calendar page of its own and never creates, changes or books events in Outlook.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the plan">
    Use Customermates Cloud on Pro, Business, Enterprise or a trial. Your role
    needs Manage on Inbox messages to connect a channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Outlook">
    Open My Profile › Channels, click Connect channel and choose Outlook. The
    tab moves to a secure connection page where you sign in. Your history then
    imports in the background, usually in a few minutes and up to an hour for a
    large mailbox. If your Microsoft 365 tenant blocks user consent to
    third-party apps, an admin has to approve the connection first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose folders">
    Open the account's Folders tab and switch on the folders you want in the
    CRM, for example an archive folder, or switch off newsletters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the signature">
    On the Email tab, switch on Add a signature and write it once. Don't paste
    it into messages as well.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link the first contacts">
    Filter the inbox by Participants: not fully linked, then link each sender
    to a contact or create one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Microsoft documents the consent settings in [Configure how users consent to applications](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity/enterprise-apps/configure-user-consent): by default users can consent to apps for permissions that don't need admin consent, and admins can restrict that. If your mailbox can't sign in through the Outlook option, the IMAP option connects other email accounts over IMAP and SMTP.

## Who sees which emails

A new channel is private: only you see its threads, and no role, Admin included, can read a teammate's private threads. You can share one conversation from its **Thread settings**. On Business and Enterprise you can share the whole account, for example a team address one person connected, and every member whose role can read the inbox then sees all its threads. Each Outlook account can be connected only once per workspace, so share it rather than connecting it twice.

## AI on your Outlook threads

**Mate, in Customermates Cloud.** Mate can summarize a thread, draft a reply, file an email to a folder or send it when you ask. Mate uses hosted AI credits; each plan includes monthly hosted AI credits per active user, see [pricing](/pricing). Mate sends without a further confirmation when you ask it to send, so say "draft" when you only want a draft.

**Routines, in Customermates Cloud.** A routine can start on the **Email Received** event. Example instruction: "When an email arrives from a contact linked to an open deal, create a task on that deal assigned to me with a two-line summary, and save a reply draft in the thread. Do not send anything." The routine runs as its owner and only sees threads that person can see. Limits are in the [routines guide](/docs/app-routines).

**Your own AI client, through MCP.** Claude, ChatGPT and other MCP clients can read threads, save drafts and send email through the [MCP server](/docs/mcp), using your own AI subscription instead of hosted credits. Whether the client asks before it sends depends on the client.

## Limits worth knowing

- No Outlook add-in, sidebar or ribbon button. CRM details are looked up in Customermates.
- Outlook address-book contacts are not synchronized in either direction. Contacts are created in Customermates, by linking a sender, manually or by [XLSX import](/docs/app-records).
- Calendar data is read-only. No meeting booking, no event writes.
- No mass email or sequences. Every email is written and sent individually.
- Disconnecting an account permanently deletes the threads, messages and calendar events Customermates imported from it.
- Providers limit how fast mail can be read and sent; the current budgets are in [messaging rate limits](/docs/messaging-rate-limits).
- Self-hosted Customermates has no inbox, so no Outlook connection. Only Customermates Cloud has it.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Do I need to install an Outlook add-in?">
No. The connection runs between Customermates and your account, so there is nothing to install in Outlook and nothing to update on each computer. The trade-off: you see CRM data in Customermates, not in an Outlook pane.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I keep sending email from Outlook?">
Yes. Mail you send from Outlook lands in the same thread in Customermates, and replies join it. Use whichever app is at hand.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Customermates sync my Outlook contacts or calendar?">
Contacts: no, neither direction. Threads are matched to CRM contacts by email address instead. Calendar: events are read into Customermates read-only; nothing is written back to Outlook.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can the whole team see each other's Outlook mail?">
Only what is shared. Private threads stay private, Admins included. Single conversations can be shared on any plan with the inbox; whole accounts can be shared on Business and Enterprise.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is Microsoft Outlook a CRM?">
Outlook manages email, calendar and an address book, but it has no deals, pipeline stages or shared customer records. Microsoft's own CRM line is Dynamics 365, which connects to Outlook through the Dynamics 365 App for Outlook. Customermates keeps Outlook as your mail client and adds those records next to your threads.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Where is the data processed?">
In Customermates Cloud the application database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region. Connected accounts are transported by Unipile. Every subprocessor, including US providers, is on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [DPA](/dpa) is available.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

## What Is CRM Outlook Integration?

CRM Outlook integration connects Microsoft Outlook with your customer relationship management system, creating a seamless bridge between your email inbox, calendar, and CRM data. It allows your team to manage customer relationships from within Outlook while ensuring that every communication is captured in the CRM for the entire team to access.

Email remains the primary B2B communication channel. Your sales team sends dozens of emails to prospects and customers every day through Outlook. Without CRM integration, these conversations exist only in individual inboxes, invisible to the rest of the team and disconnected from the deal and contact records where they belong. When someone is out sick, on vacation, or leaves the company, their email context leaves with them.

Outlook integration reduces this problem by bringing supported email into the CRM and matching conversations to relevant records. Connected calendar events can be displayed read-only. Customermates does not synchronize Outlook contacts or create, edit, or book provider calendar events.

## How Outlook Integration Works in Customermates

Customermates connects supported Microsoft Outlook 365 email to the unified inbox beside LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Telegram. A supported external AI client can read allowed thread context and draft an individual reply through MCP. Email and a read-only view of the connected calendar start with Pro ([[commercial.price.pro.monthly]]/user/month); Starter ([[commercial.price.starter.monthly]]/user/month) covers core CRM without a connected mailbox. Any n8n workflow runs separately.

### Setup in Three Steps

**Step 1: Connect your Outlook account.** Open **Profile → Connected accounts** in Customermates, choose **Connect an account**, and sign in through Microsoft's hosted authentication flow. No IT department involvement needed, no Outlook plugins to install.

**Step 2: Configure your email connection.** Select the folders to import where the account supports folder selection. Calendar data is exposed read-only where supported, and there is no Outlook contact-sync direction or calendar-write setting in Customermates.

**Step 3: Start working.** Email threads from the connected account appear in the unified inbox, where participants can be linked to CRM contacts. Connected calendar events appear in a read-only view. Your team can keep writing in Outlook; conversations from the connected account still reach the CRM inbox.

### Email Synchronization

Email sync captures both sent and received emails of the connected account. When you send an email to a prospect from Outlook, the thread appears in the Customermates inbox; when they reply, the response joins the same thread. Teammates see it when account, thread, and CRM permissions allow it.

Customermates matches threads to contacts by their email addresses, and you can link a participant to a contact yourself. If the sender is not yet in your CRM, create the contact and link it to the thread.

### Read-Only Calendar Data

Read supported events from the connected Outlook calendar in CRM context. Meetings created in Outlook may appear on the relevant activity timeline, but Customermates does not create, edit, book, delete, or synchronize events back to Outlook.

Seeing upcoming and past meetings next to your CRM work gives context before a call. Meeting-frequency or relationship-health metrics are not calculated by Customermates.

### Contact Matching Without Contact Sync

Supported email conversations can be matched to CRM contacts by address. Outlook address-book contacts are not synchronized in either direction; create or update CRM contacts through the product's supported record interfaces instead.

## Core Features

**Automatic email capture.** Email threads from the connected account reach the unified inbox automatically and are matched to contacts by address. No manual forwarding and no BCC addresses.

**Conversations next to the contact.** A contact's conversations sit alongside its notes, linked tasks, and deals. Messages are linked through the contact, not attached directly to every deal or organization.

**AI-drafted replies through external clients.** Supported clients such as Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini can use MCP to read allowed thread context and draft one reply for approval. The external client, model, and subscription are yours; on cloud plans the hosted Mate assistant can also draft replies inside the CRM and asks for confirmation before sending.

**One inbox across channels.** Outlook email sits in the same unified inbox as LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Telegram, so your team answers every channel from one place inside the CRM instead of switching apps.

**Shared account support.** From the Business tier at [[commercial.price.business.monthly]] per user per month, an entire connected account, such as a sales@ or support@ mailbox, can be shared with the workspace, so several team members work the same threads without access to each other's personal inboxes. On Pro, individual threads can be shared.

**Separate n8n workflows for advanced automation.** A separately operated n8n instance can use documented interfaces and external providers for workflows beyond the native email and read-only calendar surface:

- When an email event is available, create a supported task and notify the account manager through an external [Slack](/features/slack-integration) provider
- When a deal moves to "Proposal Sent," ask an external scheduler and Outlook API to send a later follow-up
- When a new sender emails your team, create a supported CRM record through REST and apply customer-owned routing logic
- When an external calendar provider emits an event, update a supported CRM field through the workflow

**Keep working in Outlook.** Your team can keep writing in Outlook; conversations from the connected account still reach the CRM inbox without manual logging. There is no Outlook add-in, so CRM details are looked up in Customermates.

**Supported email context.** Connected email conversations can appear in the unified inbox and calendar data can be viewed read-only. Customermates does not capture every Outlook object, synchronize contacts, or write provider calendar events.

**Smoother handoffs.** When conversations live next to the CRM record, a colleague can pick up a deal with its email context instead of starting from scratch.

## Common Outlook Integration Use Cases

**Sales email tracking.** Log all emails between your sales team and prospects on the corresponding deal records. When reviewing a deal, the [sales](/features/sales) manager sees the complete email thread alongside pipeline activities and notes.

**Read-only meeting context.** Supported meetings from a connected Outlook calendar can appear read-only in CRM context. Meeting-frequency or relationship-health calculations are not native product features.

**Lead capture from email.** A customer-run workflow can use supported email data to create a CRM contact through REST and apply its own routing rules. Customermates has no native lead-routing or Outlook contact-sync engine.

**Follow-up scheduling.** After sending a proposal email, a separate workflow can create a follow-up task linked to the email thread. Store the follow-up date in a custom field; a separately operated n8n workflow can evaluate it and send a notification through an external provider. Customermates has no native reminder engine. On the cloud, a Mate [routine](/docs/app-routines) can instead check that date on a schedule and list or draft due follow-ups (Starter includes [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] routine per user, Pro [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]]).

**Out-of-office continuity.** When a team member is on vacation, their complete email history with every contact is available in the CRM. Colleagues pick up conversations seamlessly without needing access to the individual's Outlook mailbox.

**Email-triggered deal updates.** A separate n8n workflow can evaluate available email events and call supported CRM operations. Its classification logic, Outlook access, runtime, credentials, logs, limits, and delivery remain external.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Does it work with Outlook desktop or only Outlook 365?">
Customermates integrates with Microsoft Outlook 365 (Microsoft 365) through server-side sync. This covers Outlook on the web, Outlook desktop connected to a Microsoft 365 account, and the Outlook mobile app. On-premise Exchange servers are supported through IMAP configuration.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Do I need to install an Outlook plugin?">
No. The integration works through server-side OAuth authentication. There is nothing to install in Outlook, no browser extensions, and no add-ins that need maintenance.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I choose which emails are synced?">
Yes, by folder. When the account supports folder selection, you choose which Outlook folders are imported into the inbox.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is calendar sync bidirectional?">
No. Supported events from the connected Outlook calendar are read-only in Customermates. The CRM does not create, edit, book, delete, or synchronize events back to Outlook.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can multiple team members connect their Outlook accounts?">
Yes. On Pro each team member can connect one account, on Business up to three plus shared accounts. A teammate sees a conversation when account, thread, and CRM permissions allow it; Pro users can share individual threads, and Business can share a whole account.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Where is hosted data processed?">
In the managed cloud, the application database runs in the AWS Frankfurt region, and Unipile, which transports connected accounts, stores data in France. The application is hosted by Vercel in the USA, and all subprocessors are listed on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors). Self-hosted Starter does not include this cloud email integration.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

Client follow-up software makes sure the next contact with each client happens and shows whoever makes it what was said last. In Customermates that means LinkedIn and email conversations on the client record, a dated next step on each client, and drafts from Mate or your own AI client that you review and send.

It is a list you open, not a reminder that finds you. Customermates sends no reminder notifications, has no due dates built into tasks and runs no email sequences. If your team depends on those, the comparison further down names two tools that fit better.

## What does client follow-up software need to do?

Three things: show who is waiting, show what was said last, and put the next step in front of you on the day it is due. Customermates covers them like this:

| Follow-up question | Where you answer it | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| Who should I contact this week? | Saved view on contacts or deals, sorted by a custom **Next step date** | Every plan |
| Which deals have gone quiet? | Saved view on deals: **Updated at** **Older than…** 14 days, **Stage** not Won or Lost | Every plan |
| Who has not answered my last message? | Inbox filter **Last message** = **Sent**, **Last message date** **Older than…** 3 days | Pro and higher |
| What do I write? | Ask Mate on the contact or thread for a draft based on notes and history | Customermates Cloud, hosted AI credits |
| Can this run without me? | A routine that builds the list and saves drafts every Monday | Customermates Cloud, hosted AI credits |

The inbox row covers the follow-up you owe after writing last. **Last message** looks at the latest message actually sent or received in a thread, across email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram, and ignores drafts. A thread where you wrote last five days ago and heard nothing shows up there. Details are in the [inbox filters guide](/docs/app-inbox).

## What does a dated next step look like in Customermates?

This is the Friday scene from the demo further up, in the order you would work it. The names are sample data; the fields and screens are the product's.

1. **Friday, 16:42: the reply arrives.** Jonas Weber at Halden & Co. answers on LinkedIn: "Could you send the revised scope next week? Then I can take it to our board." His contact record holds his LinkedIn profile and his email address in **Contact details**, so this thread and last month's email thread both appear on the record's **Activities** and in the [inbox](/features/unified-inbox).
2. **Friday evening: you ask for a draft.** In the thread, ask Mate: "Draft a reply: revised scope on Tuesday, with the two workshop options. Set Next step to Send revised scope and Next step date to Tuesday." Mate saves the draft in the conversation and fills the two fields. Your own AI client, such as Claude or ChatGPT, can do the same over [MCP](/docs/mcp) on Customermates Cloud. Ask for a draft, not a send: Mate sends the moment you tell it to.
3. **You review and send.** The draft waits in the thread with **Send draft**, **Edit** and **Discard**. Read it against the thread, fix the wording and send it. It goes out on LinkedIn from your connected account as one individual message.
4. **Tuesday morning: it is on your dashboard.** The Upcoming widget lists Jonas under today, and your "Follow-ups" view shows his **Next step** text beside the date. You send the scope, write one line in the notes and move **Next step date** to the day you will check back. If Tuesday passes, he moves to **Overdue** on Wednesday. Nothing notifies you, so the dashboard is the reminder.

| On Jonas Weber's record | Value | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Contact details | LinkedIn profile, email address | Matches both threads to him |
| Next step | Send revised scope | A **Text** field you add |
| Next step date | Tuesday | A **Date** field you add; the views and the widget use it |
| Activities | LinkedIn thread, email thread, changes | Built in; messages need Pro or higher |
| Draft | Saved in the LinkedIn thread | Mate or your AI client; you send |

The date is the one you promised, not a fixed number of days, so the follow-up lands when the client expects it. This page covers tracking; for the wording of the message, read [how to write a sales follow-up email](/blog/sales-follow-up-email). If you sell consulting, the [CRM for consultants](/for/consultants) page applies the same dated next step to engagements and proposals.

## How do I set up a client and lead follow-up system?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Next step and Next step date">In **Data model**, add a field of type **Date** named "Next step date" and a field of type **Text** named "Next step" to **Contacts** or **Deals**, or to both if your leads live on deals. Ask Mate if you would rather have it add them. To record why you are following up, add a **Single choice** field "Follow-up reason" with options such as Proposal sent, Check-in, Renewal. Tasks have no built-in due date, so the same Date field works there too.</Step>
  <Step title="Save the weekly list">Open **Appearance**, show **Next step** and **Next step date**, set **Sort by** to Next step date, **Ascending**, then click **New view** and name it "Follow-ups". The oldest dates sit at the top. Custom date filters take fixed dates (**Before…**, **On or before…**), so sorting is what keeps this list current without editing the filter each week. Switch the view's layout to **Calendar** on the same date if you prefer days to rows, and drag a record to another day to move its next step.</Step>
  <Step title="Show it on your dashboard">On the **Dashboard**, press **Add widget** and choose **Upcoming**. Pick the list and the **Next step date** field, and filter out finished records, for example Won and Lost deals. The widget lists the records due in the next 14 days and has an **Overdue** section for earlier dates, so the next steps are in front of you when you sign in. It names the record and its date; the view from the previous step shows the step text.</Step>
  <Step title="Save a stale-deals view">On **Deals**, filter **Updated at** with **Older than…** 14 days and exclude Won and Lost in the **Stage** field. This catches deals nobody touched, whether or not someone set a date.</Step>
  <Step title="Save a waiting-for-reply view (Pro and higher)">In the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox), filter **Last message** = **Sent** and **Last message date** **Older than…** 3 days. Save it as "Waiting for reply". The day window moves with the calendar, so the view stays correct.</Step>
  <Step title="Log the outcome">After each contact, write what happened in the record's notes and move the Next step date. The record's Activities keep the linked messages and changes.</Step>
</Steps>

Choose **Share…** in the menu of the "Follow-ups" view to show it to everyone in the workspace or to selected roles. The whole team then works from one live list of follow-ups that are due, and each person still sees only the records their role allows.

## Can I automate client follow-up with Mate and routines?

Partly, and always one message at a time. Mate is the assistant in Customermates Cloud and uses hosted AI credits. On a contact, deal or inbox thread, ask it something like "Draft a short follow-up to Anna about the proposal we sent on the 2nd, based on the thread." It reads the notes, the linked conversation and the deal, and saves a draft you review in the thread.

A [routine](/docs/app-routines) runs a saved instruction on a schedule (**Every week**, Monday at 08:00, for example) or when something changes, such as a deal update or an incoming email. A routine runs as its owner, with that owner's channels and credits. This is a working Monday instruction for customer follow-up automation:

> Find my open deals whose Next step date is today or earlier, and inbox threads where my last message is more than five days old. For each, save a short follow-up draft in the thread. Do not send anything. Then list them for me with one line of context each.

The "Do not send anything" line matters. Mate sends messages without asking again when an instruction tells it to, and an unattended routine follows the instruction it has. Deletions and a few admin actions need approval, and a routine declines those. Runs per hour and credits per run are capped; the current limits are in the routines guide.

An event example: a routine set to **When something changes** for deal updates, with the instruction "If the stage changed to Proposal, set Next step date to five working days from today and create a task 'Follow up on proposal' assigned to the deal's assigned users." Changes a routine makes never start other routines, so this cannot loop.

<AcquisitionCallout title="What follow-up in Customermates does not include" variant="boundary">
  No notifications or reminder emails, no built-in due date or overdue state on tasks, no recurring tasks. No email sequences, drip campaigns or bulk sending; every message is written and sent one at a time. A view you share is read-only for your colleagues. Self-hosted installations have no inbox, no Mate and no routines.
</AcquisitionCallout>

## How does it connect to LinkedIn, email and the rest?

- **Inbox (Pro and higher):** follow-ups go out from the original channel, and a thread links to the contact through its participants, so the history shows on the record. [LinkedIn conversations](/features/linkedin-integration) work the same way as email, with the LinkedIn account you connect.
- **Tasks:** if a step needs its own owner or checklist, create a task linked to the client. Tasks have no due date of their own, so give them a Date field as in [task management](/features/task-management).
- **MCP:** your own AI client, such as Claude or ChatGPT, can read the same views and threads through [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] MCP tools and save drafts. This works self-hosted too, minus the inbox. See the [MCP guide](/docs/mcp).
- **Webhooks and n8n:** if you need a real notification, for example a message in your team chat when a Next step date passes, run that in n8n or your own scheduler against the REST API. n8n is a separate product with its own plans. See [workflow automation](/features/workflow-automation).

## Where is Customermates the wrong choice for client follow-up?

Customermates publishes this page, so read the comparison with that in mind. It is the wrong choice if you need reminders that reach you, a cadence that sends itself, or follow-up by phone or text: there are no notifications, sequences, telephony or SMS channels, and no native mobile app.

| If you need | Look at | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Automated multi-step email follow-ups that can stop when the contact replies | HubSpot Sales Hub sequences | HubSpot documents sequences as a series of timed email templates with optional reminder tasks, and contacts can be unenrolled automatically when they reply or book a meeting |
| Activities with due dates and an overdue count | Pipedrive | Pipedrive activities carry a due date, and the activities icon counts overdue activities and those due today |
| Follow-up lists, unanswered-thread views and AI drafts you review and send | Customermates | Views and inbox filters as described above; Mate and routines in Customermates Cloud |

Competitor details checked on 10 November 2026 against [HubSpot's sequences documentation](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/sequences/create-and-edit-sequences), [Pipedrive's activities article](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/activities) and [Pipedrive's overdue count article](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/what-is-the-red-number-on-the-activities-icon).

## FAQ

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="What is the best app for customer follow-up?">
It depends on how you follow up. If you send the same series of emails to many leads, you want an outreach tool built for that. If you follow up personally with a few dozen clients across email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp, you want one list of who is waiting and the conversation history next to it. Customermates is built for the second case.
</FaqItem>
<FaqItem question="Is there free client follow-up software?">
Customermates has a Free plan for one person with [[commercial.limit.free.maxRecords]] records. Date fields, saved views and the dashboard work there, so you can run the follow-up list for free. The inbox, Mate and routines start on paid plans (the inbox on Pro), and the open-source edition is free to self-host, without inbox, Mate or routines.
</FaqItem>
<FaqItem question="What tools help set up automated follow-up workflows for unanswered leads?">
Customermates helps with the review loop rather than the cadence: a saved view lists the clients whose next step is due or whose thread you wrote last, and a routine in Customermates Cloud can save draft messages for them on a schedule. For timed multi-step sequences use a sequencing tool, and note that Customermates does not place or log phone calls, so a lead you called without an answer needs a note and a next step date from you.
</FaqItem>
<FaqItem question="Does Customermates have follow-up reminders or due dates?">
No. There are no notifications, and tasks have no built-in due date. Add a custom Date field, sort a saved view by it and show it in an Upcoming widget on your dashboard. If you need a push notification, n8n or another scheduler can read that date through the API and notify you.
</FaqItem>
<FaqItem question="Is there a CRM that finds deals in email conversations and reminds me to follow up?">
Customermates does not send reminders. In Customermates Cloud, a routine triggered by incoming email can ask Mate to check whether the conversation belongs to an existing deal, update that deal and set its Next step date, within the routine limits in the [routines guide](/docs/app-routines#what-are-the-limits). The deal then appears in your follow-up view. The inbox filter for threads you sent last covers the rest.
</FaqItem>
<FaqItem question="Can I automate follow-up emails?">
Not as a sequence. A routine in Customermates Cloud can write individual drafts on a schedule, and you send them after a read. It can also send if the instruction says so, one message per conversation. Bulk or multi-step cadences need a dedicated sequencing tool.
</FaqItem>
<FaqItem question="Can I create proposals or quotes in Customermates and follow up on them?">
You can follow up on them, not create them: there is no quote or invoicing function. Keep the proposal PDF in your quoting tool or in a File field on the record, and put the next step on the client.
</FaqItem>
<FaqItem question="How do I track follow-ups when self-hosting?">
Use the Next step date column and saved views, which are part of the open-source core. There is no inbox, Mate or routine when self-hosting, so drafting runs through your own AI client over MCP, and timed actions through webhooks and n8n.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

Customer follow-up software has one job: make sure the next contact with a client happens, and that whoever makes it knows what was said last. In Customermates that works through lists you open, not pings you receive. You keep a next-contact date on the contact or deal, a saved view sorts everyone by that date, and on Pro and higher an inbox view shows every conversation where you sent the last message and got no answer.

In Customermates Cloud, Mate can also write the follow-up for you and a routine can run that check every Monday, using hosted AI credits. What Customermates does not have: reminder notifications, task due dates, overdue alerts or automated email sequences. If your team depends on those, a CRM with built-in activities and sequences fits better, and the table further down names two.

## What follow-up looks like in Customermates

| Follow-up question | Where you answer it | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| Who should I contact this week? | Saved view on contacts or deals, sorted by a custom **Next contact** date | Every plan |
| Which deals have gone quiet? | Saved view on deals: **Updated at** **Older than…** 14 days, **Stage** not Won or Lost | Every plan |
| Who has not answered my last message? | Inbox filter **Last message** = **Sent**, **Last message date** **Older than…** 3 days | Pro and higher |
| What do I write? | Ask Mate on the contact or thread for a draft based on notes and history | Customermates Cloud, hosted AI credits |
| Can this run without me? | A routine that builds the list and saves drafts every Monday | Customermates Cloud, hosted AI credits |

The inbox row covers the follow-up you owe after writing last. **Last message** looks at the latest message actually sent or received in a thread, across email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram, and ignores drafts. A thread where you wrote last five days ago and heard nothing shows up there. Details are in the [inbox filters guide](/docs/app-inbox).

## Set up a follow-up system

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a Next contact date">On **Contacts** or **Deals**, add a custom column of type **Date** and call it "Next contact". If you want to know why you are following up, add a **Single Select** column "Follow-up reason" with options such as Proposal sent, Check-in, Renewal (a new workspace's deal **Stage** column starts with Proposal, Won and Lost among its options). Tasks have no built-in due date, so the same Date column works there too.</Step>
  <Step title="Save the weekly list">Open **Appearance**, set **Sort by** to Next contact, **Ascending**, then click **New view** and name it "Follow-ups". The oldest dates sit at the top. Custom date filters take fixed dates (**Before…**, **On or before…**), so sorting is what keeps this list current without editing the filter each week.</Step>
  <Step title="Save a stale-deals view">On **Deals**, filter **Updated at** with **Older than…** 14 days and exclude Won and Lost in the **Stage** column. This catches deals nobody touched, whether or not someone set a date.</Step>
  <Step title="Save a waiting-for-reply view (Pro and higher)">In the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox), filter **Last message** = **Sent** and **Last message date** **Older than…** 3 days. Save it as "Waiting for reply". The day window moves with the calendar, so the view stays correct.</Step>
  <Step title="Log the outcome">After each contact, write what happened in the record's notes and move the Next contact date. The record's history keeps the linked messages and changes.</Step>
</Steps>

Saved views are personal, so each teammate sets the same filters and saves their own view.

## Let Mate draft follow-ups (Customermates Cloud)

Mate is the assistant in Customermates Cloud and uses hosted AI credits. On a contact, deal or inbox thread, ask it something like "Draft a short follow-up to Anna about the proposal we sent on the 2nd, based on the thread." It reads the notes, the linked conversation and the deal, and saves a draft you review in the thread.

A [routine](/docs/app-routines) runs a saved instruction on a schedule (**Every week**, Monday at 08:00, for example) or when something changes, such as a deal update or an incoming email. A routine runs as its owner, with that owner's channels and credits. This is a working Monday instruction:

> Find my open deals whose Next contact date is today or earlier, and inbox threads where my last message is more than five days old. For each, save a short follow-up draft in the thread. Do not send anything. Then list them for me with one line of context each.

The "Do not send anything" line matters. Mate sends messages without asking again when an instruction tells it to, and an unattended routine follows the instruction it has. Deletions and a few admin actions need approval, and a routine declines those. Runs per hour and credits per run are capped; the current limits are in the routines guide.

An event example: a routine set to **When something changes** for deal updates, with the instruction "If the stage changed to Proposal, set Next contact to five working days from today and create a task 'Follow up on proposal' assigned to the deal's assigned users." Changes a routine makes never start other routines, so this cannot loop.

  No notifications or reminder emails, no built-in due date or overdue state on tasks, no recurring tasks. No email sequences, drip campaigns or bulk sending; every message is written and sent one at a time. Saved views are personal, not shared team views. Self-hosted installations have no inbox, no Mate and no routines.
</AcquisitionCallout>

## How it connects to the rest

- **Inbox (Pro and higher):** follow-ups go out from the original channel, and a thread links to the contact through its participants, so the history shows on the record.
- **MCP:** your own AI client, such as Claude or ChatGPT, can read the same views and threads through [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] MCP tools and save drafts. This works self-hosted too, minus the inbox. See the [MCP guide](/docs/mcp).
- **Webhooks and n8n:** if you need a real notification, for example a message in your team chat when a Next contact date passes, run that in n8n or your own scheduler against the REST API. n8n is a separate product with its own plans. See [workflow automation](/features/workflow-automation).

## FAQ

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="What is the best app for customer follow-up?">
It depends on how you follow up. If you send the same series of emails to many leads, you want an outreach tool built for that. If you follow up personally with a few dozen clients across email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp, you want one list of who is waiting and the conversation history next to it. Customermates is built for the second case.
</FaqItem>
<FaqItem question="Is there a CRM that finds deals in email conversations and reminds me to follow up?">
Customermates does not send reminders. In Customermates Cloud, a routine triggered by incoming email can ask Mate to check whether the conversation belongs to an existing deal, update that deal and set its Next contact date, within the routine limits in the [routines guide](/docs/app-routines#what-are-the-limits). The deal then appears in your follow-up view. The inbox filter for threads you sent last covers the rest.
</FaqItem>
<FaqItem question="Does Customermates have follow-up reminders or due dates?">
No. There are no notifications, and tasks have no built-in due date. Add a custom Date column and sort a saved view by it. If you need a push notification, n8n or another scheduler can read that date through the API and notify you.
</FaqItem>
<FaqItem question="Can I automate follow-up emails?">
Not as a sequence. A routine in Customermates Cloud can write individual drafts on a schedule, and you send them after a read. It can also send if the instruction says so, one message per conversation. Bulk or multi-step cadences need a dedicated sequencing tool.
</FaqItem>
<FaqItem question="How do I track follow-ups when self-hosting?">
Use the Next contact column and saved views, which are part of the open-source core. There is no inbox, Mate or routine when self-hosting, so drafting runs through your own AI client over MCP, and timed actions through webhooks and n8n.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

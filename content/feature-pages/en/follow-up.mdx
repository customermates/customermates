## What Follow-Up Management Means in Customermates

Customermates gives teams ordinary CRM tasks and relationship context. A task has a type, name, notes, assignee, and links to contacts, organizations, deals, or services. It appears in the task views and can also be created or updated through the REST API and MCP tools.

The current Task model has no built-in due-date field, reminder schedule, overdue state, or notification transport. Customermates therefore does not claim that it will wake up later and remind a user on its own. If a workflow needs dates, store the relevant date in a custom field on the related CRM record and let a separately operated scheduler decide when to act.

## A Practical Follow-Up Workflow

1. Create a task that states the next action clearly.
2. Assign it to the responsible workspace member.
3. Link it to the relevant contact, organization, deal, or service.
4. Record the outcome in notes or update the related record after the conversation.
5. Use views, filters, and ownership to review open work with the team.

Customermates provides two mechanisms for managing follow-ups: tasks linked to the record, with a due-date column you define, and webhooks that drive multi-channel sequences in your own n8n instance. There is no built-in reminder that notifies you on its own.

**Task-based follow-ups.** Create a task linked to a contact or deal, add the subject and assignee, and store any follow-up date or priority in custom fields your team defines. Customermates has no native task due date, overdue state, deadline, or priority; filtering and external workflows can use stored values.

**Externally triggered reminders.** A separate workflow can evaluate recorded activity or customer-defined dates through the API or webhooks, then create a supported task or send a notification through an external provider. Customermates itself does not monitor inactivity or send reminder notifications.

**External multi-channel workflows.** Use a separate n8n instance and delivery providers to orchestrate a customer-defined cadence. The native inbox supports individual messages on email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Telegram, but Customermates has no sequence engine, bulk outreach, or telephony.

**Behavior-based external triggers.** A separate email or analytics provider can emit engagement events, and a customer-run workflow can request an individual follow-up while interest is fresh. Customermates has no native email-open, web-visit, or behavior-trigger sequence engine.

**Recorded follow-up context.** Supported messages, notes, and task changes remain with relevant CRM context where they are recorded. Review that history before reaching out again; do not assume activity from every external channel or workflow is captured automatically.

**Team follow-up context.** Team members can review linked tasks and filter on stored custom values. Native tasks do not have due or overdue states, and saved views are personal rather than shared team views.

**Calendar and email context.** Read supported calendar data and work with individual email conversations in the unified inbox. Calendar access is read-only, and Customermates does not schedule follow-up emails or create bookings.

**Self-hostable application and database.** Run the Customermates application and PostgreSQL database on your infrastructure. Connected workflow, AI, and messaging providers still receive the data your configuration sends.

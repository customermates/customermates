A simple CRM is one your team keeps updating because there is little to learn and the structure matches how you already work. Customermates gets there with a small data model: lists such as contacts, organizations, deals, services and tasks, fields you choose, and a deal pipeline with stages you name. It suits small client-facing teams such as consultants, agencies and founders, and it costs [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per user per month on Starter. This page covers ease of use and setup; if you are evaluating Customermates for a small service business, the [small business CRM](/for/smb) page shows the commercial picture and an example workspace.

Simple also means some things are missing on purpose. The limits are listed further down, so you can rule Customermates out quickly if you need them.

## What a new workspace contains

Most "simple CRM" pages promise a short setup. Here is what actually happens after sign-up. A new workspace starts without lists. On Customermates Cloud, the AI workspace setup asks about your business and builds lists, fields, deal stages, a dashboard and Knowledge Base pages from your answers. It runs once during onboarding, within a credit cap. You can skip it with **Start with an empty workspace** and build everything yourself. Self-hosted installations have no AI setup, so you create the lists in **Configure**, which needs the Admin role or **Edit** on the **Data model** row.

Whatever the setup produced is a starting point, not a fixed template. Stages are options of a single choice field, so you rename them, add new ones or delete them. A stage can carry a win probability, set as **Probability %** on the field in **Configure**. Customermates uses it for a deterministic weighted value per deal and a weighted pipeline total; it is a stage weight you set, not a prediction.

What else keeps the setup short:

- **Your own words for records.** In **Configure** you rename any list freely, for example Clients instead of Contacts, Projects or Jobs instead of Deals, Follow-ups instead of Tasks, and you can add lists of your own. Records and relationships stay connected.
- **One list surface for everything.** Every list opens as a table or a board, with search, filters, grouping and sorting under the same toolbar. See [records in the app](/docs/app-records).
- **Views remember your setup.** Filters, sort, layout, grouping and columns save together as a named view that appears as a chip. Changes stay temporary until you save them to the view. Views are personal to their creator, so each person keeps their own.

## Set it up in one sitting

<Steps>
<Step title="Create the workspace">
Sign up and complete the profile step. On Customermates Cloud, describe your business to the AI workspace setup, or choose **Start with an empty workspace** to skip it.
</Step>
<Step title="Shape the lists to your business">
Open **Configure** and create or rename lists under **General** so they use your team's words, then edit the stage options and probabilities on your deals list. A consultancy might use Projects with the stages Enquiry, Scoping, Proposal sent, Won and Lost.
</Step>
<Step title="Import one record type">
Use **More actions › Add from file** on the contacts list and map the spreadsheet columns to fields. The wizard reads `.xlsx` workbooks, not CSV, so save other spreadsheet formats as `.xlsx` first. Import organizations before contacts and deals, so the links can match by name. Our [Excel CRM template guide](/blog/excel-crm-template) shows a sheet layout that maps cleanly.
</Step>
<Step title="Add the one field you will miss">
Tasks have no built-in due date. If you work with follow-up dates, add a Date field such as "Next step" to deals or tasks in **Configure** with **Add › Field**, then save a view sorted by it.
</Step>
<Step title="Invite the team">
Send invitations from **Settings › Members**. New members wait for approval until an Admin, or a member whose role can manage users, assigns a role and sets them to active.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What this simple CRM leaves out

<AcquisitionCallout title="Check these before you commit" variant="boundary">
No reminders or notifications for tasks, and no recurring tasks. No mobile app; the web app runs in the browser. No email sequences or bulk sending. No file attachments on records, only images and tables inside notes. No shared team views. No built-in visual workflow builder.
</AcquisitionCallout>

Automation exists, but it lives in two separate places. In Customermates Cloud, [Routines](/docs/app-routines) run a saved instruction through Mate on a schedule or when a record changes, using hosted AI credits. A routine runs as its owner and sees what that owner sees. For rule-based flows you connect an n8n instance you run or subscribe to separately, through webhooks, the REST API or the community node.

If your team depends on push reminders on a phone, or wants a free plan for good, one of the tools below is likely a better start.

## How it connects when you need more

The simple start does not lock anything away. The REST API and webhooks are on every plan, and your own AI client (Claude, ChatGPT and others) can read and update records through [MCP](/docs/mcp). In Customermates Cloud, Mate prepares summaries and reply drafts and uses hosted AI credits. The cloud database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region; the [subprocessors](/subprocessors) page lists the providers involved, including US ones, and a [DPA](/dpa) is available.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="What is the easiest CRM to use?">
The easiest one is the one whose structure already matches your work. Look for a pipeline you can name in your own terms, few required fields and import from the spreadsheet you have. On Customermates Cloud, the AI workspace setup builds a first set of lists and stages from your description; you can edit them like any custom field and rename lists to your own terms. Test any CRM with your real data during the trial rather than judging the demo.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a free version of Customermates?">
There is no free cloud plan. The cloud has a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial without a credit card. The open-source core under AGPL-3.0 can be self-hosted without licence fees, with the Starter feature set and without the inbox or Mate. If you need free hosting as well, HubSpot and CentralStationCRM offer free plans with user and contact limits.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I switch from Excel to Customermates?">
Yes. Import each record type from an `.xlsx` workbook, matching your spreadsheet columns to fields and custom fields in the import wizard. You can edit an exported workbook and import it back as **Existing records** to update them. CSV files and Google Sheets need to be saved as `.xlsx` first, and attachments do not transfer.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How much does a simple CRM cost per month?">
For Customermates, a three-person team pays [[commercial.price.starter.monthly.seats.3]] per month on Starter, or [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.3]] on Pro with the unified inbox. Other simple CRMs range from free plans with limits to a flat per-user price, as in the comparison table above.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does a simple CRM make sense for one person?">
Yes, if you have more than a handful of open deals or clients and lose track of who you talked to last. A solo consultant on Starter gets the full record model and a pipeline. If you mostly need a contact list, a spreadsheet may still be enough.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Do I need technical skills?">
No. Adding a field, renaming stages and importing a workbook happen in the app. Technical skills only come in if you connect n8n, use the REST API or self-host.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

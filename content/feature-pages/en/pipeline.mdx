Sales pipeline software puts your open opportunities on a board, one column per stage, so you see what is in play, what it is worth and which deal needs the next step. Customermates is CRM software for small sales teams whose deals depend on conversations: consultancies, agencies, service businesses and founders who sell themselves. It is open source, AI-native and made in Germany.

<ArticleSummary title="At a glance">
  <SummaryItem label="Every plan">Opportunity board grouped by stage, stage probabilities, value and weighted value per column, personal saved views, dashboard widgets, Excel import and export, REST, webhooks and MCP. Self-hosted included.</SummaryItem>
  <SummaryItem label="Cloud only">Mate, the hosted assistant, and routines within your plan's AI credits. The unified inbox from Pro.</SummaryItem>
  <SummaryItem label="Not included">No predictive forecasting, no pipeline velocity or stage conversion analytics, no idle-deal highlighting, no shared or team views, no field-level permissions.</SummaryItem>

## See it in an example workspace

The [sales pipeline workspace](https://demo.customermates.com/en/w/sales-pipeline/dashboard) is an example with sample data, not a customer account. It has five lists: Accounts, Contacts, Opportunities, Products and Tasks. Opportunities move through New, Qualified, Discovery, Proposal sent, Negotiation, Won and Lost, and three sales reps own them.

The board shows each stage with its probability. Every column header sums the value and the weighted value of its opportunities. An opportunity's value comes from its line items: products from the Products list, quantity times unit price. Open a card and you are on the opportunity with its account, its contacts, its line items, its tasks and the next step recorded on it. Quotes sit on the opportunity as PDF files in a File field.

The dashboard shows the expected closes of the month in a **Month overview** widget, a calendar that sums the value per day, so you see which weeks carry the most value, and places the accounts on a map by their address. A calendar view of the opportunities shows the expected close dates by month, and the Calendar page puts the site visits from the task list next to them.

Your own workspace does not start with these lists or stages. A new workspace starts without lists. On Customermates Cloud, the AI workspace setup builds lists, stages, a dashboard and Knowledge Base pages from your description. It runs once during onboarding, is capped by setup credits, and you can skip it with **Start with an empty workspace**. Otherwise, people with Configure rights create the opportunities list and its stage field in **Configure**. When self-hosting, that is the only way.

## How the board works

**Stages are a field you own.** A stage is a single choice option, and each option carries a win probability from 0 to 100 percent. Rename, reorder, add or remove stages in **Configure**; a preview shows the effect on calculations and widgets before you save. Renaming a stage keeps it on every record.

**The board.** In **Appearance**, choose **Board** grouped by stage. Drag a card to another column and its stage changes. Cards show the fields you choose, such as value, account, assigned users or a next-step date.

**Column headers do the arithmetic.** Each header shows the count and the sums you pick, such as value and weighted value. Weighted value is the opportunity's value times its stage probability. Three example opportunities:

| Opportunity | Stage (probability) | Value | Weighted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Website relaunch | Qualified (20%) | 5,000 | 1,000 |
| Brand workshop | Proposal sent (60%) | 8,000 | 4,800 |
| Annual retainer | Negotiation (80%) | 12,000 | 9,600 |
| **Open pipeline** | | **25,000** | **15,400** |

That total weights your own numbers. It does not predict revenue or close dates. The probabilities above are examples, not defaults.

**Value.** An opportunity carries a currency value. When you sell from a catalog of services or products, line items can add up to the value instead: quantity times unit price per line.

**Table view.** Switch to **Table** and keep the grouping: stages become row groups with their own counts and sums, and you can sort by value or weighted value.

## From board to next step

<Steps>
  <Step title="See opportunities by stage">Open the board view of your opportunities and scan the column totals.</Step>
  <Step title="Inspect one deal">Open a card to see the account, contacts, tasks and notes linked to it.</Step>
  <Step title="Record the next action">Fill a date field such as **Next step** and add a task. Customermates has no built-in reminders, so a saved view filtered to passed dates does that job.</Step>
  <Step title="Adapt fields and stages">Add the fields you sort by, such as **Expected close** (date) or **Lost reason** (single choice), and adjust probabilities when your experience changes.</Step>
  <Step title="Let AI help where permitted">Ask Mate in the cloud, or your own AI client through MCP, to update a stage or list deals without a next step.</Step>
</Steps>

## Customer context next to the deal

An opportunity links to its account and contacts, so the people and the history are one click away. On Customermates Cloud, Pro and higher add the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) for Gmail, Outlook, IMAP, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram. A participant is matched to a contact by the identity in its Contact details field. A conversation is linked to an opportunity or account only when you or an agent do it explicitly; nothing is filed to a deal automatically.

Inbox limits by plan: Starter and self-hosted have no connected messaging accounts. Pro covers your own accounts, with [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] connected account per user included, plus threads shared individually. Sharing a whole account with the team needs Business, Max or Enterprise.

## Keeping the pipeline current

**A stale-deals view.** Filter opportunities on **Updated at** older than 14 days and stage not Won or Lost, and save it. Any edit counts as an update, not only a stage change.

**A weekly routine (cloud only).** A [routine](/docs/app-routines) runs Mate on a schedule or when a record changes, owned by one user and seeing what that user sees, within the owner's AI credits:

```text
Find opportunities in Proposal sent or Negotiation that were not updated in the last 14 days. For each one, create a task "Check in: [name]", link it to the opportunity and assign it to its owner. Do not send any messages.
```

Mate creates and updates records and sends messages as soon as it is asked, so say in the instruction what it must not do. Deletions, imports and access changes stop for approval, and an unattended routine declines them. Mate is not available when self-hosting.

**Your own AI client through MCP.** On every plan, self-hosted included, Claude, ChatGPT or another [MCP](/docs/mcp) client can read opportunities and update stages and fields your role allows, billed by your AI provider. A stage change from any source fires a `record.updated` webhook that a separate n8n workflow can pick up.

## Working as a team

Everyone whose role reads all opportunities sees the same deals; with **Assigned** read access, a person sees only their own. Three limits matter for teams:

- Saved views and dashboards are personal. There are no shared or team views, so each colleague saves their own board.
- Permissions apply to whole lists. There are no field-level permissions, so a person who can read opportunities sees every field on them.
- Whole-account inbox sharing starts on Business. On Pro, colleagues share single threads.

## Limits

<AcquisitionCallout title="What the pipeline does not do" variant="boundary">
  Weighted value is your value times your probabilities. There is no predictive forecast, no report of time in stage, stage conversion or pipeline velocity, and no automatic highlighting of idle deals. No shared or team views, no field-level permissions and no native mobile app.
</AcquisitionCallout>

For a metric such as win rate per person, Mate in the cloud can calculate it from your records, or you export the list as an Excel workbook. Chart options are on the [reporting](/features/reporting) page.

## Running more than one pipeline

There is no separate pipeline object. A second pipeline is a second single choice field, for example **Renewal stage**, with its own board view. Weighted value comes from the stage field with probabilities, so a second board shows counts and totals but no weighted figures.

## Which kind of tool fits

| Your situation | Look at |

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Does a new workspace come with pipeline stages?">
No. A new workspace starts without lists. On Customermates Cloud the AI workspace setup builds an opportunities list with stages from your description during onboarding. Otherwise you create the list and its stage field in Configure.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Customermates forecast revenue?">
No. Weighted value multiplies each opportunity's value by the probability you set for its stage. That is arithmetic on your own numbers, not a prediction, and there are no velocity or conversion analytics.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can my whole team work from the same board?">
Everyone with read access to all opportunities sees the same deals. Saved views are personal, so each colleague saves their own board view. There are no field-level permissions.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I have several pipelines?">
Yes, as separate single choice fields, each with its own board view.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Are emails logged to the deal automatically?">
No. On Pro and higher in the cloud, conversations are matched to contacts by their contact details. Linking a conversation to an opportunity is an explicit step by you or an agent.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

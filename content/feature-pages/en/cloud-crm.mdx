**Cloud CRM software** is customer-relationship software that the vendor hosts and runs, so your team uses it in a browser and installs, patches and backs up nothing itself. It is also called cloud-based CRM or hosted CRM. Customermates is one: a managed cloud CRM whose primary database is configured in Frankfurt, built on an open-source core that you can also self-host with Docker.

**What the cloud changes for follow-up.** When the CRM runs in the cloud, your LinkedIn and email conversations can sit on the client's record and Mate can draft the reply from there. On Customermates Cloud that starts with Pro, and the free trial runs [[commercial.trial.days]] days on Pro. A self-hosted core keeps client records and MCP but has no inbox and no Mate, so drafted follow-up is a cloud feature.

## What is cloud CRM software?

Cloud CRM software keeps your contacts, companies, deals and conversations on servers that the vendor runs. You open it in a browser, the vendor ships updates to every customer, and you pay a subscription, usually per user and month. Older names such as web-based CRM or CRM on demand describe the same idea.

"Cloud" describes where and by whom the software is run. "SaaS" (software as a service) describes how it is supplied and billed. The two overlap so often that vendors use them interchangeably, but they are not definitions of the same thing.

**What you gain**

- No servers and no installation: the vendor runs the application, the database, backups and updates.
- Access from any browser, and a new colleague needs only an account.
- Seats follow the team: monthly plans let you add or remove users.

**What you give up or must check**

- You depend on the vendor's operation, roadmap and pricing.
- Your data sits with the vendor and its subprocessors. Where, and under which contract, is yours to check.
- Leaving works only if the export does.

## Cloud vs on-premises vs self-hosted CRM: what is the difference?

Three setups get mixed up. A cloud (SaaS) CRM is run by the vendor. An on-premises CRM is licensed software that runs on servers you own or rent and that your IT team operates. A self-hosted CRM is software, often open source, that you install and run yourself on a server of your choice, including a rented one.

| Question                 | Cloud CRM (SaaS)                                  | On-premises CRM                                          | Self-hosted CRM                                                      |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Who runs the servers?    | The vendor                                        | Your IT team, on hardware you own or rent                | You, or a hosting provider you choose                                |
| Updates and patches      | The vendor rolls them out                         | You schedule, test and apply them                        | You pull and apply new versions                                      |
| Where is the data?       | The vendor's regions and subprocessors            | On your premises                                         | On the server you choose                                             |
| Cost model               | Subscription, usually per user                    | Licence or maintenance fee plus hardware and staff       | Often no licence fee for open-source software, plus infrastructure and operator time |
| Control                  | What the vendor lets you configure                | Full control of servers and database                     | Full control of servers and database; features depend on the edition |
| Exit and export          | Depends on the vendor's export tools and contract | Direct database access                                   | Direct database access plus the product's export                     |
| Typical fit              | Teams without IT capacity that want to start fast | Organisations with internal hosting rules and IT staff   | Teams that must or want to control where data lives and can operate it |

Open source is a licence, not a hosting model: an open-source CRM can be offered as a cloud service or run by you. The guide to [open-source CRM options](/blog/open-source-crm) compares the self-hostable ones, and [self-hosted CRM with Docker and PostgreSQL](/features/self-hosted) shows the Customermates side.

A fair rule of thumb: choose a cloud CRM unless a concrete requirement points elsewhere, such as an internal hosting policy or a team that already operates its own servers.

## What are the four types of CRM, and where does cloud fit?

CRM types describe the job, not the hosting. The four usually named are operational (daily sales, marketing and service work), analytical (reports and segments), collaborative (one common customer history across people and channels) and strategic (customer-centred planning). Any of them can be delivered as cloud CRM software, on-premises or self-hosted. The guide to [CRM systems and their types](/blog/crm-systems) explains each with examples; if the term is new, start with [what CRM means](/blog/what-is-crm).

## How do I choose cloud-based CRM software?

Judge a cloud-based CRM on these points and test the last one with real data before you commit.

| Check                  | Ask the vendor                                                                                                   | Why it matters                                                                         |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Data location and DPA  | Where is the primary database, which subprocessors touch the data, and is a data processing agreement part of the contract? | A region setting is not a residency guarantee. The contract is what binds.      |
| Export and exit        | Which formats (CSV, JSON, Excel), how many records per export, and what happens to your data after cancellation? | You can only leave a CRM that lets your data leave. |
| API and integrations   | Is there a documented REST API, are there webhooks, and how do automation tools connect?                         | Decides whether the CRM can feed your other systems without a vendor project.          |
| Price per user         | What does the plan with the features you need cost per user and month, how is it billed, and what changes at the next tier? | The entry price is rarely the price of the plan you need. [CRM cost](/blog/crm-cost) has a worked example. |
| Availability           | Does the contract state an availability figure, how is it measured and what is excluded?                         | A marketing claim is not a contract term.                                              |
| Access control         | Roles per list, an activity log, and single sign-on if you need it                                               | Decides who may see and change which records.                                          |
| Real-data test         | Import about 50 of your records, build your pipeline, then export them again                                     | Shows fit and exit in an afternoon instead of in a year.                               |

For a dated side-by-side of ten tools with licence, hosting, free tier and price, see the [CRM software comparison](/blog/best-crm-software).

### What Customermates Cloud includes

The managed product brings contacts, organizations, deals, services and tasks into one workspace. Teams define typed custom fields, work in table, card and kanban views, inspect activity history and configure deal stages with probabilities. Dashboards show current operational values, including total and weighted deal or pipeline values. REST, webhooks and MCP serve supported integrations and externally operated workflows; they do not mean every accounting, marketing or data provider is built in.

### Data location, DPA and subprocessors

The primary production database is configured in AWS Frankfurt (`eu-central-1`). This is a selected product setting, not a contractual promise that every service or transfer remains in that region. Application hosting, monitoring, connected-account transport and any provider you select have their own locations and transfer paths.

The [Data Processing Agreement](/dpa) documents measures including TLS in transit, encryption for the production database and backups at rest, role-based access, account-level segregation, monitoring and restoration procedures. It does not claim a security or compliance certification. The current [subprocessor list](/subprocessors) records the providers, purposes, known locations and transfer bases used by the managed service.

### Export and exit

Each list exports as an Excel workbook or as JSON from **More actions**, up to 5,000 records per export, and a list imports again from its JSON export or from an Excel workbook through a column-mapping wizard; the [records guide](/docs/app-records#how-do-i-import-or-export-records) has the limits. Over the REST API an export pages through the list, up to 500 records per page.

There is no whole-account export, no transfer of file attachments, no scheduled sync and no undo for an import, so plan a larger move from or to another CRM as a migration project with explicit mapping, validation and deduplication, extended through REST or MCP where needed. Under section 5 of the [Terms](/terms) you can also ask in text form, during the contract or within 30 days after it ends, for the data Customermates holds for you in CSV or JSON, free of charge and within 30 days.

### AI and automation

Every managed-cloud plan includes a Mate entitlement and a plan-specific monthly hosted-AI credit allowance per active user. Whether Mate is live depends on the hosted environment's current configuration. When enabled, Mate works inside the CRM, uses the current user's permissions and applies separate confirmation controls to sensitive actions.

External AI clients can use the [Customermates MCP server](/docs/mcp) with a model provider and subscription you choose, on every plan and in self-hosted installations. They use your provider rather than hosted credits. For automation, teams can call REST, receive webhooks, use MCP or connect a separately operated workflow tool such as n8n. Customermates includes neither the n8n runtime nor its third-party connectors.

## Where is Customermates the wrong choice?

Customermates is deliberately narrow. Choose another product, or add one next to it, when you need:

- **No native mobile app.** Customermates is used in the browser, so choose another product if you need an app for your phone.
- **Single sign-on.** SAML or OIDC sign-in with your own identity provider is not implemented, in the cloud or self-hosted.
- **A certification.** The Data Processing Agreement documents measures, but Customermates holds and claims no security or compliance certification. EU-region hosting is a hosting fact, not a certification.
- **Quotes, invoicing, telephony, SMS or embeddable web forms.** None of these exist. REST, webhooks or n8n can connect dedicated tools.
- **Email sequences or mass outreach.** Outreach is draft-first and per message.
- **Predictive forecasting, lead scoring or conversion analytics.** Dashboards, activity history, stage probabilities and weighted deal or pipeline values describe the current workspace. They are not forecasts.
- **A complete ERP, accounting, inventory, helpdesk or call-centre suite, or a turnkey migration.** Those need different products or a deliberately designed integration.

Customermates Cloud fits teams that want a managed, browser-based CRM for flexible records and pipelines, prefer explicit interfaces over a closed integration promise, and value a separately self-hostable open-source core.

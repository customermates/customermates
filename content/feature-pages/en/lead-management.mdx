Lead management software keeps every potential client in one list, shows where each one stands and who is on it, and records how they found you. Customermates does this for small client-facing teams: a lead is a contact with a stage field, a source field and an assigned teammate, and once it is qualified you open a deal for it in the [pipeline](/features/pipeline).

What it leaves out matters just as much. Customermates scores no leads, enriches no records, hosts no web forms and routes nothing round-robin. If your leads arrive by the hundred from ads and forms, you need those engines, and the table further down names tools that have them.

## What lead management covers, and which parts Customermates does

| Step | In Customermates | Needs another tool |
|---|---|---|
| Capture | Manual entry, Excel import, linking an inbox conversation to a new contact, REST, MCP | Web forms, chat widgets, ad lead forms |
| Track | A stage field on contacts, deal stages with fixed weights, change history | |
| Qualify | Your own criteria as custom fields, filled by people, Mate or your AI client | Automatic scores and firmographic data |
| Assign | Assigned users on every contact and deal, set by hand or by an instruction | Round-robin or territory routing |
| Follow up | Replies from the contact's own channel, drafts by Mate in Customermates Cloud | Multi-step email sequences |
| Measure | Dashboard charts counting leads and deal value by any single-select field | Funnel conversion rates and multi-touch attribution |

For a consultancy, an agency or a founder who gets 10 to 50 new enquiries a month, the left column is usually the whole job. The right column belongs to marketing teams with high inbound volume.

## How leads get into Customermates

- **By hand.** **Add** on the contacts list, a few fields, done.
- **From a conversation (Pro and higher).** When an unknown address writes to your connected inbox, open **Thread settings**, click **Link** next to the person and choose **Create "…"**. Every later message from that address shows on the new contact. See the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox).
- **From a spreadsheet.** Save your list as an .xlsx workbook and use **Import and export › Add from file**. Import organizations first, then contacts. A lead list bought from or built by an outside lead generation agency comes in the same way.
- **From a website form you already run.** Your form tool or a separate [n8n workflow](/features/workflow-automation) calls the REST API and creates the contact with its source value. Customermates has no form builder of its own.
- **From your AI client.** Claude, ChatGPT or another MCP client can create contacts from a pasted email signature or event list, with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools available.

## Setting it up: a three-person consultancy

A new workspace already has a single-select field **Sales Pipeline** on contacts with the options New, Contact, Qualified, In Progress, Won and Lost, and a **Stage** field on deals. You can rename both or edit the options in place.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name leads as leads">Under **My Company › Settings › Data model**, switch Contacts to **Leads** if your team talks that way. The change applies to the whole workspace.</Step>
  <Step title="Add two fields on contacts">**Source** (Single Select: Referral, Website, LinkedIn, Event, Partner) and **Fit** (Single Select: Strong, Unclear, Poor). Keep the options few, so filters stay useful.</Step>
  <Step title="Save two personal views">"New and unassigned": **Sales Pipeline** is New and **Assigned** has none. "My open leads": **Assigned** is you and **Sales Pipeline** is Contact, Qualified or In Progress, sorted by **Updated at**, oldest first.</Step>
  <Step title="Work the board">Switch "My open leads" to the board, grouped by **Sales Pipeline**, and drag a card when the conversation moves on.</Step>
  <Step title="Open a deal when it gets serious">Once a lead asks for a proposal, create a deal, link the contact and its organization, and add the services you would sell. The deal's value comes from those services and their quantities.</Step>
</Steps>

Saved views belong to the person who made them, so each colleague saves their own. The [records guide](/docs/app-records) documents filters, grouping and import.

## Tracking leads after the first reply

A lead and its deal are separate records. There is no convert button: the contact stays in the list with its full history, and the deal carries the money.

The default deal stages carry weights of 10, 20, 40, 60 and 80 percent for Prospecting through Negotiation, 100 for Won and 0 for Lost. With them, every deal gets a weighted value and the workspace shows a **Weighted pipeline** next to the **Total pipeline**. Those are fixed stage weights you set, not a prediction.

On the dashboard, a chart widget over contacts grouped by **Source** shows how many leads each channel brought. The same chart over deals, with a Source field on deals and the metric set to deal value, shows which channels bring money. Conversion rates between stages are not calculated; export the list to Excel if you want them.

To track leads in other systems as changes happen, webhooks fire on `contact.created`, `contact.updated` and `deal.updated`, among [[derived.webhooks.events.total]] events. A Slack message about a new lead runs through n8n or your own endpoint; there is no native Slack integration. Every change to a lead lands in its history, which roles with **Audit Log** read access can see.

## Mate and routines on new leads (Customermates Cloud)

In Customermates Cloud, Mate, the hosted assistant, uses hosted AI credits and works on leads in plain language: "Show me every lead with Source LinkedIn and Sales Pipeline Contact that was last updated more than two weeks ago, and draft a short follow-up in each lead's conversation. Do not send anything."

A [routine](/docs/app-routines) runs a saved instruction on its own. Set the trigger to **When something changes** with the event **Contact Created**, and give it an instruction such as:

```text
A new lead was just created. Read the contact and any linked conversation. If Source is empty and the conversation shows where they came from, set Source. Set Fit to Unclear if it is empty. Draft a reply in the conversation but do not send it. Do not change Assigned.
```

The routine runs as its owner and sees what that person sees. Mate sends messages without asking for approval when an instruction tells it to, so a routine that should only draft has to say so, as this one does. Starter includes [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] routine per user, Pro [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]].

Self-hosted, there is no Mate. Your own AI client does the same reading and writing through MCP, billed by your AI provider.

## What it does not do

- No lead scoring and no data enrichment. A Fit field holds what your team decides.
- No embedded web forms or chat widgets.
- No automatic assignment rules. A routine can follow your written rule ("assign leads from Event to Jana"), but it is an instruction, not a routing engine.
- No email sequences, drip campaigns or mass email. Follow-ups are single messages.
- No reminders or due dates on tasks. Put a date in a custom field and filter on it.
- No mobile app. The web app works on narrow screens.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="What is lead management software?">
Software that holds every potential customer in one place with their status, source, owner and history, so nobody is forgotten between first contact and signed deal. Larger suites add scoring, forms, routing and nurture campaigns on top.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What is the difference between lead tracking and lead management?">
Lead tracking records where a lead came from and where it stands now. Lead management also covers capturing, qualifying, assigning and following up. In Customermates both happen on the contact: Source answers the first question, the stage field the second.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What is the best CRM for lead management?">
It depends on volume. With hundreds of form leads a month, scoring and routing matter, and HubSpot Sales Hub or the paid editions of Zoho CRM offer them. With a few dozen enquiries that turn into conversations, the stage, the source and the message history matter more, which is what Customermates is built around.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can Customermates integrate lead tracking with my website or ads?">
Through the REST API, n8n or your form tool's own webhook, yes: they create the contact and write UTM or campaign values into custom fields. Customermates has no form builder and no ad platform connection of its own.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I import leads from an external lead generation provider?">
Yes. Save the delivered list as an .xlsx workbook and import it, or let the provider create contacts through the API. Customermates does not generate or buy leads.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there free lead management software?">
HubSpot's free CRM tools allow 2 users and 1,000 contacts, Zoho CRM's free edition 3 users and 5,000 records. Customermates has no free Cloud plan, but its AGPL core runs [self-hosted](/features/self-hosted) without a licence fee, without the inbox and without Mate.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

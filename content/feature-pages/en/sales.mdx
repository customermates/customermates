Sales CRM software is where a team records every open deal with its value, its stage and the people involved, so the next step on each one is visible to everybody and not only to whoever made the last call. Customermates is a sales CRM for small client-facing teams: consultancies, agencies, other service businesses and founders who still do the selling. Deals sit on a board by stage with a win probability per stage, and on Pro and higher the client's emails and LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram or Telegram messages appear on their contact record.

It is not an outbound machine. If your team books meetings by volume, the table of alternatives further down is the more useful part of this page.

## What the sales side of Customermates does

**Deals and their value.** A deal links to contacts, organizations, assigned users, tasks and the services you sell. Its value is calculated rather than typed: the price of each linked service times its quantity on that deal. Two workshop days at 1,500 plus ten coaching hours at 150 give a deal value of 4,500 in your workspace currency.

**Stages with win probabilities.** The stage is a single-select column called Stage. A new workspace starts with seven options and probabilities already filled in: Prospecting 10%, Qualification 20%, Demo 40%, Proposal 60%, Negotiation 80%, Won 100% and Lost 0%. You can rename, add and reorder stages, and change the probabilities under **My Company › Settings**. Each deal's weighted value is its value times the probability of its current stage.

**The board.** Switch the deals list to the board, grouped by Stage, and drag a card to move a deal. Each column header shows the number of deals, the stage probability and the total value next to the weighted value. Group the same board by **Assigned** and every person gets a column with their own count and totals; that board is read-only. The [pipeline page](/features/pipeline) shows the board in more detail.

**Saved views.** Filters, sort, grouping and layout save together as a named view, such as "My open deals by value". Views are personal, so each colleague sets the same filters and saves their own view.

**Dashboard charts.** A chart counts deals or sums deal value or weighted deal value, grouped by a single-select field such as Stage or Lead source. Charts belong to the person who made them, and **Share as a template** lets teammates start their own copy.

**Conversations on the contact (Pro and higher).** Connect Gmail, Outlook, IMAP, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram or Telegram in the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox). Once a contact carries the email address or handle, every conversation with it shows on that contact's timeline. Messages link to contacts rather than directly to deals, so from a deal the latest conversation is one click away, on the linked contact.

**AI help.** In Customermates Cloud, Mate reads a deal, its notes and the conversations with its contacts, then writes a summary, a reply draft or the update you ask for, using hosted AI credits. Your own AI client, such as Claude or ChatGPT, does the same through MCP with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools, on every plan and when self-hosting.

## Set up a sales pipeline in Customermates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Adjust the stages">Open any deal, press **Customize** and edit the Stage column. Rename the options to match how you sell, for example "Discovery call" and "Proposal sent". Then check each stage's win probability under **My Company › Settings**.</Step>
  <Step title="Create your services">Add what you sell under **Services**, each with a price (**Amount**): a retainer, a workshop day, a licence. Deal values are built from these.</Step>
  <Step title="Add three custom fields to deals">A Date field "Expected close", a Single Select "Lead source" with Referral, LinkedIn, Website and Event, and a Single Select "Lost reason" with Price, Timing, Chose a competitor and No decision.</Step>
  <Step title="Import what you already have">Use **Add from file** with an Excel workbook, one record type at a time: organizations first, then contacts, then deals. Link columns are matched by record name, so an organization must exist before a deal can point to it. CSV files are not accepted, so save them as XLSX first.</Step>
  <Step title="Save two views">"My open deals": Assigned is you, Stage is not Won or Lost, sorted by value, shown as a board. "Closing soon": a filter on Expected close, shown as a table.</Step>
  <Step title="Add two dashboard charts">Weighted deal value grouped by Stage, and deal value grouped by Lead source with a filter on Stage set to Won.</Step>
  <Step title="Connect your inbox (Pro and higher)">Under **My Profile › Channels**, connect the mailbox or LinkedIn account you sell from.</Step>
</Steps>

The [records guide](/docs/app-records) covers import, views and the board step by step.

## Tracking sales without a spreadsheet

The questions a weekly sales meeting asks, and where the answers sit after the setup above:

| Question | Where you find the answer |
|---|---|
| What is in the pipeline, and what is it worth weighted? | The column totals on the stage board, or **Total pipeline** and **Weighted pipeline** under **My Company › Settings** |
| Who is working on which deals? | The deals board grouped by **Assigned**. A deal with two assigned users appears under both. |
| Which deals should close soon? | The "Closing soon" view |
| Where do won deals come from? | The chart of won deal value by Lead source |
| Why do we lose? | A chart counting lost deals by Lost reason |
| What happened on this deal? | Its notes, its change history and, on Pro and higher, the conversations on its contacts |

Customermates does not calculate conversion rates, sales velocity or time in stage. In Customermates Cloud, Mate can work out a figure such as each person's win rate from your records with a read-only analysis tool, using hosted AI credits. Otherwise, export the deals as XLSX or read them through the REST API. [Reporting](/features/reporting) covers the chart options.

## Updating deals with Mate (Customermates Cloud)

After a call, paste your notes into Mate with an instruction like this one:

```text
Here are my notes from today's call with Bauer Consulting. Update the deal "Bauer website relaunch": set the stage to Proposal, set Expected close to 30 November and append the notes. Create a task for me to send the proposal and link it to the deal. Do not send any messages.
```

Mate makes these changes directly, and they land in the deal's history. Asked to send an email, it sends without asking again, so write "draft" when you want to read the text first. Deletions always need your approval. Your own AI client can run the same request through MCP; for jobs on a schedule or on deal changes, see [workflow automation](/features/workflow-automation).

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="What is the best sales CRM software for a small team?">
It depends on how you sell. Outbound teams that win on volume need sequences and forecasting, which HubSpot Sales Hub Professional and Pipedrive Growth include. Teams that sell through relationships, with a few dozen open deals and most of the work in email and LinkedIn conversations, need the deal and the conversation in one place. Customermates is built for that second case.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is a sales CRM hard to learn?">
Not if you keep the first version small. A new Customermates workspace already has seven deal stages with probabilities, so you can add deals on day one. The real work is agreeing on stage names and three or four custom fields, then importing what sits in your spreadsheet today.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can Customermates forecast revenue?">
No, not in the predictive sense. It calculates a weighted pipeline: each deal's value times the win probability you set for its stage. That is arithmetic on your own numbers. There is no model that learns from past deals or predicts close dates.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a free version?">
There is no free Cloud plan. You can try Customermates Cloud for [[commercial.trial.days]] days without a credit card. [Self-hosting](/features/self-hosted) the open-source core has no licence fee: it runs the Starter feature set on your own server, without the inbox, Mate or routines.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I see each salesperson's pipeline?">
Yes. Group the deals board by **Assigned** and each person gets a column with their deals, their count, total value and weighted value.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

A CRM for Claude is one that Claude can read and change itself, instead of one you copy text out of. Customermates gives Claude that through a connector: Claude looks up a client, reads the last conversation, updates the deal and writes the next task, in the same workspace your team opens in the browser.

## What you can ask Claude

- **"Which deals have had no contact for two weeks?"** Claude reads your pipeline and the conversations linked to each deal.
- **"Add the three people from this email to the right company and note what they asked for."** Claude creates the contacts and links them.
- **"Which conversations are waiting for my reply?"** With a mailbox or [LinkedIn connected](/features/claude-linkedin), Claude lists the open threads and can draft the answers.

Claude works with your own lists and fields, so the CRM uses your words for clients, deals and stages.

## What it does and what it does not

**It does:** read and write records, lists, fields and views with the permissions of the person who connected it; search the workspace's Knowledge Base; read conversations and save drafts once a channel is connected.

**It does not:** work around your roles and permissions, change anything without a call you can see in Claude, or act in the background while Claude is closed. For work on a schedule there are [routines](/docs/app-routines) with the built-in assistant Mate.

**On approval:** Customermates tells Claude to ask before it sends a message or deletes something. Whether Claude asks each time depends on the tool permissions you set in Claude.

The technical side, with every tool the connector offers, is in the [MCP guide](/docs/mcp).

## What it costs

The trial runs [[commercial.trial.days]] days on the Pro plan and needs no credit card. Paid plans start at [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per user and month for the CRM with the connector; the inbox for email and LinkedIn is part of Pro at [[commercial.price.pro.monthly]]. Without a subscription the workspace moves to the Free plan for [[commercial.limit.free.maxMembers]] person and [[commercial.limit.free.maxRecords]] records. The connector costs nothing extra, and Claude bills its own usage through your Claude plan.

## Who runs it

Customermates is an independent product. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Anthropic. The core of the CRM is open source under AGPL-3.0 and can be self-hosted; the cloud database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, and the [list of subprocessors](/subprocessors) names every provider involved.

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Does it work with ChatGPT and other AI clients too?">
Yes. The same connector works with ChatGPT, Cursor, Gemini and other clients that support MCP. The [connector guide](/docs/connect-custom-connector) has the steps for each.
</FaqItem>
<FaqItem question="Does Claude see all my data?">
Claude sees what the person who connected it may see. It acts with that person's role and permissions, in the workspace they chose at sign-in.
</FaqItem>
<FaqItem question="Do I need a paid Claude plan?">
Claude offers custom connectors on every plan; its free plan allows one. ChatGPT needs a paid plan for custom connectors.
</FaqItem>
<FaqItem question="Can I host it myself?">
The open-source core can be self-hosted and includes the connector. The inbox for email and LinkedIn and the built-in assistant Mate are part of Customermates Cloud.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

A **LinkedIn CRM integration** can mean very different things. Customermates provides three bounded surfaces: individual LinkedIn messages and InMail in the managed inbox; on-demand profile, post, engagement and connection-request operations; and customer-authorized Sales Navigator search and list operations through REST or MCP. It does not provide LinkedIn CRM Sync, bundled continuous enrichment, automatic profile matching or bulk lead import.

## LinkedIn messages in CRM context

An entitled cloud account can connect supported LinkedIn Classic, Sales Navigator or Recruiter variants. The unified inbox then presents supported conversations beside email and other connected channels. A teammate can read a thread, keep a draft, reply, or start an individual chat. Sales Navigator and Recruiter InMail use the subject and signature fields required by the connected product.

The participant can be associated with a Customermates contact, which is the bridge to related organizations, deals, services and tasks. A message is not linked directly to a deal, and the inbox is not a social-listening or campaign product.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect an entitled account">
    Use Customermates Cloud Pro or above and connect the LinkedIn product that
    your user is entitled to operate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open one conversation">
    Start with a real thread or individual message rather than a bulk list or
    automated sequence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Associate the participant">
    Connect the person in the conversation to the correct CRM contact so the
    relationship context is available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and send">
    Check the recipient, content and connected LinkedIn identity before a user
    or authorized client sends the message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Profiles, posts and relationship actions on demand

Through a connected LinkedIn account, an authorized REST or MCP client can retrieve a person or company profile, read posts, inspect comments and reactions, and list, send, accept or cancel connection requests. These are explicit, user-authorized operations against the connected provider account. They are useful when a teammate needs current context before a conversation or CRM update.

The returned provider data is not a bundled background enrichment feed. Customermates does not continuously monitor profiles, match LinkedIn identities to CRM records, write provider fields into contacts automatically, or alert on job changes. An authorized client can take an on-demand profile result and invoke a separate, permission-checked contact or organization create/update through REST or MCP, with field mapping, validation and duplicate handling defined by the user or workflow.

A customer-run workflow can also call an enrichment provider selected by the customer and write selected fields back through the Customermates REST API. The customer operates that provider and workflow; Customermates does not include the provider, source its data, choose the mapping or run a native continuous enrichment service.

<AcquisitionCallout title="On-demand context is not a bundled enrichment feed" variant="proof">
  A profile or post is fetched because an authorized user or client asks for it.
  A separate mapped CRM write or customer-run external workflow is possible;
  native continuous refresh, automatic matching and change monitoring are not.
</AcquisitionCallout>

## Sales Navigator: search, lists and CRM handoff

With a connected LinkedIn account and an active Sales Navigator subscription, Customermates exposes a focused set of REST and MCP operations. A user or authorized client can search people or companies from structured filters or a copied Sales Navigator search URL, resolve filter parameters, list or browse existing lead and account lists, and save a result to an existing list.

A search result can become the input to a **separate** CRM record-creation action. That second operation still needs explicit field mapping, validation and deduplication. It is not an automatic import, synchronization or background enrichment process.

| Capability                                     | Current state                                         |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Search people and companies                    | Supported with an active Sales Navigator subscription |
| Resolve Sales Navigator search parameters      | Supported                                             |
| List and browse existing lead or account lists | Supported                                             |
| Save a person or company to an existing list   | Supported                                             |
| Create a CRM contact or organization           | Separate authorized REST or MCP action                |
| Create a new Sales Navigator list              | Not supported; create it in Sales Navigator first     |
| Bulk import or continuous synchronization      | Not supported                                         |
| On-demand person or company profile lookup     | Supported through the connected LinkedIn account      |
| Customer-run external-provider enrichment      | Separate workflow with explicit REST writeback        |
| Bundled continuous enrichment or job monitoring | Not provided                                         |

<AcquisitionCallout title="A composed handoff is not a sync" variant="boundary">
  Search, inspect and record creation are deliberately separate operations. The
  workflow must decide what to map, check whether the record already exists and
  obtain the permissions required for the CRM write.
</AcquisitionCallout>

## Customermates versus LinkedIn's official CRM Sync

LinkedIn's official Sales Navigator CRM integrations are a different product surface. LinkedIn documents CRM Sync, embedded profiles, CRM badges, auto-save, activity writeback, data validation and reporting as Advanced Plus partner capabilities. Customermates does not claim or reproduce those features.

Customermates instead connects supported messages and exposes customer-authorized social and Sales Navigator operations through its connected-account, REST and MCP surfaces. You can review LinkedIn's own [CRM integration overview](https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a106005/integration-between-sales-navigator-and-your-crm-overview?lang=en) and [SNAP overview](https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a105094/sales-navigator-application-platform-snap-overview?lang=en) when comparing the two models.

</AcquisitionCallout>

## Plans, accounts and external providers

| Customermates plan | Shared connected-account pool per user | Account-wide sharing                 |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Starter            | None                                   | No                                   |
| Pro                | One across all supported providers     | No; individual threads can be shared |
| Business           | Three across all supported providers   | Yes                                  |
| Enterprise         | Unlimited under fair-use terms         | Yes                                  |

The allowance is one shared pool: a linked mailbox, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram or Telegram profile each consumes one connected-account slot. It is not a separate LinkedIn quota. LinkedIn accounts, Sales Navigator or Recruiter subscriptions, provider limits and provider terms are not included in the Customermates subscription. The connected-account processing path is documented in the Customermates subprocessor information. Self-hosted Customermates uses Starter entitlements and therefore does not include connected LinkedIn accounts, the unified inbox or Sales Navigator operations.

## MCP and approval boundaries

An external AI client can call the permitted messaging and Sales Navigator tools through MCP. The MCP instructions tell compatible clients to request confirmation before a send or destructive action, but the server does not add a second confirmation after an authorized client calls the tool. Review the client's confirmation behavior, the user's workspace permissions and the connected account before enabling write access.

Cloud plans include a Mate entitlement and hosted-AI credit allowance. When Mate is enabled in the hosted environment, it can use eligible tools under the user's permissions and the plan allowance. External MCP clients use their own model provider and subscription and do not consume those credits. Both paths still enforce the user's workspace permissions, plan entitlements and connected-account prerequisites; their confirmation controls are documented separately.

## Who this approach fits—and who it does not

This approach fits teams that want individual LinkedIn conversations in the same working context as their CRM records, or that want a controlled Sales Navigator search and list workflow through typed interfaces.

It is not a fit when the buying requirement is a bundled continuous enrichment service, LinkedIn CRM Sync, bulk prospect import, scraping, native job-change monitoring, multi-step outreach sequences, Lead Gen Forms, audience sync or campaign analytics. Those are different products and processing relationships.

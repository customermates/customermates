A **LinkedIn CRM integration** puts your LinkedIn conversations on the same record as the person, the deal and the email thread, so you can pick a conversation up where it stopped. In Customermates, an open-source CRM made in Germany, it means three bounded things: supported LinkedIn conversations and InMail in the managed-cloud inbox, next to your CRM records; on-demand profile, post and connection-request operations; and Sales Navigator search and list operations through REST or MCP. It is not LinkedIn CRM Sync, and it is not a sending, automation or scraping tool for LinkedIn: it does not enrich, scrape or bulk import profiles.

**How this helps you follow up.** If you sell through LinkedIn conversations, the hard part is rarely the message itself. It is remembering who the person is, which deal the conversation belongs to and what was agreed by email. A LinkedIn conversation goes quiet when nobody remembers what was promised in it. With the thread on the prospect's record, you can read the earlier exchange, see the deal it belongs to, and have Mate or your own AI client save a reply draft in the thread. You edit it and send it from the connected LinkedIn account. Customermates sends individual messages only and runs no sequences or bulk invites.

## What counts as a CRM?

A CRM keeps one record for each person and company you work with, ties every conversation and note to that record, and shows the deal and the next step, so that anyone on the team can pick the relationship up without asking around. A tool that keeps the messages but has no record, deal or next step behind them is an inbox, not a CRM.

## Can I use LinkedIn as a CRM?

LinkedIn is where the conversation happens, but the email thread, the deal and the next step live outside it, so a separate system has to hold them together. That is workable for a handful of relationships. Once you track deals and follow up by email as well, you need a CRM that has the LinkedIn conversation on its records, which is what a LinkedIn CRM integration does.

## Is LinkedIn Sales Navigator a CRM?

LinkedIn presents Sales Navigator as something that connects to a CRM. Its [help page on CRM integrations](https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a106005/integration-between-sales-navigator-and-your-crm-overview?lang=en) describes embedded profiles inside HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics, Oracle Sales and Salesforce, and a CRM Sync that moves leads and accounts from the CRM into Sales Navigator and can write selected data back, both for Advanced Plus users only (checked on 10 November 2026). Sales Navigator is for finding and researching people; the deal, the emails and the next step belong in a CRM. Customermates does not reproduce CRM Sync, and the Sales Navigator section below shows what it does instead.

## The example workspace: LinkedIn sales with sample data

The demo above and the [example workspace](https://demo.customermates.com/en/w/linkedin-sales/dashboard) show a small LinkedIn sales setup with sample data, not a customer. It has four lists: Prospects, Companies, Opportunities and Tasks. Each prospect has a connection status (Connected, Messaged, Replied, Meeting booked or Client), shown as a board. Opportunities move through the stages Identified, Conversation, Meeting booked, Proposal sent, Won and Lost.

Open the inbox and you see a supported LinkedIn conversation whose participant is matched to a prospect record. The conversation is linked to that prospect's opportunity under **Conversation records**, a link someone set on purpose. An email thread with the same person sits alongside it, so the LinkedIn exchange and the email follow-up read as one relationship. Opportunities keep the proposal as a PDF in a File field, and a calendar view shows the booked training sessions by month.

These lists and stages are an example. A new workspace starts without lists: on Customermates Cloud the AI workspace setup can build lists, stages, a dashboard and Knowledge Base pages from your description, once at onboarding and within its credit cap, or you skip it with **Start with an empty workspace**. Otherwise people with Configure rights create the lists and fields in Configure and import existing records from an Excel workbook.

## How do I follow a LinkedIn conversation in Customermates?

You connect the account yourself under Channels, on a hosted connection page that you finish within 30 minutes ([how to connect a channel](/docs/app-profile#how-do-i-connect-a-channel)). Customermates reaches LinkedIn through a messaging provider, Unipile, which the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors) lists. Customermates itself receives neither your LinkedIn password nor an OAuth token; for the connection it stores an account identifier and non-secret account metadata. Once the account is connected, its conversation history imports in the background, usually within minutes and up to an hour for large accounts. Disconnecting the account deletes the threads and messages Customermates imported from it.

An entitled cloud account can connect LinkedIn (Classic), LinkedIn (Classic & Sales Navigator) or LinkedIn (Classic & Recruiter), whichever products your LinkedIn account has. The [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) then shows supported conversations beside email and other connected channels. A teammate can read a thread, keep a draft, reply, or start an individual chat from the person's record. With a Sales Navigator or Recruiter connection, **Send as** offers Classic, Sales Navigator InMail or Recruiter InMail, and InMail uses the subject and signature fields the connected product requires. A reply draft sits in its conversation until you send it; a new InMail cannot be saved as a draft. To find conversations where you wrote last and heard nothing, use the inbox filters and the dated next step described on the [follow-up page](/features/follow-up).

Matching works through the Contact details field of your [contact records](/features/contact-management): the email addresses and social identities that connect messages to a record. A conversation reaches an opportunity or organization only when a user or an agent links it explicitly. Customermates never logs a LinkedIn conversation to a deal automatically, and the inbox is not a social-listening or campaign product.

## What limits apply to the LinkedIn connection?

LinkedIn enforces its own request limits and watches for automated activity, and exceeding them can get an account throttled or suspended. Customermates therefore keeps a conservative request budget for LinkedIn and pauses before LinkedIn would push back; a paused sync resumes on its own. The current numbers, from the [messaging rate limits](/docs/messaging-rate-limits):

| What                             | Limit                            |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Reading conversation history     | 20 per minute, 300 per day       |
| Person or company profile lookup | 1 per second                     |
| Connection request               | 50 per day, one every 10 seconds |

Sending a message or InMail and Sales Navigator searches fall under the per-endpoint and per-account caps. These are Customermates budgets for one connected account; LinkedIn's own terms and your account's standing still apply on top.

## Profiles, posts and relationship actions on demand

Through a connected LinkedIn account, an authorized REST or MCP client can retrieve a person or company profile, read posts, inspect comments and reactions, and list, send, accept or cancel individual connection requests. These are explicit, user-authorized operations against the connected provider account. They are useful when a teammate needs current context before a conversation or CRM update.

The returned provider data is not a bundled background enrichment feed. Customermates does not continuously monitor profiles, match LinkedIn identities to CRM records, write provider fields into contacts automatically, or alert on job changes. An authorized client can take an on-demand profile result and invoke a separate, permission-checked contact or organization create/update through REST or MCP, with field mapping, validation and duplicate handling defined by the user or workflow.

A customer-run workflow can also call an enrichment provider selected by the customer and write selected fields back through the Customermates REST API. The customer operates that provider and workflow. Customermates does not include the provider, source its data, choose the mapping or run a native continuous enrichment service.

## Sales Navigator: search, lists and CRM handoff

With a connected LinkedIn account and an active Sales Navigator subscription, Customermates exposes a focused set of REST and MCP operations. A user or authorized client can search people or companies from structured filters or a copied Sales Navigator search URL, resolve filter parameters, list or browse existing lead and account lists, and save a result to an existing list.

A search result can become the input to a **separate** CRM record-creation action. That second operation still needs explicit field mapping, validation and deduplication. It is not an automatic import, synchronization, lead feed or background enrichment process.

| Capability                                      | Current state                                         |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Search people and companies                     | Supported with an active Sales Navigator subscription |
| Resolve Sales Navigator search parameters       | Supported                                             |

| Save a person or company to an existing list    | Supported                                             |
| Create a CRM contact or organization            | Separate authorized REST or MCP action                |
| Create a new Sales Navigator list               | Not supported; create it in Sales Navigator first     |

## Customermates versus LinkedIn's official CRM Sync

LinkedIn's official Sales Navigator CRM integrations are a different product surface. LinkedIn documents CRM Sync, embedded profiles, CRM badges, auto-save, activity writeback, data validation and ROI reporting as Advanced Plus capabilities for certified CRM partners, in its help pages on [managing CRM Sync](https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a107066) and [CRM data validation](https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a738351) (checked on 10 November 2026). Customermates does not claim or reproduce those features.

Customermates instead connects supported messages and exposes customer-authorized social and Sales Navigator operations through its connected-account, REST and MCP surfaces. You can review LinkedIn's own [CRM integration overview](https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a106005/integration-between-sales-navigator-and-your-crm-overview?lang=en) and [SNAP overview](https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a105094/sales-navigator-application-platform-snap-overview?lang=en) when comparing the two models.

## Can a team share one LinkedIn inbox, and what does it cost?

Sharing has two scopes. On any plan with the inbox, the owner of a connected LinkedIn account can switch on **Share with team** for a single conversation; teammates whose role can read the Inbox then see it, change its state and resync it, but only the owner can reply while the account itself stays private. Sharing every conversation of an account is the **Visibility** switch on that channel, on Business, Max and Enterprise. A teammate's private conversations never appear, whatever the viewer's role, Admin included, and a reply leaves from the account that owns the conversation, so teammates answer only on accounts they own or that are shared with the workspace. The [inbox guide](/docs/app-inbox#how-do-i-share-a-single-conversation) has the steps.

| Customermates plan     | Connected accounts per user                                    | Inbox sharing                                   |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Starter or self-hosted | None                                                           | No inbox                                        |
| Pro                    | [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]]         | Your own accounts plus individually shared threads |
| Business               | [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedAccountsPerUser]]    | Whole connected accounts can be shared          |
| Max                    | [[commercial.entitlement.max.includedAccountsPerUser]]         | Whole connected accounts can be shared          |
| Enterprise             | [[commercial.entitlement.enterprise.includedAccountsPerUser]] under fair-use terms | Whole connected accounts can be shared |

The [allowance per plan](/docs/app-profile#how-many-channels-can-i-connect) is one shared pool: a linked mailbox, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram or Telegram profile each uses one connected-account slot. It is not a separate LinkedIn quota, so LinkedIn plus an email inbox for the same person needs more than the Pro allowance. LinkedIn accounts, Sales Navigator or Recruiter subscriptions, provider limits and provider terms are not included in the Customermates subscription. The connected-account processing path is documented in the Customermates [subprocessor information](/subprocessors). Self-hosted Customermates uses the Starter feature set and therefore does not include connected LinkedIn accounts, the unified inbox or Sales Navigator operations. Current prices are on the [pricing page](/pricing).

## MCP, Mate and approval boundaries

An external AI client can call the permitted messaging and Sales Navigator tools through the Customermates [MCP server](/docs/mcp), using your own AI provider. The MCP instructions tell compatible clients to request confirmation before a send or destructive action, but the server does not add a second confirmation after an authorized client calls the tool. Review the client's confirmation behavior, the user's workspace permissions and the connected account before enabling write access.

Cloud plans include a Mate entitlement and hosted-AI credit allowance. When Mate is enabled in the hosted environment, it can use eligible tools under the user's permissions and the plan allowance. Mate sends a message as soon as you ask it to send, so ask for a draft when you want to review first. Mate is not available in self-hosted installations. External MCP clients use their own model provider and subscription and do not consume those credits. Both paths still enforce the user's workspace permissions, plan entitlements and connected-account prerequisites.

## Does Customermates automate, scrape or mass-send on LinkedIn?

No. Customermates is not a sending, automation or scraping tool for LinkedIn. It sends the individual messages and connection requests that a person or an authorized client asks for, one at a time, inside the limits above. It runs no sequences or drip campaigns, sends no bulk invites or mass messages, does not scrape profiles or search results, and has no background enrichment or job-change monitoring. [Mate](/docs/app-assistant) and your own AI client can draft a reply that you review and send. LinkedIn outreach automation is a different kind of product, and Customermates does not replace one.

## Who this fits, and who it does not

This fits founders, consultants and small sales teams who hold individual LinkedIn conversations and want them in the same working context as their prospects and opportunities, or who want a controlled Sales Navigator search and list workflow through typed interfaces.

It is not a fit when the buying requirement is LinkedIn CRM Sync, a lead feed, bundled continuous enrichment, bulk prospect import, scraping, native job-change monitoring, bulk connection invites, multi-step outreach sequences, mass messaging, Lead Gen Forms, audience sync or campaign analytics. Those are different products and processing relationships.

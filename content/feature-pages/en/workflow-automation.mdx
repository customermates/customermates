CRM workflow automation means a recurring CRM step happens without anyone having to remember it. A deal moves to Won and the onboarding tasks exist. A client emails and a reply draft is waiting.

Customermates gives you two ways to build that. **Routines** in Customermates Cloud run a saved instruction through Mate, the hosted assistant, on a schedule or when a record changes, using hosted AI credits. **Webhooks, the REST API and MCP** connect the CRM to n8n or any other automation tool, on every plan and when self-hosting. There is no visual workflow builder with branches inside the CRM; that part lives in n8n, a separate product.

## Which job goes where

| Job | Use | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| Monday list of deals nobody touched for 14 days | Routine on a schedule | Any Cloud plan |
| Reply draft when a client emails | Routine on **Email Received** | Cloud, Pro and higher |
| Onboarding tasks when a deal is won | Routine on **Deal Updated**, or a webhook into n8n | Routine: Cloud. Webhook: every plan |
| Message in a team chat when a deal is created | Webhook with a body template, no n8n needed | Every plan |
| Flow with branches, waits and other apps | n8n on webhooks and REST | Every plan, plus n8n |
| Update a deal from pasted call notes | Mate chat, or your own AI client over MCP | Mate: Cloud. MCP: every plan |

Our rule of thumb: if a step must behave identically every time, such as creating the same tasks for every won deal, use a webhook and n8n. A routine is an AI run that reads, decides and writes, so its output varies a little and every run costs credits. Use it where the reading is the point.

## Routines: scheduled and event-driven AI work

Routines exist in Customermates Cloud only. A routine is a name, an instruction of up to 5,000 characters and a trigger. When the trigger fires, Mate starts a new conversation with that instruction and works on its own, as the routine's owner: it sees what the owner sees and spends the owner's credits. Changes a routine makes never trigger other routines, so they cannot loop. The [routines guide](/docs/app-routines) covers every option.

- **On a schedule:** every 15 or 30 minutes, hourly, daily, weekly or monthly. Mate can also set a custom cron schedule, for example weekdays at 09:00.
- **When something changes:** any record event on contacts, organizations, deals, services and tasks, plus inbox events except deleted emails and chats. **Changed fields** narrows an update trigger to fields such as your stage column, and **Record filters** to records such as deals assigned to you.

| Limit | Current value |
|---|---|
| Runs per routine and hour | 4 |
| Credits per run | 10, then the run stops as Partial |
| Shortest schedule interval | 15 minutes |
| Failed runs in a row before the routine pauses | 3 |
| Debounce after a run starts | 300 seconds by default; Mate or MCP can set 0 to 86,400 |
| Run history kept | 90 days |

An unattended run may create and update records, append notes, create tasks and save drafts without asking. It may also send emails and chat messages. Everything that asks for approval in a chat is refused, because nobody is there to approve it: deletions, renaming record types, team invitations, webhook resends and support requests. If you want drafts and nothing else, write it into the instruction.

### Setup 1: Monday pipeline check (any Cloud plan)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the routine">Open **Routines**, click **Add** and name it "Monday pipeline check".</Step>
  <Step title="Set the trigger">Choose **On a schedule**, then **Repeats**: **Every week**, Monday, 08:00.</Step>
  <Step title="Paste the instruction">Use the text below with your own stage names, and save.</Step>
  <Step title="Test it once">Click **Test trigger**. **Run details** shows Mate's answer, the records it read and the credits it used.</Step>
</Steps>

```text
List every deal that is not in Won or Lost and has not been updated in the last 14 days. Group them by assigned user. For each deal write one line: name, stage, value and the gist of its notes. End with the five deals I should call first this week, with one sentence each on why. Do not change any records.
```

Credit use: a weekly schedule runs four or five times a month and each run stops at 10 credits, so this routine uses at most 50 credits a month. One credit is one US cent of measured provider cost. Teammates who can see the routine see each run's status, summary and credit use; only the owner opens the full answer.

### Setup 2: reply draft when a client emails (Pro and higher)

This needs the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox), which starts on Pro.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect a mailbox">Connect Gmail, Outlook or IMAP under **My Profile › Channels**.</Step>
  <Step title="Add the routine">Choose **When something changes** and the event **Email Received**. For WhatsApp or LinkedIn, pick **Message Received**.</Step>
  <Step title="Paste the instruction">Use the text below and save.</Step>
  <Step title="Review the drafts">In the inbox, set the **Draft** filter to **has some**. Edit a draft and click **Send draft**, or discard it.</Step>
</Steps>

```text
Read the email thread from this event. If it is a newsletter, an automated notice or spam, stop and do nothing. Otherwise find the sender's contact, organization and open deals. Save a reply draft in the same thread that answers the question and proposes one next step. Never send the email. Append one line to the contact's notes with what the client asked.
```

Credit use: every run that starts uses credits, including runs where Mate decides there is nothing to do. With four runs an hour at no more than 10 credits each, the ceiling is 40 credits an hour. Because of the debounce, a second email within five minutes of a run starting gets no run of its own, an email that arrives while a run is still going is skipped too, and after four runs in an hour, further emails that hour are dropped. You can ask Mate to shorten the debounce, down to zero, but the cap of four runs an hour stays. Treat the drafts as help with most emails, not a promise for every one.

### Stage changes: routine or n8n

A routine on **Deal Updated**, with **Changed fields** set to your stage column and a record filter for Won, can create onboarding tasks, assign them to the same people as the deal and link them to the deal. In n8n the same job costs no credits and never varies: the webhook delivers the deal with a `changes` object, an IF node checks the new stage, and an HTTP Request node creates the tasks. Choose the routine when the tasks depend on what was agreed in the emails, and n8n when they are always the same.

## Webhooks, REST and n8n: rules on every plan

Customermates sends a JSON POST for each of [[derived.webhooks.events.total]] event types: [[derived.webhooks.events.records]] record events and [[derived.webhooks.events.messaging]] inbox events. Deal stage is a custom single-select column, so a stage change arrives as `deal.updated` with previous and current values in `changes`. Deliveries are signed with HMAC-SHA256 when you set a secret, retried up to five times on timeouts and server errors, and logged under **Recent Deliveries**. The [webhook reference](/docs/webhooks) lists every payload.

There is no native Slack integration, but a webhook can post to a Slack or Discord channel without n8n, using the channel's incoming webhook URL and a body template.

The [REST API](/features/api) under `/api/v1` covers the five record types, with batch endpoints for up to 100 records per request. The MCP server gives AI clients such as Claude or ChatGPT [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on the same data.

[n8n](https://n8n.io/) has a visual builder and lets you write JavaScript or Python in any step. You can self-host its Community Edition under the [Sustainable Use License](https://github.com/n8n-io/n8n/blob/master/LICENSE.md), which permits use only for your own internal business purposes or for non-commercial or personal use, or subscribe to n8n Cloud, [priced by monthly workflow executions](https://n8n.io/pricing/).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the trigger in n8n">Add a Webhook node and copy its URL.</Step>
  <Step title="Subscribe in Customermates">Under **My Company › Webhooks**, click **Add**, paste the URL, pick the events and set a **Secret**.</Step>
  <Step title="Write back through REST">Create an API key under **My Profile › API & Connectors** and store it in n8n as a Header Auth credential with the header name `x-api-key`.</Step>
  <Step title="Activate the workflow in n8n">Scheduling, waits, logs and execution limits belong to n8n. Customermates keeps the records.</Step>
</Steps>

The community node [customermates/n8n-nodes-crm](https://github.com/customermates/n8n-nodes-crm) offers dedicated CRM nodes if you prefer them to HTTP Request nodes. The [n8n guide](/docs/n8n) has the endpoints and common mistakes.

## What updates itself, and what still needs a person

- **Without AI:** every change lands in the record's activity history and reaches every webhook subscribed to that event, and weighted pipeline values follow your stage weights. On Pro and higher, once you link an email address or handle to a contact, every conversation with it appears on that contact's timeline.
- **With AI, when you ask:** paste call notes into Mate and ask it to update the deal, append the notes and create follow-up tasks. Your own AI client can do the same through MCP on every plan, self-hosted included, billed by your AI provider.
- **With AI, unattended:** routines, as above.

Our advice: let a routine propose a stage move in a note and let the person assigned to the deal make it. Stages drive the weighted pipeline, and a wrong move made by an unattended run is easy to miss.

## What Customermates does not automate

- No visual workflow builder, branching or waits inside the CRM. Use n8n or a similar tool.
- No email sequences, drip campaigns or mass email. A reply draft is one message in one conversation.
- No task due dates, reminders or notification centre. Store a date in a custom date field on the deal and let the Monday routine list deals whose date has passed, as in [follow-ups](/features/follow-up).
- No lead scoring, data enrichment or web forms. Send form entries in through REST or n8n.
- No routines, Mate or inbox in [self-hosted Customermates](/features/self-hosted). Webhooks, REST and MCP work there.
- No guarantee a routine runs: the hourly cap, the debounce or an owner without credits can skip a run. Steps that must run for every event belong on a webhook, where failed deliveries are retried and can be re-sent.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="What are examples of CRM workflow automation?">
Creating the same onboarding tasks for every won deal, posting to a team channel when a deal is created, drafting a reply when a client writes, and a weekly list of stalled deals. Fixed steps like the first two suit webhooks and n8n. Steps that need reading, like the last two, suit routines in Customermates Cloud.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Customermates have a visual workflow builder?">
No. There is no drag-and-drop builder and no branching rules inside Customermates. Routines take a plain-language instruction instead, and visual flows with branches run in n8n, connected through webhooks and the REST API.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can a CRM update itself automatically?">
Partly. Activity history, webhook events and weighted pipeline values update on their own, and on Pro and higher a linked email address brings later conversations onto the contact's timeline. Writing notes or changing deals based on what was said takes AI: Mate when you ask, a routine on a trigger, or your own AI client through MCP.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What is the difference between CRM workflow automation and marketing automation?">
Marketing automation sends campaigns to lists of contacts on a timetable. CRM workflow automation acts on single records, such as one deal or one conversation. Customermates has no email sequences, drip campaigns or mass email, so keep a marketing tool if you need those.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Do routines work when self-hosting?">
No. Routines need Mate and hosted AI credits, which exist only in Customermates Cloud. Self-hosted, you build comparable jobs with webhooks, REST and n8n, or with your own AI client over MCP.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How much does workflow automation cost in Customermates?">
Webhooks, REST and MCP come with every plan, with no Customermates charge per workflow. Routines are included per user, from [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] on Starter to [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedRoutinesPerUser]] on Business, and each run uses at most 10 of the owner's hosted AI credits. n8n bills separately.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

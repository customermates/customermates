In an early B2B startup, the founders are the sales team. Deals start with a warm intro, a LinkedIn message or a conversation at an event, and the context lives in two inboxes and a spreadsheet. A CRM for startups is useful once the second person talks to customers, or the first time a promised follow-up slips. Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM made in Germany. It keeps prospect organizations, contacts, sales opportunities and the next step in one configurable workspace, and an AI client you already use can read or update the records it is allowed to see. From Pro on Customermates Cloud, the LinkedIn and email threads sit on the prospect, and Mate or your own AI client through MCP drafts the reply and the next step for you to review, edit and send. It does not find leads for you, enrich them or send outbound on your behalf.

## What founder-led sales needs from a CRM

- **One view of who is in play.** Which companies are you talking to, who is the champion, and what was promised in the last call.
- **A next step on every open opportunity.** Founder-led deals rarely die from a "no". They die because nobody followed up while the product team needed attention.
- **A handover that does not start from zero.** When the first sales hire joins, they need the history of each opportunity, not a week of calls.
- **A process you can change after a pivot.** Stages, fields and list names change with what you sell.
- **Access for the AI tools you already use.** A founder who works in Claude or ChatGPT should be able to look up a prospect or record a meeting outcome from there.

## The example founder sales workspace

The demo opens an example workspace with sample data, not a customer's account. It starts on a compact sales dashboard and a board of opportunities. The lists:

- **Companies**: the companies you sell to.
- **Contacts**: champions, decision makers and the people who introduced you.
- **Opportunities**: each potential deal, moving through Identified, Conversation, Demo, Pilot, Decision, Won and Lost. Its value is calculated from production lines times the price per line and year, the annual recurring revenue if it closes.
- **Investors**: angels and funds you talk to about the seed round, kept as ordinary records with ticket size and round status, and shown on a separate Seed round board.
- **Tasks**: concrete commitments such as "send the security questionnaire", linked to an opportunity.

Investors are a list like any other. They are not a fundraising platform, an investor database or deal sourcing, and they have no cap table or data room. Keeping them in a separate list means they never mix with the sales numbers on the board.

Opportunities and investors keep their documents, such as a pilot agreement or a draft term sheet, in a File field. A **Pilots** calendar view shows each pilot period, the Calendar page puts investor meetings, pilot periods and expected closes on one month, and the dashboard lists the investor meetings of the coming 14 days.

## Prepare for a prospect conversation

Before a call, open the company: its contacts, the open opportunity and its stage, your notes from the last conversation and, on Customermates Cloud from Pro, the email or LinkedIn threads you linked to it. Each stage can carry a win probability, so the [pipeline board](/features/pipeline) shows value next to weighted value. That is weighting with your own percentages, useful in an investor update if you state the method, but it is not predictive forecasting, conversion analytics or pipeline velocity.

## Your own AI client through MCP

Customermates has an [MCP](/docs/mcp) endpoint on every plan, including self-hosted installations. Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor or another client, and it uses your own AI provider, which bills the model use.

A typical use for a founder: in Claude, ask which stage your opportunity with a prospect is in and who the champion is. The client reads the opportunity and the linked contact through MCP. After the call, ask it to move the opportunity to Pilot and add a note with the agreed next step. The client updates that record, and you see the change in Customermates.

What the client can do is limited by your role and permissions: it sees and changes only what you could see and change in the app. Treat it like any tool with write access. An MCP client may ask you to confirm before deleting or sending, but that is the client's behaviour, not a check in Customermates, so review what you ask it to do. Deleted records go to Trash for 30 days; other changes are not undone automatically.

## Conversations and Mate on Customermates Cloud

The [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings email from Gmail, Outlook or IMAP together with LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram. It is part of Customermates Cloud only, and the plan decides what you can connect:

- **Starter and self-hosted:** no connected messaging accounts.
- **Pro:** each person's own accounts, with [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] connected account per user included, plus threads shared individually with a co-founder.
- **Business, Max or Enterprise:** needed to share a whole account, for example a sales mailbox the first hire also replies from.

Participants are matched to contacts by email, phone or LinkedIn identity. A conversation is linked to an opportunity or organization only when a person, or an agent you ask, links it; it is never filed automatically.

Mate, the assistant inside Customermates Cloud, can summarize an opportunity, update records and draft replies. It works on every paid cloud plan within your plan's hosted AI credits and is disabled in self-hosting. Mate updates records and sends messages as soon as you ask; only deletions, imports, access changes and destructive data-model changes stop for your approval. A draft waits until you edit or send it, so ask for drafts when you want to review first. A [routine](/docs/app-routines) runs a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes, owned by one user and limited to what that user sees.

## Handing deals to the first sales hire

Assign the new hire to the opportunities they take over and filter by assigned user. A custom role can limit their read access to assigned records on prospects, contacts and opportunities. Two limits to know: a shared view is read-only for colleagues, and there are no field-level permissions. Conversations from a founder's own account stay private until the founder shares a thread, or the whole account on Business or higher.

## Integrations

Every plan, self-hosted included, has a [REST API](/features/api); webhooks and the n8n triggers that use them come with every paid plan and self-hosting. Your product can create a contact when someone signs up, and a webhook on an opportunity change can notify a separately run n8n workflow or a Slack incoming webhook; there is no native Slack app. The [integrations page](/features/integrations) lists what connects.

## What Customermates does not do for startups

- No lead enrichment, lead scoring or prospect database.
- No sequences, drip campaigns, bulk messaging or automated outbound.
- No predictive forecasting; weighted value is your own stage percentages.
- No calling, SMS, web forms, calendar booking or native mobile apps.
- No fundraising platform, cap table or data room.
- No startup discount. The free plan stops at [[commercial.limit.free.maxMembers]] person and [[commercial.limit.free.maxRecords]] records, without the inbox or Mate.

## Setting up your own workspace

The demo lists are examples. A new workspace starts without lists. On Customermates Cloud, the AI workspace setup asks how you sell and builds lists, stages, a dashboard and Knowledge Base pages from your answers; it runs once during onboarding within a credit cap, and **Start with an empty workspace** skips it. Otherwise, someone with Configure rights creates the lists and fields in Configure and imports records from an Excel workbook, one list at a time. Imports cannot be undone, so start with a small file.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="When should a startup start using a CRM?">
When a second person talks to customers, or the first time a follow-up gets lost in someone's inbox. Before that, a spreadsheet works. After that, every month adds conversations whose context someone has to reconstruct later, usually the first sales hire.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can we track investors in the same CRM?">
Yes, as ordinary records. The example workspace keeps investors in their own list with a Seed round board, separate from the sales opportunities. Customermates is not a fundraising platform: there is no investor database, cap table, data room or deck analytics.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What can an AI client do through MCP?">
Read and update the records your role allows, for example look up an opportunity before a call and record the outcome afterwards. The client uses your own AI provider. Confirmation prompts come from the client, not from Customermates, and only deletions can be restored, from Trash within 30 days.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Customermates find or enrich leads?">
No. It has no lead enrichment, lead scoring or prospect database, and no sequences or automated outbound. You add the companies and people you are actually talking to.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does a new workspace come with these lists?">
No. A new workspace starts without lists. On Customermates Cloud the AI workspace setup can build them from your description, once during onboarding; otherwise you create them in Configure. The demo lists are an example.
</FaqItem>

The free plan covers [[commercial.limit.free.maxMembers]] person and [[commercial.limit.free.maxRecords]] records without the inbox or Mate, and paid plans start at [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per user per month, billed monthly. There is no startup discount. The open-source core can be self-hosted without a licence fee, without the inbox and Mate. Our [startup CRM shortlist](/blog/best-crm-startups) compares other options.
</FaqItem>

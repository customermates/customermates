A startup needs a CRM once a second person talks to customers or investors, or the first time a follow-up gets lost in a founder's inbox. Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM built in Germany for startups that win deals in conversations: B2B founders who sell directly, service startups, and teams raising a round while they sell. Customers, funds, deals and, from the Pro plan, the email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp threads behind them sit in one workspace. It does not fit teams that sell by phone or need email sequences; the limits section names tools built for that. Customermates publishes this page.

## What a startup needs from a CRM

- **Deals start in founder inboxes and DMs.** The first customers come from a LinkedIn message, a warm intro by email or an accelerator's WhatsApp group. A CRM that only syncs email never sees the other two.
- **The first sales hire inherits everything.** They need each deal's history without a week of handover calls.
- **Customers and investors share one address book.** The same people turn up as angels, advisors and buyers, but a fund must not count in the sales pipeline.
- **Signups come from your product.** New users should reach the CRM through an API, not by copy and paste.
- **The process changes after a pivot.** Stages, fields and even the word "deal" should change in minutes.
- **Runway is counted in months.** What you pay in year three matters as much as what you pay in year one, so compare both.

## A startup setup in Customermates

Customermates has five record types. Here is one way to map them to a startup; every field name is an example.

| Record type | Used for | Example custom fields |
|---|---|---|
| Organizations | Customer companies, funds, partners | Kind (Single Select: Prospect, Customer, Fund, Partner) |
| Contacts | Champions, buyers, angels, partners at funds | Role (Single Select: Champion, Decision maker, Investor), Source (Single Select: Inbound, Outbound, Referral, Product signup) |
| Deals | Each sales opportunity, and each fund per round | Deal type (Single Select: Customer, Investor), Next touch (Date), Ticket size (Currency) |
| Services | What you sell, each with a price | Pilot, Annual licence, Onboarding package |
| Tasks | Commitments such as "send the security questionnaire" | A date field you add, because tasks have no built-in due date |

Under **My Company › Settings**, in **Data model**, you can rename record types from a preset list, for example Services to Products or Deals to Opportunities.

### Stages and a pipeline number for the investor update

A new workspace already has a **Stage** field on deals with a win probability per option: Prospecting 10%, Qualification 20%, Demo 40%, Proposal 60%, Negotiation 80%, Won 100% and Lost 0%. That fits a demo-led B2B motion, and you change options and probabilities in the same settings after a pivot.

A deal's value is the sum of its linked services, price times quantity. Say an annual licence costs €12,000. A two-licence deal in Proposal is worth €24,000 and adds €14,400 to the weighted pipeline; a one-licence deal in Demo adds €4,800. Together that is €36,000 in total and €19,200 weighted: arithmetic from your own probabilities, not a forecast, and a number an investor update can quote with its method.

Investor deals do not count here. Give each fund's deal the Deal type Investor, link no services and put the amount in Ticket size, so its deal value stays at zero and the weighted pipeline ignores it. A board filtered to Investor and grouped by a round-stage field of your own becomes the fundraising view; the [startup CRM guide](/blog/best-crm-startups) builds it step by step.

### Four saved views for the Monday review

1. **Pipeline:** deals with Deal type Customer, as a board grouped by Stage. Drag a card to change its stage.
2. **Next touch:** all deals sorted by Next touch, ascending, customers and funds alike.
3. **Stalled:** customer deals in Demo, Proposal or Negotiation whose **Updated at** is older than 14 days.
4. **New signups:** contacts with Source Product signup, created in the last 7 days.

Saved views are personal, so each co-founder sets the same filters and saves their own view.

### Handing deals to the first sales hire

- Deals have assigned users rather than one owner. Add the new hire to the deals they take over and filter by **Assigned**. In Customermates Cloud, one request to Mate does it: "Add Lena as assigned user on all customer deals in Demo or later."
- Conversations from a founder's own mailbox or LinkedIn account stay private, admins included, until the founder shares them in **Thread settings**. The hire can then read a shared thread, but replies go out from the founder's channel, so they answer from their own connected account. On Business, a founder can share the whole channel, and teammates can reply from it.
- A hire or freelancer who should see only their own accounts gets a custom role with **Read access** set to **Assigned** on contacts, organizations and deals.

### Product signups through the API

Every plan, self-hosted included, has a [REST API](/features/api) under `/api/v1` with an OpenAPI description. Your signup handler can send a `POST` to `/api/v1/contacts` with an API key in the `x-api-key` header:

```json
{
  "firstName": "Ada",
  "lastName": "Example",
  "identifiers": [{ "provider": "mail", "value": "ada@example.com" }]
}
```

The email address becomes a channel on the contact, so later inbox conversations with it land on the same record. Add `customFieldValues` with your Source column to mark the signup. In the other direction, there is no native Slack app, but a webhook on `deal.updated` with a body template can post to a Slack incoming webhook, and an n8n instance you run separately can handle longer workflows.

### Conversations in one inbox (Pro and above)

On Pro, Business and Enterprise in Customermates Cloud, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings Gmail, Outlook or IMAP email together with LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram, and you reply on the original channel. Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] connected account per user and Business [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedAccountsPerUser]], so two founders on Pro might split it: one connects the mailbox, the other LinkedIn. Link a participant to a contact once, and every conversation with that address shows on the contact's timeline.

### A weekly pipeline review with Mate (Customermates Cloud)

Mate is the assistant in Customermates Cloud. It runs on hosted AI credits that every cloud plan includes per active user, and reading inbox conversations needs Pro or higher. A [routine](/docs/app-routines) is a saved instruction Mate runs on a schedule or when a record changes. Set **Repeats** to **Every week**, Monday at 08:00, with instructions like these:

```text
List customer deals in Demo, Proposal or Negotiation that have not been
updated for 14 days, and deals of any type whose Next touch is today or
earlier. For each one, read the latest inbox conversation with its contacts.
If my last message has no answer, save a short follow-up as a draft reply in
that conversation. Do not send anything. End with a table: deal, deal type,
stage, days since the last update, draft saved yes or no.
```

An event routine can follow a win: when Stage changes to Won, create a task "Onboarding call" linked to the deal and assigned to the same people. Starter includes [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] routine per user and Pro [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]].

<AcquisitionCallout title="Ask routines for drafts, not sends" variant="boundary">
  Mate and routines can send email and chat messages without asking first,
  so a review routine has to say "Do not send anything". Filter the inbox by
  Draft, read each draft and send it yourself. Each run has a credit cap, so
  keep the list short.
</AcquisitionCallout>

Prefer your own AI client? Claude, ChatGPT and other clients connect through MCP on every plan and on self-hosted installations, and your AI provider bills the model use.

## What Customermates does not do for startups

- No email sequences, no bulk outreach and no calling or SMS.
- No web forms. Point your own site's form at the REST API instead.
- No lead scoring, no data enrichment and no help-desk tickets.
- Tasks have no due dates or reminders. Use a date field and a saved view.
- No investor database, cap table or data room, and no mobile app.
- No free hosted plan and no startup discount. The trial runs [[commercial.trial.days]] days.

## Moving in from a spreadsheet, Notion or another CRM

<Steps>
  <Step title="Save each list as an Excel workbook">
    The import reads .xlsx files only, so save any other export as .xlsx in a
    spreadsheet app first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import organizations, then contacts">
    On each list, open **Import and export** and choose **Add from file**.
    Give contacts a column with the company name, and the import links them
    to that organization. New contacts need a first and a last name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import open deals">
    Include Stage, Deal type and Next touch, then link services and quantities
    so each deal gets its value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the inbox">
    On Pro or higher, connect your accounts under **My Profile › Channels** and
    link the people you write with to their contacts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Leaving works the same way in reverse: each record type exports as an Excel workbook, and the REST API reads every record your role can see. There is no whole-account export.

## Data protection for EU startups

In Customermates Cloud, the primary production database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region. That is a product setting, not a contractual residency promise. Hosting and the other subprocessors, US providers among them, are on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [data processing agreement](/dpa) is available. Customermates holds no certification such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2, which matters once an enterprise customer's security questionnaire asks about your vendors.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="What CRM do startups use?">
It depends on how they sell. Close includes calling and SMS on every plan, Pipedrive has sequences from its Growth plan, and HubSpot, Zoho CRM and Attio offer free plans for two or three users. HubSpot and Attio also run startup programs for venture-backed companies, and [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/crm/startup-crm/) says its Starter Suite starts at $25 per user per month with a 30-day trial (checked on 8 October 2026). Customermates, which publishes this page, fits startups whose deals start in email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp and that track investors in the same system. Our [shortlist of startup CRMs](/blog/best-crm-startups) sorts the options by stage and sales motion.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="When should a startup start using a CRM?">
When a second person talks to customers or investors, or the first time a follow-up is lost in someone's inbox. Before that, a spreadsheet or a free template works. After it, every month adds conversations whose context someone has to reconstruct later, and the first sales hire is the person who pays for that.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is Customermates free for startups?">
No. There is a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial without a credit card, and plans then start at [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per user per month, billed monthly. There is no free hosted plan and no startup discount. The open-source core can be self-hosted without a licence fee, but without the inbox and Mate.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can we track fundraising in the same CRM as sales?">
Yes. Each fund is an organization, each partner a contact, and each fund per round a deal with Deal type Investor and no linked services, so it stays out of the sales pipeline. A board grouped by your own round-stage field shows where each conversation stands. A cap table, a data room and deck analytics need separate tools.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Will AI replace the CRM?">
Not for a startup that hires. An assistant can do the typing, but your co-founder, your next hire and the assistant itself all work from the same record of who said what and where each deal stands. In Customermates Cloud, Mate updates records and saves reply drafts using hosted AI credits, and Claude or ChatGPT can do the same through MCP.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Customermates suit a B2C or e-commerce startup?">
Usually not. It is built for deals you win in conversations, one account at a time. It has no email campaigns, no bulk messaging and no web forms, so a shop with thousands of buyers is better served by a marketing or e-commerce platform.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

## Why freelancers need a CRM for client management

A CRM for freelancers is not a luxury: it is the system that keeps your business running while you focus on delivering great work. As a freelancer, you are the sales team, account manager, project manager, and billing department rolled into one. Without a structured way to manage your client pipeline, leads slip through the cracks, proposals go unfollowed, and the feast-or-famine cycle never ends.

If you have ever lost a lead because you forgot to reply to an email, missed a proposal follow-up, or realized too late that a past client would have hired you again, you already know the problem. A freelance CRM gives you the discipline and visibility to run the business side of freelancing without burning out.

Common challenges a freelancer CRM solves:

- **No pipeline visibility**: You know you need more work in two months, but you have no clear picture of what is in the pipeline right now.
- **Lost leads and missed follow-ups**: Someone reached out three weeks ago. You meant to reply but got buried in a project. Now they have hired someone else.
- **Repeat client neglect**: Your best clients from last year would hire you again, but you never stay in touch between projects.
- **Proposal chaos**: You sent proposals to five prospects. Which ones are still pending? Who needs a nudge? Without a system, this means digging through email threads.
- **Income unpredictability**: Without tracking your pipeline and expected revenue, you swing between being overloaded and panicking about the next gig.
- **Scattered tools**: Client details live in spreadsheets, project notes in one app, invoices in another, and communications in your inbox. Nothing connects.

Customermates brings the client side of this into one lightweight, affordable platform. It is a CRM, not an all-in-one freelance suite: it does not create proposals or invoices, track time or take payments, so you keep your invoicing tool next to it.

## How Customermates works as your freelance CRM

Customermates is lean enough for a solo freelancer and flexible enough to grow with you. Here is how the platform maps to your freelance workflow.

### Client pipeline management

Every freelance opportunity moves through stages, from first inquiry through proposal, negotiation, signed agreement, active project, and completion. Deals in Customermates are your projects, and the deal stage is a single-select column you configure. The [kanban board](/features/pipeline) shows each project as a card, with stages such as:

- **Inquiry received**: Someone reaches out about potential work
- **Discovery call**: You discuss scope, timeline, and fit
- **Proposal sent**: Formal quote or proposal delivered
- **Negotiation**: Scope, pricing, or timeline adjustments
- **Signed**: Agreement confirmed, project scheduled
- **In progress**: Actively delivering the work
- **Completed**: Delivered and invoiced in your invoicing tool

Drag and drop opportunities between stages. See your entire pipeline at a glance and know exactly where to focus your business development energy. Each stage can carry a probability, so weighted project values show what your open pipeline is realistically worth. This is a deterministic calculation from your own weights, not a prediction.

### Proposal tracking and follow-up

Lost proposals are lost revenue. Record every proposal as a deal in the **Proposal sent** stage with its value, and add custom date fields such as "Sent on" and "Decision expected". A saved view filtered to that stage and sorted by date shows every pending proposal at a glance.

Customermates does not send reminders. On Customermates Cloud, a [routine](/docs/app-routines) can do the checking for you: every Monday, the Mate assistant lists proposals that have not moved for a week and drafts a follow-up for each one for you to review. Starter includes [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] routine per user and Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]].

Keep an eye on your win rate too. If you know you close about 40% of proposals at a certain project size, you know how many proposals you need in the pipeline to hit your revenue goals. Export your deals to Excel (XLSX) or ask an AI client connected over MCP to calculate it from your records.

### Invoicing stays in your invoicing tool

Freelancers need to get paid on time, but Customermates does not create invoices, track payments or send payment reminders. Keep QuickBooks, Xero, FreshBooks, DATEV or whatever you invoice with. If you want the two connected, n8n that you operate separately (through the Customermates community node), the REST API or [webhooks](/features/workflow-automation) can, for example:

- Create an invoice draft in your accounting tool when a project moves to "Completed"
- Write the payment status from your accounting tool into a custom field on the client
- Show clients with an open balance in a saved view before you start new work

### Project management and delivery tracking

A CRM for independent professionals should not stop at the signed contract. Customermates keeps active work next to business development:

- **Project notes and milestones**: Store deliverable timelines, revision rounds, and status updates as notes and custom fields on the project
- **Time tracking stays external**: Keep Toggl, Harvest, or Clockify; if you want hours in the CRM, write them into a custom field through REST or your own n8n workflow
- **Capacity planning**: A saved view of deals in "In progress" shows how many active projects you are juggling
- **Upcoming wrap-ups**: A custom "Expected end" date and a view sorted by it show which projects finish soon, so you can line up the next engagement before a gap appears

### Repeat client nurturing

For most freelancers, repeat clients are the most profitable and reliable revenue source. Yet most freelancers have no system for staying in touch between projects. Customermates gives you structured relationship management:

- **Client relationship history**: Every past project, note and linked conversation sits with the client record. When a client returns, you have the context immediately.
- **Check-in views**: A custom "Last contact" date field and a saved view sorted by it show who you have not talked to in months; a cloud routine can check the list and draft a check-in for you to send.
- **Client rating**: Use single-select custom fields to rate clients by revenue, ease of collaboration, payment reliability, and referral potential, and focus on the clients who matter most.
- **Referral tracking**: Record which clients refer business to you in a referral-source field, and thank and nurture your referral sources.

## Automation that keeps your freelance pipeline moving

Business development falls apart first when freelancers get busy with client work. Customermates has no built-in rules engine, but it gives you several ways to take the repetitive parts off your plate:

- **Cloud routines**: the Mate assistant runs a saved prompt on a schedule or on a CRM event, for example "every Monday, list proposals without an answer for seven days and draft follow-ups" or "when a project moves to Completed, create a testimonial request task".
- **Webhooks and REST**: Customermates sends webhooks for [[derived.webhooks.events.total]] event types, and the REST API lets your own tools create records, for example when someone fills in the contact form on your website.
- **n8n**: if you run n8n yourself, the Customermates community node connects it to your invoicing, project management and email tools.

Routines run on Customermates Cloud and use hosted AI credits. Customermates does not send email sequences or payment reminders.

## AI agents that operate your CRM

Connect Claude, ChatGPT, or another AI client to Customermates through [MCP](/docs/mcp) ([[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools); you choose and pay for the AI client. The AI does the data entry, so your pipeline stays accurate while you focus on the work:

- **Draft proposal follow-ups** based on project context and your notes
- **Move deals through the pipeline and keep client notes current** after every conversation
- **Summarize client interaction history** before calls so you walk in fully prepared
- **Analyze your revenue patterns** from your deals to identify your most profitable project types, industries, and client segments
- **Draft personalized outreach emails** that reference specific client needs and your relevant experience
- **Spot upselling opportunities**: which existing clients could benefit from additional services?

On Customermates Cloud, the Mate assistant does the same inside the app and asks for your approval before it sends a message or deletes anything.

## Unified inbox across email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and Instagram

Clients reach you across channels: email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Telegram. On Pro and above, the unified inbox brings them into one place, and you link each conversation to the right record. Our [email integration](/features/email-integration) connects Gmail, Outlook, and IMAP mailboxes. No more searching through your inbox to find that conversation from three months ago.

When a past client messages you about new work, you see their history, past projects, rates, notes, and linked conversations. The inbox is part of Customermates Cloud and not available self-hosted.

## Reporting and pipeline analytics

Understand your freelance business with [dashboards](/features/reporting):

- **Pipeline value by stage**: How much potential revenue is in each stage right now?
- **Weighted pipeline**: What your open projects are realistically worth, based on your stage probabilities
- **Projects by type or source**: grouped by single-select fields such as project type or lead source (referral, website, LinkedIn, cold outreach)
- **Won value by client**: which clients bring the most work

Dashboards show current values. For trends over time or cycle times, export your deals as an Excel workbook (XLSX) or read them through REST.

## Data protection and privacy

Freelancers handle sensitive client information: business strategies, financial data, unreleased products, and confidential communications. Your CRM needs to protect that trust.

- **Cloud database in Frankfurt**: in the managed cloud, the application database runs in the AWS Frankfurt region; hosting and other subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [DPA](/dpa) is available
- **Self-hostable**: run the open-source core on your own infrastructure and choose where your data lives
- **Open source**: inspect the code and see how data is handled
- **Paid product**: Customermates is paid for by subscriptions, not by selling your data

Customermates holds no compliance certification. Whether your use is [GDPR](https://gdpr-info.eu/) compliant depends on how you process your clients' data; the DPA and the subprocessor list help you assess it, especially if you work with European clients.

A freelancer CRM earns its place when your work depends on relationships: a former client who comes back every spring, a referral from an agency you delivered for, a proposal that waits while you are busy on another project. Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM made in Germany. For a one-person B2B business it keeps clients, the people you work with, open work opportunities and your next step together, and on Customermates Cloud from Pro it adds the email and LinkedIn conversations around them. Mate, or your own AI agent through MCP, drafts the reply and the next step, and you review, edit and send. It is not a booking tool and not a billing tool. If your day starts with appointments, invoices or a client portal, the section on what it does not do names tools built for that.

## Who this fits, and who should look elsewhere

Customermates suits solo work that is won in conversations: a developer who takes on projects from three or four agencies, a designer with a handful of retained clients, a translator whose work comes from repeat B2B customers. The question it answers is "who am I working with, what is open, and what do I do next with them?"

It does not suit a business that runs on bookings and payments. There is no booking page, no calendar booking, no web form on your site that creates records, no invoice and no payment tracking. There is also no native mobile app, no phone dialer and no SMS. If those are the reasons you are looking for software, a freelance suite fits better, and you should know that before you sign up.

## The example freelance workspace

The demo opens an example workspace with sample data, not a customer's account. It has five lists:

- **Clients**: the companies you work for or want to work for.
- **Contacts**: the people at those clients, and the people who refer you.
- **Inquiries**: each inquiry or possible project, with its stage.
- **Projects**: work you agreed to do, with a day rate, a number of days and a fee calculated from both.
- **Tasks**: concrete next steps, linked to a client or inquiry.

Inquiries move through Inquiry, First call, Proposal sent, Won and Lost; won work continues as a project with a status from Planned to Billed. Billed is only a label: billing stays in your invoicing tool. Open the client list, then one client: the detail page shows the linked contacts, the running project with its calculated fee, the open inquiry, recent notes and conversation context, and a **Next action** date. That date is a field configured in this workspace, not a built-in reminder. Tasks in Customermates have no built-in due dates, statuses or reminders, so the example adds a date field and a saved view sorted by it. Projects keep their files, such as the proposal PDF or test findings, in a File field, and a **Project calendar** view shows each project from start to deadline. The Calendar page adds the next actions and the calls from the connected Google calendar, and the dashboard lists the next actions of the coming two weeks.

## Remember the relationship

Most freelance work comes from people who already know you. The risk is not losing a lead, it is forgetting that a client said "talk to us again in March". Each client record keeps its contacts, the opportunities you discussed, your notes and, on Customermates Cloud from Pro, the email or chat threads you link to it. You configure the fields that matter to your work: a Source field with a choice of Referral, Repeat client or LinkedIn, a Referred by relationship to a contact, or a Last project date.

Fields come in the types you would expect: text, formatted text, number, money, yes or no, date, date range, single or multiple choice, web address, address and file. Phone numbers, email addresses and social handles go into the Contact details field. There are no record tags; a multiple-choice field does the same job. A proposal PDF goes into a File field on the inquiry or project.

## Track new work

An inquiry is one possible piece of work with one client; once it is won, the work continues as a project. The [pipeline board](/features/pipeline) shows each stage as a column, and you drag a card when something changes. Each stage option can carry a win probability, so the board can show an opportunity's value next to its weighted value. That is weighting with your own percentages, not a forecast.

A few saved views cover a weekly check: open opportunities in Proposal sent sorted by last update, clients sorted by Next action, and past clients you have not spoken to for a while. A saved view stores filters, sort and columns together. The [records documentation](/docs/app-records) explains views, fields and import.

## Prepare one reply with permitted context

On Customermates Cloud, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings email from Gmail, Outlook or IMAP together with LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram. The plan decides what you can connect:

- **Starter and self-hosted:** no connected messaging accounts.
- **Pro:** your own accounts, with [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] connected account per user included, plus threads you share individually.
- **Business, Max or Enterprise:** needed if you want to share a whole account with someone else.

Participants are matched to contacts by their email address, phone number or LinkedIn identity. A conversation is linked to a client or opportunity only when you, or an agent you ask, links it. Nothing is filed to an opportunity automatically. Messages inside freelance marketplaces are not an inbox channel.

Mate, the assistant in Customermates Cloud, can read a client record and its linked conversation and draft a reply for you to check. It runs on every paid cloud plan within your plan's hosted AI credits and is not available in self-hosting. Mate creates, updates and sends as soon as you ask; only deletions, imports, access changes and destructive data-model changes stop for your approval. Ask for a draft, and it waits in the conversation until you edit or send it. A [routine](/docs/app-routines) lets Mate run a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes, for example a Monday list of opportunities whose Next action date has passed. Starter includes [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] routine per user and Pro [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]].

If you prefer your own AI client, connect Claude, ChatGPT or another client through [MCP](/docs/mcp). It works on every plan, including self-hosted, and your AI provider bills the model use.

## Use it alongside billing and booking tools

Customermates handles the client relationship and new work. Invoicing, payments, time tracking, contracts and appointment booking stay in their own tools. A webhook fires when a record changes, so an n8n workflow you run separately, or your own script against the [REST API](/features/api), can pass an accepted opportunity to your accounting tool, provided that tool has an API.

If one of the missing pieces is the main reason you are shopping, look at a tool built for it. Customermates publishes this page; vendor details were checked on 8 October 2026 on each vendor's own website.

## Setting up your own workspace

The demo lists are examples. A new workspace starts without lists, and you decide what goes in it:

- **On Customermates Cloud**, the AI workspace setup asks about your work and builds lists, stages, a dashboard and Knowledge Base pages from your answers. It runs once during onboarding, within a credit cap. You can skip it with **Start with an empty workspace**.
- **Otherwise**, anyone with Data model rights creates lists and fields in Data model, then imports existing records from an Excel workbook under **More actions**, **Add from file**, one list at a time. An import cannot be undone, so try a small file first.

If you are coming from a spreadsheet, our [Excel CRM template guide](/blog/excel-crm-template) shows which columns carry over.

## Pricing and ownership

Prices are per user per month, billed monthly. There is a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial on Customermates Cloud without a credit card, and a free plan for [[commercial.limit.free.maxMembers]] person and [[commercial.limit.free.maxRecords]] records, without the inbox, Mate or routines.

| Setup | Plan | Per month |
|---|---|---|
| Clients, opportunities and views, no inbox | Starter | [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] |
| Your own mailbox or one chat account in the inbox | Pro | [[commercial.price.pro.monthly]] |
| More connected accounts and Mate usage | Business | [[commercial.price.business.monthly]] |

Every paid plan includes the REST API, webhooks and MCP. Hosted AI usage for Mate grows with the plan; the [pricing page](/pricing) has the details. The open-source core runs on your own server without a licence fee, but a [self-hosted installation](/features/self-hosted) has the Starter feature set: no inbox, no Mate and no AI workspace setup.

On Customermates Cloud the primary database is in the AWS Frankfurt region. Subprocessors, US providers among them, are on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [data processing agreement](/dpa) is available. Customermates holds no certification such as ISO 27001, and whether your use meets GDPR depends on how you process your clients' data. Each list exports as an Excel workbook or JSON; there is no whole-account export.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Do freelancers really need a CRM?">
Not on day one. A spreadsheet works while you have a few clients and remember every open proposal. A CRM starts to pay off when proposals wait for weeks while you deliver, when past clients write on different channels, or when you want one place to see what to do next with each client.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is Customermates a CRM for solopreneurs or only for teams?">
It works for one person. Starter is billed per user, so a solopreneur pays for one seat. Team features such as roles and shared threads are there if you add a partner later, but you do not need them to start.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can Customermates send invoices or take bookings?">
No. It has no invoices, payments, time tracking, booking page or calendar booking. Keep those tools and connect them through webhooks, the REST API or an n8n workflow you run separately, if they offer an API.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a mobile app, a web form or SMS?">
No. There is no native mobile app, no web form that creates records from your website, and no telephony or SMS.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does a new workspace come with these lists?">
No. A new workspace starts without lists. On Customermates Cloud the AI workspace setup can build lists, stages, a dashboard and Knowledge Base pages from your description, once during onboarding. Otherwise you create them in Data model. The demo lists are an example.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a free CRM for freelancers?">
HubSpot's free CRM covers up to two users and 1,000 contacts, and CentralStationCRM has a free plan for 3 users and 200 contacts (checked on 8 October 2026). Customermates has a free plan for [[commercial.limit.free.maxMembers]] person and [[commercial.limit.free.maxRecords]] records, without the inbox, Mate or routines, a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial, Starter at [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per month, and an open-source core you can host yourself. Our [free CRM guide](/blog/free-crm) compares the options.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

## Why coaches need a CRM for their coaching business

If you are a life coach, business coach, executive coach, or health coach, your CRM for coaches should do more than store contact details. Your practice is built on relationships, and every missed follow-up, lost session note, or forgotten prospect costs you revenue and credibility. A coaching CRM gives you the structure to keep business development running while you focus on client work.

Common challenges that a CRM for coaching business helps with:

- **Client details scattered across tools**: Session notes in one app, contact info in another, payment history in your email. You waste time searching instead of coaching.
- **Follow-ups fall through the cracks**: A prospect had a discovery call weeks ago, expressed genuine interest, and then you forgot to send the proposal. Without a system, warm leads go cold.
- **No pipeline visibility**: How many prospects are evaluating your program? What is the open pipeline worth? Without a coaching CRM, estimating income is guesswork.
- **Administrative overload**: Coaches spend hours each week on manual data entry, copy-pasting between tools, and updating spreadsheets.
- **Scaling breaks your systems**: Moving from 10 to 50 clients means your informal tracking collapses.
- **No referral tracking**: Past clients send referrals, but you have no record of who referred whom.

A CRM for coaches helps by centralizing your client data and giving you a clear view of your pipeline and open follow-ups.

## How Customermates works as your coaching CRM

Customermates is a CRM, not a scheduling or session-delivery app. It covers the relationship and business development side of a coaching practice. Here is how it maps to your coaching workflow.

### Client relationship tracking

Every client and prospect gets a record: contact details, notes, activity history, deal information, and custom data fields. When a client reaches out, you have the context at hand, not just what you remember from three months ago.

Link contacts to companies and deals, and record the referral source in a custom field. [Contact management](/features/contact-management) keeps every relationship in view, even with many active clients.

### Coaching pipeline with Kanban view

Coaching sales are not one-call closes. They involve initial inquiries, discovery calls, proposal discussions, enrollment decisions, and onboarding. Customermates gives you a [visual pipeline](/features/pipeline) with a Kanban board where you customize stages for your coaching sales process:

- **New inquiry**: First contact, initial interest expressed
- **Discovery call scheduled**: Qualifying conversation booked
- **Discovery call completed**: Needs assessed, fit confirmed
- **Proposal sent**: Program details and pricing delivered
- **Enrolled**: Client has committed
- **Active client**: Coaching engagement underway

Drag and drop prospects between stages and see where to focus. The stage column is shared by all deals; if you run several programs, such as group coaching, one-on-one, and corporate workshops, add a "Program" single-select field and use a saved view or grouping per program.

### Follow-ups and task management

The biggest revenue leak for coaches is inconsistent follow-up. Customermates tasks are linked to clients and deals, but they have no due dates or reminders, and there is no built-in rules engine. Use these instead:

- **Saved views**: prospects in "Proposal sent" sorted by last update, or clients whose "Package end date" field falls in the next 30 days.
- **Cloud routines**: on Customermates Cloud, a [routine](/docs/app-routines) runs a saved prompt on a schedule or on a CRM event, so the Mate assistant can list quiet prospects every Monday and draft check-in messages for you to review.
- **Your own automation**: [webhooks](/features/workflow-automation) and the REST API let an external tool such as n8n (operated separately) create a task when a discovery call is marked completed, or add a new lead from your intake form tool.

Customermates does not send email sequences or reminder sequences to groups of participants.

### Custom fields for coaching data

Every coaching practice tracks different data. Customermates lets you add typed custom fields for what matters:

- Coaching niche (life, business, executive, health, career)
- Program type (one-on-one, group, corporate, hybrid)
- Session count and frequency
- Package value and payment status
- Referral source
- Client goals and outcomes
- Preferred communication channel
- Package end date

Your CRM for coaching business should model your actual practice, not force you into a generic sales template.

### Unified inbox and communication tracking

On Pro and above, email, WhatsApp, and Instagram messages come together in one [unified inbox](/features/email-integration), and you link each conversation to the client's record. Connect Gmail, Outlook, or IMAP mailboxes to keep client communication in one place, and let the Mate assistant draft replies that you confirm before they are sent.

For coaches who rely heavily on email and messaging, this cuts down context-switching.

## AI clients that operate your CRM

Connect Claude, ChatGPT, or another AI client through the MCP server ([[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools) and let it do the data entry. You choose and pay for the AI client. It can create client records, move prospects between pipeline stages, and log session notes, and it can also:

- **Draft follow-up emails** based on session notes and previous communications
- **Summarize client interaction history** before meetings so you walk in prepared
- **Review your pipeline** to show which programs have the most open deals and where prospects stall
- **Compose personalized outreach** that references specific client goals
- **Spot patterns in your client base**: for example, which referral sources bring the highest-value clients

The AI handles drafting and review. You focus on coaching and building relationships.

## Reporting and pipeline analytics

Understand your practice's business development with [dashboards](/features/reporting) built from your own records:

- **Pipeline value by stage**: How much sits in proposal stage vs. enrolled vs. active?
- **Won deals by program type**: Which coaching programs close most often?
- **Deal value by referral source**: Which channels bring the most valuable clients?
- **Weighted pipeline totals**: Calculated from the probabilities you set on each stage

Build dashboards from current CRM values, export a view as an Excel workbook (XLSX), or read data through the REST API for reports in a BI tool you operate.

## Built for data security and privacy

Coaches handle sensitive personal information: client goals, personal challenges, and confidential business details.

- **Cloud database in Frankfurt**: In the managed cloud, the application database runs in the AWS Frankfurt region; hosting and other subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors)
- **DPA available**: A [data processing agreement](/dpa) under Art. 28 GDPR
- **Self-hostable**: For coaches and coaching firms with strict data requirements, run the open-source core on your own infrastructure and choose where data lives
- **Open source**: Inspect the code and audit the security
- **Role-based access**: Control who can view, edit, or delete which records

This matters especially for life coaches and executive coaches where client confidentiality is the foundation of the relationship.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="What is a CRM for coaches?">
A CRM for coaches is a customer relationship management platform for coaching professionals: life coaches, business coaches, executive coaches, health coaches, and career coaches. It focuses on managing client relationships, tracking your coaching pipeline from inquiry to enrollment, and keeping detailed client records. Unlike generic sales CRMs built for high-volume selling, a coaching CRM emphasizes relationship depth and pipeline discipline tailored to how coaches sell.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Do I need a CRM as a solo coach?">
Often, yes. Solo coaches have no assistant or support team to catch dropped follow-ups. When you are the coach, the salesperson, and the administrator at once, a CRM gives you one place for clients, open proposals, and notes, starting at [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]]/month with Customermates.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How is Customermates different from coaching-specific tools like Paperbell or CoachAccountable?">
Paperbell and CoachAccountable focus on scheduling, session delivery, payments, and client portals. Customermates is a CRM that focuses on the business development side: your sales pipeline, prospect and client relationships, dashboards, and AI clients through MCP. It does not book sessions or take payments. If you need a tool to run coaching sessions, those platforms serve that purpose; many coaches use one of them next to a CRM.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can Customermates replace my spreadsheet for tracking coaching clients?">
Yes. Spreadsheets break down as your practice grows: they do not link conversations to client records, offer no pipeline board, and make it hard to see where prospects stall. Customermates gives you records, a pipeline, a unified inbox on Pro, and dashboards, and you can import your existing spreadsheet as an Excel workbook (XLSX).
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Customermates send follow-up reminders?">
No. Tasks have no due dates or reminders. Use saved views sorted by date fields to see what is due. On Customermates Cloud, a routine can have the Mate assistant check your pipeline on a schedule and draft follow-ups, and an external tool can do the same through the REST API.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How does Customermates handle GDPR for coaching client data?">
GDPR compliance depends on how you use any tool. Customermates provides a [DPA](/dpa), discloses its subprocessors (including US providers under standard contractual clauses) on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and runs the cloud database in the AWS Frankfurt region. It holds no certification such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2. For maximum control, especially with executive clients under NDA, you can self-host the open-source core, and you or your IT provider can audit the code.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I integrate Customermates with my other coaching tools?">
Yes, through the REST API, webhooks, and [n8n](/features/workflow-automation), which you operate separately and which connects through the Customermates community node. That lets you build workflows with scheduling platforms (Calendly, Cal.com), payment processors (Stripe, PayPal), email marketing tools, and accounting software. You build and run those workflows yourself.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How does a CRM help me get more coaching clients?">
A CRM helps you follow up consistently. It shows every inquiry and open proposal, makes it visible when prospects go quiet, and lets you see which referral sources bring your best clients. Whether that turns into more clients depends on the follow-up you do with it.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

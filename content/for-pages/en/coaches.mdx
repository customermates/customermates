After a discovery call the sale often hangs on who follows up, and how fast. Inquiries go quiet, and a package ends without anyone starting the renewal conversation. Customermates keeps those moments in view. Email and WhatsApp threads sit on the client record, each package has an end date and dated next steps, and Mate or your own AI agent (Claude, ChatGPT or any client that speaks MCP) drafts the reply and the next step for you to review, edit and send. The inbox needs Customermates Cloud from Pro, and the number of connected accounts depends on the plan. It does not book sessions or take payments, so many coaches pair it with a delivery tool; the table further down names some.

## What a coaching practice needs from a CRM

Coaching sells differently from most small businesses, and the differences decide which fields and views you need:

- **The discovery call is the sale.** Many clients decide after one conversation. What matters is how quickly you follow up afterwards, and with what.
- **You sell packages, not hours.** A 10-session package or a six-month program has a start, an end and a renewal conversation that someone has to start.
- **The payer is often not the client.** In executive and leadership coaching, HR or a manager books and pays, and the coachee is the person you work with. Both belong in the record.
- **Referrals are often the main channel.** If you never record who sent a client, you cannot thank them or see which relationships bring work.
- **Clients write wherever they like.** One sends email, another sends WhatsApp voice notes, a third found you on Instagram.

## A coaching setup in Customermates

A new workspace starts without lists: you create the lists you need (contacts, organizations, deals or your own) in **Configure**, or let AI setup build them in the cloud. Here is one way to map five lists for a coaching practice; every field name is an example you can change.

| Record type | Used for | Example fields |
|---|---|---|
| Contacts | Clients, prospects, referrers, HR contacts | Role (single choice: Coachee, Sponsor, Referrer), Referred by, Coaching focus (single choice: Leadership, Career, Business, Health) |
| Organizations | Companies that pay for coaching | Relationship (single choice: Prospect, Active, Past) |
| Deals | Each package or program | Stage, Format (single choice: 1:1, Group, Team workshop), Package starts, Package ends |
| Services | What you sell, each with a price | Coaching session, Discovery workshop, Team day |
| Tasks | Next steps, assigned to a person | A "Follow up on" date field you add, because tasks have no built-in due date |

A contact can link to several organizations and deals, so the HR contact who booked three coachees appears on all three packages.

### Stages and package value

The deal stage is a single choice field you define, and each option gets a win probability. A starting point: Inquiry 10%, Discovery call booked 25%, Discovery call done 50%, Offer sent 70%, Enrolled 100%, Not now 0%. Keep "Enrolled" as the won stage and track the active period with the two date fields, so the board shows the sales funnel and your views show the running packages.

A deal's value is the sum of its line items, each a service times quantity. If the service "Coaching session" carries your rate of €180 and a package includes 10 sessions, the deal is worth €1,800, and in Offer sent its weighted value is €1,260. That is arithmetic from your own probabilities, not a prediction. On the [pipeline board](/features/pipeline) each package is a card you drag between stages.

### Saved views for Monday morning

1. **Waiting after the discovery call:** deals in Discovery call done or Offer sent, sorted by last update, oldest first.
2. **Packages ending:** deals in Enrolled, sorted by Package ends.
3. **Referrers:** contacts with Role Referrer, sorted by last update, so you can see whom you have not thanked yet.
4. **Corporate clients:** organizations with Relationship Active or Past, sorted by last update.

If you work with an assistant or a second coach, share a view such as **Referrers** from its menu, and you both see the same list, always current.

### Client messages in one inbox (Pro and above)

In Customermates Cloud from Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings email from Gmail, Outlook or IMAP together with WhatsApp, Instagram, LinkedIn and Telegram. You reply on the original channel. Add a sender's address or number to a contact once, and every conversation with them appears on the contact.

Check the account count before you choose a plan. Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] connected account per user, so a coach who wants a mailbox and WhatsApp in the inbox needs Business, which includes [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedAccountsPerUser]]. Conversations from your own accounts stay private: colleagues see a thread only after you share it.

### Weekly renewal check with Mate (Customermates Cloud)

Mate is the assistant inside Customermates Cloud. It runs on hosted AI credits that every paid cloud plan includes per active user; reading inbox conversations needs Pro or higher. Before a session, ask in the chat:

> Summarize my history with Anna Weber: her package, the last three conversations and anything she asked me to send.

For renewals, set up a [routine](/docs/app-routines), a saved instruction that Mate runs on a schedule or when a record changes. Choose **Every week** under **Repeats**, Monday at 08:00, with instructions like these:

```text
Find deals in the stage Enrolled whose Package ends date is within the
next 21 days. For each one, read the latest inbox conversation with the
client. Save a short draft that asks how the work is going and offers a
call about next steps. Do not send anything. End with a list: client,
package end date, draft saved yes or no.
```

Each run has a credit cap, so keep the instruction to one clear job; the [routine limits](/docs/app-routines) list the current values.

<AcquisitionCallout title="Ask for drafts, not sends" variant="boundary">

A second routine can react to a change instead: when a deal's Stage changes to Enrolled, create a task "Send intake questionnaire" linked to that deal and assigned to you. Starter includes [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] routine per user and Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]].

Prefer your own AI client? Connect Claude, ChatGPT or another client through [MCP](/docs/mcp). It works on every plan and on self-hosted installations, with model use billed by your AI provider.

## What Customermates does not do for coaches

- No session booking, payments, contracts, client portal, worksheets or course delivery.
- No session counter. Count a package's sessions in your booking tool, or keep the number used in a Number field on the deal and update it yourself.
- Tasks have no due dates, reminders or notifications. Add a date field and a saved view, or let a routine do the checking.
- No newsletters, email sequences or bulk sends to a group of clients.
- No mobile app.

If one of these is the reason you are shopping, look at a tool built for delivery. Customermates publishes this page; vendor details were checked on 8 October 2026 on each vendor's own website.

| If you need | Look at | What the vendor's site lists |

| Packages, booking and payments with a client portal | [Paperbell](https://paperbell.com/) | Packages of appointments, payments through your own Stripe or PayPal account, contract signing, a client portal for booking and a free plan |
| Session notes, action items and client progress | [CoachAccountable](https://www.coachaccountable.com/tour) | Session notes, actions, metrics, worksheets, courses and a client portal |
| Coaching engagements with goals and contracts | [Simply.Coach](https://simply.coach/) | Goal and development planning, subscription and session packages, contracts, invoicing and payments, prospect management |
| Proposals, contracts and payments in one client flow | [HoneyBook](https://www.honeybook.com/life-coach-software) | Proposals, online contracts, scheduler, payments and a client portal; [available](https://www.honeybook.com/product/integrations/chatgpt) in the U.S., Canada, the UK and Australia |
| A free contact database to start | [HubSpot free CRM](https://www.hubspot.com/products/crm) | Free for up to two users and 1,000 contacts |

One workable split is two systems: a coaching platform for delivery and Customermates for inquiries, renewals and conversations. A [webhook](/docs/webhooks) fires when a deal changes, so an n8n workflow you run separately can pass data to the other tool, provided that tool has an API.

## Client confidentiality

Coaching conversations are personal. Decide what belongs in a CRM: contact details, package, stage and the outline of the relationship. Detailed session notes often fit better in a delivery tool or your own files. If you record health or mental-health details, that is special category data under [Art. 9 GDPR](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-9-gdpr/), with stricter conditions.

- In Customermates Cloud, the primary production database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region. Hosting and the other subprocessors, US providers and Mate's AI providers among them, are on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [data processing agreement](/dpa) is available.
- A custom role with Assigned read access limits an associate coach to the clients assigned to them.
- The activity log records who changed which record.
- Customermates holds no certification such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="What software is best for coaches?">
It depends on the job. For booking, payments and a client portal, a coaching platform such as Paperbell, CoachAccountable or Simply.Coach fits. In the U.S., Canada, the UK or Australia, HoneyBook combines proposals, contracts and payments. If your problem is follow-up on inquiries and renewals across email and WhatsApp, a CRM such as Customermates fits; it is our own product, so weigh that.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Do I need a CRM as a solo coach?">
A spreadsheet works while you can remember every open inquiry. It stops working when discovery calls run in parallel with full client weeks. A solo coach pays [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per month on Starter, or [[commercial.price.pro.monthly]] on Pro with one mailbox in the inbox.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can Customermates count the sessions left in a package?">
No. It has no session counter. It records the package value from sessions times your rate, and the start and end dates, so you can see which packages end soon. Count sessions in your booking tool, or keep the number used in a Number field you update yourself.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How do coaches get more clients with a CRM?">
Mostly through follow-up and referrals. The view of people waiting after a discovery call shows who has not heard from you, and the Referred by field shows which past clients send new ones. Whether that turns into clients depends on the conversations you then have.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Will AI replace the CRM?">
AI changes who types into the CRM more than whether you need one. Mate or an MCP client such as Claude can log a call, move a deal and save a reply draft, but it needs client history, stages and dates to work from, and those live in the CRM. Say in each request or routine whether it should send or only save a draft.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I connect Calendly or Stripe?">
There is no ready-made connection. Through the REST API, webhooks and the Customermates node for [n8n](/docs/n8n), which you run separately, you can build workflows with tools that have their own API, such as a booking or payment service.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

## What a CRM for therapists is for

A **CRM for therapists** manages the business side of a private practice: the inquiries that arrive by email and contact form, the steps between first contact and a first session, and the referral relationships with physicians, colleagues, schools and employers that keep a caseload full. It is not an electronic health record and it is not where session notes, diagnoses or treatment plans belong.

Keeping that line clear is the most important design decision. Clinical records belong in practice management or EHR software built and contracted for protected health information. A CRM holds the relationships around the practice.

Therapists in private practice usually run into the same problems:

- **Inquiries wait too long.** A prospective client emails on Sunday, you answer on Wednesday, and they have already booked with someone else.
- **Nobody knows where an inquiry stands.** Was the consultation call offered, held, or never answered?
- **Referral sources are invisible.** You know a few physicians send clients, but not who, how often, or when you last thanked them.
- **The waitlist lives on paper.** When a slot opens, finding the right person to call takes an hour.

## How therapists use Customermates

### An intake pipeline from inquiry to first session

Use deals as inquiries and configure the stages to match your process, for example: **Inquiry → Consultation offered → Consultation held → Intake sent → First session booked → Not a fit / Referred on**. The [kanban board](/features/pipeline) shows every open inquiry at a glance, and a view of inquiries without a reply is your daily to-do list. Keep the entries to contact and scheduling details, not clinical content.

### Referral source tracking

Create organizations for the practices, clinics, schools and employers that refer to you, and contacts for the people there. A single-select **Referral source** field on each inquiry lets you group and count inquiries per source, so you see which relationships send clients who fit your specialism. See [contact management](/features/contact-management).

### A waitlist you can actually use

A **Waitlist** stage plus fields such as preferred times, session format (in person or online) and requested specialism turns the waitlist into a filterable view. When a slot opens, filter and call.

### Inquiry email in one place

On Customermates Cloud from Pro, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings a connected Gmail, Outlook or IMAP account into the CRM, and you link each conversation to the contact who made the inquiry. LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram can be connected too, which helps with referral partners and professional networking. Conversations from your connected accounts stay private until you share a single conversation with the team in the CRM; sharing a whole account requires Business. The [Gmail CRM integration guide](/blog/gmail-crm-integration) explains folders, sharing and replies in detail.

### AI for drafting, not for clinical content

On Customermates Cloud, the Mate assistant can draft a reply to an inquiry or a thank-you note to a referral partner for you to review; it asks before sending anything. External AI clients connect through [MCP](/docs/mcp) with the provider you choose. Whichever you use, keep clinical information out of the CRM and out of prompts.

## Privacy and data protection

Therapy inquiries are sensitive even before someone becomes a client, so be deliberate about where data goes:

- **Store the minimum.** Name, contact details, how they found you and where the inquiry stands are enough for the business side.
- **Keep clinical records elsewhere.** Session notes, assessments and diagnoses belong in your clinical system. In the United States, software that holds protected health information on your behalf generally needs a [business associate agreement](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/covered-entities/sample-business-associate-agreement-provisions/index.html) under HIPAA; Customermates is not positioned as a HIPAA system.
- **Know where the CRM runs.** On Customermates Cloud, the application database runs in the AWS Frankfurt region; hosting and other subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [DPA](/dpa) is available. Customermates holds no compliance certification. If you need full control over the infrastructure, you can [self-host](/features/self-hosted) the open-source core.
- **Limit access.** Roles control who can read and change records; conversations from your connected accounts stay private until you share a single conversation.

## Therapist CRM or practice management software?

| Job                                               | Where it belongs                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Session notes, diagnoses, treatment plans         | Practice management or EHR software                         |
| Client scheduling, billing and insurance claims   | Practice management software                                |
| Inquiries before intake and their status          | A CRM such as Customermates                                 |
| Referral partners and how often they refer        | A CRM such as Customermates                                 |

Practice management suites such as SimplePractice combine scheduling, notes and billing; on 27 September 2026, [SimplePractice](https://www.simplepractice.com/pricing/) listed plans from $49 per month. A CRM does not replace that. It is worth adding when your problem is the pipeline of new clients and the relationships that feed it, and when you want that work outside the clinical system.

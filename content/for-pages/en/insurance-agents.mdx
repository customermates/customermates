## CRM or AMS: which does an insurance agency need?

An agency-management system typically maintains policies, carrier downloads, documents, claims activity, commissions, and accounting. A CRM focuses on prospective business, client relationships, referral sources, conversations, and sales follow-up. Some insurance suites combine both categories, but the capabilities should still be evaluated separately.

Customermates is a general CRM. It does not replace an AMS, policy-administration system, carrier portal, claims platform, rating engine, or commission ledger.

| Insurance-agency need | Customermates | AMS or specialist system |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prospect and relationship context | Contacts, organizations, relations, notes, and activity | Authoritative insured and policy file |
| Sales opportunity | Deals, custom stages, filters, and kanban | Quote generation and carrier submission |
| Renewal conversation | Reference fields, filtered views, and tasks | Policy term, carrier data, and renewal processing |
| Claims relationship follow-up | Contact context and a manual next action | FNOL, claim file, adjustment, and settlement |
| Integration | REST, webhooks, MCP, and separate n8n option | Carrier, comparative rater, document, and accounting interfaces |

## A supported insurance-sales workflow

Create a deal for a prospective relationship or renewal conversation. Connect it to the client contact, the relevant organization, and the introducer where useful. Configure stages around the commercial interaction rather than carrier processing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the prospect context">
    Add the person or business and preserve the source of the relationship
    without copying a complete policy or claims file into the CRM.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the sales stages">
    Use stages such as inquiry, needs discussion, quote prepared externally,
    customer decision, won, or closed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add bounded reference fields">
    Store a product category, carrier reference, renewal month, referral source,
    or external AMS identifier when the agency's data policy permits it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record the next action">
    Attach a task to the relevant CRM record. The task has no native due date,
    recurrence, or automatic renewal schedule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue insurance operations in the AMS">
    Quoting, submission, binding, endorsements, claims, documents, and
    commissions stay in the system that owns those records.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Renewal follow-up without a renewal engine

A renewal month or external policy reference can help a user filter CRM opportunities and prepare a relationship review. A customer-operated scheduler or separate [n8n workflow](/features/workflow-automation) can create a task from an approved source date. Customermates does not calculate policy terms, start reminder sequences, send bulk renewal campaigns, or prevent a policy from lapsing.

The same boundary applies to claims. A user can record that a relationship follow-up is needed, but Customermates does not accept first notice of loss, maintain the claim file, assign adjusters, calculate reserves, adjudicate coverage, or issue settlement communications.

## Cross-sell context and commissions

Custom fields may hold high-level product references selected by the agency. Filtered views can help a user inspect those records. Customermates does not infer coverage gaps, recommend products, score clients, or run cross-sell campaigns.

Commission rates, statements, reconciliation, clawbacks, and revenue forecasting belong in the AMS or accounting system. A configurable numeric field is not commission accounting and should not be described as one.

## Individual conversations, AI, and integrations

The managed [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) is available from Pro upward. It brings supported individual email and messaging conversations into the CRM, where participants can be associated with contact context. It is not a bulk campaign or sequence tool, and a message is not automatically attached to a policy or claim. Self-hosted deployments do not include the inbox.

An authorized external AI client can use supported [MCP](/docs/mcp) actions on CRM records. It does not quote insurance, determine coverage, analyze claims, calculate commissions, or replace a licensed professional's review.

Customer-built connections can use the [REST API and webhooks](/features/api), MCP, or the separate n8n integration. The customer owns carrier and vendor contracts, field mapping, permissions, validation, retries, and duplicate handling. No native carrier-download, comparative-rater, policy, or claims integration is claimed.

## Data and deployment boundaries

Keep policy documents, coverage details, health information, claim files, commission statements, and regulated advice in systems approved for them. Customermates permissions operate by resource and action, not by field or individual record. It carries no insurance or GDPR compliance certification.

Starter includes the core CRM. Pro adds the managed unified inbox and one connected account per user. Self-hosted deployments use Starter entitlements and do not include the unified inbox. See the [pricing page](/pricing) for current plan details.

Customermates fits an insurance agency that already has an agency management system (AMS) and wants a separate place for selling: new prospects, renewal reviews, cross-sell conversations and the people who refer business. It is an open-source, AI-native CRM built in Germany. It does not manage policies, download carrier data, rate quotes or reconcile commissions. If you want one system that does all of that, you need an AMS or an insurance suite, and the table further down names some.

## CRM or AMS: what an agency actually needs

EZLynx, which sells an AMS, [puts the split in one line](https://www.ezlynx.com/blog/posts/insurance-crm-vs-insurance-agency-management-system-whats-the-difference/): agencies choose a CRM "when they sell more than they service, and an AMS when they service more than they sell." The AMS is the source of truth for active policies, claims history and carrier data. A CRM tracks who you are talking to, what you offered and what happens next.

Many products sold as insurance CRMs are really both. AgencyBloc describes its agency management system as built for health, life and group benefits agencies, with commission reconciliation. HawkSoft lists carrier downloads, accounting and a rater. If you have no AMS yet, start there. If you have one and your sales follow-up still lives in a notebook or a spreadsheet, a CRM beside it makes sense.

What insurance selling asks of that CRM:

- **Renewal dates set the rhythm.** A renewal review only helps if it happens weeks before the renewal date, while there is still time to remarket. You need a list sorted by that date.
- **Households and businesses.** A personal-lines client is one person; a commercial client is a company with an owner, an office manager and a bookkeeper.
- **Referral partners matter.** Mortgage brokers, real estate agents and accountants send business, and they go quiet if nobody follows up.
- **Quotes happen elsewhere.** The rater or carrier portal produces the quote. The CRM only needs to know that a quote went out, for what, and when to chase it.
- **Clients write on WhatsApp.** Many clients send a photo of a letter or a quick question on WhatsApp rather than email.

## An insurance setup in Customermates

Customermates has five record types. One way to map them for an agency; every field name below is an example you create yourself.

| Record type | Used for | Example custom fields |
|---|---|---|
| Contacts | Prospects, clients and people at business clients | Client status (single select: Prospect, Client, Former client), Lead source, AMS client ID |
| Organizations | Business clients and referral partners | Type (single select: Business client, Referral partner), Partner category (Mortgage, Real estate, Accounting) |
| Deals | Each new-business opportunity, cross-sell or renewal review | Stage, Line of business (Auto, Home, Life, Health, Commercial), Carrier, Renewal date (Date), Estimated premium (Currency), Quote reference |
| Services | Lines of business you sell | Optional; only linked services (price times quantity) make up the deal value |
| Tasks | Next steps assigned to someone | A "Due on" date field, because tasks have no built-in due date |

A contact can link to several deals, so one client can have an auto renewal and a life opportunity open at the same time. Keep the policy number as a reference field if your data policy allows it, and treat the AMS as the record of the policy itself.

### Stages and weighted values

The stage is a single-select field you define, and each option gets a win probability in **My Company › Settings**. A starting point: New lead 10%, Needs analysis 25%, Quote sent 50%, Decision pending 75%, Bound 100%, Lost 0%. For renewal reviews, a second single-select field, Renewal status, with Review due, Contacted, Remarketed, Renewed and Moved, gives you its own board when you group by it. The weighted pipeline is deal value times stage probability. The deal value comes from linked services; a custom Estimated premium field is not part of the calculation. It is arithmetic from your own numbers, not a forecast, and it is not commission.

### Saved views for the week

1. **Renewals coming up:** deals with a Renewal date on or after today's date, sorted ascending. Custom date fields filter with fixed dates (before, after, between), not "next 60 days", so update the date when you open the view or let a routine do the counting.
2. **Quotes waiting:** deals in Quote sent, sorted by last update, oldest first.
3. **Stalled:** deals not updated for more than 14 days, using the **Older than…** filter on the updated date.
4. **Quiet referral partners:** organizations of type Referral partner, sorted by last update.

Saved views are personal, so each colleague sets the same filters and saves their own view.

### Client email and WhatsApp in one inbox (Pro and above)

On Pro, Business and Enterprise in Customermates Cloud, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings Gmail, Outlook or IMAP email together with WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram and Telegram. You reply on the original channel, and a conversation linked to a contact shows on that contact's timeline. Conversations attach to contacts, not to deals or policies.

Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] connected account per user. An agent who wants a mailbox and WhatsApp in the inbox needs Business, which includes [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedAccountsPerUser]]. Conversations from your own accounts stay private until you share a thread, or share the whole account on Business and above.

### A renewal routine with Mate (Customermates Cloud)

Mate is the assistant in Customermates Cloud and uses hosted AI credits. Before a review call, ask it to summarize a client's deals, notes and recent messages. For the weekly prep, create a [routine](/docs/app-routines): choose **Every week** under **Repeats**, Monday at 07:30, with instructions like these:

```text
Find deals whose Renewal date is between today and 45 days from today and
whose Renewal status is Review due. For each one, read the latest inbox conversation
with its contacts and save a short draft asking for a renewal review call.
Do not send anything. Then list: client, line of business, carrier,
renewal date, draft saved yes or no.
```

<AcquisitionCallout title="Ask for drafts, not sends" variant="boundary">
  Mate and routines can send email and chat messages without asking first,
  so tell a renewal routine to save drafts only. A routine runs as its owner,
  with the owner's channels and credits, and each run has a credit cap. Mate
  does not check coverage, compare tariffs or give advice.
</AcquisitionCallout>

A second routine can react to an event instead: when a deal's stage changes to Bound, create a task "Enter in AMS" assigned to the same person. Starter includes [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] routine per user and Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]].

## Connecting quotes and the AMS

There is no native connector to carriers, raters or any AMS. Three ways to keep the CRM current:

- **Your own integration.** If your rater or AMS can call an API or send webhooks, a script or an [n8n workflow](/features/workflow-automation) you run separately can update the deal through the [REST API](/features/api): set the stage to Quote sent and write the quote reference and premium. Customermates sends webhooks for [[derived.webhooks.events.records]] record events, so a bound deal can trigger a step in the other direction.
- **Excel round trips.** Export from the AMS, save as .xlsx and import one record type at a time. A column mapped to Record ID updates existing records instead of creating duplicates.
- **An AI client.** Claude, ChatGPT or another client can create and update records through [MCP](/docs/mcp) on every plan, with model use billed by your AI provider.

## What it does not do, and what to look at instead

- No policy records, carrier downloads, comparative rating, e-signature, claims handling or commission reconciliation.
- Tasks have no due dates, reminders or notifications. Use a date field and a saved view, or a routine.
- No email sequences, renewal campaigns or bulk messaging. Follow-up is one conversation at a time.
- No web forms, no telephony or SMS, no mobile app and no file storage on records.
- No field-level permissions. A custom role with Assigned read access limits a producer to their own records. Keep health information and claim files out of the CRM.

## For insurers and MGAs managing brokers

The same setup works for a carrier or MGA distribution team: brokerages as organizations, their producers and principals as contacts, and each partner onboarding or joint opportunity as a deal. Appointments, production data and commission statements stay in the core systems. Customermates is not a policy, claims or underwriting platform.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Does Customermates replace an agency management system?">
No. It covers selling and relationships: prospects, renewal reviews, referral partners and client conversations. Policies, carrier downloads, quoting, claims, documents and commissions stay in your AMS.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How can an agency connect quoting activity to CRM records?">
The quote is produced in your rater or carrier portal. If that tool has an API or sends webhooks, a separately run n8n workflow or a short script can update the matching deal through the Customermates REST API, for example stage Quote sent plus quote reference and premium. Without an API, record the quote by hand or import an .xlsx export with a Record ID column.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How much does a CRM for insurance agents cost?">
Customermates costs [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per user per month on Starter and [[commercial.price.pro.monthly]] on Pro with the inbox, billed monthly. A two-agent office with a mailbox and WhatsApp each pays [[commercial.price.business.monthly.seats.2]] a month on Business. The self-hosted core has no licence fee and no inbox. An AMS is priced separately by its vendor.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What is the best free CRM for insurance agents?">
Customermates has a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial, not a free cloud plan. HubSpot advertises a free tier on its [insurance CRM page](https://www.hubspot.com/products/crm/insurance). The open-source Customermates core is free to self-host, without the inbox and Mate. If you need policy tracking at no cost, a free general CRM will not give you that; an AMS will, at a price.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can Customermates remind me of renewals?">
Not with reminders or notifications. Put the renewal date on the deal, sort a saved view by it, and in Customermates Cloud let a weekly routine list upcoming renewals and save draft messages for you to review.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Will AI replace the CRM for insurance agents?">
AI changes who types into the CRM. Mate or an MCP client can log a call, move a deal and draft a renewal message, but it needs renewal dates, stages and client history to work from, and those live in the CRM. Advice, suitability and coverage decisions stay with you.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

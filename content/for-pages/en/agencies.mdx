Customermates is a **CRM for agencies** that need one place for prospects, client organizations, new-business deals, services and follow-up tasks. It supports the relationship and sales side of agency work; it does not replace proposal, project-delivery, time-tracking or invoicing software.

## What an agency can manage

| Agency workflow                   | Customermates capability                          | Product boundary                                            |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Prospects and client stakeholders | Related contact and organization records          | No automated enrichment                                     |
| New-business pipeline             | Deals, configurable stages and kanban views       | A stage named “Proposal” is not a proposal document         |
| Scope under discussion            | Services, quantities and amounts linked to a deal | No SOW or project-delivery model                            |
| Pipeline value                    | Stage probabilities and weighted deal totals      | No predictive close forecast                                |
| Next actions                      | Tasks linked to records and responsible users     | No native sequence or scheduler                             |
| Client conversations              | Cloud Pro+ inbox for supported channels           | No self-hosted inbox and no direct message-to-deal relation |

## A practical new-business workflow

1. Add the prospect and link them to the client organization.
2. Create a deal and select the current stage.
3. Attach the services, quantities and amounts being discussed.
4. Assign a record-linked task for the next action.
5. Use the board and weighted totals to review the pipeline.
6. Hand the signed work to your existing project and finance systems.

This gives business development and account teams shared context without forcing delivery work into the CRM.

## Client communication in the hosted inbox

The [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) is available in the managed cloud from Pro upward. It brings supported email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram conversations into one list. Conversations can be matched to contact context; they are not directly attached to a deal.

Pro includes one connected account per user. Business includes three per user and supports shared accounts. Enterprise includes unlimited connected accounts under fair-use terms and supports shared accounts. The inbox is not available when self-hosting.

## Connect the rest of the agency stack

Customermates exposes [REST and webhooks](/features/api), and external AI clients can work with supported CRM records through [MCP](/docs/mcp). A separate [n8n integration](/features/workflow-automation) can coordinate systems when you design and operate the workflow.

Those interfaces are connection points, not a promise of turnkey synchronization. Your project-management, proposal, time-tracking and accounting systems remain responsible for their own records and automation.

## Agency CRM or professional-services CRM?

This page focuses on broad agency new-business intent. The [professional-services CRM](/for/professional-services) page explains the wider relationship-and-pipeline model for expertise-led businesses.

## Cloud or self-hosted

The core contacts, organizations, deals, services, tasks, REST, webhooks and MCP are available in the managed cloud and [self-hosted edition](/features/self-hosted). Self-hosting uses Starter entitlements and excludes the unified inbox, connected calendar view and hosted Mate capability.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Does Customermates manage agency projects?">
No. It manages relationship and new-business context. Delivery plans, milestones, capacity and project execution belong in a project-management or PSA system.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can it create proposals, contracts or invoices?">
  No. Customermates can represent the deal and services being discussed, but it has no native proposal, SOW, contract,
  quote or invoice document workflow.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can an agency track follow-ups?">
  Tasks can be linked to contacts, organizations, deals, services and users. Customermates does not include a native
  timed sequence or scheduler; use your chosen external workflow system if you need scheduled automation.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Which channels are in the unified inbox?">
  Supported connections cover Gmail/Google, Outlook/Microsoft and IMAP email, LinkedIn variants, WhatsApp, Instagram and
  Telegram. The inbox is a managed-cloud entitlement from Pro upward.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can a self-hosted agency use AI clients?">
Yes. Self-hosted Customermates supports external AI clients through MCP. It does not include the hosted Mate assistant, hosted AI credits or the unified inbox.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

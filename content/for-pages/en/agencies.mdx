Customermates is a **CRM for marketing agencies** and creative studios that win clients through inquiries, pitches and relationships. Clients and prospects, their contacts, each pitch, signed retainers and the next action stay connected until won work is handed to the tools that run delivery. It is an open-source, AI-native CRM made in Germany, and it covers new business and client relationships, not delivery planning, campaigns or invoicing.

## The pitch board in the example workspace

The [agency workspace](https://demo.customermates.com/en/w/agencies/dashboard) is an example with sample data, not a customer account. It has six lists: **Clients**, **Contacts**, **Pitches**, **Projects**, **Retainers** and **Tasks**. Pitches move through **Inquiry**, **Briefing**, **Pitch preparation**, **Proposal sent**, **Negotiation**, **Won** and **Lost**.

The pitch board shows every open pitch as a card in its stage, with its owner and estimated value. Each stage carries a win probability, so the board also shows a weighted value per pitch. That is pipeline weighting from your own probabilities, not a forecast.

Open a client from a card and the relationship is in one place:

- **The people:** the marketing lead who briefed you, the managing director who signs, the procurement contact.
- **The pitches:** the current one in Pitch preparation and earlier ones that were won.
- **The projects:** won work as records with a status, budget, hours budget and hours used; hours left is calculated from the two.
- **The retainer:** a record with fields such as monthly fee, term, start date and renewal date. Contract value is calculated as monthly fee times term, and the client shows its retainer MRR. It describes the agreement; it does not create invoices or recurring billing.
- **The conversation:** an email thread with the marketing lead, linked to the pitch. The contact was matched by email address in the Contact details field; the link to the pitch was set explicitly by a person, never automatically.

## From inquiry to project and retainer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture the inquiry">
    Add the person and the client or prospect, and open a pitch in Inquiry.
    Note the source, such as a referral or an existing client, in a field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take the briefing">
    Move the pitch to Briefing, record budget and scope in your own fields and
    assign an owner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare and send the pitch">
    Work through Pitch preparation and Proposal sent. Linked tasks and a
    next-step date field show what is due and who owns it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow up and negotiate">
    Reply on the original thread from the inbox, or ask Mate for a draft from
    the client record and conversation, then read and send it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record the outcome">
    Move the pitch to Won or Lost. For won work, create the project or retainer
    record and keep delivery plans, time tracking and billing in your project and
    finance tools.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What belongs in the CRM and what stays in the agency stack

| Agency moment | Customermates holds | Another system holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inquiry and briefing | Clients, contacts, pitch stages, owners and your own fields | Enrichment, lead scoring and outbound sequences |
| Pitch and negotiation | Estimated value, weighted value, follow-up context | Pitch decks, proposals, statements of work and contracts |
| New-business review | Pitch board, stage probabilities and weighted totals | Predictive forecasting and finance planning |
| Signed work | Project and retainer records with status, budget, hours entered by hand and renewal date | Delivery plans, capacity, time tracking, invoicing and accounting |
| Campaigns | Nothing | Ad platforms, email marketing and client reporting |

## Client conversations beside the relationship

In Customermates Cloud, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings email from Gmail, Outlook or IMAP together with LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram. You reply on the original channel. Participants are matched to contacts by their email address, phone number or LinkedIn identity; linking a conversation to a pitch or client is a deliberate step by a person or agent.

The plan decides how far the inbox reaches:

- **Starter and self-hosted:** no connected messaging accounts.
- **Pro:** each person's own connected accounts, [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] per user included, and individually shared threads.
- **Business, Max and Enterprise:** whole-account sharing, so a shared new-business mailbox can be opened to the account team. Business includes [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedAccountsPerUser]] connected accounts per user.

## AI help and connections

Mate, the assistant in Customermates Cloud, can summarize a client before a pitch meeting, move a pitch to its next stage and save a follow-up draft in the conversation. It runs on hosted AI credits included per active user on every cloud plan and is not available self-hosted. Mate sends when you tell it to send, so ask for a draft when you want to review first. A [routine](/docs/app-routines) can run Mate on a schedule or when a record changes. The example workspace has two: every Monday a renewal check marks retainers renewing within 60 days as Renewal due and creates a task, and when a pitch moves to Proposal sent Mate saves a follow-up draft for review.

Every plan, self-hosted included, has a [REST API and webhooks](/features/api) and [MCP](/docs/mcp) for external AI clients such as Claude or ChatGPT, which can read or update the records your role permits. A separately operated [n8n workflow](/features/workflow-automation) can pass a won pitch to your project tool.

## Working as an agency team

Before you invite the whole agency, know the limits:

- **Saved views are personal.** There are no shared or team views; each person saves their own pitch board filters.
- **No field-level permissions.** Roles control access to lists and records, so a role with Assigned read access can limit a freelancer to their own pitches, but you cannot hide one field such as a fee from someone who can read the record.
- **Whole-account inbox sharing starts at Business.** On Pro, threads are shared one at a time.
- **No client sub-accounts or white-label workspaces.** Customermates is your agency's own CRM, not a portal you resell to clients.

## Setting up your own workspace

A new workspace starts without lists. Pitches, Projects and Retainers are example lists from the demo, not what every account contains. In Customermates Cloud, the AI workspace setup asks what the workspace is for and builds lists, stages, a dashboard and Knowledge Base pages from your answers. It runs once at onboarding, within a capped amount of AI credit, and you can skip it with **Start with an empty workspace** (see [onboarding](/docs/app-onboarding)).

Otherwise, people with Configure rights create the lists, fields and stages in Configure and import existing clients and contacts from an Excel workbook. An import cannot be undone, so try it with a few rows first.

## Cloud or self-hosted

Lists, fields, the pitch board, tasks, REST, webhooks and MCP work in Customermates Cloud and in the [self-hosted edition](/features/self-hosted). Self-hosting has the Starter feature set: no unified inbox, no Mate and no AI workspace setup. Prices per user per month, billed monthly, are on the [pricing page](/pricing).

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Does Customermates manage agency projects or campaigns?">
Not as a project tool. The example workspace keeps a simple Projects list with status, budget and hours used, entered by hand, so won work sits beside the client. Delivery plans, capacity, time tracking and campaigns belong in project management, PSA and marketing tools.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can it create proposals, contracts or invoices?">
No. A pitch can carry its stage, value and the link to the proposal document, but Customermates has no proposal, contract or invoice workflow. Retainers are records, not recurring invoices.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does a new workspace come with Pitches, Projects and Retainers?">
No. A new workspace starts without lists. In Customermates Cloud the AI workspace setup can build lists and stages from your description once at onboarding. Otherwise people with Configure rights create them and import records from Excel.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can the team share a pitch board view?">
No. Saved views are personal, so each person saves their own filters on the shared records. Everyone with access sees the same pitches and stages.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can we share a new-business mailbox with the team?">
On Business, Max and Enterprise you can share a whole connected account. On Pro each person connects their own accounts and shares individual threads. Starter and self-hosted have no connected messaging accounts.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can an agency track follow-ups?">
Tasks can be linked to clients, contacts and pitches. They have no built-in due dates or reminders, so add a date field and sort a saved view by it. There are no email sequences or bulk sends.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can we give clients their own white-label workspace?">
No. There are no client sub-accounts, client portals or white-label workspaces.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

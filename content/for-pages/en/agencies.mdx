A CRM for agencies keeps each client, pitch and retainer on one record, with the email and LinkedIn conversations beside it, so the person who sold the work and the person who delivers it see the same history. Customermates is that kind of CRM for marketing agencies and creative studios of 2 to 10 people, and it stops where delivery starts: planning, campaigns, time tracking and invoicing stay in the tools built for them.

A pitch goes out, the briefing notes sit in one person's inbox, and a client who signed last week asks who their contact is. In Customermates, clients and prospects, their contacts, each pitch, signed retainers and the next action stay on one record until won work is handed to the tools that run delivery. From Pro on Customermates Cloud, the email and LinkedIn threads sit on the client, and Mate or your own AI agent through MCP drafts the reply and the next step for you to review, edit and send. It is an open-source, AI-native CRM made in Germany.

## What does a CRM for agencies have to handle? [#what-a-crm-for-agencies-must-handle]

Agencies sell on briefings and pitches, then live on retainers and several people per client. These are the jobs a CRM has to cover, what Customermates does for each in its [example agency workspace](https://demo.customermates.com/en/w/agencies/dashboard), and where it stops.

| Agency job | What Customermates does | Where it stops |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pitches and new business | Pitches move across a board from Inquiry to Won or Lost. Each has an owner, an estimated value, a stage probability that gives a weighted value, and a next step with its date. | The weighted value is pipeline weighting from your own probabilities, not a forecast. No proposal generation, lead scoring or outbound sequences. |
| Client management and contacts | One record per client with its contacts: the marketing lead who briefed you, the person who signs, procurement. The brief and the proposal sit on the pitch as PDFs. | Nothing is enriched or scored for you; fields are filled by people, an Excel import or your own AI client. |
| Retainers and renewals | A retainer record holds the monthly fee, term, start and renewal date; contract value is calculated and the client shows its retainer MRR. Every Monday a routine marks retainers that renew within 60 days as Renewal due and creates the task. | The record describes the agreement. No invoices and no recurring billing. |
| Projects after the win | A Projects list with status, budget, hours budget and hours used, with hours left calculated, plus a calendar view of production windows. | Hours are typed in. No time tracking, capacity planning or delivery plans. |
| Client conversations on the record | Email (Gmail, Outlook, IMAP), LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram threads in one inbox, matched to contacts and linked to the pitch by a person. | Cloud only, from Pro; sharing a whole mailbox starts at Business. No SMS or phone calls. |
| Follow-up | Every weekday morning a routine saves reply drafts for pitches whose next step date has come, or creates a follow-up task when there is no email thread. The drafts wait in the Inbox; the routine never sends. | Tasks have no built-in due dates or reminders, so nobody is notified automatically. |
| Approvals and sign-off | A person moves the pitch to Won or Lost and attaches the signed agreement to the retainer as a PDF. Mate saves reply drafts for you to review and send. A project status such as Client review shows where work waits for the client. | No approval workflow, client portal or proofing. Client sign-off on creative work stays in the tools built for it. |

The inbox starts at Pro, Mate and routines at Starter, all on Customermates Cloud; the [self-hosted edition](/features/self-hosted) has none of them.

## When is Customermates the wrong CRM for an agency? [#when-customermates-is-the-wrong-choice]

Customermates is built to follow up on pitches and client conversations. Choose another tool if one of these decides your purchase:

- **You want sales and delivery in one tool.** Customermates has no time tracking, capacity planning, invoicing or proposal documents, and its Projects list only holds what you enter by hand. A PSA or project tool covers that; Customermates can sit beside it for new business.
- **You run outbound sequences or bulk email.** There are no email sequences, bulk sends or lead scoring. Messages go out one at a time.
- **Clients need a login.** There are no client portals, client sub-accounts or white-label workspaces.
- **Some people must not see one field.** Roles control lists and records, not single fields, so a fee field is visible to anyone who can read the record.
- **Your team works from phones.** Customermates is responsive web software with no native mobile app.
- **You want the inbox and Mate self-hosted.** The [self-hosted edition](/features/self-hosted) has the Starter feature set: no unified inbox and no Mate.

If one of these decides it, the comparison pages set Customermates side by side with [Pipedrive](/compare/pipedrive-alternative), [HubSpot](/compare/hubspot-alternative), [Attio](/compare/attio-alternative), [Twenty](/compare/twenty-alternative), [Breakcold](/compare/breakcold-alternative).

## The pitch board in the example workspace

The [agency workspace](https://demo.customermates.com/en/w/agencies/dashboard) is an example with sample data, not a customer account. It has six lists: **Clients**, **Contacts**, **Pitches**, **Projects**, **Retainers** and **Tasks**. Pitches move through **Inquiry**, **Briefing**, **Pitch preparation**, **Proposal sent**, **Negotiation**, **Won** and **Lost**.

The pitch board shows every open pitch as a card in its stage, with its owner and estimated value. Each stage carries a win probability, so the board also shows a weighted value per pitch. That is pipeline weighting from your own probabilities, not a forecast.

Open a client from a card and the relationship is in one place:

- **The people:** the marketing lead who briefed you, the managing director who signs, the procurement contact.
- **The pitches:** the current one in Pitch preparation and earlier ones that were won. A pitch keeps the client brief and the proposal as PDFs in a File field, and its presentation date and time.
- **The projects:** won work as records with a status, budget, hours budget and hours used; hours left is calculated from the two. A calendar view shows each project's production window by month.
- **The retainer:** a record with fields such as monthly fee, term, start date and renewal date. Contract value is calculated as monthly fee times term, and the client shows its retainer MRR. It describes the agreement and holds the signed agreement as a PDF; it does not create invoices or recurring billing.
- **The conversation:** an email thread with the marketing lead, linked to the pitch. The contact was matched by email address in the Contact details field; the link to the pitch was set explicitly by a person, never automatically.

## Client conversations beside the relationship

In Customermates Cloud, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings email from Gmail, Outlook or IMAP together with LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram. You reply on the original channel. Participants are matched to contacts by their email address, phone number or LinkedIn identity; linking a conversation to a pitch or client is a deliberate step by a person or agent.

The plan decides how far the inbox reaches:

- **Starter and self-hosted:** no connected messaging accounts.
- **Pro:** each person's own connected accounts, [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] per user included, and individually shared threads.
- **Business, Max and Enterprise:** whole-account sharing, so a shared new-business mailbox can be opened to the account team. Business includes [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedAccountsPerUser]] connected accounts per user.

## AI help and connections

Mate, the assistant in Customermates Cloud, can summarize a client before a pitch meeting, move a pitch to its next stage and save a follow-up draft in the conversation. It runs on hosted AI credits included per active user on every paid cloud plan and is not available self-hosted. Mate sends when you tell it to send, so ask for a draft when you want to review first. A [routine](/docs/app-routines) can run Mate on a schedule or when a record changes. The example workspace has four. Every Monday, a renewal check marks retainers renewing within 60 days as Renewal due and creates a task, and a digest summarizes the pitch board without changing it. Every weekday morning, one routine saves reply drafts for pitches whose next step date has come, and when a pitch moves to Proposal sent, another saves a follow-up draft for review.

Every plan, self-hosted included, has a [REST API](/features/api) and [MCP](/docs/mcp) for external AI clients such as Claude or ChatGPT, which can read or update the records your role permits; webhooks come with every paid plan and self-hosting. A separately operated [n8n workflow](/features/workflow-automation) can pass a won pitch to your project tool.

## Working as an agency team

Before you invite the whole agency, know the limits:

- **Shared views are read-only.** Share a pitch board view with the whole agency or with selected roles and everyone works from the same filters; only its owner changes it.
- **No field-level permissions.** Roles control access to lists and records, so a role with Assigned read access can limit a freelancer to their own pitches, but you cannot hide one field such as a fee from someone who can read the record.
- **Whole-account inbox sharing starts at Business.** On Pro, threads are shared one at a time.
- **No client sub-accounts or white-label workspaces.** Customermates is your agency's own CRM, not a portal you resell to clients.

## Setting up your own workspace

A new workspace starts without lists. Pitches, Projects and Retainers are example lists from the demo, not what every account contains. In Customermates Cloud, the AI workspace setup asks what the workspace is for and builds lists, stages, a dashboard and Knowledge Base pages from your answers. It runs once at onboarding, within a capped amount of AI credit, and you can skip it with **Start with an empty workspace** (see [onboarding](/docs/app-onboarding)).

Otherwise, people with Data model rights create the lists, fields and stages in Data model and import existing clients and contacts from an Excel workbook. An import cannot be undone, so try it with a few rows first.

## Cloud or self-hosted

Lists, fields, the pitch board, tasks, REST, webhooks and MCP work in Customermates Cloud and in the [self-hosted edition](/features/self-hosted). Self-hosting has the Starter feature set: no unified inbox, no Mate and no AI workspace setup. Prices per user per month, billed monthly, are on the [pricing page](/pricing).

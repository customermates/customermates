Customermates is a **CRM for agencies** that turns referrals, inquiries and relationships into a visible new-business pipeline. Prospects, client organizations, opportunities, services, owners and next actions stay connected until the work is ready for an explicit handoff to delivery.

## From an inquiry to a clean delivery handoff

An agency opportunity rarely starts as a tidy deal. It may arrive through a referral, an existing client, email or a social conversation. The first useful step is to create the person and client organization, then open a deal when the opportunity is real enough to track.

Custom fields can hold the qualification details your agency actually reviews. Services, quantities and amounts record the commercial scope currently being discussed. The owner and linked task make the next action visible without turning the CRM into a project plan.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture the relationship">Add the prospect, connect the client organization and preserve the source or referral context in supported fields.</Step>
  <Step title="Qualify the opportunity">Create a deal, assign an owner and move it through the new-business stages your agency uses.</Step>
  <Step title="Represent the scope">Link services, quantities and amounts so the board carries more than a deal name.</Step>
  <Step title="Keep momentum visible">Attach the next task to the relevant record and review weighted pipeline totals with the team.</Step>
  <Step title="Hand off won work">Move the signed scope into the project and finance systems that own delivery, time and billing.</Step>
</Steps>

## What belongs in the CRM and what belongs in the agency stack

| Agency moment | Customermates owns | Another system owns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prospecting and qualification | People, organizations, deal stages, owners and custom fields | Enrichment, scoring and outbound sequences |
| Commercial discussion | Deal value, linked services and follow-up context | Proposal, statement of work and contract documents |
| New-business review | Board, stage probabilities and weighted totals | Predictive forecasting and finance planning |
| Delivery handoff | Final relationship and commercial context | Projects, capacity, time, invoicing and accounting |

<AcquisitionCallout title="A stage called Proposal is still only a stage" variant="boundary">
  The pipeline can show where an opportunity sits, but it does not generate a proposal, contract or invoice. Keeping that distinction explicit prevents a sales status from becoming a second, stale version of the signed scope.
</AcquisitionCallout>

## Bring supported conversations into the relationship context

The [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) is available in managed cloud from Pro upward. It brings supported conversations into one list and lets participants be associated with CRM contacts. The contact relationship is the bridge to relevant client and pipeline context because messages do not have a direct deal relation.

Use the dedicated inbox page for the complete channel set, account-sharing model and plan boundaries. Self-hosted Customermates does not include connected messaging accounts or the unified inbox.

## Connect the rest of the agency stack

Customermates exposes [REST and webhooks](/features/api), while external AI clients can work with supported CRM records through [MCP](/docs/mcp). A separately operated [n8n integration](/features/workflow-automation) can coordinate a workflow you design and verify.

## Cloud or self-hosted

Core contacts, organizations, deals, services, tasks, REST, webhooks and MCP are available in managed cloud and the [self-hosted edition](/features/self-hosted). Self-hosting uses Starter entitlements and leaves the unified inbox, connected calendar view and hosted Mate capability out of the deployment.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Does Customermates manage agency projects?">
No. It manages relationship and new-business context. Delivery plans, milestones, capacity and project execution belong in a project-management or PSA system.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can it create proposals, contracts or invoices?">
  No. Customermates can represent the deal and services being discussed, but it has no native proposal, SOW, contract,
  quote or invoice document workflow.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can an agency track follow-ups?">
  Tasks can be linked to contacts, organizations, deals, services and users. Customermates does not include a native
  timed sequence or scheduler; use your chosen external workflow system if you need scheduled automation.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Which channels are in the unified inbox?">
  Supported connections cover Gmail/Google, Outlook/Microsoft and IMAP email, LinkedIn variants, WhatsApp, Instagram and
  Telegram. The inbox is a managed-cloud entitlement from Pro upward.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can a self-hosted agency use AI clients?">
Yes. Self-hosted Customermates supports external AI clients through MCP. It does not include the hosted Mate assistant, hosted AI credits or the unified inbox.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

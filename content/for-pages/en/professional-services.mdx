Customermates is a **CRM for professional services** teams that need to connect client relationships with new-business pipeline. It keeps contacts, organizations, deals, services and tasks together without pretending to be a professional-services automation suite.

## What this CRM covers

| Business-development need      | How Customermates supports it                                         | Boundary                                                  |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Client and stakeholder context | Contacts and organizations with relations, notes and activity history | Not a document-management system                          |
| Opportunity pipeline           | Deals, configurable stages and kanban views                           | Not proposal or SOW creation                              |
| Pipeline value                 | Stage probabilities, weighted deal values and weighted totals         | Not predictive revenue forecasting                        |
| Services in a deal             | Services with quantities and amounts linked to deals                  | Not project delivery or resource planning                 |
| Follow-up work                 | Tasks linked to contacts, organizations, deals, services and users    | Not native scheduling or time tracking                    |
| Extensions                     | REST, webhooks, MCP and a separate n8n integration option             | External systems keep their own runtime and data boundary |

## One practical professional-services workflow

1. Create the client organization and its decision-makers.
2. Open a deal and move it through the stages your team defines.
3. Link the services, quantities and amounts being discussed.
4. Add record-linked tasks so the next action stays with the client context.
5. Review the board, dashboards and weighted pipeline totals.

The proposal, contract, delivery plan, time entries and invoice remain in the specialist tools responsible for them. Use the [REST API and webhooks](/features/api), or connect a separate [n8n workflow](/features/workflow-automation), when those systems need CRM data.

## Where it fits

Customermates fits consultancies, agencies, accounting and legal teams that sell expertise through relationships and staged opportunities. The broad workflow is shared, while the language and fields can differ by practice:

- [agencies](/for/agencies) can track new-business opportunities and client relationships;
- [accountants](/for/accountants) and [law firms](/for/lawyers) can model their own relationship and pipeline fields.

It is not a fit if you expect the CRM itself to allocate delivery capacity, generate proposals, record billable time, issue invoices or replace project management.

## Cloud or self-hosted

The core relationship and pipeline records are available in managed cloud and [self-hosted Customermates](/features/self-hosted). Self-hosting uses the Starter entitlement baseline and supports external AI clients through MCP.

The [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) is cloud-only from Pro upward. It can bring supported email and social conversations into one list and associate conversations with contact context. It does not turn Customermates into a delivery or billing suite.

## AI-operable, with explicit boundaries

External AI clients can use [MCP](/docs/mcp) to work with supported CRM records under the user's permissions. MCP instructions tell clients to request confirmation before sends and deletes, but the server executes a permitted tool once the client calls it. Verify that operating control in the client you choose. The model provider and client remain external when you use MCP.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is Customermates a PSA platform?">
No. It is a relationship and pipeline CRM. It does not provide native project delivery, capacity planning, utilization management, time tracking or billing.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can it create proposals or invoices?">
  No. Customermates has no proposal, SOW, quote or invoice document workflow. You can keep the opportunity,
  stakeholders, services and follow-up context in the CRM and connect a specialist system through REST, webhooks or an
  external workflow.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How are service values represented?">
  Services can be linked to a deal with quantities and amounts. Deal stages can carry probabilities, which Customermates
  uses to calculate deterministic weighted deal values and pipeline totals.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can tasks replace project management or time tracking?">
  No. Tasks can be linked to CRM records and users, but they are not a project-delivery, scheduling or time-entry
  system.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is the unified inbox available when self-hosting?">
No. The unified inbox and connected calendar view are managed-cloud features from Pro upward. Self-hosted deployments use Starter entitlements and can connect external AI clients through MCP.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

Customermates is a **CRM for professional services** teams that sell expertise through relationships and staged opportunities. It connects the client organization, its stakeholders, the opportunity, the services under discussion, the weighted value and the next task in one business-development workspace.

## Follow one opportunity from relationship to next action

Imagine a consultancy speaking with a client organization about a multi-part engagement. The buying group includes an executive sponsor, a day-to-day contact and a procurement stakeholder. In Customermates, those people stay related to the same organization while the opportunity moves through the stages that match the firm's sales process.

The team can attach the services, quantities and amounts currently under discussion, then keep the next follow-up as a task on the relevant record. Opening the deal reveals the commercial context without pretending that the CRM is also the delivery workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build the client context">
    Create the organization, add the people involved and preserve the
    relationships that matter to the opportunity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shape the opportunity">
    Open a deal, assign an owner and move it through the stages your team
    defines.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Represent the commercial scope">
    Link the relevant services, quantities and amounts to the deal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the next action visible">
    Attach a task to the contact, organization, deal or service so the follow-up
    stays with its context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hand off deliberately">
    When work is won, pass the signed scope to the project, time and finance
    systems responsible for delivery.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How weighted pipeline values work

Deal stages can carry probabilities. Customermates multiplies the deal value by the configured stage probability and adds those deterministic values into weighted pipeline totals. This makes assumptions visible and repeatable. It is not predictive revenue forecasting, enrichment or an automated close score.

Use weighted value as a review aid: inspect the underlying deal, confirm that the stage still reflects reality and update the record when the commercial situation changes.

## Where this professional-services CRM fits

The shared model works across consultancies, [agencies](/for/agencies), accounting practices and law firms, while each practice can choose its own stages, fields and language.

## Cloud, self-hosted and AI access

Core relationship and pipeline records are available in managed cloud and [self-hosted Customermates](/features/self-hosted). Self-hosting uses the Starter entitlement baseline and can expose supported records to an external AI client through [MCP](/docs/mcp).

The [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) is a managed-cloud feature from Pro upward. It can bring supported conversations into one list and associate participants with contact context. External MCP clients and model providers remain customer-selected services with their own data and control boundaries.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is Customermates a PSA platform?">
No. It is a relationship and pipeline CRM. It does not provide native project delivery, capacity planning, utilization management, time tracking or billing.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can it create proposals or invoices?">
  No. Customermates has no proposal, SOW, quote or invoice document workflow.
  You can keep the opportunity, stakeholders, services and follow-up context in
  the CRM and connect a specialist system through REST, webhooks or an external
  workflow.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How are service values represented?">
  Services can be linked to a deal with quantities and amounts. Deal stages can
  carry probabilities, which Customermates uses to calculate deterministic
  weighted deal values and pipeline totals.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can tasks replace project management or time tracking?">
  No. Tasks can be linked to CRM records and users, but they are not a
  project-delivery, scheduling or time-entry system.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is the unified inbox available when self-hosting?">
No. The unified inbox and connected calendar view are managed-cloud features from Pro upward. Self-hosted deployments use Starter entitlements and can connect external AI clients through MCP.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

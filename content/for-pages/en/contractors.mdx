## Why contractors need a CRM

Running a contracting business means juggling estimates, job sites, subcontractors, and client expectations, often from the cab of your truck. A CRM for contractors replaces the scattered notes, text messages, and spreadsheets with a single system that tracks every lead, every quote, and every job from start to finish.

Without a contractor CRM, common problems stack up fast:

- **Estimates go cold**: You sent a quote last week. The homeowner never responded, and without a follow-up reminder the job goes to a competitor who chased it.
- **Leads disappear**: A referral calls while you are on-site. You jot down a number, but it gets buried in your phone or truck console.
- **Repeat clients are forgotten**: A customer you did excellent work for two years ago needs you again, but you have no system to stay in touch or track their history.
- **Subcontractor coordination falls apart**: Managing availability, rates, and contact details for your regular subs adds complexity that spreadsheets cannot handle.
- **No visibility into your pipeline**: You do not know how many estimates are pending, what your close rate is, or where your next month of revenue is coming from.

A construction contractor CRM brings structure to the business side of contracting so you can focus on the work itself. Customermates gives you exactly the tools you need, [pipeline management](/features/pipeline), [contact tracking](/features/contact-management), and [workflow automation](/features/workflow-automation), without enterprise-level complexity or cost.

## How Customermates works as a contractor CRM

Customermates is built to be simple enough for a one-person operation and powerful enough for a growing crew. Here is how it supports every stage of a contracting job.

### Estimate-to-invoice pipeline

Track every opportunity from first call through site visit, estimate, negotiation, acceptance, and job completion. The [Kanban pipeline view](/features/pipeline) lets you drag jobs between stages so you always know where every deal stands. Set follow-up dates on estimates so no quote sits unanswered.

### Job and project tracking

Log job details, scope, address, materials, timeline, and budget, directly on each deal using custom fields. Track active jobs alongside your sales pipeline so you never overcommit your schedule. For more complex projects, use [project management features](/features/project-management) to break work into tasks and milestones.

### Subcontractor management

Create contact records for every subcontractor with their trade, rates, availability, insurance status, and past performance notes. When a new job comes in, you can quickly find the right sub and see your full history with them in [contact management](/features/contact-management).

### Unified inbox for every client conversation

Homeowners reach you by email, WhatsApp, and Instagram, not just one channel. Customermates brings all of them into one native unified inbox (email, WhatsApp, Instagram, LinkedIn, and Telegram) tied to the right job, and the same AI agents can read, draft, and send across every channel. When a past customer messages back, you know exactly what work you did, when, and how it went. [Email integration](/features/email-integration) and messaging capture conversations automatically, no manual data entry from the job site.

### Scheduling and workload visibility

See your upcoming jobs, site visits, and follow-ups in one view. Avoid double-booking crews or missing estimate appointments. Custom fields let you track job dates, crew assignments, and milestones without switching between apps.

### Automation with n8n

Connect your separately operated [n8n instance](/features/workflow-automation) through API and webhooks for workflows you own. n8n is not included in Customermates:

- Send follow-up through your own automation emails 3 days after submitting an estimate
- Trigger a satisfaction survey after job completion
- Send annual check-in reminders to past clients
- Notify your team when a deal moves to "Accepted" stage
- Request referrals from completed jobs automatically

### AI agents that operate your CRM

Connect Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini directly to Customermates through a native MCP integration. The AI does the data entry: it moves deal stages, drafts estimate follow-ups, keeps contact notes current, enriches new leads with property details, and analyzes your win rate across job types and seasons. Your pipeline stays accurate without anyone typing it up from the cab of the truck.

## Key features of a CRM for general contractors

### Pipeline management with Kanban view

Visualize your entire business on a Kanban board, New Lead, Site Visit Scheduled, Estimate Sent, Follow-Up Needed, Accepted, In Progress, Completed. Drag cards between stages as jobs progress. Filter by job type, value, or date to focus on what matters. This requires a scheduler in your own n8n instance or another external service through the API; Customermates does not run scheduled follow-ups or reminders itself.

### Custom fields for job data

Track everything that matters to your trade: job type, property address, square footage, estimated value, materials needed, permit status, and projected timelines. Custom fields live directly on your deal records, no separate systems required.

### Client profiles and job history

Build a complete history with every client. Track past jobs, values, satisfaction ratings, and notes. Identify your best customers for repeat business and referrals. See every interaction in one timeline.

### Mobile-friendly access

Access your CRM from the job site on your phone. Add notes after a site visit, update deal stages, check client details, and log calls, all without driving back to the office.

### Open source and self-hostable

Customermates is fully [open source](https://github.com/customermates/customermates). You can inspect the code, contribute, or self-host on your own infrastructure for complete data control. No vendor lock-in, no surprise feature removals, no forced upgrades.

### GDPR-compliant and EU-hosted

Client addresses, property details, and financial information deserve proper protection. Customermates is GDPR-native and hosted in the EU region. For contractors handling sensitive commercial or residential client data, this matters. You can also self-host for maximum control.

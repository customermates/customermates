## Why solar companies need a CRM

A CRM for solar companies keeps an installation business on track. The solar sales cycle is long and complex: a homeowner sends an inquiry today, a rep runs a site assessment next week, an engineer designs the system, a proposal goes out, financing options are discussed, permits are filed, equipment is ordered, a crew is booked, the install happens, and then inspection, commissioning, and warranty begin. That is months of touchpoints per deal, and many solar companies are juggling dozens of these projects simultaneously.

Without a solar CRM to manage this pipeline, revenue leaks at every stage:

- **Leads go cold**: Solar leads are expensive, whether from paid ads, canvassing, or referrals. Without timely follow-up, prospects go with the competitor who responded within the hour instead of within the week.
- **Site assessments create bottlenecks**: Roof measurements, shading analysis, electrical panel inspections, and energy usage data are collected in the field but never make it into a central system. The office team wastes time chasing reps for missing details.
- **Proposals sit unanswered**: A solar proposal takes serious engineering and design effort. When proposals go out and nobody follows up at the right moment, that effort is wasted.
- **Permit tracking falls through the cracks**: Every solar install requires permits from the local AHJ (Authority Having Jurisdiction) and often interconnection approval from the utility. Lose track of a submission and the project stalls for weeks.
- **Financing conversations get lost**: Many homeowners choose solar loans, leases, or PPAs. Tracking which financing option each customer is considering, and whether they have been approved, is critical but hard to manage in spreadsheets.
- **Installation handoffs are disconnected**: The crew arrives at a site without the right equipment because the sales notes never made it to operations.
- **Post-install follow-up is forgotten**: Warranty registrations, monitoring setup, and referral requests fall through the cracks after the install is done.

The right solar sales CRM connects every stage of this pipeline so nothing gets lost, no lead goes cold, and no project stalls because of a missed handoff.

## How Customermates works as your solar CRM

Customermates gives solar installers and EPC companies a central system for managing the entire lead-to-install pipeline, with no per-lead fees and monthly billing. It is a CRM, not a design, scheduling or invoicing tool: keep your design software and accounting next to it.

### Lead capture and qualification

Every lead gets a complete profile: contact details, property address, roof type, energy usage, inquiry source, and communication history. Customermates has no built-in web forms, so leads from your website, a canvassing app, or a referral partner arrive through the REST API, or through a separately operated [n8n workflow](/blog/n8n-for-beginners-build-your-first-workflow) using the Customermates community node. Qualify leads with custom fields for roof suitability, homeownership status, electricity spend, and financing profile before sending a rep to the site.

### Site assessment tracking

When your rep visits the property, they can update structured deal fields from a phone browser: roof measurements, shading observations, electrical-panel condition, and structural concerns. Keep site photos in your existing file system and record a link in a custom field; Customermates does not upload files to deal records.

### Proposal and design management

Track every proposal as a deal stage with its value. Custom fields hold the system design: panel count, inverter model, battery storage option, estimated annual production (kWh), and projected savings. Customermates does not create proposal documents; link the proposal from your design tool in a link field, and note revisions such as a different panel brand or a battery add-on on the deal. A dashboard shows how many deals sit in each stage and what they are worth.

### Financing options tracking

Solar financing is often the make-or-break factor. Track which financing path each customer is exploring (cash purchase, solar loan, lease, or PPA) and at what stage of approval they are. Custom fields for lender name, loan amount, interest rate, monthly payment, and approval status keep the financial picture clear.

### Permit management

Solar permits are a known bottleneck. Track permit applications, submission dates, approval status, and inspector notes in custom fields on each deal. A saved view that filters deals in the **Permit submitted** stage and sorts them by your submission date field shows which permits have been pending longest. On Customermates Cloud, a [routine](/docs/app-routines) can run that check every morning and list the permits that have waited more than 20 days. Track interconnection applications with their own status field when the utility requires its own approval process.

### Installation handoff and crew coordination

Once permits are approved, the project moves to your installation stages. Custom fields hold the assigned crew, planned install date, and equipment delivery status. The same deal record that started as a lead now contains every detail the installation crew needs: system design, permit approvals, customer preferences, and site-specific notes from the assessment. Customermates does not plan crews or book appointments; keep that in your scheduling tool and record the result on the deal.

### Post-install warranty and monitoring

The relationship does not end at commissioning. Track warranty registrations, monitoring system setup, and maintenance dates in custom fields. A saved view sorted by commissioning date shows which customers are due a 30, 90 or 365 day check-in, and that is a good moment to ask for referrals. Happy customers are a natural source of new leads, so a consistent post-install process also feeds your pipeline.

## Key features of a solar CRM

### Lead-to-install pipeline with Kanban view

See every project at a glance: New Inquiry, Qualified, Site Assessment Booked, Site Assessment Complete, Proposal Sent, Financing Review, Contract Signed, Permit Submitted, Permit Approved, Installation Planned, Installation Complete, Inspection, Commissioned. Drag deals between columns as they progress. Filter by rep, region, system size, or project value. The deal stage is one configurable column, and each stage can carry a probability for weighted pipeline totals.

### Follow-ups across the solar sales cycle

Solar decisions take weeks or months. Customermates has no built-in rules engine or reminders, but it gives you three ways to keep follow-ups from slipping:

- **Saved views**: proposals in **Proposal Sent** sorted by last update, or financing applications sorted by submission date, show what needs a call today.
- **Routines on Customermates Cloud**: the Mate assistant runs a saved prompt on a schedule or on a CRM event, for example listing proposals without response after five days and drafting a follow-up for each one for your review. Starter includes [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] routine per user and Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]].
- **External automation**: webhooks and the REST API feed a separately operated [n8n](/blog/n8n-for-beginners-build-your-first-workflow) or another scheduler that creates tasks or sends messages from your own accounts.

An AI client connected over MCP can also [summarize notes](/blog/chatgpt-summarize-notes) and help you decide which leads to call first, based on the project value and history you give it.

### Custom fields built for solar

Add the fields your solar business actually needs:

- **System specs**: kWp capacity, panel count, panel brand, inverter model, battery storage and capacity, microinverters vs. string inverter
- **Site details**: Roof pitch, azimuth, available area (sq ft / sq m), shading factor, electrical panel capacity
- **Financials**: System cost, financing type, lender, loan amount, monthly payment, federal/state incentives applied, net cost to customer
- **Project status**: Permit application date, permit approval date, interconnection status, inspection date, PTO (Permission to Operate) date
- **Post-install**: Warranty registration date, monitoring platform, first-year production (kWh), customer satisfaction score

### Source tracking

Know which channels deliver your best solar leads. A single-select field for lead source (paid search, social ads, canvassing teams, referral partners, home shows, real estate partnerships) lets you group deals by source and compare how many reach **Contract Signed** and what they are worth. Stop spending on channels that generate tire-kickers and double down on what produces signed contracts.

### Team dashboards

Dashboards show deal counts, values, and weighted pipeline totals by stage and by rep, calculated from your live records. Group deals by owner to see who has too many open proposals and who needs more leads.

### Mobile access for field teams

Site assessors, sales reps, and installation crews need CRM access from the roof, not the office. Update deal fields, record links to externally stored site photos, check permit status, and view notes from a phone or tablet browser. There is no native app; Customermates runs in the browser.

### Data protection and self-hosting

In the managed cloud, the application database runs in the AWS Frankfurt region; hosting and other subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [DPA](/dpa) is available. This matters for solar companies handling sensitive homeowner data such as energy usage, financial details, and property information. If you need full control over where data lives, self-host the open-source core. Learn more in [CRM best practices](/blog/crm-best-practices).

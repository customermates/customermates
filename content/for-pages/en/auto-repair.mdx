## Why Auto Repair Shops Need a CRM

A CRM for auto repair connects your service advisors, mechanics, and customers. Auto repair shops juggle walk-ins, booked jobs, parts orders, warranty questions, follow-ups, and customer communication, often across many active jobs per day. When these processes live in spreadsheets, whiteboards, and sticky notes, service quality drops and work gets lost.

Here are the pain points an auto repair CRM helps with:

- **Customers do not come back**: You did a great brake job, but six months later the customer forgets about you when they need an oil change. A CRM keeps the next service date on the vehicle record so you can see who is due.
- **Vehicle history is scattered**: Was it the alternator or the starter motor you replaced last year? Without records tied to the customer and vehicle, every visit starts from scratch. [Contact management](/features/contact-management) links every repair to the right owner.
- **Quotes go cold**: You inspect a vehicle and send a quote for additional work. The customer says they will think about it. You never follow up, and the job never happens.
- **No view of the business**: How many cars are in the shop right now? Which quotes are outstanding? Without a board or dashboard, you are guessing.
- **Parts notes are lost**: You ordered a specific part for a customer's vehicle but cannot find the note when they come in.
- **Warranty questions take too long**: Customers call asking if a repair is still under warranty, and nobody can find the answer quickly.

Customermates is a focused CRM for these problems. It is not a shop management system: it does not book appointments, write repair orders, manage inventory, or produce invoices. Most shops run it next to their shop management or invoicing tool.

## What Is a CRM for Auto Repair?

An auto repair CRM (Customer Relationship Management) is software that helps auto repair shops organize customer data, keep vehicle service history, track repair quotes and jobs, and keep customer communication in one place.

Shop management platforms add scheduling, repair orders, inspections, parts inventory, and invoicing. A CRM such as Customermates focuses on customers, vehicles, open quotes, jobs in progress, and conversations.

AI can help too: an AI client connected to the CRM can draft service recommendations from inspection notes, summarize a vehicle's history, or draft a follow-up after a quote, for you to review.

## Key Features of Auto Repair CRM Software

Here is how Customermates covers the daily work of an automotive service business.

### Vehicle History Tracking

Customermates has no dedicated vehicle object, so shops model vehicles with the records that exist. A common setup: the owner is a contact (or an organization for fleets), and each vehicle is a service record linked to the owner, with custom fields for make, model, year, license plate, VIN, mileage, and warranty dates. Each repair job is a deal linked to the owner and vehicle, with notes on parts used, diagnostic codes, and quotes given. When a customer calls, the history is on the record.

### Service Due Dates

Customermates does not send service reminders. Store the next service date or mileage in a custom field on the vehicle, and use a saved view sorted by that date to see who is due this month. On Customermates Cloud, a [routine](/docs/app-routines) can run on a schedule and have the Mate assistant list vehicles due for service and draft messages for you to review; the assistant asks for confirmation before anything is sent. If you want fully automated reminders, an external tool such as n8n (operated separately) can read the dates through the REST API and send them.

### Job Pipeline

Track every job from initial inquiry through diagnosis, quote, approval, work in progress, quality check, and completion on a drag-and-drop [kanban board](/features/pipeline). The deal stage is one configurable column; separate scheduled maintenance, diagnostics, bodywork, and warranty repairs with a custom "Job type" field and group or filter the board by it, or by assigned mechanic. The board shows which jobs wait for parts and which vehicles are ready for pickup. Customermates does not manage bay schedules or appointment slots.

### Parts Notes

Use custom fields and notes to record parts ordered, part numbers, supplier information, expected arrival dates, and costs against each job. Customermates is not an inventory management system, but it gives your service advisors the parts context they need when talking to the customer.

### Unified Inbox for Customer Communication

On Pro and above, customer email and WhatsApp come together in one [unified inbox](/features/email-integration), and you link each conversation to the customer. When a customer asks about their car, anyone on your team can see the conversation next to the customer record. Reply on the original channel, for example with a WhatsApp message when a vehicle is ready. SMS and phone calls are not channels.

### Warranty Tracking

Record warranty start dates, expiry dates, and coverage details against each repair and vehicle with custom fields. When a customer calls about a potential warranty claim, your service advisor can pull up the information in seconds. A saved view sorted by warranty expiry shows what runs out soon; there are no automatic alerts.

### Quote Follow-Up

Do not let quoted work disappear. A saved view of jobs in the "Quote sent" stage, sorted by last update, shows which quotes need a call. On Customermates Cloud, a routine can list quotes that have not moved for a week and draft a follow-up for each. Customermates tracks the quote as a stage and a value; it does not create quote documents.

### Fleet and Commercial Accounts

Track commercial customers with multiple vehicles as organizations. Link every vehicle and job to the organization to see the whole fleet in one view. Useful for delivery companies, taxi services, rental agencies, and local businesses that depend on their vehicles.

### AI Clients That Operate Your CRM

Connect Claude, ChatGPT, or another AI client through the MCP server ([[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools) and let it do the data entry: create customer and vehicle records, move jobs between stages, log notes, draft service recommendations from inspection notes, draft follow-ups after quotes, and summarize vehicle histories. You choose and pay for the AI client.

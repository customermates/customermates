## HVAC CRM and HVAC field-service software are different

Search results for “CRM for HVAC” often describe complete field-service management suites. Those products may combine customer records with booking, dispatch, technician availability, work orders, routing, equipment history, inventory, estimates, payments, and invoicing.

A sales CRM solves a narrower problem: Which lead needs attention? Who are the stakeholders at a commercial account? Which replacement opportunity is waiting on a decision? What is the next relationship action? Customermates answers those questions.

| HVAC need | Customermates | Field-service or trade software |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer and account context | Contacts, organizations, relations, notes, and activity | Service location and operational customer file |
| Installation or replacement opportunity | Deals, custom stages, filters, and kanban | Site survey, estimate, booking, and job execution |
| Commercial relationship | Account contacts, services under discussion, and tasks | Contract service calendar and SLA execution |
| Next sales action | Record-linked task | Technician availability, appointment, and route |
| Integration surface | REST, webhooks, MCP, and separate n8n option | Work orders, equipment, inventory, invoice, and payment |

## A supported HVAC sales workflow

Create a deal for an installation, replacement, commercial-service, or maintenance-renewal opportunity. The deal represents the commercial conversation. It does not become the job, work order, asset register, or invoice.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture the customer context">
    Create the homeowner, property manager, general contractor, or commercial
    account and connect the people involved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the opportunity">
    Add a deal for the replacement, installation, service agreement, or other
    commercial need the customer is evaluating.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure sales stages">
    Use stages such as new inquiry, assessment completed externally, quote
    prepared externally, proposal sent, follow-up, won, or closed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep a bounded operational reference">
    Store an external site, equipment, estimate, or FSM identifier in a custom

  </Step>
  <Step title="Record the next sales action">
    Attach a task to the relevant record, then run appointment, dispatch, job,
    and billing activity in the operational system.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Installation sales and commercial accounts

Replacement and installation sales are a strong CRM fit because the customer often evaluates a high-value proposal over several conversations. A configurable pipeline makes the commercial stage visible without claiming that Customermates creates an estimate or books the installation.

Commercial HVAC relationships can involve property owners, facility teams, general contractors, and multiple sites. Organizations and contacts preserve that business context. The authoritative site list, installed equipment, warranties, maintenance history, and service-level obligations remain in field-service or asset software.

A maintenance agreement approaching renewal can be represented as a commercial opportunity. A custom month or external contract reference can support a filtered review. Customermates does not schedule maintenance, calculate service intervals, or generate recurring visits.

## Follow-up, seasonal demand, and messaging

Tasks capture ordinary next actions but have no native due dates or recurrence. A customer-operated scheduler or separate [n8n workflow](/features/workflow-automation) can create a task from an approved date source. There is no built-in seasonal campaign, instant lead-routing, automated assignment, or maintenance-reminder engine.

The managed [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) is available from Pro upward for supported individual conversations. Participants can be associated with contact context. A message is not automatically attached to a property, piece of equipment, service job, or deal. Self-hosted deployments do not include the inbox.

An authorized external AI client can use supported [MCP](/docs/mcp) actions on CRM records. It can update supported relationship and opportunity data within user permissions. It does not predict equipment failure, optimize routes, dispatch technicians, generate a service report from unverified field data, or enrich a property automatically.

## Connecting CRM and field operations

A customer-built connection can use the [REST API and webhooks](/features/api), MCP, or the separate n8n integration to exchange a minimal customer or opportunity reference with an FSM, accounting system, or other tool. The customer owns the external provider, mapping, validation, permissions, retries, and duplicate handling.

No native ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, QuickBooks, Xero, GPS, routing, calendar-write, equipment, inventory, or invoicing connector is claimed. Customermates has no CRM record importer; any migration must be separately prepared and validated through supported interfaces.

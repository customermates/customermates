## Why lawyers need a CRM

As a lawyer, your practice depends on relationships. Whether you handle family law, immigration, criminal defense, or estate planning, your clients come through referrals, word of mouth, and repeat business. Yet many solo lawyers and small-practice attorneys manage these relationships with spreadsheets, sticky notes, and memory. The result: lost leads and good relationships that go quiet.

A CRM for lawyers changes that. It gives you a single system for every client, every matter from the relationship point of view, and every referral source.

Unlike [CRM solutions built for large law firms](/for/law-firms), Customermates is designed to work for individual lawyers and small practices, without the complexity, training overhead, or enterprise pricing that makes much legal CRM software impractical for solo practitioners.

### Pain points a lawyer CRM solves

- **Leads slipping through the cracks**: A potential client calls, you are in court, and the note gets lost. Without a system, that consultation never happens.
- **No referral tracking**: A colleague has sent you five clients this year, but you have no record and no way to reciprocate or thank them properly.
- **Scattered client information**: Notes in a folder, emails in your inbox, messages on your phone. No single view of a client relationship.
- **Relationships that go quiet**: Former clients who were satisfied could return or refer others, but you never stay in touch after the matter closes.
- **Conflict of interest checks**: Before accepting a new client, you need to search your client history quickly.

Customermates is not practice management software. It does not track time, bill clients, manage trust accounts, docket deadlines, or store case documents, so you run it next to your practice management and billing tools.

## How Customermates works as CRM software for lawyers

Customermates is a flexible, open-source CRM that adapts to how solo lawyers work. Here is what it does for your practice.

### Client intake and lead management

Stop losing potential clients. Every inquiry, whether from your website, a phone call, or a referral, becomes a deal in your [pipeline](/features/pipeline): you create it by hand, let an AI client or the Mate assistant create it from an email, or send it in through the REST API from your website form tool. You see every lead on a kanban board, from first contact through consultation, engagement letter, and active representation.

A saved view of inquiries sorted by last update shows who is still waiting for an answer. On Customermates Cloud, a [routine](/docs/app-routines) can run that check for you: every morning, the Mate assistant lists inquiries without a reply and drafts a response for you to review.

### Matter tracking

Track each matter as a deal. Link multiple matters to the same client over time and see the complete relationship history. Typed custom fields let you record practice area, matter type, jurisdiction, court, opposing counsel, and key dates as date fields.

Customermates has no due dates, reminders, or deadline alerts. Use [tasks](/features/task-management) as a record-linked checklist of next steps, and keep statute of limitations and filing deadlines in your calendar or docketing system.

### Billing stays in your billing tool

Customermates does not track time or create invoices. You can record a fee arrangement or billing status in custom fields and read deal data through the REST API, while your accounting or practice management tool creates and stores invoices. A separately operated [n8n workflow](/features/workflow-automation) can pass data between the two.

### Referral tracking and network management

Referrals are the lifeblood of solo practice. Record who referred each client on the matter, then group or filter matters by referral source to see who sends work, using [contact management](/features/contact-management).

Create custom fields for referral source, referral date, and reciprocity status. A saved view of referral sources sorted by a "Last thanked" date field shows who you should get back to.

### Unified inbox

On Pro and above, the [unified inbox](/features/email-integration) brings email (Gmail, Outlook, IMAP), LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Telegram conversations into Customermates, where you link each one to the client. When a former client messages about a matter from three years ago, the earlier conversation and your notes are right there. SMS and phone calls are not channels, and the inbox is not available self-hosted.

No more searching through separate inboxes trying to remember what was discussed.

### Automation through open interfaces

As a solo lawyer, you handle everything yourself. Customermates has no built-in workflow builder, but it offers webhooks, a REST API, and a community node for [n8n](https://n8n.io/), which you operate separately. With your own n8n workflow you can, for example:

- Send a thank-you through your own email service when a matter with a referral source is won
- Post new web inquiries from your form tool into your intake pipeline
- Build a weekly pipeline summary from the REST API

On Customermates Cloud, routines let the Mate assistant run a saved prompt on a schedule or on a CRM event. Starter includes [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] routine per user and Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]].

### AI agents that operate your legal CRM

External AI clients such as Claude or ChatGPT connect through [MCP](/docs/mcp) with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools, so an AI agent can read and write your CRM directly. It does the data entry, such as opening matters, updating intake stages, and logging referral notes, and can draft follow-up correspondence, summarize a client's history, or list which referral sources sent the most matters. You choose and pay for the AI client. On Customermates Cloud, the built-in Mate assistant works inside the app, drafts replies in the unified inbox on Pro, and asks for your confirmation before it sends a message or deletes anything. An external MCP client decides for itself whether it asks before writing.

## Key features of Customermates as a legal CRM

### Pipeline management with kanban view

See your practice at a glance. The deal stage is one configurable column, so you set the stages from initial inquiry to consultation, engagement, active work, and resolution, and drag matters between them. To keep intake and business development apart, add a single-select field such as "Track" and save a view for each.

### Complete client profiles

Notes, matters, linked conversations, and activity are recorded against the client. When a client calls, you see their history immediately, and when you consider a new matter, you can search your records for conflicts.

### Custom fields for legal practice

Add fields for practice area, matter type, jurisdiction, engagement date, fee arrangement, opposing counsel, court, and conflict check status. Customermates adapts to your practice without forcing you into a rigid template.

### Open source and self-hostable

Unlike proprietary legal CRM software, Customermates is open source. You can inspect the code and run it on your own servers, which keeps privileged client data on infrastructure you control. The self-hosted version has the Starter feature set, without the unified inbox or Mate, and you own backups and updates.

### Data protection

Client confidentiality and data protection are non-negotiable for lawyers. In the managed cloud, the application database runs in the AWS Frankfurt region; hosting and other subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [DPA](/dpa) is available. Customermates holds no certification. If you need full control over where data lives, self-host the open-source core.

## Getting started

Setting up Customermates for your legal practice:

1. **Create your account:** no credit card required. The first [[commercial.trial.days]] days are free.
2. **Bring in your records:** import contacts and matters from an Excel workbook (XLSX), one record type at a time, use the REST API, or let an AI client connected over MCP create them. There is no CSV import.
3. **Set up your stages:** name the deal stages for intake and active matters to match your workflow.
4. **Add custom fields:** define fields for practice area, matter type, referral source, fee arrangement, and anything else your practice needs.
5. **Connect your email:** on Pro, connect Gmail, Outlook, or IMAP to the unified inbox and link conversations to your clients.
6. **Connect automations:** set up webhooks or an n8n workflow you operate, or a routine on Customermates Cloud.

## Why lawyers need a CRM

As a lawyer, your practice depends on relationships. Whether you handle family law, immigration, criminal defense, or estate planning, your clients come through referrals, word of mouth, and repeat business. Yet most solo lawyers and small-practice attorneys manage these relationships with spreadsheets, sticky notes, and memory, losing track of leads, forgetting follow-ups, and leaving money on the table.

A CRM for lawyers changes that. It gives you a single system to track every client, every case, every referral source, and every billable interaction. Instead of juggling tools, you get one place that keeps your solo practice running smoothly.

Unlike [CRM solutions built for large law firms](/for/law-firms), Customermates is designed to work for individual lawyers and small practices, without the complexity, training overhead, or enterprise pricing that makes most legal CRM software impractical for solo practitioners.

### Pain points a lawyer CRM solves

- **Leads slipping through the cracks**: A potential client calls, you are in court, and the note gets lost. Without a system, that consultation never happens.
- **No referral tracking**: A colleague has sent you five clients this year, but you have no record and no way to reciprocate or thank them properly.
- **Scattered client information**: Case notes in a folder, emails in your inbox, billing in accounting software. No single view of a client relationship.
- **Missed follow-ups**: Former clients who were satisfied could return or refer others, but you never stay in touch after the matter closes.
- **Time tracking gaps**: Billable hours get lost because you reconstruct time entries at the end of the week instead of logging them as they happen.
- **Conflict of interest checks**: Before accepting a new client, you need to search your entire client history quickly and accurately.

## How Customermates works as CRM software for lawyers

Customermates is a flexible, open-source CRM that adapts to how solo lawyers actually work. Here is what it does for your practice.

### Client intake and lead management

Stop losing potential clients. With Customermates, every inquiry, whether from your website, a phone call, or a referral, goes into your [pipeline](/features/pipeline). You see every lead at a glance on a Kanban board, from first contact through consultation, engagement letter, and active representation.

Set up automated follow-ups so no prospect waits too long for a response. According to [Clio's Legal Trends Report](https://www.clio.com/resources/legal-trends/), law firms that respond to leads within one hour are seven times more likely to convert them. A lawyer CRM makes that response time achievable even when you are in court or in a meeting.

### Case and matter tracking

Track every active matter as a deal in your pipeline. Link multiple matters to the same client over time and see the complete relationship history. Custom fields let you record practice area, matter type, jurisdiction, court dates, opposing counsel, and case status, everything you need without switching between apps.

Use [task management](/features/task-management) to set deadlines, create checklists for case milestones, and ensure nothing falls through the cracks. When a statute of limitations is approaching or a filing deadline is coming up, you will know.

### Time management and billing support

Solo lawyers lose an average of 2-3 billable hours per week to poor tracking. Customermates helps you log time against specific contacts and matters so you can reconstruct your week accurately. Link every email, note, and activity to a matter, and use that timeline when generating invoices.

Customermates is not a billing or time-entry suite. Record a billing status or value in custom fields, read supported deal data through REST, and let an accounting tool create and store invoices. A separately operated [n8n workflow](/features/workflow-automation) may pass data to that external system.

### Referral tracking and network management

Referrals are the lifeblood of solo practice. Customermates lets you record who referred each client, track referral patterns over time, and maintain your professional network systematically using [contact management](/features/contact-management).

Create custom fields for referral source, referral date, and reciprocity status. Set reminders through your own automation to thank referral sources, check in with former clients, and nurture relationships that generate business.

### Native unified inbox

Every client email and WhatsApp message is linked to the right contact and matter through the [unified inbox](/features/email-integration). Email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Telegram sit on one timeline, so when a former client messages about a matter from three years ago, you have the full context instantly, every message, every note, every document reference in one place. The unified inbox is included on Pro and above.

No more searching through separate inboxes trying to remember what was discussed or what was promised.

### Automation with n8n

As a solo lawyer, you wear every hat. Customermates connects with [n8n](https://n8n.io/) to automate the repetitive tasks that eat into your day:

- Have your own n8n instance send appointment reminders before consultations; Customermates does not schedule them itself
- Trigger follow-up emails after a matter closes
- Create recurring check-in reminders for dormant client relationships
- Generate weekly pipeline reports so you know where your practice stands
- Route new web inquiries directly into your intake pipeline

### AI agents that operate your legal CRM

Customermates connects natively to Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini through [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] MCP tools, so an AI agent can read and write your CRM directly. It does the data entry, opening matters, updating intake stages, logging referral notes, and can draft follow-up correspondence, summarize client interaction histories, or analyze your referral patterns to identify your most productive relationship sources. The agent handles the administrative overhead so you can focus on practicing law, and because it can read and send across the unified inbox, replies go out without leaving the CRM.

## Key features of Customermates as a legal CRM

### Pipeline management with Kanban view

Visualize your entire practice at a glance. Create separate pipelines for client intake, active matters, and business development. Drag and drop cases between stages, from initial inquiry to consultation, engagement, active work, and resolution.

### Complete client profiles

Every email, note, matter, meeting, and interaction is recorded against the client. When a client calls, you see their full history immediately. When you consider a new matter, you can check for conflicts in seconds.

### Custom fields for legal practice

Add fields for practice area, matter type, jurisdiction, engagement date, fee arrangement, opposing counsel, court, and conflict check status. Customermates adapts to your specific practice needs without forcing you into a rigid template.

Unlike proprietary legal CRM software, Customermates is fully open source. You can inspect the code, host it on your own servers, and maintain complete control over privileged client data. For lawyers, this is not just a feature, it is an ethical obligation met.

Client confidentiality and data protection are non-negotiable for lawyers. Customermates is GDPR-native, hosted in the EU region, and designed with privacy as a default, not an afterthought. Self-hosting gives you the ultimate control over attorney-client privilege protections.

A small business needs a CRM once more than one person talks to the same customers, or once a follow-up slipped because it lived in someone's inbox. Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM made in Germany for small service teams that sell through relationships: B2B service firms, consultancies, agencies and owner-led companies whose work is won in email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp threads. It keeps people, organizations, open deals and their conversations connected, so anyone on the team can see where a customer stands and what happens next.

## What the example workspace shows

The [small business workspace](https://demo.customermates.com/en/w/small-business) is an example workspace with sample data for a small service team, not a customer account. It has five lists: Contacts, Organizations, Deals, Services and Tasks. Deals move through the stages New, Qualified, Conversation, Proposal sent, Won and Lost.

1. **The dashboard** opens first and shows the team's open deals by stage, so the weekly review starts from one screen.
2. **A customer record** shows the organization with its linked contacts, open deals and notes.
3. **The deal** carries the stage, the services under discussion, the assigned users and a configured next step date field, so the next action is visible without opening anyone's inbox.
4. **The conversation** with that customer sits in the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox). Its participants are matched to contacts by email address, phone number or LinkedIn profile, and the thread was linked to the deal by a person on the team.

## One inquiry from first message to next step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture who wrote">
    A prospect writes by email. Create or open the contact and link it to the
    organization, so colleagues see every person you know there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a deal">
    Create a deal in New, assign it to the person who answers, and add the
    services under discussion so the deal value follows from them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the conversation with the deal">
    On Pro, the thread arrives in the inbox. Link it to the deal yourself, or
    ask Mate to do it. Conversations are never attached to a deal
    automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the next action">
    Enter the next step date on the deal or create a task linked to it. Tasks
    are records you assign; dates and status are fields you configure, and
    there are no built-in reminders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the pipeline">
    Move the deal through Qualified, Conversation and Proposal sent. The
    [pipeline](/features/pipeline) board shows totals and weighted totals per
    stage, calculated from the probabilities you set.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Lists that fit your business

The example lists show one way a small service team can work. They are not what every new workspace contains: a new workspace starts without lists. On Customermates Cloud, the AI workspace setup builds lists, stages, a dashboard and Knowledge Base pages from your description of the business. It runs once during onboarding within a credit cap, and you can skip it with "Start with an empty workspace". Otherwise, people with Configure rights create the lists and fields themselves and import records from Excel workbooks. Self-hosted installations have no AI setup.

Rename lists to your own words, add fields such as a lead source or a lost reason, and link records across lists. How setup, configuration and import work step by step is on the [simple CRM](/features/simple-crm) page. For people and organizations in detail, see [contact management](/features/contact-management).

## Inbox and AI, with their plan limits

The unified inbox is part of Customermates Cloud. It brings Gmail, Outlook and IMAP email together with LinkedIn, [WhatsApp](/features/whatsapp-integration), Instagram and Telegram, and you reply on the original channel.

- **Starter and self-hosted:** no connected messaging accounts.
- **Pro:** each person connects their own accounts, [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] connected account per user included, and can share individual threads.
- **Business, Max and Enterprise:** a whole connected account can be shared, so several people can answer a team mailbox.

Mate, the assistant in Customermates Cloud, summarizes conversations, updates records and saves reply drafts within the hosted AI credits of your plan. It creates and updates records and sends messages as soon as you ask, so say "save a draft" when you want to review a reply first; drafts wait until you edit or send them. Deletions, imports, access changes and destructive changes to lists stop for your approval. A [routine](/docs/app-routines) lets Mate work on a schedule or when a record changes; it belongs to one user and sees what that user sees. Mate is not available when you self-host. Claude, ChatGPT and other clients can read and update permitted records through [MCP](/docs/mcp) on every plan, using your own AI provider.

## What stays in other systems

Customermates owns customer relationships and the sales side of your business. Other jobs stay with the tools built for them.

| Job | Where it stays |
|---|---|
| Quotes, invoices and bookkeeping | Your accounting software. Pass won deals on through [webhooks or the REST API](/features/api). |
| Appointment booking | Your booking tool. Customermates does not create or book calendar events. |
| Phone calls and SMS | Your phone system. There is no telephony or SMS. |
| Lead capture forms | Your website or form tool, sending contacts through the REST API. There are no web forms. |
| Specialist work such as projects, scheduling or a shop | The system that runs that work, connected through the API, webhooks or n8n. |

Also not included: native mobile apps (the web app runs in the browser on a phone), email sequences or bulk sending, lead scoring, shared team views and single sign-on. If one of these decides your purchase, the [guide to choosing a small business CRM](/blog/crm-for-small-business) compares other tools.

In Customermates Cloud, the database runs in the AWS Frankfurt region. The [subprocessors page](/subprocessors) lists the providers involved, including US ones, and a [data processing agreement](/dpa) is available. Customermates holds no certification such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Which CRM fits a small business?">
One your team keeps current. If deals come from calls, look at a CRM with calling built in. If you need web forms and email sequences, a sales automation tool fits better. If your customers decide in email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp threads with several people involved, Customermates fits. The [small business CRM guide](/blog/crm-for-small-business) walks through the choice.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does a new workspace come with lists and stages?">
No. A new workspace starts without lists. On Customermates Cloud, the AI workspace setup can build lists, stages, a dashboard and Knowledge Base pages from your description, once during onboarding and within a credit cap. You can also start empty and create lists yourself in Configure. The example workspace shows one possible result.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Are conversations added to the right deal automatically?">
No. Participants are matched to contacts by email address, phone number or LinkedIn profile. A conversation is linked to a deal or organization only when a person or Mate links it.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can Customermates remind me of follow-ups?">
Not with built-in reminders. Add a date field such as a next step to deals or tasks and save a view sorted by it. On Customermates Cloud, a routine can ask Mate to list deals whose next step has passed.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does it replace my accounting or booking tool?">
No. There are no quotes, invoices, calendar booking, web forms, telephony or SMS. Keep those tools and connect them through the REST API, webhooks or n8n where useful.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a mobile app?">
No native mobile app. The web app runs in the browser and adapts to narrow screens.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a free version?">
No hosted free plan. There is a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial without a credit card, and the open-source core can be self-hosted without a licence fee, with the Starter feature set and without the inbox and Mate.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

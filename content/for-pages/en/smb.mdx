A small business needs a CRM once more than one person talks to the same clients, or once a follow-up has slipped because it lived in someone's inbox. Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM built in Germany for client-facing teams of roughly 1 to 50 people: consultancies, agencies, B2B service firms and founder-led companies that win work through email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp. It is a poor fit if you sell by phone, run email campaigns or need single sign-on today; the limits section names what to look at instead. ## What changes as a small business grows

A team of 3 runs into different problems than a team of 40, and needs a different plan.

| Team size | What usually breaks | What the CRM has to do | Customermates plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 to 5 | Follow-ups live in one person's head and inbox | One list of clients and open deals, little setup | Starter, or Pro once conversations should land on the record |
| 6 to 20 | Two people chase the same client; nobody knows the latest message | Shared records, assigned users per deal, each client's conversations on the contact | Pro, with one connected account per user |
| 21 to 50 | Teams need separation; managers ask who changed what | Roles per team, an audit log, a team mailbox several people answer | Business, for [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedAccountsPerUser]] accounts per user and shared accounts |

Above that size, or when IT requires single sign-on and field-level permissions, an enterprise suite is the honest recommendation.

## A small-business setup in Customermates

Customermates has five record types. This mapping fits a B2B service business; field names are examples you create yourself.

| Record type | Used for | Example custom fields |
|---|---|---|
| Organizations | Client companies and partners | Segment (Single Select: Prospect, Client, Partner) |
| Contacts | Everyone you talk to at a client | Role in deal (Single Select: Decision maker, Champion, Buyer, Technical), Source (Single Select: Referral, Website, LinkedIn, Event) |
| Deals | Each piece of work you could win | Next step (Date), Lost reason (Single Select) |
| Services | What you sell, each with a price | Retainer, Project day, Workshop |
| Tasks | Commitments like "send the case study" | A date field you add, because tasks have no built-in due date |

A deal can link several contacts, which covers B2B work where a buyer, a decision maker and a technical reviewer all have a say. The Role in deal field on each contact tells a colleague who to call about what.

### Stages and a pipeline number you can explain

A new workspace has a **Stage** field on deals with a win probability per option: Prospecting 10%, Qualification 20%, Demo 40%, Proposal 60%, Negotiation 80%, Won 100% and Lost 0%. Rename them to match how you sell, for example Discovery instead of Demo. A deal's value is the sum of its linked services, price times quantity, and the [pipeline](/features/pipeline) shows totals and weighted totals per stage. The weighted number is your value times your own probabilities, so you can explain it to a partner or a bank. It is not a prediction.

### Saved views for the weekly review

1. **My open deals:** deals where you are an assigned user, as a board grouped by Stage.
2. **Stalled:** deals in Proposal or Negotiation whose **Updated at** is older than 14 days.
3. **Next steps:** open deals sorted by Next step, earliest first.
4. **New from referrals:** contacts with Source Referral, created in the last 30 days.

Saved views are personal, so each colleague sets the same filters and saves their own view.

### People, roles and the audit log

Deals have assigned users rather than a single owner, so two people can share an account. A custom role can set **Read access** to **Assigned** on contacts, organizations and deals, which lets a junior consultant or a freelancer see only their own clients. Permissions are per resource and action. The audit log records changes to records and workspace settings for roles that have the audit permission, on every plan and in the self-hosted edition.

### Conversations on the record (Pro and above)

In Customermates Cloud, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings Gmail, Outlook or IMAP email together with LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram, and you reply on the original channel. A conversation shows on a contact's timeline when a participant matches that contact's email address, phone number or profile. Threads from a personal account stay private until their owner shares them. On Business, a whole connected account can be shared, so a team mailbox is visible to everyone who answers it.

### Marketing handoff without a marketing platform

Customermates has no campaign object, forms, tracking or attribution model. What it can do is keep the source of each contact in a field like Source, written by hand, by import or by your website through the [REST API](/features/api). Your analytics or email tool stays in charge of clicks and campaigns. The CRM then answers the questions sales needs: where did this client come from, is there an open deal, and who is assigned.

### A Monday routine with Mate (Customermates Cloud)

Mate is the assistant in Customermates Cloud and uses hosted AI credits included per active user on every cloud plan; reading inbox conversations needs Pro or higher. A [routine](/docs/app-routines) runs a saved instruction on a schedule or when a record changes. Set it to repeat every week on Monday morning with an instruction like this:

```text
List deals in Proposal or Negotiation that have not been updated for 10 days.
For each, read the latest inbox conversation with its contacts. If my last
message has no answer, save a short follow-up as a draft reply in that
conversation. Do not send anything. End with a table: deal, stage, assigned
users, days since the last update, draft saved yes or no.
```

<AcquisitionCallout title="Ask for drafts, not sends" variant="boundary">
  Mate and routines can send email and chat messages without asking first,
  so the instruction has to say "Do not send anything". A routine runs as its
  owner and uses only that owner's channels.
</AcquisitionCallout>

Prefer your own AI? Claude, ChatGPT and other clients connect through [MCP](/docs/mcp) on every plan and on self-hosted installations, and your AI provider bills the model use.

## What Customermates does not do for small businesses

- No calling or SMS, no email sequences and no mass email.
- No web forms, lead scoring or data enrichment.
- No quotes, invoices or time tracking. Pass won deals to your accounting tool through webhooks or the API.
- Tasks have no due dates or reminders. Use a date field and a saved view.
- No native Slack app, no mobile app and no SSO.
- Self-hosting gives you the open-source CRM with REST, webhooks and MCP, but no inbox and no Mate.

If one of these decides your purchase, look at a tool built for it. Customermates publishes this overview; vendor details were checked on 8 October 2026 on each vendor's own website.

## Moving in from a spreadsheet or another CRM

<Steps>
  <Step title="Save each list as an Excel workbook">
    The import reads .xlsx files only. Save any other export as .xlsx first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import organizations, then contacts">
    On each list, open **Import and export** and choose **Add from file**.
    A company-name column on contacts links them to their organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import open deals">
    Include Stage and Next step, then link services so each deal gets a value.
    Check for duplicates first: an import cannot be undone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the inbox and invite the team">
    On Pro or higher, each person connects their accounts under **My Profile ›
    Channels**. Create roles before you invite people.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Records export the same way, one record type at a time, and the REST API reads every record your role can see. There is no whole-account export.

## Data protection

In Customermates Cloud, the primary production database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region. Hosting and other subprocessors, US providers among them, are on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [data processing agreement](/dpa) is available. Customermates holds no certification such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2. If you need full control over where data lives, self-host the open-source core.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Which CRM is best for a small business?">
The one your team will keep up to date. Start from how you sell: if deals come from calls, look at a calling-first CRM; if you need sequences and forms, a sales-automation tool; if work is won in email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp threads with several people at each client, Customermates fits. The [guide to choosing a CRM](/blog/how-to-choose-crm) walks through the questions.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How much does a CRM cost per month?">
For Customermates, [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per user on Starter and [[commercial.price.pro.monthly]] on Pro, so five people on Pro pay [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.5]] per month. When you compare, check whether a vendor's listed price assumes annual billing; HubSpot's Sales Hub Starter, for example, is cheaper per seat billed annually than billed monthly.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a free version?">
No hosted free plan. There is a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial without a credit card, and the open-source core can be self-hosted without a licence fee, but without the inbox and Mate.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I keep using Excel as my CRM?">
For one person and a few dozen clients, yes. A spreadsheet stops working when two people edit it, when you need to see the last message to a client, or when nobody notices a deal that stalled. Our [Excel CRM template](/blog/excel-crm-template) is a reasonable start, and an .xlsx sheet is also what Customermates imports when you move.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does it work for B2B sales with several decision makers?">
Yes. Link several contacts to one deal and record each person's part in a single-select field. All conversations with those contacts show on their timelines from Pro, so whoever picks up the deal sees what was said.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Which CRM has a native WhatsApp integration for growing companies?">
Customermates includes WhatsApp in the unified inbox on Pro, Business and Enterprise in Customermates Cloud, alongside email, LinkedIn, Instagram and Telegram. Self-hosted installations have no inbox.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

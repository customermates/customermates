## Why consultants need a CRM

If you are an independent consultant or run a consulting firm, your business lives and dies by relationships. New engagements come from repeat clients, referrals, and your professional network, not from inbound marketing funnels. But when you are deep in delivery, business development gets neglected, the pipeline dries up, and the feast-or-famine cycle continues.

A CRM for consultants solves this by giving you the structure to maintain a healthy pipeline alongside client delivery. Instead of relying on memory, sticky notes, and scattered spreadsheets, a consultant CRM centralizes every relationship, every proposal, and every follow-up in one place.

Common challenges a consulting CRM addresses:

- **Feast-or-famine revenue**: When you are busy delivering, you stop prospecting. When the project ends, the pipeline is empty and you are scrambling for the next engagement.
- **No systematic follow-up**: You met potential clients at conferences, exchanged cards, and then never followed up. Warm leads go cold because nothing shows you who is waiting.
- **Proposal tracking gaps**: You sent three proposals last month. Which ones are still pending? Who needs a follow-up call? Without a CRM for consulting firms, these questions require digging through email.
- **Repeat client neglect**: Past clients are your best source of new work, but without a system, you lose touch between engagements and miss expansion opportunities.
- **Referral blindness**: Colleagues and past clients refer business to you, but you have no record of who referred whom, when, or how to reciprocate.
- **Knowledge loss**: Project notes, client preferences, and engagement history live in your head or in scattered documents. When you onboard a subcontractor or grow your team, critical context disappears.

Customermates gives consultants the discipline and structure they need, without the complexity or price tag of tools built for large enterprise sales teams. It is a CRM, not a practice management suite: it does not track time, produce proposals as documents or send invoices.

## How Customermates works as your consultant CRM

Customermates is lean enough for a solo consultant and flexible enough for a growing consulting firm. Here is how the platform maps to your consulting workflow.

### Client relationship tracking

Every client and prospect gets a record: contact details, company, linked engagements, notes, activity history and custom data fields. When you walk into a client meeting, you have the full picture, not just what you personally remember from six months ago.

Link contacts to organizations, organizations to engagements, and engagements to team members. [Contact management](/features/contact-management) keeps every relationship in view, even when you juggle 20+ active relationships simultaneously.

### Engagement pipeline management

Consulting deals are not simple one-call closes. They involve discovery calls, scoping sessions, proposals, negotiations, and sometimes months of follow-up. Deals in Customermates are your engagements, and the deal stage is one single-select column you configure. The [kanban board](/features/pipeline) shows each engagement as a card you drag between stages, for example:

- **Initial contact**, First conversation, relationship building
- **Discovery**, Needs assessment, problem definition
- **Scoping**, Defining deliverables, timeline, and approach
- **Proposal sent**, Formal proposal or SOW delivered
- **Negotiation**, Terms, pricing, and timeline discussions
- **Won / Lost**, Engagement signed or opportunity closed

Each stage can carry a probability, and Customermates calculates weighted engagement values and weighted pipeline totals from it. That is a deterministic calculation from your own stage weights, not a prediction. Different practice areas or service lines share the same stage column; a custom single-select field such as "Service line" plus saved views or grouping shows each practice's engagements separately.

### Proposal management and follow-up

Record every proposal as an engagement in the **Proposal sent** stage with its value, and add custom date fields such as "Proposal sent on" and "Decision expected". A saved view filtered to that stage and sorted by the decision date shows which proposals need a call.

Customermates does not send reminders. On Customermates Cloud, a [routine](/docs/app-routines) can do the checking for you: every Monday, the Mate assistant lists proposals that have not moved for ten days and drafts a short follow-up for each one for you to review. Starter includes [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] routine per user and Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]].

### Retainer and recurring engagement tracking

Many consultants work on retainer agreements or have clients with recurring engagement patterns. Model them with services and custom fields such as renewal date, engagement terms and monthly fee, then:

- Keep a saved view of retainers sorted by renewal date, so renewals in the next 60 days sit at the top
- Group engagements by client to see who has had no new work for a while
- Let a cloud routine list retainers that renew soon and draft a review invitation for you to send
- Use tasks for the check-ins you want to do, assigned to the right consultant

This is where a CRM for consulting firms pays for itself, by making renewals and quiet clients visible before revenue slips.

### Time tracking stays in your time tracker

Consultants bill for their time, but Customermates does not track time. Keep Toggl, Harvest or Clockify, and if you want hours next to the engagement, write them into a number field through the REST API, [webhooks](/features/workflow-automation) or n8n, which you operate separately and connect through the Customermates community node.

### Knowledge management for consulting

Your accumulated knowledge about clients, industries, and engagement approaches is one of your most valuable assets. Customermates notes and custom fields hold:

- **Client preferences and working styles**, How does each client like to communicate? What are their decision-making processes?
- **Engagement notes and lessons learned**, What worked well in the last project? What should you do differently next time?
- **Industry-specific insights**, Notes about regulatory changes, market trends, and competitive dynamics that inform future proposals
- **Referral network**, Who knows whom, who has referred business before, and which relationships to nurture

Custom fields let you track project type, industry vertical, engagement model (fixed-fee, retainer, time-and-materials), estimated duration, day rate, and any other data specific to your consulting practice. Keep proposals and SOW documents in your file storage and store the link on the engagement; records do not hold file attachments.

## Automation that keeps your consulting pipeline moving

Business development requires consistent follow-up, the thing that breaks down first when consultants are deep in delivery work. Customermates has no built-in rules engine, but it gives you several ways to automate the repetitive parts:

- **Cloud routines**: the Mate assistant runs a saved prompt on a schedule or on a CRM event, for example "every Monday, list proposals without movement for ten days and draft follow-ups" or "when an engagement moves to Won, create a kickoff task".
- **Webhooks and REST**: Customermates sends [webhooks](/features/workflow-automation) for [[derived.webhooks.events.total]] event types, and the REST API lets external tools read and write records.
- **n8n**: if you run n8n yourself, the Customermates community node connects it, for example to create a check-in task when an engagement closes or to push signed engagements to your accounting tool.
- **Saved views**: filters such as "Scoping for more than three weeks" or "Past clients without an open engagement" turn follow-up into a list you work through.

Routines run on Customermates Cloud and use hosted AI credits. Customermates does not send nurture sequences or mass emails.

## AI agents that operate your CRM

Connect Claude, ChatGPT, or another AI client through [MCP](/docs/mcp) ([[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools); you choose and pay for the AI client. Agents create client records, move opportunities between pipeline stages, and log engagement notes, so the pipeline stays current, and they can also:

- **Draft proposal follow-ups** based on the engagement's notes and history
- **Summarize client interaction history** before meetings so you walk in fully prepared
- **Analyze your win rate** by industry, project type, or engagement size from the records they read
- **Spot cross-selling candidates** among existing clients
- **Draft personalized outreach emails** that reference specific client needs and your relevant experience

On Customermates Cloud, the Mate assistant does the same inside the app and asks for your approval before it sends a message or deletes anything.

## Unified inbox and communication tracking

On Pro and above, email (Gmail, Outlook, IMAP), LinkedIn, and WhatsApp come together in one [unified inbox](/features/email-integration), and you link each conversation to the client's record. When multiple team members communicate with the same client, everyone sees the conversation history. No more asking colleagues "what did we last discuss with this client?"

For consultants who rely heavily on email and messaging for client communication, this removes a lot of context-switching and information hunting each week. The inbox is part of Customermates Cloud and not available self-hosted.

## Reporting and pipeline analytics

Understand your consulting firm's business development with [dashboards](/features/reporting):

- **Pipeline value by stage**: How much is in proposal stage vs. negotiation vs. signed?
- **Weighted pipeline**: What your open engagements are realistically worth, based on your stage probabilities
- **Engagements by service line or referral source**: grouped by the single-select fields you define
- **Workload by consultant**: engagements and tasks grouped by owner

Dashboards show current CRM values. For time-based metrics such as cycle time or proposal conversion rate, export a view as an Excel workbook (XLSX) or read the data through REST into a BI tool you operate.

## Data protection and access control

Consulting firms handle sensitive client information, strategic plans, financial data, organizational assessments, and confidential business intelligence. Data protection is not optional for a consulting CRM.

- **Cloud database in Frankfurt**: in the managed cloud, the application database runs in the AWS Frankfurt region; hosting and other subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [DPA](/dpa) is available
- **Self-hostable**: for firms with strict data governance policies, run the open-source core on your own infrastructure and choose where the data lives
- **Open source**: inspect the code and review how data is handled
- **Roles and permissions**: control who can read, edit or delete each kind of record
- **Audit log**: every plan records who changed what

Customermates holds no compliance certification such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2. Whether your use is [GDPR](https://gdpr-info.eu/) compliant depends on how your firm processes client data; the DPA and the subprocessor list give you what you need for that assessment.

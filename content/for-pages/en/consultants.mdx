## What consultants need from a CRM

Many tools sold as consultant CRMs bundle the pipeline with projects, timesheets and billing. Separate the two jobs before you compare them. Winning work depends on relationships and timing; delivering it depends on hours, budgets and documents. A CRM for consultants has to do the first job well, and on that side consulting has its own shape:

- **A client is a company with several people.** The sponsor who hired you, your day-to-day contact and procurement at renewal time all belong to one organization.
- **Proposals wait for weeks.** The risky part is the silence after you send one, so you need a list sorted by when an answer is due.
- **Conversations are spread out.** The referral comes in on LinkedIn, scoping runs by email and the managing director prefers WhatsApp. A contact list sees none of it.
- **Past clients go quiet while you deliver.** They are the cheapest source of the next engagement and the first thing to slip in a busy project.
- **Client mail is confidential.** Once you work with associates, it matters who can read which conversation.

## A consultant setup in Customermates

Customermates has five record types. Here is one way to map them to consulting work; every field name is an example.

| Record type | Used for | Example custom fields |
|---|---|---|
| Organizations | Client companies | Relationship (single select: Prospect, Active client, Past client), Industry |
| Contacts | People at the client | Role (single select: Sponsor, Decision maker, Day-to-day contact, Procurement), Referred by |
| Deals | Each proposal or engagement | Stage, Engagement model (Fixed fee, Retainer, Time and materials), Proposal sent on, Decision expected, Proposal link, Renewal date |
| Services | Your offerings, each with a price | Advisory day, Discovery workshop, Monthly retainer |
| Tasks | Next steps, assigned to a person | A "Due on" date field you add, because tasks have no built-in due date |

Under **My Company › Settings** you can also rename record types from a preset list, for example Deals to Opportunities, Services to Offerings and Tasks to Follow-ups.

### Stages and weighted values

The deal stage is a single-select field you define, and each option gets a win probability in the same settings. A starting point for consulting: Conversation 10%, Scoping 25%, Proposal sent 50%, Negotiation 75%, Won 100%, Lost 0%.

A deal's value comes from the offerings linked to it, price times quantity. Say the offering "Advisory day" carries your day rate of €1,200 and a proposal includes 12 of them: the deal is worth €14,400, and in Proposal sent its weighted value is €7,200. That is arithmetic from your own probabilities, not a forecast. On the [pipeline board](/features/pipeline) each deal is a card you drag between stages.

### Four saved views for Monday morning

1. **Proposals waiting:** deals in Proposal sent, sorted by Decision expected.
2. **Stalled:** deals in Scoping or Proposal sent whose last update is older than 14 days, oldest first.
3. **Retainers by renewal:** deals with Engagement model Retainer, sorted by Renewal date.
4. **Quiet past clients:** organizations with Relationship Past client, sorted by last update, oldest first. "Last update" refers to the organization record itself, so this view works if you update its notes after each contact.

Saved views are personal, so each colleague sets the same filters and saves their own view.

### Client conversations in one inbox (Pro and above)

In Customermates Cloud on Pro, Business and Enterprise, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings email from Gmail, Outlook or IMAP together with LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram. You reply on the original channel. Link a participant to a contact once, and every conversation with that address appears on the contact's timeline.

Two details change the plan you need. Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] connected account per user, so a consultant who wants a mailbox and LinkedIn in the inbox needs Business, which includes [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedAccountsPerUser]]. And conversations from your own accounts stay private: colleagues, admins included, see a thread only after you share it, or after you share the whole account on Business and above.

### Meeting prep and a weekly client review with Mate (Customermates Cloud)

Mate is the assistant inside Customermates Cloud. It runs on hosted AI credits that every cloud plan includes per active user; reading inbox conversations needs Pro or higher. Before a call, ask in the chat:

> Summarize my relationship with Northwind over the last 90 days: emails, LinkedIn messages, deal changes and open follow-ups.

For the weekly review, set up a [routine](/docs/app-routines), a saved instruction that Mate runs on a schedule or when a record changes. Choose **Every week** under **Repeats**, Monday at 08:00, and give it instructions like these:

```text
Find deals in the stages Proposal sent and Negotiation that have not been
updated for 10 days. For each one, read the latest inbox conversation with
its contacts. If the last message was sent by me and has no answer, save a
short, friendly follow-up as a draft reply in that conversation. Do not send
anything. End with a list: client, deal, stage, days since the last update,
draft saved yes or no.
```

<AcquisitionCallout title="Ask for drafts, not sends" variant="boundary">
  Mate and routines can send email and chat messages without asking first, so
  tell a review routine to save drafts only. Filter the inbox by Draft: has
  some, read each draft and send it yourself. Each run has a credit cap, so
  keep the list short.
</AcquisitionCallout>

A second routine can react to events instead: when a deal's Stage changes to Won, create a task "Kickoff call" linked to that deal and assigned to the same person. Starter includes [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] routine per user and Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]].

Prefer your own AI client? Connect Claude, ChatGPT or another client through [MCP](/docs/mcp). It works on every plan and on self-hosted installations, with model use billed by your AI provider. The messaging tools need Pro or higher in the cloud.

## What Customermates does not do for consultants

- No time tracking, no billing, no proposal or contract documents, no project delivery and no client portal.
- Tasks have no due dates, reminders or notifications. Add a date field and a saved view, or let a routine do the checking.
- No email sequences or bulk outreach. Follow-ups are written one conversation at a time.
- No file storage on records. Keep the proposal in your file storage and its link on the deal; files in messages stay in the inbox thread.
- No mobile app and no calendar booking.

If one of these is why you are shopping, look at a tool built for it. Customermates publishes this page; vendor details were checked on 8 October 2026 on each vendor's own website.

| If you need | Look at | What the vendor's site lists |
|---|---|---|
| Proposals, contracts and payments in one client flow | [HoneyBook](https://www.honeybook.com/) | Proposals, contracts, invoicing, payments, scheduling and a client portal; available to businesses in the US, Canada, the UK and Australia |
| Time tracking, projects and billing in one tool | [MOCO](https://www.mocoapp.com/) | Time tracking, projects, planning, quotes, billing and leads; names consultancies, agencies and IT service providers; Swiss vendor |
| Professional services automation (PSA) for a growing firm | [BigTime](https://www.bigtime.net/) | Time and expense tracking, resource management, scoping and quoting, invoicing and payments; names consulting |
| Warm-intro paths and relationship strength scores | [4Degrees](https://www.4degrees.ai/) | Relationship strength scoring with email and calendar sync; names consulting next to private-market firms |
| A free contact database for one or two people | [HubSpot free CRM](https://www.hubspot.com/products/crm) | Free for up to two users and 1,000 contacts |

A workable split is two systems: Customermates for relationships and pipeline, and a tool such as MOCO or BigTime for hours and billing. A webhook fires when a deal changes, so an n8n workflow you run separately can pass a won deal to the other tool, provided that tool has an API.

## Client data and confidentiality

- In Customermates Cloud, the primary production database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region. That is a product setting rather than a contractual residency promise. Hosting and the other subprocessors, US providers among them, are on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a data processing agreement is available.
- A custom role with Assigned read access limits an associate or subcontractor to the records assigned to them.
- The audit log records who changed which record, on every plan.
- Customermates holds no certification such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2. If a client contract requires one, check that before you move their data.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="What is the best CRM for consultants?">
It depends on the job. If time tracking and billing belong in the same tool as the pipeline, a consulting suite such as MOCO or BigTime fits. For proposals, contracts and payments in one client flow in the US, Canada, the UK or Australia, look at HoneyBook. If your work comes from relationships and conversations and you want AI to prepare follow-ups, Customermates fits; it is our own product, so weigh that.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does an independent consultant need a CRM?">
A spreadsheet works while you can remember every open proposal. It stops working when proposals wait for weeks and a busy project pulls your attention away from them. A solo consultant pays [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per month on Starter, or [[commercial.price.pro.monthly]] on Pro with one mailbox in the inbox.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is a CRM consultant the same as a CRM for consultants?">
No. A CRM consultant is a person or firm you hire to select, set up or customize a CRM. A CRM for consultants is software a consulting business uses to manage its own clients. If you are planning a rollout with or without outside help, our [guide to CRM implementation](/blog/crm-implementation) walks through it.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can Customermates track time and handle billing?">
No. Customermates has no time tracking and no billing. Keep hours and invoicing in your time tracker or accounting tool. If you want hours next to the deal, write them into a custom field through the REST API or an n8n workflow you run separately.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Customermates map relationships across client accounts?">
It links one contact to several organizations and deals, so you can see whom you know at each client and which proposals they touch. It does not score relationship strength or find warm-intro paths in your mailbox. Relationship-intelligence tools such as 4Degrees are built for that.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Will AI replace the CRM?">
AI changes who types into the CRM more than whether you need one. Mate or an MCP client such as Claude can log a call, move a deal and save a reply draft, but it needs client history, stages and dates to work from, and those live in the CRM. Mate sends a message without asking when you tell it to send, so say in each request or routine whether it should send or only save a draft.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

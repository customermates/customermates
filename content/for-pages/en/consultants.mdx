## What consultants need from a consulting CRM

Many tools sold as consultant CRMs bundle the pipeline with timesheets and billing. Winning work and delivering it are different jobs: the first depends on relationships and timing, the second on hours and documents. For the first job, consulting has its own shape:

- **A client is an organization with several people.** The sponsor who hired you, your day-to-day contact and procurement at renewal all belong to one client.
- **Engagements move slowly.** Between the first introduction and a decision there are weeks of discovery, scoping and silence after the proposal.
- **Conversations are spread out.** The introduction arrives on LinkedIn, scoping runs by email. A contact list sees neither.
- **Past clients go quiet while you deliver.** They are the most likely source of the next engagement and the first thing to slip during a busy project.
- **Client correspondence is confidential.** Once associates join, it matters who can read which conversation.

## One engagement in the example workspace

The [consulting workspace](https://demo.customermates.com/en/w/consultants/dashboard) is an example with sample data, not a customer account. It has four lists: **Clients**, **Contacts**, **Engagements** and **Tasks**. Engagements move through **Introduction**, **Discovery**, **Scoping**, **Proposal sent**, **Decision**, **Won** and **Lost**. Won engagements then move across a second board, **Active engagements**, through **Not started**, **In delivery**, **Wrapping up** and **Completed**.

Open one engagement in Proposal sent and you see what a consultant checks before the next call:

- **The client** with its contacts and their role in the engagement: the sponsor, the day-to-day contact and the person who signs off.
- **The fee**, calculated from two fields you fill in: the estimated days times the day rate. Each stage has a win probability, and the weighted value is that probability applied to the fee. It is pipeline weighting, not a forecast. A duration field counts the days from the planned start to the end.
- **The conversations.** An email thread and a LinkedIn conversation with the sponsor, linked to this engagement. Contacts are matched to conversations by their email address or LinkedIn identity in the Contact details field; the link from a conversation to the engagement or client was made explicitly by a person, never automatically.
- **A next-step date** in an ordinary date field, recorded by hand after the last call. Tasks have no built-in due dates or reminders, so the date lives in a field you configure and a saved view sorts by it.

- **The proposal** as a PDF in the engagement's Documents field, a File field. Search finds the text inside it, and Mate can read it before a call.

"Proposal sent" is a stage, not a document: you write the proposal in your own tool and add the PDF to the engagement.

Won engagements also appear in a **Delivery calendar** view, each one spanning its start and end date. The Calendar page shows the engagement periods, the next-step dates and the meetings from the connected Google calendar on one timeline, and the dashboard lists the next steps of the coming 14 days.

## Client conversations next to the engagement

In Customermates Cloud, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings email from Gmail, Outlook or IMAP together with LinkedIn, including supported Sales Navigator conversations, plus WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram. You reply on the original channel. The plan decides what you get:

- **Starter and self-hosted:** no connected messaging accounts.
- **Pro:** your own connected accounts, [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] per user included, and you can share individual threads with colleagues.
- **Business, Max and Enterprise:** whole-account sharing, so a partner can share a full mailbox with an associate. Business includes [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedAccountsPerUser]] connected accounts per user.

Conversations from your own accounts stay private until you share them. That suits client confidentiality: an associate sees only the threads you choose.

## Meeting prep and follow-up drafts with Mate

Mate is the assistant inside Customermates Cloud. It runs on hosted AI credits included per active user on every paid cloud plan, and is not available in self-hosted installations. Before a call, ask in the chat for a summary of an engagement: its stage, the latest email and LinkedIn messages and the next-step date. After the call, ask it to update the stage and save a short follow-up as a draft in the conversation.

Mate can send messages when you tell it to send, so ask for drafts when you want to read first. Deletions, imports and access changes always stop for your approval. A [routine](/docs/app-routines) runs Mate on a schedule or when a record changes: for example every Monday, list engagements in Proposal sent whose next-step date has passed and save a draft reply for each. The example workspace has two: one adds a short brief to the engagement before each of the day's calendar meetings, the other creates a task for close contacts you have not met in two months. A routine belongs to one user and sees what that user sees. Starter includes [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] routine per user and Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]].

Prefer your own AI client? Connect Claude, ChatGPT or another client through [MCP](/docs/mcp). It works on every plan and on self-hosted installations, with model use billed by your AI provider, and can read or update the records your role permits.

## Working with associates and partners

Customermates fits a small firm, with limits worth knowing before you invite a team:

- **Shared views are read-only.** Share a view with the whole firm or with selected roles and everyone works from the same filters, but only its owner changes it.
- **No field-level permissions.** Roles control access to lists and records. A custom role with Assigned read access limits an associate or subcontractor to the records assigned to them, but you cannot hide a single field such as a day rate.
- **Whole-account inbox sharing starts at Business.** On Pro, threads are shared one at a time.
- The audit log records who changed which record.

## Setting up your own workspace

A new workspace starts without lists. The consulting lists above are an example, not what every account contains. In Customermates Cloud, the AI workspace setup asks what the workspace is for and builds lists, stages, a dashboard and Knowledge Base pages from your answers. It runs once at onboarding, uses a capped amount of AI credit, and you can skip it with **Start with an empty workspace** (see [onboarding](/docs/app-onboarding)).

Otherwise, people with Data model rights create the lists, fields and stages in Data model, then import records from an Excel workbook (.xlsx, up to 5,000 rows per file). Import clients first, then contacts, then open engagements. An import cannot be undone, so test it with a few rows. If a team member needs a list renamed, for example Engagements to Mandates, that is a setting in Data model too.

## What Customermates does not do for consultants

- No time tracking, billing, proposal or contract writing, project delivery or client portal.
- Files in messages stay in the inbox thread, not on the engagement. Add the proposal itself to a File field on the engagement.
- No email sequences or bulk outreach. Follow-ups are written one conversation at a time.
- No task due dates or reminders built in, no mobile app and no calendar booking.
- No relationship-strength scoring or contact enrichment.

If one of these is the reason you are shopping, look at a tool built for it. Vendor details below were checked on 8 October 2026 on each vendor's own website.

| If you need | Look at | What the vendor's site lists |
|---|---|---|
| Time tracking, projects and billing in one tool | [MOCO](https://www.mocoapp.com/) | Time tracking, projects, planning, quotes, billing and leads; names consultancies and agencies |
| Professional services automation for a growing firm | [BigTime](https://www.bigtime.net/) | Time and expense tracking, resource management, quoting, invoicing and payments |
| Proposals, contracts and payments in one client flow | [HoneyBook](https://www.honeybook.com/) | Proposals, contracts, invoicing, payments and a client portal; US, Canada, UK and Australia |

A workable split is two systems: Customermates for relationships and engagements, and a tool such as MOCO or BigTime for hours and billing. A webhook fires when an engagement changes, so an n8n workflow you run separately can pass won work to the other tool if it has an API.

Views yes, fields no. Share a view with the whole firm or with selected roles; colleagues see it read-only and only its owner changes it. Permissions work on lists and records, not on single fields. A role with Assigned read access limits someone to the records assigned to them.

No. Participants are matched to contacts by their email address or LinkedIn identity. A conversation is linked to an engagement or client only when you or an agent link it explicitly.

<FaqItem question="Can Customermates track time and handle billing?">
No. Keep hours and invoicing in your time tracker or accounting tool. If you want hours next to the engagement, write them into a number field through the REST API or an n8n workflow you run separately.

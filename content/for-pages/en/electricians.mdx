## What a CRM for electricians should do

A **CRM for electricians** is the place where every enquiry, quote, job and customer conversation of an electrical business is recorded, so nothing depends on one person's memory or phone. For an **electrical contractor CRM**, that means three things: a pipeline that shows where every job stands, a record for each customer and site with its history, and one place where customer messages arrive.

The typical failure points in an electrical business are well known:

- **Quotes go quiet.** You price a rewire or a panel upgrade after a site visit, the week fills up with service calls, and nobody checks back.
- **History lives in someone's head.** What was installed at this address, when it was last tested and what was quoted is known to the electrician who did it, not to the office.
- **Messages are scattered.** Customers email plans, send photos on WhatsApp and message on Instagram, often to a personal phone.
- **No overview.** How many quotes are open, what they are worth and which jobs are waiting to be booked is a guess.

Customermates is a focused electrician CRM for exactly these problems. It is not a field service management suite: it does not dispatch crews, plan routes or produce invoices. Most electrical contractors run it next to their accounting or invoicing tool.

## How electrical contractors use Customermates

### A job pipeline from enquiry to completion

Deals in Customermates are your jobs. The deal stage is a single-select field you configure, so the pipeline can follow your actual process: **Enquiry → Site visit → Quote sent → Accepted → Booked → In progress → Completed**. The [kanban board](/features/pipeline) shows each job as a card you drag between stages, and grouping by owner, organization or a custom field shows the same jobs by electrician, customer or job type.

Each stage can carry a probability. Customermates uses it to calculate weighted job values and weighted pipeline totals, so you can see what your open quotes are realistically worth. That is a deterministic calculation from your own stage weights, not a prediction.

### Customer and site records with the full history

Contacts and organizations hold the people and companies you work for, and deals and services link to them. A landlord with twelve properties, a facilities manager at a factory and a homeowner each get a record that shows every job, note and conversation. See [contact management](/features/contact-management).

### Custom fields for electrical work

Add typed custom columns for what your trade needs, for example:

| Field                      | Type          | Example values                             |
| -------------------------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Job type                   | Single select | Service call, rewire, panel upgrade, EV charger, solar, inspection |
| Sector                     | Single select | Residential, commercial, industrial        |
| Last inspection            | Date          | 2026-03-14                                 |
| Next inspection due        | Date          | 2031-03-14                                 |
| Certificate or report no.  | Text          | EICR-2026-0418                             |
| Document folder            | Link          | Link to the job folder in your file storage |

Records do not hold file attachments. Keep photos, test results and certificates in your file storage and store the link on the job.

### Follow-up on open quotes

A saved view that filters jobs in the **Quote sent** stage and sorts them by last update shows exactly which quotes need a call. On Customermates Cloud, a [routine](/docs/app-routines) can do the checking for you: every Monday morning, the Mate assistant lists quotes that have not moved for a week and drafts a short follow-up for each one for you to review. Starter includes [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] routine per user and Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]].

### Customer messages in one inbox

On Pro and above, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings email (Gmail, Outlook, IMAP), WhatsApp, Instagram, LinkedIn and Telegram into Customermates. You link each conversation to the customer, answer from the office on the original channel, and share a conversation with the electrician on the job. SMS and phone calls are not channels.

### AI that works on your records

External AI clients such as Claude or ChatGPT connect through [MCP](/docs/mcp) and can create and update jobs, add notes from a site visit or summarize a customer's history. Customermates registers [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] MCP tools; you choose and pay for the AI client. On Customermates Cloud, the built-in Mate assistant does the same inside the app and asks for approval before it sends a message or deletes anything.

You visit the site, send the price and get busy, and the quote goes quiet. A WhatsApp enquiry arrives while you are on a roof, and by evening it is buried. Customermates keeps every enquiry on the customer record. On Customermates Cloud from Pro, Mate drafts the reply and the next step when you ask or on a routine, or your own AI agent (Claude, ChatGPT or any client that speaks MCP) does it through MCP. It does not schedule crews, write quotes, send invoices or run as a native phone app. For all of that, field-service software such as Jobber, Housecall Pro or, in Germany, HERO or Plancraft fits better; the table below shows where the line runs.

## CRM or job management software?

Most products sold as a CRM for tradespeople are job management suites with a customer list inside. They are the right choice when the field side is your bottleneck. A CRM is the better choice when the jobs run fine and the leaks are earlier: the call-back you forgot, the quote that sat for three weeks.

| Your main need | Customermates | Field-service or trade software |
|---|---|---|
| Logging every enquiry, whichever channel it came in on | Yes: jobs on a board, WhatsApp and email in the inbox on Pro | Varies by product |
| Seeing which quotes need a call | Saved views by stage and date, Mate drafts in Cloud | Varies by product |
| Booking and dispatching technicians | No | Core feature |
| Writing quotes and invoices, taking payments | No | Core feature |
| Native app for the crew, offline use | No, the web app runs in the phone browser | Core feature |
| Time tracking, site diary, materials | No | Often included |
| Open source and self-hosting | Yes, AGPL core | Varies by product |

Some firms run both: job software for the work itself and Customermates for the commercial side, such as builders bidding on tenders or HVAC firms selling replacements and maintenance contracts. That only makes sense if the overlap does not double your typing.

## What a trade business needs from a CRM

- **Enquiries come in everywhere.** A WhatsApp with three photos, a missed call, an email from a property manager, a message on Instagram. If they don't land in one list, some never get an answer.
- **Quotes go quiet.** You visit the site, send the price and get busy. The customer who hears nothing for ten days often books someone else.
- **Two kinds of work.** A dripping tap is booked the same day. A heat pump, a rewire or a roof runs through a site visit, a quote and weeks of waiting. A pipeline has to show both without burying the large jobs.
- **Repeat work has a date.** Boiler services, electrical inspections, gas safety checks, HVAC maintenance, window cleaning rounds. The money is in calling customers before they call someone else.
- **Some customers are companies.** Landlords, property managers and general contractors send many jobs across many sites, and you need their history in one place.

## A trade setup in Customermates

A new workspace starts without lists: you create the lists you need (contacts, organizations, deals or your own) in **Data model**, or let AI setup build them in the cloud. Clients instead of Contacts and Jobs instead of Deals fit most trades, and you can rename any list later in **Data model** under **General**. Every field name below is an example.

| Record type | Used for | Example custom fields |
|---|---|---|
| Contacts (Clients) | Homeowners, tenants, site contacts | Customer type (single choice: Private, Landlord, Commercial), Site address (address), Next service (date) |
| Organizations | Landlords, property managers, general contractors | Account type (single choice), Payment terms (text) |
| Deals (Jobs) | Each enquiry and job | Stage, Job type (single choice), Site address, Quote sent on (date), Follow up on (date), Planned for (date), Photos and documents (file) |
| Services | Your standard items, each with a price | Boiler service, Socket installation, Call-out fee |
| Tasks | Next steps, assigned to a person | A "Due on" date field you add, because tasks have no built-in due date |

### Stages and the value of open quotes

The stage is a single choice field you define, and each option can carry a win probability. A starting point: New enquiry 10%, Site visit 25%, Quote sent 40%, Accepted 90%, Booked 100%, Done 100%, Lost 0%. Same-day repair calls skip from New enquiry to Booked; large jobs walk through every stage. Filter the board by Job type when you want to see only one kind.

A job's value is the sum of its line items, each a service times quantity. A heat pump installation quoted at €14,000 sits in Quote sent at a weighted €5,600. That is arithmetic from your own probabilities, not a forecast. On the [pipeline board](/features/pipeline) each job is a card you drag between stages, and you can assign several people to it.

### Four saved views for the morning

1. **New enquiries:** jobs in New enquiry, newest first.
2. **Quotes to chase:** jobs in Quote sent, sorted by Follow up on, earliest first. Sort by Quote sent on instead to see the oldest quotes first.
3. **Booked this week:** jobs in Booked, sorted by Planned for. This is a list, not a schedule; it does not check who is free.
4. **Services due:** clients with a Next service date, sorted by it, soonest first. Date filters on your own fields take fixed dates (before, after, between), so a sort keeps this view current without editing it.

Share **Services due** with the whole crew or with selected roles, so the office and the vans work from the same list.

On the dashboard, an **Upcoming** widget on Follow up on lists the jobs due in the coming days, with an **Overdue** section. Customermates sends no reminders; the list is what you open each morning.

### WhatsApp and email in one inbox (Pro and above)

On Pro and higher in Customermates Cloud, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings in email from Gmail, Outlook or IMAP, WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram and LinkedIn. You reply on the original channel. The filter **Participants: not fully linked** shows conversations with people who have no matching contact yet, which in a trade business usually means new enquiries. Link the sender once and every conversation with that number or address appears on the client's timeline. Photos a customer sends stay in the conversation; download the ones you need and add them to a File field on the job.

Plan for two details. Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] connected account per user, so a sole trader who wants WhatsApp and a mailbox in the inbox needs Business, which includes [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedAccountsPerUser]]. A team on Pro can split it: the office connects WhatsApp, the owner connects email. And conversations stay private to whoever connected the account until they share a thread; sharing a whole account needs Business. Phone calls and SMS are not channels.

### An evening enquiry round with Mate (Customermates Cloud)

Mate is the assistant inside Customermates Cloud and runs on hosted AI credits that each paid cloud plan includes per active user; reading inbox conversations needs Pro or higher. A [routine](/docs/app-routines) runs a saved instruction on a schedule. Choose **Every day** at 18:00 and write something like:

```text
Look at inbox conversations where the last message was received today
and the sender is not linked to a contact. For each one that is a job
enquiry, create a job in the stage New enquiry with the job type and site
address if the customer gave them. Save a short draft reply that thanks
them and asks for photos and two times that suit them for a site visit.
Do not send anything. End with a list: name, channel, job type, draft
saved yes or no.
```

A second routine on Monday morning can find jobs in Quote sent with no update for ten days and save a polite follow-up draft for each.

<AcquisitionCallout title="Ask for drafts, not sends" variant="boundary">

Starter includes [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] routine per user and Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]]; the inbox round above needs Pro, because Starter has no inbox. Prefer your own agent? Connect Claude, ChatGPT or any client that speaks [MCP](/docs/mcp), on any plan or a self-hosted server (minus the inbox), with model use billed by your AI provider. Your client follows the permissions of the account it connects with; whether it asks before acting depends on the client.

## What Customermates does not do for trades

- No scheduling board, dispatch, routes or technician availability. A job with a Planned for date is a date you can see in a view, not a booking.
- No quotes, invoices or payments. Write the quote in your trade or accounting software and record its value on the job.
- No time tracking, site diary, materials or stock.
- No native iOS or Android app and no offline mode. The web app runs in your phone's browser.
- Tasks have no due dates, reminders or notifications. Add a date field and a saved view, or let a routine do the checking.
- No phone system or SMS, and no bulk messages to all customers.

## Moving in from a spreadsheet or phone contacts

Save your customer list as an Excel workbook (.xlsx) or a CSV file. Import landlords and contractors as organizations first, then contacts with a column naming their company, so the import links them. Open jobs come last, with stage, job type and quote dates. Records import and export one record type at a time; the [records guide](/docs/app-records) has the limits. In Customermates Cloud the primary database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region, the [subprocessors](/subprocessors) are listed, and a data processing agreement is available.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="What is the best CRM for tradespeople?">
It depends on where you lose work. If booking jobs, sending invoices and running a crew app are the daily pain, a job management suite such as Jobber, Housecall Pro or ServiceTitan fits, or HERO and Plancraft in Germany. If the jobs run well and enquiries and quotes slip, a CRM such as Customermates fits. It is our own product, so weigh that.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does a sole trader need a CRM?">
Not always. A notebook and your phone work while you remember every open quote. It stops working when enquiries arrive on three channels and quotes wait for weeks. A sole trader pays [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per month on Starter, or [[commercial.price.pro.monthly]] on Pro with WhatsApp or email in the inbox.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a free version?">
Customermates Cloud has a free plan for [[commercial.limit.free.maxMembers]] person and [[commercial.limit.free.maxRecords]] records, without the inbox, Mate or routines, and a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial of Pro without a credit card. You can also self-host the open-source core without a licence fee, with the Starter feature set and without the inbox, Mate and routines.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can Customermates create quotes and invoices?">
No. Write quotes and invoices in your trade or accounting software and record the value on the job. A webhook fires when a job is updated, for example when its stage changes, so an n8n workflow you run separately can pass an accepted job to another tool, provided that tool has an API.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I use it on my phone on site?">
Yes, in the phone's browser. Parts of the interface, such as the inbox, switch to a narrow-screen layout where the list and the conversation take turns. There is no native app and no offline mode, and the trial is the quickest way to check how it feels on your phone.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can it handle WhatsApp enquiries?">
Yes, on Pro and above in Customermates Cloud. Connect a WhatsApp account under Settings › Channels & calendars and its conversations appear in the inbox, where you reply and link the sender to a client. Self-hosted installations have no inbox.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I track maintenance contracts and annual services?">
Yes, with fields you add. Put a Next service date and a Contract type single choice on the client, and save a view sorted by Next service, soonest first. Customermates sends no reminders on its own; in Cloud a monthly routine can list due customers and save a draft message for each.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

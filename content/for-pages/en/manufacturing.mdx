## Why manufacturing companies need a CRM

A CRM for manufacturing bridges the gap between your production systems and your revenue pipeline. Manufacturing sales are different from retail or SaaS: long B2B sales cycles stretching across months, account structures with multiple decision-makers, distributor networks that add layers of relationship management, and repeat orders that sustain your business for years. Losing track of any of these moving parts costs real revenue.

Yet many manufacturers still manage their sales and customer relationships with spreadsheets, ERP add-ons that were never designed for relationship management, or the knowledge in individual salespeople's heads. When a key account manager leaves, years of relationship context walk out the door.

A CRM for manufacturing brings these pieces together. It does not replace your ERP or production planning system; it complements them by managing the relationships, the deal [pipeline](/features/pipeline), and the conversations that drive revenue.

## Key challenges in manufacturing sales

Manufacturing companies face [sales](/features/sales) and relationship management challenges that generic tools do not address well:

- **Complex account structures**: One customer might have multiple plants across different regions, each with its own procurement team, engineering contacts, and decision-makers. Understanding who influences what decision at each location is critical for effective [account management](/features/account-management).

- **Long sales cycles with many touchpoints**: Technical products require specification reviews, samples, testing, certifications, and multi-level approvals. A single deal can involve dozens of interactions over many months, all of which need to be tracked in your [sales pipeline](/blog/sales-pipeline).

- **Distributor network management**: Many manufacturers sell through distributors, which adds another layer of relationships. Tracking territory assignments and key contacts at each partner requires a clear [contact management](/features/contact-management) structure.

- **Repeat order tracking**: Manufacturing thrives on recurring business. Knowing when a customer last ordered and reaching out before they reorder from a competitor requires [sales tracking](/features/sales-tracking) data you can act on.

- **Technical data alongside commercial data**: You need product specifications, certification requirements, minimum order quantities, and compliance standards next to contact details and deal values, linked to the right accounts.

- **ERP integration gaps**: Your ERP handles orders and production, but it was not built for relationship management. Bridging ERP data and sales activity needs a CRM with open [CRM integration](/features/crm-integration) interfaces.

## How Customermates helps manufacturing companies

Customermates adapts to the multi-layered relationships and extended sales processes of manufacturing, giving your sales team structure without the weight of a large enterprise CRM. It does not create quotes, orders, or invoices; those stay in your ERP.

**Organizations and multi-contact management**

Link contacts to organizations and use a link field to record which plant belongs to which parent company. Use [contact management](/features/contact-management) to assign multiple contacts per organization, such as procurement, engineering, quality, and management, and see the activity history across stakeholders.

**Pipeline management for long sales cycles**

Map your sales process on a visual [pipeline](/features/pipeline), from initial inquiry through technical review, quote sent, sample approval, and purchase order. The deal stage is one configurable column, and each stage can carry a probability for weighted deal values and pipeline totals. To separate new business from reorders and distributor deals, add a single-select field such as "Deal type" and save a view or group the board by it. Learn more about structuring your [sales pipeline](/blog/sales-pipeline).

**Distributor tracking**

Manage your distributor network alongside direct accounts. Record territory assignments, key contacts, and your own performance ratings at each distributor with custom fields on organization records, and group deals by distributor to see where the pipeline comes from.

**Automation through open interfaces**

Customermates has no built-in workflow builder, no reminders, and no email sequences. Automation runs through interfaces you control, described under [workflow automation](/features/workflow-automation). With the REST [API](/features/api) and webhooks, and n8n operated separately through the Customermates community node, you can sync customers and orders with your ERP or create a [follow-up](/features/follow-up) task when a reorder date recorded in a custom field comes up. On Customermates Cloud, routines let the Mate assistant run a saved prompt on a schedule, for example listing accounts whose expected reorder date is next month and drafting a check-in for each. Starter includes [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] routine per user and Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]].

**Unified inbox for deal context**

On Pro and above, the [unified inbox](/features/email-integration) brings email ([Outlook](/features/outlook-integration), Gmail, IMAP), LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Telegram into Customermates. You link each conversation to the customer, distributor, or partner, so the team sees the conversation history next to the deal without searching separate inboxes. SMS and phone calls are not channels, and the inbox is not available self-hosted.

## Feature highlights for manufacturing

### Pipeline management with kanban view

Visualize your sales pipeline on a kanban board. Track deals from initial inquiry to purchase order and see which opportunities need attention and which quotes are waiting for a response. Custom [pipeline](/features/pipeline) stages match your actual quoting and approval process.

### Organizations with multi-contact support

Assign multiple contacts to each organization, such as procurement specialists, engineers, quality managers, plant managers, and executive decision-makers. See the activity history across stakeholders, so every team member has context for every conversation. See how [account management](/features/account-management) works in Customermates.

### Custom fields for manufacturing-specific data

Add typed fields that reflect your business: product lines, required certifications, annual volume commitments, contract terms, preferred shipping methods, minimum order quantities, lead times, and expected reorder date. Track this data directly on deals, contacts, and organizations.

### API and webhooks for ERP integration

Connect Customermates with your ERP, order management, or production planning system through the REST [API](/features/api) and webhooks. There is no native SAP or Dynamics connector; you or your integrator build the connection, for example with a separately operated n8n workflow. Learn more about [CRM and ERP integration](/blog/crm-and-erp).

### AI agents that operate your manufacturing CRM

External AI clients such as Claude or ChatGPT connect through [MCP](/docs/mcp) with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools, so an AI agent can read and write your CRM directly and do the data entry reps avoid: moving deals through stages, updating distributor records, and logging quote follow-ups. Ask an agent to draft follow-up emails after technical meetings, summarize account history before customer visits, or list accounts without recent activity. MCP access is included in every plan; you choose and pay for the AI client. On Customermates Cloud, the built-in Mate assistant works inside the app with monthly hosted AI credits and asks for confirmation before it sends a message or deletes anything.

### Dashboards and weighted pipeline

Build [dashboards](/features/reporting) that show open deals by stage, weighted pipeline value by product line or distributor, and deals per owner. Weighted values come from the probabilities you set per stage; this is a deterministic calculation, not predictive forecasting.

### Data protection and self-hosting

Customer specifications, pricing agreements, and distributor data deserve careful handling. Every plan has an audit log and roles with permissions per record type and action. In the managed cloud, the application database runs in the AWS Frankfurt region; hosting and other subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [DPA](/dpa) is available. If you need full control over where data lives, [self-host](/features/self-hosted) the open-source core. Read more about our [cloud CRM](/features/cloud-crm) option.

<FaqItem question="Can Customermates integrate with our ERP system?">
Through open interfaces, yes; natively, no. Customermates provides a REST [API](/features/api) and webhooks, and you can connect n8n, which you operate separately, through the Customermates community node (see [workflow automation](/features/workflow-automation)). With these, you or your integrator can connect SAP, Microsoft Dynamics, or another ERP with an API and sync customer records and deal information. Read our guide on [CRM and ERP integration](/blog/crm-and-erp).
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How does Customermates handle complex account structures?">
Customermates uses organizations with multiple contacts and typed custom fields. You can add fields for plant location, region, and parent company (as a link field), and see the activity history across every stakeholder, from plant-level procurement to corporate decision-makers. See our [contact management](/features/contact-management) features.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is Customermates suitable for both direct sales and distributor-based sales models?">
Yes. Customermates has one configurable deal stage column rather than multiple pipelines, so you add a single-select field for the sales channel and save a view or group the board for direct and distributor deals. Distributor organizations get their own records, custom fields, and activity history, giving you visibility across both channels in the same [pipeline](/features/pipeline).
</FaqItem>

It starts at [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]]/user/month, with the inbox on Pro at [[commercial.price.pro.monthly]]/user/month.

## What a healthcare CRM needs to cover

Searchers often use “healthcare CRM” for software that manages patient communication, referrals, appointments, outreach, or care journeys. Some products combine those capabilities with electronic health records or practice management. A general CRM should not imply that it provides them simply because it can store contacts and custom fields.

Customermates is narrower. Use it for the business relationships around care delivery: professional referral networks, partner practices, laboratories as organizations, suppliers, service providers, and cooperation opportunities. The CRM provides the relationship context and a configurable commercial pipeline. It does not make clinical decisions or run a patient workflow.

| Need | Customermates | Healthcare system |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Partner and supplier context | Organizations, contacts, relations, notes, and activity | Contract or procurement record where required |
| Partnership development | Deals, configurable stages, filters, and kanban | Clinical referral processing |
| Next non-clinical action | Tasks linked to supported CRM records | Appointments, care plans, and clinical work queues |
| System connection | REST, webhooks, MCP, and a separate n8n integration option | EHR, practice management, and approved integration engine |

## A practical partner-development workflow

Imagine a clinic developing a relationship with another practice, a professional network, or a service partner. The organization record holds the business, the contacts hold the people involved, and a deal represents the cooperation opportunity. The deal does not represent a patient referral or episode of care.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the organization">
    Add the partner practice, network, supplier, or service provider and keep
    its business context in one place.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the stakeholders">
    Associate the relevant business contacts so the team can see who owns the
    relationship and who participated in prior discussions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shape the relationship pipeline">
    Configure stages such as identified, contacted, meeting planned,
    cooperation under review, active partner, or closed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record the next action">
    Attach a task to the relevant CRM record. Date-driven task creation needs a
    customer-operated scheduler or separate workflow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hand regulated work to the clinical system">
    When the relationship produces a real referral or care process, continue
    it in the system approved to hold and operate that record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to store—and what not to store

Useful CRM fields are non-clinical: partner type, region, relationship stage, internal owner, source, or an external-system reference. Those values help a business-development or partnership team filter its pipeline and prepare the next conversation.

Patient identity, referral reason, diagnosis, treatment, insurance status, appointment details, and care-team coordination belong in the clinical or practice system. Resource permissions in Customermates are not field- or record-level access controls, and Customermates carries no healthcare or GDPR compliance certification.

## Messaging, automation, and AI boundaries

The managed [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) is available from Pro upward for supported email and messaging channels. It can bring individual conversations into the CRM and associate participants with contact context. It is not a patient campaign engine, and a message is not automatically attached to a clinical record or deal. Self-hosted deployments do not include the unified inbox.

An authorized external AI client can use supported [MCP](/docs/mcp) actions on CRM records. The client, model provider, permissions, and confirmation experience remain separate. Protected health information should not enter that path unless the organization has independently approved the complete data flow and legal basis.

For system-to-system work, Customermates exposes the [REST API and webhooks](/features/api). The separate [n8n integration](/features/workflow-automation) is another option. A customer-built connection owns the external provider, mapping, validation, duplicate handling, retries, and compliance; Customermates does not ship a preconfigured EHR or practice-management connector.

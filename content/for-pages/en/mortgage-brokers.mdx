## CRM versus LOS for mortgage brokers

Mortgage software is often marketed under one broad “CRM” label even when it includes origination, document collection, product sourcing, underwriting, disclosures, and lender submission. Those operational capabilities are not ordinary contact and pipeline features.

A mortgage CRM manages the relationship around an enquiry. A loan-origination system manages the application and its regulated evidence. Customermates is the former.

| Need | Customermates | LOS or specialist platform |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enquiry and introducer context | Contacts, organizations, relations, notes, and activity | Authoritative borrower and application file |
| Commercial opportunity | Deals, custom stages, filters, and kanban | Product sourcing, application, and lender submission |
| Next relationship action | Tasks linked to CRM records | Document checklist, appointments, and regulated milestones |
| Conversation context | Managed unified inbox from Pro | Official archive and application correspondence |
| Integration surface | REST, webhooks, MCP, and separate n8n option | LOS, sourcing, document, e-signature, and lender interfaces |

## A supported enquiry pipeline

The pipeline should describe what the brokerage knows about the relationship, not pretend to execute lending. A contact represents the person, organizations represent referral partners or businesses, and the deal represents the potential engagement.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture the enquiry">
    Create the contact and record the referral source without copying identity
    documents, bank statements, credit data, or a complete application.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the introducer">
    Associate the relevant estate agent, accountant, financial advisor, builder,
    or other referral organization where useful.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure relationship stages">
    Use stages such as new enquiry, discovery complete, requirements confirmed,
    options prepared externally, customer deciding, won, or closed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add bounded reference fields">
    Store a high-level finance type, target month, referral source, or external
    LOS identifier rather than reproducing the regulated application.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record and hand off the next action">
    Attach a task for manual follow-up, then continue documents, advice,
    underwriting, and submission in the systems that own them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Referral partners without invented attribution

Estate agents, accountants, advisors, and builders can be represented as contacts and organizations. A referral-source field lets a user filter opportunities by the recorded source. Customermates does not calculate reciprocation, referral value, or partner performance automatically, and it does not send scheduled partner campaigns.

The CRM is useful when a broker needs to answer practical questions: Which enquiries still need a conversation? Which opportunities are waiting on a customer decision? Which introducer is connected to this relationship? Those are CRM questions. Which lender fits, what rate applies, whether affordability passes, and which disclosure is due are mortgage-platform questions.

## Follow-up without a lending automation suite

Tasks hold the next manual action but do not provide native due dates or recurrence. A customer-operated scheduler or separate [n8n workflow](/features/workflow-automation) may create a task or call an approved messaging provider from an authoritative date or status. Customermates has no built-in sequence engine, document-request workflow, rate alert, calendar booking, or application-status campaign.

The managed [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) is available from Pro upward. It brings supported individual conversations into the CRM and allows participants to be associated with contacts. A message is not automatically attached to the correct loan or deal, and the inbox is not the regulated document archive. Self-hosted deployments do not include it.

An external AI client can use supported [MCP](/docs/mcp) actions after authorization. It can operate supported CRM records within user permissions. It does not compare rates, assess affordability, underwrite, recommend a lender, or prove compliance.

## Integrating with the mortgage stack

Keep the LOS, sourcing platform, document system, e-signature service, lender portal, and compliance archive authoritative. If one of those systems exposes a suitable interface, a customer-built connection can exchange a minimal CRM identifier or reviewed status through the [REST API and webhooks](/features/api), MCP, or a separate n8n integration.

The customer owns provider agreements, mapping, permissions, validation, retries, and duplicate handling. No native LOS, lender, sourcing, document, or rate-provider integration is claimed. Customermates also has no CRM record importer; migration requires a separately prepared and validated creation process through supported interfaces.

## Data and deployment fit

Borrower identity, credit information, income, bank statements, property documents, advice, and signed disclosures are sensitive and regulated. Keep them in systems approved for those records. Customermates permissions operate by resource and action, not by field or individual record, and no mortgage or GDPR compliance certification is claimed.

Starter includes the core CRM. Pro adds the managed unified inbox and one connected account per user. Self-hosted deployments use Starter entitlements and do not include the unified inbox. See the [pricing page](/pricing) for current plan details.

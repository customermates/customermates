## Why construction companies need a CRM

A CRM for construction is the difference between a firm that knows its bid pipeline and one that guesses. Construction companies manage many active bids, multi-year client relationships, shifting subcontractor networks, and long project timelines simultaneously. When that information lives in spreadsheets, inboxes, and the heads of individual estimators, things fall through the cracks.

The problems compound as construction firms grow:

- **Bid pipeline blind spots**: Leadership cannot see how many bids are outstanding, their total value, or which ones are closest to a decision.
- **Lost follow-ups on proposals**: An estimator submits a detailed proposal worth six figures. Nobody follows up. The general contractor awards it to a competitor who called back.
- **Subcontractor coordination gaps**: Keeping trade partner specialties, insurance status, past performance, and pricing notes for dozens of subcontractors in spreadsheets does not scale.
- **Scattered project history**: A client calls about a building you worked on three years ago. Without a central system, it takes hours to reconstruct the details.
- **No estimating-to-project handoff**: When a bid is awarded, the context of the sale gets lost on the way to the project team.
- **Change order chaos**: Scope changes during construction get lost between email threads, leading to disputes.

Construction CRM software gives every team member, from estimators to project managers to executives, a single source of truth for relationships, bids, and awarded jobs. Customermates is a CRM, not a construction management suite: it does not schedule crews, manage budgets, or produce invoices.

## How Customermates works as construction CRM software

Customermates adapts to how construction companies operate, from preconstruction through award and into long-term client relationships. You configure the stages and custom fields yourself.

### Bid pipeline management

Every bid becomes a deal in your [pipeline](/features/pipeline). Track each opportunity from initial inquiry through qualification, estimating, proposal submission, negotiation, and award. The Kanban board gives estimators and business development managers a visual overview of the pipeline: drag bids between stages, filter by project type or value, and spot stalled bids early. Stage probabilities produce weighted bid values and weighted pipeline totals.

Customermates does not send follow-up reminders. Keep the submission date in a custom date field and use a saved view of the "Proposal sent" stage sorted by that date, so no bid sits unanswered. On Customermates Cloud, a [routine](/docs/app-routines) can have the Mate assistant list bids without movement every week and draft follow-ups for you to review.

### Project tracking after award

When a bid converts to an active project, it stays in your system. Record key dates, the project phase, and the responsible project manager on the deal with custom fields, and link the project to the owner, architect, general contractor, and key subcontractors, so everyone has context.

Customermates is not project management software. It supports tasks linked to records, but schedules, budgets, and field coordination belong in your [project management](/features/project-management) tool.

### Subcontractor relationship management

Your subcontractor network is one of your most valuable assets. Use [contact management](/features/contact-management) to keep records for every trade partner: specialties, certifications, insurance status and expiry date, past project history, performance notes, and pricing notes. When a new project requires a concrete subcontractor, filter your network by trade and find the right partner quickly.

Link subcontractors to the projects they have worked on. Over time, this helps you build stronger teams for future bids.

### Client communication and history

Notes and activity history are recorded on the client record. On Pro and above, email, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp come together in one [unified inbox](/features/email-integration), and you link each conversation to the client. When a developer you worked with two years ago calls about a new project, the relationship history is at hand.

This matters in construction because relationships span years and often involve repeat business.

### Change order tracking

Scope changes during construction are inevitable. Record change orders as notes on the project deal, or as linked service records with custom fields for description, cost impact, approval status, and responsible party. The activity history shows who changed what and when. Store the signed change order documents in your file storage and keep the link on the record.

### Estimating workflow support

Use custom fields and pipeline stages to mirror your estimating workflow: estimate requests, assigned estimator, bid/no-bid decision, estimate amount, and submission date. For automatic notifications when a bid decision date approaches, send [webhooks](/features/workflow-automation) and REST data to an external tool such as n8n (operated separately), or use a cloud routine.

## Key features of the best CRM for construction

### Pipeline management with Kanban view

Visualize your bid pipeline on a Kanban board. Define stages that match your workflow: Lead, Qualification, Estimating, Proposal Sent, Negotiation, Awarded, Under Contract, Lost. Drag bids between stages as they progress. Filter by project type, estimated value, region, or assigned estimator.

### Custom fields for construction data

Track the data points that matter to your firm: project type (commercial, residential, industrial, infrastructure), estimated contract value, square footage, permit status, project location, bonding requirements, expected start date, and completion date. Typed custom fields live directly on deal and contact records.

### Organization and contact relationships

Construction projects involve multiple stakeholders. Link contacts to organizations and projects, and record each contact's role in a custom field: owner, architect, general contractor, construction manager, subcontractor, supplier. Track which firms you have worked with repeatedly and identify your strongest referral sources.

### Automation with webhooks and n8n

Customermates has no built-in workflow builder. It emits [webhooks](/features/workflow-automation) and exposes a REST API, and n8n (operated separately) connects through the Customermates community node. With these you can:

- Notify the project team when a bid is awarded
- Create a task checklist when a project moves to "Under Contract"
- Flag proposals that have had no update for five days
- Alert leadership when high-value bids reach the negotiation stage

### AI clients that operate your CRM

Connect Claude, ChatGPT, or another AI client through the MCP server ([[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools) and let it do the data entry: log bid activity, move deals between stages, draft proposal cover letters, and summarize lengthy RFP documents. You choose and pay for the AI client.

### Data protection and self-hosting

Bid details, client financials, and subcontractor agreements are sensitive. In the managed cloud, the application database runs in the AWS Frankfurt region; hosting and other subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [DPA](/dpa) is available. If you need full control over where data lives, for example for government contracts, self-host the open-source core.

### Open source and transparent

Customermates is [open source](https://github.com/customermates/customermates). You can inspect the code, contribute improvements, or self-host and choose where your data lives. No vendor lock-in and no forced migrations.

## Construction CRM vs. generic CRM

Generic CRMs like [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/) or [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/) are built for standard B2B sales cycles. Construction works differently: projects involve multiple stakeholders, months-long bid cycles, estimating workflows, and relationships that span years.

Adapting a large CRM to construction often means customization and ongoing maintenance. Customermates is smaller: configurable stages, typed custom fields, and saved views that you set up yourself, with the flexibility of open source.

Compared to construction platforms like Buildertrend or Buildr, which combine client management with project management, scheduling, and financial tools, Customermates focuses on the CRM and relationship side, without bundling project management and accounting features you may already have elsewhere. If you are an individual contractor rather than a construction company, see our [CRM for contractors](/for/contractors) page.

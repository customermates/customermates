A **CRM for cleaning companies** replaces the patchwork of spreadsheets, group chats, and notes that many cleaning businesses rely on. Whether you run a residential maid service, a commercial janitorial operation, or a specialized cleaning company, Customermates gives you one system for clients, properties, estimates, contracts, and follow-ups. It is open source and can be self-hosted.

## Why cleaning companies need a dedicated CRM

Cleaning businesses look simple from the outside, but behind the scenes the complexity is real. You are juggling recurring contracts, one-time deep cleans, multiple client properties, crews, and clients who expect reliable communication. Growth makes everything harder unless you have a system.

Common pain points a cleaning company CRM helps with:

- **Leads slip through the cracks**: A property manager emails about office cleaning, but you are busy on site. By the time you respond, they have already signed with a competitor.
- **Client property details get lost**: The office on Main Street needs the conference room done first. The residential client is allergic to certain products. Without notes tied to the right record, every crew starts blind.
- **Estimates never become contracts**: You send a quote after a walkthrough, but nobody follows up. The prospect goes quiet. A structured estimate-to-contract pipeline shows which quotes need a call.
- **Contract renewals are forgotten**: A 12-month contract expires next week, but nobody noticed. The client assumes you have moved on.
- **No business overview**: How many active contracts? What is their monthly value? Which clients have open complaints? Without a [CRM dashboard](/blog/crm-best-practices), growing the business is guesswork.

Customermates is a CRM, not a cleaning scheduler or field service suite. It does not plan visits, dispatch crews, or send invoices. Many cleaning companies keep a scheduling or invoicing tool next to it.

## How Customermates works as a cleaning business CRM

Customermates gives your cleaning company a central place for every client, property, estimate, contract, and follow-up task, with custom fields and views that match your operations.

### Recurring service contracts

Customermates does not schedule visits or create recurring jobs. What it does well is keep each recurring contract visible: record the contract as a deal linked to the client (with its services attached), with custom fields for frequency (daily, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, quarterly), start date, and renewal date. When a client changes their frequency, the update is in one place and visible to everyone.

For visit reminders or crew notifications, use your scheduling tool, or connect an external workflow tool such as [n8n](/blog/n8n-for-beginners-build-your-first-workflow) (operated separately) through the REST API or webhooks.

### Client property details

Every client record stores contact information, and custom fields and notes hold property addresses, square footage, room count, key holder contacts, cleaning preferences, and product restrictions, along with an activity history. Keep sensitive access details such as alarm codes in a system built for secrets rather than a CRM field.

For cleaning companies managing multiple properties per client, record the client as an organization and each property as a linked deal, so you see the full relationship at a glance.

### Crew assignment

Use a custom single-select field for the assigned crew or set the record owner, then group or filter by crew to see which team covers which properties. Crew leaders can check property instructions and update stages from a phone browser; there is no native app. Customermates does not check availability or plan routes.

### Estimate-to-contract pipeline

Track every opportunity through a visual Kanban pipeline: New Inquiry, Site Walkthrough Scheduled, Walkthrough Complete, Estimate Sent, Follow-Up, Negotiation, Contract Signed, Active. Move deals between columns as they progress and see where each opportunity stands.

Customermates does not create follow-up tasks or reminders by itself. A saved view of the Estimate Sent stage sorted by last update shows which estimates need a call. On Customermates Cloud, a [routine](/docs/app-routines) can have the Mate assistant list unanswered estimates every few days and draft follow-ups for you to review.

### Inspection checklists and quality tracking

Log quality inspections and client feedback as notes and custom fields on property records. Track a satisfaction score field over time and spot patterns before they become complaints. Custom fields can hold the checklist results a crew leader records on site: rooms checked, issues noted.

### Contract renewal tracking

Keep the renewal date in a custom date field and use a saved view sorted by that date, so every contract gets attention before it lapses. There are no automatic renewal tasks; a cloud routine or an external tool through the API can do the checking. A 2014 [Harvard Business Review article](https://hbr.org/2014/10/the-value-of-keeping-the-right-customers), citing research by Frederick Reichheld of Bain & Company, reports that increasing customer retention rates by 5% can increase profits by 25% to 95%. The figure is not specific to cleaning, but it shows why renewals deserve attention.

## Key features for cleaning companies

### Kanban pipeline view

See all opportunities and active contracts on a visual board. Filter by service type (residential, commercial, industrial), contract value, renewal date, or assigned salesperson. Drag and drop to update the stage.

### Custom fields for cleaning operations

Property size, room count, cleaning frequency, required supplies, key holder contacts, inspection scores, certifications needed: configure Customermates to match how your cleaning company operates.

### Follow-ups through views, routines, or your own automation

Customermates has no built-in rules engine. Saved views show unanswered estimates and upcoming renewals. On Customermates Cloud, routines let the Mate assistant check them on a schedule. For fully automated steps, such as acknowledging a new inquiry or alerting the account manager 60 days before renewal, connect [n8n](/blog/n8n-for-beginners-build-your-first-workflow) (operated separately) through webhooks and the REST API.

### Unified inbox for client communication

On Pro and above, client email and WhatsApp come together in one [unified inbox](/features/email-integration), and you link each conversation to the client. Answer schedule questions and send inspection results without leaving the CRM, and let the Mate assistant draft routine replies that you confirm before sending. SMS is not a channel.

### AI clients that operate your CRM

Connect Claude, ChatGPT, or another AI client through the MCP server ([[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools) and let it do the data entry: create client and property records, move estimates through the pipeline, log feedback, draft cleaning proposals from walkthrough notes, and summarize client feedback across properties. You choose and pay for the AI client.

### Mobile access

Crew leaders check property details, update job stages, and log inspection results from a phone browser. There is no native iOS or Android app.

### Data protection and self-hosting

In the managed cloud, the application database runs in the AWS Frankfurt region; hosting and other subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [DPA](/dpa) is available. For companies with stricter data-location requirements, especially those serving [healthcare](/for/healthcare) or government facilities, self-host the open-source core.

## Integrations for cleaning companies

Customermates connects to the tools cleaning companies already use through the REST API, webhooks, and [n8n](https://n8n.io) (operated separately):

- **Email**: Connect Gmail, Outlook, or IMAP mailboxes to the unified inbox on Pro and above
- **Calendar**: Read-only calendar access on Pro and above; Customermates does not create or schedule visits
- **Invoicing and accounting**: Keep Lexware Office, sevDesk, Xero, QuickBooks, or another invoicing tool for billing, and connect it through the API if you want records in sync
- **WhatsApp**: In the unified inbox on Pro; SMS is not supported
- **Web forms**: Customermates has no built-in forms, but an external form tool can create leads through the REST API or n8n
- **AI clients (MCP)**: Draft proposals, summarize feedback, and identify patterns

A SaaS trial often goes quiet instead of ending in a "no": the champion's last LinkedIn message sits unanswered while the team ships, and nobody writes on the day the trial runs out. A CRM for SaaS keeps every account that trials, buys, expands or churns in one place, with the people in it, the deals around it and the conversations behind it. Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM made in Germany for product-led SaaS teams who shape their own lists and connect their own product through a REST API, webhooks and n8n. On Customermates Cloud from Pro, the LinkedIn and email threads sit on the account, and Mate or your own AI client through MCP drafts the reply and the next step for you to review, edit and send. It has no native billing or product-analytics sync.

"SaaS CRM" also means a CRM that is itself delivered as a cloud service, and Customermates is that too, so the first section below separates the two meanings.

## What is a SaaS CRM?

The phrase has two meanings, and search results mix them.

| Meaning | What it describes | Where to read more |
|---|---|---|
| A CRM delivered as SaaS | Any CRM you use in the browser without running servers, as opposed to software you install yourself | [Cloud CRM](/features/cloud-crm) explains cloud versus self-hosted and how to choose |
| A CRM for SaaS companies | A CRM that fits a business that sells subscriptions: accounts with several users, trials, plans, renewals and usage | The rest of this page |

Customermates covers both. It runs as Customermates Cloud, a hosted service with paid plans billed monthly per user and a Free plan for one person, and as a self-hosted installation of the open-source core. What it does not do is understand subscriptions on its own. There is no built-in subscription, trial or billing model, so you define those as fields and lists and decide how product data reaches them.

## What does a SaaS company need from a CRM?

- **Follow-up on every conversation.** A trial user's reply, a champion's LinkedIn message and a question after the demo each need an answer and a dated next step, wherever the conversation happened.
- **Accounts, not just people.** A B2B product is bought by a company and used by many users. The record you open before a call is the account, with its users as linked contacts.
- **Lifecycle on the record.** Trial, paying, at risk and churned, with the dates that matter: trial end, renewal, last activity.
- **Product data next to the relationship.** Plan, seats and a usage number belong beside the notes and deals, not in a separate dashboard that sales never opens.
- **Sales-led and self-serve at once.** Most deals close without a call, while larger accounts need a pipeline and an owner.
- **An API your own engineers can use.** A SaaS team usually prefers a documented API and webhooks to a catalog of ready-made connectors.
- **A way to hand over.** When the first customer success hire arrives, the account history is in the CRM and not in someone's inbox.

## How do you follow up on trials, demos and LinkedIn conversations?

Most SaaS follow-up happens outside the product: a trial user answers the welcome email, the champion replies on LinkedIn after the demo, a prospect asks for a security answer before the review. Where those threads live decides whether the next step happens.

1. **Connect LinkedIn and email.** On Customermates Cloud from Pro, threads appear on the contact whose email address, phone number or LinkedIn profile matches, and WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram work the same way. You or Mate link a thread to the account or deal when it belongs there.
2. **Date the next step.** Add a Next step date to Accounts and Deals. The dashboard's Upcoming widget lists what is due and what is overdue, and a Trials ending view sorts accounts by Trial ends.
3. **Let Mate or your own AI client draft the reply.** Ask for a draft, or set up a [routine](/docs/app-routines) that drafts a check-in when Lifecycle changes to At risk. The draft waits in the conversation until you edit and send it.

The sections below show how to model the accounts and how to connect your product.

## How do you model accounts, trials and subscriptions in Customermates?

A new workspace starts without lists, so everything below is an example you create yourself in **Configure**, or, on Customermates Cloud, describe your business in one sentence and let Mate build the lists, pipeline and dashboard. Every field name is yours to choose.

| List | Used for | Example fields |
|---|---|---|
| Accounts | One record per customer company or trial | Plan (single choice), Lifecycle (single choice: Trial, Paying, At risk, Churned), MRR (money), Seats (number), Trial ends (date), Renewal date (date), Last active (date), Product account ID (text), Owner (member) |
| Contacts | The people in each account, including the admin who signed up | Role (single choice), Contact details (email and LinkedIn), linked to the account |
| Deals | Sales-led new business and expansion | Stage with a win probability, value, expected close date, linked to the account and its contacts |
| Subscriptions (optional) | Accounts that hold several products or plans | Plan, MRR, start and end date, linked to the account |

Accounts and contacts are linked by a relationship, and one contact can belong to several accounts. If you keep Subscriptions as its own list, a calculated field on Accounts can total the MRR of the linked subscriptions, and another calculated field can multiply MRR by 12 for annual recurring revenue ([how calculated fields work](/docs/concepts#how-do-i-build-a-calculated-field)). Without a Subscriptions list, MRR is a plain money field on the account that you or your integration set.

Two things to know. First, MRR here is a number someone or something writes into a field; Customermates does not compute it from invoices. Second, tasks have no built-in due date or status, so a follow-up date is another date field you add.

Useful saved views for a SaaS team:

1. **Trials ending:** accounts with Lifecycle Trial, sorted by Trial ends, earliest first.
2. **Renewals:** paying accounts sorted by Renewal date.
3. **Expansion board:** deals on the [pipeline board](/features/pipeline), one card per deal, linked to its account.
4. **Quiet accounts:** accounts with a Last active date older than 14 days ([filter reference](/docs/filter-syntax)).

A saved view can be shared with everyone in the workspace or with selected roles from its **Share…** menu. Colleagues see the same filters, but only the records their own role allows. To group accounts by plan, size or lifecycle in the first place, the [customer segmentation guide](/blog/customer-segmentation) shows the fields and views.

## How does product data get into the CRM?

Customermates has no native integration with a billing tool or a product-analytics tool, so product data arrives through the API: your backend, a scheduled job or an n8n workflow writes it into the fields above.

| Product event | How it reaches Customermates |
|---|---|
| Someone signs up or starts a trial | Your backend calls `POST /api/v1/records/mutate` to create the account and its contact |
| Plan or MRR changes in your billing tool | Your own webhook handler or an n8n workflow updates the account fields |
| A daily usage number or last-active date | A scheduled job writes it; bulk writes take up to 100 records per request |
| A CRM field changes, such as Lifecycle set to At risk | A signed webhook posts to your endpoint, an n8n workflow or a Slack incoming webhook |
| Someone asks a question about accounts | Your AI client reads them over MCP |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an API key">
    Open **Settings → API keys** and create a key; a role needs **Create** on
    **API & Webhooks** for that, which the Admin role has. The key acts with
    the permissions of the person who owns it, so there are no per-key scopes,
    and on Customermates Cloud that person is a member of your workspace like
    any other. The [API keys guide](/docs/api-keys) covers expiry and rotation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read your lists and fields">
    Call `POST /api/v1/model/discover` and `POST /api/v1/model/read` to get the
    stable IDs of your Accounts list and its fields. They stay the same when
    you rename a field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write on signup">
    Send a create mutation with an idempotency key, so a retried job does not
    write the account twice. Records are addressed by their own ID, so keep
    your product's account ID in a text field and find the record with an
    exact-match query before you write.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update from your billing and usage data">
    Write plan, MRR, seats and last-active from the system that owns them.
    That system stays the source of truth; the CRM holds a copy for the people
    who sell and support.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Subscribe to changes">
    Add a webhook for **Record updated** on the Accounts list, with a secret
    and, if you like, only the Lifecycle field as the trigger. Every delivery
    is signed with HMAC-SHA256, retried up to 10 attempts in total and can
    arrive out of order, so deduplicate by event ID. Webhooks need a paid plan
    on Customermates Cloud and are included when self-hosted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The [REST API page](/features/api) lists every route and the OpenAPI document, and the [webhooks reference](/docs/webhooks) lists the event payloads. If you prefer visual flows to code, the Customermates community node for n8n covers the same record API, and its trigger creates the webhook subscription for you. n8n is a separate product with its own plans and is not included; the [n8n guide](/docs/n8n) and the [workflow automation page](/features/workflow-automation) show the setup.

<AcquisitionCallout title="Your product data does not appear by itself" variant="boundary">
  Customermates reads nothing from your database, billing tool or analytics
  tool. If the integration is not built, the account fields stay as stale as
  the last person who edited them. Plan the first sync as an engineering
  task, and decide which system owns each value.
</AcquisitionCallout>

## What can your own AI client do with account data?

Customermates has an [MCP server](/docs/mcp) with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools on every plan, self-hosted included. Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor or another client, and it reads and updates the records your role allows, using your own AI provider and subscription. The [MCP server explainer](/blog/what-is-an-mcp-server) covers what the protocol is and how clients connect.

For a SaaS team, a typical use is account research before a call: ask the client which accounts have a trial ending this week, and it reads the Trial ends field of your Accounts list and the linked contacts. After the call, ask it to set the Lifecycle and add a note. What the client may change is limited by your role. Any confirmation before deleting or sending is the client's own behavior, not a check in Customermates, so review what you ask it to do.

## What changes on Customermates Cloud?

Some features exist only in Customermates Cloud. The [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings email from Gmail, Outlook or IMAP together with LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram, and the plan decides what you can connect:

- **Free, Starter and self-hosted:** no connected messaging accounts.
- **Pro:** each person's own accounts, [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] connected account per user included, plus threads shared one at a time.
- **Business, Max or Enterprise:** needed to share a whole account, for example a shared sales or success mailbox.

Participants are matched to contacts by email, phone or LinkedIn identity. A conversation is linked to an account only when a person, or an agent you ask, links it.

Mate, the assistant inside Customermates Cloud, summarizes an account, updates records and drafts replies when you ask, within your plan's hosted AI credits. A [routine](/docs/app-routines) can run Mate when a record changes, for example when Lifecycle changes to At risk, to save a check-in draft for you to review. Mate sends a message as soon as you ask it to, so ask for drafts when you want to read first. Mate and routines are not available on Free or when self-hosted.

## Where is Customermates the wrong choice for a SaaS company?

- No native billing or product-analytics integration, so you build or buy the connection.
- No lead scoring, health scoring or enrichment.
- No sequences, drip campaigns or bulk messaging.
- No embeddable web forms: a demo-request form on your site needs your own backend to write to the API.
- No SSO or SAML sign-in, no certification such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2, no native mobile apps.
- There are no field-level permissions, so a role cannot hide one field such as MRR from a colleague who can open the account.

If one of these decides the purchase, look at a tool built for it. Customermates publishes this page, so read the table as pointers, not a ranking. Vendor details were checked on 8 November 2026 on each vendor's own website.

| If you need | Look at | What the vendor's site lists |
|---|---|---|
| Website forms and contact scoring in the CRM | [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/products/crm) | A form builder among the free tools; standard contact scoring on the Professional tier |
| Workflows, sequences and enrichment in the CRM | [Attio](https://attio.com/pricing) | Workflows and Sequences under Automations; enrichment as its own section |
| Many ready-made connections to other business apps | [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/products/crm) | The free CRM connects to over 2,000 business apps, through third-party and HubSpot-built integrations |
| A free hosted start for a founding team | [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/products/crm), [Attio](https://attio.com/pricing) | HubSpot: free for up to 2 users and 1,000 contacts. Attio: Free plan for up to 3 seats |

Product analytics, billing and subscription management, support desks and in-app messaging stay in the tools built for them. Customermates is the place where sales and success see the account, not where those systems run.

## Should a SaaS company self-host its CRM?

Some SaaS teams prefer to run their CRM next to their own infrastructure. Customermates is open core, and a [self-hosted installation](/features/self-hosted) runs with Docker Compose on PostgreSQL 16. It has the Starter feature set with REST, webhooks and MCP, but no inbox, no Mate and no AI workspace setup, and you operate the server, backups and updates. Self-hosting is not a compliance guarantee by itself.

In Customermates Cloud, the primary database is in the AWS Frankfurt region. Subprocessors, US providers among them, are on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [data processing agreement](/dpa) is available. Customermates holds no certification such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2, which matters when a customer's security questionnaire asks about your vendors.

<FaqItem question="Which CRM is best for a SaaS company?">
There is no single answer. If you need website forms, contact scoring or many ready-made integrations, HubSpot or Attio may fit better. If your engineers would rather write product data into a configurable CRM through an API, keep billing and analytics as the source of truth and want MCP for your own AI client, Customermates is built for that. The table above lists what each vendor's site names.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Customermates integrate with Stripe, Lemon Squeezy or my billing tool?">
Not natively. There is no billing integration and no product-analytics integration. A billing tool that can call a webhook, or has an n8n node, can update your account fields through the REST API. You build and operate that connection.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can Customermates track trials, MRR and renewals?">
Yes, as fields and lists you define: a trial end date, a renewal date, MRR as a money field and a lifecycle choice. There is no built-in subscription or billing model, so Customermates does not calculate MRR from invoices; you or your integration write the value.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does it score leads or product usage?">
No. There is no lead scoring, health scoring or enrichment. If you want a score, compute it in your own pipeline, write the number into a field and filter or sort on it.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does the API cost extra?">
No. The REST API and MCP are on every plan and in the self-hosted edition, and webhooks come with every paid plan and the self-hosted edition. A key acts with its owner's permissions. On the Free plan, REST and MCP requests together are limited to [[commercial.limit.free.apiRequestsPerMinute]] per minute and [[commercial.limit.free.apiRequestsPerDay]] per day per workspace.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is Customermates itself a SaaS CRM?">
Yes, in the cloud sense: Customermates Cloud is a hosted service with a Free plan for one person and paid plans billed monthly per user, and the [cloud CRM page](/features/cloud-crm) explains what that means. You can also self-host the open-source core without the inbox and Mate.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

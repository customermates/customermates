## Why realtors need a CRM

A CRM is the backbone of a successful real estate practice. Whether you are a solo agent or part of a team, your income depends on capturing leads fast, nurturing them through long decision cycles, and building a referral network that generates repeat business year after year.

Without a proper realtor CRM, deals slip through the cracks. Open house visitors never get a follow-up. Past clients buy their second home with another agent because you lost touch. A hot lead from Zillow goes cold because you responded two days late.

Past clients often buy their next home with whichever agent stayed in touch. A real estate agent CRM makes staying in touch a habit instead of a memory test.

Here are the challenges the best CRM for realtors solves:

- **Slow lead response:** A new inquiry comes in from your website, Zillow, or an open house sign-in sheet. If you do not respond quickly, the prospect moves to another agent.
- **No long-term nurturing:** A buyer is not ready for 6 to 12 months. Without a list of who to call when, you forget about them until they close with someone else.
- **Open house follow-up gaps:** You collect 15 names at an open house on Saturday. By Monday, you have followed up with three. The rest are lost.
- **Referral network neglect:** Past clients, lenders, inspectors, and attorneys are your best lead sources, but maintaining hundreds of relationships requires structure.
- **Scattered showing notes:** Property feedback from showings lives in texts, emails, and sticky notes. Nothing is linked to the buyer profile.
- **Pipeline confusion:** You are managing buyers, sellers, and rental clients simultaneously with no clear view of where each deal stands.
- **Commission tracking gaps:** Pending commissions, split agreements, and closing dates are tracked in spreadsheets that go stale.

Customermates gives realtors the [pipeline visibility](/features/pipeline) and [contact management](/features/contact-management) to follow up on more leads, keep relationships warm, and build a referral network over time.

## How Customermates works as your realtor CRM

Customermates handles both sides of real estate, active transactions and long-term relationships. You configure the fields, the deal stages and the saved views to match how you work.

### Buyer pipeline management

Track every buyer from first inquiry through closing on a visual Kanban board. The deal stage is one configurable column, so define stages that match your actual workflow:

- New lead received
- Initial consultation
- Pre-approval confirmed
- Active showings
- Offer submitted
- Under contract
- Inspection and appraisal
- Clear to close
- Closed, keys handed over

Drag buyers between stages as deals progress. See at a glance how many active buyers you have, where bottlenecks are forming, and which deals need attention today. Learn more about [pipeline management](/features/pipeline).

### Seller pipeline management

For your listing side, add a single-select "Side" field (buyer, seller, rental) to each deal and save a view per side, grouped by stage. Seller stages can sit in the same stage column:

- Listing appointment
- CMA presented
- Listing agreement signed
- Pre-market preparation
- Active on MLS
- Showings and feedback
- Offer received and negotiation
- Under contract
- Closing

Track days on market, showing feedback, and price adjustments in custom fields and notes on each deal. Know which listings need a strategy change before it is too late.

### Lead capture and speed-to-lead

Realtors generate leads from open houses, web forms, social media, yard signs, and referrals. Customermates has no built-in web forms or lead routing: you add leads manually, import them from an Excel workbook (XLSX), or create them through the [REST API](/features/api) from your own integration or a separately operated n8n instance ([workflow automation](/features/workflow-automation)).

Use custom fields to qualify leads immediately:

- Buyer or seller
- Timeline (ready now, 3-6 months, 6-12 months)
- Budget range or expected list price
- Preferred neighborhoods
- Pre-approval status
- Lead source (open house, website, referral, Zillow)
- Lead temperature (hot, warm, cold)

If your lead source can call a webhook or n8n, your own workflow can send an instant response from your mail system and create the contact in Customermates at the same time.

### Showing feedback tracking

Every showing generates valuable data. Record showing details, buyer feedback, and agent notes directly on the buyer's deal or contact:

- Property address and MLS number
- Showing date
- Buyer reaction (loved it, liked it, not interested)
- Specific feedback (too small, needs renovation, wrong neighborhood)
- Next step (second showing, submit offer, keep looking)

When you review a buyer's profile before your next meeting, you have the history of the properties they have seen and their reaction to each one. Customermates does not book showings; keep your calendar for that, and on Pro you can see it read-only next to the CRM.

### Referral network management

Your sphere of influence is your most valuable asset. Customermates helps you work every relationship systematically using [contact management](/features/contact-management) with custom columns and saved filter views:

- **Past clients:** Track purchase date, property address, home anniversary, and family details in custom fields, and keep a view sorted by the next anniversary.
- **Referral partners:** Lenders, inspectors, attorneys, title companies, contractors. Note referrals sent and received on each partner to see your most valuable relationships.
- **Sphere of influence:** Friends, family, neighbors, community contacts. Classify them with a single-select "Relationship" field and a "Next check-in" date.
- **Future prospects:** People who attended open houses, requested market reports, or expressed interest but are not ready yet.

Filter your network by next check-in date or relationship type to see who deserves attention this week.

### Open house follow-up

Open houses are one of the highest-value lead sources for realtors, but only if you follow up. Customermates has no built-in email sequences or reminders. A practical setup:

1. **Day 0:** Enter or import the sign-in sheet as contacts with the lead source "Open house" and a "Next check-in" date.
2. **Day 1:** Work a saved view of new open house leads and call or message each one.
3. **Day 3 onwards:** Update the next check-in date after each touchpoint so the view always shows who is due.

On Customermates Cloud, a [routine](/docs/app-routines) can have the Mate assistant list open house leads whose check-in date has passed every morning and draft a follow-up for each, which it sends only after your confirmation. If you want timed emails, build them in your own n8n instance or email tool using webhooks and the REST API ([workflow automation](/features/workflow-automation)).

### Market updates

Position yourself as the local market expert. Group contacts by a "Neighborhood interest" field to see who cares about which area. Customermates does not send newsletters or mass emails; use your email marketing tool for the send and keep the CRM as the source of who is interested in what. Topics that work well:

- Recent sales and price trends
- New listings matching their criteria
- Days on market averages
- Inventory levels

Your connected AI assistant can draft personalized market commentaries based on each contact's interests, saving writing time while keeping the communication personal.

## Key features for realtors

### Visual pipeline with Kanban board

Manage your entire deal flow visually. Drag and drop deals between stages, see weighted commission values based on the stage probabilities you set, and prioritize your daily activities based on pipeline status. Explore [pipeline management](/features/pipeline).

### Contact management with rich profiles

Build comprehensive profiles for every person in your database with [contact management](/features/contact-management). Track property preferences, transaction history, referral source, family details, communication preferences, and key dates like birthdays and home anniversaries.

### Email integration

On Pro and above, connect your mailbox (Gmail, Outlook or IMAP) to the unified inbox with [email integration](/features/email-integration) and link each conversation to the client record. When a past client calls, you have their history, including the property they purchased and the conversations since.

### Automation with n8n, webhooks and REST

Customermates has no built-in workflow builder. It sends webhooks when records change and exposes a REST API, and a separately operated n8n instance connects through the Customermates community node. With your own workflows you can build, for example:

- **Lead responses:** an instant reply from your mail system when a new lead is created
- **Home anniversary emails:** sent from your email tool based on the purchase date field
- **Listing status updates:** a message to sellers when a deal changes stage
- **Closing checklists:** tasks created for inspection, appraisal, and closing milestones

Explore [workflow automation](/features/workflow-automation) to see what is possible.

### AI agents that do your data entry

Connect AI clients such as Claude or ChatGPT to Customermates through its MCP server ([[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools); you choose and pay for the AI client. The agent keeps records current for you and works the routine:

- **Record showing notes and update contacts** straight from a conversation, so the pipeline stays accurate without manual entry
- **Draft listing descriptions** from property details
- **Personalize buyer outreach emails** based on search criteria and showing history
- **Summarize client history** before a meeting or call
- **Move deals between stages** as the conversation progresses

### Custom fields for real estate data

Track everything that matters: property addresses, price ranges, bedroom and bathroom counts, square footage, neighborhoods, lot size, closing dates, commission amounts, and referral sources. Filter your database by any criteria to find the right contacts instantly.

### Dashboards and reporting

Build [dashboards](/features/reporting) from your own records:

- Deals closed per month and quarter
- Deals and commission by lead source
- Commission income tracking
- Pipeline value and weighted pipeline value
- Referrals per partner

Customermates does not calculate pipeline velocity or predictive forecasts.

### Data protection and self-hosting

Client financial information, property preferences, mortgage details, and personal data deserve serious protection. In the managed cloud, the application database runs in the AWS Frankfurt region; hosting and other subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [DPA](/dpa) is available. If you need full control over where data lives, self-host the open-source core.

## Getting started

Setting up Customermates as your real estate agent CRM takes minutes, not days:

1. **Create your account:** No credit card required. The first [[commercial.trial.days]] days are free.
2. **Import your records:** from an Excel workbook (XLSX), one record type at a time, or through the REST API or an AI agent connected over MCP. There is no CSV import.
3. **Set up your pipeline:** configure stages and a "Side" field for buyers and sellers.
4. **Add custom fields:** Define fields for property preferences, budget, timeline, neighborhoods, referral source and next check-in date.
5. **Connect your email (Pro):** Link Gmail, Outlook or IMAP and assign client conversations to contacts.
6. **Connect automation if you need it:** Use webhooks, REST or your own n8n instance for lead responses and anniversary emails, or a cloud routine for a daily follow-up list.

The Customermates core is open source, and you can export your records to XLSX at any time, one record type at a time.

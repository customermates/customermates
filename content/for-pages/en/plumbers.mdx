## Why plumbers need a CRM

A **CRM for plumbers** records every enquiry, estimate and customer conversation in one place, so work is won on follow-up rather than lost to a busy week. Plumbing businesses live on two kinds of work: urgent call-outs that arrive by phone and message at any hour, and planned jobs such as bathroom refits, boiler swaps and repiping that need an estimate and a follow-up before anyone says yes.

Both leak revenue in the same places:

- **Estimates are sent and forgotten.** The biggest jobs need a quote, and a quote without a follow-up is often a job for someone else.
- **The customer's history is missing.** When a landlord calls about a leak, nobody in the office knows what was replaced at that address two years ago.
- **Messages arrive everywhere.** Photos of a dripping valve on WhatsApp, a refit brief by email, a question on Instagram.
- **Repeat work is invisible.** Annual boiler services and landlord safety checks are recurring revenue only if someone knows who is due.

Customermates is a focused plumbing CRM for these problems. It is not dispatch software: it does not schedule engineers, plan routes or send invoices. It keeps the commercial side of your business in order next to the tools you already use for that.

## How a plumbing business runs on Customermates

### One pipeline for call-outs and planned jobs

Deals are your jobs, and the stage is a field you configure. Many plumbing businesses use two views of the same pipeline:

- **Call-outs:** Logged → Attended → Completed → Paid
- **Planned work:** Enquiry → Survey → Estimate sent → Accepted → Booked → Completed

The [kanban board](/features/pipeline) shows each job as a card; grouping by owner shows each engineer's jobs, and grouping by a job type field separates emergencies from refits. Stage probabilities turn open estimates into a weighted pipeline value, calculated from your own weights.

### Property and customer history

Organizations and contacts hold landlords, letting agents, property managers and homeowners. Link each job to the customer and, for landlords and agents, to the right organization, so the history of every property is one click away. See [contact management](/features/contact-management).

### Fields for plumbing work

Custom columns let you record what matters to your trade: job type (emergency, repair, installation, refit, service), property type, boiler make and model, last service date, next service due, access notes, and a link to the photo folder in your file storage. Records do not store file attachments, so keep the photos where they are and store the link.

### Following up on estimates

A saved view of jobs in **Estimate sent**, sorted by last update, is your daily follow-up list. On Customermates Cloud, a [routine](/docs/app-routines) can prepare it for you: each morning the Mate assistant lists estimates older than five days and drafts a short follow-up message for each, which you review before anything is sent. Starter includes [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] routine per user and Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]].

### Annual services and landlord checks

Put a **Next service due** date on the customer or the job, and a view filtered to the next 30 days shows who to contact. That turns annual boiler services and landlord safety checks into planned work instead of a memory exercise.

### WhatsApp, email and Instagram in one inbox

On Pro and above, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings WhatsApp, email, Instagram, LinkedIn and Telegram into the CRM. Link a conversation to the customer once and every later message shows up on their record; reply from the office on the channel the customer used. Phone calls and SMS are not channels.

### AI for the paperwork

External AI clients such as Claude or ChatGPT connect through [MCP](/docs/mcp) and can create jobs from a description, add survey notes to the right record, or summarize a customer's history before a visit. Customermates registers [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] MCP tools, and you choose and pay for the AI client. On Customermates Cloud, the built-in Mate assistant does the same work inside the app.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="What is the best CRM for plumbers?">
The best CRM for plumbers depends on where jobs get lost. If scheduling and dispatch are the bottleneck, field service software fits. If estimates go unanswered and customer messages are scattered, a focused CRM such as Customermates gives you a job pipeline, property history and one inbox from [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per user per month.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can Customermates schedule and dispatch plumbers?">
No. Customermates shows which jobs are waiting to be booked and who owns them, but it has no dispatch board or route planning. Use your scheduling tool for that and keep the customer and estimate side in the CRM.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I track annual boiler services?">
Yes. Add a next-service date to the customer or job and save a view filtered to the coming weeks. That view is your list of customers to contact.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does it handle WhatsApp messages from customers?">
Yes, on Customermates Cloud from Pro. WhatsApp, email, Instagram, LinkedIn and Telegram conversations appear in one inbox, linked to the customer, and you reply on the original channel.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can it create estimates or invoices?">
No. Customermates records the job, its value and its stage, but does not produce estimate or invoice documents. Keep your invoicing tool and connect it through the REST API, webhooks or n8n if you want the data to match.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Customermates work on a phone?">
Yes, in the mobile browser. There is no native iOS or Android app.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

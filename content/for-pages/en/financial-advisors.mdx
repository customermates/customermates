Customermates fits a financial advisor or mortgage broker who needs a clear prospect pipeline, a record of who referred whom and a list of which clients are due a review. It does not fit a firm that wants its CRM to pull custodian feeds, hold the compliance archive or run the advice process. For that, an advisor-specific system such as Redtail or Wealthbox, or the platform of your network or pool, is the better choice. This page shows the setup for the first case and names the tools for the second.

Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM built in Germany for small client-facing teams. It keeps contacts, organizations and deals together and, from the Pro plan, the email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp conversations around them.

## What an advisory practice needs from a CRM

"CRM for financial advisors" covers two jobs that are easy to blur. One is relationship work: prospects, introducers, conversations and the next step. The other is regulated operations: holdings, portfolio values, risk profiles, suitability documentation, signed forms and the record of every client communication. Advisor-specific CRMs bundle both. A general CRM like Customermates does the first job only.

On the relationship side, advisory work has a few habits a generic sales CRM handles badly:

- **The client is often a household.** Two partners, sometimes adult children or a family business, and one review meeting for all of them.
- **Reviews follow a calendar, not a pipeline.** Existing clients need an annual or semi-annual review, and missing one costs more than a slow prospect.
- **New business comes through introducers.** Accountants, lawyers, estate agents and other advisors send clients, and you want to know who sent whom.
- **Communication is regulated.** Which channel you may use with a client is a compliance question before it is a software question.

## A setup for advisors in Customermates

Customermates has five record types. The mapping below is one sensible way to use them; every field name is an example you create yourself.

| Record type | Used for | Example custom fields |
|---|---|---|
| Contacts | Each client or prospect | Client status (single select: Prospect, Client, Former client), Next review (date), Preferred channel (single select), Referred by |
| Organizations | Households, client businesses and referral partners | Type (single select: Household, Business, Referral partner), Partner profession |
| Deals | Each prospective engagement or mortgage enquiry | Stage, Engagement type (single select: Planning, Investment advice, Mortgage, Insurance), Decision expected (date), External reference |
| Services | What you offer, with a price | Financial plan, Annual retainer, Mortgage advice fee |
| Tasks | Next steps assigned to a person | A "Due on" date field you add, since tasks have no built-in due date |

A contact can belong to several organizations, so one client can sit in a household and be linked to the accountant who introduced them. Under **My Company › Settings** you can rename record types from a preset list, for example Contacts to Clients, Deals to Opportunities and Tasks to Follow-ups.

### Stages for new business

The deal stage is a single-select field you define, and each option carries a win probability. A starting point: Introduction 10%, Discovery meeting 25%, Proposal discussed 50%, Paperwork in specialist system 80%, Won 100%, Not proceeding 0%. The deal's value comes from linked services, so a pipeline total is arithmetic from your own fee and probabilities, not a prediction. The [pipeline board](/features/pipeline) shows each deal as a card you drag between stages.

Keep the stages commercial. "Paperwork in specialist system" means the advice, application or onboarding now runs elsewhere, and the CRM only records that it happened.

### Saved views for the review cycle

1. **Reviews coming up:** contacts with Client status Client, sorted by Next review, earliest first.
2. **Prospects waiting:** deals in Discovery meeting or Proposal discussed, sorted by Decision expected.
3. **Stalled:** deals whose last update is older than 30 days, using the **Older than…** date filter.
4. **By introducer:** deals filtered to those linked to a referral partner organization, to see where new business comes from.

Custom date fields filter by fixed dates, so a "next 30 days" list is a sort on Next review rather than a moving window. Saved views are personal, so each colleague sets the same filters and saves their own view.

### Client conversations in one inbox (Pro and above)

In Customermates Cloud on Pro, Business and Enterprise, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings email from Gmail, Outlook or IMAP together with LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram, and you reply on the original channel. Link a participant to a contact once, and that person's conversations show on the client's timeline. Conversations from your own accounts stay private until you share a thread, or share the whole account on Business and above.

Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] connected account per user. An advisor who wants a mailbox and WhatsApp in the inbox needs Business, which includes [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedAccountsPerUser]].

<AcquisitionCallout title="The inbox is not your books-and-records archive" variant="boundary">
  US securities rules treat business messages as records. On 14 August 2024 the
  [SEC charged 26 broker-dealers and investment advisers](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2024-98)
  for failing to preserve "off-channel" communications. Customermates does not
  record, retain or supervise messages for regulatory purposes. Connect only
  channels your firm approves and archives elsewhere, and ask your compliance
  officer before you link WhatsApp.
</AcquisitionCallout>

### A weekly review list with Mate (Customermates Cloud)

Mate is the assistant inside Customermates Cloud and runs on the monthly hosted AI credits each cloud plan includes per active user. A [routine](/docs/app-routines) is a saved instruction Mate runs on a schedule or when a record changes. Choose **On a schedule**, **Every week** under **Repeats**, Monday at 08:00, with instructions like these:

```text
List contacts with Client status "Client" whose Next review date falls in
the next 30 days and who have no linked task named "Book review". For each,
create a task "Book review" linked to the contact and assigned to me.
Do not send any messages. End with a list: client, household, next review
date, preferred channel.
```

Mate sends messages without asking first when told to, so a routine that should only prepare work has to say so. Unattended runs refuse anything that needs approval, such as deletions. Starter includes [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] routine per user and Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]].

If you would rather use your own AI client, Claude, ChatGPT and others connect through [MCP](/docs/mcp) on every plan and on self-hosted installations. Your AI provider bills the model use. Mate and MCP clients work with CRM records within your permissions; neither gives investment advice or checks suitability.

## Mortgage brokers and finance brokers

The same setup works for a mortgage practice, with one change in vocabulary. A deal is the enquiry, not the loan application. Stages might run New enquiry, Requirements confirmed, Options prepared externally, Client deciding, Won and Closed. Estate agents, builders and accountants are referral-partner organizations.

Applications, credit checks, product sourcing, bank submission and the borrower's documents belong in your loan-origination system or, in Germany, a platform such as [Europace](https://www.europace.de/), which lists more than 800 product providers and an application workflow with a document file. Customermates has no native connection to either. If the platform has an API, a workflow you build with the [REST API and webhooks](/features/api) can pass a reference number or status between them.

## What Customermates does not do for advisors

- No custodian, portfolio, planning or loan-origination connector, and no calculated assets under management. An AUM figure in a custom field is only what someone typed.
- No suitability or advice documentation, no e-signature and no communication archive.
- Tasks have no due dates, reminders or notifications. Use a date field, a sorted view or a routine.
- No email sequences or bulk mailing. Client messages are written one conversation at a time.
- No file storage on records; keep documents in your document system and its link in a field.
- No mobile app and no calendar booking.

## Moving your client list in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export from your current tool as Excel">
    Customermates imports Excel workbooks (.xlsx), one record type at a time.
    Leave portfolio data, risk profiles and documents out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import organizations first">
    Households and referral partners, with the Type field filled in. On the

    compliance has approved it.
  </Step>

## Client data

- In Customermates Cloud the primary production database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region. Hosting and other subprocessors, US providers among them, are on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [data processing agreement](/dpa) is available.
- Permissions apply per resource and action, not per field. A role with **Assigned** read access limits a paraplanner or associate to the records assigned to them.
- The audit log records who changed which record, on every plan.
- Customermates holds no certification such as SOC 2 or ISO 27001. If your broker-dealer, network or regulator requires one for a CRM, Customermates is not an option for you.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="What software do financial advisors usually use?">
An advisory practice usually runs several systems: a CRM, financial-planning software, a portfolio or custodian platform and an archive for communications. Advisor-specific CRMs such as Redtail and Wealthbox connect to that stack. Customermates covers the CRM part for practices that want a simpler relationship tool and keep the rest in specialist systems.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What is the best CRM for finance brokers?">
It depends on how much of the regulated process you want in one tool. If applications, product sourcing and lender submission should live with the client record, use your loan-origination platform's CRM. If you need a separate pipeline for enquiries and referral partners, a general CRM such as Customermates works beside it. Customermates is our own product, so weigh that.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I track households and assets under management?">
Model a household as an organization and link each member as a contact. An AUM figure can sit in a currency field, but Customermates does not calculate, sync or verify it, so the authoritative value stays in the portfolio or custody system.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I message clients on WhatsApp through Customermates?">
Technically yes, from the Pro plan in Customermates Cloud. Whether you may is a compliance question: regulators expect business communications to be preserved, and Customermates is not a recordkeeping archive. Use only channels your firm approves and captures elsewhere.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How much does a CRM for a small advisory practice cost?">
On Customermates, a solo advisor pays [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per month on Starter, or [[commercial.price.pro.monthly]] on Pro with one mailbox in the inbox. Three users on Pro come to [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.3]] per month, billed monthly.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

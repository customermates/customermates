## What financial-advisor CRM buyers usually need

Advisor CRM searches often combine two different needs. Relationship management covers prospective clients, existing relationships, introducers, conversations, and next actions. Wealth-management operations cover holdings, portfolio values, custody, financial plans, suitability, regulated documents, and official recordkeeping.

Customermates is a general relationship CRM. It can organize the first group without claiming to provide the second. That distinction matters when comparing it with advisor-specific platforms that include custodian feeds, household models, compliance archives, or financial-planning integrations.

| Need | Customermates | Specialist financial system |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prospect and client context | Contacts, organizations, relations, notes, and activity | Regulated client file |
| New-business workflow | Deals, configurable stages, filters, and kanban | Advice and onboarding controls |
| Relationship attributes | User-defined fields and views | Calculated portfolio and risk data |
| Next action | Tasks linked to CRM records | Booking, review calendar, and supervised workflow |
| Integration surface | REST, webhooks, MCP, and separate n8n option | Custodian, planning, document, and compliance connectors |

## A practical prospect-to-client workflow

Represent a prospective engagement as a deal, not as a portfolio or account. The opportunity can connect the individual, their organization where relevant, the introducer, and the services under discussion. The stages should reflect commercial progress rather than regulated advice status.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture the relationship context">
    Create the contact, associate the relevant organization, and record the
    referral source or introducer without importing a full regulated client
    file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the opportunity stages">
    Use stages such as new inquiry, discovery planned, evaluating fit, scope
    discussed, won, or closed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add bounded business fields">
    Store values such as relationship tier, preferred contact method, referral
    source, review month, or an external-system reference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the next action with the record">
    Attach a task for the next manual follow-up. Tasks do not provide native due
    dates, recurring schedules, or a supervised review workflow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hand regulated work to the specialist system">
    Once advice, onboarding, custody, or documentation begins, continue that
    process in the platform that owns it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reviews, AUM references, and households

A review month or relationship status can be stored as a custom value and used in filtered views. A customer-operated scheduler or separate [n8n workflow](/features/workflow-automation) can create a task from an approved date source. Customermates has no native scheduler, reminder sequence, or automated review campaign.

If a team keeps an AUM reference in a custom field, it remains manually supplied context. Customermates does not calculate, synchronize, reconcile, or verify assets under management. Do not use that field as a portfolio ledger, performance report, or billing authority.

Contacts and organizations can represent related people and entities, but there is no advisor-specific household object, trust administration, beneficiary workflow, or automatic relationship inference. Build only the associations needed for the CRM conversation and keep the complete financial structure in the authoritative platform.

## Referral partners and individual conversations

Accountants, attorneys, mortgage brokers, and other introducers can be represented as contacts and organizations. A referral-source field and configurable views help a user review where opportunities came from. The CRM does not run referral-request campaigns or calculate attribution automatically.

The managed [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) is available from Pro upward and brings supported individual conversations into the CRM. Participants can be associated with contact context; messages are not automatically attached to advice records or deals. There is no bulk outreach or sequence engine, and self-hosted deployments do not include the inbox.

An external AI client can use supported [MCP](/docs/mcp) tools after authorization. It can operate supported CRM records within the requesting user's permissions. It does not become a financial planner, portfolio analyst, suitability engine, or compliance reviewer.

## Data, deployment, and integration boundaries

The managed Customermates database runs in an EU region. That fact is not a GDPR or financial-services certification. Application hosting, connected channels, external AI clients, and automation providers have their own boundaries. Resource permissions are not field- or record-level controls.

Customer-built integrations can use the [REST API and webhooks](/features/api), MCP, or the separate n8n community integration. The customer owns provider contracts, field mapping, validation, retries, duplicate handling, and compliance. No native custodian, portfolio, planning, or regulatory connector is claimed.

Starter includes the core CRM. Pro adds the managed unified inbox and one connected account per user. Self-hosted deployments use Starter entitlements and do not include the unified inbox. See the [pricing page](/pricing) for current plan details.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is Customermates portfolio-management software?">
No. It does not calculate performance, synchronize holdings, reconcile accounts, custody assets, or create financial plans. It is a configurable CRM for relationships and commercial opportunities.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can a financial advisor track AUM in Customermates?">
A manually maintained AUM reference can be stored in a custom field if the firm's data policy allows it. Customermates does not calculate, verify, or synchronize that value, so the authoritative amount remains in the portfolio or custody system.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Customermates provide compliance recordkeeping?">
No. Keep regulated advice, suitability evidence, signed documents, supervisory records, and the official communication archive in approved systems. Customermates carries no financial-services compliance certification.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Customermates integrate with custodians or planning tools?">
No native connector is claimed. A customer may build a bounded connection through REST, webhooks, MCP, or a separately operated n8n workflow when the other system exposes a suitable interface.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

Accounting firms looking for a CRM often need two different things. Practice management software runs the work: deadlines, time, billing, the client portal. A CRM runs the relationship: who the client is, which engagements are open, what you are waiting for and what was said. Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM built in Germany that covers the second job for small firms. If you want one system for both, an accounting practice management suite fits better, and the table further down names some.

## What an accounting firm needs from a CRM

- **The client is often a household or a company with several people.** The owner signs, the office manager sends the receipts, the spouse files jointly. They belong on one record.
- **Engagements repeat.** Monthly bookkeeping, quarterly payroll returns, the annual return. Each cycle is a separate piece of work with its own status.
- **The bottleneck is missing documents.** In busy season, much of the chasing is "we still need your 1099s" or "the bank statements for March are missing".
- **Prospects come from referrals.** A client mentions you to a colleague, and the first email sits in someone's inbox until it is forgotten.
- **Client data is confidential.** Staff should see the clients they work on, and you need to know who changed what.

## An accounting setup in Customermates

Customermates has five record types. Here is one way to map them; every field name is an example. You can rename record types under **My Company › Settings** from a preset list, for example Deals to Jobs or Projects.

| Record type | Used for | Example custom fields |
|---|---|---|
| Organizations | Client businesses and households | Entity type (Single Select: Individual, Sole proprietor, Partnership, S corp, C corp, Nonprofit), Fiscal year end (Date) |
| Contacts | People at the client | Role (Single Select: Owner, Bookkeeper, Spouse, Referral source), Referred by |
| Deals | Each engagement and each cycle, for example "Miller LLC: 2026 return" | Stage, Engagement type, Documents (Single Select: Complete, Partial, Missing), Documents requested on, Filing due (Date), Engagement letter (Link) |
| Services | What you sell, each with a price | Monthly bookkeeping, Payroll, Individual return, Business return, Advisory hour |
| Tasks | Next steps for a person | A "Due on" date field you add, because tasks have no built-in due date |

### Stages for engagements

The deal stage is one Single Select field you define, and each option can carry a probability. Because there is one stage field, use the Engagement type field to tell onboarding from recurring work. One stage list that works for both:

Inquiry · Proposal sent · Engagement letter signed · Documents requested · Documents received · In preparation · In review · Filed · Closed

A deal's value comes from the services linked to it, price times quantity. Weighted totals are plain arithmetic from your own probabilities. On the [pipeline board](/features/pipeline) each engagement is a card you drag between stages, and you can group the board or table by assigned person to see each preparer's load.

### Saved views for the week

1. **Missing documents:** deals in Documents requested with Documents set to Partial or Missing, sorted by Documents requested on, oldest first.
2. **Filing soon:** deals not in Filed or Closed, sorted by Filing due. Custom date fields accept Before, After and Between filters, not "next 30 days", so sort by the date or set a Between range.
3. **Letters not back:** deals in Proposal sent, sorted by last update.
4. **My engagements:** filtered to you under Assigned.

Saved views are personal, so each colleague sets the same filters and saves their own view.

### Client conversations in one inbox (Pro and above)

In Customermates Cloud on Pro, Business and Enterprise, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings email from Gmail, Outlook or IMAP together with LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram. Link a sender to a contact once, and later conversations with that address appear on the client's timeline. Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] connected account per user, Business [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedAccountsPerUser]]. Conversations from your own accounts stay private until you share a thread, or share the whole account on Business and above. Files that clients send stay in the inbox thread.

### A weekly document chaser with Mate (Customermates Cloud)

Mate is the assistant in Customermates Cloud and uses hosted AI credits; reading the inbox needs Pro or higher. Set up a [routine](/docs/app-routines) with **Repeats** set to **Every week**, Monday at 08:00, and instructions like these:

```text
Find deals in the stage Documents requested where Documents is Partial or
Missing and Documents requested on is more than 10 days ago. For each one,
read the latest inbox conversation with its contacts. Save a short, polite
draft reply asking for the missing documents, using what the notes say is
missing. Do not send anything. End with a list: client, deal, days waiting,
draft saved yes or no.
```

<AcquisitionCallout title="Ask for drafts, not sends" variant="boundary">
  Mate and routines can send email and chat messages without asking first,
  so tell a routine to save drafts only. Read each draft in the inbox and
  send it yourself. Each run has a credit cap, so keep the list short.
</AcquisitionCallout>

A second routine can react to an event: when a deal's Stage changes to Engagement letter signed, create a task "Send document request" linked to that deal and assigned to the same person. Starter includes [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] routine per user and Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]].

For next year's cycle you can ask Mate in the chat: "Create a new deal for every Individual return deal in Filed, named with next year, at stage Documents requested." Prefer your own AI client? Connect Claude, ChatGPT or another client through [MCP](/docs/mcp), on every plan and on self-hosted installations.

## What Customermates does not do for accountants

- **No deadline control.** Tasks have no due dates, reminders or notifications. A Filing due field and a sorted view show what is coming; binding deadline tracking belongs in your practice or tax software.
- No time tracking, billing, proposals or e-signatures. Store the link to the signed engagement letter on the deal.
- No client portal and no file storage on records.
- No recurring engagements created on a schedule by the CRM itself; use Mate, an MCP client, or the REST API with an n8n workflow you run separately.
- No native QuickBooks or Xero integration. Connect them through the [REST API and webhooks](/features/workflow-automation).
- No mobile app.

If one of these is the reason you are shopping, look at a tool built for it. Customermates publishes this page; vendor details were checked on 8 October 2026 on each vendor's own website.

| Practice management with tax workflow add-ons | [Canopy](https://www.getcanopy.com/pricing) | Client portal, CRM, document management with e-signatures, time and billing; Standard, Plus, Premium and Enterprise plans |
| Proposals and engagement letters with automatic billing | [Ignition](https://www.ignitionapp.com/) | Online proposals, client agreements with e-signing, payment details collected in the proposal |

A workable split is practice software for deadlines, time and billing, and Customermates for clients, engagements and conversations. A webhook fires when a deal changes, so an n8n workflow can hand a signed engagement to the other tool if it has an API.

## Client data and confidentiality

- In Customermates Cloud, the primary production database is configured in the AWS Frankfurt region. Hosting and the other subprocessors, US providers among them, are on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [data processing agreement](/dpa) is available.
- A custom role with Assigned read access limits a staff member to the records assigned to them. The audit log records who changed which record, on every plan.
- Customermates holds no certification such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2.
- If you prepare US tax returns, check the rules on using and disclosing tax return information in [26 U.S.C. § 7216](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/7216) before you store that data in any outside system. Keeping return figures in your tax software and only status in the CRM avoids most of the question.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is QuickBooks a CRM?">
QuickBooks describes itself as cloud-based accounting software. Its site lists a Customer Hub that keeps customer details, notes and history in one place and suggests follow-ups with sales leads. Check that against what you need: if you want each engagement tracked through stages and client conversations next to the record, a separate CRM can sit next to the books in QuickBooks or Xero.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What is the best practice management software for accountants?">
It depends on what you want in one tool. Karbon and Canopy, for example, combine workflow, client portal, time tracking and billing. If you already have practice software and need a clearer view of clients, engagements and conversations, a CRM such as Customermates sits next to it. Customermates is our own product, so weigh that.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How do CPA firms manage deadlines and client deliverables?">
Usually in practice management or tax software that keeps the deadline list. A CRM adds the client side: which engagement waits on which documents, and what was said. In Customermates, keep a Filing due date on each deal and sort a saved view by it. Customermates sends no reminders.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can Customermates handle engagement letters?">
It tracks them. Use a stage for Engagement letter signed, a date field for when you sent it and a link field to the signed file. Writing, sending and signing the letter happens in a tool such as Ignition or your practice software.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does a small accounting firm need a CRM?">
A spreadsheet works while one person knows every client. Once two or three people share clients and busy season stacks up open engagements, a shared record of each client and engagement saves the "who is waiting on what" meeting. A solo practitioner pays [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per month on Starter.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can Customermates connect to QuickBooks or Xero?">
Not natively. Customermates has a REST API, webhooks and a community node for n8n, which you run separately. Through those you can sync client details with tools that offer an API, but you build and run the workflow yourself.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

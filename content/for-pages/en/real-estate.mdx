A real estate CRM keeps track of who is looking for what, which listing is at which stage, and which past client you have not called in a year. Customermates does that job for solo agents, small teams and brokerages that win business through relationships: buyers with their search criteria, sellers with their listings, referral partners and past clients, plus, from the Pro plan, the email and WhatsApp conversations around them. It is an open-source, AI-native CRM built in Germany. It is not real estate software in the full sense: it has no listing database, no MLS or portal feed and no transaction paperwork. The limits section below names tools that have them.

## What agents and brokers need from a CRM

Whether a CRM helps an agent comes down to five specific jobs:

- **Search profiles you can query.** When a three-bedroom house in a certain neighborhood comes in, you need the five buyers who fit, not a list of 900 names.
- **Two kinds of client at once.** A seller with a listing and a buyer with a budget move through different steps, and many people are both.
- **Inquiries on several channels.** Portal leads arrive by email, buyers answer on WhatsApp, and a referral partner calls. If those messages stay on your phone, the record is always behind.
- **Long gaps.** A buyer who is "six months out" or a client who bought five years ago is easy to lose. The next deal often comes from exactly those people.
- **Clear ownership in a team.** In a brokerage, every inquiry needs one agent responsible for it, and an agent's own conversations should stay private unless shared.

## A real estate setup in Customermates

Customermates has five record types. Here is one way to map them to an agent's work; every field name below is an example you create yourself. Under **My Company › Settings** you can rename Contacts to Clients.

| Record type | Used for | Example custom fields |
|---|---|---|
| Contacts | Buyers, sellers, landlords, tenants, referral partners | Role (single select: Buyer, Seller, Landlord, Tenant, Partner, Past client), Search area (single select), Property type (single select), Bedrooms (single select: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5+), Max budget (currency), Financing (single select: Unclear, Pre-approved, Cash), Lead source (single select), Next check-in (date) |
| Organizations | Lenders, law and title firms, developers, other brokerages | Partner type (single select), Referrals sent (text) |
| Deals | Each listing and each buyer or rental mandate | Stage, Side (single select: Listing, Buyer, Rental), Address (text), Asking price (currency), Expected commission (currency), Listing link (link), Agreement ends (date) |
| Tasks | Viewings and next steps, assigned to an agent | Viewing at (date and time), Feedback (single select: Liked it, Second viewing, Not a fit) |
| Services | Rarely needed by agents | Leave empty, or use for fixed-fee services such as a valuation |

People link to deals, so interested buyers can sit on the listing they asked about, and a seller's listing and later home search can sit on the same contact. Tasks have no built-in due date, which is why the viewing gets its own date field.

### Stages for listings and buyers

The deal stage is a single-select field, and each option can carry a win probability. One stage list can serve both sides if you filter by Side:

| Stage | Listing side | Buyer side | Probability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead | Valuation requested | First inquiry | 10% |
| Appointment | Listing presentation | Buyer consultation | 20% |
| Agreement signed | Listing agreement | Buyer agreement | 40% |
| Active | On the market | Touring homes | 50% |
| Offer | Offer received | Offer made | 70% |
| Under contract | Under contract | Under contract | 90% |
| Closed | Closed | Closed | 100% |
| Lost | Lost | Lost | 0% |

On the [pipeline board](/features/pipeline) each deal is a card you drag between stages. Weighted values come from your own probabilities times the deal value, and the deal value comes from linked services, so for commission tracking the Expected commission field is usually simpler. Either way it is arithmetic, not a forecast.

### Saved views for the morning check

1. **Active listings:** deals with Side Listing in Active or Offer, sorted by Agreement ends.
2. **Buyers ready to move:** contacts with Role Buyer and Financing Pre-approved, sorted by Next check-in.
3. **Who is due:** contacts with a Next check-in date, sorted by it, earliest first, so the people to call sit at the top. A sort keeps this view current; a filter on a custom date takes a fixed date.
4. **Stalled leads:** deals in Lead or Appointment with an updated date **Older than…** 14 days.
5. **Sphere:** contacts with Role Past client or Partner, sorted by Next check-in.

Saved views are personal, so each colleague sets the same filters and saves their own view.

### Matching buyers to a new listing

Customermates does not match buyers to properties automatically, and there is no property database to match against. What works is a filter. When a three-bedroom home in Riverside comes in at $480,000, filter contacts by Role Buyer, Search area Riverside, Bedrooms 3 or 4, and Max budget at least 480,000. Currency fields support greater-than and less-than filters, and single-select fields support "is one of" ([filter reference](/docs/filter-syntax)). Link the buyers you call to the listing deal so you can see later who heard about it.

In Customermates Cloud, a [routine](/docs/app-routines) can do the lookup for you. Choose **When something changes**, the event **Deal Updated**, the changed field Stage, and record filters Side = Listing and Stage = Active. Instructions like these:

```text
This listing just went active. Find contacts with Role Buyer whose Search
area and Property type match the listing and whose Max budget is at least
the Asking price. Link them to this deal. Create one task "Call matching
buyers" linked to the deal and those contacts, assigned to me. Do not send
any messages.
```

### Inquiries and conversations in one inbox (Pro and above)

In Customermates Cloud on Pro, Business and Enterprise, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) brings email from Gmail, Outlook or IMAP together with WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram and Telegram. You reply on the original channel. If a portal forwards inquiries to your mailbox, they arrive here with your other mail. Link a sender to a contact once, and every later conversation with that address appears on their timeline.

Two details decide the plan. Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] connected account per user, so an agent who wants both a mailbox and WhatsApp in the inbox needs Business, which includes [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedAccountsPerUser]]. And your conversations stay private: colleagues, admins included, see a thread only after you share it. Sharing a whole account, such as an office inquiry mailbox, needs Business. SMS and phone calls are not channels.

### A daily follow-up routine with Mate (Customermates Cloud)

Mate is the assistant inside Customermates Cloud. It uses hosted AI credits included per active user on every cloud plan; reading inbox conversations needs Pro or higher. Set up a second routine with **On a schedule**, **Every day** at 08:00:

```text
Find inbox conversations where the last message came from the other person
more than one day ago and I have not answered. For each one linked to a
buyer or seller, save a short reply draft in that conversation that answers
their question or proposes a next step. Do not send anything. End with a
list: name, channel, what they asked, draft saved yes or no.
```

<AcquisitionCallout title="Tell Mate to draft, not send" variant="boundary">
  Mate and routines can send email and WhatsApp messages without asking first
  when told to send. A routine runs as its owner, with that owner's channels,
  so say "do not send" in the instructions. Then filter the inbox by Draft,
  read each draft and send it yourself.
</AcquisitionCallout>

Starter includes [[commercial.entitlement.starter.includedRoutinesPerUser]] routine per user and Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedRoutinesPerUser]]. If you prefer your own AI client, connect Claude, ChatGPT or another client through [MCP](/docs/mcp); it works on every plan and on self-hosted installations, with model use billed by your AI provider.

### Teams and brokerages

Deals and contacts have assigned users ("Assigned"), so each inquiry has a responsible agent. A custom role with Assigned read access limits an agent to the records assigned to them, and the audit log records who changed which record. Customermates has no automatic lead routing: you assign each inquiry yourself, or an n8n workflow you run separately assigns it through the [REST API](/features/api).

## Property managers

Customermates is not property management software. It has no rent collection, tenant ledger, maintenance tickets, owner statements or tenant portal. Where it fits a property management firm is the relationship side: winning new owners as clients, tracking leasing inquiries for vacant units, and keeping owner and vendor conversations in one inbox. Model buildings or owner associations as organizations, owners and tenants as contacts, and each new management mandate as a deal. Run the operations in dedicated software and keep the two connected through the REST API or webhooks if you need to.

## What Customermates does not do for real estate

- No listing database, property photos, flyers or brochures, and no MLS, IDX or portal syndication.
- No web forms or landing pages for lead capture, and no automatic lead routing.
- No drip campaigns, email sequences or bulk email. Market updates go out from your newsletter tool.
- No texting, calling or dialer, and no mobile app.
- No showing booking or calendar write. On Pro you can see your calendar read-only next to the CRM.
- No file storage on records. Keep documents in your e-signature or file tool and put the link on the deal.
- No task due dates or reminder notifications. A date field plus a saved view, or a routine, does that work.

If one of these is the reason you are shopping, look at a tool built for it. Customermates publishes this page; vendor details were checked on 8 October 2026 on each vendor's own website.

| If you need | Look at | What the vendor's site lists |
|---|---|---|
| A US agent CRM with lead routing, calling and texting | [Follow Up Boss](https://www.followupboss.com/pricing) | Lead distribution, automated Action Plans, calling and texting (calling is an add-on on Grow), Zillow Pro, mobile apps |
| One flat price for a small US team with drip campaigns | [Wise Agent](https://wiseagent.com/pricing.asp) | CRM plan for up to 5 team members on a shared login, transaction management, lead automation and routing, drip campaigns |
| German broker software with portal transfer and exposés | [Propstack](https://www.propstack.de/) or [FLOWFACT](https://flowfact.de/) | Propstack: ImmoScout24, Immowelt and Kleinanzeigen interfaces, property files, iOS and Android app. FLOWFACT: transfer to more than 150 portals, search-profile matching |
| Rent, accounting and maintenance for rentals | [Buildium](https://www.buildium.com/pricing/) | Online rent payments, accounting, maintenance requests, resident and owner portals, leasing |

A common split is two systems: listing software or an MLS tool for the properties, and Customermates for the people and conversations around them.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="What CRM do realtors use?">
In the US, agents can choose CRMs built for real estate, such as Follow Up Boss or Wise Agent, and many brokerages supply their own tools. In Germany, broker software such as Propstack, FLOWFACT or onOffice also manages the properties themselves; Propstack and FLOWFACT list interfaces to portals such as ImmoScout24. Agents who mostly need follow-up, search profiles and their conversations in one place can also use a general CRM set up as described above. Customermates is our own product, so weigh that.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How much does a real estate CRM cost?">
Vendor pricing pages show a wide range. On 8 October 2026, Follow Up Boss listed its Grow plan at $69 per user per month billed monthly, and Wise Agent listed its CRM plan at $49 per month for up to five team members on a shared login ([Follow Up Boss pricing](https://www.followupboss.com/pricing), [Wise Agent pricing](https://wiseagent.com/pricing.asp)). Customermates costs [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per user per month on Starter and [[commercial.price.pro.monthly]] on Pro with the inbox.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can Customermates match buyers to properties automatically?">
No. There is no property database and no automatic matching. You filter buyers by search area, property type, bedrooms and budget when a listing comes in, or in Customermates Cloud a routine does that lookup when a listing goes active and links the matching buyers to it.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does it connect to the MLS, Zillow or ImmoScout24?">
Not natively. Inquiries that portals send to your mailbox land in the unified inbox on Pro and higher. For anything else, your own integration or an n8n workflow you run separately can create contacts through the [REST API](/features/api).
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is Customermates a good fit for property management?">
Only for the relationship side: winning owners, leasing inquiries, and owner and vendor conversations. Rent collection, accounting, maintenance and tenant portals need property management software such as Buildium.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a free real estate CRM?">
Customermates has a free trial of [[commercial.trial.days]] days and an open-source core you can self-host without a licence fee, without the inbox and Mate. In Germany, FLOWFACT lists FLOWFACT Mini as free broker software for solo agents and founders, with a minimum contract term stated on [its website](https://flowfact.de/).
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

## Why law firms need a CRM

Law firms are relationship businesses. Clients arrive through referrals, return for repeat matters, and stay because of trust. Yet many firms still manage these relationships through partner address books, scattered email threads, and institutional memory that vanishes when someone leaves.

A CRM for law firms centralizes client contacts, referral sources, and business development opportunities in a single system. Instead of relying on memory, your firm gets a structured, shared record of who you work with and where each prospective matter stands.

Here are the challenges legal CRM software addresses:

- **Lost leads and slow intake**: Prospective clients contact your firm but never hear back quickly enough. They move on to the next attorney.
- **No referral visibility**: Who referred this client? How many matters has that source sent this year? Without data, you cannot nurture your best referral relationships.
- **Forgotten relationships**: A client engaged you for a contract matter two years ago. Without a system, nobody thinks of them when their next legal need arises.
- **Conflict check gaps**: Before accepting new matters, you need to search existing and past relationships quickly, not dig through filing cabinets and email inboxes.
- **Siloed practice areas**: Your litigation client may also need corporate counsel, employment advice, or estate planning. Without cross-practice visibility, those opportunities go to a competitor.

Customermates covers the relationship and business development side. It is not practice management software: it does not keep time, bill clients, manage trust accounts, docket court deadlines, or store case documents. Run it next to your practice management and billing tools.

## How a CRM for lawyers works in practice

A law firm CRM should follow the way legal practices operate, from the first call through matter resolution and beyond. Here is how Customermates adapts to the legal workflow.

### Client intake and lead management

The client intake process is where many law firms lose potential business. Leads come in through your website, phone calls, referrals, and networking events. Without a CRM for lawyers, these leads sit in an email inbox or on a sticky note.

In Customermates, each inquiry becomes a deal in your [pipeline](/features/pipeline). You create it by hand, ask an AI client or the Mate assistant to create it from an email, or send it in through the REST API from your website form tool. The deal stage is one configurable single-select column, so intake can follow stages like:

- New inquiry received
- Initial consultation scheduled
- Conflict check completed
- Engagement letter sent
- Retainer received
- Matter opened

Each lead moves through these stages visually on a kanban board, and you can see at a glance how many prospects are at each stage.

### Matter tracking

Once a client is retained, the deal in Customermates can represent the matter from the relationship point of view. Link multiple matters to the same client over time to build a complete relationship history.

Use typed [custom fields](/features/contact-management) to capture legal-specific data:

- Practice area (litigation, corporate, family, real estate, IP, etc.)
- Matter type and sub-type
- Responsible attorney and billing partner
- Court or jurisdiction
- Opposing counsel and parties
- Key dates as custom date fields

Custom date fields are for reference and sorting; Customermates has no reminders, due dates, or deadline tracking, so court and limitation deadlines belong in your docketing or practice management system.

### Conflict checks

Before accepting a new matter, law firms must screen for conflicts of interest. In Customermates you can search and filter contacts, organizations, and deals to find earlier relationships with opposing parties or related entities.

A conflict search is only as good as the data behind it. Customermates helps by keeping relationships in one shared system instead of individual partner records, but it is not a dedicated conflict-check product and does not replace your firm's formal conflict procedure.

### Referral network management

Referrals are the lifeblood of most law firms. Record the referral source on every matter, then group or filter deals by that source to see which relationships send work.

Use a single-select field to mark contacts as referral sources, and custom fields to record:

- Referral source on each matter (a link to the contact or organization)
- Referral date and matter type
- Estimated matter value
- Reciprocal referrals sent

This turns your referral network from an informal arrangement into a visible business development channel.

### Client communication in one unified inbox

On Pro and above, the [unified inbox](/features/email-integration) brings email (Gmail, Outlook, IMAP), LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Telegram conversations into Customermates. You link each conversation to the client, so when a client calls about a prior engagement, another attorney in your firm can pull up the history. SMS and phone calls are not channels, and the inbox is not available self-hosted.

This matters in firms where multiple attorneys work on different matters for the same client. No one should have to say, "Let me check with the partner who handled that."

### Working alongside billing software

Customermates is not billing software and does not create invoices, track time, or manage trust accounts. It can exchange data with your billing workflow through the [REST API, webhooks, and a separately operated n8n workflow](/features/workflow-automation), for example:

- Sending a webhook to your own automation when a matter reaches the "Retainer received" stage
- Creating a client record in your accounting or practice management tool from a won deal
- Writing a billing reference back to the matter as a custom field

Customermates has no native QuickBooks, Xero, or legal billing connector; these connections are customer-built.

### AI agents that operate your firm's CRM

External AI clients such as Claude or ChatGPT connect through [MCP](/docs/mcp) with [[derived.mcp.tools.total]] tools, so an AI agent can read and write your CRM directly and do the data entry attorneys avoid: opening matters, updating intake stages, and logging referral notes. You choose and pay for the AI client. Ask an agent to:

- Draft follow-up emails and client communications
- Summarize a client's interaction history before a meeting
- List which referral sources sent matters this year

On Customermates Cloud, the built-in Mate assistant does the same inside the app, drafts replies in the unified inbox on Pro, and asks for your confirmation before it sends a message or deletes anything. An external MCP client decides for itself whether it asks before writing; the MCP server does not enforce a confirmation.

### Dashboards and reporting

Use [dashboards](/features/reporting) to see:

- Matters and prospects by stage
- Weighted pipeline value by practice area
- Matters grouped by referral source
- Open matters per responsible attorney

These are views of your own records, not predictive analytics or conversion reports.

### Data protection and self-hosting

Client confidentiality is non-negotiable in legal practice, so it matters where CRM data lives:

- **Managed cloud:** the application database runs in the AWS Frankfurt region; hosting and other subprocessors, including US providers, are listed on the [subprocessors page](/subprocessors), and a [DPA](/dpa) is available.
- **[Self-hostable](/features/self-hosted):** run the open-source core on your own infrastructure and decide where client data lives.
- **Open source:** the code is public, so you can inspect how data is handled.
- **No certification:** Customermates holds no ISO 27001, SOC 2, or legal-sector certification.

For firms handling sensitive matters, such as mergers, criminal defense, or family law, self-hosting keeps the CRM data on infrastructure your firm controls. The self-hosted version has the Starter feature set, without the unified inbox or Mate.

## Getting started with CRM for your law firm

1. **Create your account** at [customermates.com/auth/signup](/auth/signup); no credit card required. Your first [[commercial.trial.days]] days are free.
2. **Bring in your records:** import contacts, organizations, and matters from an Excel workbook (XLSX), one record type at a time, use the REST API, or let an AI client connected over MCP create them. There is no CSV import.
3. **Configure your stages:** set up the deal stages for intake and matters. To separate intake, active matters, and business development, add a single-select field such as "Track" and save a view for each.
4. **Add custom fields:** practice area, matter type, referral source, billing partner, conflict status, and any other data your firm tracks.
5. **Connect your automations:** set up webhooks or an n8n workflow you operate, or a routine on Customermates Cloud.
6. **Invite your team:** add attorneys, paralegals, and staff, and use roles to control who can create, view, edit, or delete each record type; read access can be limited to records a user is assigned to.

Your data remains accessible: export records as XLSX one record type at a time, read them through the REST API, or self-host the open-source core.

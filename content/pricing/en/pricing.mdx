faq:
  title: Frequently asked questions
  faqs:
  - id: pricing-faq-1
    title: Why is your CRM so affordable?
    content: We operate without investors and deliberately keep our costs low. This lets us keep prices low for our customers.
  - id: pricing-faq-2
    title: How does your CRM remain reliable long-term?
    content: We grow organically and invest directly in the product and support. This keeps the product maintained for the long term.
  - id: pricing-faq-3
    title: Why a specialized CRM?
    content: Large CRM suites bundle marketing, service and sales and charge for modules many small teams never use. Customermates focuses on customer records you configure, the unified inbox and AI access through MCP. You pay for that and extend the rest when needed through the REST API, webhooks or your own n8n instance.
  - id: pricing-faq-4
    title: How do I set up a new workspace?
    content: "A new workspace starts without lists. On Customermates Cloud, AI setup builds lists, stages, a dashboard and Knowledge Base pages from your description once at onboarding, within a credit cap, and you can skip it to start with an empty workspace. Otherwise people with Data model rights create lists and fields in Data model and import records from an Excel workbook."
  - id: pricing-faq-5
    title: What is not included in any plan?
    content: "Customermates does not include sequences or mass outreach, quotes or invoicing, telephony or SMS, web forms, or native mobile apps. The inbox works through supported provider integrations, not the official WhatsApp Business API or LinkedIn CRM Sync. SSO and white-label are not built and are only available as contracted Enterprise work."
  - id: pricing-faq-6
    title: What counts as a connected account?
    content: "A connected account is one linked mailbox, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, or Telegram profile that belongs to a single user. Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] connected account per user, Business includes [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedAccountsPerUser]] per user, Max includes [[commercial.entitlement.max.includedAccountsPerUser]] per user, and Enterprise is [[commercial.entitlement.enterprise.includedAccountsPerUser]] under fair use. If you need more than [[commercial.entitlement.max.includedAccountsPerUser]] accounts per user, Enterprise is the right plan. Starter and self-hosted instances have no connected accounts. On Pro, each person works in their own accounts and can share individual threads; sharing a whole connected account with the team needs Business, Max or Enterprise."
  - id: pricing-faq-7
    title: How does monthly billing work?
    content: "You pay per active user. When you add or remove users, the change is prorated on your next invoice. Plan upgrades apply immediately with a prorated charge for the remainder of the billing period, and downgrades apply immediately with the unused portion credited."
  - id: pricing-faq-8
    title: How does Mate usage work?
    content: "Every paid cloud plan includes a Mate entitlement and a monthly Mate usage allowance per active user, stated as a multiple of the Starter allowance: Starter 1x, Pro 3x, Business 10x, Max 20x, and Enterprise as contracted. Business gives the most usage per euro. Usage counts only when Mate is enabled in the hosted environment: hosted AI processing in Customermates then counts toward it. Usage follows the actual provider cost, including model calls and server-side semantic retrieval, so a simple question uses a small share and a multi-step request more. In the app, the Mate meter shows how much of the monthly allowance is used as a percentage. Paid allowances reset monthly; unused usage does not roll over. Every active trial user has the Pro allowance of 3x. Active members’ allowances form the shared workspace pool; each member also has an individual allocation. New paid seats receive an exact prorated allowance for the remaining billing period. Upgrades increase the current allowance immediately; downgrades reduce it immediately. External MCP clients use your own AI provider for model calls. Server-side semantic retrieval can count toward Mate usage, including through MCP. Mate is cloud-only. There are no top-ups or automatic overage charges."
  - id: pricing-faq-9
    title: Can one login use several workspaces?
    content: "Yes. One login can belong to several fully separate workspaces, for example one for sales and one for bookkeeping, and switch between them. Each workspace has its own plan, subscription and invoices, and seats count per workspace: a person who is active in two workspaces is a paid seat in both. The first workspace you create includes the [[commercial.trial.days]]-day free trial; every additional workspace starts by choosing a plan. A self-hosted instance runs any number of workspaces without billing."
  - id: pricing-faq-10
    title: What do I get when I self-host?
    content: "A self-hosted instance always has the Starter feature set: core CRM, MCP, REST API, webhooks and the n8n interfaces, without billing. The unified inbox, Mate, routines and AI setup run only on Customermates Cloud. MCP with your own AI client works on a self-hosted instance, and that client uses your own AI provider."
  - id: pricing-faq-11
    title: Where are my data and connected-account credentials stored?
    content: "The cloud database runs in AWS Frankfurt, and connected accounts sign in through Unipile's hosted flow, so Customermates never receives provider passwords or OAuth tokens. The full answer is in the docs: https://customermates.com/docs/architecture-security#where-are-customer-data-and-credentials-stored"
  - id: pricing-faq-12
    title: "What is the Free plan?"
    content: "Your first Cloud workspace starts with the [[commercial.trial.days]]-day Pro trial, no credit card; every further workspace starts by choosing a plan. If the trial ends without a paid plan, the workspace moves to Free instead of being locked, and nothing is deleted. Free is for trying Customermates on your own: [[commercial.limit.free.maxMembers]] person, up to [[commercial.limit.free.maxRecords]] records and [[commercial.limit.free.fileStorageBytes]] file storage per workspace. It has no inbox or connected accounts, no Mate, no routines, no webhooks and no n8n trigger. The REST API and MCP are included with up to [[commercial.limit.free.apiRequestsPerMinute]] requests per minute and [[commercial.limit.free.apiRequestsPerDay]] per day per workspace, so you can bring your own Claude or ChatGPT. Records and files above a limit stay; only adding more is refused. You can move to a paid plan at any time. A self-hosted instance stays free without these limits."
  - id: pricing-faq-13
    title: "What happens to my team when a workspace moves to Free?"
    content: "Free keeps [[commercial.limit.free.maxMembers]] active person per workspace: the person who created the workspace, or else the earliest admin. Everyone else is paused, not removed. Paused people cannot open the workspace, and their records, notes and history stay. Open invitations and join requests are cancelled, routines are paused and existing webhooks stop sending. An admin can hand the active seat to another admin. As soon as the workspace chooses any paid plan, every paused person is back automatically and counts as a paid seat again, and routines and webhooks resume."

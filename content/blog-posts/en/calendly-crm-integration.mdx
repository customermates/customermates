# Connecting Calendly to a CRM: native integrations, webhooks and the edge cases

Calendly has native integrations with three CRMs: HubSpot from its Standard plan, Salesforce on Teams or Enterprise, and Microsoft Dynamics 365 with Power Automate on Enterprise. Every other CRM connects through an app in that CRM's marketplace, a connector platform such as Zapier or Zoho Flow, or Calendly's webhooks. Webhooks need a paid Calendly plan, and you create them through Calendly's API, not in its settings.

Whichever route you take, decide before you build what one booking should create in the CRM and what a reschedule should change. The reschedule is the easy part to get wrong, because Calendly reports it as a cancellation plus a new booking.

Customermates, an AI-native CRM, publishes this guide. Customermates has no Calendly connector of its own; the section on it below shows the three routes that work instead. Vendor details were checked on 8 October 2026 on the vendors' own pages, linked at the end.

<ArticleSummary title="At a glance">
  <SummaryItem label="Native">HubSpot, Salesforce and Microsoft Dynamics 365, each tied to a specific Calendly plan.</SummaryItem>
  <SummaryItem label="Every other CRM">A marketplace app, a connector platform such as Zapier, Zoho Flow or n8n, or your own webhook receiver.</SummaryItem>
  <SummaryItem label="Webhooks">Not on Calendly's Free plan, and created through the API. The core events are invitee.created, invitee.canceled and routing_form_submission.created.</SummaryItem>
  <SummaryItem label="Main trap">A reschedule fires a cancellation and a new booking. Check the rescheduled flag before you close a deal as cancelled.</SummaryItem>

## What Calendly's native CRM integrations do

| CRM | Calendly plan | What it does, according to Calendly |
|---|---|---|
| HubSpot | Standard, Teams or Enterprise | Creates and updates contacts and leads when a meeting is booked, logs activities including reschedules and cancellations, and adds answers to booking questions to the HubSpot record. Teams adds routing by HubSpot assignment. |
| Salesforce | Teams or Enterprise | Creates a lead, contact or opportunity for a new prospect, creates events or tasks on any Salesforce object, maps booking data to a custom object and routes known leads to their Salesforce owner. Enterprise can limit the sync to specific groups, such as the sales team. |
| Microsoft Dynamics 365 | Enterprise | Listed on the pricing page as "Microsoft Dynamics 365 and Power Automate for CRM workflows". Calendly gives no further detail there. |

Two details matter when you choose. Calendly's HubSpot page talks about contacts, leads, activities and routing, and says nothing about deals. If every booking should open a deal in HubSpot, test that before you rely on it. Both pages also describe logging meeting summaries from Calendly Notetaker to the record, which only applies to meetings recorded with it.

If your CRM is on this list and your Calendly plan qualifies, start with the native integration. There is nothing extra to run, and Calendly maintains it.

## Connecting a CRM that Calendly does not support natively

Searches like "Calendly Pipedrive integration" or "Calendly Freshworks integration" end in one of four routes:

| Route | Example | Choose it if | Watch for |
|---|---|---|---|
| App in the CRM's marketplace | Pipedrive Marketplace lists Calendly Sync by Outfunnel, which creates contacts, leads, deals, activities or notes. The Freshworks Marketplace lists Calendly Events for CRM. | An app exists for your CRM and covers what you need | Third-party apps have their own terms. The Freshworks app works the other way round: it schedules, reschedules and cancels Calendly events from inside the CRM. |
| Connector platform | Zapier's trigger "Invitee Created or Rescheduled", or Zoho Flow from "Event scheduled by invitee" to "Create or update contact" in Zoho CRM | Nobody on the team writes code, and your CRM has a connector on the platform | One more account to pay for and watch. Check how the trigger treats reschedules. |
| Webhooks into n8n or your own code | n8n's Calendly Trigger node, with the events "Event created" and "Event canceled" | You need lookups and branching, or your CRM has no connector anywhere | Someone has to run and monitor it. n8n can be self-hosted or used as n8n Cloud. |
| AI client with two MCP connectors | Calendly's MCP server in Claude or ChatGPT, next to your CRM's connector | You review bookings in batches and want to confirm each write | It runs when someone asks. It is not a sync. |

The long tail of CRMs (Keap, Agile CRM, LeadConnector, Pipeline CRM and many more) usually sits in the first two rows. Search the CRM's own marketplace first, then Zapier's Calendly page.

## What one booking should create

Calendly's API gives you the invitee's name, email and answers to custom questions, plus the event type, the host and the start time. Map them before you build anything:

| Calendly data | Where it goes in the CRM | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Invitee email | Contact email, used as the lookup key | Repeat bookers must find their existing contact |
| Invitee name | Contact first and last name | Most CRMs keep these as two fields |
| "Company" question | Organization, found or created | The contact and deal then sit under the account |
| Other questions with fixed choices | A select field on the deal | You can filter and group by it later |
| Free-text answers | The deal's notes | Free text in a select field breaks filtering |
| Event type | Deal name and a lead-source field | Shows which booking page brings deals |
| Host | Deal owner or assigned user | Round-robin bookings land with the right person |
| Start time | A date field on the deal | Lets you sort upcoming calls in a list view |
| Calendly invitee or event link | A text field on the deal | Lets a later cancellation find the right deal |

The last row is the one people skip, and it is the one the cancellation step needs.

## Reschedules, repeat bookers and no-shows

- **Reschedules.** When an invitee reschedules, Calendly fires invitee.canceled for the old time and invitee.created for the new one. The cancellation carries `rescheduled: true`, and the new booking points back to the old one through `old_invitee`. A workflow that ignores the flag marks the deal as cancelled and then opens a second one.
- **Repeat bookers.** Search the CRM for the email before you create a contact, and lowercase it first. People type their address differently from one booking to the next.
- **No-shows.** Zapier offers the Calendly trigger "Invitee No Show Created". Calendly's webhooks overview lists only bookings, cancellations and routing-form submissions, so if you build on webhooks, plan to mark no-shows by hand. Moving the deal to a no-show stage beats guessing from a timer.
- **Routing forms.** routing_form_submission.created fires when someone submits a routing form, even if no meeting is booked. Use it to capture people who qualified but never picked a time. The subscription has to use organization scope.

## Where Customermates fits

Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM for small client-facing teams such as consultancies and agencies. It has no Calendly connector, so bookings come in through one of three routes.

### Route 1: Calendly webhooks into the REST API

This works on every Customermates plan and on a self-hosted installation. You need a Calendly plan with webhooks and a place to run the workflow. The example uses n8n, which runs separately and is not part of Customermates.

<Steps>
<Step title="Create an API key">
In Customermates, open **My Profile › API & Connectors**, click **Add** and choose **Standard API key**. The key acts with your permissions. In n8n, store it as a Header Auth credential with the header name `x-api-key`. See [API keys](/docs/api-keys).
</Step>
<Step title="Prepare the deal columns">
Add a "Call booked" and a "Cancelled" option to your deal stage column, which is a **Single Select** column. Add a **Date & Time** column "Meeting", a **Single Select** column "Lead source" with one option per event type, and a **Text** column "Calendly booking". `GET /api/v1/deals/configuration` returns the column ids and options the API expects.
</Step>
<Step title="Receive the booking">
Add n8n's Calendly Trigger with "Event created" and "Event canceled". Alternatively, use a Webhook node and subscribe its URL through Calendly's API.
</Step>
<Step title="Find or create the contact">
Send `POST /api/v1/contacts/search` with the invitee's email as `searchTerm` and keep only an exact match. If there is none, send `POST /api/v1/contacts` with `firstName`, `lastName` and `identifiers: [{ "provider": "mail", "value": "<email>" }]`.
</Step>
<Step title="Create the deal">
Send `POST /api/v1/deals` with a name such as "Anna Weber, intro call", the contact id in `contactIds`, the host's Customermates user id in `userIds`, and `customFieldValues` for stage, meeting time, lead source and the Calendly link. Free-text answers go into `notes`, which accepts Markdown. Map host emails to user ids once, in the workflow.
</Step>
<Step title="Handle the cancellation">
On invitee.canceled, find the deal by its "Calendly booking" value with an `equals` filter (see [filter syntax](/docs/filter-syntax)). If `rescheduled` is true, update "Meeting" from the new booking. If it is false, set the stage to "Cancelled" with `PUT /api/v1/deals/{id}`.
</Step>
</Steps>

One safeguard is built in. Customermates stores email addresses in lowercase and refuses a second contact with an address that another contact already holds ("This channel is already linked to another contact."). A missed lookup therefore fails visibly instead of creating a duplicate. The [n8n guide](/docs/n8n) lists every endpoint and the relation rules for updates.

### Route 2: the calendar, on Pro and higher in Customermates Cloud

If Calendly is set to send **Calendar Invitations**, the host's Google or Outlook calendar creates the event and adds the invitee as a guest. Connect that same account in Customermates under **My Profile › Channels** with **Connect channel**, and the meeting arrives without any workflow tool. Calendar events appear in the **Account & calendar** category of an [activity timeline](/docs/app-dashboard), and on the record of a contact whose email matches a guest. Customermates only reads the calendar. It never books or changes meetings.

To turn bookings into records, a routine can sweep the calendar. Routines run in Customermates Cloud only and use hosted AI credits from the owner's monthly allowance. Choose **On a schedule**, set **Repeats** to **Every hour**, and give it instructions like these:

> Look at my calendar events that start in the next 14 days. For each event with a guest whose email does not end in @ourfirm.com, find the contact by that email or create one with the guest's name and email. If that contact has no deal in stage "Call booked", create one named "[guest name], intro call", set "Meeting" to the event start and assign it to me. Copy the event description into the deal's notes. Do not send any email or message. List what you created.

The last two sentences matter. A routine runs as its owner, with the owner's channels, and it may send messages without asking unless its instructions say otherwise. There is also an event trigger for calendar changes, **Calendar Event Changed**. It fires on every change to the connected calendar, and events that arrive shortly after a run has started are ignored, so a schedule is the safer choice for bookings. [Routines](/docs/app-routines) lists the current limits.

### Route 3: your own AI client

Calendly runs an MCP server at `https://mcp.calendly.com/` with tools that list scheduled events and their invitees. Calendly says no paid plan is needed to connect it, although some actions depend on your plan. Add it to Claude or ChatGPT next to the [Customermates connector](/docs/connect-custom-connector), then ask: "List the Calendly meetings booked since Monday with their invitees. Find or create each invitee as a contact in Customermates and create a deal in stage Call booked." The Customermates side works on every plan and on self-hosted installations, and it uses your own AI provider rather than hosted credits. Customermates executes what the client calls, so set approvals for write tools in your AI client.

- **Calendar route on Pro:** [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.3]] per month. <AcquisitionCallout title="Where Customermates is not the right fit" variant="boundary">
There is no Calendly connector, so every route needs either a workflow you run or the calendar plus a routine. On Pro, the Gmail or Outlook mailbox is the user's [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] included connected account; Business includes [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedAccountsPerUser]]. Tasks have no due dates or reminders, so put the follow-up date in a date column on the deal. Customermates does no lead scoring; store routing-form answers in single-select fields and filter by them. A self-hosted installation has REST, webhooks and MCP, but no calendar, inbox, Mate or routines. If you want Calendly to pick the host from the CRM owner at the moment of booking, that is a Calendly feature for HubSpot and Salesforce.
</AcquisitionCallout>

## Test before you trust it

Run these six bookings against a test event type before you switch the integration on for real:

1. A new invitee. The contact, the deal and every mapped field arrive.
2. The same invitee again, with the email typed in capitals. No second contact appears.
3. A reschedule. The deal's meeting time moves, and the stage does not change to cancelled.
4. A real cancellation. The stage changes and nothing else is created.
5. A booking with an optional question left empty. The workflow still completes.
6. A no-show, if you track no-shows through Zapier.

## FAQ

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Does Calendly have a CRM?">
No. Calendly is a scheduling platform. It keeps contacts for the people you meet (Zapier lists contact triggers that need a paid Calendly plan), but it has no pipeline. Its CRM integrations send bookings into HubSpot, Salesforce and Dynamics 365, where stages and deal reporting live.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Calendly work with Salesforce and HubSpot?">
Yes, natively. The HubSpot integration is available on Calendly's Standard, Teams and Enterprise plans and creates contacts or leads and logs activities. The Salesforce integration needs Teams or Enterprise and can create a lead, contact or opportunity, plus events or tasks on any Salesforce object.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Which Calendly plan do I need for a CRM integration?">
Not Free. HubSpot and webhooks start at Standard, Salesforce needs Teams or Enterprise, and Microsoft Dynamics 365 is listed for Enterprise. Calendly's MCP server for AI clients is the exception: Calendly says it needs no paid plan, though some actions depend on the plan.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How do I connect Calendly to Pipedrive, Freshworks or another CRM?">
Look in the CRM's own marketplace first. Pipedrive lists Calendly Sync by Outfunnel, and the Freshworks Marketplace lists Calendly Events for CRM. If nothing fits, use a connector platform such as Zapier or Zoho Flow, or point Calendly's webhooks at n8n or your own code and call the CRM's API.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How should a rescheduled Calendly meeting update the CRM?">
Calendly sends a cancellation for the old time and a new booking for the new one. The cancellation carries rescheduled set to true. Update the meeting time on the existing deal in that case, and only change the stage when the flag is false.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Customermates integrate with Calendly?">
Not with a native connector. Bookings reach Customermates through Calendly webhooks and the REST API on any plan, through the host's connected Google or Outlook calendar on Pro and higher in Customermates Cloud, or through an AI client that has both Calendly's and Customermates' MCP connectors. Customermates does not book meetings or write calendar events.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

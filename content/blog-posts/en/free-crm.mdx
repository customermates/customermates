# Which free CRM fits, and what happens when you outgrow it

Yes, you can run a CRM for free. HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce, Capsule, Bitrix24 and CentralStationCRM all offer a hosted plan that costs nothing and has no end date, but each one caps users, contacts or storage. The other free route is an open-source CRM such as SuiteCRM, EspoCRM or Customermates that you install on your own server: no licence fee and no seat count, but you pay for the server and the hours to run it.

A free trial is a third thing and not a free plan. Pipedrive and Freshsales, for example, offer trials of paid plans. The useful question is not which free CRM is best, but which cap your team will hit first and what the step after it costs.

## Free CRM plans compared

Limits checked on 8 October 2026 on each vendor's own page, linked in the sources line at the end. Vendors change free plans without notice, so open the page again on the day you sign up.

| Product | Free users | Contact or record cap | Other limits worth knowing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HubSpot free tools | Up to 2 | 1,000 contacts | HubSpot branding on live chat, the meetings link and marketing emails; 2,000 marketing email sends a month |
| Zoho CRM Free Edition | Up to 3 | 5,000 records across all modules | 1 GB file storage |
| Salesforce Free Suite | 2 user licences | Not stated on the page | You must log in at least once every 60 days to keep it |
| Capsule Free | Up to 2 | 250 contacts | Contacts, tasks and a customizable sales pipeline |
| Bitrix24 Free | 1 to 2 | Not stated on the page | 5 GB storage, 1 sales pipeline, 2 apps from the Bitrix24 Market |
| CentralStationCRM Starter | 3 | 200 contacts | 100 MB of files |

Read the table by its smallest number. A three-person consultancy with 400 client contacts has already outgrown CentralStationCRM's free tier on contacts, Capsule's on contacts and seats, and HubSpot, Salesforce and Bitrix24 on seats. Zoho is the only plan in the table that fits that team on day one.

### Which cap you will hit first

| Your situation | Look at first | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solo founder or two partners, fewer than 250 contacts | Capsule, HubSpot, Salesforce Free Suite | Two seats are enough, and the contact caps are not yet binding |
| Three people | Zoho CRM Free Edition, CentralStationCRM Starter | They are the plans in the table that allow a third user |
| Many contacts, few users | Zoho CRM Free Edition, HubSpot free tools | 5,000 records and 1,000 contacts are the highest stated caps in the table |
| You want CRM, tasks, chat and calendars in one place | Bitrix24 Free | It bundles collaboration tools with a basic CRM |
| More than three users, or no caps at all | A self-hosted open-source CRM | No seat count, but someone must run the server |
| You want a German-language product | CentralStationCRM Starter, or Customermates self-hosted | CentralStationCRM publishes its plans in German with euro prices; Customermates is built in Germany and has a German interface |

If you want HubSpot or Zoho and expect to stay a while, the [HubSpot alternatives](/compare/hubspot-alternative) and [Zoho CRM alternatives](/compare/zoho-crm-alternative) pages show what their paid tiers compete with.

## Three kinds of free

| Model | What is free | What you still pay | Fits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Permanent hosted plan | An account within the published caps | The paid tier once you pass a cap, and the migration if you leave | One to three people with modest contact lists |
| Open-source self-hosting | The licence, with no seat count | Server, domain, email delivery, updates, backups and your time | Teams with someone who can run Docker and a database |
| Free trial | Full access to a paid plan for a fixed number of days | The subscription afterwards | Testing a paid product with real work before buying |

The free plan's price is zero, but the step after it is not. HubSpot's free tools move to a Starter tier, Zoho to a paid edition, and CentralStationCRM's next package (Team, €24 a month plus VAT for 3 users and 3,000 contacts) already costs money for the same three people. Price that step before you put two years of client history into a free plan.

## The open-source route: free licence, paid operations

[SuiteCRM](https://github.com/salesagility/SuiteCRM) and [EspoCRM](https://www.espocrm.com/download/) are both published under the AGPLv3 and can be downloaded and run on your own server. Both vendors also sell hosting and services: SuiteCRM offers hosting, support and its SuiteASSURED programme, and EspoCRM offers a cloud version and paid extensions. The Customermates core is AGPL as well, and our [open-source CRM guide](/blog/open-source-crm) compares the self-hosted options in more depth.

The licence costs nothing. What does cost something:

- a server or VM, a domain and TLS;
- an email delivery provider, so sign-up and invitation emails arrive;
- someone who applies updates, watches the disk and tests a backup restore;
- the time to fix it when an update fails on a Monday morning.

For a team of five, a small VPS can cost less per month than five paid seats. For a team without anyone comfortable on a command line, it is usually more expensive, because the hours are real even when nobody invoices them.

## How to test a free CRM in one afternoon

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick one real, non-sensitive workflow">
    Create an organization and a contact, open a deal, add a task, move the deal one stage, and find the whole history again a day later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the caps into a sheet">
    Copy users, contacts or records, storage, pipelines and automation limits from the vendor's own pricing page. Add the date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the exit first">
    Export your contacts and deals before you import anything important. Check which format you get, whether deals keep their links to contacts, and what you would have to rebuild by hand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Price the first upgrade">
    Work out the monthly cost after you add the next teammate or pass the contact cap. Compare that number, not the zero.
  </Step>
  <Step title="For self-hosting, name an owner">
    Write down who installs, updates and restores the system. If nobody's name fits, choose a hosted plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where Customermates fits

*Disclosure: Customermates publishes this guide.* Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM built in Germany for small client-facing teams such as consultants, agencies and founders. It is free in one form and paid in the other, and the two differ in what they include.

**Self-hosted: free, with no seat count.** You run the AGPL core with Docker Compose: one app container and one PostgreSQL container. A self-hosted workspace has no subscription and no trial, and the app counts no seats. You get the Starter feature set: contacts, organizations, deals, services and tasks, custom columns, saved views, dashboards, roles, the audit log, REST, webhooks and MCP for your own AI client. You do not get the unified inbox, Mate or hosted AI credits. You need your own Resend API key for email delivery. The [self-hosting docs](/docs/self-hosting) walk through the install.

**Cloud: paid, with a trial.** Customermates Cloud has no free plan. The trial runs [[commercial.trial.days]] days with no credit card. After it, a three-person team pays [[commercial.price.starter.monthly.seats.3]] a month on Starter, which has no inbox, or [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.3]] on Pro, which adds the unified inbox for email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram conversations. Billing is monthly only. Every cloud plan includes monthly hosted AI credits per active user for Mate, the assistant in Customermates Cloud; see [pricing](/pricing) for the allowances.

A concrete starting setup for a small consultancy, in either edition:

1. Import organizations first from an Excel workbook (`.xlsx`), then contacts, then deals. Import runs one record type at a time, and link columns are matched by record name, so contacts find their organization. CSV is not accepted; save it as `.xlsx` first. The [records docs](/docs/app-records) list the current file limits.
2. Keep the default deal **Stage** column (Prospecting, Qualification, Demo, Proposal, Negotiation, Won, Lost) or rename the options to your sales steps. Stage weights give a weighted pipeline value.
3. Add one custom single-select column such as "Service line" to deals, and save a personal board view grouped by **Stage** and filtered to the deals assigned to you.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is HubSpot CRM still free?">
Yes. On 8 October 2026, HubSpot's own page described its free CRM as free with no expiration date, for up to two users and 1,000 contacts. Marketing emails, live chat and the meetings link carry HubSpot branding on the free tools; the paid tiers remove it.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What is the best free CRM?">
The one whose caps your team will not hit within a year. For two people, Capsule, HubSpot and the Salesforce Free Suite all fit. For three people, Zoho CRM's free edition and CentralStationCRM Starter allow the third user. Beyond three users, the free option is a self-hosted open-source CRM.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a free CRM with unlimited users?">
Not among the hosted free plans in this guide: they stop at two or three users. Self-hosted open-source CRMs such as SuiteCRM, EspoCRM and Customermates have no seat count, so the limit is your server and your time.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What is the difference between a free CRM and a free trial?">
A free plan has no end date but caps what you can store and who can log in. A trial gives you a paid plan in full for a fixed number of days: 14 at Pipedrive, 21 at Freshsales and [[commercial.trial.days]] at Customermates Cloud. Pipedrive has no free plan.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Should I start with a spreadsheet instead of a free CRM?">
A spreadsheet works for one person and a short list. Once deals, contacts and tasks need to stay linked, or two people edit the same list, a free CRM is the better start. The [Excel CRM template guide](/blog/excel-crm-template) shows where the spreadsheet approach stops.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Customermates have a free plan?">
Self-hosting is free: no subscription, no trial and no seat count, with the core under the AGPL. It uses the Starter feature set without the inbox or Mate. Customermates Cloud has no free plan; it has a [[commercial.trial.days]]-day trial with no credit card, then monthly billing per user.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

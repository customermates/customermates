# CRM App: How to Choose a Mobile CRM That Your Team Will Use

A CRM app is the phone or tablet version of a customer relationship management system: you look up a contact before a meeting, log what was agreed afterwards and move the deal, without waiting until you are back at a desk. Whether you need a native app from the App Store or Google Play depends on one question: does your team work where the signal drops? If it does, pick a CRM whose app works offline. If your team mostly works from offices and home and only checks the CRM on the phone between meetings, a CRM that runs well in the phone's browser can be enough.

Customermates publishes this guide. It is a web app without native iOS or Android apps, so it is not on the shortlist of native apps below and has its own labelled section further down. Vendor details were checked on 8 October 2026 on each vendor's own pages.

## Native app or browser: decide first

A feature count does not settle this. The deciding factor is where your people are when they need to write something down.

| Your situation | What you need | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reps visit customers on site, in basements, plants or rural areas | A native app with offline editing | Notes typed without signal must sync later instead of getting lost |
| Trade fairs and events with many new contacts a day | A native app with a business card scanner | Typing 40 cards into a phone browser in the evening rarely happens |
| Calls from the phone should be logged automatically | A native app with call tracking | A browser cannot see the phone's call log |
| Consultants and agencies who prepare meetings on the phone and write follow-ups later | A responsive web app can be enough | Reading a contact's history needs no offline mode |
| Most client conversations run through email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp | The conversations in the CRM, on any screen | The phone is mostly where you read and answer them |

If two rows describe your team, the first one that applies wins. Offline needs are hard to fix later, everything else is not.

## What a CRM app has to do on a phone

A short list, with a test for each point. Run the tests on your own phone during the trial, not in a sales demo.

- **Find a person in seconds.** Search by name or company from the first screen. Test: look up a contact while standing, one-handed.
- **Show history before the meeting.** Last conversations, open deals, notes. Test: can you tell what was promised last time in under a minute?
- **Capture notes right after the meeting.** Your phone keyboard's dictation works in any text field, native app or browser, so voice-to-text is less of a differentiator than it looks. What differs is whether the note saves without signal.
- **Move a deal.** Change the stage from the phone without scrolling through a desktop layout.
- **Work offline, if you need it.** Test: switch on airplane mode, edit a record, switch it off again and check the record on a laptop.
- **Respect your data rules.** Ask where the data is stored, whether the app supports your company's device management and what happens to cached data when a phone is lost. For a GDPR review, also ask for the data processing agreement.

## Native CRM apps worth a look

Listed alphabetically. The order is not a ranking, and each vendor's app does more than the lines below; they cover what matters for the decision above.

### HubSpot

HubSpot's [mobile app](https://www.hubspot.com/products/crm/mobile) runs on iOS and Android and, in HubSpot's words, "You can download HubSpot's mobile app for free." It lets you update deal stages, log notes and check reports and dashboards, and it includes Breeze Assistant, HubSpot's AI assistant. Calls can go out through HubSpot-provided phone numbers with caller ID linked to the CRM. The free tools cover up to 2 users, according to the [HubSpot pricing page](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/crm). The app page does not describe offline editing, so test that yourself if you need it.

### Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales

Dynamics 365 Sales runs in Microsoft's app for phones and tablets. According to [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/sales/features-in-mobile-offline), the Sales Hub app works offline once an administrator has assigned a mobile offline profile to your user. Offline, you can create and edit accounts, contacts, leads and opportunities, while business process flows are not supported. It fits companies that already run on Microsoft and have someone to configure it.

### Pipedrive

Pipedrive has apps for Android and iOS. Its [features page](https://www.pipedrive.com/en/features) says you can "schedule sales activities wherever you are, even when offline", make calls from the app that are tracked in Pipedrive, add a photo to a deal or contact and use "Nearby" to see which clients are close. It suits small sales teams that work a pipeline stage by stage and spend part of the week on the road.

### Salesforce

The Salesforce mobile app "is included with every Salesforce license" and is downloaded from the App Store or Google Play, according to [Salesforce Trailhead](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/salesforce1_mobile_app/salesforce1_mobile_app_intro), which also says it "has offline capabilities". It is customizable like the rest of Salesforce, which helps organizations with an admin and adds setup work for a small team.

### Zoho CRM

Zoho CRM's [mobile apps](https://www.zoho.com/crm/mobile/) for Android and iOS let you update records offline and sync later, scan a business card into a contact and use the Zia assistant. The [pricing page](https://www.zoho.com/crm/zohocrm-pricing.html) lists a free edition for 3 users that includes access to the mobile apps. For a small field team on a tight budget, that is a way to try a native app at no cost; check during the trial which offline features the free edition gives you.

## Where Customermates fits, and where it does not

*Disclosure: Customermates publishes this guide.* Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM built in Germany for small client-facing teams: consultants, agencies, other relationship-driven service businesses and founders. It has **no native mobile app**. It is a web app that you open in the phone's browser, where the sidebar turns into a slide-out menu. There is no offline mode, no push notifications and no call logging. If your team needs any of those, choose one of the native apps above.

What it does well on a phone is the read-and-reply part of client work. Contacts, organizations and deals sit next to the conversations from connected email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp accounts in the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox), which needs Pro or higher; Starter has no inbox. Before a meeting you open the contact and see the latest messages from your connected accounts in its activity timeline. After it, you answer from the same screen. One team-wide timeline across every channel, with shared accounts, is part of Business.

## Test a CRM app before you commit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick two candidates">
    Take the row from the decision table that matches your team and choose two products. Five parallel trials means nobody uses any of them properly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Load real data">
    Import twenty open deals with their contacts. Demo data hides how long names, missing fields and messy notes look on a small screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use it for one real day out of the office">
    Prepare two meetings and log both outcomes on the phone only. Note where you had to wait for a laptop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the airplane-mode test">
    Edit a record offline, reconnect and check the record on a laptop. If the edit is missing and you need offline work, the product is out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check what the phone keeps">
    Ask the vendor what stays cached on the device, how a lost phone is handled and whether your device management tool is supported.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQ

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="What are CRM apps?">
A CRM app is the mobile version of a CRM: an iOS or Android app, or a web app that works in a phone's browser. You use it to look up contacts, log notes, move deals and see what is due next while away from your desk. Native apps can add offline editing, call tracking and business card scanning, which a browser cannot do.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Which is the best CRM app?">
There is no single best one. For field work without signal, pick an app that edits offline: Pipedrive, Zoho CRM, Salesforce and Dynamics 365 Sales describe this on their own pages. For a free start, HubSpot's app is a free download and the Zoho CRM free edition includes the mobile apps for 3 users. For service teams whose deals run through email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp, a CRM with those conversations inside can matter more than a native app.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a free CRM app?">
Yes. HubSpot's mobile app is a free download, and its free tools cover up to 2 users. Zoho CRM's free edition covers 3 users and includes access to its mobile apps. Customermates has no free hosted plan and no native app; see the [free CRM guide](/blog/free-crm) for more options.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What does a mobile CRM cost per month?">
Usually you pay for the CRM plan and nothing extra for the app: HubSpot's app is a free download, Salesforce says the app is included with every Salesforce license, and the Zoho CRM free edition includes the mobile apps. With other vendors, check whether the app is part of your plan. In Customermates, a team of five on Pro pays [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.5]] per month, with no native app but with the unified inbox in the browser.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Do CRM apps work offline?">
Some do, and the details differ. Zoho CRM lets you update records offline and sync later. Pipedrive lets you schedule activities offline. Salesforce says its app has offline capabilities, and Dynamics 365 Sales works offline once an administrator assigns an offline profile. Run the airplane-mode test above during the trial instead of relying on a feature list.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Customermates have a mobile app?">
No. Customermates is a web app that you use in the phone's browser, without offline mode or push notifications. In Customermates Cloud you can ask Mate to summarize or draft from the phone, using hosted AI credits, and Claude can reach the CRM through a custom MCP connector.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

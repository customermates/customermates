# Salesforce Pricing: What Each Edition Costs and What Your Team Will Pay

Salesforce Sales Cloud costs $0 (Free Suite, two users), $25 (Starter Suite), $100 (Pro Suite), $195 (Core), $395 (Advanced) or $550 (Max) per user per month. Only Starter Suite can be paid month to month. Pro Suite and everything above it is billed annually, and Pro Suite needs a contract. Salesforce shows the same figures in euros, so Starter Suite is €25 and Max is €550.

Prices were checked on 8 October 2026 on [Salesforce's Sales Cloud pricing page](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/pricing/). Service Cloud uses the same paid price points, from $25 to $550. Customermates, which publishes this guide, sells a CRM too; the one section about it is labelled.

  <SummaryItem label="Cheapest paid edition">Starter Suite at $25 per user per month, billed monthly or annually, with no user maximum.</SummaryItem>
  <SummaryItem label="The big jump">Pro Suite is $100, four times Starter, and adds quoting, forecasting and more customization. It is billed annually under a contract.</SummaryItem>

## Salesforce Sales Cloud editions and prices

| Edition | Per user per month (USD or EUR) | Billing | What it adds |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Free Suite | 0 | Free, no contract | Leads, accounts, contacts, opportunities and cases for two users, up to 100 template emails a month |
| Starter Suite | 25 | Monthly or annually | Sales flows and lead routing, AI that syncs emails, events and contacts, email marketing |
| Pro Suite | 100 | Annually, contract required | Quoting and forecasting, more customization and automation, AgentExchange access |
| Core | 195 | Annually | Everything in Professional Edition plus Momentum and Premier Success, Slack Business+, Tableau Next, 500,000 Flex Credits per org per year |
| Advanced | 395 | Annually | More security, AI and data capacity, Sales Programs, backup and recovery, 1 million Flex Credits per org per year |
| Max | 550 | Annually | Agentforce for Sales, Spiff, Sales Planning, Maps, Slack Enterprise+, 2.75 million Flex Credits per org per year |

In pounds, Starter Suite is £20 and Pro Suite £80. Salesforce labels the Starter and Pro prices as starting prices and adds "Transaction fees apply" on its [small business editions page](https://www.salesforce.com/editions-pricing/small-business/). The pricing page also says it is "provided for information purposes only and subject to change", and that detailed pricing comes from a sales representative.

### What changed in September 2026

On 3 September 2026 Salesforce [announced new editions](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/salesforce-simplifies-editions-2026/): Core at $195, Advanced at $395 and Max at $550. Max replaces Agentforce 1 Edition at the same price. Salesforce measures Core against the legacy Enterprise Edition and Advanced against the legacy Unlimited Edition, and the pricing page no longer lists those two. Every new edition bundles Slack, Tableau Next and the Premier Success Plan, which Salesforce otherwise sells at [30% of net license fees](https://www.salesforce.com/services/success-plans/).

Before that, Salesforce raised list prices of Enterprise and Unlimited editions by an average of 6% on 1 August 2025, and said there were no planned changes for Starter and Pro ([Salesforce news, 2025](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/pricing-update-2025/)). If a comparison site still lists Essentials, Professional, Enterprise or Unlimited, it describes the old line-up. Existing contracts on those editions can still exist, so a renewal quote may use names that no longer appear on the pricing page.

## Which edition you actually need

Find the most demanding thing on your list. That row sets your edition.

| If you need | Lowest edition | Per user per month |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| A CRM for one or two people at no cost | Free Suite | 0 |
| A third user, sales flows, lead routing, email marketing | Starter Suite | 25 |
| Monthly billing without an annual commitment | Starter Suite | 25 |
| Quoting and forecasting, deeper customization | Pro Suite | 100 |
| Premier Success support, Slack Business+, Tableau Next | Core | 195 |
| The option to add Agentforce AI | Core plus add-on | 195 + 125 |
| More security tooling, backup and recovery, more data capacity | Advanced | 395 |
| Agentforce, Sales Planning and Spiff in one edition | Max | 550 |

**Is Starter Suite enough, or should you go straight to Pro?** Starter is enough when your process is a pipeline of leads and opportunities with email in sync and some lead routing. Move to Pro when you need quoting or forecasting in the CRM, or more customization and automation than the Starter flows give you. The step costs $75 per user per month, or $900 per user per year, and it moves you from monthly billing to an annual contract. If you are unsure, start on Starter monthly. Changing editions later is easier than getting out of a contract you signed too early.

## Add-ons, AI and credits

The edition price is per user. Several costs sit on top of it.

| Item | Listed price | Note |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Agentforce for Sales add-on | from $125 per user per month | Unmetered employee use; can be added to Core and above |
| Agentforce user license | $5 per user per month | Requires Flex Credits |
| Flex Credits | $500 per 100,000 credits | 20 credits per standard Agentforce action, 30 per voice action |
| Conversations | $2 per conversation | Alternative consumption model |
| Sales Programs | from $100 per user per month | Included in Advanced and Max |
| Revenue Intelligence | from $220 per user per month | |
| Revenue Cloud | from $200 per user per month | |
| Partner Relationship Management | from $10 per login per month | |
| Premier Success Plan | 30% of net license fees | Included in Core, Advanced and Max |

Sources: [Sales Cloud pricing](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/pricing/), [Agentforce pricing](https://www.salesforce.com/agentforce/pricing/), [success plans](https://www.salesforce.com/services/success-plans/).

The credit arithmetic is simpler than it looks. One credit costs half a cent at list price, so a standard Agentforce action costs $0.10 and a thousand actions cost $100. The credits included per org, not per user, are worth $2,500 a year in Core (500,000), $5,000 in Advanced (1 million) and $13,750 in Max (2.75 million) at that rate. Salesforce offers three ways to buy consumption: pre-purchase, pre-commit with monthly billing against a baseline, and pay as you go.

Searches for "Salesforce Sales Planning pricing" usually end at Max: Sales Planning is listed as part of that edition, not as a separate line on the Sales Cloud pricing page.

## What a team actually pays

License fees only, at list price, per year:

| Edition | 3 users | 5 users | 10 users |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Starter Suite ($25) | $900 | $1,500 | $3,000 |
| Pro Suite ($100) | $3,600 | $6,000 | $12,000 |
| Core ($195) | $7,020 | $11,700 | $23,400 |
| Advanced ($395) | $14,220 | $23,700 | $47,400 |
| Max ($550) | $19,800 | $33,000 | $66,000 |

A worked case: an eight-person consultancy on Pro Suite pays $9,600 a year. The partners now want Agentforce in the CRM. AI can only be added from Core upward, so the team moves to Core ($18,720, with 500,000 Flex Credits for the org) and adds Agentforce for Sales at $125 per user ($12,000). The bill goes from $9,600 to $30,720 a year, more than three times as much, before any implementation work. Max for the same eight people would cost $52,800 and includes the AI plus 2.75 million credits.

The license is usually not the whole budget. Salesforce's own pages don't publish implementation prices, so get these quoted before you sign:

- **Setup and data migration** by an implementation partner or your own admin.
- **Administration.** Someone has to maintain flows, fields, permissions and reports.
- **Integrations** with your email, accounting or website, if they are not covered by your edition.
- **Transaction fees,** which the Starter and Pro listings mention without a figure.

## Is Salesforce worth it for a small business?

It depends on which edition your process needs. Free Suite and Starter Suite are priced like most small-business CRMs. A team that needs quoting and forecasting inside the CRM, or that expects to grow into a large sales organization with an admin, gets a platform built for that growth.

The cost question starts at Pro Suite. From there you pay per user under an annual contract, and the Agentforce add-on and included Flex Credits start at Core. For a five-person service business that mostly needs contacts, deals and its client conversations in one place, those editions buy a lot of capacity it will not use. The [Salesforce alternative comparison](/compare/salesforce-alternative) and [HubSpot vs. Salesforce](/compare/hubspot-vs-salesforce) go through the options for that case.

## Frequently asked questions about Salesforce pricing

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="How much does Salesforce cost per user?">
Sales Cloud costs $25 (Starter Suite), $100 (Pro Suite), $195 (Core), $395 (Advanced) or $550 (Max) per user per month, and Free Suite costs nothing for up to two users. Only Starter Suite can be billed monthly. AI through Agentforce for Sales adds from $125 per user per month on Core and Advanced.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a free version of Salesforce?">
Yes. Free Suite gives two users lead, opportunity and case management and up to 100 template emails a month, without a contract or credit card. Salesforce says it stays free as long as you log in at least once every 60 days. A third user means moving to Starter Suite.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is Salesforce Starter enough, or should I go straight to Pro?">
Start with Starter unless you already know you need quoting, forecasting or deeper customization. Starter is billed monthly; Pro Suite costs four times as much and needs an annual contract. Upgrading after a few months on Starter costs less than a year of Pro you only half use.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I pay for Salesforce monthly?">
Only for Starter Suite. Pro Suite, Core, Advanced and Max are billed annually, and Salesforce marks Pro Suite as "Contract required". Agentforce consumption can be bought pay as you go or with a monthly pre-commit.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How much does Agentforce cost?">
The Agentforce for Sales add-on starts at $125 per user per month and can be added to Core and above. Usage-based options are Flex Credits at $500 per 100,000 credits (20 credits per standard action, so $0.10 per action) or $2 per conversation. Core and Advanced include 500,000 and 1 million Flex Credits per org per year; Max includes Agentforce for Sales and 2.75 million. Our [Agentforce guide](/blog/agentforce) explains what the agents do.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is Salesforce expensive compared to other CRMs?">
At the entry level, no: Starter Suite at $25 is in the range of other small-business CRMs. From Pro Suite at $100 upward it is more expensive than most CRMs aimed at small teams, and the annual contract and AI add-ons raise the total further. Compare the same team size over twelve months; the [CRM cost guide](/blog/crm-cost) shows how.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

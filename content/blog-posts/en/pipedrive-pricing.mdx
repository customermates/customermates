# Pipedrive Pricing: Every Plan, Limit and Real Team Cost

Pipedrive costs $14, $39, $59 or $79 per seat per month for its Lite, Growth, Premium and Ultimate plans if you pay for the whole year up front. Paid month to month, the same plans cost $24, $49, $79 and $99. Pipedrive charges the same figures in euros and pounds, its prices exclude VAT, and there is no free plan, only a 14-day trial that needs no credit card.

Prices were checked on 8 October 2026 on [Pipedrive's pricing page](https://www.pipedrive.com/en/pricing). Plan limits and billing rules come from Pipedrive's knowledge base, linked at the end. Customermates, which publishes this guide, sells a CRM too; the one section about it is labelled.

## Pipedrive plans and prices

Pipedrive uses the same numbers in US dollars, euros and pounds, so the table shows them once. Read them in your billing currency, before VAT.

| Plan | Billed yearly, per seat per month | Billed yearly, per seat per year | Billed monthly, per seat per month | Extra cost of paying monthly |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Lite | 14 | 168 | 24 | +71% |
| Growth | 39 | 468 | 49 | +26% |
| Premium | 59 | 708 | 79 | +34% |
| Ultimate | 79 | 948 | 99 | +25% |

Pipedrive advertises yearly billing as "up to 42%" cheaper. Only Lite reaches that (14 instead of 24). On Growth and Ultimate the yearly discount is about 20 percent, on Premium about 25 percent. In money, yearly billing saves 120 per seat per year on Lite and Growth and 240 on Premium and Ultimate.

Premium is the plan Pipedrive marks as most popular. Some third-party price lists still show the old plan names (Essential, Advanced, Professional, Power and Enterprise) or older prices. Pipedrive's knowledge base treats those as the previous plan generation, so check the date of any comparison you read.

## Which plan you actually need

The cheapest plan is rarely where a working sales team ends up. Find the most demanding feature you need in this table; that row sets the plan for every seat.

| If you need | Lowest plan | Billed yearly, per seat per month |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Pipeline, contacts, calendar, AI-assisted report building | Lite | 14 |
| Two-way email sync with open and click tracking | Growth | 39 |
| Automations and email sequences | Growth | 39 |
| WhatsApp conversations in Pipedrive | Growth | 39 |
| Deal and product revenue forecast reports | Growth | 39 |
| A shared team inbox, or several synced mailboxes per person | Premium | 59 |
| Chatbot, live chat and web forms (LeadBooster) without an add-on fee | Premium | 59 |
| Proposals, contracts and e-signatures (Smart Docs) without an add-on fee | Premium | 59 |
| AI email writing and summaries | Premium | 59 |
| Custom permission sets, required fields and formula fields | Premium | 59 |
| Phone support | Premium | 59 |
| Security rules, device history and audit log | Ultimate | 79 |
| A sandbox account for testing | Ultimate | 79 |
| Email and phone number enrichment | Ultimate | 79 |

Some things don't affect the choice because every plan has them: native Android and iOS apps, SAML single sign-on, API access, webhooks and the Pipedrive MCP server for AI assistants.

In practice, Lite suits a founder or a two-person team that wants a deal board and keeps email in the mailbox. Growth is the realistic entry for a team that wants its email inside the CRM. Premium is for teams that capture inbound leads on their website or share a mailbox such as sales@. Ultimate is for organizations whose IT or audit requirements call for access rules and an audit log.

## Usage limits on each plan

Every plan has hard limits. The automation, sequence, field and team inbox limits apply to the whole company, so they do not grow when you add seats.

| Limit | Lite | Growth | Premium | Ultimate |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Leads and deals (per company, max. 300,000) | 2,500 × seats | 5,000 × seats | 15,000 × seats | 20,000 × seats |
| Custom fields (per company) | 30 | 100 | 300 | 500 |
| Reports (per seat) | 15 | 50 | 250 | 500 |
| Automations (per company) | none | 50 | 150 | 250 |
| Email sequences (per company) | none | 5 | 25 | 50 |
| Synced email accounts (per seat) | none | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| Team inboxes (per company) | none | none | 1 | 5 |

When you reach a limit, Pipedrive blocks you from adding more of that item manually. You can archive or delete old records, buy a top-up, or move to a higher plan. A ten-person team on Growth shares 50 automations and 5 sequences, which is tight if each salesperson wants their own follow-up sequence.

## Add-ons and what they cost

Add-ons can be booked on any plan. Pipedrive's pricing page lists starting prices in euros; the final price depends on the tier and volume you choose.

| Add-on | Listed from (EUR) | What it covers | Included in |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| LeadBooster | 32.50 | Chatbot, live chat, web forms and Prospector | Premium, Ultimate |
| Smart Docs | 32.50 | Proposals, quotes and contracts with e-signatures | Premium, Ultimate |
| Projects | 16 | Project delivery after a deal is won | Premium, Ultimate |
| Campaigns | 13.33 | Email marketing campaigns | No plan |
| Web Visitors | 41 | Shows which companies visit your website | No plan |

One number helps with the add-on question: the step from Growth to Premium costs 240 per seat per year on yearly billing (20 a month, in your billing currency), or 360 per seat per year on monthly billing. For five seats that is 1,200 a year. If the only Premium feature you want is LeadBooster or Smart Docs, compare that step with the add-on price for the tier you need.

## Billing rules that change the bill

These rules come from Pipedrive's support articles. Once a team grows, shrinks or changes plan, they move the bill more than the list price does.

- **Every seat is billed, assigned or not.** Pipedrive charges per seat. A seat you keep open for a new hire costs the same as a seat someone uses.
- **One plan for the whole account.** Pipedrive bills new seats on the plan the account already has, and its billing articles describe one plan per company account. In practice you can't keep four people on Growth and put only the sales lead on Premium.
- **New seats are charged at once.** If there is no open seat, adding a user creates one, billed immediately and prorated for the rest of the billing cycle.
- **Upgrades apply now, downgrades later.** An upgrade takes effect immediately, with a credit for the unused part of the old plan. A downgrade takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle, which on yearly billing can be months away.
- **Yearly billing is prepaid and not refundable.** You pay the full year at the start. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the term but get no money back. A switch from yearly to monthly billing only starts when the yearly term ends.
- **Monthly billing can be cancelled at any time.** Cancellation takes effect on the next payment date.
- **Free implementation help above US$400 a year.** Pipedrive's pricing FAQ says implementation is free when you buy a plan worth more than that.

## Contract and data location

Customers in the EU contract with Pipedrive's EU company in Estonia, and a data processing addendum is available. For subprocessors outside the EEA, Pipedrive says it relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. Pipedrive hosts on Amazon Web Services in several regions, including Frankfurt and Dublin for EU customers, but it does not guarantee a specific data center; you confirm yours with Pipedrive support. If your data protection rules require a fixed storage location, settle that before you sign a yearly contract.

## What a team actually pays

Plan prices only, before VAT and add-ons:

| Team and plan | Billed yearly (per year) | Billed monthly (per year) | Difference |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 3 seats on Growth | 1,404 | 1,764 | 360 |
| 5 seats on Growth | 2,340 | 2,940 | 600 |
| 5 seats on Premium | 3,540 | 4,740 | 1,200 |
| 10 seats on Premium | 7,080 | 9,480 | 2,400 |
| 20 seats on Ultimate | 18,960 | 23,760 | 4,800 |

A concrete case: a five-person consultancy on Growth, billed yearly, pays 2,340 a year. The team now wants one shared inbox for hello@. Team inboxes start on Premium, and the upgrade applies to all five seats, so the bill rises to 3,540. That is 1,200 a year for one mailbox feature. If the team also buys a sixth seat now for a hire who starts in three months, that seat costs another 708 for the year, used or not.

## How to price Pipedrive for your team

  <Step title="List the features you can't work without">
    Look each one up in the plan table above. The highest plan on your list is the plan for every seat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Count seats, not people">
    Include seats you plan to hold open for new hires or freelancers, because Pipedrive bills them too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the company-wide limits">
    Automations, sequences, custom fields and team inboxes are counted per company. If your process needs more, plan for a top-up or the next plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Price the add-ons against the next plan">
    Get the add-on price for your volume and compare it with the Premium step of 240 per seat per year.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the billing cycle last">

## Frequently asked questions about Pipedrive pricing

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="How much does Pipedrive cost per month?">
Billed yearly, Pipedrive costs $14 (Lite), $39 (Growth), $59 (Premium) or $79 (Ultimate) per seat per month. Billed monthly, the same plans cost $24, $49, $79 and $99. Pipedrive charges the same figures in euros and pounds, before VAT. A team that wants its email inside the CRM needs at least Growth, because email sync and automations start there.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Pipedrive have a free plan?">
No. Pipedrive offers a 14-day free trial without a credit card, and you can invite as many colleagues to the trial as you like. After that you need a paid plan. If you need a CRM that stays free, our guide to [free CRM software](/blog/free-crm) compares the options.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can different users be on different Pipedrive plans?">
Not within one company account. Pipedrive bills added seats on the plan the account already has, so all users share one plan. If one person needs a Premium feature, you pay Premium for every seat.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I get a refund if I cancel a yearly Pipedrive plan?">
No. Pipedrive says yearly subscriptions can't be refunded because they are prepaid. After cancelling you keep access until the end of the term. A user with account settings access cancels under Billing with "Cancel my subscription". Export your data first: after the access ends you can no longer reach it, and Pipedrive permanently deletes closed accounts after six months.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is Pipedrive cheaper than HubSpot?">
It depends on team size and plan. HubSpot has free tools for up to two users, which Pipedrive does not. On the paid side, HubSpot's Sales Hub Professional and Enterprise plans require a one-time onboarding fee ($1,500 and $3,500 on [HubSpot's pricing page](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/sales)), while Pipedrive includes implementation help on plans above US$400 a year. The [HubSpot vs. Pipedrive comparison](/compare/hubspot-vs-pipedrive) goes through features and prices side by side.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

# Pipedrive Pricing: Every Plan, Limit and Real Team Cost

Pipedrive pricing runs from $14 to $79 per seat per month for its four plans, Lite, Growth, Premium and Ultimate, when you pay for the whole year up front. Paid month to month, the same plans cost $24, $49, $79 and $99. Pipedrive lists the same figures in euros and pounds, its prices exclude VAT, and there is no free plan, only a 14-day trial that needs no credit card.

Prices were checked on 8 November 2026 on [Pipedrive's pricing page](https://www.pipedrive.com/en/pricing). Plan limits and billing rules come from Pipedrive's knowledge base, linked at the end. No list price changed since the check on 8 October 2026; this update adds Pipedrive's top-ups and its Nova meeting intelligence, corrects which plans include device history and the audit log, and notes that the WhatsApp integration is still a beta. Customermates, which publishes this guide, sells a CRM too; the one section about it is labelled.

## How much does Pipedrive cost per seat?

Pipedrive sells its CRM in four packages, which it calls plans, and prices them per seat. This guide explains what each plan costs, what limits it carries and which bills it produces. Pipedrive lists the same numbers in US dollars, euros and pounds, and its knowledge base says your location decides the billing currency. Read the table in your billing currency, before VAT.

| Plan | Billed yearly, per seat per month | Billed yearly, per seat per year | Billed monthly, per seat per month | Extra cost of paying monthly |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Lite | 14 | 168 | 24 | +71% |
| Growth | 39 | 468 | 49 | +26% |
| Premium | 59 | 708 | 79 | +34% |
| Ultimate | 79 | 948 | 99 | +25% |

Pipedrive advertises yearly billing as "up to 42%" cheaper. Only Lite reaches that (14 instead of 24). On Growth and Ultimate the yearly discount is about 20 percent, on Premium about 25 percent. In money, yearly billing saves 120 per seat per year on Lite and Growth and 240 on Premium and Ultimate.

Premium is the plan Pipedrive marks as most popular. Some third-party price lists still show the old plan names or older prices. Pipedrive's knowledge base maps them to the current plans: Essential became Lite, Advanced became Growth, Professional and Power became Premium, and Enterprise became Ultimate. Check the date of any comparison you read.

Pipedrive publishes the same plans on localized pricing pages, for example in [Dutch (prijs)](https://www.pipedrive.com/nl/pricing) and [Polish (cennik)](https://www.pipedrive.com/pl/pricing); the German edition of this guide (Pipedrive Kosten) works through the euro prices and German VAT.

## Which Pipedrive plan do you actually need?

The cheapest plan is rarely where a working sales team ends up. Find the most demanding feature you need in this table; that row sets the plan for every seat.

| If you need | Lowest plan | Billed yearly, per seat per month |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Pipeline, contacts, calendar, AI-assisted report building | Lite | 14 |
| Two-way email sync with open and click tracking | Growth | 39 |
| Automations and email sequences | Growth | 39 |
| WhatsApp conversations in Pipedrive (beta) | Growth | 39 |
| Forecast view and revenue forecast reports | Growth | 39 |
| A shared team inbox, or several synced mailboxes per person | Premium | 59 |
| Chatbot, live chat, web forms and Prospector (LeadBooster) without an add-on fee | Premium | 59 |
| Smart Docs and Projects without an add-on fee | Premium | 59 |
| Contracts and e-signatures | Premium | 59 |
| AI email writing and summaries | Premium | 59 |
| Custom scoring and company data enrichment | Premium | 59 |
| Custom permission sets, required fields and formula fields | Premium | 59 |
| Phone support | Premium | 59 |
| Security alerts and security rules, extended phone support hours | Ultimate | 79 |
| A sandbox account for testing | Ultimate | 79 |
| Email and phone number enrichment for contacts | Ultimate | 79 |

Some things don't affect the choice because every plan has them: native Android and iOS apps, single sign-on (SSO), device history and audit log, API access, webhooks, Pipedrive Nova meeting intelligence (call briefs, transcripts and CRM update suggestions) and the Pipedrive MCP server for AI assistants, whose usage is subject to token limits by plan.

In practice, Lite suits a founder or a two-person team that wants a deal board for its [sales pipeline](/blog/sales-pipeline) and keeps email in the mailbox. Growth is the realistic entry for a team that wants its email inside the CRM. Premium is for teams that capture inbound leads on their website or share a mailbox such as sales@. Ultimate is for organizations whose IT or audit requirements call for alerts, access rules and extended phone support.

## What are the usage limits on each plan?

Every plan has hard limits. The automation, sequence, field and team inbox limits apply to the whole company, so they do not grow when you add seats.

| Limit | Lite | Growth | Premium | Ultimate |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Leads and deals (per company, max. 300,000) | 2,500 × seats | 5,000 × seats | 15,000 × seats | 20,000 × seats |
| Custom fields (per company) | 30 | 100 | 300 | 500 |
| Reports (per seat) | 15 | 50 | 250 | 500 |
| Automations (per company) | none | 50 | 150 | 250 |
| If/else conditions (per automation) | none | 3 | 10 | 20 |
| Email sequences (per company) | none | 5 | 25 | 50 |
| Synced email accounts (per seat) | none | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| Team inboxes (per company) | none | none | 1 | 5 |
| Teams, visibility groups, permission sets (each, per company) | none | none | 15 | 25 |
| Data enrichment credits (per company) | none | none | 100 | 500 |

When you reach a limit, Pipedrive blocks you from adding more of that item manually and deletes nothing. You can archive or delete old records, buy a top-up, or move to a higher plan. A ten-person team on Growth shares 50 automations and 5 sequences, which is tight if each salesperson wants their own follow-up sequence.

Top-ups raise a single limit without a plan change. Each one adds a fixed block and can be bought in multiples up to a maximum:

| Top-up | Adds | Maximum purchases | Available from |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| API tokens (max. 100 million per company) | 250,000 | 10 | Growth |
| Leads and deals (max. 300,000 per company) | 25,000 | 10 | Growth |
| Custom fields | 50 | 5 | Growth |
| Reports | 50 | 10 | Growth |
| Automations | 50 | 10 | Growth |
| Teams, custom visibility groups and custom permission sets | 10 each | 10 | Premium |

Pipedrive does not publish top-up prices. Its [top-ups article](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/top-ups) says the price depends on monthly or annual billing and that you see the full cost when you select the top-up in Billing. Ask for that figure before you plan around a top-up instead of a plan upgrade.

## What do Pipedrive add-ons cost?

Add-ons can be booked on any plan and follow the plan's billing cycle. Pipedrive's pricing page lists starting prices in euros without naming a billing cycle; the final price depends on the tier and volume you choose.

| Add-on | Listed from (EUR) | What it covers | Included in |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| LeadBooster | 32.50 | Chatbot, live chat, web forms and Prospector | Premium, Ultimate |
| Smart Docs | 32.50 | Document management (see the note below) | Premium, Ultimate |
| Projects | 16 | Project delivery after a deal is won | Premium, Ultimate |
| Campaigns | 13.33 | Email marketing campaigns | No plan |
| Web Visitors | 41 | Shows which organizations visit your website | No plan |

For Web Visitors, Pipedrive's [feature page](https://www.pipedrive.com/en/features/web-visitors-add-on) gives fixed monthly prices by the number of identified organizations: 49 for up to 200, 99 for 201 to 500 and 299 for 501 to 2,000, in euros, with a 14-day free trial. The pricing page shows "from 41" for the same add-on and does not explain the difference.

Smart Docs is described differently on different Pipedrive pages. The pricing page calls it "manage all your documents in one place" and lists "contracts and e-signatures" as a Premium feature. The knowledge base describes Smart Docs as syncing and editing documents from Google Drive, OneDrive or SharePoint, and the older Smart Docs feature page still ties quotes and e-signatures to it under the previous plan names. If contracts with e-signatures are the reason for the add-on, confirm with Pipedrive which one you are buying.

One number helps with the add-on question: the step from Growth to Premium costs 240 per seat per year on yearly billing (20 a month, in your billing currency), or 360 per seat per year on monthly billing. For five seats that is 1,200 a year. If the only Premium feature you want is LeadBooster or Smart Docs, compare that step with the add-on price for the tier you need.

## Which billing rules change the Pipedrive bill?

These rules come from Pipedrive's support articles. Once a team grows, shrinks or changes plan, they move the bill more than the list price does.

- **Every seat is billed, assigned or not.** Pipedrive charges per seat and recommends filling free seats or removing unused ones. A seat you keep open for a new hire costs the same as a seat someone uses.
- **One plan for the whole account.** Pipedrive's seat article says all seats added to an account are on the plan already being billed, and its pricing example multiplies the seat count by one plan price. In practice you can't keep four people on Growth and put only the sales lead on Premium.
- **New seats are charged at once.** If there is no open seat, adding a user creates one, billed immediately and prorated for the rest of the billing cycle.
- **Removed seats leave at renewal.** Removing a seat takes effect at the next renewal date, which on yearly billing is the end of the term.
- **Upgrades apply now, downgrades later.** An upgrade takes effect immediately, with a credit for the unused part of the old plan. A downgrade takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle, which on yearly billing can be months away.
- **Yearly billing is prepaid and not refundable.** You pay the full year at the start. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the term but get no money back. A switch from yearly to monthly billing only starts when the yearly term ends; a switch from monthly to yearly starts at the next billing date.
- **Monthly billing can be cancelled at any time.** Cancellation takes effect on the next payment date.
- **Add-ons and top-ups follow the plan's billing cycle.**
- **VAT is extra.** Prices exclude VAT. Pipedrive bills VAT to EU customers at their member state's rate unless a valid VAT number is provided.
- **Free implementation help above US$400 a year.** Pipedrive's pricing FAQ says implementation is free when you buy a plan worth more than that.

## Where does Pipedrive store your data?

Customers in the EU contract with Pipedrive's EU company in Estonia, and a data processing addendum is available. For service providers outside the EEA, Pipedrive says they are either certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or have signed the EU Commission's standard contractual clauses, and that it does not rely on the Framework as its transfer mechanism. Pipedrive hosts on Amazon Web Services in several regions, including Frankfurt and Dublin for EU customers, but it does not guarantee a specific data center; you confirm yours with Pipedrive support. If your data protection rules require a fixed storage location, settle that before you sign a yearly contract.

Plan prices only, per year, before VAT and add-ons. Billed yearly:

| 1 | 168 | 468 | 708 | 948 |

Billed monthly (twelve payments):

| Seats | Lite | Growth | Premium | Ultimate |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 288 | 588 | 948 | 1,188 |

A concrete case: a five-person consultancy on Growth, billed yearly, pays 2,340 a year. The team now wants one shared inbox for hello@. Team inboxes start on Premium, and the upgrade applies to all five seats, so the bill rises to 3,540. That is 1,200 a year for one mailbox feature. If the team also buys a sixth seat now for a hire who starts in three months, that seat costs another 708 for the year, used or not.

## How do you price Pipedrive for your team?

<Steps>
  <Step title="List the features you can't work without">
    Look each one up in the plan table above. The highest plan on your list is the plan for every seat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Count seats, not people">
    Include seats you plan to hold open for new hires or freelancers, because Pipedrive bills them too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the company-wide limits">
    Automations, sequences, custom fields and team inboxes are counted per company. If your process needs more, plan for a top-up or the next plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Price the add-ons against the next plan">
    Get the add-on price for your volume and compare it with the Premium step of 240 per seat per year.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the billing cycle last">
    Yearly billing is cheaper but prepaid and not refundable. If your headcount or plan could change within a year, start monthly and switch once the setup has settled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently asked questions about Pipedrive pricing

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="How much does Pipedrive cost per month?">
Billed yearly, Pipedrive costs $14 (Lite), $39 (Growth), $59 (Premium) or $79 (Ultimate) per seat per month. Billed monthly, the same plans cost $24, $49, $79 and $99. Pipedrive lists the same figures in euros and pounds, before VAT. A team that wants its email inside the CRM needs at least Growth, because email sync and automations start there.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Pipedrive have a free plan?">
No. Pipedrive offers a 14-day free trial without a credit card, and you can invite as many colleagues to the trial as you like. After that you need a paid plan. If you need a CRM that stays free, our guide to [free CRM software](/blog/free-crm) compares the options.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I extend the Pipedrive free trial?">
Pipedrive does not describe an extended trial on its pricing page or in its trial articles, which speak of a 14-day trial. Without billing details, access is restricted once the trial ends. If you need more time to test, ask Pipedrive support before the trial runs out.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Pipedrive charge per license?">
Pipedrive charges per seat, which is what other vendors call a license. A seat is billed whether or not a user occupies it, so a deactivated user's seat stays on the bill until you remove it, and the removal takes effect at the next renewal date.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a minimum number of Pipedrive seats?">
Pipedrive names no minimum on its pricing page or in its billing articles, and its own example multiplies the seat count by one plan price. The tables above start at one seat.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can different users be on different Pipedrive plans?">
Not within one company account. Pipedrive says all seats added to an account are on the plan already being billed, so all users share one plan. If one person needs a Premium feature, you pay Premium for every seat.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Are automations, reporting and AI included in the Pipedrive price?">
Reporting and AI-assisted report creation are in every plan, with report limits per seat. Automations and sequences start on Growth with company-wide limits; beyond them you buy a top-up or move up a plan. Nova meeting intelligence is included in all plans at no additional cost, and AI email writing and summaries start on Premium.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I get a refund if I cancel a yearly Pipedrive plan?">
No. Pipedrive says yearly subscriptions can't be refunded because they are prepaid. After cancelling you keep access until the end of the term. A user with account settings access cancels under Billing with "Cancel my subscription". Export your data first: after the access ends you can no longer reach it, and Pipedrive permanently deletes accounts that have been closed for six months.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is Pipedrive cheaper than HubSpot?">
It depends on team size and plan. HubSpot's free plan covers up to two users, and Pipedrive has no free plan to match it. On the paid side, HubSpot says Professional and Enterprise buyers are required to purchase onboarding, and its [pricing page](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/sales) does not print the amount, while Pipedrive includes implementation help on plans above US$400 a year. Our [HubSpot pricing guide](/blog/hubspot-pricing) covers its plans, and the [HubSpot vs. Pipedrive comparison](/compare/hubspot-vs-pipedrive) goes through features and prices side by side.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

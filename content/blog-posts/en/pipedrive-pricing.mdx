# Pipedrive Pricing 2026: Every Plan, Add-on and Real Team Cost

**Short answer:** Pipedrive has four plans, Lite, Growth, Premium and Ultimate. With annual billing they cost €14, €39, €59 and €79 per seat per month. With monthly billing the same plans cost €24, €49, €79 and €99. Pipedrive lists the same figures in US dollars and pounds, prices exclude VAT, and there is no free plan, only a 14-day free trial.

All prices on this page come from [Pipedrive's pricing page](https://www.pipedrive.com/pricing) and its [knowledge base](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/what-features-do-the-pipedrive-plans-have), as of September 2026. Pipedrive changes plans and prices from time to time, so confirm the figures there before you buy.

Pipedrive is a well-liked CRM for sales teams: the visual pipeline is easy to adopt and the product stays focused on deals. The per-seat price is only part of the bill, though. Which plan you need depends on when email sync, automations, LeadBooster and permissions switch on, and the billing cadence alone can change the total by a quarter or more.

| Lite     | €14                             | €168                     | €24                              | +71%                    |

Pipedrive advertises annual billing as "up to 42%" cheaper, which is the Lite plan's gap. On the plans most teams buy, the saving is between about a fifth and a quarter.

Premium is the plan Pipedrive marks as most popular. Its annual price is now €59 per seat, so older comparisons that quote €49 for Premium reflect an earlier price.

## What Each Pipedrive Plan Includes

### Lite: €14 per seat per month (annual) or €24 (monthly)

Lite organizes the basics: leads, deals, contacts, calendar and the visual pipeline, AI-assisted report creation, access to the app marketplace, and Pipedrive's Nova meeting assistant.

What Lite leaves out matters more. Full email sync and email tracking, automations, email sequences and forecasting all start on Growth. LeadBooster, Projects and Smart Docs are paid add-ons on Lite. Lite suits a founder or a two-person team that only needs a deal board.

### Growth: €39 per seat per month (annual) or €49 (monthly)

Growth is where Pipedrive becomes a working sales CRM:

- Full two-way email sync with Gmail, Outlook or other providers, with open and click tracking
- Automations and email sequences for follow-ups
- Forecast and subscription reports
- Meeting scheduler and a contact timeline

Growth allows **50 active automations per company**. LeadBooster, Projects and Smart Docs are still paid add-ons, and there is no team inbox or custom permission sets.

### Premium: €59 per seat per month (annual) or €79 (monthly)

Premium bundles the tools that are add-ons on the lower plans:

- LeadBooster (chatbot, live chat, web forms, Prospector) for lead capture and routing
- Projects and Smart Docs, including contracts and e-signatures
- Custom scoring and company data enrichment
- Team inboxes, multiple synced email accounts and AI email tools
- Required and pipeline-specific fields, formula fields and custom permission sets
- Phone support

The active automation limit rises to **150 per company**. If you would otherwise buy LeadBooster or Smart Docs on Growth, Premium is often the cheaper route.

### Ultimate: €79 per seat per month (annual) or €99 (monthly)

Ultimate adds account security rules and alerts (for example password, IP and time-based restrictions), phone number and email enrichment, a sandbox account for testing, extended phone support, and partner discounts. The active automation limit is **250 per company**. There is no plan with unlimited automations.

## Pipedrive Add-ons and What They Cost

Add-ons are billed on the same cycle as your plan. Pipedrive's pricing page lists these starting prices:

| Add-on       | Listed from | What it does                                          | Included in              |
| ------------ | ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| LeadBooster  | €32.50      | Chatbot, live chat, web forms and Prospector          | Premium and Ultimate     |
| Smart Docs   | €32.50      | Proposals, contracts, templates and e-signatures      | Premium and Ultimate     |
| Projects     | €16         | Project delivery after the deal is won                | Premium and Ultimate     |
| Campaigns    | €13.33      | Email marketing campaigns, priced by subscriber tier  | Not included in any plan |
| Web Visitors | €41         | Shows which companies visit your website              | Not included in any plan |

The "from" prices are entry tiers. Campaigns gets more expensive as your subscriber list grows, Web Visitors is priced by the number of unique organizations visiting your site per month, and Prospector usage runs on credits. Check the add-on configurator for the tier you actually need.

## What a Team Actually Pays for Pipedrive

Pipedrive prices scale linearly per seat. Plan prices only, before VAT and add-ons:

| Team and plan         | Annual billing (per year) | Monthly billing (per year) | Difference |
| --------------------- | ------------------------- | -------------------------- | ---------- |
| 5 seats on Growth     | €2,340                    | €2,940                     | €600       |
| 10 seats on Premium   | €7,080                    | €9,480                     | €2,400     |
| 20 seats on Ultimate  | €18,960                   | €23,760                    | €4,800     |

Three things move the real number:

1. **The plan you are pushed into.** A team that needs email sync and follow-up automations cannot stay on Lite. A team that needs lead capture forms or e-signatures pays for add-ons on Growth or moves to Premium.
2. **Billing cadence.** Annual billing is cheaper but charges the full year up front. Monthly billing keeps you flexible at a 25 to 71 percent premium, depending on the plan.
3. **Add-ons that stay separate.** Campaigns and Web Visitors are not in any plan, so an email marketing or website-intent workflow adds its own line to the invoice.

Pipedrive's FAQ also states that implementation help is free on plans billed above US$400 per year, which covers most paying teams.

## Is Pipedrive Worth the Price?

Pipedrive is worth it when the visual pipeline is what your team needs and it will actually use it every day. It is fast to set up, sales reps adopt it without much training, the mobile app is solid, and the marketplace covers most common tools.

It gets expensive when you need everything around the pipeline: shared inboxes, lead capture, documents and permissions all sit on Premium, and email marketing and website visitor tracking remain add-ons even there. Pipedrive is also cloud-only and not open source, so self-hosting is not an option.

## The Bottom Line on Pipedrive Pricing

Pipedrive's list prices are clear, but the plan you need is usually one step higher than the plan you start on. Growth is the minimum for email sync and automations, Premium is where lead capture, documents, team inboxes and permissions come together, and monthly billing adds 25 to 71 percent on top.

Before you commit, price the plan that contains your must-have features, for your real seat count, on the billing cadence you will use.

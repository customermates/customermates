# Copilot for Sales: Sales Agent, Licensing and Cost

Microsoft Copilot for Sales is now called **Sales agent**. It is an Outlook add-in and a Teams app that bring data from Dynamics 365 Sales or Salesforce Sales Cloud into Microsoft 365, where it summarizes email threads, suggests reply drafts and writes meeting recaps. Microsoft's FAQ says it is available as part of a Microsoft 365 Copilot license, and it always needs one of those two CRMs underneath. It is not a CRM itself.

So the real price is a Copilot seat plus a CRM seat plus a qualifying Microsoft 365 plan. Below you will find what each Copilot product actually is, the setup steps, a cost example for five sellers, and a decision table. Customermates publishes this guide and appears in it once, in a labelled section for teams that do not run Dynamics 365 or Salesforce.

## Five things called "Copilot" in sales

Most confusion around this search comes from names. These are the products people mean:

| Name you see | What it is | Where it runs | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Copilot for Sales, now Sales agent | AI that brings CRM data into Microsoft 365 | Outlook add-in, Teams app, Microsoft 365 Copilot chat | Microsoft 365 Copilot license and Dynamics 365 Sales or Salesforce |
| Copilot in Dynamics 365 Sales | AI chat and summaries inside the Dynamics CRM | Dynamics 365 Sales app | A Dynamics 365 Sales license; features differ by plan, see the comparison table on the pricing page |
| Microsoft 365 Copilot | General AI across Word, Excel, Outlook and Teams | Microsoft 365 apps | A qualifying Microsoft 365 plan |
| Copilot Studio | Microsoft's tool to build your own agents, which can call tools on an MCP server | Copilot Studio | Its own licensing; check with Microsoft |
| Copilot CRM, now Homeworks | An unrelated field-service CRM for home-service businesses | Its own app | Its own subscription |

The last row matters if you searched "Copilot CRM": [Homeworks](https://www.home.works) is the former Copilot CRM, a platform for lawn care, landscaping, window cleaning and similar businesses, with scheduling, routes and invoicing. It has nothing to do with Microsoft.

Microsoft's own documentation calls Sales agent "the evolution of the Copilot experience for sales teams" and says it will become the preferred way to reach Copilot assistance inside Dynamics 365 Sales as well.

## What Sales agent does

Sales agent works in three places, according to [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-sales-copilot/sales-m365-copilot-faq):

- **Outlook.** A Sales pane next to the email shows the matching CRM record. It summarizes threads, suggests reply drafts and lets the seller view and edit CRM fields. Summaries appear only for threads longer than 1,000 characters, about 180 words.
- **Teams.** Recorded meetings get recaps with action items, tasks and sales keywords. Microsoft states unlimited call recording and processing hours. Meeting insights need at least one external participant who is a CRM contact.
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot chat.** Sellers ask questions such as "Summarize account Fabrikam" once an admin has configured which CRM tables Sales agent may read.

It respects the access rules of the connected CRM. Admins can expose up to 40 fields per record in the Sales pane.

The documented limits are just as useful for a decision. Sales agent does not support shared mailboxes, Dynamics 365 or Exchange on-premises, or US GCC, GCC High and DoD. Meeting recap and preparation need Exchange Online. Salesforce must be Sales Cloud Professional edition or above. Supported languages and regions are listed in Microsoft's [Copilot international availability report](https://releaseplans.microsoft.com/availability-reports/?report=copilotproductreport).

## How to set up Sales agent

The setup is admin work in two admin centers and in the CRM. It follows Microsoft's install and FAQ pages:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check licenses">Each seller needs a Microsoft 365 Copilot license and a CRM seat. Microsoft's FAQ says a user without the Copilot license sees a message that they do not have the right license.</Step>
  <Step title="Assign CRM roles">In Dynamics 365, admins need System Administrator or System Customizer, and sellers need Salesperson or Sales Manager. Salesforce has its own permission list.</Step>
  <Step title="Deploy the app">Deploy it in the Microsoft 365 admin center for Outlook and in the Teams admin center for Teams. It can take up to 48 hours to appear in Outlook.</Step>
  <Step title="Connect the CRM">Each user consents to connect their work account with their CRM account. Data sharing does not start before that consent.</Step>
  <Step title="Configure Sales chat">Choose the CRM tables and fields Sales agent may use, and set up the account, opportunity and lead summaries.</Step>
  <Step title="Test with one deal">Open an email from a real customer, check that the right record appears, and edit one field. Then record one Teams call with that contact.</Step>
</Steps>

## What Copilot for Sales costs

There is no separate Copilot for Sales price on Microsoft's pages anymore. You pay for the pieces underneath. Prices checked on 8 October 2026 from Microsoft's and Salesforce's US pricing pages:

| Component | Price per user per month | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Microsoft 365 Copilot | $30.00, paid yearly ($31.50 paid monthly on an annual term) | [Microsoft 365 Copilot](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/enterprise) |
| Microsoft 365 Copilot Business, up to 300 users | From $18.00 paid yearly, a promotional price through 31 December 2026 (from $21.00 before) | [Copilot pricing](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/pricing) |
| Dynamics 365 Sales Professional | $65.00, paid yearly | [Dynamics 365 Sales pricing](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics-365/products/sales/pricing) |
| Dynamics 365 Sales Enterprise | $105.00, paid yearly, includes Copilot in Dynamics 365 | same page |
| Dynamics 365 Sales Premium | $150.00, paid yearly | same page |
| Salesforce Sales Cloud Pro Suite | $100.00, billed annually | [Sales Cloud pricing](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/pricing/) |

Microsoft 365 Copilot is sold as an add-on to a qualifying Microsoft 365 plan. The [Copilot Business page](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/business) lists Researcher, Analyst and Facilitator as included agents and does not name Sales agent. If you plan to use the cheaper Business plan, ask Microsoft whether Sales agent is covered before you buy.

**Example for five sellers on Dynamics 365 Sales Professional:** 5 × ($30 + $65) = **$475 a month**, or $5,700 a year on annual commitment. Add the Microsoft 365 plan your company already pays for. On Salesforce Pro Suite the CRM part is $100 instead of $65, so the same team pays $650 a month before Microsoft 365.

## Which option fits your team

| Your situation | Look at | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sellers live in Outlook and Teams, CRM is Dynamics 365 or Salesforce | Sales agent | It puts the existing CRM next to the inbox and the meeting |
| Sellers work mainly inside the Dynamics 365 app | Copilot in Dynamics 365 Sales | It is built into the Dynamics 365 Sales app; no separate add-in rollout |
| CRM is Salesforce and you want the AI inside Salesforce | [Agentforce](/blog/agentforce) | Salesforce's own agents; its pricing page says AI can be added to Core and above |
| You already pay for Microsoft 365 Copilot but have no CRM | Decide on the CRM first | Sales agent has nothing to read without Dynamics 365 or Salesforce |
| Small team on Gmail, or on WhatsApp and LinkedIn as much as email | A CRM with its own AI and inbox | Sales agent covers Outlook and Teams only |

On "is it worth it": Sales agent pays off when sellers already spend the day in Outlook and Teams and the CRM is already the system of record. If you would have to buy the CRM, the Copilot seats and an admin's time just to get email summaries, compare the total cost against a smaller CRM first. The [Microsoft Dynamics alternatives comparison](/compare/microsoft-dynamics-alternative) covers that trade-off.

## Where Customermates fits

Customermates is an open-source, AI-native CRM for small client-facing teams such as consultancies and agencies, built in Germany. It is one option for the last row of the table, and it is not a Sales agent replacement for a company that runs Dynamics 365 and records customer calls in Teams.

A typical setup for a five-person team:

- **Records.** Contacts, organizations and deals, with deal stages as a single-select column and stage weights for a weighted pipeline value. Deals have assigned users, and each person keeps their own saved views, for example "My deals in Proposal".
- **Inbox.** On Pro or higher, the [unified inbox](/features/unified-inbox) links email from Outlook, Gmail or IMAP, plus LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram conversations, to the same records. Pro includes [[commercial.entitlement.pro.includedAccountsPerUser]] connected account per user, Business [[commercial.entitlement.business.includedAccountsPerUser]]. The [Outlook integration](/features/outlook-integration) connects the mailbox server-side; there is nothing to install in Outlook.
- **Mate, in Customermates Cloud.** The in-app assistant uses hosted AI credits. Ask it "Summarize the last thread with Acme and save a reply draft that proposes two meeting slots". Mate sends email immediately when you tell it to send, so ask for a draft when you want to review first. Details are in the [Mate documentation](/docs/app-assistant).
- **A routine, in Customermates Cloud.** Trigger "When something changes", event "Deal updated", under "Changed fields" the Stage column. Instructions: "When a deal moves to Proposal, read the last thread with the contact and save a reply draft. Do not send anything." The routine runs as its owner, with that owner's channels. See [routines](/docs/app-routines).
- **Your own AI client.** If your team already uses Claude or ChatGPT, connect it over MCP with the [custom connector](/docs/connect-custom-connector). Microsoft documents that Copilot Studio agents can connect to an existing MCP server; Customermates has no published guide for that client yet, so test one read before relying on it.

**Cost.** Five users on Pro cost [[commercial.price.pro.monthly.seats.5]] a month, billed monthly, including the inbox and monthly hosted AI credits per active user ([see pricing](/pricing)). Starter at [[commercial.price.starter.monthly]] per user has no inbox. Self-hosting the AGPL core is free of license fees and keeps REST, webhooks and MCP, but has no inbox and no Mate.

**What it does not do.** Customermates does not record or transcribe meetings, has no Outlook, Teams or Word add-in, so CRM details are looked up in Customermates itself, does not write calendar events and has no mobile app. If meeting recaps from Teams are the reason you are looking at Copilot for Sales, stay with Microsoft.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="What is Copilot for Sales?">
The former name of Microsoft's Sales agent: an Outlook add-in and Teams app that connect Microsoft 365 with Dynamics 365 Sales or Salesforce Sales Cloud. It summarizes email threads, suggests reply drafts, writes meeting recaps and lets sellers view and update CRM records without leaving Outlook.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How much does Microsoft Sales Copilot cost?">
Microsoft no longer lists a separate price. Its FAQ says Sales agent is available as part of the Microsoft 365 Copilot license, which costs $30 per user per month paid yearly in the US. You also need a qualifying Microsoft 365 plan and a CRM: Dynamics 365 Sales Professional is $65 per user per month, Enterprise $105, Salesforce Sales Cloud Pro Suite $100, all billed annually. Microsoft 365 Copilot Business starts at $18 paid yearly, but its page does not name Sales agent. Prices checked on 8 October 2026 on the [Microsoft 365 Copilot](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/enterprise), [Dynamics 365 Sales](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics-365/products/sales/pricing) and [Sales Cloud](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/pricing/) pricing pages.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Copilot for Sales work with Salesforce?">
Yes. Sales agent works with Dynamics 365 Sales and Salesforce Sales Cloud, Professional edition and above. Salesforce's own AI for sales is Agentforce, which runs inside Salesforce rather than in Outlook.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does Copilot for Sales work in Outlook?">
Yes, Outlook is its main surface: on Windows from version 2206, on Mac from 16.78, and on the web. It does not support shared mailboxes, and meeting recaps need Exchange Online.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is Microsoft Copilot a CRM?">
No. Copilot is Microsoft's AI assistant brand. Microsoft's CRM is Dynamics 365 Sales, licensed separately. Sales agent needs Dynamics 365 Sales or Salesforce to have any customer data to work with.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What is Copilot CRM?">
Usually one of two things: Microsoft Copilot used with a CRM, as described on this page, or the field-service software formerly called Copilot CRM, now Homeworks, which serves lawn care, landscaping and other home-service businesses.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

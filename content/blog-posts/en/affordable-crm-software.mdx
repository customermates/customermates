# Affordable CRM Software: Entry Prices, Flat Rates and the Fees That Follow

The cheapest CRM that still works for a small team is one of three things: a free plan that fits your headcount, a low per-seat tier paid a year in advance, or a flat-rate plan that covers everyone. On 8 October 2026, HubSpot's free tools covered up to 2 users and the Zoho CRM free edition up to 3. Among the paid tiers here, the lowest list price per seat was €12 a month, for monday CRM Basic (billed annually, at least 3 seats) and for Customermates Starter (billed monthly). HubSpot Sales Hub Starter lists at $20 per seat per month; HubSpot was also showing a time-limited offer of $7 per seat on annual billing. For a team of exactly five, Bitrix24 Basic charges one flat $49 a month for the whole organization on annual billing.

What you end up paying depends less on the sticker price than on four things: how you are billed, seat minimums, one-time fees, and which tier holds the feature you need. Below you will find the yearly cost for five users, the CRMs without per-seat pricing, a checklist for pricing a plan before you sign, and where Customermates, the publisher of this guide, fits. Vendor prices were checked on 8 October 2026 on each vendor's own pricing page, linked at the end.

Billing cadence moves the price more than the choice of vendor. monday CRM Basic costs 50% more billed monthly, and HubSpot's time-limited $7 Starter offer applies to annual billing only; billed monthly, Starter is $20. One-time fees matter too: HubSpot's $1,500 onboarding fee is more than three months of five Professional seats on annual billing. Customermates bills monthly only, so there is no annual discount to weigh against the freedom to drop seats.

## CRMs without per-seat pricing

Most CRMs charge per seat. Two vendors in the table price per package instead, and a self-hosted open-source CRM has no seat count at all.

- **Bitrix24** is free for 1 to 2 users. Basic covers 5 users for $49 a month billed annually ($69 monthly), Standard covers 50 users for $99 ($144 monthly), and Professional 100 users for $199 ($289 monthly). The price is per organization.
- **CentralStationCRM** has a free Starter package for 3 users and 200 contacts. The paid packages cost €24 a month for 3 users, €75 for 10, €149 for 20 and €289 for 40, all excluding VAT. CentralStationCRM says every package has the same features; they differ in users, contacts and storage.
- **A self-hosted open-source CRM** costs no licence fee per person. You pay for the server, backups and the hours someone spends on updates. Our [open-source CRM guide](/blog/open-source-crm) compares the self-hosted options.

A flat rate is cheapest when your team fills its band and most expensive right after you cross it. Monthly cost by team size, with Bitrix24 on annual billing:

| 5 people | $49 (Basic) | €75 (Small Office) | [[commercial.price.starter.monthly.seats.5]] |

At ten or twenty people, a package that fits is clearly cheaper than paying per seat. Per-seat pricing wins when headcount changes often or when a few people need access only for a while, because you add and remove one seat at a time.

## What turns a cheap plan into an expensive one

| Cost driver | Example from the vendors' pages | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annual prepayment | monday CRM Basic is €12 per seat billed annually and €18 billed monthly | Prepay only for seats you are sure to keep for a year |
| Seat minimum | monday CRM Basic, Standard and Pro each require at least 3 seats | A solo user pays for three |
| One-time onboarding | HubSpot Professional requires $1,500 once, Enterprise $3,500 | Add the fee to year one before you compare |
| Feature in a higher tier | Pipedrive's sequences start on Growth; its LeadBooster with web forms comes with Premium and Ultimate | Price the tier that holds all your must-haves |
| User cap on a free plan | 2 users on HubSpot, 3 on Zoho CRM, 1 to 2 on Bitrix24 | Expect the paid tier to start with your next hire |
| Package jump | A fourth user moves CentralStationCRM from €24 to €75 a month | Check the next band, not only the current one |
| Taxes | CentralStationCRM, Pipedrive and Less Annoying CRM list prices before tax; Zoho adds local taxes | Compare gross prices if you cannot reclaim VAT |

## Check the price before you commit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Count seats for the next twelve months">
    Include the next hire and any freelancer who needs access. A free plan for 2 users is no help if you will be four people by spring.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the lowest tier with all your must-haves">
    Write down what you cannot work without, such as email sync, sequences or a second pipeline, and look up the first tier that has all of it. That tier is your real entry price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Price both billing options">
    Multiply the seat price by seats and twelve months, once for annual and once for monthly billing. Prepay only if headcount is stable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add one-time fees, minimums and tax">
    Onboarding fees, seat minimums and prices quoted before VAT belong in the same sum.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the way out">
    Ask which file format the export uses, whether it covers every record type, and whether there is an API. A cheap CRM that is hard to leave gets expensive at the next price change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run real deals through the trial">
    Pipedrive and monday CRM give 14 days, Less Annoying CRM and CentralStationCRM 30 days, and Customermates [[commercial.trial.days]] days. Put twenty current deals in and see whether the team keeps them up to date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### The lowest-cost ways to run it

- **Starter with your own AI client.** Create a key under **My Profile › API & Connectors › Add** and pick Claude or ChatGPT under **Quick connections**. The client then reads and updates records through [MCP](/docs/mcp), and its model calls run on your own AI provider.
- **Pay only for active people.** A seat is a member with status **Active**. Set someone who no longer needs access to **Inactive** under **My Company › Members**, and the seat count drops, prorated on your next bill. That also pauses the routines they own.
- **Self-host the open-source core.** It runs with Docker Compose and PostgreSQL, counts no seats and bills nothing. You pay for the server and an email delivery account, and you run updates and backups yourself. Self-hosted, there is no inbox, no Mate, no routines and no hosted credits; REST, webhooks and MCP stay. The [self-hosting guide](/docs/self-hosting) has the setup.

## FAQ

<Faq>
<FaqItem question="Is there a completely free CRM?">
Yes, within limits. HubSpot's free tools cover up to 2 users without a credit card, the Zoho CRM free edition covers 3 users with no time limit, Bitrix24 Free covers 1 to 2 users, and CentralStationCRM's free Starter covers 3 users and 200 contacts. Each cap is where paying starts. An open-source CRM you host yourself has no licence fee, but the server and the upkeep still cost money or time. The [free CRM guide](/blog/free-crm) compares the free plans in detail.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What is the cheapest CRM for a team of five?">
On 8 October 2026, the lowest yearly totals for five users at list prices among the plans in the table above were Bitrix24 Basic at $588 on annual billing, followed by monday CRM Basic at €720 on annual billing and Customermates Starter at [[commercial.price.starter.monthly.seats.5.months.12]] on monthly billing. HubSpot Sales Hub Starter costs $1,200 a year at its $20 list price, or $420 while HubSpot's time-limited annual-billing offer of $7 per seat runs. Check that the tier has your must-haves before you compare totals.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is there a CRM without per-seat pricing?">
Yes. Bitrix24 charges per organization: Basic covers 5 users and Standard 50. CentralStationCRM sells packages for 3, 10, 20 and 40 users at one monthly price each. A self-hosted open-source CRM has no seat count; you pay for hosting and upkeep instead.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Which CRMs cost less than Pipedrive?">
Pipedrive Lite costs €14 per seat per month on annual billing, and there is no free plan. Lower listed seat prices include monday CRM Basic (€12 on annual billing, at least 3 seats) and Customermates Starter ([[commercial.price.starter.monthly]], billed monthly). HubSpot Sales Hub Starter lists at $20, with a time-limited offer of $7 on annual billing when checked. If you rely on Pipedrive's sequences, you are comparing against Growth at €39, and the cheaper tools must cover that too. The [Pipedrive pricing breakdown](/blog/pipedrive-pricing) lists every plan.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What does Zoho CRM cost?">
The free edition covers up to 3 users and Zoho calls it free forever. The paid editions are Standard, Professional, Enterprise and Ultimate. Zoho shows their prices in your region's currency, adds local taxes, and says annual plans save up to 34%, so check the figure for your country on Zoho's pricing page.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I start with Excel instead of a CRM?">
For one or two people with a short client list, yes. Keep one workbook per record type, so you can import it later without retyping: Customermates imports `.xlsx` files one record type at a time. Import organizations first, then contacts, then deals, because link columns are matched by record name. CSV files are not accepted and need to be saved as `.xlsx` first. A [ready-made Excel CRM template](/blog/excel-crm-template) has the layout.
</FaqItem>
</Faq>

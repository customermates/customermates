# Attio Pricing: Plans, Workspace Price, Credits and What 1, 3 and 5 Users Pay

Attio pricing starts with a free plan for up to three users. Paid plans are priced per workspace: Plus costs $40 and Pro $100 a month when billed annually ($50 and $125 billed monthly), each with one user included, and every further user adds $35 (Plus) or $79 (Pro) a month on annual billing, or $44 and $99 on monthly billing. The top plan, Ultra, replaced Enterprise and costs $1,000 a month, billed annually, with five users included. Attio lists the same plans in euros and pounds, and its AI features and workflows run on monthly credits that every plan includes.

We checked every Attio figure on 12 November 2026 on Attio's [US dollar](https://attio.com/pricing/usd), [euro](https://attio.com/pricing/eur) and [pound](https://attio.com/pricing/gbp) pricing pages and in five help-center and program pages linked at the end. We converted no currency ourselves, and Attio's pricing page does not say whether its prices include tax. Older comparison pages and earlier versions of this guide quote a per-seat price and an Enterprise plan that no longer match what Attio lists: the plans article says Ultra replaced Enterprise for new workspaces starting in November 2026. Customermates, which publishes this guide, sells a CRM too; the one section about it is labelled.

## How much does Attio cost per month?

Attio CRM pricing is per workspace. Each paid plan has a base price that includes one user (Ultra: five), and every further user adds a monthly fee. The pricing page shows two figures for each price, one for annual billing and one for monthly billing, with a Monthly and an Annual switch. Read the column that matches how you will pay.

| Plan | Base price per workspace, billed annually | Base price, billed monthly | Users included | Each extra user, annual / monthly |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Free | $0 | $0 | up to 3 | not available |
| Plus | $40 | $50 | 1 | $35 / $44 |
| Pro | $100 | $125 | 1 | $79 / $99 |
| Ultra | $1,000 | not offered | 5 | $189 |

All figures are US dollars per month. Attio's pricing page puts "about 20% less" on annual billing for Plus and Pro: $50 to $40 and $125 to $100 are 20 percent, and the extra-user fees ($44 to $35, $99 to $79) are about 20 percent. Ultra is billed annually only, so $1,000 a month is a $12,000 yearly commitment. Our yearly totals further down are our own arithmetic (base price plus extra users, times 12). If you pay annually, Attio lets admins preview an upcoming invoice up to 90 days before renewal, so check the exact amount there.

Attio shows euros and pounds as separate price lists. The numbers are not the dollar numbers with a symbol swapped:

| Plan | EUR, base annual / monthly | EUR, extra user annual / monthly | GBP, base annual / monthly | GBP, extra user annual / monthly |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Free | €0 | not available | £0 | not available |
| Plus | €35 / €45 | €31 / €39 | £30 / £38 | £27 / £34 |
| Pro | €85 / €110 | €74 / €93 | £70 / £90 | £59 / £74 |
| Ultra | €850, annual only | €167 | £750, annual only | £142 |

All figures are per month. Attio's pricing page picks one currency page by location (when we opened it on 12 November 2026 it showed euros), and [attio.com/pricing/usd](https://attio.com/pricing/usd), [attio.com/pricing/eur](https://attio.com/pricing/eur) and [attio.com/pricing/gbp](https://attio.com/pricing/gbp) open the three lists directly. Its billing article says you can pay in USD, GBP or EUR.

What else the official pages say about the Attio cost:

- **Trial.** New workspaces start with a 14-day trial of Pro. At the end an admin can continue on any plan, Free included.
- **Startup discount.** Venture-backed startups with up to 10 users get 80% off the annual Pro plan and credits in the first year and 30% off in the second year, if they apply within 90 days of creating the workspace. The startup page adds that the company must be incorporated less than 5 years ago.
- **Payment.** All plans pay by credit card. Pro and Ultra can be invoiced, and Attio says it accepts PO, ACH, check or wire for those.
- **Tax.** The pricing page does not say whether prices include VAT or sales tax. The billing article lets admins save a VAT number in the billing details. Ask Attio or read a preview invoice before you budget a tax-inclusive total.
- **Governance add-on.** Pro can add SSO, SCIM, audit logs and admin-only exports for $30 a user a month billed annually (€25, £22), on every user in the workspace. Ultra includes them.

Comparison pages in search results quote other numbers for Plus and Pro, some of them per-seat prices from before November 2026. Check the date on any page you read, and compare it with Attio's own page.

## Is Attio free forever?

Attio's Free plan costs nothing, and neither the pricing page nor the help center names an end date for it. They do not promise it stays free either: the pricing FAQ describes it as the plan "for individuals getting started". Treat it as a plan with limits, not as a lifetime guarantee. What the Free plan includes, according to the pricing page and Attio's plans article:

- **3 users.** More people need a paid plan for everyone.
- **3 objects.** Companies and People, plus one more standard object: Deals, Users or Workspaces. Free has no custom objects.
- **50,000 records.**
- **Email.** One synced mailbox per member, 10 emails at once and 200 sends per user per month. Every email carries "Sent with Attio".
- **No access controls.** The whole workspace is visible and editable to all users.
- **Three Insight reports.**
- **AI credits.** 100 seat credits per seat and 250 workspace credits per month, with no option to buy more.
- **Apps and API.** Attio says the App Store apps and API access are on all plans, and its comparison table marks Ask Attio, AI agents, workflows and the MCP server on all four plans too, within the credits.

A new workspace does not start on Free. It starts on the 14-day Pro trial, which Attio's help center says gives "access to all Pro features". When the trial ends, an admin chooses a plan, and Free is one of the options, so what you tried during the trial, such as custom objects or sequences, is not part of the plan you pick if it is Free.

## Which Attio plan do you need?

The plan is set by the most demanding thing anyone on the team needs, because Attio's help center says all workspace members must be on the same plan. These are the limits Attio publishes:

| Limit | Free | Plus | Pro | Ultra |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Users | up to 3 | up to 10 | unlimited | unlimited |
| Objects | up to 3 | up to 5 | 12, then extra objects at $400 to $500 a month each | unlimited |
| Records | 50,000 | 250,000 | 1,000,000 | 3,000,000 |
| Emails sent per user per month | 200 | 1,000 | unlimited | unlimited |
| Emails in one bulk send | 10 | 50 | 50 | 50 |
| Synced email and calendar accounts per user | 1 | 1 | 2 | 3 or more |
| File storage | 50 GB | 50 GB | unlimited | unlimited |
| Teams | none | none | up to 10 | unlimited |
| Access permissions | none | basic | advanced | advanced |

Records beyond the limit are sold in blocks of 1 million records on Pro and Ultra, at $240 to $300 a month per million. Find the feature you cannot work without in the next table; that row sets the plan for every user.

| If you need | Lowest plan |
| --- | --- |
| Automatic enrichment of companies and people, Ask Attio, workflows, API and webhooks, MCP server | Free, within the credits |
| Employee count and ARR enrichment, funnel reports, "Sent with Attio" removed, basic workspace permissions | Plus |
| Custom objects and custom relationship attributes | Pro |
| Formula attributes and record templates | Pro |
| Email sequences | Pro |
| Call recording and AI call insights (Call Intelligence) | Pro |
| Teams, plus access rules by team and by person | Pro |
| Payment by invoice | Pro |
| Single sign-on, SCIM provisioning and audit logs | Ultra, or Pro with the Governance add-on |
| SQL access through the API and MCP, unlimited objects and teams | Ultra |

In practice, Free fits one to three people who want people, companies and one pipeline object with enrichment. Plus fits four to ten people whose data fits five objects and who do not need sequences. Pro is where a team ends up when it wants its own record types, automation with sequences or recorded calls. Ultra is for organizations with security and admin requirements, and it is the plan Attio sells instead of Enterprise: its plans article lists "Ultra plan (formerly Enterprise)" and says Ultra replaced Enterprise for new workspaces starting in November 2026. Ultra is available to buy without talking to sales. Attio lists migration help on Plus, Pro and Ultra as "Talk to sales" and publishes no price for it, so an unknown cost is not zero.

## How do Attio credits work, and what do they cost?

Attio meters AI features and workflow automations in credits, and the help center describes two kinds. Seat credits are personal: each seat gets a monthly allotment for Ask Attio that cannot be shared with other members. Workspace credits are shared by everyone and cover workflows and all AI features, Ask Attio included.

| Credits per month | Free | Plus | Pro | Ultra |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Seat credits per seat (Ask Attio only) | 100 | 500 | 1,000 | 2,500 |
| Workspace credits per workspace (shared) | 250 | 1,500 | 10,000 | 20,000 |
| Extra workspace credit packs | not available | available | available | available |

How they interact, in Attio's words: Ask Attio uses seat credits first, and when they run out it continues on workspace credits unless an admin has switched that off. Admins can cap how much of the workspace pool Ask Attio may use. A member who exhausts both pools cannot use Ask Attio until the credits renew, which happens monthly on the billing date (on annual plans, on the same day of each month as the annual billing date).

What a credit buys, from Attio's own examples (Ask Attio on its Auto model):

| Task | Credits |
| --- | ---: |
| Prepare for your next meeting | 10 to 20 |
| Summarize your last call | 10 to 20 |
| Draft a follow-up email from the last meeting | 15 to 20 |
| Daily briefing | 30 to 50 |
| Find customers who asked for a feature and draft individual emails | 50 to 80 |

Workflows draw on workspace credits. In Attio's new workflows, triggers, logic and data lookups are free, and blocks that write data, send or receive something externally or use AI cost credits: creating or updating a record, adding it to a list or creating a task is 1 credit each, and AI blocks are billed by token usage. AI attributes cost 1 credit per record to recalculate, or 10 per record for the web agent type. Emails sent directly from sequences do not use workspace credits; credits are only consumed when a workflow enrolls people.

Our arithmetic: a Plus seat's 500 credits cover about 25 to 50 prompts like the meeting-prep example if nothing else uses them (500 divided by 20 or by 10), and a Free seat's 100 credits cover 5 to 10. Heavy Ask Attio use, web research or AI blocks in workflows will run through the allowance faster, because Attio says cost varies with query complexity, the CRM data needed, the model chosen and whether web research is involved.

Extra workspace credits are sold in packs on paid plans (the plans article says the Free plan has no option to buy more). The pricing page prints each pack twice, as "/mo" figures: the first is the price it shows under its Monthly setting, the second under Annual.

| Extra credits per month | USD, monthly / annual | EUR, monthly / annual | GBP, monthly / annual |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| +5,000 | $85 / $70 | €85 / €70 | £70 / £55 |
| +10,000 | $150 / $120 | €150 / €120 | £120 / £95 |
| +25,000 | $330 / $260 | €285 / €225 | £250 / £200 |
| +50,000 | $595 / $475 | €515 / €410 | £445 / £360 |

## How do Attio seats and billing work?

These rules come from Attio's billing and member articles and its pricing page. Once a team grows, shrinks or changes plan, they move the bill more than the list price does.

- **One plan per workspace.** All members share a plan. Each workspace has its own subscription and is billed separately, so a person in two workspaces takes a seat in each.
- **The base price covers the first users.** On the pricing page Plus and Pro include 1 user and Ultra includes 5; every user beyond that is a paid seat with its own monthly fee. Attio's billing article says the bill depends on your plan, your seats and your add-ons.
- **A seat is a place, not a login.** Attio defines a seat as a paid spot occupied by one active member or one pending invite. Billing follows the seat count, not how many people use the CRM. If you have 10 seats you can have up to 10 members and invites together.
- **Add seats before you invite.** If you need more people than seats, an admin adds seats first: Workspace settings, Billing, the plus next to Seats.
- **Suspending a member frees the seat, but not the charge.** Attio says to lower the seat count if you will not refill the seat, otherwise you keep paying for it. To replace someone, suspend the old member, then invite the new one.
- **Add-ons scale with seats.** The Governance add-on on Pro is billed per seat, so each added user raises its cost too.
- **Billing interval is set per workspace.** It is monthly or annual, and Attio bills at the start of the interval. Ultra is annual only.
- **Upgrades apply now, downgrades later.** An upgrade is billed pro rata immediately. A downgrade takes effect at your next renewal. Switching from monthly to annual credits the unused part of the monthly payment toward the annual price.
- **Canceling ends access at the end of the billing period.** Export your data first. Deleting a workspace removes access immediately and cannot be undone.

Attio's pages do not state a refund rule for annual plans, whether prices include tax, volume discounts or the price of migration help. Ask before you commit to an annual payment.

## How much does Attio cost for 1, 3 and 5 users?

Base price plus the extra users, times 12, before tax and with no credit packs or add-ons. These are our own calculations from the figures in the tables above: three users on Plus billed annually are $40 + 2 x $35 = $110 a month, or $1,320 a year. Free is listed for one and three users because it caps at three. Ultra includes five users, so it costs the same for one, three or five.

**US dollars (USD per year):**

| Users | Free | Plus, billed annually | Plus, billed monthly | Pro, billed annually | Pro, billed monthly | Ultra, billed annually |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | $0 | $480 | $600 | $1,200 | $1,500 | $12,000 |
| 3 | $0 | $1,320 | $1,656 | $3,096 | $3,876 | $12,000 |
| 5 | not available | $2,160 | $2,712 | $4,992 | $6,252 | $12,000 |

**Euros (EUR per year):**

| Users | Free | Plus, billed annually | Plus, billed monthly | Pro, billed annually | Pro, billed monthly | Ultra, billed annually |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | €0 | €420 | €540 | €1,020 | €1,320 | €10,200 |
| 3 | €0 | €1,164 | €1,476 | €2,796 | €3,552 | €10,200 |
| 5 | not available | €1,908 | €2,412 | €4,572 | €5,784 | €10,200 |

A concrete case: a five-person agency needs a pipeline, email sync and Ask Attio, and Plus's five objects are enough. It pays $2,160 a year billed annually, or $2,712 billed monthly. Then it wants its own record types for projects and partners, plus email sequences. Custom objects and sequences are Pro features and the plan applies to all five users, so the bill rises to $4,992 billed annually, an extra $2,832 a year. If that agency also needs single sign-on, it adds the Governance add-on on Pro at $30 per user per month ($1,800 a year for five users) or moves to Ultra at $12,000 a year. A team of only three pays nothing on Free, but when a fourth person joins, everyone moves to Plus.

## Is Attio CRM good, and why choose it over another CRM?

Attio is a serious product for teams that want a flexible data model and AI built into the CRM, and whether it is good for you depends on the job. We have not rated it, and we do not rely on review sites for prices. From Attio's own pricing and help pages, these are the reasons a team picks it:

- **A configurable data model.** Objects, attributes and relationships, with custom objects from Pro.
- **Enrichment on every plan.** Companies and people fill in automatically, Free included, with more attributes on higher plans.
- **Email and calendar sync.** Interaction dates such as first and last contact are filled from synced mail and calendars.
- **AI and automation on every plan.** Ask Attio, workflows, an API and an MCP server are on all four plans, within the credits.
- **A free plan and mobile apps.** Three people can start at no cost, and Attio's site links iOS and Android apps.

And these are the reasons teams look at the price again: the features that make Attio different from a plain contact list, such as custom objects, sequences and call recording, start on Pro; every extra user adds a monthly fee on the same plan; and heavy AI use is metered, so a credit pack can add to the bill. If you are weighing a switch, the [Attio alternative comparison](/compare/attio-alternative) puts the two products side by side, and our guide to a [customizable CRM](/blog/customizable-crm) explains what a flexible data model needs.

description: Answers about following up on client conversations with Customermates, setup, open source and hosting, why the CRM is affordable and why a focused CRM.
faq:
  faqs:
    - id: followup-faq-1
      title: How does Customermates help me follow up on every client conversation?
      content: Connect LinkedIn and email; on Customermates Cloud from Pro you can also connect WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram. Conversations sit on the client record when the contact details match or you link them. Mate, the built-in assistant, or your own AI agent (Claude, ChatGPT or any agent that speaks MCP) drafts the reply and the next step when you ask or on a routine you set up, and you review, edit and send. The next step sits on the client with a date, and the dashboard shows what is due. Customermates sends no reminders, so due steps show up on the dashboard.
    - id: followup-faq-2
      title: How do I set up my workspace?
      content: "On Customermates Cloud, describe your business in one sentence and Mate builds the workspace: lists, pipeline, dashboard and playbook. You can skip that and create lists yourself in Configure, or import an Excel workbook. A self-hosted instance starts empty."
    - id: followup-faq-3
      title: Is it open source, and where is my data?
      content: "The core is open source (AGPL-3.0) and can be self-hosted with the Starter feature set: no inbox and no Mate. The Customermates Cloud database runs in the AWS Frankfurt region; application hosting and other providers, including US providers, are listed on the subprocessors page. Customermates is made in Germany."
    - id: pricing-faq-1
      title: Why is your CRM so affordable?
      content: We operate without investors and deliberately keep our costs low. This lets us keep prices low for our customers.
    - id: pricing-faq-2
      title: How does your CRM remain reliable long-term?
      content: We grow organically and invest directly in the product and support. This keeps the product maintained for the long term.
    - id: pricing-faq-3
      title: Why a specialized CRM?
      content: Large CRM suites bundle marketing, service and sales and charge for modules many small teams never use. Customermates focuses on customer records you configure, conversations from supported channels and AI access through MCP. You extend the rest through the REST API, webhooks or your own n8n instance.
    - id: pricing-faq-4
      title: How does your CRM improve productivity?
      content: We focus on a clear interface that needs little training. On Customermates Cloud a guided setup drafts your workspace for review, so you spend less time configuring and more time on your customers.
    - id: pricing-faq-5
      title: Why is your CRM a strong choice?
      content: We offer the CRM core most small client-facing teams actually need, plus MCP access for your own AI client, open APIs, and interfaces for a separate n8n instance. That gives you an open-source, AI-native CRM with flexible extensions and clear product boundaries.

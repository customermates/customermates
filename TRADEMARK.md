You may not remove, hide or replace the Customermates name, logo or notices in the software or in a product built from it, and you may not offer Customermates under another brand, without a written enterprise agreement that grants white-labelling. Embedding Customermates in your own product is also by agreement, and the Customermates branding stays visible. Ask through the [contact page](https://customermates.com/contact).

## Forks must rename and drop the logo

If you publish a modified version of Customermates, you must give it a different name and remove the Customermates logo and any wording that suggests it is the official product or comes from Customermates. Distribution of modified versions is limited by the [Sustainable Use License](./LICENSE.md) in any case: only free of charge and for non-commercial purposes, with a prominent notice that you changed the software.

## Fair use of the name

You may use the Customermates name to say truthfully what your own work is or does: that your tool works with Customermates, that you offer services for Customermates, that you wrote an article or a tutorial about it, or that you run it for your own team. Use the name as plain text, do not use the logo in your own logo or product name, and do not suggest that Customermates sponsors, endorses or is responsible for your work. Packages, integrations and templates for Customermates may say "for Customermates" or "works with Customermates"; their names must not be easy to mistake for an official Customermates package.
